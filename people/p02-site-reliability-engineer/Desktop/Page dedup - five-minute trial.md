# Why the five-minute rule fell over

9 April — note to self after the March readback

I tried treating anything inside five minutes as a repeat. It misses one delivery I meant to catch and takes out one I need to keep.

- **5 March, M04 → M05 (01:00 → 01:07 UTC).** The first item was still open when its reminder came through. Seven minutes would put M05 outside the cut-off and add a spurious first call.
- **8 March, M09 / M10 (02:10 / 02:13 UTC).** Both items were independently open. Three minutes would put M10 inside the cut-off and lose a first call.

Drop the cut-off as a rule. Use the time gap to find the pair, then trace the open item and its acknowledgement. If both deliveries point to the same continuous opening, link the second back to the first. A missing acknowledgement tells me to follow up the handover, even if I can already establish that the second page was a reminder. Keep a separate first call where the earlier item was cleared or the second item opened independently.
