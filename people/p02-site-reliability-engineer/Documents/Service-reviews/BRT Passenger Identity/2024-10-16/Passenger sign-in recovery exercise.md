# Passenger sign-in recovery exercise

16 October 2024, 09:00 UTC (10:00 BST)

Emma Wilkinson: decision owner. Daniel Jennings: operator. Carys Morgan: independent timekeeper.

## Scope and aim

Rehearse recovery of a simulated Passenger Identity sign-in failure in the exercise environment. This is not an incident. The target is 20 minutes from Carys's cue to a verified recovery, not just to completion of the operator's work.

## Running the exercise

At 09:00 UTC Carys gives the simulated failure cue and starts the clock. Daniel establishes the exercise scope and brings the proposed restoration and verification evidence to Emma as a recorded decision request. Daniel holds restoration until Emma's decision is recorded. Acknowledgement that the request has been received is not approval to begin.

After approval Daniel carries out restoration within the exercise environment and tells Carys when it is ready for verification. Carys records the results of two consecutive successful exercise sign-ins, one minute apart. She stops the clock when she logs the second success. She may ask for a precise time without prompting Daniel's actions.

If 20 minutes passes, Carys keeps the clock running to the same finish. Anyone may stop the exercise if it becomes unsafe or leaves its agreed scope; record a stop and its reason rather than calling it a recovery. Read the times back together afterwards.
