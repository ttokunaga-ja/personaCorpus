Passenger Identity rehearsals: decision owner

Reliability team | Revised 18 October 2024 | Emma Wilkinson

This page applies to Passenger Identity recovery rehearsals. The decision and access described here are confined to the exercise record.

Roles

Operator — Daniel Jennings. Record the request with its scope and verification evidence. Wait for the recorded decision before starting restoration. A receipt acknowledgement is not approval. During a transfer keep restoration on hold.

Primary — Emma Wilkinson. Own the decision initially. Record the decision in the exercise record, or explicitly hand ownership over. If a logged request has no decision after two minutes, initiate the transfer to the named, checked delegate. Once the delegate has acknowledged ownership, do not issue a parallel approval.

Delegate — Andrew Cartwright. Check the scope, then acknowledge acceptance of sole decision ownership in the exercise record. Only after that acknowledgement, record the decision and tell the operator the outcome. The named owner after acceptance is Andrew; there is one decision owner, not two simultaneous approvers.

Observer — Carys Morgan. Issue the simulated cue, keep the clock and record the request, transfer, decision and verification markers. Ask for precise times if needed; do not coach the decision or stop the clock merely because the target has passed.

If the handover cannot be completed

A transfer may take place at two minutes without a decision on the logged request, or immediately if Emma explicitly hands over. A proposed delegate is not the owner until they accept in the record. If no checked delegate accepts, hold restoration and contact Anjali Shah. Do not borrow or share another person's access to make the entry.

Andrew's exercise-only access was rechecked on 18 October: reading the request, recording a rehearsal decision and seeing both verification results all passed. Check access again before relying on a delegate for a later rehearsal.
