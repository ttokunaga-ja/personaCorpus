19 September 2024 · Customer Support · Internal

At today's review, Operations agreed to keep the 10-minute archive interval for a test under load in the isolated archive. The 15-minute interval will serve as the comparator. For internal questions about what was approved, see the signed-off trial scope (BRT-CD-20240919-001 v1.0, PDF).
