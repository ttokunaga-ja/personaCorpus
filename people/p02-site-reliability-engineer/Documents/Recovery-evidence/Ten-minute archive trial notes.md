# What the ten-minute trial showed

Daniel — for the afternoon review

The exercise environment was configured on a ten-minute capture schedule. At the 10:00 UTC reference on 11 May the eligible set had finished at 09:55 and covered records through 09:51 UTC. We confirmed the set at 10:16. Its point was nine minutes old, within the inclusive 15-minute limit by six minutes.

The schedule describes when captures are intended to run. Coverage is the latest record time represented by the set; it is the point we compare with the reference. Completion tells us whether the set was finished by the reference and therefore eligible. It cannot move the coverage marker forward.

This is one observed pass under the exercise conditions, not a guarantee for every later point. Keep the 19 August 2024 result in the evidence: coverage 09:38 against 10:00, 22 minutes old and seven outside the limit. The two observations do not establish the cause of the difference.
