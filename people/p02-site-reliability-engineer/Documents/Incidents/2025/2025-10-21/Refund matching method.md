# Use the refund due date

Matthew, Claire — for the Westbury readback, start with refunds due on 20 or 21 October UTC that were still outstanding at their own due time. All included due times are at or before the 21 October 10:00 UTC cutoff. The due date determines the row; it is not the date a customer called or the time a submission was attempted.

Match each refund in that cohort to its original booking. Check the whole cohort before reporting the number without a booking: an unchecked refund is not a confirmed match or a confirmed missing booking. Then classify the latest submission state at 10:00 UTC as submitted or awaiting submission. A submission is a processing stage, not proof of money reaching the customer's account.

Count a refund once even if the desk received repeated calls or there were multiple submission attempts. Keep the booking match separate from the submission state so we can answer both questions without treating a late refund as a missing booking.
