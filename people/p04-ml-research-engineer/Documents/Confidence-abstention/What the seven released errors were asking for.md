# What the seven released errors were asking for

June 8, 2026 — Vanessa Nguyen, shared with Emily Chen

At 2 p.m. Emily and I read the seven errors among the 72 documents that move from hold to automatic routing at 0.45, along with one correctly predicted control from each intake route. We chose the requested action from the body before opening the saved prediction and reference. The phrases below are from our joint reading record. A routing change does not change the saved label.

## Change versus check: three

- **CA-281** (Shared inbox): “Thursday instead” asks for a change, so the reference is Reschedule. The later Monday confirmation is background, not the action requested now. The saved Schedule check would route a change as a confirmation.
- **CA-342** (Shared inbox): “move the appointment to Friday” names the action and the new day. That supports Reschedule rather than the saved Schedule check.
- **CA-718** (Web form): “can we change the visit to Tuesday” asks to change the visit, not just confirm its time. The reference is Reschedule; the saved prediction is Schedule check.

## Current status versus past cancellation: two

- **CA-614** (Service desk relay): after the account of an earlier cancellation, “Has the pump repair been signed off?” asks about the current state of the repair. Job status is the reference; Cancel request is the saved prediction. The earlier cancellation is not a present instruction to cancel.
- **CA-547** (Shared inbox): “please tell me whether the work is complete” asks for progress on the work. That supports Job status, while the saved prediction is Cancel request. A request for a completion update is not itself a cancellation.

## Price versus work: two

- **CA-390** (Web form): “what a replacement seal would cost” asks for a price. The reference is Quote request, not the saved New work request; the wording does not order the work.
- **CA-433** (Shared inbox): “please send a price for the replacement valve” asks for a price to be sent. Quote request fits the stated action; the saved New work request would treat the request as work intake.

## Direct controls

The three correct controls make their requested actions explicit: CA-126 (Web form), “please move my visit to Wednesday,” is Reschedule; CA-462 (Shared inbox), “is the repair complete yet,” is Job status; CA-803 (Service desk relay), “please quote for a replacement filter,” is Quote request. Each saved prediction matches its reference. They are useful contrasts, not a representative sample of correct releases.

Across the seven, the request and other context can point toward adjacent actions. This reading explains the observed labels in these selected cases; it does not establish a general cause, a field error rate, or the cost of correcting a wrong route. The July guide asks us to identify the requested action in the full body rather than infer it from a destination.
