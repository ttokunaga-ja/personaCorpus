# The two cases we lost at the join

October 9 — Fatima and I read the two fixed-holdout records missed only by the first-and-last window. Both effective evidence endpoints fall in the >50–75% band of the full body. These are not the August development examples.

In one record, the operative repair instruction itself is in the middle. The beginning describes the condition and the ending closes the record, but neither supplies that instruction. Removing the middle removes the action, not merely an explanation of it.

In the other, the reinspection instruction remains intelligible only with a condition in the middle that determines its deadline. Reading the surviving words without that clause changes when the action is required. The missing unit is a qualifier, not the whole instruction.

For the next comparison, inspect each record separately: retain the reference label, each window's actual prediction, and the effective evidence and whether the complete phrase is present. Recovering the repair instruction does not establish that the deadline clause will also survive or that the second prediction will be corrected. Do not infer a prediction from evidence visibility alone.
