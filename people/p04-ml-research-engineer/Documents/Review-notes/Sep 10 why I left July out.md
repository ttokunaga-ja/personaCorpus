# July beside April?

For Michael: I tried putting R184 at 0.873 immediately to the right of the April linear classifier at 0.832. It looks like a before-and-after feature improvement, but it is not that comparison. April's score is on the fixed 935 evaluation documents, with the majority-class baseline at 0.021. July's R184 is on a different 1,200 evaluation documents; its comparison is R173 on those same July documents, 0.852. Even the comparison model is different.

The horizontal distance between 0.832 and 0.873 cannot tell us what adding character n-grams did. For N8 I propose keeping the April figure to the two methods actually compared there. The July report can stand on its own conditions and same-set comparison, rather than adding a third bar that invites a misleading subtraction.
