# February 11 — wording without the split figure

Rejected: “Cases are split and scored in a common evaluation set.” This can be read as a score for each case, and it hides the fact that linked bodies must stay together during allocation.

Try two sentences instead:

“Intake documents linked by supporting records are treated as one case and assigned to the same partition. In the common evaluation partition, each received body is scored separately.”

The two methods must be evaluated on the same labeled bodies. The three bodies in the sketch are only a schematic; neither that number nor the size of the box is a dataset count. The portal does not take the figure, so the sentences must carry the distinction on their own.
