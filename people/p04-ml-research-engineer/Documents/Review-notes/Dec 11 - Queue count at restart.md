# Queue count at restart

December 11, 2024 · before I run the lunch comparison

I need two counters. When I take the next item, it leaves **waiting to start** and goes into **in review**. It stays in **outstanding** until I finish it. The shortcut for the balance table is review arrivals through that minute minus review completions through that minute.

For Thursday's replay, the seven morning reviews are already done and 18 more have arrived by restart. At 13:00 PST I pick up the first of those 18. The split immediately after pickup is 17 waiting plus one in review. Still 18 outstanding. The first completion is at 13:03, so that's where the balance drops to 17.

When I label the results, keep “waiting to start” for the first counter and “outstanding” for the sum. Subtracting starts in the second column would draw a drop at 13:00 that never happened.
