# June 2 — keep the predicted label

Before opening the metric draft: removing a hold changes where the document goes, not the model's answer. The automatic route receives the saved top prediction for that document; it does not switch to the runner-up label. Compare that same prediction against the same reference label under both cutoffs.

The July 2024 guide left 126 bodies on Hold without a gold label. Those are not part of the 800 reference-labelled documents in this comparison. Don't fold the two kinds of hold together: one is a human disposition with no gold label; the other is a threshold applied to a saved prediction on a labelled document.
