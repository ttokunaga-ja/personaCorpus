# Forum abstract — possible order

Start with the question: can a single request be read from the received body alone? The reader needs the scope before the categories. A day or object may be present without an identifiable action.

Then explain the judgment: two independent readings and the phrases each reader used; compare the evidence even when their candidate labels match. If the body does not establish one intent, record Hold and leave the reference label empty. The July guide gives a compact schedule pair.

End with the comparison: group linked documents by case before allocating partitions; score individual bodies. Both methods use the same labeled evaluation documents. This is a plan, not a result.

Alternative opening: introduce the intake channels first. I am wary of that order: it could imply the channel is evidence for an intent before we have stated the body-only rule. Keep the channel description subordinate to the reading question.
