# Agreement from the same missing clue

May 23, 2024

Fatima and I went through the 33 disagreements from the 240 independent pilot reads today. That is a useful starting queue, but the 207 matching choices should not be treated as automatically supported.

“Checking on the visit.” could be a question about the time of the visit or about the progress of the work. If we both select the same interpretation, the match tells us we chose alike; it does not put either missing detail into the sentence. Neither a time enquiry nor a progress enquiry is established by these words alone.

For the next read, I want each of us to keep the phrase that supports the candidate even when our candidates match. We can compare the words as well as the labels and return to the original body where the evidence is unclear. This is a proposal for the next procedure, not another count of the pilot disagreements.
