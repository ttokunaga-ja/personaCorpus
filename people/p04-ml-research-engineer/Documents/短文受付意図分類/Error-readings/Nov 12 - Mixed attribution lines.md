# Mixed attribution lines — November 12

Vanessa, here's the difference I wanted to flag after the cue count. Keep the received line breaks when you compare these.

```text
Please move my appointment to Friday.
> My appointment is on Tuesday.
```

Here the second line starts with a visible `>`. Start the cut there and the whole first sentence survives. We can still read the request to move the appointment to Friday, but Tuesday has gone with the older line.

```text
Please move my appointment to Friday, and as I said
in my earlier message, the appointment is on Tuesday; please confirm the change.
```

Here “as I said” starts on the first line and the attribution finishes on the second. The old Tuesday wording is followed on that same second line by “please confirm the change,” which belongs to the current reply. Cutting at the line with “earlier message” leaves the dangling “as I said” and drops the live confirmation request along with Tuesday. Moving the cut up a line would take the reschedule request too.

I would record the attribution as wrapped and the boundary as unclear. The attribution helps us spot older wording. The line containing it can still hold words the sender wants us to act on now.
