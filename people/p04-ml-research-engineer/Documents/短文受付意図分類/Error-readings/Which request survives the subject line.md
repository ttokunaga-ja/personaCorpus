# Which request survives the subject line?

July 9, 2024

Question from my first pass: am I assigning the request in the body, or the one I expect after seeing a subject? The reading view excludes the subject. To check myself, I tried the contrast with “Invoice correction” removed.

- “Could you send the invoice again?” still names an action and its object. Request document is supported by “send the invoice again”; it does not ask to correct an invoice.
- “Please fix this.” requests a fix, but “this” does not identify what is to be fixed. The subject might suggest an invoice correction, but it is not part of the text the reader receives. I cannot establish Correct record from this body alone.

Show Fatima the second wording at the boundary discussion. Ask whether either of us can point to the object in the received body without importing the subject. Keep the initial candidate as a reading, not as proof of the writer's intent.
