| Readable source date | The date of the appointment being moved, with its supporting words in the text read. Record the original's source date on the received-body row. |
| Readable requested date | The date the current reply asks to move it to, with its supporting words in the text read. |
| Reason for blank date | Identify which date is blank and why the words available for that reading do not establish it. |
