9 September, after the Room 2 consultation

I asked, “Can the next person use your sentence?” and the person I was speaking with waited for me to say whether the time was right. I had intended the question to send them back to the evidence, but it sounded like another request for an answer check.

We tried a different starting point in the pair: without looking at the advice sentence, the listener first tried to find the supporting clause and the counter record entry from the writer's references. Where the listener could not get there, the writer added the missing location and they tried again. That interrupted the habit of asking me for the conclusion; they had a route to check together before deciding what the sentence could support.

Next consultation, give someone the listener's role first. Ask them to find the clause and record before I ask whether the next person can use the sentence. Then bring the advice back and check whether it follows from what they found.
