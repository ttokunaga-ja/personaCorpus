12 June, after class

With the table still on the screen, I heard “stops” for the nine in the lunch row. After the room sketch went out, I heard “readers” in the talk about moving the notice. We had gone from the entrance line to a reason for moving it without stopping to ask what the sketch could tell us. I left “cabinet in the way?” on the board beside “9 stops / 20 min”. Keep the question mark when I bring this back.

Kept all 14 anonymous paragraphs for Erin and me to read together. I didn't mark them in class. When we read them, look first at the words attached to nine, then at how the writer uses the cabinet. A clear request to move the notice can still rest on a misreading of the count.

Try two starts on the paper next time: “At the entrance line we counted …” and “From the sketch, I think …”. Give each its own line before asking what the writer wants the room coordinator to do.
