Summer courses for staff

15 June 2026 · Janice Mitchell

Two courses with Elmwood Adult Learning Cooperative are planned for July to September. Please discuss a nomination with your immediate manager before the deadline.

Reading Conditions at Work

36 planned places, primarily for counter staff, with places for duty leads and shift staff. Practise reading a notice alongside instructions and records, then write a decision note identifying the applicable instruction and the facts supporting the message to visitors.

Building Proposals from Evidence

24 planned places for team leads, staff responsible for improvement proposals and coordinating roles. Compare options and prepare a one-page recommendation with evidence and an action for an unconfirmed condition.

Nomination

Speak with your immediate manager about the course relevant to your work. Managers should submit nominations by 3:00 p.m. on Wednesday, 24 June. We will notify staff when places are confirmed.
