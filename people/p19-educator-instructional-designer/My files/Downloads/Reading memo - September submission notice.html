Bring your work-in-progress decision memo to Room 2 on Wednesday 9 September at 13:00. The optional consultation lasts 30 minutes. Submit your one-page decision memo by Friday 11 September at 16:00, Toronto time. Give the advice you would offer at the counter, identify the applicable source and section, and connect it to the recorded facts. Include what still needs checking and whom you would ask.
