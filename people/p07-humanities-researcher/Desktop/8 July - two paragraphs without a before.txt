8 July — two paragraphs without a before

§2

On 29 January 1866 the secretary communicated to the treasurer the committee's decision to defer a demand for an outstanding subscription. The instruction at CR/14/A f.173r withholds the demand until 12 February and directs the treasurer to resume ordinary reminders after the 12th. It describes the timing of collection for that account; it neither cancels the debt nor establishes the member's use of the room.

§4

On 19 March 1866 the secretary instructed the attendant that a member in arrears could consult books in the room, but that the arrears were to be collected before a book was taken away (CR/14/A f.186r). On 16 May 1867 the secretary conveyed an individual permission to take one book out and return it the following Thursday, leaving the outstanding account with the treasurer (f.184r, LA-042). These are instructions for distinct occasions; their dates do not establish a change of general rule or a causal connection between them.

Not to use: “Exceptions came to be permitted.” The surviving passages do not establish when exceptions began. The verso receipt of 17 May is not the date of the permission or of the return.
