Open CR/13/A f.088r first. Put my finger on the instruction to list the guests separately and put the presenting member beside each name. Then read him the sentence at old note 58. Is there an actual entry admitting a guest on this sheet, or have I followed Lenoir from the instructions at the door into a different book? If it is a different book, I want the entry and the date.

Next CR/14/B f.199r, beside Hartmann p. 114 (old note 62). Read back to the October limit on f.198r and mark where the committee says it will look at the journal. Ask which winter Thursdays I can actually point to. Keep the dates of any later notices beside the duty entries for those nights.

Once those two are on the table, work through the rest from the sentences. Keep the catalogue to one side, so we can use it for finding leaves without mistaking it for the copy.
