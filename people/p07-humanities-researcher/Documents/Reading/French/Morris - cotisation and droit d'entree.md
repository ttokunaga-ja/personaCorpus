# Cotisation / droit d’entrée

Cotisation: contribution associated with membership, potentially recurrent. Droit d’entrée: a charge at admission. They should not be put into one column merely because both are sums paid by a member. Neither label alone tells me the interval or proves that the sum was actually collected.

Beside an amount, copy the surrounding words about échéance and renewal: is it annual, quarterly, or another period? Note who receives it (trésorier, another officer, or an intermediary), and the occasion on which payment is due. If an entry seems to cover several people, preserve the collective entry and look for its breakdown rather than assigning the whole sum to the named payer.

For the July reading: check the local wording against the rule and any receipt or account beside it. An isolated « droit » or « cotisation » is a prompt to inspect context, not a decision about the payment type. Leave doubtful readings open and retain the French alongside any English gloss.
