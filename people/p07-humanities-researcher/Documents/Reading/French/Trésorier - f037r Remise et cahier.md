# Remise: the money and the person carrying it

CR/12/B f. 037r, ll. 6–8. The treasurer asks the attendant to hand him the envelope containing two francs offered for coal. The object of *remettre* is the envelope and its contents; the recipient is the treasurer. The following instruction, *porter au cahier*, concerns the subscriptions, entered separately. It does not turn the two francs into a subscription instalment.

The attendant is the person asked to carry the money, not necessarily the donor. Nor does the request say that the attendant owes two francs personally. This matters beside f. 040, where a total brought by an attendant had sometimes been entered under the carrier's name: the treasurer's question there does not identify the individual shares.

For a doubtful entry: first read the address and opening to identify writer and addressee; then mark the object of *remettre* and the recipient; then follow the next sentence to see what is to be entered and under whose name. Check the envelope instructions at f. 036 and the surrounding letters before assigning a payer. Keep the French and any uncertainty with the gloss. Compare the earlier note on *cotisation* and *droit d’entrée*: neither the word nor the carrier alone establishes the purpose of a payment.
