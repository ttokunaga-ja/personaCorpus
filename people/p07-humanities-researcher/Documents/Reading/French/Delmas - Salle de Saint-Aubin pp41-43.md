# Delmas — who approved the room?

Émile Delmas, *Les débuts de la salle de Saint-Aubin*, pp. 41–43. Reading for the opening, 6 June 2025.

Delmas builds the sequence from the regulations and collection papers in box 12. I can follow his account on the page. Before giving the sequence to the society in my own prose, I need to be able to point to three quite different records.

**41 · Who established it?** Delmas attributes the society’s establishment to the town’s approval. For CR/12/A f.018 the inventory gives a gap. Look for a signed establishment decision, or a copy of it, naming the approving body, giving the date and identifying the society covered by the decision. Permission to use a room would need to be distinguished from approval to establish the society.

**42 · When did the rule begin to apply?** Delmas takes the day of posting as the effective date. CR/12/A f.020 falls in the same gap. I would need a dated copy of the posting and the clause that says when the rule takes effect. Match the rule named in the posting to the rule in the clause before joining the dates.

**43 · What was put up at the door?** Delmas reads the collection papers as evidence that the names of those in arrears were displayed at the entrance. Candidate CR/12/B f.061 is missing. Start with the collecting record for the names, then look for a posting copy or a secretary’s direction that specifies whose names, what was to go up and where. For an actual display, look for a dated posted list or a record that the direction was carried out.

My copy of **CR/12/A f.012r** is the secretary’s notice of 4 February 1861. It gives one franc fifty centimes per quarter and a first payment deadline of 15 February, and directs the treasurer to enter each remittance. I can cite the terms of payment and the recording instruction from it. I cannot get an approving authority, a date of effect tied to posting, or any instruction to display names from that face.
