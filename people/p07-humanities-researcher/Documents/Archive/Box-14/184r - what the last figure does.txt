CR/14/A f.184r–v; LA-042. We began with the date line rather than the enlarged final digit alone. The last figure has a sloping finishing stroke with no return; the paper line crossing the horizontal does not make a closed 4.

We then looked at the same hand's “17” in the fourth body line. The 7 has the same sloping finish and no return. On the recto we read “jeudi 16 mai 1867”. The weekday is a separate check: 16 May 1867 was Thursday; 16 May 1864 was Monday.

On turning the leaf, the receipt is in another hand: “17 mai 1867”. That is the date of receipt on f.184v, not the date of the secretary's communication on f.184r and not the return date.

Old reading: 1864. Revised reading: recto sent 16 May 1867; verso received 17 May 1867. The old order of the chapter passages needs to be revisited; the correction does not by itself tell us when a practice began.
