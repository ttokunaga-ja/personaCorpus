Mapleton Falls Borough

Quarry Road resurfacing — Addendum 1

Procurement reference: PW-23-03

Posted: April 18, 2023

Bid submission deadline: May 2, 2023

To prospective bidders

This addendum clarifies the limits of the Quarry Road resurfacing work described in the bid documents. The starting point is Weighhouse Gate. The ending point remains Briar Street. Read the bid documents' description of the project limits accordingly; the segment is Weighhouse Gate to Briar Street.

The deadline for submission of bids remains May 2, 2023. All other bid requirements remain in effect. Bidders must acknowledge receipt of Addendum 1 with their bids so that the borough can determine that the clarification was received. Include the procurement reference and the addendum number in the acknowledgment.

Mapleton Falls Borough Procurement Office
