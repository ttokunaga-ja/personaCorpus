For Fran — Quarry gate, May 16

Candidate: The old weighhouse gate beside Quarry Road, a landmark named in the borough's 2023 resurfacing bid documents.

The picture shows the gate sign and the Quarry Road sign, not the entire stretch. PW-23-03 names Weighhouse Gate to Briar Street. The April 18, 2023 addendum establishes the Briar Street endpoint; it is not visible here. Use the gate as a way to distinguish the stretch in the bid documents. Don't say the photograph shows where construction began: the bid boundary and a construction start are different claims.
