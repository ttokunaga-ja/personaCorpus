Arrival cards — December 10

I printed the card double-sided and folded it for the readback. From the receiver's side the folded edge still exposed the source field. That makes the card a poor way to see what was actually heard.

For Thursday's check: put the schedule with the speaker, a blank readback sheet with the receiver, and the answer key separately with the checker. Do not turn the key toward the receiver before the readback. If a calendar date is not heard, leave that field blank and ask for it; do not infer a date from Friday or “tomorrow.” Check only after the receiver has spoken.
