# Say the time limit before asking

Ayesha — a small card for the desk, in the order we want the choice to be heard.

“Thank you for joining. We want to understand how you do this work and where it is difficult. You can pause or stop at any time, and you can skip a question.”

“If you agree to recording, the research team will use the audio to listen again. We keep it for 180 days after the interview. If you would rather not be recorded, I can take notes instead.”

“May I record our conversation?” Pause and wait. If no: “That's fine; I'll take notes without recording.” If yes: “Thank you. I'll start recording now.” Start only after permission.

Today, when I indicated that identifying information might appear, you stopped the share and recording, asked me to describe the work without those details, and asked again before resuming audio. Keep that pause: don't let the explanation of the work depend on showing a name or destination.

If someone asks about using a quote beyond what they agreed to, or about a use outside this research: “I'll take that question back to Jennifer before we decide.” Don't promise the additional use in the room.

Jennifer
