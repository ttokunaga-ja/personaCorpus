# My tally hid the source

29 October

Yesterday I made a stroke each time Adam went back to the route list, then crossed the first four strokes and started the next group. At 22 minutes I had the stroke and the row. I let him go on and never came back to ask which item on the row he'd looked at. At 27 I can at least point to stop 04, where he opened the Note. The space beside that mark is still empty where the other piece of information ought to go.

Keep the tally. It gets me back to six places in the session. Put the sources next to the strokes, with the screen item separate. The old time on a piece of paper and the time written into a handoff draft both took him back to Window. If I write down only Window, I lose the difference again.

On the next one, let the person finish a pass through the story before circling back. First ask what they had in front of them or had just heard. Then ask what they looked at when they went back, and what they put the two together for if they describe a comparison. Leave a gap in the account if I couldn't get that part.
