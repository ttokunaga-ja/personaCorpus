# Unresolved needed its own place

January 28, 2025

I nearly used Other action as the place to put anything I couldn't defend as a comparison. On the 22nd I had UX-034 there because the person came back to the same row and I couldn't find a second source. Brian had Unresolved. All I could show him was the return to the row. I couldn't point to an account of what the person compared or what else they went there to do. We left the interview Unresolved. Compare UX-044, where the person told us they were selecting the next route and the selection actually moved to a different route. I have something to cite for Other action there.

Adam's interview (UX-041) caught me going the other way. At 09 minutes he compared his note of the earlier window with Window. At 13 he compared a relief vehicle number he'd heard with Vehicle. At 18 he checked the callback message against Held, and at 33 he checked the time in his handoff draft against Window. Four explained checks are enough for Cross-check on the interview sheet. On the event sheet, the return to the same row at 22 and the opening of stop 04's Note at 27 still have unknown comparison targets. I can write down the Note he opened without knowing what he was trying to verify by opening it.

Next time I tally this, keep the unfinished slips beside the tally. Fill in what I saw, then ask what they had in hand and what they did next. If I get a second source, write both sides down. If they describe a different next action, note where in the interview they said it. If I can't get either explanation, leave the slip marked Unresolved even when the screen field is filled in. The interview code gets one row. The unanswered returns keep their own lines.

We finished with six Cross-check, three Other action and three Unresolved across the 12 interviews. Four participants had already handled routine and exception handoffs independently and still checked another source. I can put away the novice explanation and keep those two unanswered returns on Adam's sheet.
