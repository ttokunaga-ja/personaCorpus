# Tablet handling field notes

November 20, 2024 · Kenton

My notes gave the tablet move too much weight. In T3 the worker took it from E to F at 19:56. The event that changed the last confirmed bin came six minutes later, when the worker checked the stock at F-12 and pressed Save at 20:02. On return to E at 20:05, that was still a valid confirmation in F. The E shelf label, stock and the worker’s own notes were what got the count going again at E-22.

T4 is where my shorthand can mislead the next reader. I had F-19 and a time in the margin. Brian left the operation unconfirmed on his first pass. The sequence we checked on Friday has F-18 saved at 20:12 and the F-19 quantity entered at 20:13. F-18 was the reference on return to F. The worker recounted F-19 at 20:19 and saved at 20:22. Keep that last time on its own line.

## For my next field sheet

| When the worker took the tablet | Evidence of confirmation |
|---|---|
| Record the pickup time and where the worker was headed. Put the return time on its own line. | Record the aisle, bin and minute of Save and what I could see that confirms it. Put a visible quantity entry on a separate line marked Entry. |

If I lose sight of the worker’s hands, leave the confirmation cell blank. At the first pause after they return, ask which bin they last checked and saved. Put the answer in the margin as a follow-up and keep the direct observation separate. If I still can’t establish the Save, leave the cell blank.
