## Detailed action notes

The revised narrative should avoid presenting a solution before defining the decision. The preferred sequence is: the current Finance workstream problem; the risk of maintaining the present state; the limited pilot response; the measures and controls that will test it; and the next decision gate. It should also state what the pilot will not do: it will not select an enterprise platform, clean all historic data, or change accounting policy.

The financial chart needs a precise legend. Nina will show the specific allocations currently under consideration—¥9.6M for process redesign and training, ¥7.4M for data remediation and reporting configuration, and ¥4.1M for contingency and assurance—within the ¥42.8M envelope. ### Pilot assurance

Theo will add a pilot assurance section. It must name the weekly exception report as an evidence artifact and specify who prepares, reviews, and accepts it. If reported exceptions exceed the agreed tolerance for two consecutive reporting cycles, the plan requires a pause and Finance workstream diagnosis. The partner view is that this safeguard demonstrates discipline rather than fragility.

Keiko will test a sample of claims from every executive section. A source label, hypothesis label, or decision label is required. If the source is an interview or workshop, the pack should state the limitation: participants’ views may not represent all functions, and quantitative effects require separate validation. This protects the team from over-reading consensus.
