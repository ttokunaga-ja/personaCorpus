**Working status:** In progress

## Outcome for today

By close of day, the team should have a concise, evidence-backed view of the Client Alpha Phase 1 recommendation, a clear list of decisions that require client input, and an updated view of Alpha–Beta capacity. The objective is not to complete every analysis thread. It is to make the next steering conversation easier: the audience should be able to see the proposed move, its finance implication, the evidence confidence, and the owner of each unresolved dependency.

The portfolio link matters. Client Beta Phase 2 is moving in parallel and shares specialist capacity with Alpha. A locally attractive Alpha choice that consumes constrained capacity or obscures a financial control is not yet a portfolio-ready recommendation. We will therefore use the Finance anchor of ¥42.8M as a visible guardrail rather than a retrospective check.

## Priorities in order

1. Establish the decision path for the Alpha synthesis.
   Avery will identify the two or three decisions the client must make next, then test whether the current draft gives each decision a clear owner, evidence basis, and date. The synthesis should favor a practical first move over a broad future-state narrative.

2. Confirm the minimum Finance control pack.
   Nina will convert the existing spend and scope view into a lightweight gate. The working proposal is four fields: accountable owner, supporting evidence, spend implication, and due date. This is enough structure to surface exceptions without creating a heavy approval process.

3. Make the evidence confidence visible.
   Theo will classify inputs as decision-ready, directional, or unavailable. Decision-ready inputs can support a recommendation. Directional inputs can explain a hypothesis but should carry a limitation. Unavailable inputs should become either a named follow-up or an explicit boundary on the conclusion.

4. Reconcile shared staffing.
   Keiko will compare planned Alpha work against Beta Phase 2 demand by person and week. The output is a simple allocation view with a clear answer to whether the team has headroom for the planned synthesis and Finance work. Any conflict should be framed as a sequencing choice, not treated as an individual scheduling issue.

## Questions that must close or be deliberately carried

- Which Alpha workstream has the strongest near-term value case, and what observable outcome supports it?
- What evidence is required before Finance can approve the next release or confirm the current forecast?
- Which specialist days are genuinely shared with Beta Phase 2, and what is the cost of protecting them?
- What data quality threshold is sufficient for a steering recommendation?
- Which matters require client confirmation, and which can the project team resolve through working judgment?

An answer does not need to be perfect to be useful. It does need an owner, a confidence level, and a decision consequence. Where the answer is provisional, we will document the assumption and set a revalidation date.

## Finance and portfolio guardrails

The team will maintain a simple forecast-to-plan bridge in the live calculation workbook. Any projected movement of more than ¥0.5M from the approved Alpha envelope is a discussion item for Nina and Avery before it appears as an implied commitment in a deck. The consolidated tracker is the source for portfolio headroom; the Alpha and Beta workbooks are operating views, not competing versions of the total.

The budget is not a reason to suppress a valuable option. It is a prompt to show the trade-off clearly: what would be deferred, what capacity would move, and what evidence would justify the change. If a decision cannot be reconciled to the portfolio view, it should be flagged rather than smoothed over.

## End-of-day definition of done

The working deck contains a coherent narrative, with claims that are either supported or visibly marked as draft. The question log has a named owner and due date for every material open item. The staffing plan shows available capacity and any conflicts across Alpha and Beta. The live calculation has no formula errors and the Finance control pack has a documented next gate.

At 16:30, Avery will decide whether the synthesis is ready for client pre-wire or requires one additional evidence pass. If it is not ready, the team will specify the smallest next test rather than expanding the scope. That is how today’s work stays useful to Client Alpha Phase 1 while keeping the Summit Vale portfolio under control.
