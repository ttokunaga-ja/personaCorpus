I receive a consolidated view several days after close, then spend time resolving questions about margin and cash. [14:25] Avery: We also heard that commercial rebates and service credits can obscure margin timing. Is that a material risk?

[14:34] Avery: How should we distinguish achievable savings from an aspirational sourcing pipeline?

The Finance workstream should reconcile any claimed savings to the ledger, even if that is only a directional test in Phase 1.
