## Synthesis

The prior-work map suggests that performance is only intelligible when the model boundary and the measurement boundary travel together. HF-Eval-S1/S2 v3.2 has 2,400 synthetic records: S1 contributes 1,600 and S2 contributes 800, spanning retrieve, route, refine, resolve, and abstain.
