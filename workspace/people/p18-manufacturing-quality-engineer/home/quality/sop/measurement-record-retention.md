# Measurement Record Retention

ForgeLine retains controlled Product Alpha and Product Beta measurement evidence
with the associated inspection decision. This synthetic procedure is effective
2026-08-11 and uses NCR-026 when escalation is required.

## Required record elements

- Product identifier, required characteristic, method, and actual result.
- The +/- 0.18 mm acceptance boundary where it applies.
- Reviewer decision, disposition, and NCR-026 linkage for exceptions.

## Review cadence

Quality Engineering completes a quarterly completeness check and keeps only the
approved revision in the controlled record set.
