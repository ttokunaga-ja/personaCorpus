# ±0.18 mm quick guide

**Applies to:** ForgeLine Product Alpha inspections on 2026-08-11.

## Use the acceptance band correctly

1. Confirm the nominal requirement on the controlled inspection record.
2. Measure the defined feature at IP-03 with a clean, zeroed gage.
3. Compare the actual millimetre result against nominal ±0.18 mm.
4. Record the actual result, gage status, and PASS or HOLD.

## When to hold

Place the sample in HOLD if the reading is outside the acceptance band, the gage fails to hold zero, or the measurement surface is damaged. Start the **NCR-026** containment workflow and wait for quality disposition.

## A useful reminder

The tolerance is a decision boundary, not a rounding instruction. Record the observed value as measured; do not alter it to force a pass.
