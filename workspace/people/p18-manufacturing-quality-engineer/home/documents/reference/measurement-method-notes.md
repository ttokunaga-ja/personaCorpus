# Measurement method notes

## Controlled reference

ForgeLine Product Alpha reference date: 2026-08-11. This note supports synthetic quality training around NCR-026 and the +/- 0.18 mm acceptance band.

## Method sequence

1. Confirm the measuring device is clean, zeroed, and appropriate for the stated feature.
2. Stabilize the part on the defined datum before recording the observed value.
3. Compare the observed value to the nominal and the +/- 0.18 mm band.
4. If a result is outside the band, preserve the observation and route the decision through the NCR-026 control path.

## Recording practice

Record the feature name, nominal, observed value, device status, and reviewer decision. A measurement is evidence; it is not a substitute for a disposition decision.
