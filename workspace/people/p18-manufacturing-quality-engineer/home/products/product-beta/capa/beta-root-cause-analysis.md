# Product Beta NCR-026 Root Cause Analysis

**Organization:** ForgeLine  
**Review date:** 2026-08-11  
**Product:** Product Beta  
**Containment tolerance:** ±0.18 mm

## Problem statement

During final dimensional review, the Product Beta locating feature measured +0.24 mm against the approved nominal position. The condition was recorded as NCR-026 before release. No customer material was involved; this is a synthetic internal quality record.

## 5-Why analysis

1. Why was the feature out of tolerance? The finish-pass offset was not applied after a tooling adjustment.
2. Why was the offset not applied? The adjustment record did not require independent verification.
3. Why was verification absent? The work instruction treated setup and release checks as one activity.
4. Why were the activities combined? The CAPA template lacked a control-point field for dimensional sign-off.
5. Why did the template lack the field? Prior lessons did not flow into the controlled template review.

## Root cause and corrective direction

The systemic root cause is an incomplete controlled setup-to-release workflow. ForgeLine will separate adjustment, measurement, and release authorization; require a second-person verification of the ±0.18 mm criterion; and trend the first ten Product Beta lots after implementation.
