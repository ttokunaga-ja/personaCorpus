# Product Beta severity rationale

**ForgeLine | Design FMEA review | 2026-08-11**

This rationale applies Product Beta failure-mode ratings to the same synthetic control framework used for Product Alpha. The critical fit feature remains a dimensional tolerance of **±0.18 mm**. NCR-026 is the reference nonconformance for evaluating containment and verification coverage; it is not a claim about a real product or facility.

## Rating basis

| Severity | Design consequence | FMEA treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 9 | Functional loss at release gate | Mandatory design action and independent verification |
| 7 | Reduced assembly robustness | Control plan linkage and closure evidence |
| 5 | Detectable process variation | Sampling control and trend review |
| 3 | Cosmetic or documentation effect | Routine review in the next revision |

The Product Beta interface-fit scenario is rated 7 because an out-of-tolerance condition can prevent intended assembly alignment, while planned dimensional verification provides a controllable detection path. The rating remains synthetic and is used only to keep the FMEA, action matrix, and verification linkage aligned.

## Review decision

Residual risk is acceptable only after the action owner records objective evidence, the verification link is complete, and the closure register is updated. Any recurrence resembling NCR-026 restarts the review at containment rather than relying on a prior rating.
