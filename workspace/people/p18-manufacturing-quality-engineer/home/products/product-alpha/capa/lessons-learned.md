# CAPA Lessons Learned: NCR-026

ForgeLine's Product Alpha fixture controls now distinguish between durable fixture geometry and replaceable wear components. The key lesson is operational: a position check alone cannot detect a degraded locating interface.

For future Product Alpha and Product Beta changes, quality planning will require a condition-based replacement trigger, an explicit dimensional acceptance criterion, and traceable first-piece evidence. The shared dimensional criterion remains +/-0.18 mm unless a formally approved product control changes it.

## Reuse checklist

- Identify wear items during FMEA review.
- Define a visual and dimensional replacement trigger.
- Verify the first piece after replacement.
- Retain evidence for the defined monitoring window.
