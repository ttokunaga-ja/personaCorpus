Validation sequence

  1. Confirm company code, posting date, GL account, and cost center are populated.
  2. Compare amount and source reference to the retained support package.
  3. Apply the internal materiality tier before posting an adjustment.
  4. Record preparer and reviewer roles; reviewer must be independent of preparation.
