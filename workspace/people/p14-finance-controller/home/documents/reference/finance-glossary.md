# Cedar Works Finance Glossary

**Synthetic internal reference | FY2026 Q1 | Owner: Controllership**

This guide uses Cedar Works operating terms only. It is not an accounting standard or external advice.

## Core close terms

| Term | Working definition |
|---|---|
| Accounting period | The reporting month being closed. FY2026 Q1 contains January, February, and March 2026. |
| Close calendar | The controlled sequence from period end through controller sign-off and reporting release. |
| Cutoff | Recording revenue, cost, and balance-sheet activity in the period supported by the underlying performance or obligation date. |
| Accrual | A booked estimate for activity earned or incurred before period end when the routine document has not yet posted. |
| Reversal | A scheduled entry that unwinds a prior-period accrual in the next period after routine processing resumes. |
| Reconciliation | A documented comparison between two records, with differences identified, supported, and resolved or approved as open. |

## Revenue and margin terms

| Term | Working definition |
|---|---|
| Revenue trigger | Cedar Works internal evidence that the promised deliverable or service milestone was completed and accepted for billing. |
| Contract evidence | The approved order, change record, or internal deal record that defines the deliverable, amount, and terms. |
| Deferred revenue | Amount billed or collected before the related performance is complete; held as a liability until the trigger is met. |
| COGS | Direct costs associated with delivered inventory or services, recorded to account 5000. |
| Gross margin | Revenue less COGS, evaluated by business unit and cost center. |

## Control and ERP terms

| Term | Working definition |
|---|---|
| Prepared by | The Cedar Works role that assembled the entry, schedule, or reconciliation. |
| Reviewed by | The independent role that checked support, arithmetic, coding, timing, and required approvals. |
| Posting date | The ERP date that determines the accounting period. |
| Document date | The source-document date; it does not replace the supported posting date. |
| Cost center | The internal responsibility code used to attribute operating expense or margin. |
| Materiality tier | Cedar Works internal threshold used to route review, approval, and disclosure of an item or variance. |
