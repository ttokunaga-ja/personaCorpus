## Ownership and update rules

- Support owns intake, severity confirmation, customer-facing summaries, and the 4-hour checkpoint record.
- Engineering owns technical diagnosis, mitigation status, and the next investigative action for each known issue.
- Customer Success owns account-impact coordination, update commitments, and renewal or adoption risk signals.
- The issue count and state of record live in the [known-issue board](team-known-issue-board.html); escalation channels and role contacts live in the [cross-team escalation roster](cross-team-escalation-roster.csv).
- Update the board at each checkpoint or immediately after a material change. If no change occurred, record “no material change” with the next review time.

## Safe sharing and archive

This folder is for internal operational sharing only. Use role labels, case IDs, and aggregate impact; do not add customer names, personal contact details, credentials, logs containing secrets, or unredacted ticket exports. Archive the dated snapshot after the next business-day review, retaining only the final status and approved redacted notes under the team’s controlled archive process.
