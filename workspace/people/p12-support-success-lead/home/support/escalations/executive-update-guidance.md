# Executive update guidance

Use a brief update when a P1 exists, a first-response deadline is breached, or a customer commitment changes.

## Required structure

1. **Situation:** number of open cases and the priority/SLA split.
2. **Impact:** verified customer effect, without guessing at root cause.
3. **Action:** current mitigation and accountable role.
4. **Risk:** deadlines or blockers, including whether a breach occurred.
5. **Next checkpoint:** exact JST time and update owner.

## Current example

At the frozen 09:00 UTC / 18:00 JST snapshot, HelioDesk has 17 unresolved cases: P1 1, P2 5, P3 8, P4 3. Eleven are within first-response SLA, four are at risk, and two are breached. The P1 is breached Customer Alpha case `HD-260804-2718` (`open`); Customer Beta case `HD-260804-1934` is also breached (`pending_customer`). The bridge is active and the next leadership checkpoint is 18:30 JST.

Avoid naming individuals, sharing unsupported timelines, or combining customer details across accounts.
