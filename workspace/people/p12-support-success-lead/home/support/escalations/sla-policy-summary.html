HelioDesk first-response SLA

Effective 2026-08-04. Every new support case receives a meaningful human first response within 4 hours of creation, measured in JST.

StateDefinitionRequired action
WithinMore than 60 minutes remainMaintain owner and next update.
At risk60 minutes or less remain without a qualifying responseAssign responder and notify Duty Manager.
BreachedDeadline passed without a qualifying responseSend recovery acknowledgement and log cause.
Meaningful response: an acknowledgement that names an accountable owner and a concrete next-update time. Automated receipts do not qualify.

For P1 cases, open the incident bridge immediately and inform the Support Success Lead. Record all updates with a case ID and JST timestamp.
