KI-1051 · Investigating

Session timeout

Long-running inactive browser tabs can request sign-in earlier than expected.

Scope
Inactive browser tabs; this does not indicate an account-access change.
Workaround
Save draft work, refresh the tab, and sign in again before continuing.
Owner
Identity Platform Liaison
Validation
After sign-in, verify that the saved draft is still available.

If you cannot resume work, contact support with KI-1051 and the approximate time of the sign-in prompt.
