KI-1054 · Validating

Notification lag

Email and in-app notices can arrive later than the ticket event they describe.

Scope
Notification delivery timing; the ticket activity feed contains the latest visible state.
Workaround
Open the ticket activity feed before repeating an action solely because a notice is delayed.
Owner
Messaging Reliability Coordinator
Validation
Match the activity timestamp to the eventually delivered notice.

Do not repeat an action simply because a notice is late. Contact support with KI-1054 if the activity feed is inconsistent.
