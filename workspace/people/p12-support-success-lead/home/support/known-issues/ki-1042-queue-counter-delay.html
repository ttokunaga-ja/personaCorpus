KI-1042 · Monitoring

Queue counter delay

Some queue totals may trail new or reassigned ticket activity by up to 12 minutes.

Scope
Queue counters only; ticket records and assignment activity remain the current source of truth.
Workaround
Refresh the queue, or use filtered ticket search to confirm the current workload before changing assignments.
Owner
Support Operations Lead
Validation
Compare the queue total with filtered search after a 15-minute observation window.

If the values still differ, contact support with KI-1042 and an approximate time. Support targets a response within four hours.
