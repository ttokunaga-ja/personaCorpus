# Customer-safe workaround language

Use direct, low-risk instructions: describe what the customer can see, give one bounded action, and explain how to verify the result. Prefer “may be delayed” over certainty and “check the activity feed” over assumptions about notification delivery.

| Use | Avoid |
| --- | --- |
| “Queue totals may take up to 12 minutes to refresh.” | “The queue is broken.” |
| “Retry the corrected file once after 10 minutes.” | “Keep retrying until it works.” |
| “Save your draft before signing in again.” | “You will not lose work.” |
| “Check ticket activity before repeating an action.” | “Notifications are always reliable.” |

When a workaround does not resolve the symptom, ask the customer to share the issue ID, approximate time, and non-sensitive ticket reference with support. A response is targeted within the four-hour SLA; this is a response commitment, not a promise of a fix.
