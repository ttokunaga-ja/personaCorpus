HELIODESK SUPPORT — ESCALATION & SEVERITY REFERENCE
Synthetic internal training reference | Example snapshot: 2026-08-04

ACKNOWLEDGEMENT SLA
Every new case: acknowledgement due within 4 elapsed hours of reported_at_utc.
An acknowledgement records ownership, current severity, and next update time.

SEV 1 — CRITICAL SERVICE INTERRUPTION
Definition: A widespread production outage, confirmed data loss/corruption risk,
or security-sensitive service failure with no safe workaround for multiple customers
or a core supported function.
Action: Page Incident Commander immediately; engage Technical Escalation Lead and
Security Liaison where relevant. Start customer/internal updates every 30 minutes.
Owner: Incident Commander after explicit acceptance; original case owner remains
accountable until acceptance is recorded.

SEV 2 — MAJOR IMPAIRMENT
Definition: A major supported workflow is unavailable or materially degraded for a
customer or meaningful cohort; workaround is unavailable, unsafe, or impractical.
Action: Escalate to Technical Escalation Lead within 30 minutes of classification.
Provide updates at least every 2 hours until stabilized or reclassified.

SEV 3 — LIMITED IMPACT
Definition: A non-critical feature failure, intermittent defect, or degraded workflow
with a reasonable workaround and limited user scope.
Action: Investigate in normal priority order; route to Product Support Specialist
when reproduction or product triage is required. Update at the committed next time.

SEV 4 — REQUEST / LOW IMPACT
Definition: How-to question, documentation gap, cosmetic defect, or low-impact
request with no material interruption to supported operations.
Action: Handle in standard queue order; set a clear next action and update time.

CLASSIFICATION GUARDRAILS
- Severity reflects current observable impact and urgency, not contract tier,
  requester seniority, case age, or emotional tone.
- Reclassify when evidence changes; log the reason and notify the requester.
- A suspected credential, token, session, or personal-data exposure is never copied
  into the case. Stop forwarding evidence; engage Security Liaison immediately.
- Do not close an escalated case until the receiving role's outcome and a customer-
  facing resolution or next action are recorded.
