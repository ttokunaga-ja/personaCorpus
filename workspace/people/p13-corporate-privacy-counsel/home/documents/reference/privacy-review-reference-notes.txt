Purpose
This note records a reusable review sequence for fictional privacy matters. It is a training reference, not legal advice or a record of a real matter.

1. Define the question
- State the decision being supported and the minimum information needed.
- Identify the accountable business owner and the privacy reviewer.
- Separate preservation scope from ordinary retention analysis.

2. Bound the material
- Start with the smallest time range and source set that answers the question.
- Confirm whether the requested fields include direct identifiers, sensitive categories, or derived attributes.
- Use a Restricted Review label for a limited packet; record recipients and expiry.

3. Check controls
- Verify access is role-based and a transfer gate exists for any inter-region route.
- Confirm vendor assurance is current for the specific service and data class.
- Do not treat a DLP label as approval to disclose.

4. Matter Alpha reminder
- The preservation register lists 42 targets.
- Routine deletion stays paused only for records inside the documented hold scope.
- Each open exception needs an owner, rationale, safeguard, and decision date.
- Revalidate or close exceptions by 2026-07-12; escalate any unresolved item.

5. Closeout record
Capture the source index, decision, handling label, recipients, follow-up date, and any residual question. Avoid copying the full underlying data into the review note.
