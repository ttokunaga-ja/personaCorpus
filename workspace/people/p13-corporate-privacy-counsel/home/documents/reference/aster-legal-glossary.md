# Aster Legal Privacy Operations Glossary

**Reference identifier:** AL-REF-GLO-042  
**Status:** Internal synthetic reference; training-only  
**Anchor:** Matter Alpha | 42 preservation targets | exception review due 2026-07-12

This glossary uses the fictional Aster Legal framework. It is not a statement of any real law, regulator, or legal advice.

## Core terms

| Term | Working meaning | Matter Alpha use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Access map** | A maintained record of systems, roles, and approved data paths. | Confirm each of the 42 targets has an accountable system owner. |
| **Collection purpose** | The documented operational reason for gathering a data element. | Compare requested fields to the stated review objective before release. |
| **Data class** | A handling label describing sensitivity and permitted controls. | Use `Restricted Review` for hold-related extracts. |
| **De-identification** | A documented transformation that removes direct linkage for the intended use. | It does not cancel a preservation duty unless counsel records the basis. |
| **Exception register** | The dated log of approved departures from a standard control. | Open items must be revalidated or closed by 2026-07-12. |
| **Hold custodian** | A role responsible for preserving a listed source or record set. | Custodians acknowledge scope, source, and escalation path. |
| **Lawful basis note** | A short internal rationale for the authorized processing purpose. | Keep it separate from the preservation rationale. |
| **Minimum necessary set** | The smallest fields, period, and audience that can complete a task. | Use before sharing a review packet or vendor response. |
| **Processing inventory** | A structured description of purposes, systems, recipients, and controls. | Update when a preserved source changes owner or location. |
| **Retention trigger** | The event that starts a normal retention period. | A legal hold overrides routine disposition while it applies. |
| **Review packet** | A bounded, labeled set prepared for a specific internal decision. | Include source index, handling label, and return-or-destroy instruction. |
| **Transfer gate** | A documented approval point before an inter-region access route is used. | Record destination category, safeguards, and expiry. |
| **Vendor assurance** | Evidence that a service provider can meet stated handling controls. | Tie evidence to the specific service and data class, not generic marketing. |

## Practical reminders

- A label communicates handling; it does not create authority to disclose.
- Preservation, minimization, and retention should be assessed together when the same records are involved.
- Escalate unclear scope rather than expanding a collection set by analogy.
- Record the decision, owner, and next review date in the exception register.
