# Universal Design Checklist

**Maple Learning professional-development note | 2026-09-14**

## Before release

- [ ] The task names its learning goal in learner-facing language.
- [ ] Directions identify the product, audience, and success evidence.
- [ ] The synthetic exemplar is labeled as synthetic and does not contain personal data.
- [ ] A text-first option is available for core instructions.
- [ ] Visual structure uses headings, concise chunks, and descriptive labels.

## Rubric coherence

- [ ] The 20-point rubric remains Evidence E6, Reasoning R6, Organization O4, Format F4.
- [ ] The expectation applies consistently to A-101 *Evidence Map*, A-102 *Source Comparison*, A-103 *Claim-Evidence-Reasoning Brief*, B-201 *Scenario Map*, B-202 *Model Test*, and B-203 *Design Critique*.
- [ ] Feedback distinguishes evidence, reasoning, organization, and format.

## Review lens

- [ ] Course Alpha 2026 Term 1 and Course Beta 2026 Term 2 share the same synthetic Maple Learning world.
- [ ] Materials remain appropriate for ML26-S001 through ML26-S036.
- [ ] No credentials, contact details, or real learner records appear.
