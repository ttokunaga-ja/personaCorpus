# Professional Development Reflection

**Maple Learning | Snapshot: 2026-09-14**

This reflection records an instructional-design review across Course Alpha 2026 Term 1 and Course Beta 2026 Term 2. The shared synthetic learning environment contains ML26-S001 through ML26-S036 and uses a 20-point rubric: Evidence E6, Reasoning R6, Organization O4, and Format F4.

## What strengthened the learning sequence

Course Alpha's A-101 *Evidence Map*, A-102 *Source Comparison*, and A-103 *Claim-Evidence-Reasoning Brief* make the progression visible: collect evidence, compare sources, then build a claim. Course Beta extends that sequence through B-201 *Scenario Map*, B-202 *Model Test*, and B-203 *Design Critique*.

## Next design move

Keep instructions short, publish one worked synthetic example per task, and separate feedback prompts into evidence, reasoning, organization, and format. This keeps the rubric interpretable while preserving learner choice in how each artifact is expressed.

## Review question

At the next design review, check whether the learning sequence still gives ML26-S001 through ML26-S036 an accessible path to demonstrate transfer without changing the 20-point rubric.
