# Third-Party Assessment Boundaries

## Scope

Third-party assessments gather enough information to evaluate control alignment while respecting
vendor confidentiality and the organization’s data-minimization obligations.

## Boundary decisions

The assessment accepts questionnaires, assurance reports, and scoped clarifications. It does not
request customer records, employee identifiers, or unrestricted system detail. Analysts record
only the observations needed to support a risk decision and retain source materials according to
the vendor-risk schedule.

| Boundary risk | Control |
|---|---|
| Excessive collection | questionnaire data-minimization review |
| Unsupported rating | evidence citation requirement |
| Inconsistent reassessment | common scoring rubric |
| Vendor material reused outside purpose | request-scoped access and retention label |

## Governance

Assessment owners approve scope before outreach. Significant findings receive a documented
response plan and a reassessment date. The vendor-risk lead periodically samples concluded
assessments for evidence quality, fairness of scoring, and correct handling of sensitive content.
