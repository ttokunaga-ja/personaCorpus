# Incident Evidence Access Model

## Principle

Incident evidence access must be need-based, time-bounded, and reviewable. The model protects
both the integrity of records and the privacy of people represented in those records.

## Access tiers

| Tier | Permitted activity | Constraint |
|---|---|---|
| Coordinator | assign case and track tasks | no alteration of source evidence |
| Investigator | review scoped evidence | time-limited case membership |
| Legal/records advisor | advise on hold and retention | purpose-specific access |
| Auditor | inspect approved package | read-only export |

## Threat handling

Overbroad case membership, access after closure, and unlogged exports are material assurance
risks. The case system records grants and revocations, automatically reviews membership at key
milestones, and requires a reason for each export. Closure removes routine access while records
retention continues independently.

## Review

After each significant case, the evidence custodian samples access events and confirms that
membership, export approvals, and retention decisions align with the case record.
