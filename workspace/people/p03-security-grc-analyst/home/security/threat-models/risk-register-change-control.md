# Risk Register Change-Control Model

## Purpose

Preserve the integrity of risk statements, ratings, treatment decisions, and closure evidence.
The register is a decision system, not merely a status spreadsheet.

## Change requirements

Every material change records the prior value, new value, reason, authoring role, approver, and
effective date. Rating changes need linked evidence; acceptance changes need an accountable
business sponsor and expiry. Closure needs independent verification that treatment was executed.

## Threats

| Threat | Control |
|---|---|
| Rating reduced to meet a deadline | independent challenge review |
| Risk closed with no proof | mandatory verification reference |
| Owner silently reassigned | notification and acceptance workflow |
| Expired exception remains open | daily expiry queue |

## Monitoring

The GRC lead reviews high-impact changes weekly and reports overdue acceptances to the risk
committee. A quarterly historical sample checks that the register’s current state can be traced
to the decisions and evidence available at the time of change.
