# Exception Approval Abuse Cases

## Intent

Exceptions provide a transparent path for time-bounded, accountable risk acceptance. They must
not become a bypass for baseline controls.

## Abuse cases

| Abuse case | Defensive response |
|---|---|
| Request is split to avoid review threshold | aggregate related requests by control and owner |
| Approver has a conflict of interest | conflict declaration and alternate approver |
| Expiry omitted or extended indefinitely | mandatory expiry and renewal workflow |
| Compensating control is vague | measurable control statement and validation date |
| Closure asserted by requestor | independent closure check |

## Approval standard

Each request identifies the affected control, business rationale, affected asset class, risk
rating, compensating controls, owner, and expiry. The approver assesses whether the residual
risk fits delegated authority. A rejected request remains auditable so recurring patterns can
inform control improvement.

## Oversight

Monthly reports show open exceptions by control, age, and expiry horizon. The risk committee
reviews outliers and directs corrective action when exceptions indicate a design weakness.
