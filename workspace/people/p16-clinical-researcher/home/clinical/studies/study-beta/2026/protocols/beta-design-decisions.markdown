# Study Beta 2026 design decisions

## Purpose
Study Beta is a Sakura Clinical exploratory feasibility study using a wholly synthetic cohort of 36 (18/18). It is not confirmatory.

## Decisions
- Keep 2.5 mg as a protocol-defined review threshold, not an action rule.
- Evaluate feasibility, data completeness, and operational workflow only.
- Keep Study Alpha snapshot 2026-09-02 and synthetic cohort 48 as a separate contextual anchor.
- Exclude real PHI, clinical advice, efficacy inference, and participant-level recommendations.
