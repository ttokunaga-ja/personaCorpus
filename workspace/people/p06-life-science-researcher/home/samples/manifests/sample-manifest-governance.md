# Synthetic sample manifest governance

This folder holds wholly synthetic Orchid Cellworks inventory records for Study Alpha. The cohort A-24 identifiers are fabricated laboratory aliases and are not linked to people, patients, specimens, or clinical systems.

## Controls

- Register storage movement against the synthetic run `ALPHA-A24-R03`.
- Treat the assay 6.8 ng/mL reference as a simulated QC anchor only.
- Review the map and manifest together on 2026-07-13 or at the next synthetic inventory checkpoint.
- Do not introduce names, dates of birth, contact details, credentials, or real sample identifiers.

## Reconciliation

The JSON manifest is the authoritative structured record. The CSV and workbook are operational views; values reconcile by sample ID, rack position, and remaining volume.
