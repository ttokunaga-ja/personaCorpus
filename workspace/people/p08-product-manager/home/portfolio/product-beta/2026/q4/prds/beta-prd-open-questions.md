# Product Beta Q4 open questions

**Context:** Juniper Works is planning Product Beta Q4 after Product Alpha Q3. Alpha adoption is 18.4% across 1,240 synthetic workspaces; M-27 guided rollout is 2026-09-18.

## Questions requiring decisions

1. Which administrator role is permitted to change retention policy during the pilot?
2. What report freshness threshold should display a warning rather than a neutral state?
3. Does the SSO enforcement path require a workspace-wide exception workflow?
4. Which Alpha telemetry fields must be stable before Beta cohort expansion?

## Working assumptions

- The initial cohort is feature-flagged and reversible.
- Product Beta does not replace all legacy reporting in Q4.
- Every governed action has an owner and audit record.

## Decision cadence

Product, Engineering, Design, and Support review these questions weekly through the 2026-10-06 pilot-readiness checkpoint.
