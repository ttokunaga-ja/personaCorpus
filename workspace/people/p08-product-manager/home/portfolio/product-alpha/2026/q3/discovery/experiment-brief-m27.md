# Experiment brief: M-27 guided rollout

## Objective

Test whether a guided first-outcome flow can move Product Alpha Q3 adoption from the 18.4% baseline toward the 26.0% Q3 target before the 2026-09-18 gate.

## Design

Sixteen synthetic workspaces complete the same outcome task in either current or guided flow. The guided flow adds: a success-state preview, permission scope/reversibility copy, a single weekly digest choice, and an export field preview.

## Measures

- Primary: first useful outcome completed within 10 minutes.
- Comprehension: participant accurately explains who can see the result and how access changes can be reversed.
- Calmness: notification opt-out rate and stated burden.
- Confidence: export confidence on a five-point scale.

## Decision rule

Advance only if guided flow improves completion, permission comprehension reaches 80% or more, notification opt-outs remain at or below 12%, and median export confidence is 4/5 or higher. Use synthetic labels such as Participant A-01 in all notes.
