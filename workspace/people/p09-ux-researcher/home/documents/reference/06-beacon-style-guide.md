# Beacon Field Research style guide

**Scope:** internal, synthetic research artifacts for Study Alpha (anchor: 2026-07-13 / UX-184 / planned n=24).

## Voice

Write with calm precision. Prefer concrete verbs and short sentences. State what was observed before explaining what it may mean. Use plain language with participants and stakeholders; define internal terms at first use.

## Evidence labels

- **OBS**: directly observed action or event.
- **QUOTE**: participant wording, materially preserved.
- **INF**: interpretation with evidence links and confidence.
- **DEC**: decision, owner, date, and trade-off.
- **FOLLOW-UP**: answerable next research action.

## Findings

Use tentative claims: “may indicate,” “in these sessions,” or “warrants follow-up.” Never turn a small qualitative sample into a prevalence claim. Write counterevidence and coverage gaps beside the pattern.

## Accessibility

Ask about participation preferences rather than diagnoses. Describe visual material aloud, offer breaks, allow a participant to skip activities, and document method changes neutrally.

## Naming

Use `study-alpha_YYYY-MM-DD_session-UX-184_topic_v01` for working exports. Keep dates ISO-formatted and use ASCII hyphens. Do not place personal contact details, recordings, or real participant data in synthetic artifacts.
