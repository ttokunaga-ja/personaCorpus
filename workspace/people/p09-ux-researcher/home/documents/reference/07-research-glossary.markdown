## Terms

**Accessibility accommodation** - A participant-selected adjustment to enable equitable participation; not a diagnosis.

**Assumption** - A belief used for planning that needs review; it is not evidence.

**Confidence** - A qualitative statement about evidence strength, coverage, and alternatives.

**Coverage** - The contexts, behaviors, or perspectives examined; not proof of representativeness.

**Evidence link** - A stable pointer from a claim to an observation, quote, or artifact.

**Finding card** - A concise record of claim, evidence, scope, confidence, implication, and next step.

**Inference (INF)** - An interpretation drawn from evidence and labelled as such.

**Observation (OBS)** - A directly seen or recorded action/event, described without a motive.

**Planned n** - A target session count used to organize work. It is a planning aid, not a statistical promise.

**Saturation signal** - A practical indication that additional similar sessions are yielding fewer new themes; it must be scoped and documented.

**Synthetic participant** - A fictional stand-in used only for corpus content; never a real person or personal record.

**UX-184** - The synthetic session identifier used as the Study Alpha reference anchor.
