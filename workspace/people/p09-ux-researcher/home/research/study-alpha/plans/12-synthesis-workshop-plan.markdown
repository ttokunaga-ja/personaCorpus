# Synthesis Workshop Plan

Beacon Field Research | Study Alpha | 2026-07-13 | session UX-184 | n=24

## Purpose
Turn coded Study Alpha session evidence into a shared, traceable opportunity map without overstating the n=24 sample.

## Participants
Moderator, two analysts, accessibility partner, and one observer representative. No participant identities are used.

## 90-minute agenda
1. Re-state evidence rules (10 min).
2. Review codebook and contradictions (20 min).
3. Cluster comprehension, navigation, recovery, and accessibility signals (30 min).
4. Draft opportunity statements with evidence IDs (20 min).
5. Confirm handoff and unanswered questions (10 min).

## Outputs
Prioritized opportunity statements, confidence notes, accessibility implications, and a decision-ready analysis handoff.
