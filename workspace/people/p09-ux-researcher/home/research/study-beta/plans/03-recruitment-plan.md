# Study Beta Recruitment Plan

## Target mix

Recruit eight synthetic-coded participants who conduct or support field research at least monthly. Aim for four participants with less than two years of practice and four with two or more years, across interview, observation, and mixed-method workflows. Reserve access paths for keyboard-only navigation, screen magnification, captions, and variable bandwidth.

## Inclusion and exclusion

Include adults able to participate in an en-US remote session and discuss a non-sensitive, fictional workflow. Exclude anyone asked to disclose client data, employer-confidential information, or health information. We do not require camera use, a particular device, or disclosure of disability status.

## Contact and screening

Use synthetic participant codes, not names or contact details, in all study artifacts. The screener asks about workflow frequency, experience band, preferred device, and accommodations. Selection is based on the target mix, not speed, confidence, or employer.

## Accessibility and fairness

Offer captions, extra time, breaks, keyboard-friendly tasks, and a no-camera choice in the invitation. Confirm accommodations privately with the participant and record only the support to provide, not a diagnosis. / 配慮事項は診断名ではなく必要な支援だけを記録する。

## Recruitment stop rule

Hold two alternate codes only if a scheduled participant withdraws. Do not over-recruit or retain unneeded screening responses.
