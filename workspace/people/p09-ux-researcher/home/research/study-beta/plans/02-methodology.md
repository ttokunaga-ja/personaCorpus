# Study Beta Methodology

## Design

Eight moderated, remote think-aloud sessions will use an interactive, non-production prototype and fictional project content. Sessions are 60 minutes, with a facilitator and optional observer. Participants may pause, skip any question, use their own assistive setup, or end the session without explanation.

## Flow

1. Welcome, voluntary consent, and accessibility check (8 min)
2. Context and current practice discussion (7 min)
3. Four prototype tasks with neutral probes (30 min)
4. Reflection, confidence ratings, and close (10 min)
5. Buffer and facilitator notes (5 min)

## Evidence collection

The team records task outcomes, observable behavior, verbatim paraphrase notes, and prototype screen/state references. If a participant agrees, a local session recording supports note verification; otherwise only typed notes are retained. Observer notes distinguish observation from interpretation.

## Analysis boundaries

This qualitative sample is designed to expose workflow and accessibility risks, not to estimate population prevalence. We will avoid comparative performance claims, collect no sensitive personal data, and use participant codes only. See `14-analysis-plan.pdf` for coding and decision rules.

## Remote contingencies

If the prototype or connection fails, switch to static annotated screens, record the limitation, and do not score the interrupted task as a failure. Captions and a dial-in/audio alternative are available on request.
