# Study Beta Synthesis Framework

## Unit of evidence

Each note is one observation tied to a session ID, task, prototype state, outcome, and confidence rating. A finding needs at least one observable behavior and a concise participant explanation or a clearly marked analyst inference.

## Tags

| Dimension | Tags |
|---|---|
| Workflow | capture, save-state, attach, review, handoff, recovery |
| Experience | newer, experienced |
| Access | keyboard, magnification, captions, bandwidth, none-reported |
| Outcome | complete, recovery, blocked, interrupted |
| Confidence | 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 |

## From notes to decisions

1. Independently tag notes before the Jul 24 synthesis.
2. Cluster evidence by task and workflow moment, preserving counterexamples.
3. Rate severity: critical (prevents core work or creates material visibility/consent risk), major (repeated recovery), moderate (slows understanding), minor (preference).
4. Identify the smallest design or content change that can be evaluated next.

Frequency is a directional signal, not a population estimate. Findings retain the participant code and session only; no names, employer details, or raw recordings appear in the synthesis deck.
