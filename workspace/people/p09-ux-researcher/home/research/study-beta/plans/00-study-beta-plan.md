# Study Beta Plan - Beacon Field Research

**Study window:** July 20-24, 2026  
**Decision partner:** Beacon Field Research product team  
**Prior context:** Study Alpha, July 13, 2026, session UX-184, n=24

## Purpose

Study Beta evaluates whether a revised mobile field-note prototype lets mixed-experience researchers capture, review, and hand off observations without losing confidence in what is saved, shareable, or recoverable. This is a formative, moderated remote study; it is not a benchmark or a release approval.

## Objectives and decisions

1. Identify whether participants can begin a note, attach evidence, and recognize save state.
2. Test whether review and handoff language communicates ownership and visibility.
3. Find accessibility and remote-use barriers before the next prototype iteration.
4. Decide which two workflow issues must be addressed before an unmoderated validation.

The team will prioritize a change when it is observed in at least 3 of 8 sessions, blocks a core task, or creates a material consent/privacy misunderstanding.

## Scope and sample

Eight 60-minute remote sessions will run with participant codes only: P-B201 through P-B208. We will balance newer and experienced field-research practitioners, include at least two participants who regularly use keyboard navigation or screen magnification, and offer captions, breaks, and a no-camera option. No production data, accounts, or recordings are used.
