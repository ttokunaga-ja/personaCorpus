# Study Beta Research Questions

Beacon Field Research is carrying forward a bounded question set from Study Alpha (July 13, 2026; session UX-184; n=24). Alpha indicated that capture intent was clear, while save confidence and handoff expectations required a closer prototype study.

## Primary questions

1. Can participants initiate and complete a field note without facilitator instruction?
2. What cues make the saved/draft distinction understandable or ambiguous?
3. Can participants attach, review, and remove synthetic evidence while preserving confidence in the note?
4. How do participants interpret collaborator visibility, export, and handoff controls?
5. Which interaction or language barriers affect keyboard, magnification, caption, or low-bandwidth use?

## Measures

For each task, record outcome (complete, complete with recovery, not complete), first-point hesitation, confidence rating (1-5), observed workaround, and participant explanation. Timing is contextual only; it will not be used to rank participants.

## Decision signals

* A core-task failure is urgent when it prevents capture, review, or handoff.
* Repeated uncertainty in 3+ sessions is a candidate design issue.
* An accessibility barrier is retained even if reported by one participant when it removes an equivalent path.
* Findings require behavior plus supporting explanation; isolated preferences are labeled as such.

Operational note / 運用メモ: ask neutral follow-ups such as “What are you expecting here?” rather than naming a control or correcting a misunderstanding.
