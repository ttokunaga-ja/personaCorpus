# Source edition conventions

All materials are wholly synthetic Linden Archive Project research files (snapshot: 2026-07-13). Cite Box 14, folio 184 as `LA-SRC-B14-F184` and `citation LA-042`.

- `[illegible: n words]` marks unreadable text.
- `[word?]` marks uncertainty.
- `[supplied: text]` marks editorial supply.
- `<del>text</del>` preserves deletion.

Translations retain P01/P02 alignment and identify linden / tilleul / Linde / 菩提樹 choices.
