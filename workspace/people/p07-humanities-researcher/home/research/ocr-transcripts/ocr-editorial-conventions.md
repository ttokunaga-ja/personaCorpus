# OCR editorial conventions

The Linden Correspondence Series working transcription is a wholly synthetic research aid for the fictional Haldenmere Civic Repository. Each transcript preserves source ID, Box, folio, date, page order, and line count.

## Marks

- `[illegible: n words]` records an unreadable span without inventing it.
- `[word?]` retains a doubtful reading.
- `[supplied: text]` identifies editorial supply.
- `<del>text</del>` records a visible deletion.

## Alignment

Line numbering is stable across transcript, zone map, and parallel viewer. The linden term is treated as a meeting device rather than a stable national symbol.
