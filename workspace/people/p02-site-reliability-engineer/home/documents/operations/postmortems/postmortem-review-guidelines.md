# Postmortem review guidelines

## Purpose

Northstar Transit Cloud reviews incidents to improve system behavior, not assign blame. Use UTC, identify observable evidence, and separate facts from hypotheses.

## Review standard

1. State customer and operator impact in measurable terms.
2. Record containment decisions and why recovery was considered stable.
3. Name contributing conditions without treating correlation as cause.
4. Turn learning into owner, due date, and acceptance evidence.

## Quality gate

A reviewer confirms timeline consistency, synthetic-data handling, action ownership, and links to recurring patterns. See postmortem-action-register.xml for the canonical local register.
