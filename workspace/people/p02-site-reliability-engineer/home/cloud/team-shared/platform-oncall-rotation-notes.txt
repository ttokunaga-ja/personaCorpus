Northstar Transit Cloud — Platform on-call rotation notes
Effective period: 2026-07-20 through 2026-08-16
Prepared: 2026-07-17

Rotation intent
This rotation incorporates the operational lessons from INC-260713 without
assigning incident decisions to individuals. Roles are coverage positions;
the scheduling roster is maintained elsewhere.

Coverage lanes
- Primary On-call SRE: first responder for Checkout SVC-17 alerts, triage,
  and incident-state updates.
- Secondary On-call SRE: validates mitigation steps and assumes command-log
  ownership when the primary is coordinating.
- Identity Operations: owns token-validation and fallback verification during
  cross-service incidents.
- Incident Commander: confirms incident posture changes and approves any
  exception that affects the release hold in DEC-EB-20260714-01.

Rotation changes
The first weekly overlap will include a 30-minute Checkout/Identity handoff
review. The Primary On-call SRE must read checkout-identity-handoff-20260713.txt
before taking the first shift in the period. The Secondary On-call SRE must
practice the queue-depth and synthetic-probe checks referenced by
ACT-260713-01.

  review to the weekly rotation agenda by 2026-07-20.
- ACT-ROT-20260717-02 — Identity Operations: confirm fallback audit-event

Escalation note
If the remaining error budget drops below the policy threshold during this
period, the On-call SRE pauses nonessential rollout work and contacts the
Incident Commander role. This is a policy safeguard, not an automatic
declaration of incident severity.
