# Project Beta Specification Review Guide

**Issue:** PB-26.07.29-C  
**Date:** 2026-07-29  
**Classification:** Synthetic internal construction planning artifact

## Purpose

Use this guide to review the Project Beta specification package as a coordinated set. It supports procurement and field-planning discussion; it does not provide a permit set, site-access plan, or construction authorization.

## Review sequence

1. Start with the outline specification to confirm submittal, quality, and closeout expectations.
2. Review concrete, envelope, and MEP sections for shared interfaces: sleeves, transitions, drainage, access panels, and asset labels.
3. Open the markup scan and comment register; carry all Open items into the next coordination agenda.
4. Use the IFC-like coordination export and BCF issue package only as a synthetic model-review companion.
5. Record final disposition, evidence reference, and responsible trade in the revised closeout index.

## Critical coordination checks

- Confirm envelope transition membranes preserve drainage and air-barrier continuity.
- Verify concrete patch records include preparation, ambient condition, cure method, and acceptance evidence.
- Confirm MEP access panels remain serviceable after finishes and are named consistently in controls and electrical records.
- Require mockup, adhesion, pressure, and functional-test evidence where the applicable section calls for it.
