# Decision: MTR-09 is net recognized GMV

**Decision ID:** DEC-2026-0714-MTR09  
**Request:** SR-2026-0713-09  
**Effective scope:** Mosaic Retail FY26 Q3 reporting as of 2026-07-13  
**Decision owner:** Mika Tan, BI Data Analyst  
**Business approver:** Erin Park, VP Merchandising  
**Finance concurrence:** Jonah Lee, Finance Business Partner

## Decision

Report **MTR-09 = net recognized GMV**. For this reporting cut, the approved value is **¥84.2M**, calculated as completed gross merchandise value of ¥86.0M minus refunds of ¥1.8M posted through 2026-07-13. The dashboard card must not substitute gross GMV for this metric.

## Why

The stakeholder variance view needs a consistently comparable realized-sales measure. Showing gross orders on the headline card would overstate the measure after posted refunds and would break alignment with the plan and prior-period comparisons.

## Definition boundary

Included: completed merchandise transactions across Online and Stores. Excluded: cancelled or uncompleted orders, taxes, shipping, gift-card load, and refunds posted after the reporting cutoff. The snapshot is refreshed when a new reporting cutoff is issued; historical values are not silently restated in a current request.

## Evidence

- Channel tie-out: Online ¥46.3M + Stores ¥37.9M = ¥84.2M.
- Regional tie-out: East ¥26.4M + West ¥22.8M + Central ¥18.6M + North ¥16.4M = ¥84.2M.
- Category tie-out: Home ¥24.8M + Electronics ¥22.1M + Apparel ¥19.3M + Beauty ¥10.7M + Other ¥7.3M = ¥84.2M.
- Variance reference: plan ¥80.0M; prior period ¥78.6M.

## Implementation and review

Close only after Erin Park or a named Merchandising delegate confirms the published wording. Any formula or cutoff change requires Finance Business Partner review and a successor decision record.
