# Mosaic Retail Analytics Glossary

| English | Japanese | Working meaning |
|---|---|---|
| GMV | 流通総額 | Eligible completed order-line value after approved adjustments. |
| order line | 注文明細 | The atomic record used by metric MTR-09. |
| refund | 返金 | Negative adjustment recorded at the originating order-line grain. |
| dimension | ディメンション | A controlled reporting attribute such as channel or region. |
| lineage | 系譜 | The documented path from source system to published metric. |
