# MTR-09 Frequently Asked Questions

## Why is the grain an order line?

The order-line grain supports category, channel, and refund analysis without allocating an order total.

## Are cancelled orders included?

No. Only completed eligible lines are included. Cancelled, test, and fraudulent lines are excluded.

## What number should appear in the FY26 Q3 control example?

Use GMV ¥84.2M, backed by the numeric control value ¥84,200,000.
