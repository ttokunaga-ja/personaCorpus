# What we'd give back on the peak bill

Daniel,

Following your check with Richard: taking out only the £500 overflow reservation would bring the monthly allocation from £18,400 to £17,900. It would also give up a route to extra capacity. We should not call that a free saving just because the route has been idle.

Before we reopen it, I need to establish whether the remaining normal and warm capacity can cover the relevant peak and the separate burst and worker-replacement cases, and what capacity can actually be obtained without the reservation, including how long it takes to become available. We also need the supplier's terms for availability and any usage charge. A price line does not translate directly into a number of attempts per minute.

Please keep the reservation in the August readback. I'll bring the evidence back before we propose a cut.

Matthew
