# Slide 3 — Two columns: sizing the proposal

Document reference: BRT-RB-20250428-001

Left — From demand to ceiling:
- Forecast busiest band: 2.30× = 2,300/min
- Short bursts: +0.25× = 250/min
- Worker replacement: +0.25× = 250/min
- Proposed ceiling: 2.80× = 2,800/min

Right — What the allowance means:
- Protect capacity for a short burst and for retaining service capacity while a worker is replaced.
- Together the allowances are 0.50× of the reference, or 500/min; this is not an observed headroom percentage.
- Provision ahead of peak: the old ceiling is already below forecast at the start of the 16:00–17:00 UTC band. Waiting until the busiest hour leaves that earlier shortfall exposed.

Speaker notes: These are sizing allowances, not measured results. Palmer's forecast does not include BRT reserve. The comparison does not establish a sustainable ceiling by itself.
