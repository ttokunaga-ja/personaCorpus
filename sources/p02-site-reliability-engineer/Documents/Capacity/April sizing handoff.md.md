Anjali approved the 2.80× planning ceiling at the review (BRT-MT-20250428-001). On the 1,000 eligible attempts/min reference, that is 2,800/min. Louise's busiest-band forecast is 2,300/min; the remaining 500/min is split into two 250/min sizing allowances. None of this is a measured pass yet.

Please record the workload and the rates used for each case, along with the observed result and any constraint:
