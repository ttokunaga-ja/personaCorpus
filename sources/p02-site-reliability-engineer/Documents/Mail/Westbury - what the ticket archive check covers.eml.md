Please find attached the issued ticket archive recovery guidance for Westbury (BRT-CU-20260511-001, version 1.0).

In the 11 May exercise, the recovered archive covered ticket records through 09:51 UTC at the 10:00 UTC exercise reference. The nine minutes between those times describes the age of the observed archive point, and met the 15-minute target for this check. It is not a measure of how long the recovery took, and this single exercise does not establish the boundary for a later recovery.

The guidance uses a 09:52 UTC record to illustrate the position after the observed boundary. If you need to rely on a later or uncertain record, please ask Support through your usual channel to confirm its coverage first, giving the record time and the details needed to locate it.
