## Friday readback

At the change of watch, carry each pending item with its outgoing owner and next check visible until the incoming engineer explicitly acknowledges the item by name and time. The watch changes at the published boundary even if an item has not yet been accepted.

Matthew accepted it at 17:03 BST after reading back ownership and the planned 17:20 BST Support check. Receipt delivery remained unverified at the end of the exercise; the planned check was not observed.
