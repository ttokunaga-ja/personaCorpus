# Tonight's handover — one owner per line

10 April 2024 · Daniel, for the incoming duty engineer

Andrew and I read this through after yesterday's review. Tonight we're trying new wording on the sheet. Keep reminders in place.

Read back each open item from the previous watch with the person handing over. Put the name of the person taking it alongside the next check. Here's how I'd write an accepted Fare Payments item and a receipt query nobody has picked up yet:

| Carried item | Owner for this watch | Next check | Acknowledgement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fare Payments repeat alert, still open | Daniel Jennings | 23:00 BST | Daniel Jennings — accepted at handover |
| Receipt query awaiting Support | Unassigned | To agree | OUTSTANDING |

If nobody has taken the item, write **Owner: unassigned** and **Acknowledgement: OUTSTANDING** on its line. Leave any already agreed next check visible. If a check still needs agreeing, write **to agree** and flag that at handover too. Read the outstanding line back to the outgoing engineer before they leave. Update it when someone accepts, with their name and the agreed check.

If another reminder comes in for the same still-open item, add its arrival time and acknowledgement to the existing line. Keep the owner and next check where the next reader can see them. A fresh opening after clearance gets its own line.
