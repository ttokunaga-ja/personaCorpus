# Night pages — what the eight of us said

9 April 2024 · Daniel Jennings, for Anjali Shah

I've put the seven conversations with the rest of the March rota alongside my own handover notes. We have a tally of 46 night-call deliveries across the eight of us. I'm still checking which deliveries were repeats.

## Having to acknowledge twice

Andrew remembered responding to a page and then seeing a second notification while the same item was still unresolved. He had to deal with the second notification as well, even though the work it pointed to was already in front of him. Another colleague described stopping to find the first acknowledgement before deciding whether to respond again. In both accounts the second delivery took time out of the watch.

Matthew pointed out where that comparison can go wrong. Once an opening has been cleared, the alert can legitimately return under the same display label. On 22 March the 01:12 and 02:06 UTC pages look alike, but the recorded clearance between them means the later page starts a new opening.

## Carrying an item into the next watch

One colleague recalled carrying an unresolved item forward with a check still due. Another had received a further notification after taking over and first had to establish whether it belonged to the item from the previous watch. The March records give a useful date-boundary example. The item paged at 23:58 UTC on 17 March was still open at 00:03 UTC on 18 March. The second delivery belongs to that item.

## Unable to tell who took it

One colleague could see the open item on the sheet but couldn't tell whether the incoming engineer had accepted it. Another kept a note beside the handover because the shared instruction left the next check unclear.

My own example is Andrew's sheet for my 8–9 April watch, BRT-NH-20240408-001. The Fare Payments repeat last seen at 15:40 UTC and the receipt query awaiting Support at 15:52 UTC share one box and the instruction “Tell Payments if it comes back.” The single received tick has no name, time or item against it. I can see that the sheet was marked received. I can't tell who took the receipt query or who was meant to pick up the next Payments check.

For our discussion, I'd trace the second deliveries back to the open items first. Then we can look at which carried items had a person expecting to make the next check. The tick on the old sheet can't answer that for either line.
