# April night review — handover first

Reference  BRT-MT-20240409-001     |     Issued  09 Apr 2024  
Prepared by  Daniel Jennings

Meeting: 9 April 2024, 13:00 UTC (14:00 BST)  
Present: Anjali Shah, Operations Director, and Daniel Jennings, SRE  
Distribution: Reliability and Operations

## Agenda

Review March's night-call deliveries and decide whether to change reminders or the handover first.

## Evidence reviewed

The eight-person rota received 46 night-call deliveries in March 2024 (UTC). The delivery reconciliation shows 28 first calls, 10 reminder repeats and 8 parallel-route repeats. The last two categories account for 18 of the 46 deliveries. The first-call figure counts deliveries at the start of an opening and does not establish an incident total.

Andrew's 8 April sheet, BRT-NH-20240408-001, carries a Fare Payments repeat and a receipt query awaiting Support in one box. “Tell Payments if it comes back” gives the incoming reader no clear action for the receipt query. The one received tick gives neither item an identifiable acknowledgement or time. From the sheet we cannot reliably distinguish a missed first acknowledgement from a further notification after someone has already accepted the item.

## Decision and actions

Anjali asked us to fix handover wording first and keep the existing reminders in place. The repeat count tells us how many additional deliveries people received. The old sheet cannot tell us which reminders were still needed to get someone to accept a first call.

| Owner | Action | When |
|---|---|---|
| Daniel Jennings and Andrew Cartwright | Trial one line per carried item, with a named owner, the next check and an acknowledgement against that item by the person accepting it. Leave acknowledgement visibly outstanding where nobody has accepted the item. | 10 April evening handover |

Before revisiting reminders, check the trial handover records for evidence of who actually accepted each item and whether the first acknowledgement can be traced.
