# Slide 2 — Figure

Title: How an open item crosses the watch

Diagram, four boxes from left to right:

1. Outgoing engineer states the open item, its owner and next check.
2. Incoming primary gives their name and acknowledges the item.
3. Incoming primary reads back the item owner and next check.
4. Outgoing engineer verifies the readback and signs off.

Caption: If nobody accepts the item, leave the acknowledgement outstanding and follow the watch contact order.

Document reference: BRT-RB-20251103-001

Speaker notes: Ask the group to identify both the person accepting the handover and the person who will do the follow-up. They can be different. If the two people read back different times, settle which time goes on the item before sign-off.

# Slide 6 — Heading and body

Title: Before the outgoing engineer leaves

- Acknowledge by name as the incoming primary.
- Say back who owns each open item and when it will next be checked.
- Keep an unaccepted item visible and use the watch contact order to resolve it.

Document reference: BRT-RB-20251103-001

Speaker notes: Use this recap after the individual choices have been recorded. Ask each trainee to say whose acceptance they would wait for. If anyone misroutes an item, hold the 6 November slot at 10:00 GMT for a supervised repeat.
