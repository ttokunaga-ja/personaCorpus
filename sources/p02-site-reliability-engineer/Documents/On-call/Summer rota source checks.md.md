# Summer rota: source checks for Andrew

Then compare the scheduled primary's diary with that whole interval. Record separately whether the other person's diary permits relief for the same interval; a clear primary diary is not a relief commitment.

- Check Friday 17:00 BST through Monday 09:00 BST for the ordinary windows.
- The 23 August start runs to **Tuesday 27 August 09:00 BST**, after the bank holiday. A diary view showing only Saturday and Sunday cannot settle that row. Ask specifically about Monday evening as well as the rest of the extension.
- Mark overlapping leave as an exception, not a swap or an acceptance. Bring those rows to the check and leave the actual primary open until we have a readback or written acceptance.
