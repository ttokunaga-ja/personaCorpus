# Tick beside the verification window?

Daniel and I talked through adding one more tick next to the window on the change sheet. It would be quick to see, but I can tick a window without writing what the first watched checkout actually did. All 14 boxes could then look complete while neither the result nor the person who checked it is readable afterwards.

Park this. Put the observed outcome and time in the change record and have the second engineer put their name and readback time there. If those records are missing, leave the verification open for the next owner rather than making the sheet look finished.
