# When a Fare Payments change is complete

9 July 2025 · Daniel Jennings

For Matthew and the duty engineers, from the next change.

## Rollout and handover

When rollout ends, the change owner records the rollout end time separately. Tell duty what changed, the route available if we need to go back and what the watch covers. Hand over any issue still being watched to the duty engineer. An end stamp says when rollout ended; it does not establish that verification or the change is complete.

## Record before closeout

The change owner puts the following in the change record:

- The fallback route confirmed for the change and the time of that confirmation.
- The time of the first watched checkout and its observed outcome.

The second engineer records their own name, the time of their closeout readback and what they confirmed. A spoken all-clear on the call is not a substitute for that record. Once the required evidence is present, record change completion separately from rollout end.

If any record is absent, leave verification and closeout open. The handover to duty must identify the outstanding item, a named owner and the next check time, so that the receiving engineer knows who will follow it up. A missing record does not by itself show that a check was not performed.

## Evidence found later

Enter a late record with the date and time it is actually recorded, identifying the evidence recovered and its source. Include the original check time only if the evidence supports it. Do not use the rollout end or call time as a guessed completion time, and do not backfill an unsupported change completion time. Leave any still unsupported verification open with an owner and next check.

The 9 July review left the three historical verification records unresolved; this guidance does not close those gaps.
