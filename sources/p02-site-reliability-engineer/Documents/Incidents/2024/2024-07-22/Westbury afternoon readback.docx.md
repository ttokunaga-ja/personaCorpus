# Westbury afternoon readback

BRT-MT-20240722-001  
22 July 2024  
Meeting: 11:30 UTC  
Attendees: Daniel Jennings, Reliability Engineering; Matthew Pickering, Fare Payments

## Agenda

Compare the Westbury desk's afternoon observation with accepted-booking confirmation data and agree when duty should prepare.

## Evidence readback

The comparison assigns each Westbury booking to its UTC acceptance half-hour and counts it if still unconfirmed at 120 seconds. The combined 15:00–15:30 and 15:30–16:00 slots were:

| Date | Delayed confirmations, 15:00–16:00 UTC |
|---|---:|
| 17 July 2024 | 17 |
| 18 July 2024 | 20 |
| 19 July 2024 | 21 |

The two half-hours recur as the higher-count period on all three days. The earlier Friday preview grouped by confirmation time and is not suitable for choosing when to prepare.

## Decision and actions

Daniel and Matthew agreed to use 15:00 UTC (16:00 BST) as the working start of the busy period and to prepare from 14:30 UTC (15:30 BST).

| Action | Owner | When |
|---|---|---|
| Brief duty on the preparation and first check | Daniel Jennings | Before the 14:30 UTC preparation start |
| Engineering contact if confirmations fall behind | Matthew Pickering | When duty reports a lag |
