# Which time goes in the Westbury comparison?

For Matthew and duty — 22 July 2024

Use the time the Westbury booking was accepted. The export has four half-hour UTC slots, each including its start and excluding its end: 14:30–15:00, 15:00–15:30, 15:30–16:00 and 16:00–16:30. Each accepted booking appears once. One accepted at exactly 15:00 UTC belongs in the 15:00–15:30 slot, not the preceding one. That is 16:00 BST at the desk.

For each accepted booking check whether it is still without confirmation at 120 seconds. Keep that result with its acceptance slot even if confirmation arrives in a later slot. The numerator is the number still unconfirmed at 120 seconds among the accepted Westbury bookings in that slot; the accepted count is the denominator. It is not a count of all customers or of confirmations completed in the slot.

The Friday preview on Desktop groups confirmations by completion time. A later bar can include a booking accepted earlier, and a booking that has not yet confirmed is missing entirely. It cannot tell us when the affected bookings began to arrive, so do not use it to set the preparation time. Compare the three days on the acceptance clock instead.
