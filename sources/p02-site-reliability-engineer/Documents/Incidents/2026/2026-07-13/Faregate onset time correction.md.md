# Why I moved the start time

INC-260713 — 13 July 2026

Matthew and I agreed the correction at 10:20 UTC. I had used 08:12 UTC as the provisional start because that is when the on-call page arrived. It is the detection time, not evidence that the first affected checkout occurred then.

The dashboard clock in the early sketch read 09:07 in Bristol local time, BST. That is 08:07 UTC. Matthew confirmed the time zone and the underlying observation: this was the first confirmed affected Faregate attempt. I have therefore moved the recorded start to 08:07 UTC. The five minutes between that observation and the page are a gap between the first confirmed observation and alert receipt, not proof of an earlier onset.

Recovery was observed at 08:41 UTC. The subsequent watch completed at 08:56 UTC; those are separate milestones. Keep the early clocks sketch for the original reading and the reason for checking it.
