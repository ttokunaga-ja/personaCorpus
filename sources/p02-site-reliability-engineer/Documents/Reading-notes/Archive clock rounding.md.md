# Rounding the archive clock

5 September 2024

Made up a pair to check Sanjay's rule before either observation day. I put the new start at 11:30:05 UTC and the previous write's finish at 11:30:42 UTC.

On my sketch both landed at 11:30 UTC, so I wrote ~~0 seconds of overlap~~.

With the seconds back in, 11:30:42 − 11:30:05 = **37 seconds**. For those 37 seconds the previous write is still open after the new one starts. Keep the seconds and the earlier write's reference on the working sheet.
