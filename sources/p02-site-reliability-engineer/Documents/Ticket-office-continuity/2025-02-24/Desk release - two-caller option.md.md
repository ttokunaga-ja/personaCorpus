# Two callers on the same desk sheet

25 February 2025

I wondered whether a second caller could work through the list at the same time as Hannah. The difficulty is not just the duplicate call. If Coach Yard answers the second caller while Hannah is still waiting for the first readback, each caller has only part of the receipt record. Neither list alone tells us which desks have actually read the message back.

At yesterday's review we agreed a single owner: Hannah sends to the desks and keeps the follow-up with her list. I will settle the words with her ahead of the cue. I should not start a parallel round from Support when a desk is missing; Hannah can follow it up against the same list.

Claire
