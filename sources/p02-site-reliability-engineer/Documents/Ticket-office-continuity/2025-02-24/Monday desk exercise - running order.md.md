# Fairfield desk exercise — Monday 24 February

Claire / Hannah — this is the running order for the staffed ticket office practice. Times below are UTC. We are exercising the switch with one exercise booking only; live customers remain in the ordinary queue and desks continue serving them as usual. If the exercise interferes with a live customer, pause it and tell the call.

## Before 10:00

- Claire clears the wording with Hannah and makes sure it says exercise before asking anyone to switch the exercise booking.
- Hannah has the list of 12 desks and is ready to notify them. Daniel observes the call and keeps the times separately.
- Confirm everyone can hear the call and that the exercise booking is identifiable. Do not use a live booking for the practice.

## On the call

- **10:00 UTC:** Daniel gives the exercise cue and notes the time.
- **10:03 UTC:** planned release of the switch message. Claire clears the words; Hannah notifies the desks and asks each to read back receipt.
- Daniel records when the message is actually released, then each desk readback as heard. Keep those clocks distinct; a ring or an open line is not a readback.
- Hannah checks the list and reports coverage. Daniel notes any missing or uncertain time rather than filling it from the roll call.
- Hannah gives the exercise stand-down after the desk check. Daniel records that separately from the message and readbacks.

Afterwards we will compare the notes, including any missed desk or uncertain time, before drawing conclusions about the handoff.
