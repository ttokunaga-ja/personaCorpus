# Count the readback once

For the next observer: start all desk timings at the common cue, not when the desk is called in the roll call. The September cue was 09:20:00 UTC. Hannah kept the list and called for each desk in the agreed order.

A valid readback needs both the desk's name and “exercise switch received”. At 09:21:56 UTC I heard “Riverside here”. That identified the desk but did not repeat the message, so the accepted count remained 11. Four seconds later Riverside completed the readback at 09:22:00 UTC. That is one accepted Riverside response, not two: the count became 12 at 120 seconds.

Keep the partial call in the exception notes rather than using its time as the accepted receipt. The last complete response was within the inclusive 180-second limit, so Hannah did not need a fallback call.
