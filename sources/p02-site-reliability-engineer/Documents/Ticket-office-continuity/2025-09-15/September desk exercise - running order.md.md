# Fairfield desk exercise — Monday running order

Prepared 12 September 2025 for Claire and Hannah. All exercise times below are UTC.

## Before the cue

Use an exercise-only booking. Leave the live queue alone and continue serving customers as usual. Claire and Hannah agree the exercise wording and the desk list before we begin. Hannah checks that all 12 desks are staffed and listening on the existing group channel; Riverside and Coach Yard still need the Monday relief-cover check. Agree the final order after that check.

**Stop before the cue** if the wording or desk list is not agreed, or if the listening arrangement cannot be confirmed. Resolve the gap with Claire and Hannah before starting the clock.

## Run

- Daniel gives Hannah the common cue at 09:20:00 UTC and starts the observation clock.
- Hannah sends one exercise message on the desk group channel, then calls for individual readbacks in the agreed order. Each desk gives its name and repeats “exercise switch received”. Hannah keeps the single receipt list.
- Daniel records the cue, accepted readbacks and any incomplete or missing response in the exception log. A desk name alone is not a complete readback.
- At 09:23:00 UTC, 180 seconds from cue, check whether all 12 distinct desks have completed their readbacks. A complete twelfth readback at exactly 180 seconds meets the limit. If one is missing, Hannah rings the missing desk and records the follow-up on her list.
- After the roll call, compare Hannah's list with Daniel's clock and record any uncertainty. Claire, Hannah and Daniel review the evidence before deciding what to issue.
