# How much of the gain can we claim?

20 January 2026, 11:48 UTC  
For Emma, ahead of the afternoon review

Emma, I think we can compare the elapsed times directly, provided we keep the exercise conditions in the sentence. In both runs Carys started the clock with the simulated sign-in failure cue and stopped it at the second of two consecutive successful exercise sign-ins, one minute apart. The target was 20 minutes in both. Being ready to verify was an intermediate marker, not the finish.

| Interval | 16 October 2024 | 20 January 2026 | Reduction |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Cue to request | 4 min | 3 min | 1 min |
| Request to recorded decision | 12 min | 2 min | 10 min |
| Decision to ready for verification | 10 min | 7 min | 3 min |
| Verification | 8 min | 6 min | 2 min |
| Cue to verified finish | 34 min | 18 min | 16 min |

October finished 14 minutes over target; January finished 2 minutes under. The decision interval went from 12 minutes to 2, accounting for 10 of the 16-minute difference. The other six minutes are spread across the remaining intervals; we do not know how much of that is familiarity with the rehearsal or other differences between runs.

The January observation does show the single-owner route working in this scripted case. I logged the request at 09:03; you deliberately left it undecided to exercise the trigger. At 09:05 Andrew confirmed scope and recorded his acceptance of ownership before recording the decision. I held restoration until the decision and then proceeded. His own exercise access had passed all three preflight checks on Monday. Carys observed the sequence and called the stop at 09:18.

That is evidence that the transfer and checked authority could be used in this exercise, not proof that the role change alone caused all ten minutes of reduced decision wait, let alone the full sixteen. The deliberately unanswered request and a rehearsed scenario are material conditions. One repeat cannot establish a production recovery time, the effect of delegation in a live incident, or that a future exercise will finish within 20 minutes. We should retain both records separately and describe these as exercises, outside incident and availability totals.

Daniel
