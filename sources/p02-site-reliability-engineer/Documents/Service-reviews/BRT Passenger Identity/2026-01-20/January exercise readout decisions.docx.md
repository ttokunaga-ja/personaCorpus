## Evidence reviewed

The October baseline finished in 34 minutes, 14 minutes over target; the January repeat finished in 18 minutes, two under. The four intervals were 4, 12, 10 and 8 minutes in October and 3, 2, 7 and 6 in January. The decision wait accounts for 10 of the 16 minutes' difference; the other six cannot be attributed to the transfer from this comparison alone.

His 19 January preflight had checked that he could read the request, record and read back a practice decision, and see both verification results using his own exercise access. All three passed.
