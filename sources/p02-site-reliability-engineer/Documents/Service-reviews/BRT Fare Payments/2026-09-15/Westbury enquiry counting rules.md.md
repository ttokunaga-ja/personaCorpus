# What the desk counts as an enquiry

For Stephen and Anjali — Claire's rule for the Westbury comparison, 15 September 2026.

Count a newly opened desk question once per case and question, under the category recorded when it is first opened. A later call following up that same question adds no new enquiry. This is a count of distinct questions, not calls or passengers. General ticketing questions are outside this tally.

The two completed Monday–Sunday weeks use the same rule and UTC dates:

| Week | Booking-confirmation delay | Refund progress | Total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 10–16 August 2026 | 11 | 6 | 17 |
| 7–13 September 2026 | 4 | 2 | 6 |

There were eleven fewer enquiries in the second comparison week. We have not controlled for booking volume, so the comparison does not establish what caused the change.
