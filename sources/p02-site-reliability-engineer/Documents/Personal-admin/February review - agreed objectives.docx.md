## Agenda

Review the timing of Daniel's required first post-incident reports and agree a measure and practical checks for the next half-year.

## Evidence and timing

The register extract covers closures from 1 September 2025 through 19 February 2026. Each required first report is counted once by closure date. The deadline is 17:00 Europe/London on the next UK working day, inclusive; the first formal issue time, converted to UTC, is compared with that deadline. A saved draft is not an issue.

| Check | Evidence and result |
|---|---|
| Baseline | 18 of 22 issued on time, 81.8%, rounded to 82%. Cohorts: Sep–Oct 4/5, Nov 3/4, Dec 4/4, Jan 4/5, Feb through 19th 3/4. |
| Weekend rollover: RR-221 | Closed Friday 6 February 16:20 UTC; due Monday 9 February 17:00 GMT (17:00 UTC); issued 9 February 09:16 UTC, on time. |
| Draft versus issue: RR-218 | Closed Thursday 29 January 15:10 UTC; due Friday 30 January 17:00 GMT (17:00 UTC). Draft saved at 16:42 UTC; first formal issue Monday 2 February 09:07 UTC, late. |
| March–August measure | At least 95% of Daniel's required first reports issued by 17:00 local on the next UK working day, using first formal issue. |

December's 4/4 is a small cohort and does not by itself establish a sustained result.

## Decisions and actions

Daniel will check the recorded closure and calculate the deadline before submission, distinguish estimates from confirmed impact against the evidence, and capture the first formal issue UTC. If he is off before the deadline, he will leave the deadline and outstanding evidence with a named duty handover recipient and check that the issue timestamp is captured.

Anjali will meet Daniel on 1 June 2026 at 10:00 BST to review the March–May results against the measure. The 95% figure is the objective for March–August, not a result already achieved.
