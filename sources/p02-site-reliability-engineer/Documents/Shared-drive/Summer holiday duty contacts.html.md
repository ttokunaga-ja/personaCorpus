# Summer holiday duty contacts

Updated 21 June 2024 · Support and Operations desks

Use the [summer weekend duty sheet](../On-call/Summer%20weekend%20duty.pdf) for the dated eight-window rota and the current primary. Times are UK local time (BST).

Call the primary first. If there is no answer, check the sheet for a named relief and call that person; if still unanswered, contact Anjali Shah. If the sheet shows a dash instead of a relief, go straight to Anjali after the unanswered primary call.

| Window | Primary | If unanswered |
|---|---|---|
| 19 July 17:00–22 July 09:00 BST | Matthew Pickering | Anjali Shah; no agreed relief |
| 9 August 17:00–12 August 09:00 BST | Sanjay Mehta | Anjali Shah; no agreed relief |

When passing on an active follow-up, give its current owner, what remains open and the next check time. Ask the incoming engineer to read back ownership and the next check. Until that person explicitly accepts, keep the outgoing owner and next check visible; a change of duty alone does not transfer the item.
