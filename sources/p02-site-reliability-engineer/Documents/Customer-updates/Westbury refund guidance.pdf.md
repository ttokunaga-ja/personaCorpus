# Westbury refund timing and booking advice

Reference: BRT-CU-20251021-001  
Issued: 21 October 2025  
Prepared by: Claire Hutchinson, Customer Support  
For: Stephen Cartledge and the Westbury desk

## What we checked

We identified 62 Westbury refunds that were still outstanding at their individual due times on 20 and 21 October UTC. At our 10:00 UTC snapshot on 21 October, all 62 had been matched to their original bookings. We found no missing bookings in this group.

## Refund progress

Of the 62 refunds, 54 had been submitted for processing and eight were awaiting submission at 10:00 UTC. Submission is a processing step; it does not confirm that the money has arrived in the customer's account. After submission, allow an indicative 3–5 UK working days. This is a guide, not a confirmed arrival date.

For refunds still awaiting submission, we will provide a further update by 15:00 BST on Wednesday 22 October, even if some are still waiting.

## What to tell callers

Please keep the existing booking and do not take it again solely because a refund is late. Ask Support through your existing channel to check the refund status against the original booking. We can then tell you whether submission has been recorded and share the latest available timing.
