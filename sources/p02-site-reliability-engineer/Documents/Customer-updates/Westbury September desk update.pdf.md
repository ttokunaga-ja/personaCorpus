# Westbury September desk update

Reference: BRT-CU-20260915-001
Issued: 15 Sep 2026
Prepared by: Claire Hutchinson, Customer Support
For: Stephen Cartledge, Westbury Coach Cooperative

## What the desk figures show

Stephen, we compared the questions opened by the Westbury desk in two complete Monday–Sunday weeks, using UTC dates. There were 17 in 10–16 August 2026 and 6 in 7–13 September 2026: eleven fewer in the second week. The first week comprised 11 booking-confirmation delay questions and 6 refund-progress questions; the second comprised 4 and 2 respectively.

Each new question is counted once per case and question under its first category. A follow-up call on that same question is not counted again. General ticketing questions and passenger totals are outside these figures. The comparison alone does not tell us why the numbers changed.

## Afternoon preparation

The BRT duty team continues to prepare from 15:30 BST ahead of the working busy start at 16:00 BST. If a confirmation question recurs, please tell Support the time and the kind of question so that we can look at the appropriate period.

## Refund questions

Please keep the existing booking; there is no need to enter it again simply because a refund is pending. Use your existing Support route to ask about refund progress. Support can check the relevant status and explain the next update.
