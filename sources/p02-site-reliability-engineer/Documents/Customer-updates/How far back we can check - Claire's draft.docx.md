# How far back we can check ticket records

Document: BRT-CU-20260511-001  
Version: 0.1 — draft for Daniel's review  
Date: 11 May 2026  
Prepared by: Claire Hutchinson, Customer Support  
For: Stephen Cartledge, Westbury Coach Cooperative, following review

## 1. What the check showed

In the 11 May exercise, the reference time was 10:00 UTC. The complete archive set available at that reference covered ticket records through 09:51 UTC. The point was nine minutes before the reference, within the 15-minute target for this check. This describes the age of the observed archive point; it is not a measure of how long recovery work took. The result is from this exercise and should not be read as a promise that every future recovery point will be the same.

The archive was within nine minutes of recovery.

Daniel — please review the preceding sentence: does “within nine minutes of recovery” make the point and the reference clear enough for Stephen?

## 2. Reading the boundary

For a record timed at 09:51 UTC in the exercise, the archive's confirmed coverage reached that time. A record timed at 09:52 UTC is just one minute later, but falls after the observed boundary. The check does not establish whether that later record is present. The 10:00 UTC reference is the time against which the archive point was compared, not a statement that records through 10:00 were recovered.

## 3. If a record falls after the boundary

Please raise a later or uncertain record with BRT Support for a separate confirmation before relying on it. Give Support the record time and the details needed to locate it through the usual support channel. Support can check the individual case and explain what has been confirmed; do not assume either that the record is present or that it has been lost from this exercise result alone.
