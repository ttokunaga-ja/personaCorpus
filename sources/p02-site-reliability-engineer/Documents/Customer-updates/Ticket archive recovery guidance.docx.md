# Ticket archive recovery guidance

管理欄の版と宛先を次に差し替える。

Version: 1.0  
For: Stephen Cartledge, Westbury Coach Cooperative

既存の3節の見出しを使い、それぞれの本文を以下で差し替える。発行版は変更履歴とコメントの表示・記録がない確定文面とする。

## 1. What the check showed

In the 11 May exercise, the recovered archive covered ticket records through 09:51 UTC at the 10:00 UTC reference. The archive point was nine minutes before the reference and met the 15-minute target for this check. The nine minutes measures the age of the archive point. We measured coverage in this exercise, so the point to use in any future recovery will need to be confirmed at the time.

## 2. Reading the boundary

A ticket record timed at 09:51 UTC in the exercise falls within the confirmed coverage. A record timed at 09:52 UTC falls one minute after that boundary. Its presence in the recovered archive needs a separate check with BRT Support before you rely on it.

## 3. If a record falls after the boundary

If you need to check a later record, or you are unsure which side of the boundary it falls on, contact BRT Support through your usual channel. Give us the record time, its time zone and the details we need to locate the record. Please ask us to confirm whether it is available before using it to answer a ticket query.
