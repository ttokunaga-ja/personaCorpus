# Westbury desk guidance: refund still pending

Updated: 21 October 2025, 15:05 BST  
BRT Customer Support

Navigation: [Refund stages and actions](#refund-stages-and-actions) | [Contact Support](#contact-support)

## Refund stages and actions

Keep the original booking. A delayed refund does not mean the booking should be taken again. Ask Support to check refund progress against the original booking.

| Status | What to tell the caller | Desk action |
|---|---|---|
| Submitted | Allow an indicative 3–5 UK working days from submission. Submission is not confirmation of arrival in the account. | Ask Support for the recorded submission status if needed. |
| Awaiting submission | A further update will be provided by 15:00 BST on 22 October, including if submission is still outstanding. | Keep the existing booking and ask Support for the latest status. |

## Contact Support

Use your existing Support channel for an individual refund status check against the original booking. Do not re-enter the booking merely because a refund is pending.
