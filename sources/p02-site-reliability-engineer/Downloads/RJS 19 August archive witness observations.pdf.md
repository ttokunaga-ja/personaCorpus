# Ticket archive recovery: witness observations

Reference: RJS-WS-20240819-001  
Issued: 19 Aug 2024  
Prepared by: Patrick Sutcliffe, RJS Data Storage Ltd  
To: Daniel Jennings and Sanjay Mehta, BRT Booking Systems Ltd

## Scope

I joined Daniel and Sanjay for the readback of the isolated exercise archive against the fixed 19 August 10:00 UTC reference. The following records what we read and corroborated; it is not an investigation of the cause of the gap.

| Observation | UTC | Witnessed meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Reference | 19 Aug 2024 10:00 | Fixed comparison time for the check. |
| Coverage marker | 19 Aug 2024 09:38 | Selected set covers records through this point. |
| Completion stamp | 19 Aug 2024 09:44 | Writing of that set was complete before the reference. |
| Joint readback | 19 Aug 2024 10:18 | Daniel, Sanjay and I corroborated the readings and agreed the complete set was usable for the exercise. |

## Result and recommendation

The latest complete eligible set's coverage point is 22 minutes before the reference: 10:00 minus 09:38. Against the inclusive 15-minute limit, the check misses by seven minutes. The 09:44 completion stamp establishes completion before the reference; it does not move the coverage marker forward. Neither interval is the elapsed time to restore.

I recommend that RJS and BRT review the interval between captures and retain both timestamps for a comparable repeat. The clock evidence establishes the observed gap and set completion, but does not establish what caused the seven-minute miss or whether an interval change alone will resolve it.
