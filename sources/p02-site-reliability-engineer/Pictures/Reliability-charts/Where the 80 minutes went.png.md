# BRT Fare Payments — where the 80 minutes went

Q4 2024, 1 October 00:00 to 1 January 00:00 UTC (end exclusive). Horizontal stacked bar, with the horizontal axis labelled “Counted unavailable time (minutes)” from 0 to 80. One bar labelled “Q4 2024”.

| Segment, in order from zero | Start (minutes) | End (minutes) | Duration | Legend |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| November Faregate | 0.0 | 42.0 | 42.0 min | November Faregate |
| December Faregate | 42.0 | 64.8 | 22.8 min | December Faregate |
| Other counted interruptions | 64.8 | 80.0 | 15.2 min | Other interruptions |

Bracket the first two segments together: “Faregate: 64.8 of 80.0 minutes — 81% of downtime”. This is a share of unavailable time, not a checkout failure rate. Source: Fare Payments availability Oct to Dec 2024.csv; values are elapsed seconds divided by 60.
