# Twelve minutes waiting for a decision

Horizontal elapsed-time strip, one bar from the 09:00 UTC exercise cue to the 09:34 UTC finish. Horizontal axis: elapsed time (minutes), from 0 to 34; boundaries labelled 0, 4, 16, 26 and 34. The four adjoining segments are:

| Start (min) | End (min) | Duration (min) | Label |
|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 0 | 4 | 4 | Cue to request |
| 4 | 16 | 12 | Request to recorded decision |
| 16 | 26 | 10 | Decision to ready for verification |
| 26 | 34 | 8 | Verification |

Emphasise the 4–16 segment and label it “12 min waiting for recorded approval”. Mark the 20-minute target on the same scale, inside the restoration segment, and label the remaining distance to the 34-minute finish “14 min after target”. The endpoint is Carys's second successful exercise sign-in.
