## Page 1 — Scope and reading the lines

This extract presents comparable priced work from three FY23 contracts. Original and revised columns are before tax and cover the same selected work. The five numbered work lines on pages 2–6 are separate items; the indented outlet protection amount on page 4 is a component of line 3, not another work line. A component is not added to its parent. These are estimates, not records of quantities actually installed or payments made. They do not represent the entire contracts.
