# Revised quantity schedule — Mill Road

Contract reference: PW-23-02  
Date: August 29, 2023  
Contractor: Keller Municipal Paving Inc  
Issuing department: Mapleton Falls Borough Public Works  
Distribution: Field representative and Keller Municipal Paving Inc

## Purpose and limits

Stone Culvert to Pump House Lane. Use this schedule to lay out the revised milling area near the drainage outlet. The estimate carried a full-width cut at that location. Set out a partial-width cut around the outlet and leave the opening and its supporting edge in place.

<!-- page break -->

## Field layout

| Location | Estimate layout | Direction for staking and cutting |
|---|---|---|
| Approach to drainage outlet | Full-width milling | Mark where the full-width cut begins to taper into the partial-width cut. |
| At drainage outlet | Full-width milling | Mill within the marked cut and keep the outlet and its supporting edge clear. |
| Beyond drainage outlet | Full-width milling | Mark the return to the scheduled roadway cut after clearing the outlet. |

The field representative and the contractor shall confirm the marks together before milling. Refer any obstruction that prevents the marked cut to Public Works before moving the line.

<!-- page break -->

## Planned quantity

| Work item | Revised planned quantity | Unit | Limits used for the estimate |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Milling in revised area | 840 | square yards | Partial-width cut around the drainage outlet, including the approach and return transitions |

Use the planned area for field layout. Measure completed work separately under the contract measurement provisions.
