# Quarry Road Resurfacing Agreement

Contract reference: PW-23-03  
Contract date: June 6, 2023  
Contractor: Keller Municipal Paving Inc  
Department: Mapleton Falls Borough Public Works

## Parties and work

Mapleton Falls Borough, acting through Public Works, engages Keller Municipal Paving Inc to resurface Quarry Road from Weighhouse Gate to Briar Street. The Contractor shall prepare and resurface the existing roadway and complete the pavement edge and localized subbase repairs shown in the contract schedules. Protect the adjacent ditch and drainage features while opening and restoring the repair areas.

<!-- page break -->

## Incorporated documents and limits

The scope, drawings, quantity and price schedules and Quarry Road Resurfacing Addendum 1 form part of this agreement. The April 18, 2023 addendum identified Weighhouse Gate as the starting point and retained Briar Street as the ending point before bids closed on May 2, 2023. The limits stated in this agreement incorporate that bid clarification. Use the drawings and schedules within those limits to locate the items to be measured.

<!-- page break -->

## Measurement and payment

Measure surface milling and resurfacing by square yard where specified in the quantity schedule. Record ditch-edge pavement repair and localized subbase repair under their respective pay items. Identify the limits of each repair area in the field record. Submit progress statements with the measured quantities and scheduled unit prices. Public Works shall verify the quantities and acceptance of the work before payment. The schedule quantities are estimates for pricing.

<!-- page break -->

## Original price

| No. | Price field | USD | Basis |
|---|---|---:|---|
| 1 | Original Work Subtotal | 2,800,000 | Work items; excludes materials tax |

The Original Work Subtotal excludes tax on materials. State estimated materials tax separately in any price summary that includes it in the total.

<!-- page break -->

## Written changes

After award, any revision to the work or quantities must be set out in a written change order identifying the affected items and the effect on measurement and price. Both parties shall sign before the revised work proceeds. Apply Measurement and Payment and the scheduled unit prices to existing items identified in the order. The parties shall agree in writing to a price for any new item. The bid limits remain as stated in Incorporated Documents and Limits unless changed by signed order.

<!-- page break -->

## Closeout

Retain the field records supporting the claimed quantities. At completion, request a final measurement. Before final payment, Public Works shall check the final statement against accepted measurements and signed orders and identify any work still outstanding.
