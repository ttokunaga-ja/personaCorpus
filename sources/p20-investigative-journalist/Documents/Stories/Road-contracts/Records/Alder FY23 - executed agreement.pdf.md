# Road Resurfacing Agreement — Alder Road

Contract reference: PW-23-01  
Contract date: May 23, 2023  
Contractor: Keller Municipal Paving Inc  
Department: Mapleton Falls Borough Public Works

## Agreement and limits

Mapleton Falls Borough, through Public Works, and Keller Municipal Paving Inc agree to the FY2023 resurfacing of Alder Road from Depot Gate to Linden Avenue. The Contractor shall mill the existing surface, prepare the exposed pavement and place the specified surface course in the sections identified in the contract drawings. Measure localized repairs directed by Public Works under the applicable items in the quantity schedule.

<!-- page break -->

## Documents and administration

The scope, section drawings and quantity and price schedules identified as PW-23-01 are incorporated into this agreement. Use the drawings to locate each section and the quantity schedule to establish its pay items, units and prices. Refer a conflict among the documents to Public Works before proceeding with the affected work. Public Works shall give its interpretation in writing.

<!-- page break -->

## Measurement and progress payments

Submit each progress statement by pay item, with the measured quantity and the applicable contract unit price. Public Works shall verify the field measurements and review the statement before payment. The quantities used to establish the original work subtotal are estimates. Payment is based on accepted work measured under the incorporated schedule.

<!-- page break -->

## Original price

| No. | Price field | USD | Basis |
|---|---|---:|---|
| 1 | Original Work Subtotal | 2,400,000 | Work items; excludes materials tax |

The Original Work Subtotal covers the original work items before materials tax. State estimated tax on taxable materials separately in the applicable price summary.

<!-- page break -->

## Changes

A revision to work locations or quantities requires a written change order identifying the affected sections and pay items and stating how the revision affects quantities and price. Both parties shall sign the order before the Contractor proceeds with the revised work. Scheduled unit prices apply to existing items identified in the order. Agree to any new item and its price in writing.

## Records and completion

Keep the measurements supporting progress statements available for review. On completion, request a final measurement. Public Works shall review the final statement against the accepted measurements and signed changes and identify any outstanding work before final payment.
