# Change Order 02 — Alder Road

Contract reference: PW-23-01  
Change number: CO-02  
Date: September 12, 2023  
Contractor: Keller Municipal Paving Inc  
Department: Mapleton Falls Borough Public Works

## Change and execution

The parties agree to give priority to the soft pavement area toward Depot Gate. Reassign surface-course quantity no longer needed in the sections identified on the allocation sheet to the Depot Gate side. The work remains within the contract limits from Depot Gate to Linden Avenue.

Mapleton Falls Borough, Public Works  
/s/ Eleanor Briggs  
Eleanor Briggs, Director of Public Works  
Date: September 12, 2023

Keller Municipal Paving Inc  
/s/ Thomas Keller  
Thomas Keller, President  
Date: September 12, 2023

## Work allocation

Use the PW-23-01 section drawing, CO-02 allocation sheet, to identify the Depot Gate treatment area and the sections giving up surface-course quantity. Confirm the limits of soft-area treatment with Public Works before placing the surface course. Record the work by section and charge accepted quantities to the applicable existing pay items at their contract unit prices.

Read this order together with the allocation sheet. The incorporated schedule is revised for the work shown in this order. The remaining work and payment provisions continue under PW-23-01.
