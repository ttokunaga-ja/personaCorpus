# Road Resurfacing Agreement — Alder Road

Contract reference: PW-22-01  
Contract date: May 24, 2022  
Contractor: Keller Municipal Paving Inc  
Department: Mapleton Falls Borough Public Works

## Agreement

Mapleton Falls Borough, acting through Public Works (the Borough), and Keller Municipal Paving Inc (the Contractor) enter into this agreement for FY2022 resurfacing of Alder Road between Depot Gate and Linden Avenue. The Contractor shall mill the existing pavement, prepare the exposed surface and place replacement pavement within the limits shown in the attached scope and quantity schedule. Work at the edges of the cut shall leave a continuous transition to the pavement remaining in place.

<!-- page break -->

## Scope and quantity schedule

The attached scope and quantity schedule is incorporated into PW-22-01. It identifies the work limits, pay items, estimated quantities, measurement units and unit prices. Estimated quantities establish the basis of the bid. The Borough will pay for quantities measured and accepted under this agreement. Refer any discrepancy between the agreement and the schedule to Public Works for written direction before working in the affected area.

<!-- page break -->

## Measurement and payment

The Contractor shall submit progress statements by pay item, showing the quantity performed, the scheduled unit and the contract unit price. Public Works shall verify the quantity of eligible work in place and approve the measured amount for payment. The supporting measurement shall identify the limits of the work covered by each statement.

## Changes in quantities

A change in the scheduled quantities or work limits requires written approval by both parties before the changed work starts. The written change shall identify the affected work and the basis for payment. Apply the scheduled unit price where the change uses an existing pay item. For a new pay item, agree to the unit and price in the written change. A field instruction to measure or mark an area does not authorize a change to the quantities.
