# Change Order 01 — Quarry Road Resurfacing

Contract reference: PW-23-03  
Change number: CO-01  
Date: October 3, 2023  
Contractor: Keller Municipal Paving Inc  
Department: Mapleton Falls Borough Public Works

## Change and signatures

Field review identified the approach to Weighhouse Gate as the higher priority for subbase repair. Reduce the ditch-edge pavement repair area and reassign the corresponding quantity to localized subbase repair on the approach before the gate. The affected work is within the contracted segment from Weighhouse Gate to Briar Street.

The parties execute CO-01 as follows.

Mapleton Falls Borough, Public Works  
/s/ Eleanor Briggs  
Eleanor Briggs, Director of Public Works  
Date: October 3, 2023

Keller Municipal Paving Inc  
/s/ Thomas Keller  
Thomas Keller, President  
Date: October 3, 2023

## Changed work

| Existing pay item | Location | Original estimated quantity | Change | Revised estimated quantity | Unit |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Ditch-edge pavement repair | Along ditch edge | 1,200 | −400 | 800 | square yards |
| Localized subbase repair | Approach before Weighhouse Gate | 600 | +400 | 1,000 | square yards |

The two items carry the same contract unit price. The reduction and addition of 400 square yards offset in the work subtotal. Use the revised estimates to allocate work. Measure the accepted area separately for payment.

<!-- page break -->

## Application and payment

Substitute the estimates in Changed work for the corresponding entries in the incorporated quantity schedule. Confirm the approach repair limits with Public Works before work begins and record the area under each existing pay item. Measurement and Payment in the executed agreement governs progress statements for the revised work. The original bid limits established by Addendum 1 continue to apply.
