# Engineer review — Mill Road shoulder milling

Contract reference: PW-22-02  
Date: August 4, 2022  
Contractor: Keller Municipal Paving Inc  
Issuing department: Mapleton Falls Borough Public Works

## Existing condition

The drainage outlet at the Stone Culvert end of the Mill Road work lies close to the proposed shoulder cut. Carrying the normal milling width through the outlet area could disturb the opening or remove support at its edge. The approach and departure each need enough sound pavement to tie the adjusted cut back into the scheduled milling line.

<!-- page break -->

## Conditional recommendation

Use a narrower milling pass in the immediate outlet area if the field measurements confirm that the opening and its supporting edge can be left intact and that the cut can be tied into the adjoining pavement on both sides. Keep the outlet clear during cutting and surfacing. If the available width does not permit both protection of the outlet and a sound transition, return the sketch to Public Works for further review before cutting at this location.

<!-- page break -->

## Measurements to return

For Keller's field crew: measure from the outlet edge to the proposed line of cut. Record the available shoulder width on both sides of the outlet. Show the outlet, both measurements and the transitions back to the normal cut on the same sketch. Mark any obstruction that would alter the line. Return the measurements and sketch to Public Works before work at the outlet.
