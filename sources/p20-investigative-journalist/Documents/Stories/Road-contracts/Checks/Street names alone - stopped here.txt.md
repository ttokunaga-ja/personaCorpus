June 3 — stopped merging by street name

I tried grouping the archive by road name alone. That collapsed Alder FY2021, Linden Avenue–Foundry Lane (PW-21-01), into the same row as FY2022 and FY2023, Depot Gate–Linden Avenue (PW-22-01 and PW-23-01). It also collapsed Quarry, Briar Street–Hill Street (PW-21-03 and PW-22-03), with Weighhouse Gate–Briar Street (PW-23-03). Those are different stretches; on Alder the same Depot–Linden stretch also has two separate award years.

Undo the merge. Keep award fiscal year, both endpoints and award reference in separate fields for every contract. Use the award archive working index to return to the public notice. A shared street name is a search term, not a contract key.
