Veronica — for the August source check
July 16, 2026

Please keep the date’s role and the original record in separate columns. A date attached to a receipt, a registration, a signature or a field entry does not by itself identify the same event. Sorting all four into one chronology and calling the first date “approval” or “work began” would mix different actions.

Alder FY23, PW-23-01 CO-02: the March paper’s Depot Gate truck entry has Date September 7, 2023 and Entry “Received.” It records a Keller materials truck at the gate, not its use or a start under CO-02. First release p.46 records the parties’ execution signatures dated September 12. Put “vehicle entry” and “execution signature” beside their own records. Neither tells us the delegated decision date.

Quarry FY23, PW-23-03 CO-01: the March traffic-control staging sheet is dated September 28, 2023, with Work category “Traffic preparation” and the construction-work field blank. First release p.95 carries the October 3 execution signatures. The preparation date is not a construction start, and the signature date is not a field report. Keep the source and field name beside each date; the changed subbase work still needs a contemporaneous start record.

For a register, distinguish the date an entry was received or routed from a decision field, if one is found. We should be able to point Richard to the specific field, not just the date on top of a page.
