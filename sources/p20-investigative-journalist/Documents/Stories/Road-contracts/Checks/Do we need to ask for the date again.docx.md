# Keller: questions before we cut the dates

Document no.: ACN-INV-20250625-MEM-001  
Date: June 25, 2025  
Prepared by: Leslie Crawford  
To: Richard Espinoza  
Status: Unsent working questions; Richard's review, June 27

## Scope

Richard, before I put a request to Raymond, here is the rough list. The records already give us the execution dates for Alder CO-02 and Quarry CO-01 and a planned Mill quantity. I have left two weak date questions in place for us to cut or recast; neither should carry the reporting on its own.

## Questions and records

**Rough date questions**

- Alder CO-02 was signed September 12, 2023, and Quarry CO-01 was signed October 3, 2023.
- Where is the completed measurement sheet for the Mill milling area, and may we see it?

**Better line of inquiry**

1. Alder: Who decided the Depot Gate area was soft and needed priority? What observation or measurement supported that judgment? Was retaining the original allocation considered, and where are the field log, limits confirmation and acceptance record?
2. Mill: Who decided a partial-width cut was needed around the outlet, and what would a full-width cut have affected? How were the approach and return included in the planned 840 square yards? Where is the completed measurement sheet?
3. Quarry: Who judged the gate approach subbase needed priority over the ditch edge? What evidence and comparison supported that choice, who confirmed the limits, and where are the field and acceptance records?

The June 23 table identifies the source fields. The truck slip records a vehicle received, not its use; the barrier sheet records traffic preparation with the construction field blank. Neither settles the start of the changed work. The Mill figure is a plan, not a completed measurement.

## Next steps and priority

First ask for the people and original records behind the three necessity judgments; second ask about alternatives and the limits or measurements; then pursue the work and acceptance records. We can state the signed dates from the orders rather than spending questions simply seeking confirmation. I have not decided whether to show Keller the photographs: the views locate 2025 surface features, but a seam alone cannot establish the timing or extent of FY23 work. Let's settle the questions and what a photo could usefully identify before sending one.
