## What the records say and what to ask

| Location and record | What we can say before asking | Four points for Keller |
|---|---|---|
| Alder, PW-23-01 CO-02, release pp. 46–48 | Signed September 12, 2023; surface-course allocation shifted toward the soft area on the Depot Gate side. | What observations led to the soft-area decision? How was the gate approach prioritized against retaining ditch-edge repair? Who confirmed repair limits? What contemporaneous record supports the decision? |
