# Dates I can't call approvals yet

Document no. ACN-INV-20241114-MEM-001  
Version v1.0  
Date November 14, 2024  
Prepared by Leslie Crawford  
To Keith Sandoval and Richard Espinoza

## Scope under review

The August release's approval-related compilation, pp. 117–124, is a place to look, not a complete decision trail. I cannot match each change to a person with authority and a date of approval from this set alone. The date on an executed order does not tell us when the changed work began. The release index identifies the compilation as multiple-contract material; it does not say the set is complete.

| No. | Record and date | What it establishes | What we still need |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Alder FY2023, PW-23-01, CO-02, p. 46, signed September 12, 2023 | Date on the executed change order. | Decision and effective date; delivery and receipt; daily record tied to the changed work. |
| 2 | Quarry FY2023, PW-23-03, CO-01, p. 95, signed October 3, 2023 | Date on the executed change order. | Decision and effective date; delivery and receipt; daily record tied to the changed work. |
| 3 | Mill FY2022, PW-22-02, engineer review p. 17, August 4, 2022 | Conditional recommendation; approval field blank. | Whether the condition was met, who decided, and record of the relevant work. |

## Records still needed

For each change, trace three separate events: the signature on an instrument; its transmission and receipt at each desk; and the work actually performed under the changed scope. A routing or receipt mark can help locate a file without being the decision itself. Ask for the decision record with its authority and effective date and for field logs that identify the same change, not merely the same road.
