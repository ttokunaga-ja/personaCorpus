# Alder and Quarry — possible opening

Document no.: ACN-INV-20240912-OUT-001  
Version: v0.1  
Date: September 12, 2024  
Prepared by: Leslie Crawford  
To: Richard Espinoza  
Status: Working draft for Richard's review only

## Proposed opening

Two Mapleton Falls road contracts appear to have grown by a combined $310,000 between the original work subtotals and the revised totals in the first records release. At Alder Road the two figures are $2,400,000 and $2,542,000, an apparent difference of $142,000. The Quarry Road figures are $2,800,000 and $2,968,000, an apparent difference of $168,000. The apparent differences could give readers a concrete entry into the question of how road work changes after a contract is signed.

## Structure under consideration

Richard, I've limited this opening to the two FY2023 pairs in “First pass - which totals move.xlsx.” The Alder agreement (PW-23-01) gives “Original Work Subtotal” at release p. 31 and CO-02 gives “Revised Total” at p. 48. For Quarry (PW-23-03), the corresponding entries are on pp. 76 and 98, the latter in CO-01. Use the release page numbers in the source notes so the desk can find all four entries.

The first paragraph follows the difference between columns as printed. I've still got the tax basis marked unconfirmed for all four amounts. Before we can put a dollar figure in the lead, we need to see what each subtotal and total includes and check whether we can compare like fields. Even if that works, the result covers these two pairs out of the 18 contracts we're following.

## Editorial decision

Keep the price paragraph on the desk for now. An alternative is to start with the reasons the orders give for shifting work. On Alder, the signed order (p. 46) directs more of the surface work toward the soft area at the Depot Gate end. On Quarry, the signed order (p. 95) shifts work from the ditch edge to subbase repairs on the gate approach. We could lead with those two decisions and then ask what the borough recorded about who authorized the changes and when. The orders give us a place to start the reporting. We still need to pin the approvals to dated records.
