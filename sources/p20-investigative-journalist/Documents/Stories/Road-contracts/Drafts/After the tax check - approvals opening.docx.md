# Alder and Quarry — opening on the change orders

Prepared by Leslie Crawford  
To Richard Espinoza  
Status Working draft for editorial review

## Proposed opening

On Alder Road, a soft stretch of pavement near Depot Gate became the priority under a change order signed by Mapleton Falls Borough and its paving contractor. The order redirected surfacing quantities no longer needed in other sections to the Depot Gate end. It kept the work within the original contract limits, from Depot Gate to Linden Avenue.

On Quarry Road, a field review cited in the change order put a higher priority on repairing the layer beneath the pavement on the approach to Weighhouse Gate. The order reduced the repair area along the ditch and shifted that quantity to the gate approach. The two orders record why the work was redirected. Who approved the changes on those grounds, and when, are the next questions in the records.
