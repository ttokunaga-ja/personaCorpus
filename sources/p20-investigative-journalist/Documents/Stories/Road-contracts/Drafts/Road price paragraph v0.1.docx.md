# What the price lines show

Document no.: ACN-INV-20260424-DRF-001  
Date: April 24, 2026  
Prepared by: Leslie Crawford  
Version: v0.1

The estimated price of selected work on three FY2023 road sections rose from $500,000 to $610,000, an increase of $110,000, or 22 percent, according to a Public Works extract of the original and revised price fields. At Mill Road, the revised work included an allowance for protecting the drainage outlet alongside the price for work around the outlet. The comparison uses estimates excluding tax; it does not show what the borough ultimately paid or how much work was completed.

Editor’s source note: PW-FY23-PRICE-EXTRACT, pp. 2–6; Prices copied before the desk read. The revised column includes the $28,000 outlet protection line separately. See first-pass chart for the proposed comparison.
