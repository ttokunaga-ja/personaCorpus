# Three changes, three explanations

Document no.: ACN-INV-20250721-DRF-001  
Date: July 21, 2025  
Prepared by: Leslie Crawford  
Version: v0.3  
For: Richard Espinoza, July 24, 2025

Keller Municipal Paving says its crews changed the emphasis of work on three Mapleton Falls road segments for different reasons: a soft area near Depot Gate, an existing drainage outlet and weak subbase on the approach to Weighhouse Gate. The available records describe changes to the work or planned quantities; they do not independently establish why each change was needed or what was completed.

## Alder Road

For the Depot Gate end of Alder Road, Keller spokesman Raymond Salazar said the field crew reported that the pavement moved under load. The company said it prioritized the soft area on the gate side on that basis. Salazar did not provide a comparison with the original allocation, the name of the person who confirmed the limits, or a daily report or acceptance record.

The signed September 12, 2023 change order for PW-23-01 redirects surface-course quantity toward the soft area and calls for the treatment limits to be confirmed with Public Works. It describes the change, not the underlying assessment or completed work. A truck entry at Depot Gate five days earlier records a vehicle's receipt; it does not establish its use or the start of this change. On June 12, 2025, a rectangular patch was visible from the public sidewalk. That surface view cannot establish the earlier condition beneath it.

## Mill Road

Salazar said Keller used a partial-width cut around the outlet on Mill Road to protect the existing opening and its supporting edge. The company said a full-width cut risked damaging those features. Its July 15 schedule describes an approach taper, the outlet section and a return as parts of one planned 840-square-yard area; it does not give the area of each part.

The Borough's August 29, 2023 revised PW-23-02 quantity schedule also lists 840 square yards as planned. Keller's schedule draws on that Borough schedule: the two documents give the same planned figure, not independent confirmation of the reason for the cut or a measurement of completed work. Salazar did not provide a completed measurement sheet. A June 12, 2025 view from the public shoulder shows the outlet and a seam curving around it, but the surface cannot establish the area or depth milled in 2023.

## Quarry Road

Salazar said the field crew judged the gate-approach subbase weak and gave it priority over ditch-edge repair. He could not identify a diagnostic record, the person who confirmed the repair limits, or the accepted extent from the material available for the company's response. The stated priority is Keller's explanation, not an independent diagnosis.

The signed October 3, 2023 PW-23-03 change order transfers 400 square yards in the estimates from ditch-edge pavement repair to localized subbase repair before Weighhouse Gate. A September 28 traffic staging sheet records barricades but leaves construction work blank; it does not establish the start or need of the subbase work. Photographs taken from the public road on July 2, 2025 show the gate approach and ditch in relation to one another. They do not show the subbase or the area accepted in 2023.

## Editorial note

Richard — necessity, completed quantities and start dates remain open. Keep Keller's account attributed and the 2025 views limited to surface observations.

- Alder: Leslie will ask Raymond Salazar at Keller for the daily report, assessment and treatment-limit or acceptance records, including any comparison with the original allocation. She will also ask Dorothy Hensley at the Borough's Public Records Office for any Public Works records of the assessment, limits and acceptance. The gate entry does not establish a start date.
- Mill: Miguel will ask Dorothy Hensley for the completed measurement and acceptance sheet for PW-23-02 and compare any returned limits and units with the planned schedule. Leslie will ask Raymond Salazar whether Keller holds a completed measurement sheet or the contemporaneous basis for the outlet decision. The planned 840 square yards is not a completed quantity; the start date remains unconfirmed.
- Quarry: Keith will ask Dorothy Hensley for Public Works records of the subbase decision, inspection and accepted limits under CO-01. Leslie will ask Raymond Salazar to identify the crew's underlying assessment, the person who confirmed the limits and any contemporaneous work or acceptance record. The barrier staging sheet does not establish when repair began.
