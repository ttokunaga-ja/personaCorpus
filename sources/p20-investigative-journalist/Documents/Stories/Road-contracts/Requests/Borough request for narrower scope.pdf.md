# Request for clarification of search scope

Notice no.: MFB-PRO-20240726-001  
Date: July 26, 2024  
To: Leslie Crawford, Allegheny Civic News Cooperative  
From: Dorothy Hensley, Public Records Office, Mapleton Falls Borough

Dear Ms. Crawford:

I am writing about your July 25 request, ACN-INV-20240725-REQ-001, for six categories of records concerning 18 resurfacing contracts. Searching all 18 entries across all six categories at once will require several different searches and a careful matching of the results. The categories are not all filed under a single contract reference.

## How the records are located

Executed contracts are generally located by procurement fiscal year and procurement reference number. Change requests and written justifications, engineer review or recommendation memoranda, and revised scope or quantity schedules are principally located by road name and the endpoints of the work. Signed change orders will also need to be matched to the corresponding contract and road segment. Council or delegated approval records may require a search by the agenda item name; a road name alone may not identify the relevant item.

The same road name can refer to different stretches, and the same stretch can appear in more than one procurement year. Please identify the fiscal year and road endpoints you would like us to search first, together with the procurement reference where available. That will help us distinguish the entries and choose the appropriate search routes. If you have an agenda item name for a particular entry, please include it as well.

This is a request to clarify priorities and scope. No records are enclosed with this notice, and it does not determine whether any particular record exists or is available for release. Once you identify the priority entries, we can proceed with the searches and communicate about the results.
