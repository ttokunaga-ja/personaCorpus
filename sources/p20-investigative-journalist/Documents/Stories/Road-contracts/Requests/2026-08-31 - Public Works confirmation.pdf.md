# Confirmation of two Public Works fields

Reference: MFB-PRO-20260831-001  
Date: August 31, 2026  
To: Leslie Crawford, Allegheny Civic News Cooperative  
From: Dorothy Hensley, Public Records Office

Dear Ms. Crawford:

I am writing in response to your August 28 inquiry about two entries in the August 22 supplemental release (MFB-PRO-20260822-001). Eleanor Briggs, Director of Public Works, checked both entries against the department's decision register and completed-measurement book on August 31, 2026.

## PW-23-01 CO-02 — decision register, S036

The September 11, 2023 entry is the record of delegated approval for Change Order 02 on Alder Road. The parties' execution signatures, dated September 12, 2023, are on the change order at page 46 of the initial release. The register and the signed order record separate actions.

## PW-23-02 — completed-measurement book, S092

The September 5, 2023 entry is Public Works' confirmed measurement of completed milling on the Mill Road section from Stone Culvert to Pump House Lane. The revised quantity schedule in the initial release lists the separate planned quantity of 840 square yards. The completed-measurement entry is divided as follows.

| Recorded category | Area (square yards) |
|---|---:|
| Approach | 186 |
| Around outlet | 477 |
| Return | 149 |
| Total | 812 |

This letter confirms the meaning of the two fields you identified. The 93 pages previously released as S001–S093 are available through the arrangements in the August 22 letter and are not enclosed with this response.
