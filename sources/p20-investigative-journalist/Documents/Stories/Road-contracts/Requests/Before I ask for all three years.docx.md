# Before I ask for all three years

Document no.: ACN-INV-20240712-MEM-001
Date: July 12, 2024
Prepared by: Leslie Crawford
To: Keith Sandoval

## Scope

Keith, I propose that the first request cover all 18 Mapleton Falls resurfacing awards in FY2021–FY2023, six per award year. The question Richard and I settled on is: “Which road jobs were allowed to change, and on what grounds?” The archive gives us references, award years, awardees and bid endpoints, but it cannot tell us which jobs changed after execution. We should preserve each year and reference even where the endpoints repeat.

## Records needed

| Existing record | Question it helps answer |
| --- | --- |
| Executed contracts, including incorporated schedules | What was agreed at the outset, and what schedule was incorporated? |
| Signed change orders | Was a change formally recorded, and who signed and when? |
| Change requests and written justifications | What change was requested, and what reasons were given? |
| Engineer review or recommendation memoranda | What technical review or recommendation accompanied the request? |
| Council or delegated approval records | What decision was made, by whom and when? An agenda listing alone is not a decision. |
| Revised scope or quantity schedules | What scope or quantities were recorded after a change, and how do they relate to the signed order? |

None of these categories establishes that a change exists for every award. We need the actual records, including any documents that show a request was not approved, to follow the evidence.

## Next step

I'll use the notices and photographs to check search terms, especially the endpoints that recur across years. The Quarry addendum is a pre-bid source for the Weighhouse Gate–Briar Street description; the gate photograph identifies a landmark. Neither establishes a later approval.

Please check the 18 references and stretches with me before filing. I will ask for existing records and electronic copies, not ask the office to create our comparison table. If the office's filing system uses different identifiers, we can ask what information will help it locate the records.
