## Scope

Keith and I settled the first searches this afternoon, after Dorothy's July 26 scope inquiry, MFB-PRO-20240726-001. The unit is the procurement year paired with the road segment, not a road name by itself. These five entries cover two procurement years and three stretches:

| Procurement year | Reference | Road | Endpoints |
|---|---|---|---|
| FY2022 | PW-22-01 | Alder Road | Depot Gate–Linden Avenue |
| FY2022 | PW-22-02 | Mill Road | Stone Culvert–Pump House Lane |
| FY2023 | PW-23-01 | Alder Road | Depot Gate–Linden Avenue |
| FY2023 | PW-23-02 | Mill Road | Stone Culvert–Pump House Lane |
| FY2023 | PW-23-03 | Quarry Road | Weighhouse Gate–Briar Street |

Keep Alder and Mill in both years. Each repeats the same endpoints, so retaining both procurement references gives us a way to catch records assigned to the wrong year when files are organized by road. This is a search choice, not a finding that either contract changed.

For Quarry, the April 18, 2023 addendum identifies Weighhouse Gate–Briar Street before the May 2 bid deadline. It helps identify the bid segment; it is not a post-award change order or an approval. Briar Street–Hill Street is a separate stretch and is not included in the FY2023 Quarry entry.

## Records needed

For each of the five entries request the same six categories as July 25: executed contracts including incorporated schedules; signed change orders; change requests and written justifications; engineer review or recommendation memoranda; council or delegated approval records; and revised scope or quantity schedules. That is 30 entry-and-category combinations to search, not a count of documents that exist or have been received.

## Next steps

I will refile with the five references and endpoints. The procurement year is a locator, not a restriction to records created, circulated, approved, or work performed in that year. Dorothy explained that contracts, road files and approval records have different search routes; we should give her identifiers for each rather than ask her to construct our comparison.

The other 13 contracts remain parked for a later inquiry: all six FY2021 entries and the seven remaining entries in FY2022 and FY2023. This reduces the immediate search while retaining paired years on two stretches and a distinct Quarry segment. It does not resolve the status of those 13 contracts. No records have been delivered in response to the scope inquiry, and the presence or absence of changes remains to be established from records.
