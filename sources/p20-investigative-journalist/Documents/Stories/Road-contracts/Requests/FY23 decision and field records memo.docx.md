# Ask for the record behind each date

Document no.: ACN-INV-20260603-MEM-001  
Version: v1.0  
Date: June 3, 2026  
Prepared by: Leslie Crawford  
To: Keith Sandoval

Keith,

The February 6, 2025 request asked for decisions and work records across four entries. I want our next request to identify three specific gaps, using the FY23 references rather than the road names alone. The July 2025 desk check separates the company’s account from what the records establish; it did not turn the supporting papers into proof of a decision or completed work.

| Record to locate | What we have | Why it is not a substitute |
|---|---|---|
| PW-23-01 Alder, CO-02: delegated decision entry, including decision maker and date | First release p.46 carries the parties’ September 12 execution signatures; the March paper records a September 7 truck entry at Depot Gate. | Execution is not the delegated decision. The gate slip is a receipt for a vehicle, with no CO-02 or load-use field. |
| PW-23-02 Mill: completed measurement of the revised milling area, including its components and confirmation | First release p.63 and Keller’s schedule each give 840 square yards as the planned total. | Keller draws on the same planned schedule; neither document records completed measurement. |
| PW-23-03 Quarry, CO-01: contemporaneous daily report or start record for the changed subbase work at Weighhouse Gate | First release p.95 is the October 3 execution; the September 28 paper describes traffic preparation. Keller gives its account of the need for the work. | The staging sheet’s construction-work field is blank. A company explanation of need does not establish when the changed work began. |

Please use these three descriptions when checking with the records office. Ask for the actual field and decision records, not another copy of the signed order or the planned quantity schedule. Keep the earlier four-contract request and its unresolved items in our file; this narrower follow-up does not settle them.

Leslie
