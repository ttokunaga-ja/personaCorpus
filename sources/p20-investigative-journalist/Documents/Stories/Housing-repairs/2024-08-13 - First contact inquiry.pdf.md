# First contact records for Parkview repairs

ACN-INV-20240813-REQ-001
August 13, 2024

Theresa Coleman
Parkview Court Tenants Association

Dear Ms. Coleman,

Thank you for explaining the association's records and sharing the copies with identifying details removed. We are looking at the 22 requests you discussed with me on August 6. The association's entry date does not itself tell us when a resident first contacted management.

Could you tell me whether there are records that might establish a contact with management before a request was reported to the association? In particular, might a resident have a receipt or acknowledgment issued by the management office, or a copy of the resident's own first message or other communication to management? We would need the date and enough context to identify the nature of the contact, without names, addresses or unit numbers.

By August 16, could you let me know whom we should approach for each kind of record and whether an anonymized copy might be available? If a resident would need to give permission or provide a copy individually, please let me know; I am not asking the association to supply a record it does not hold.

Thank you for your help.

Leslie Crawford
Investigative Reporter
Allegheny Civic News Cooperative
leslie@allegheny-civic.example
