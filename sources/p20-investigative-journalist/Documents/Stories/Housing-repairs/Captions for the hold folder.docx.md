## Use and limits

The frames can explain a fixing, a filled joint and a pipe connection. The pipe caption describes the outside of the coupling at the moment photographed; it does not say the leak stopped. The unpainted window edge does not tell us which stage of painting we are seeing. None of the pictures establishes a completion date or the interval after a request.

## Hold

Keep the pictures and these captions in the hold folder as candidates. They can serve as explanatory photographs, subject to publication approval; the housing story itself remains on hold pending the records check. Dates and claims about work need their own support.
