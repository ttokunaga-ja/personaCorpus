# Repair date fields: question for a desk read

ACN-INV-20240809-MEM-001 | v0.2 | August 12, 2024
To: Veronica Huang
From: Leslie Crawford

## Working proposal

The association register covers the 22 requests Theresa discussed on August 6. The resident-slip bundle has signed slips with a completion date for 14. We can report the number for which completion dates are verified, but the association's recorded intake is not necessarily the first contact with management. Calculating a management waiting time requires evidence of that first contact as well as the completion date. The other eight have no verified completion date in this bundle and must not be assigned a duration.

| R-17 | July 29, resident slip: Visit measurement date | Measurement is not a completion entry; the completion field is blank. |
| R-20 | August 2, resident slip: Return visit appointment booked | The booking date is not a record of completed work; the completion field is blank. |
| R-22 | August 5, association register: Date recorded; Entry type: Ticket transcription | This is a register entry date, not a completion field; the original ticket is not in the bundle. |

## Questions for Veronica

Can the association intake dates for the 14 be treated as the dates residents first contacted management? The association's notice asks residents to contact management first, so I do not know that the two starts coincide. If they cannot be equated, is the association-to-completion interval useful with a precise label, or would it invite a claim about management's response that the records cannot support? Please also check the field names in the three returns above before we discuss the pitch.
