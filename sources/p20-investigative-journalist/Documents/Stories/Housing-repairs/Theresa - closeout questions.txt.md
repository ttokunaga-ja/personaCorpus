March 17, 2026

Before I contact Theresa: what do I need from the eight files?

For each date, first ask which field it belongs to: receipt of the request, a visit, completion of work, or an entry in the association's records. Don't copy a visit or an entry date into the completion column. R-17 is the warning: the July 29, 2024 visit note records measurement, not replacement.

Where a resident confirmed work, ask whether the original written record gives an explicit date when the work was finished. A later date when the association entered the information is a separate fact. If the underlying record does not say when the work ended, leave the completion date blank and note what the available date does mean.

Ask Theresa for copies we can match by reporting code, with no names, addresses or other resident identifiers. That should let us compare the records without bringing identifying details into the desk file. Keep the eight answers separate; an unclear date in one file should not determine the others.
