# Reporting a repair concern
Parkview Court Tenants Association  
Reference: PCTA-RPR-20240729-001  
July 29, 2024  
Prepared by Theresa Coleman

If something in your home or a shared area needs repair, please report it first to the management office through its usual repair contact. Give the office enough detail to identify the problem and ask how to follow up.

If you would also like to tell the tenants association how the request is progressing, that is entirely your choice. It helps to keep a note of the date you first contacted management, what needed attention, and whether anyone visited. If a worker gives you a resident copy of a work slip, please keep it. The names of the fields and any dates on that copy may help you ask a follow-up question later.

You do not have to report a repair to the association. When the association records a concern, that entry only records what was brought to us; it does not mean that management has arranged the work or that the work is complete. For an urgent problem, contact management through its emergency procedure rather than waiting for the association to respond.

Theresa Coleman
