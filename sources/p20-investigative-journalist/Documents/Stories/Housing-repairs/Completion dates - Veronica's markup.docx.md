# Housing repairs — working passage

Version: v0.2

## Lead

We could verify completion dates for 19 of the 22 repair requests, according to records reviewed with the tenants association. The requests were among those discussed in a 2024 interview about waits for repairs at Parkview Court.

## How we checked

Fourteen of the 22 requests already had documented completion dates. On March 24, Theresa Coleman of the Parkview Court Tenants Association walked through the records with Veronica Huang and me. We revisited the eight entries for which we had not previously established a completion date. Three signed work-ticket entries and two residents' written accounts supplied dates of finished work. For the other three, the copies showed a measurement, an appointment and a transcription, respectively; none supplied a completion date.

For R-17, the July 29, 2024 entry records a visit to measure a door brush, not completion of a replacement. For R-20, August 2, 2024 is the date a return visit about a washbasin drain was booked; the entry does not establish whether the visit or any work took place. For R-22, August 5, 2024 is the date a communal-lighting ticket was transcribed into the association register; the original ticket's completion field has not been established. These gaps do not establish that any of the three jobs remained unfinished. We need the replacement completion ticket, any report of work after the booked return visit, and the original lighting ticket, respectively, before drawing a conclusion about their completion dates.

## Desk note

The 19 combines the 14 previously verified completion dates and five newly verified dates for this set of 22 requests. It is not a measure of the condition of every home at the time of reporting. The three remaining records do not establish whether their work was finished. Hold this passage for Veronica's check before publication.
