Before counting, point us to the field that says the work was finished, and the date attached to that statement. A date on a visit or register is not automatically a completion date.

R-12: On the resident's written account, where is the date the window-frame gap was filled? Please distinguish that from the association register entry dated November 14, 2024.

R-15: Where does the resident's writing say the sink drain gasket replacement was finished? Please show us that separately from the January 21, 2025 association registration.

R-17: Does the underlying record contain an entry saying the door brush was replaced and giving the completion date? The July 29 measurement alone does not say so.

R-20: Is there a work report after the return visit, with a statement and date of completion? August 2 is the date the visit was booked.

R-22: Did the original lighting ticket have a completion field before it was transcribed? Can we see the original and any completion entry? August 5 is the register transcription date.

If a completion entry cannot be shown, leave that date blank. Do not convert a measurement, appointment or transcription date into a completion date.
