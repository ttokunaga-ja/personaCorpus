# Housing repairs — working passage

Leslie Crawford · For Veronica Huang  
Status: Hold

## Lead

Nineteen of 22 repair requests were completed, according to records reviewed with the tenants association. The requests were among those discussed in a 2024 interview about waits for repairs at Parkview Court.

## How we checked

Fourteen of the 22 requests already had documented completion dates. On March 24, Theresa Coleman of the Parkview Court Tenants Association walked through the records with Veronica Huang and me. We revisited the eight entries for which we had not previously established a completion date. Three signed work-ticket entries and two residents' written accounts supplied dates of finished work. For the other three, the copies showed a measurement, an appointment and a transcription, respectively; none supplied a completion date.

## Desk note

The 19 combines the 14 previously verified completion dates and five newly verified dates for this set of 22 requests. It is not a measure of the condition of every home at the time of reporting.
