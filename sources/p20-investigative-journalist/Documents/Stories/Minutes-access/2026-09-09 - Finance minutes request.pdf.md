# Public records request: approved Finance Committee minutes

Document no.: ACN-INV-20260909-REQ-001  
Date: September 9, 2026  
Prepared by: Leslie Crawford  
To: Dorothy Hensley, Public Records Office, Mapleton Falls Borough

Dear Ms. Hensley:

I am requesting the approved minutes of the Finance Committee meeting of February 13, 2025. My earlier request was MIN-25-107. Your June 10, 2025 notice, MFB-PRO-20250610-002, said the Clerk maintained an unapproved draft; neither the draft nor an approved copy accompanied that notice.

Please also provide existing approval and posting records that show the date the minutes were approved and the first date they were available for public inspection. If the approval record is located before the other material, I would appreciate receiving it separately.

If an approved copy cannot be found, please tell me how the draft was handled and identify the repositories or offices searched. If another record identifies the approval or initial availability date, please identify it as well. Please let me know if you need clarification of the meeting or the records requested.
