## Page 60 — Counter operations review criteria

Library · LP-25-04 · Changeover review  
Apply the site-coordination and changeover provisions of the January 27 specification. Read the proposed sequence against the work as it would take place at the counter.

| Point in the changeover | What the written offer should establish | What Library staff will confirm before work |
|---|---|---|
| Before disconnection | Which positions the installer proposes to take out first and which remain usable | Initial work window, allowed work area and clear route to the active terminal |
| First removal and fitting | Checkout remains available while the affected position is out of service | Terminal to keep running and how staff will direct patrons to it |
| Transfer to the next position | A newly fitted terminal completes checkout and is usable before the earlier active position is released | Who witnesses the checkout and checks the ready screen and patron approach |
| A failed test or late finish | Where the work can stop while leaving checkout available | Keep the active terminal in service and agree a revised window |
| Return to full use | Each device is tested and released for its intended use | Record checks for four terminals and two printers and confirm acceptance |

Scheduling with Library staff is required. To support continuity in this review, the written sequence must also identify a workable staged handoff or explicitly show how at least one terminal stays in use throughout.

## Page 64 — Staff recommendation

Mapleton Falls Borough Library · LP-25-04 · March 13, 2025

Staff recommends Lanton Counter Systems’ Q01 at USD 63,400 before tax. The amount covers four self-checkout terminals, two card printers, installation and working demonstration, and 36 months of on-site maintenance including covered parts and labor.

Mercerwick Library Supply’s Q03 is USD 62,900 on that same basis, USD 500 below Q01. Alderfen Access Equipment’s Q04 is USD 64,200 on a combined basis. The reason for recommending Q01 is its written plan to replace and test in stages while keeping at least one terminal available throughout the changeover. That is the evidence staff relied on to address continued checkout service. Q03 includes installation and demonstration and says the installation will be arranged with Library staff. The reviewed form, service papers and Purchasing transmittal do not establish the same continuity commitment for Q03.

Please circulate the price comparison and the Q01 sequence with this recommendation. Include the Q03 work-sequence finding so the difference in written support can be considered alongside the $500 difference in price. Confirm the actual windows, position sequence and operating checks with the supplier before any work is scheduled.

Gerald Scott  
Mapleton Falls Borough Library
