# Library checkout equipment records request
Document no.: ACN-INV-20251022-REQ-001  
Date: October 22, 2025  
From: Leslie Crawford, Allegheny Civic News Cooperative  
To: Dorothy Hensley, Public Records Office, Mapleton Falls Borough

Dear Ms. Hensley,

Please provide the existing records below for LP-25-04, the spring 2025 purchase of replacement equipment for the Library's checkout counter. The nine submitted quotations and their supporting records are within this request.

| No. | Record | Scope |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Issued specification | Original specification and instructions to suppliers, including the response form and any revisions issued to submitters. |
| 2 | Quotations and intake | All nine responses with enclosures and any later sheets or clarifications filed with them. Please include the receipt record linking each response to papers received separately. |
| 3 | Warranty and maintenance | Manufacturer warranty terms and any separately quoted or separately contracted maintenance. Please include the price, service period, on-site coverage, parts and labor provisions, and exclusions as recorded. |
| 4 | Scoring and selection | Completed scoring or comparison records, staff evaluations or recommendations, and the routing materials and attachments used to circulate them for consideration. |
| 5 | Meetings | Minutes for meetings where LP-25-04 was considered, with the associated agenda items and any attachments incorporated into those minutes. |

If the maintenance terms are filed separately at the Library, please include those records in the search. I would prefer copies of existing electronic files. For paper-only records I can arrange to inspect them or collect copies. If you need a narrower description to locate a particular file, please email me at the address below.

Thank you,
