# Counter equipment — issue specification
Issuing unit: Mapleton Falls Borough Library  
Reference: LP-25-04  
Issued: January 27, 2025  
Issued by: Gerald Scott, Mapleton Falls Borough Library  
Distribution: Borough Purchasing and prospective quotation submitters

## Page 1 — Invitation and required scope

The Borough requests quotations for replacement self-checkout equipment at the Library's public counter. Quote the equipment and the work required to leave it in use, as specified below. Return the Borough response form on pages 11 and 12 and identify any additional sheets. Purchasing will file later papers with the response to which they belong.

| Item | Quantity | Scope to include |
|---|---:|---|
| Self-checkout terminals | 4 | Complete units for the public counter, with the connections and accessories required to complete a checkout. |
| Card printers | 2 | Complete units, with connections and accessories required to print a usable card from the staff workstation. |
| Delivery, installation and demonstration | One installation covering all six units | Bring in and install the new units, disconnect the units being replaced and demonstrate operation on each installed device. Include normal mounting and connection materials. |
| On-site maintenance | 36 months from acceptance | Fault intake, site attendance, replacement parts and labor for covered equipment. State any exclusions on the response form. |

Give the manufacturer and model for each type of unit and identify what accessories your equipment price includes. Describe alternate quantities or scope in the exceptions field. Include manufacturer warranty terms even when you also offer maintenance. Use the tax field to state the basis of your prices. A proposed alternate should be described clearly enough for Purchasing to identify which requirement changes.

## Page 2 — Equipment and connections

Each terminal must complete a checkout and return to the ready screen for the next patron. Each card printer must receive a job from the staff workstation and produce a card the staff member can read and check. Allow for the connections, mounts and short leads required for the offered equipment at the counter. Identify any work the Borough would have to arrange before installation, such as an outlet or data connection at a location the supplier cannot use as found.

| Supplier to confirm | Information to supply with response |
|---|---|
| Terminals and printers | Manufacturer, model and number of units of each type. Identify any accessory on which the proposed operation depends. |
| Counter connection | What must be available at each equipment position, what connections are included and what must be arranged by the Borough. |
| Removal from service | Method for disconnecting, labeling and handing the displaced equipment back to Library staff. Identify any work needed before the first unit comes off the counter. |
| Commissioning | Which checks the installer will perform and any setup or staff participation needed to run them. |

Leave existing mounting surfaces and staff connections usable after each stage. Move displaced units only to the holding point directed by Library staff. Leave all cables clear of the public approach before reopening a work position.

## Page 3 — Site coordination and work windows

Arrange work windows with the Library before bringing equipment into the building. Put deliveries and any work that blocks a patron aisle outside public service hours. During public service hours, confine work to the area released by Library staff and keep the patron route to the active terminal clear. Staff will tell the installer which position can be taken out of service at the start of each window. The installer must check back before taking another position offline.

Show the proposed window for each interruption, its duration, the devices affected and whether any equipment will remain out of service when the window ends. If work extends beyond an agreed window, stop at a point where staff can operate the counter safely and agree a new window before resuming. Advise the Library of any proposed full interruption before setting an installation date, and identify it expressly in the quotation.

## Page 4 — Changeover sequence to include with offer

Give the order of disconnection, fitting and testing and show how checkout can remain available. Identify how at least one terminal will remain usable during each stage of the replacement, or describe the exception if the proposed procedure requires an interruption. A working terminal has to be connected, tested and accessible to patrons. Identify any temporary position the Library would need to make available.

Use this schedule in a supporting sheet if the sequence does not fit on the response form. Enter the proposed time or duration for each step and name the unit or position that stays in service.

If an installed unit fails a check, leave the previously available terminal in service, advise Library staff and agree how to complete the affected work. The submitted sequence will be read with the offer so staff can judge how the changeover would affect service at the counter.

## Page 5 — Installation and handover procedure

1. At delivery, reconcile the equipment with the offer and note any substitution before opening an installation position. Assign the counter position labels with Library staff. Enter the model and serial number of each unit on the equipment-specific inspection sheets.
2. Check each terminal in its installed position. Run a checkout using the Library's designated test item, verify the checkout result and confirm that the terminal is ready for the next transaction. Check that the unit is secure and the public approach is clear.
3. At each printer, send a test job from the staff workstation. Confirm the job reaches the correct device, the card feeds and prints legibly, and the device is ready for a second job.
4. Record a failed or incomplete check against the affected unit. Correct the condition and repeat the test. Give Library staff the operating instructions, warranty documents and maintenance contact information before asking for acceptance.
5. Library staff record acceptance after the required installation and operating checks are satisfactory. Delivery and equipment count alone do not establish acceptance. Record the acceptance date and confirm the date from which maintenance runs.

## Page 8 — Maintenance and warranty instructions

Quote 36 months of maintenance beginning at acceptance. Include attendance at the Library and the parts and technician labor needed to correct covered faults. State how staff report an outage, what information they should supply, and who arranges a visit if remote diagnosis does not restore operation. Identify charges outside the offer, including consumables and any exclusions for damage or use. If travel or diagnostics incur a separate charge, state that in the exclusions field.

| Response form entry | What the Library needs to see |
|---|---|
| Fault reporting | Service desk or other point of contact, hours for reporting a fault and arrangements for site attendance following diagnosis. |
| Start and duration | The event from which the term runs and the number of months covered. State any period that would begin at delivery instead of acceptance. |
| On-site coverage | Which units can be serviced at the counter and which faults would require removal. Identify any service offered remotely only. |
| Parts | Whether covered replacement parts are included and the provisions for replacements billed separately. |
| Labor | Whether site labor is included and when labor or travel charges fall outside that coverage. |
| Warranty | Manufacturer defects coverage and duration, including return arrangements if applicable. Enter this independently of the maintenance offer. |

## Page 9 — Price entries and exceptions

Use U.S. dollars and enter prices on the basis stated in the tax field. Enter the equipment price including delivery, installation and demonstration when those services are included. On a separate line give the price of the requested maintenance if you are quoting it separately. If you offer maintenance on a different basis, describe the offered term and scope and enter its price on the applicable service line with the exception clearly identified. State explicitly if a limited service is free of charge.

| Offer structure | Entries to complete |
|---|---|
| Separate prices | Equipment and maintenance amounts, with the terms for each. Leave the combined price field empty. |
| Equipment only | Equipment amount and what it covers. Identify maintenance as unpriced. Leave the maintenance and combined price fields empty. |
| One all-in price | Combined price and a list of the work and maintenance covered. Leave the individual prices empty unless they are separately quoted on that basis. |
| Alternate scope | Offered quantities, installation or service departures, and any charges payable separately. Identify any other tax basis in words. |

If any element is included at no charge, identify the element and write “no charge.” An empty line will be read as an unstated price. State whether tax is included or excluded. If neither description is sufficient, explain how tax is treated.

## Page 10 — Returning the response

Return both pages of the signed Borough response form to Borough Purchasing by 4:00 p.m. Eastern time on February 21, 2025. Deliver a sealed paper response by mail or in person to Borough Purchasing at the Borough office. Mark the outside “LP-25-04 — Counter equipment.” Keep a copy of the two response pages and any enclosures.

| Before sending | Check |
|---|---|
| Identification and scope | Supplier and date entered, models and counts for both kinds of equipment, accessories and exceptions identified. |
| Price and terms | Price basis and tax stated, any combined scope described, manufacturer warranty and maintenance shown separately. |
| Installation and service continuity | Included work stated, proposed sequence described, supporting sheets named. |
| Authorization | Authorized signatory, signature and date entered. Both pages returned with any named attachments. |

Purchasing assigns the receipt number. If a supporting sheet is supplied later, identify the submitter, procurement reference and original receipt number, and indicate whether the sheet supplements or changes any entry. The Library will review the offered scope, price and work sequence. The return of a quotation does not authorize delivery or installation.
