## Page 8 — Release page 46: Q04 coverage clarification

From: Gerald Scott, Library  
To: Alderfen Access Equipment  
Reference: LP-25-04 / Q04

**Inquiry.** If the service desk sends someone out for a covered fault and a part has to be replaced, does the combined price cover the part and the work? Is there a separate amount to enter on our maintenance line?

**Supplier response.** Covered parts and the work to replace them come under the quoted package. Card stock and damage outside normal use remain outside the service schedule. The offer is for the package at the stated price. No equipment or service allocation is supplied.

**Library entry.** Retain the supplier's combined amount and the scope together. Leave the separate price lines unfilled.
