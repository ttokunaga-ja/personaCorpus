# Maybe lead with the cheaper quote

Document no.: ACN-INV-20260114-DRF-001  
Date: January 15, 2026  
Prepared by: Leslie Crawford  
Version: v0.2  
For: Leslie Crawford  
Reviewed by: Veronica Huang

One offer for Mapleton Falls Borough's library checkout equipment came in $500 below the price the library staff recommended. Mercerwick Library Supply's Q03 was $62,900, compared with $63,400 for Lanton Counter Systems' Q01, when the equipment, installation and 36 months of on-site parts and labor are compared before tax. Three of the nine submitted quotes could be compared on those terms. The other six differed in equipment, installation, service or stated tax basis, or left one of those terms unspecified.

For staff, the question was what would happen at the checkout counter while the old terminals came out. Gerald Scott's March 13 evaluation cites Lanton's written plan to replace and test the terminals in stages, with at least one terminal available throughout. The staff recommended Q01 even though its comparable price was higher. In Mercerwick's submission, installation was to be arranged with library staff. I haven't found a written commitment from Mercerwick that spells out how a terminal would stay available during the work.

[Hold for meeting records: LP-25-04 appears on the March 18 Library Advisory Committee agenda at item 4 and on the April 8 Borough Council agenda at purchasing item 7B. The March 13 staff recommendation is at release p. 64. The routing slips at pp. 71 and 73 show where it went. Leave the discussion, any vote and any decision by either body out of the story while the Jan. 12 minutes request is pending.]

The comparison covers four self-checkout terminals and two card printers, installation and a working demonstration. Lanton also submitted a $44,800 equipment-only offer. In the Lanton offer that staff recommended, the same equipment figure appears alongside a separate $18,600 maintenance charge. Scott explained that difference in May.
