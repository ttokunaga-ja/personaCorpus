# How the library chose its equipment
Document no.: ACN-INV-20251020-PIT-001  
Date: October 20, 2025  
Prepared by: Leslie Crawford  
To: Richard Espinoza

## Reporting question
Which offers met the same requirements, and what reasons did the Borough put on the record for its choice? At 9:35 this morning we agreed to follow the nine spring checkout-equipment quotes. Readers should be able to see what the library expected to buy, what the prices actually covered and whose reasons we're reporting.

Put the quotes on one footing: four self-checkout terminals, two card printers, installation and a working demonstration, a common tax basis (before tax), and 36 months of on-site maintenance with parts and labor. Keep the other offers visible, with the term that keeps each out of the price comparison.

## What the public record shows

Gerald Scott explained on May 20 that one figure covered equipment and maintenance and the other covered equipment alone. Take the apparent $18,600 difference out of the article's conclusion. We still need the submitted forms to establish which prices can be compared and the selection and meeting records to establish how the purchase was handled.

## What we need to obtain

| Record series | Known at the pitch meeting | Next thing to obtain |
|---|---|---|
| Submitted quotes | Nine offers define the reporting scope. We haven't checked the original price and quantity fields across the set. | Ask for all nine original responses and the intake record. Match attachments and later pages to the original submissions. |
| Conditions | Gerald Scott explained on May 20 that one of the figures was equipment only and the other included maintenance. | Get the issued specification, warranty terms and separate service sheets. Log the evidence for quantities, installation, tax and the duration and scope of maintenance. |

| Meetings | We don't yet have a meeting record showing how a recommendation was handled. | Use the circulation file to identify the relevant bodies and dates. Find the agendas for locating items and the minutes for what was considered and decided. |

I'll send the records request. Once the terms are in, I'll ask Miguel to check the arithmetic and take any unresolved scope questions back to Gerald.
