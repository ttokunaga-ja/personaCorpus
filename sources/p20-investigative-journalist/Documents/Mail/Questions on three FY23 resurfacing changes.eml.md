Hi Raymond,

I am examining three FY2023 road contracts and would appreciate Keller's account of the decisions behind the changes. The page references below are to the Borough's August release. Please respond by 5:00 p.m. EDT on July 16, and let me know if another person can help locate the underlying records.

Alder Road, Depot Gate–Linden Avenue: PW-23-01 CO-02, signed September 12, 2023, reassigns surface-course quantity toward the soft area near Depot Gate (release pp. 46–48).

1. What observations led the crew to identify that area as soft and in need of priority treatment?
2. What comparison was made with continuing under the original allocation, and what would that alternative have meant?
3. Who measured and confirmed the limits of the soft area and the proposed treatment?
4. Which contemporaneous daily report or acceptance record records the decision and work, and may we see it?

Mill Road, Stone Culvert–Pump House Lane: the August 29, 2023 revised quantity schedule for PW-23-02 changes the outlet layout from full-width to partial-width milling and lists 840 square yards as the planned area, including transitions (release pp. 61–64).

5. Why was a full-width cut not retained around the outlet?
6. What effect did Keller expect if full-width milling remained there?
7. How were the approach taper, the partial-width outlet section and the return laid out, and how was the 840-square-yard estimate derived?
8. Where is the completed measurement sheet for the actual area, and may we see it?

Quarry Road, Weighhouse Gate–Briar Street: PW-23-03 CO-01, signed October 3, 2023, shifts 400 square yards of planned repair from the ditch edge to localized subbase repair on the gate approach (release pp. 95–99).

9. What observations led the field crew to judge the approach subbase deteriorated?
10. How did Keller weigh that work against retaining the planned ditch-edge repair?
11. Who confirmed the limits of the subbase repair and how were they established?
12. What record made at the time supports the decision, and may we see it?
