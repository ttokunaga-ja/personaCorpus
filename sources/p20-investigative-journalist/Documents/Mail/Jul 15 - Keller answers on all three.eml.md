Hi Leslie,

Here is what Keller can provide on your questions.

Alder (1–4): The field crew reported that the pavement moved under load, and we prioritized the Depot Gate side on that basis. I do not have a comparison record for continuing the original allocation, the name of the person who confirmed the limits, or a daily report or acceptance record to provide with this response.

Mill (5–8): We used a partial-width cut to protect the existing outlet and its supporting edge. Leaving the cut full width around the outlet risked damage to those features. The planned 840 square yards includes the approach taper and return as well as the partial-width outlet section. The attached schedule describes the three parts together; it does not give a separate area for each part. I am not providing a completed measurement sheet with this response.

Quarry (9–12): The field crew judged the gate-approach subbase weak and gave it priority over the ditch-edge repair. I cannot identify a diagnostic record, the person who confirmed the repair limits, or the accepted extent from the material available for this response. I do not have a contemporaneous record of the decision to provide here.

Regards,
Raymond Salazar
Keller Municipal Paving Inc
