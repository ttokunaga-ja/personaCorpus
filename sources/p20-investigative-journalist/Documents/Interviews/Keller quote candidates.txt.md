Keller lines for Veronica — July 15

From Raymond Salazar's July 15 answer to the July 9 questions. These are candidates, not independent confirmation of the reasons.

Alder, questions 1–4
Direct quote: “The field crew reported that the pavement moved under load, and we prioritized the Depot Gate side on that basis.”
Leslie's note: Salazar is reporting what the crew reported. CO-02 records the reallocation, not the assessment or its confirmation. He supplied no comparison record, confirming name, daily report or acceptance record.

Mill, questions 5–8
Direct quote: “We used a partial-width cut to protect the existing outlet and its supporting edge.”
Leslie's note: This is the company's stated purpose. The public and Keller schedules give the same planned 840 square yards, including the transitions; neither establishes the completed area. Keller supplied no completed measurement sheet.

Quarry, questions 9–12
Direct quote: “The field crew judged the gate-approach subbase weak and gave it priority over the ditch-edge repair.”
Leslie's note: Keep the field crew as the actor making the judgment and setting the priority. CO-01 records the planned transfer; it does not independently establish the diagnosis or accepted extent. Salazar did not identify the diagnostic record or confirming person.
