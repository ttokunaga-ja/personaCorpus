# Theresa Coleman — interview questions
Document no.: ACN-INV-20240802-MEM-001  
Date: August 2, 2024  
Prepared by: Leslie Crawford  
Version: v0.1  
Interview: August 6, 2024, 2:00 p.m.

## Opening and consent

Confirm Theresa's name and role. On August 1 she agreed to be named as the association representative and to recording for reporting and quote checking. Ask each permission again on the recording. Keep resident names, addresses and unit numbers out of the conversation.

## How a concern reaches the association

How does a resident bring a repair concern to the association? What does the July 29 notice ask a resident to do first? Is reporting to the association voluntary? Can a resident have contacted management before the association hears about it?

If the answer is “when it was reported,” ask: reported to management or to the association? How would you know? Are these all repair requests at Parkview or only those brought to the association?

## Reading the register

When you open the association register, what does the date column record? Are entries made on the day of a conversation, or can an existing ticket be copied in later? Can that date alone establish when management first heard about the problem? Ask Theresa to describe one entry and identify the actual field before interpreting it.

## Resident copies

What does a resident's work slip show? Please read the field names before giving a date. Which field, if present, records completion? Is there a signature field and was it filled in? How would you distinguish a visit, measurement or appointment from completed work?

If she says an item is “done,” ask whether she read a completion field herself or heard that from a resident. What can be said when a slip is missing or the completion field is blank? Do not treat either as proof the work is unfinished.

## Close

Could you share paper copies of the register and any resident-provided slips with names, addresses and unit numbers removed? Can the same reporting code follow the request across the two sets without identifying a home? Confirm what is and is not available and how the copies will reach me.
