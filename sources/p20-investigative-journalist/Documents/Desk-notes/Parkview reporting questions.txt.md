July 29, 2024

Parkview — first questions

Theresa's handout tells residents to contact management first; telling the association is optional. What can a set of concerns voluntarily brought to the association actually tell us about waiting? It is a set of reports to the association, not a list of every repair request at Parkview.

Someone may have contacted management before telling the association. An association entry date is therefore not automatically the beginning of the wait. When someone says “first contact,” ask each time: contact with whom, and what record gives the date? Ask to see the field on the resident's work slip before calling any date a completion date. Do not ask for names or addresses to establish the sequence.
