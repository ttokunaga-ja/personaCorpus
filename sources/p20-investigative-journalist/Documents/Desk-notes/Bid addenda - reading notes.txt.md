Reading order for a resurfacing file — April 16

1. Bid deadline: put the deadline and any pre-deadline addenda together. An addendum issued before bids close revises the material bidders use; it is not itself a post-contract change order.
2. Execution: find the signed contract and incorporated schedules. Record its date and the segment it covers. Award is not the same event as execution.
3. Change request: find the request and its written reason, if there is one. A request is not proof of approval.
4. Decision: look for the dated council or delegated approval and the signed order. A meeting agenda or a signature without its authority and context may not settle when or why the change was approved.
5. Revised schedule: compare the revised scope or quantities with the incorporated original and the signed order. Keep the revision's date and source beside the comparison.

Keep the later approval date in its own field; keep the date work happened on site in another. Neither should be substituted for the bid deadline or the addendum date. If any step is missing, leave it open and ask for the record, not a guessed sequence.
