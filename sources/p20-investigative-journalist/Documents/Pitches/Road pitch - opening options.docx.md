# Road jobs: three possible openings

Document no.: ACN-INV-20240405-OUT-001  
Date: April 5, 2024  
Prepared by: Leslie Crawford

## 1. Lead with the biggest award

Initial first choice. Start with the largest award in the three-year set, then show where the other 17 awards stand. A ranked list would give readers a way into the scale of the borough's resurfacing work. Before writing this opening, obtain the amounts behind every notice and check that the figures measure the same thing. Do not infer a later increase from the size of an award.

## 2. Start at Alder

Initial second choice. Take the reader along Alder Road and use the endpoints to distinguish the Linden Avenue–Foundry Lane award from the Depot Gate–Linden Avenue awards. The same road name covers more than one segment and the latter appears in two award years. A map and the notices could make the distinction tangible, but a visit alone cannot tell us which year's work is visible.

## 3. Start with the approval trail

Initial third choice. First obtain executed contracts and signed records of any changes, then follow a documented decision from request and justification through review and approval. This opening depends on records we do not yet have. If a trail is incomplete, say where it stops rather than supply a reason for the decision.

## Desk note

The public amounts would be award-time information. Even a carefully checked ranking would not establish which work changed after contracting, who approved it, or why. That makes the first opening a weak basis for the story; discuss the order with Richard before settling the pitch.
