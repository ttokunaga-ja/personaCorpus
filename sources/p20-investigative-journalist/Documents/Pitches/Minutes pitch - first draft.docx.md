# How long residents wait for minutes

Document no.: ACN-INV-20250805-PIT-001  
Date: August 5, 2025  
Prepared by: Leslie Crawford  
Version: v0.1

## Question

How long does it take to get public meeting minutes from Mapleton Falls Borough, and what does that wait tell us about how the records system works?

## The story

One request, MIN-25-108, took 51 calendar days from receipt on May 19 to the July 9 dispatch of the minutes. In another, MIN-25-107, Dorothy Hensley's June 10 notice said the February 13 Finance Committee minutes were an unapproved draft held by the Clerk; neither a draft nor approved minutes accompanied it. The wait for an answer can leave a requester without the record itself.

We could follow the time it takes to get minutes, using the first response dates as our measure. The long wait in 108 suggests a public records process worth examining. I want to ask requesters and the borough what happens between a meeting and a response and whether people can find the minutes when they need them.

## Evidence and reporting still needed

Fourteen requests, MIN-25-101 through MIN-25-114, are being matched to the intake slips and dispatch record by request number. I stopped the first pass because matching the rows by position joined different requests; the 106 resubmission kept the same number. Keith and I need to verify every received and response date before using a distribution.

What would make waiting time a fair measure of the operation of the public records system? We need the rules and applicable deadlines, the borough's explanation of its handling, and the dates minutes were approved and actually available. A response may be a status notice rather than the minutes. We do not yet have the evidence to decide whether any delay was unlawful or to evaluate the whole system.
