# Parkview: opening options

ACN-INV-20240813-OUT-001 | v0.1 | August 13, 2024
Leslie Crawford

## 1. The register line

Begin with one entry in the association's register, then explain how a request reaches that book. The line gives us an association record, not the resident's first contact with management or a completed repair. It sets the boundary well, but on its own does not show what we can verify after intake.

## 2. The resident's slip — preferred

Begin with the completion field on a resident's work slip and explain what a date there can and cannot tell us. Then move back to the association register and the 22 requests Theresa discussed. Fourteen have dated completion fields on signed slips; for eight the available papers do not establish a completion date. Show the reader the field and the missing link instead of treating every date as the same event. Protect identifying details.

## 3. An elapsed-time calculation — set aside

An interval from association entry to completion would be measurable for the 14, but it does not necessarily begin when management first heard from the resident. We do not have consistent first-contact dates. A calculation at the top would suggest a management response time we cannot establish. Seek first-contact evidence before considering such a measure; do not generalize these 22 association requests to the building.
