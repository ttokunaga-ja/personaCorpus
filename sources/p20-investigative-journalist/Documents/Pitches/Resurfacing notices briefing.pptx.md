## Slide 2 — Heading and body

Which road jobs were allowed to change, and on what grounds?

The archive identifies 18 awards, six in each of FY2021, FY2022 and FY2023. Award year is the organizing year here. All 18 list Keller Municipal Paving Inc as the awardee. These are starting points for reporting, not a count of changed contracts.

Speaker notes: Richard and I agreed on this question April 9. A ranking of initial awards cannot answer it. We don't yet know which contracts changed.
