# Road resurfacing: where should we begin?

Document no.: ACN-INV-20240408-PIT-001  
Version: v0.2  
Date: April 8, 2024  
Prepared by: Leslie Crawford  
To: Richard Espinoza, Editor in Chief

## The reporting question

Which road jobs were allowed to change, and on what grounds? What can three years of Mapleton Falls resurfacing awards tell us about the borough's road work, and what would we need to learn if a contract amount later changed? I would begin with the 18 awards in the borough archive, six in each of FY 2021, FY 2022 and FY 2023, and follow the records rather than assume a change was improper.

## What is public

The archive gives an award reference, award year, road and bid endpoints for each entry, and names Keller Municipal Paving Inc as the awardee. It is a starting list, not a record of what happened after execution. Road name alone is not a safe key: Alder's Depot Gate–Linden Avenue segment appears in both FY 2022 and FY 2023, while the FY 2021 Alder segment runs from Linden Avenue to Foundry Lane.

## What we need

If an amount or scope moved after award, we need the executed agreement and its schedules, the signed change order, the request and written justification, technical review, the council or delegated approval record, and the revised scope or quantity schedule. Each record has a different job: an award notice identifies the starting procurement; a signed order and its supporting papers may show the decision and grounds. We cannot yet say which of the 18 changed or why.

## Two ways to organize the story

**Award-value comparison.** Assemble and verify a comparable list of the 18 award amounts, then rank it. This could show the size of the awards but would leave post-contract decisions unanswered. The archive page I have indexed supplies the identifying information; the amounts still need checking against the individual notices before any ranking is published.

**Road-by-road trail.** Use the reference, award year and endpoints to follow each job from the original award into any requested and approved change. Alder offers an accessible example of why the identifiers matter, but we should not select a conclusion before seeing the records.

## Decision for Tuesday

Richard, which approach should be the center of the reporting: the award comparison or the road-by-road trail? I can keep the other as context. I will bring the archive index and the opening options; we should also decide what to do if records for only part of the 18 can be obtained.
