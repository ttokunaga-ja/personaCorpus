# Parkview repair records

ACN-INV-20240815-PIT-001 | v1.0 | August 15, 2024
To: Richard Espinoza
From: Leslie Crawford

## The question

For repair requests received by the association, how much of the interval can the records trace?

I propose a reported piece following only the 22 requests that reached the Parkview Court Tenants Association and that its representative Theresa Coleman discussed in our on-the-record interview on August 6. Reporting to the association is voluntary. These 22 are not a count of all requests or an account of conditions in every household.

## What the records show

The association register gives its own dates and descriptions. Resident work slips supply another set of fields: 14 signed slips contain completion dates. For the other eight, the copies now in hand do not establish a completion date: two slips have a blank completion field and six are absent. A measurement, an appointment and a register transcription each have dates, but none is a completion date. Nor does a missing date establish that work was not done.

I would open on the completion field of a resident slip, then show the reader how far we can work back through the association register. Coleman consented to named quotation as the association's representative. The copies shared for review exclude resident identifiers; the reporting should keep them out.

| | Position |
|---|---|
| What we can write | In these 22 association requests, completion dates are documented on 14 signed resident slips. The register records what reached the association. |
| Hold | Eight completion dates remain unverified, including two blank fields and six missing slips. The association date cannot stand in for the first contact with management; no management response-time calculation is supported. |
| Next inquiries | Seek first-contact acknowledgments or residents' own initial communications, and separately seek resident slips with completion fields. Theresa says the association cannot provide first-contact copies as a set; residents who have them would need to agree to a search. |

## Request

Please approve continued reporting on these records and the missing first-contact and resident-copy evidence. I am not asking for a publication slot yet. We can return with the new records and a narrower account of any interval they actually support.
