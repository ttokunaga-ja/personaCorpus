# Start with the two envelopes?

Document no.: ACN-INV-20250811-OUT-001  
Date: August 11, 2025  
Prepared by: Leslie Crawford

## Opening to try

Two requests sit either side of the middle of the list. On the 25th day, the Finance Committee answer says the Clerk holds an unapproved draft. On the 27th, the work-session notice says the location question has gone to the Clerk. I wanted to put the two notices side by side and use them to compare how long it took to get the minutes.

## Where it breaks

Neither notice supplied the minutes. The two envelopes can introduce what the office said, but they cannot carry a conclusion about when a reader could obtain the records. The 26-day median is the middle of first substantive response times for these 14 requests; it is not a median time to obtain minutes.

## Reporting order

First find the Finance approval record and when an approved copy became available. For the work session, ask the Clerk for the index or other record showing where minutes or a draft were kept. Separate evidence of first public access from a current listing. If we can establish access dates, revisit the opening; otherwise keep the intake-to-response figures in a small box explaining the office response, with the two notice types identified.
