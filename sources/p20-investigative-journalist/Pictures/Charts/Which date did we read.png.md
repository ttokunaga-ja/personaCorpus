# Which date did we read?

Desk comparison: four contracts. Dates identify different records and observations; they are not a timeline of authorization and construction.

| Contract and fiscal year | Earlier date and source | Later date and source field |
|---|---|---|
| PW-22-01 Alder FY22 | Jul 11, 2022 — Wheeler later read a gate copy; copy not obtained | Jul 14, 2022 — Register received, change file |
| PW-22-02 Mill FY22 | Aug 8, 2022 — Wheeler saw road markings; underlying paper not obtained | Aug 11, 2022 — Routed review received, technical review packet |
| PW-23-01 Alder FY23 | Sep 7, 2023 — Wheeler saw materials truck; gate entry | Sep 12, 2023 — Order signed, CO-02 |
| PW-23-03 Quarry FY23 | Sep 28, 2023 — Wheeler saw barricades; traffic preparation sheet | Oct 3, 2023 — Order signed, CO-01 |

Diagram: four horizontal rows, one for each contract, earlier source on the left and the later field on the right. An arrow between the dates indicates chronological comparison only. Legend: observation or later reading; register or routed receipt; signed order. No line represents a construction start or approval. The dates are calendar dates, not quantities on a numerical axis.
