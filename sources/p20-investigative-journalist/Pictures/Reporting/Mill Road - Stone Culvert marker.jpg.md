A May afternoon view from the sidewalk beside Mill Road. A small marker beside low stone masonry reads “STONE CULVERT”. The marker is in the foreground, with the road surface continuing away along Mill Road toward Pump House Lane in the same frame. Stone Culvert is the starting endpoint of the Stone Culvert–Pump House Lane stretch in the FY2022 and FY2023 notices. Pump House Lane is beyond the readable view; no distant street sign can be read. The visible pavement and marker do not establish which year's work was done here.
