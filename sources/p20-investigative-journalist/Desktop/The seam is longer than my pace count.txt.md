June 13

I walked the seam at Mill yesterday and thought the pace count might give us an area. Strike that. The width changes along the outlet and I cannot see how the edge turns back at the end. A length guessed from steps, multiplied by an invented width, would not measure the cut.

For the table: release p.63 says 840 square yards PLANNED. Completed measurement sheet not yet seen. Leave the completed-area field empty; ask where the measurement record is. The photograph is a location reference, not a quantity record.
