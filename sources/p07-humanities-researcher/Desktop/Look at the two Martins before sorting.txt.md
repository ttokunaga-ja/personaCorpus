16 April — before Stefan arrives

Open 049r l. 8 first: Claire Martin, rue du Four. Then 051r l. 4: Claire Martin, rue des Tanneurs. Read the details on the sheets before sorting the index.

On screen, try each of the two identical spellings and follow its leaf and line back to the right entry. Do the same with the paper copies on the table. Check whether there is an account reference connecting the addresses; I have not found one. Do not merge the lines simply to make the sort look tidy.
