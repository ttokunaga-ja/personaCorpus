25 April — before Roger

1. If the societies set different subscription amounts, what is the common measure? A nominal amount alone may conceal a different collection period or an exception. Can I compare the terms first, then ask what evidence exists for payment and use?

2. A name and address on a circulation list tells me where something was sent. It does not tell me who came to the room or a meeting. Which record would let me make that distinction, and what should remain unknown if it has not survived?

I keep trying to establish the publication route first and let the rest follow from it. Stop there: the route may be a separate question. Ask Roger whether the book needs to begin with the operation of the societies instead.
