8 January

Box 12: the inventory puts 043–054 in CR/12/B, perception et comptes. When the copies arrive, keep the date on which money was received separate from any term named in the description. The year on a payment need not be the year of the term it settles.

For anything paid across the turn of the year, first put it in order by receipt date; then read the words describing the old term and leave those words alongside it. Do not silently move the receipt back into December just because the account refers to December. Check each leaf and line before making a count.
