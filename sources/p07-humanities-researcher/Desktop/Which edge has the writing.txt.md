The old pencil note says only “inside” twice. That is not enough for Camille to know which edge I need.

CR/14/A 153r: the ends of the lines run towards the binding. Ask for the binding edge and the ends of the lines together.

CR/14/B 208r: the writing is on the recto at the folded-over side. Ask to see the fold and the handover field beneath it, as far as the leaf will open safely.

Give the cote, leaf and face in each request, and name the edge. Do not ask her to flatten either leaf.
