15 June

Les Veillées du jeudi: I had placed the two entries next to one another as if they described the same copy. MRC/02 f.18r l.8, 12 May 1865, gives only “B.” for the destination. MRC/03 f.9v l.3 records receipt at Dépôt de Rochemont on 19 May. There is a week between them and the title is the same, but I cannot find a copy number or a sender in the latter entry that would join it to the first.

Leave the Rochemont receipt on its own. Do not draw a line from B. to it on the strength of the title and interval. Check the receiving record if Isabelle can find it.
