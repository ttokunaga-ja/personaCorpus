13 November — preparing Monday

Note 13 begins with 185r. If I take the first folio in the note as the evidence for the borrowing condition, I cannot check where permission to read in the room ends and the condition on taking a book away begins. A note can gather several leaves for different parts of the paragraph.

Ask Florian: which sentence in the chapter are we trying to support? Find that sentence first. Then open the leaves and choose the face and body lines that actually contain its condition, including the neighbouring lines needed to distinguish consultation in the room from removal. Do not just copy the first folio in a multiple-leaf note.
