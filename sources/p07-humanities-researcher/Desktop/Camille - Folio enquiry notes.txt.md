15 July — before writing to Camille

Start with the two references in the surviving ranges.

CR/13/B f.126 (old 60). Ask for both faces. Is there a date at the end of one side that runs into the beginning of the other? Look for the change of attendant and any entry showing the shift actually took place. Take down what the heading says about who could come in.

CR/14/B f.217 (old 63). Ask where to look for a signed decision apart from the text of a circular. Is there an attachment or a note pointing to one? I need the date of the vote and the last day the decision applied, so keep the back of the sheet in the request.

Put the seven missing candidates on a separate list by box. Ask Camille about old shelfmarks or references to a contemporary duplicate or counterpart. Leave requests for copies of the missing leaves off the email. First find out what could be consulted and how long it might take.
