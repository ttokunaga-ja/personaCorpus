Putting the sums on one scale
18 March

Japanese monthly rule: 5 sen × 12 = 60 sen per year.
York quarterly rule: 6d × 4 = 24d per year.

Both are annualised amounts under the stated rules, assuming payment at the stated rate for the whole year. They are not actual annual receipts.

I thought of using the resulting amounts to put ease of participation in order. Stop there. The instalments fall at different times, I do not have comparable information on reductions or exemptions, and I cannot put either sum against a comparable household budget. Annualising makes the intervals alike on paper; it does not establish the burden or a ranking of access. Keep the amounts with their original periods and discuss the guest and borrowing conditions separately.
