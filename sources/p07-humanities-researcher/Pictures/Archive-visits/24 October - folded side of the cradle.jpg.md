## Photograph

A view from obliquely above the grey book cradle immediately before CR/14/B 208r is photographed. The support is open at a modest angle; the folded-over side of the leaf and the narrow shadow running along its fold can be seen. A small scale lies alongside the support without touching the original paper. The far right side of the cradle is unloaded and visible behind the leaf. The camera is far enough away that the document's writing cannot be followed; no legible text is required in the image. No hand presses the binding.
