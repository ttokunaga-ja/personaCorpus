MRC/04, Bureau des dépôts, open at f.2v–3r on two angled supports on the Montclair reading table. Judith is on the writer’s side, with the bottom edges nearest her and the gutter running away from her. The camera looks down at an angle wide enough to show the entire width of both leaves and both slopes of the supports. The left leaf is the intake leaf and the right is the handover leaf. Both sets of rules stop at the gutter. No line is drawn through the fold.

## Structure of the opening

- Left, f.2v: **four** columns in this order — intake date, person bringing the book in, title, control number. Three interior vertical dividers. The title is the broad field, and the narrow control-number field is immediately beside the gutter.
- Right, f.3r: **three** columns in this order — control number, handover date, recipient. Two interior vertical dividers. The narrow control-number field is immediately beside the gutter, and the recipient field extends to the right-hand table boundary. Beyond that boundary is unruled paper.
- An outer rule bounds each table. Each interior divider is a single rule. The two narrow fields facing across the gutter are the two control-number fields. There are no additional narrow columns or vertical dividers on either leaf.
