A close photograph of the closed MRC/01 on the Montclair reading table, taken after Isabelle gave permission. The fore-edge is nearest the camera and runs diagonally across the frame. The low oblique angle also gives a view along the nearer end of the spine. The brown cover is closed over the uneven stack of leaves. Two separate wedges rest on the table and cradle the book at the joint, with both inner slopes visible below the spine. No hands are in the frame.

A lighter repair strip runs along the spine. At the exposed end of the binding, small ends of linen thread emerge from the original sewing holes beside the strip. Both the edge of the repair and the row of holes are in view, so the two features can be distinguished. There is no legible text in the frame.
