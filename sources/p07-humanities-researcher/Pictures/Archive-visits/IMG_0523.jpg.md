Judith’s close view of the upper part of MRC/08, Accusés de réception, as she looked at it with Isabelle. The book rests open on its support without the leaf being folded back. The left leaf, f.2v, is upright in the photograph. Its free outer edge is on the **left** and the binding is on the **right**. A little of the facing leaf beyond the gutter shows how the book is open.

## Position of the entry and the edge

Across f.2v from the left, show the free paper edge, the wide unruled outer margin, the first vertical rule and the table. The three table columns run left to right as date, item, recipient. The line for 21 March has ink in the date and item cells and an empty recipient cell. At the same height, to the **left of the first vertical rule**, there is a short, clearly visible handwritten addition. It belongs to that first row. A small loss interrupts the free paper edge on the **left**. It lies entirely outside the ruling, leaves the addition intact and does not reach any of the cells. Keep the loss, the addition and the empty recipient cell visible in one frame.

## Inscription on f.2v

| Date | Objet | Reçu par |
|---|---|---|
| 21 mars 1864 | Lectures du soir | |
| 25 mars 1864 | Le jardin des lectures | Martin |

The addition in the left margin beside 21 March reads « reçu au dépôt ». The line of ink is visible in the photograph. The consultation copy shows the words and the entries at a readable scale. There are no added labels or captions in the photograph.
