# Les Veillées du jeudi: where the trail stops

A chronological working diagram, not a map. Three dated entries are arranged from left to right; no distance or geographical position is implied.

5 May 1865 — MRC/02 f.18r l.7: Valperche ───→ Salle de Brévaux. Label the solid arrow “handed over; origin stated”.

12 May 1865 — MRC/02 f.18r l.8: “B.”. Place this in its own box with a short line ending at the box and the label “destination unresolved”. Do not join it to the 5 May handover.

19 May 1865 — MRC/03 f.9v l.3: “Dépôt de Rochemont — receipt”. Put this in a separate box without a connecting line. Label it “independent entry: copy number and sender not established”.

Legend: solid arrow = handover with stated origin; stopped line = unresolved destination; separate box = independent entry, connection unproved.
