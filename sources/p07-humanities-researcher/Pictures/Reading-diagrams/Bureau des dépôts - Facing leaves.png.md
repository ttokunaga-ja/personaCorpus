## Text on the diagram

**Bureau des dépôts — MRC/04, ff. 2v–3r**

**f. 2v · Intake at the bureau**

| Reçu le | Reçu de | Ouvrage | N° de contrôle |
|---|---|---|---|
| 16 février 1864 | Martin | Le foyer du dimanche | 47 |

**f. 3r · Handover entered by the bureau**

| N° de contrôle | Remis le | Remis à |
|---|---|---|
| 47 | 18 février 1864 | Salle de Brévaux |

**Writer’s seat** (nearest the foot of the open register)

**→ Order of entry in this register**

Susan — the clerk carried 47 across the fold. It links the intake of 16 February with the entry for handover on 18 February. « Reçu de » places Martin at the intake. To check reception at the Salle de Brévaux, we still need a receipt from the room.

Source: consultation copy MRA-20240807-COP-003, p. 2, and opening photograph IMG_0519.

## Placement and arrows

Show the opening from the writer’s seat, with the lower edges nearest the seat label and the gutter running away from it. Place f. 2v on the left and f. 3r on the right. Use the photograph for the position of the gutter and the breaks in the rules. Use the copy for the headings and entries in the same positions.

Give the left table four columns with three single internal dividers. The broad « Ouvrage » field comes before the narrow number field at the gutter. Give the right table three columns with two single internal dividers. Its narrow number field faces the left number field across the gutter, and « Remis à » ends at the right boundary of the table. The paper beyond that boundary is unruled. Bound each table separately and leave the fold clear of table rules.

Run the arrows above the entry row so all the writing stays legible. The first runs from « Reçu de » to the left cell containing 47. The second crosses the gutter from that cell to the right cell containing 47. The last leads from the right 47 past « Remis le » to « Remis à ». Point the arrows at the fields they connect. The path runs through the clerk’s entries on the two leaves.
