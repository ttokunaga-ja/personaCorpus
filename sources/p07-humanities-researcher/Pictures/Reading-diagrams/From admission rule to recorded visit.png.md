# From admission rule to recorded visit

Diagram for discussion with Susan. Three parallel columns, with no arrows implying that one event causes the next.

Left column: **Rules**. Text: “Amount and interval; entitlement to enter. A stated condition is not evidence of payment.”

Centre column: **Collection receipts**. Text: “Date, amount and name as recorded. A single name may represent a combined payment.”

Right column: **Visit or use records**. Text: “Date, place and recorded unit: visit, person or request. Payment does not establish a visit.”

Between the first and second columns, a separate box labelled **Date · name · place** with short lines to each column; between the second and third, another box with the same words and short lines to each column. Caption under both boxes: “Clues for checking a possible match, not proof of identity or attendance.”

Below the three columns, a box labelled **Remission letters**. Text: “Who authorised the exception? For whom and for what period? Which condition or practice does it qualify?” Dashed lines lead from this box to each of the three columns. Label on these lines: “may alter interpretation”.
