Dear Roger,

Julie and I have checked the 32 transcriptions against the copies and corrected the English at seven passages. Two of those corrections change the account of conditions in Chapter 2, so I have replaced the two provisional paragraphs and attached the revised note, together with the seven passages in French and English.

Could you look particularly at CR/13/A f. 085r, ll. 3–5? The invitation allows one companion per member; the heating gift is voluntary and the next sentence welcomes those who give nothing. I had treated it as a charge for the guest's admission.

At CR/14/A f. 186r, ll. 7–9, read with the preceding sentences, the arrears are to be collected before a book leaves the room. The member may still read on the premises. My earlier paragraph had put payment before entry. The revised note keeps those two conditions distinct and leaves the question of actual visits open until we have independent evidence.

Best,
Judith
