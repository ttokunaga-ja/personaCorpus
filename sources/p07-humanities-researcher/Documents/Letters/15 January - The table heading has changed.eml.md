Dear Roger,

Julie and I read the box 12 receipts against the working Table 2.1 yesterday. I have attached the corrected table and note (LHT-20260112-EVD-001, v0.3). The old heading, “Participants”, suggested a count of people; I have changed it to “Payment entries”.

The four receipt-date quarters still give 42, 49, 45 and 48, a total of 184 entries. Bernard's instalment and later balance are two receipts on different dates; Roux's single handover for three subscriptions is one entry. The table therefore counts recorded receipts, not individuals. In particular, the January 1864 receipt of a December balance belongs in the January–March receipt quarter.

May I use the revised heading and note in our internal working chapter, and add this sentence to the discussion: “The number of payment entries is not a denominator for an attendance rate, since the accounts do not establish how many people came to the room.” I would like the next draft to explain to the reader what the four periods count and why the total cannot bear a claim about visits.

This is for the working draft; I am not asking to replace the chapter version sent to Edward in February.

Best,
Judith
