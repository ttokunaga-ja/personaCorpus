Dear Judith,

Here is the table with my comments at the heading and the note. Bernard's two instalments are two receipts; Roux's single handover for three subscriptions is one. So 184 is the total number of times payments were entered as received. It is not even a count of the people who handed over the money, let alone a count of participants.

As we agreed this morning, I would use “Payment entries”. Could you also put a sentence in the text saying that the denominator for receipts is different from the one needed for attendance? The accounts do not tell us how many people came into the room.

Best,
Julie
