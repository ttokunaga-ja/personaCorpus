8 January 2025 — paper packet from Camille Laurent, York

Twelve additional leaves for consultation. This is the order in which they came out of the envelope, front to back:

Box 12
  CR/12/A 014–015
  CR/12/B 041–042
Box 13
  CR/13/A 089–090
  CR/13/B 124–125
Box 14
  CR/14/A 176–177
  CR/14/B 205–206

Six pairs, two leaves each. Keep the two sides of a leaf under the same reference when I turn it over at the table. Leave each archive slip with its pair as I work through the boxes.

To put them away, start with 205–206 at the bottom and lay on 176–177, 124–125, 089–090, 041–042 and finally 014–015. Keep the smaller leaf number on top within each pair. Slide the stack back into Camille's January envelope so that 014–015 comes out first.

July's ten leaves and September's thirty-two stay in their own packets. Keep this envelope with the reading copies. Any reproduction for publication needs a separate permission from the archive.
