# Three mornings at Saint-Aubin

22–24 July 2024. One box at a time; catalogue references below are Camille’s inventories, not readings of the missing leaves.

## Monday — box 12: whose name?

CR/12/A, 012–013: the notice sets a quarterly subscription of 1 franc 50 centimes and a first due date of 15 February 1861. It allows a member to convey the sum for another; the treasurer keeps a receipt counterfoil and gives the secretary an account. The notice is a rule and instruction, not evidence that each person attended.

CR/12/B, 040: the treasurer describes a remittance for three members. He asks which of the two records should retain the bearer’s name and each person’s share. His account of the earlier practice says that, when a duty attendant conveyed money, the total was entered under that attendant’s name. The letter does not establish whose name was used for this particular remittance. A single receipt cannot be counted as one individual payer without further checking.

ASR-20240722-INV-001 places the gaps at A 018–020 and B 061–063. These are catalogue positions, not blank entries in a surviving account.

## Tuesday — box 13: invited or present?

CR/13/A, 087–088 invites each member to bring one person to the reading and instructs the attendant to list companions separately from members. An invitation and an instruction to record an arrival do not themselves establish who came. CR/13/B, 123 makes a different distinction: two successive requests by the same person were two lines but one visit. One other name is difficult to read and was to be checked separately; do not identify it by resemblance.

ASR-20240723-INV-001 locates missing leaves 094–098 in A. It notes that another series is needed to check a visit against an addressee. Photograph of the table arrangement taken with Camille’s permission, 10:18 local time; no document text in frame.

## Wednesday — box 14: who allowed the exception?

CR/14/A, 179–180: the committee granted a delay in collection until the end of February; the secretary told the attendant not to stop reading in the room, while requests to take a book away were to be referred separately. Record the committee as the authorising body and the secretary as the writer of the instruction. This one case does not establish a general rule.

CR/14/B, 198–199: the committee’s October trial extended Thursday opening from seven to eight, with a handover and lighting assigned to the later attendant. The journal was to distinguish the extended hour. The unchanged subscription conditions and an extra hour describe an opportunity, not an observed increase in attendance.

ASR-20240724-INV-001 locates A 165–168 and B 212–215 as missing. Use the three inventories for the empty leaf numbers; no text can be read from the gaps. The ten leaves requested as paper copies are listed separately in the return envelope. Compare payment, invitation, actual use and exceptions as distinct evidence before writing a conclusion about participation.
