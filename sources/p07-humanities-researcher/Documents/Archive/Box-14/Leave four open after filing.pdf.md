## What has been filed

Camille examined the twelve existing leaves requested from carton 14 and supplied eight faces for research consultation on 24 October. The index identifies each face, its file and page; the remaining four leaves are recorded separately as readings still open.

| Status | Leaves | Count |
|---|---|---:|
| Enquired about | CR/14/A 153, 154, 157, 158, 161, 164; CR/14/B 193, 194, 208, 209, 211, 216 | 12 |
| Re-copied for consultation | A 153r, 154r, 157v, 158r; B 193r, 194r, 208r, 209r | 8 |
| Transcription on hold | A 161r, 164v; B 211r, 216r | 4 |

The twelve are individual leaves, each counted once. A face in the index is a means of finding the relevant image, not an additional leaf. The eight are in four two-page PDFs. In particular, 157v is page 1 of the receipt-endorsement copy and 194r is page 2 of the Wednesday notice copy.

## Keep the sets apart

This enquiry is separate from the six items comprising ten leaves received in July 2024. It is also separate from the nineteen missing leaves in the inventories of boxes 12–14. In box 14 the missing positions are A 165–168 and B 212–215. The four held leaves above are present; their relevant writing could not be established within the permitted opening, so they are not catalogue gaps.

## Starting again and permission

For a future visit, start with *Feuillets laissés en attente* beside *Index des huit reprises*. A 161r needs the line ends at the binding; A 164v needs the writing distinguished from the backing; B 211r needs the overlapping margins distinguished; B 216r needs the inaccessible inner edge. Ask the archive what can safely be seen before proposing another view. Do not supply a transcription from the present evidence.

The eight copies are for research consultation. Publication or reproduction requires a separate request to the archive; receiving a reading copy is not permission to publish it.
