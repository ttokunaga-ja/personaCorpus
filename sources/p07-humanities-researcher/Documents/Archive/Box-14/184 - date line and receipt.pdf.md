# 184: date line and receipt

LHT-20260708-PAP-001 | 8 July 2026 | Judith Morris | For Julie Perrin | v1.0

CR/14/A f.184r–v; LA-042. Reading shared after our independent examination on 8 July.

## CR/14/A f.184r, date line

| French | English |
|---|---|
| jeudi 16 mai 1867 | Thursday 16 May 1867 |

The final figure has a sloping finishing stroke without a return, matching the 7 in the same hand's « 17 » in line 4. The old reading was 1864. As an independent check, 16 May was a Thursday in 1867, but a Monday in 1864.

## CR/14/A f.184r, body lines 2–4

| French | English |
|---|---|
| Le comité vous autorise, pour cette fois, à laisser sortir un livre. | The committee authorises you, for this occasion, to let one book be taken out. |
| Veuillez en demander le retour jeudi prochain ; | Please ask for it to be returned next Thursday; |
| laissez au trésorier le compte impayé, relevé 17. | leave the outstanding account with the treasurer, account statement 17. |

The secretary writes to the attendant. The permission concerns one book and this occasion; the account remains with the treasurer. The request for a return is not itself evidence that the book was returned.

## CR/14/A f.184v, receipt

| French | English |
|---|---|
| Reçu le 17 mai 1867. | Received on 17 May 1867. |

This endorsement is in another hand. Its 17 May date is the date of receipt, not the date of the recto communication on 16 May and not the requested return date. The two sides should be cited separately when their dates are used.
