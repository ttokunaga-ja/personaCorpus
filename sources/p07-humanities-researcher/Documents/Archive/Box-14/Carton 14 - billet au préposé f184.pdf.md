# CR/14/A f.184r

Date line: jeudi 16 mai 1867

Body, line 1: Monsieur le préposé,

Body, line 2: Le comité vous autorise, pour cette fois, à laisser sortir un livre.

Body, line 3: Veuillez en demander le retour jeudi prochain ;

Body, line 4: laissez au trésorier le compte impayé, relevé 17.

Le secrétaire de la Société de lecture de Saint-Aubin-sur-Rive

# CR/14/A f.184v

In another hand: Reçu le 17 mai 1867.

## Paper and writing

The recto is a short handwritten note with its date above the body and open margins around it. The last figure of the year is somewhat faint, with a descending rightward finishing stroke. The 7 in the same hand's “17” on body line 4 has the same sloping finish. The verso contains the short receipt in a different hand and retains the surrounding blank paper. The consultation copy shows the full recto followed by the full verso, including dates, writing and margins.
