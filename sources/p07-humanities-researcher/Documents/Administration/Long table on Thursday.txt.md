Thursday 27 March — long table

Susan and I have swapped the morning slot. She needs to spread the boxes from 10.00, so I must return her two borrowed weights before then and clear my papers from the table.

I'll carry on with my collation at my desk in the afternoon. Keep the references with the notes rather than leaving loose sheets on the table.
