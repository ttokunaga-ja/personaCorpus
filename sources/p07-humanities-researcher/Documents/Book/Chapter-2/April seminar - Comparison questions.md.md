# After the reader: a question for April

4 March 2025

Opened the 26 February revision of *Dues and participation* again, §§2–4. The reader's questions have helped me separate an amount asked for from a sum received. For April I need a second separation: what is paid is one question; who may do what is another. A rule about subscriptions does not by itself tell me who came into a room or took a book away.

Keep the French passages at hand as checks on the terms, not as extra cases to be fitted into a general sequence. At CR/13/A 085r the companion is introduced and the offering for heating is voluntary; the next sentence welcomes those who give nothing. At CR/14/A 186r consultation in the room is permitted, while arrears are to be received before a book is taken away. Neither passage licenses moving a payment condition from one act to another.

For the comparison with Abe, start with one Japanese and one British example. Ask which document states a condition, who is addressed and what act it covers; then ask separately what any surviving account or attendance record can show was done. Do not make a count of people out of a notice or an instruction. The French examples can help me notice a misreading without becoming evidence for a connection between the two cases.
