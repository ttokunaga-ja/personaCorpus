§2の第2段落を次に差し替える。

On 29 January 1866 the secretary communicated to the treasurer the committee's decision to defer a demand for an outstanding subscription. The instruction at CR/14/A f.173r withholds the demand until 12 February and directs the treasurer to resume ordinary reminders after the 12th. It describes the timing of collection for that account; it neither cancels the debt nor establishes the member's use of the room. [1]

§4の第2段落を次に差し替える。

On 19 March 1866 the secretary instructed the attendant that a member in arrears could consult books in the room, but that the arrears were to be collected before a book was taken away (CR/14/A f.186r). On 16 May 1867 the secretary conveyed an individual permission to take one book out and return it the following Thursday, leaving the outstanding account with the treasurer (f.184r, LA-042). These are instructions for distinct occasions; their dates do not establish a change of general rule or a causal connection between them. [2, 3]

注2を次に差し替える。注1と注3はそのまま残す。

2. LA-042, CR/14/A f.184r, secretary to attendant, 16 May 1867: permission for one book to be taken out and returned the following Thursday, with the outstanding account left to the treasurer. The former reading of 1864 is superseded: the final figure agrees with the 7 in the recto's line 4, and 16 May 1867 was a Thursday. The separate hand on f.184v records receipt on 17 May 1867, not the date of the instruction or the book's return.
