# Saturday invitation: a possible condition of entry

LHT-20240926-PAP-001 | 26 September 2024 | Judith Morris | Working proof for Chapter 2

## An invitation and an offer

The secretary's invitation of 28 October 1862 appears to place two actions together: each member may bring one companion, and members and guests are invited to make an offering for heating. In the portion I have selected, the movement from the permitted companion to the offering could be read as part of a single procedure for entry. On that reading the invitation does more than identify whom a member may bring: it associates the guest's admission with money for the room.¹

This is a tentative reading of the selected lines, not evidence that any named person attended or paid. Even if the passage establishes a condition, a separate record of attendance would be needed to connect that condition to a visit. The July copies already require us to keep invitation, collection and use apart.

## Source note
