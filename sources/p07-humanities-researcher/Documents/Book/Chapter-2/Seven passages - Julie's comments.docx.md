## 変更履歴

以下はすべてコメント、Julie Perrin、22 October 2024 14:21 BST。対象は各対訳表の英訳中の語句。

- 対象「quarterly donation」：The term has already passed and the secretary asks the member to regularise the account before directing payment to the treasurer. This looks like a reminder for the quarterly subscription, not a donation; the lamp offering is accounted for separately.
- 対象「subscription instalment for coal」：The envelope contains an offering for coal. The next line tells the attendant to enter subscriptions separately. The attendant is asked to hand over the envelope; that does not establish that the money is the attendant's own subscription.
- 対象「admission charge for heating」：The French calls this an offrande libre, and the next sentence welcomes those who give nothing. Does that next sentence, read with the one-companion sentence before it, leave any payment condition on the guest's admission?
- 対象「waiver of dues until 12 February」：The direction says not to ask before 12 February and to resume ordinary reminders after the 12th. Is this a postponement of collection rather than cancellation of the debt? There is no amount or condition of use here.
- 対象「subscription instalment for the stove」：The member has not yet settled the subscription and explicitly asks that this franc not be entered against it. The following sentence says he intends to bring it on Friday: it is a separate offer, not evidence that it was paid.
- 対象「Before admitting him, take from him the charge at the door」：The preceding sentences allow him to consult books in the room and then introduce the case of a book taken away. Does reading those sentences with “Avant de laisser sortir le livre” change the condition from admission to taking the book out? The next line sends the sum to the treasurer.
- 対象「rebate of dues」：The attendant asks the treasurer to return the unused 75 centimes so that they can go back to the person who gave them for the lamp; the last sentence excludes subscriptions. Is this a return of the balance of the gift rather than a rebate of dues?
