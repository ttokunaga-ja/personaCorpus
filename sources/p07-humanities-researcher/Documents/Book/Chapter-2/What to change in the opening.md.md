# What to change in the opening

24 February — work from the copy I sent Edward on the 12th.

**R01 — §1, first paragraph.** Put the scope before the three examples. I am comparing the conditions the documents record for paying dues, receiving an invitation, reading in the room and taking a book away. Keep the secretary, attendant and treasurer in the paragraph to show why each document was written. Take out the suggestion at the end that the comparison will recover who actually used the room.

**R02 — §2, before the letter of 4 March.** Start by separating a notice of what is due from a record of money received. Keep CR/12/A f. 023r as the first example of a demand. The secretary asks for one franc fifty centimes for the quarter (ll. 4–5), keeps the lamp offering separate (l. 6) and promises a receipt (l. 7). Count the letter as a reminder. For a claim that this member paid, I would need the corresponding receipt or a completed account entry.

**R06 — §1, note 1.** Add the figures after the three inventory references, before the reference to the July selection. The inventories for boxes 12–14 give 235 numbered positions, with 216 leaves available for copying and 19 missing. Leave the actual copy selections in the following note. The catalogue gives the positions of the missing leaves. Their contents are unknown, so I cannot use a gap there as evidence that a practice went unrecorded.
