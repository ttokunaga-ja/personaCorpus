## Four editing instructions

1. **Note 4 — §2, opening paragraph, from the reminder of 4 March 1861 through the sentence on evidence of payment.** Use CR/12/A f. 023r, ll. 4–6 for the quarterly subscription of one franc fifty and the separate lamp offering. Retain the distinction between the demand and a receipt or completed account entry. Do not turn the reminder or the promised receipt into proof that this member paid.

2. **Note 9 — §3, opening paragraph, from the companion allowance through the welcome to those who give nothing.** Use CR/13/A f. 085r, ll. 3–5: one companion per member, a voluntary heating gift, and the explicit welcome to non-givers. Keep all three together. Do not describe the gift as a charge for entry or infer how many people attended.

3. **Note 12 — §3, the passage on the balance of the Thursday lamp gift and its return.** Use CR/14/B f. 202r, ll. 3–5 for the request to return the unused 75 centimes to the attendant for the original giver and the instruction that the balance is not for subscriptions. Keep the direction of the proposed return clear. Do not describe it as a rebate of dues or as proof the money reached the giver.

4. **Note 13 — §4, opening paragraph, from permission to consult on the premises through the instruction concerning removal.** Use CR/14/A f. 186r, ll. 3–9. Preserve the permission to read in the room at ll. 3–4; the request to take a book away and settlement come at ll. 5–6, followed by collection before release and handover to the treasurer at ll. 7–9. Describe payment of arrears as a condition of removal in this case, not of reading inside. Do not infer that a loan or payment occurred.

When incorporating these passages, Florian and I will compare the wording and each destination note against the opened leaves once more. The submitted v1.1 remains separately preserved.
