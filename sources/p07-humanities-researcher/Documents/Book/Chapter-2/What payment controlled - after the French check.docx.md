元の題を「What payment controlled — after the French check」に、管理欄を以下に替える。

LHT-20241016-PAP-001 | 28 October 2024 | Judith Morris | For Roger Hewitt | v0.2

「Two provisional paragraphs for Chapter 2」の2段落を、次の2段落に差し替える。

In the earlier draft I read the heating payment in the invitation of 28 October 1862 as an admission charge for a member's guest. That reading does not survive the next sentence. CR/13/A f. 085r, ll. 3–5 permits each member to bring one companion, then says that members and guests may make a voluntary gift for heating and that those who give nothing will also be welcomed. The secretary's invitation thus sets a limit on companions, but does not make the heating gift a condition of welcoming them. The short proof of 26 September had isolated the preceding lines; the full passage and revised translation are in *Dues and gifts in context*, LHT-20241025-PAP-001.¹

I also took the instruction in CR/14/A f. 186r, ll. 7–9 as a payment required before an indebted member could enter and read in the room. The preceding sentences instead allow that member to continue consulting books on the premises and introduce the settlement of the account if he asks to take a book away. The secretary directs the attendant to receive the outstanding subscription before the book leaves and then pass the money, with the member's name, to the treasurer. The condition concerns borrowing outside the room; it should not be extended to reading inside. See the French and revised translation in LHT-20241025-PAP-001.¹

「Note」の注1を次に差し替える。

1. The invitation and the instruction describe proposed terms, not recorded attendance or use. Neither a payment nor a demand for one establishes who visited or how many visits occurred. That question remains open pending independent evidence of attendance or use; see *July: what the copies can support*, LHT-20240730-PAP-001.
