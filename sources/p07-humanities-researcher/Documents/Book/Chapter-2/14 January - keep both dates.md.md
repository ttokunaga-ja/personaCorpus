# 14 January — keep both dates

York, 10:00–11:20 GMT. Judith Morris and Julie Perrin, reading the box 12 receipts against the working Table 2.1.

We keep the four counts, 42, 49, 45 and 48, and the total of 184. The old heading “Participants” invites a count of people; change it to **Payment entries**. The unit is one recorded receipt of subscription money. It is not an estimate of distinct people.

Bernard's 0.50 fr. on 10 April and 1.00 fr. on 8 May (CR/12/B 043r l. 4 and 044r l. 6) are two receipts towards the same term. Roux's 4.50 fr. for three people on 9 October (049r l. 5) is one receipt; the entry does not divide the sum among them. Retain the date of receipt and the description of the term separately. Thus a January receipt of a December balance stays in the January–March receipt quarter.

Julie will check the French terms and examples of receipt dates against the leaves. I will revise the table note and check the quarterly formulas against the receipt list. Neither this total nor the name in a receipt establishes attendance.
