# Chapter 2 · Dues and participation

## §2 Assessing and collecting dues

LHT-20260112-EVD-001 | 15 January 2026 | Judith Morris | Working table for Roger Hewitt | v0.3

**Table 2.1. Payment entries in the subscription accounts, by quarter of receipt**

| Quarter of receipt | Payment entries |
|---|---:|
| April–June 1863 | 42 |
| July–September 1863 | 49 |
| October–December 1863 | 45 |
| January–March 1864 | 48 |
| Total | 184 |

Note: One payment entry is one receipt of subscription money as recorded in the accounts. Instalments received on different dates are separate entries: Marguerite Bernard's 0.50 franc on 10 April and 1.00 franc on 8 May are counted twice. Conversely, Étienne Roux's handover of 4.50 francs for three subscriptions on 9 October is one entry; the record does not give individual allocations. The periods in the table follow receipt dates, not necessarily the terms to which the money was assigned. The total is not a count of people and cannot serve as a denominator for an attendance rate.

Source: Société de lecture de Saint-Aubin-sur-Rive, CR/12/B ff. 043r–054r; reading copies supplied by Camille Laurent. For the entry-level transcription and receipt-quarter classification, see *Receipts behind the counts.csv*. Bernard: 043r l. 4 and 044r l. 6; Roux: 049r l. 5. On the distinction between receipt and term, 052r l. 6 records a December balance received on 15 January 1864.
