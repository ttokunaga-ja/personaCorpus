# Two dates that stay in 1866

LHT-20260709-PAP-001 | 9 July 2026 | Judith Morris | For Roger Hewitt, discussion of revision | v1.0

## Three instructions, three occasions

| Date and source | Who directs whom | What the paper permits or directs |
|---|---|---|
| 29 January 1866, CR/14/A f.173r | Secretary, communicating the committee's decision, to treasurer | Withhold the demand for the outstanding subscription until 12 February; resume ordinary reminders after the 12th. The debt is not cancelled. |
| 19 March 1866, CR/14/A f.186r | Secretary to attendant | A member in arrears may consult in the room; arrears are to be collected before removal of a book. The instruction also addresses the account handover. |
| 16 May 1867, CR/14/A f.184r, LA-042 | Secretary to attendant | For this occasion permit one book to leave, to be returned the following Thursday; leave the outstanding account with the treasurer. |

The separate hand on f.184v records receipt on 17 May 1867. It is not the date of the recto instruction or the return of the book. Julie and I read the final figure against the 7 in the recto's line 4, the weekday and the verso on 8 July. The former 1864 reading is superseded.

## What the corrected order removes

| Claim in v0.1 | What can still be said from the cited record |
|---|---|
| An individual loan practice in 1864 prepared the January 1866 deferment. | The January letter specifies a time-limited deferment and the return to ordinary reminders. The 1867 permission cannot precede it. No continuous committee record establishing that causal link has been identified. |
| The March 1866 requirement narrowed the earlier practice shown by LA-042. | The March letter distinguishes consultation from removal. The 1867 paper records one later permission; neither establishes a general practice or a rule change between the two. |

## Scope of revision

Replace the causal paragraph in each of §2 and §4 and its affected note, retaining the adjacent paragraphs so that Roger can read the result in context. Keep the January deferment, the March distinction and the individual permission as separately dated evidence. The date correction does not require withdrawal of the whole chapter or alteration of the submitted 2025 chapter.

Table 2.1 and its January note count 184 payment entries by receipt date, not people or visits. The reading of LA-042 does not change that counting rule or the table's count. The two passages and their source notes are the proposed scope of this revision.

Sources: CR/14/A f.173r ll.4–8; f.186r ll.2–9; f.184r–v; reading with Julie Perrin, 8 July 2026; “Table note - payment entries”.
