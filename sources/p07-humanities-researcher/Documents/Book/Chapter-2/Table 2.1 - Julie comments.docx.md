**Table 2.1. Participants in the subscription accounts, by quarter of receipt**

| Quarter of receipt | Participants |
|---|---:|
| April–June 1863 | 42 |
| July–September 1863 | 49 |
| October–December 1863 | 45 |
| January–March 1864 | 48 |
| Total | 184 |

Note: The table brings together the recorded participants in the surviving receipt papers, arranged by the quarter in which the money was received. The descriptions of the terms to which the sums were assigned remain in the individual entries. The table is a working summary for §2, not a reconstruction of visits to the room.

Source: Société de lecture de Saint-Aubin-sur-Rive, CR/12/B ff. 043r–054r, receipt papers; reading copies supplied by Camille Laurent. Four quarters comprise ff. 043r–045r, 046r–048r, 049r–051r and 052r–054r respectively.
