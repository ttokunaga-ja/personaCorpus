# Seven passages for Julie

LHT-20241017-EVD-001 | 17 October 2024 | Judith Morris
For Julie Perrin | v0.1

## Scope

Julie, these are the seven passages I should like to read against the copies with you on Thursday. I have left my first English wording in place, including the words that may be wrong. Please check the person addressed, the person who receives the money and the limits of each instruction as well as the payment terms.

## Passages under review

### CR/12/A f. 023r, ll. 4–6 — 4 March 1861

| French | First English wording |
|---|---|
| 4. Veuillez remettre au trésorier la cotisation du trimestre,
5. soit un franc cinquante centimes, dès que possible.
6. L'offrande pour la lampe demeure comptée à part. | Please give the treasurer the quarterly donation of one franc fifty centimes as soon as possible. The offering for the lamp remains accounted for separately. |

The secretary addresses a member; the treasurer receives the money. Does “donation” obscure the relation to the overdue account in the preceding lines?

### CR/12/B f. 037r, ll. 6–8 — 16 February 1863

| French | First English wording |
|---|---|
| 6. Veuillez me remettre l'enveloppe contenant les deux francs
7. de cette offrande pour le charbon dès votre passage.
8. Portez séparément au cahier les cotisations reçues. | Please hand me the envelope containing the two-franc subscription instalment for coal when you next come. Enter the subscriptions received separately in the book. |

The treasurer addresses the attendant and asks for the envelope to be handed to him. Is the attendant paying, or carrying another person's money? The separate entry needs checking.

### CR/13/A f. 085r, ll. 3–5 — 28 October 1862

| French | First English wording |
|---|---|
| 3. Chaque sociétaire pourra venir avec une personne.
4. Membres et invités pourront faire une offrande libre pour le chauffage.
5. Nous accueillerons aussi ceux qui ne donneront rien. | Each member may come with one person. Members and guests may pay an admission charge for heating. We shall also welcome those who give nothing. |

The secretary addresses members; the recipient of any money is not named here. My wording sits uneasily with the last sentence. Can the admission reading in the Chapter 2 paragraph stand when the three lines are read together?

### CR/14/A f. 173r, ll. 5–7 — 29 January 1866

| French | First English wording |
|---|---|
| 5. Le comité accorde une dispense jusqu'au 12 février :
6. ne lui demandez pas cette cotisation avant cette date ;
7. après le 12, reprenez les rappels ordinaires. | The committee grants a waiver of dues until 12 February: do not ask him for this subscription before that date; after the 12th resume ordinary reminders. |

The secretary conveys the committee's decision to the treasurer. Is “waiver” too broad for a direction bounded by a date? No amount or use condition appears in these lines.

### CR/14/A f. 182r, ll. 4–6 — 15 February 1866

| French | First English wording |
|---|---|
| 4. En attendant, je désire offrir un franc pour le poêle ;
5. je vous prie de recevoir cette somme à cet usage,
6. sans la porter au compte de ma cotisation en retard. | Meanwhile, I wish to offer one franc as a subscription instalment for the stove; please receive this sum for that purpose without entering it against my overdue subscription. |

A member writes to the secretary, whom he asks to receive the franc. The last clause troubles my “instalment”: does it make the offered sum separate from the arrears?

### CR/14/A f. 186r, ll. 7–9 — 19 March 1866

| French | First English wording |
|---|---|
| 7. Avant de laisser sortir le livre, recevez de lui
8. la cotisation qui reste due à la société.
9. Remettez ensuite cette somme au trésorier avec son nom. | Before admitting him, take from him the charge at the door still due to the society. Then hand the sum to the treasurer with his name. |

The secretary instructs the attendant to take the money from the member and pass it to the treasurer. I have made this a condition at the door in the chapter. Can that reading stand alongside the preceding instruction about the book?

### CR/14/B f. 202r, ll. 3–5 — 13 October 1868

| French | First English wording |
|---|---|
| 3. Je vous prie de me rendre les 75 centimes qui restent,
4. afin que je les remette à la personne qui les avait donnés
5. pour la lampe du jeudi. Ce reste ne va pas aux cotisations. | Please return to me the remaining 75 centimes as a rebate of dues, so that I may give them back to the person who had given them for Thursday's lamp. This balance does not go towards subscriptions. |

The attendant addresses the treasurer and asks to receive the balance for return to the original giver. Is “rebate of dues” contradicted by the final sentence?

## Implication for Chapter 2

The paragraphs on the Saturday invitation and payment before entry remain provisional. Please look especially at 085 and 186 with their neighbouring lines before I use either interpretation. An invitation or collection instruction alone cannot establish actual attendance or use.
