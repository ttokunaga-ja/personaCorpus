# After both of us opened the leaves

17 November 2025, after the afternoon check

Florian and I opened the four candidates again together between 2.00 and 2.45. For each, we found a leaf and body lines that match the sense of the quotation in the chapter. The twenty-place exercise had sixteen references needing no correction and these four candidates to resolve. They are mistakes in the exercise copy, not a record of corrections to the submitted February chapter.

| Source note | Candidate in exercise | Confirmed reference | What we checked |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | CR/12/A 024r | CR/12/A 023r, ll. 4–6 | Quarterly subscription and lamp offering treated separately. |
| 9 | CR/13/A 084r | CR/13/A 085r, ll. 3–5 | One companion and the welcome to those who give nothing. |
| 12 | CR/14/B 201r | CR/14/B 202r, ll. 3–5 | Return of the unused 75 centimes, separate from subscriptions. |
| 13 | CR/14/A 185r | CR/14/A 186r, ll. 3–9 | Consultation in the room at ll. 3–4; removal and handover at ll. 5–9. |

At 186r we paid particular attention to the boundary: permission to read indoors precedes the instruction to collect arrears before a book goes out and pass the money on. The latter is not a condition for reading at the table. We checked the later lines as well as the first apparently relevant ones.

Keep the marked exercise and the two-person sheet together. Put these four references into the candidates for the next update of the exercise register and the working chapter's notes; before returning them, record both checkers, the date and destination, then compare each note with its sentence. The submitted file stays as submitted. The four held box-14 leaves from October are a different set and remain on hold.
