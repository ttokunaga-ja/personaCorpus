# Dues and gifts in context

LHT-20241025-PAP-001 | 25 October 2024 | Judith Morris | For Roger Hewitt and Julie Perrin

## Seven passages after the French check

The following pairs retain the lines around each corrected phrase. Julie and I compared all 32 transcriptions with the paper copies on 24 October. The passages specify different movements of money; an instruction or invitation does not by itself show a visit or use.

### CR/12/A f. 023r, ll. 1–7 — 4 March 1861

| French | English |
|---|---|
| 1. Le terme fixé pour le versement est passé<br>2. et je n'ai pas encore reçu avis de votre paiement.<br>3. Je vous prie de bien vouloir régulariser votre compte.<br>4. Veuillez remettre au trésorier la cotisation du trimestre,<br>5. soit un franc cinquante centimes, dès que possible.<br>6. L'offrande pour la lampe demeure comptée à part.<br>7. Le trésorier vous remettra une quittance pour la cotisation. | 1. The date fixed for payment has passed<br>2. and I have not yet received notice of your payment.<br>3. Please put your account in order.<br>4. Please give the treasurer the quarterly subscription,<br>5. one franc fifty centimes, as soon as possible.<br>6. The offering for the lamp remains accounted for separately.<br>7. The treasurer will give you a receipt for the subscription. |

The secretary reminds the member of an overdue obligation and directs the payment to the treasurer. “Quarterly subscription” replaces “donation”: the separate lamp offering is not the debt. No condition of admission or use is stated.

### CR/12/B f. 037r, ll. 1–9 — 16 February 1863

| French | English |
|---|---|
| 1. J'ai appris qu'une enveloppe a été laissée pour la caisse.<br>2. Elle porte le nom de son expéditeur et son montant.<br>3. Je vous remercie de l'avoir gardée jusqu'à mon retour.<br>4. La somme a été offerte pour l'achat de charbon ;<br>5. elle ne doit pas être confondue avec les cotisations.<br>6. Veuillez me remettre l'enveloppe contenant les deux francs<br>7. de cette offrande pour le charbon dès votre passage.<br>8. Portez séparément au cahier les cotisations reçues.<br>9. Je vérifierai les inscriptions avec vous à la caisse. | 1. I have learned that an envelope was left for the funds.<br>2. It bears its sender's name and the amount.<br>3. Thank you for keeping it until my return.<br>4. The sum was offered for the purchase of coal;<br>5. it must not be confused with subscriptions.<br>6. Please hand me the envelope containing the two francs<br>7. of this gift for coal when you next come.<br>8. Enter the subscriptions received separately in the book.<br>9. I shall check the entries with you at the cash desk. |

The treasurer asks the attendant to hand him an envelope from its sender. The attendant is the intermediary, not necessarily the giver. The offered two francs are a gift for coal, not the “subscription instalment” of my first wording; subscriptions have their own entry. The passage sets no participation condition.

### CR/13/A f. 085r, ll. 1–6 — 28 October 1862

| French | English |
|---|---|
| 1. Nous vous invitons à la lecture qui aura lieu samedi<br>2. dans la salle de la société, à l'heure accoutumée.<br>3. Chaque sociétaire pourra venir avec une personne.<br>4. Membres et invités pourront faire une offrande libre pour le chauffage.<br>5. Nous accueillerons aussi ceux qui ne donneront rien.<br>6. Veuillez présenter votre accompagnant au préposé à l'entrée. | 1. We invite you to the reading to be held on Saturday<br>2. in the society's room at the usual hour.<br>3. Each member may bring one person.<br>4. Members and guests may make a voluntary gift for heating.<br>5. We shall also welcome those who give nothing.<br>6. Please introduce your companion to the attendant at the entrance. |

The secretary invites the members; members and guests may offer the gift, but no recipient of the money is named. The next sentence welcomes non-givers. My “admission charge” incorrectly made the guest's entry depend on payment: the invitation permits one companion per member without making the heating gift a condition. It does not establish who attended.

### CR/14/A f. 173r, ll. 1–8 — 29 January 1866

| French | English |
|---|---|
| 1. Le comité a examiné la demande du sociétaire<br>2. dont je vous ai entretenu lors de notre dernière séance.<br>3. Il m'a chargé de vous faire connaître sa décision<br>4. au sujet de la cotisation encore due par ce membre.<br>5. Le comité accorde une dispense jusqu'au 12 février :<br>6. ne lui demandez pas cette cotisation avant cette date ;<br>7. après le 12, reprenez les rappels ordinaires.<br>8. Veuillez inscrire cette décision sur votre état. | 1. The committee has examined the member's request<br>2. about which I spoke to you at our last meeting.<br>3. It has asked me to communicate its decision<br>4. concerning the subscription still owed by this member.<br>5. The committee grants a deferment until 12 February:<br>6. do not ask him for this subscription before that date;<br>7. after the 12th resume the ordinary reminders.<br>8. Please enter this decision on your statement. |

The secretary communicates the committee's decision to the treasurer, who is to defer demands on the member and resume reminders after the 12th. The subscription remains due; “deferment of collection” replaces “waiver of dues”. No amount, payment recipient or condition of use is specified in this decision.

### CR/14/A f. 182r, ll. 1–7 — 15 February 1866

| French | English |
|---|---|
| 1. Je vous remercie de votre lettre au sujet de la salle.<br>2. Je n'ai pas encore réglé ma cotisation à la société.<br>3. Je tâcherai de vous faire savoir quand je pourrai le faire.<br>4. En attendant, je désire offrir un franc pour le poêle ;<br>5. je vous prie de recevoir cette somme à cet usage,<br>6. sans la porter au compte de ma cotisation en retard.<br>7. Je compte vous l'apporter vendredi à la salle. | 1. Thank you for your letter about the room.<br>2. I have not yet paid my subscription to the society.<br>3. I shall try to let you know when I can do so.<br>4. Meanwhile, I wish to offer one franc for the stove;<br>5. please receive this sum for that purpose,<br>6. without entering it against my overdue subscription.<br>7. I intend to bring it to you at the room on Friday. |

The member asks the secretary to receive the franc for the stove and proposes to bring it on Friday. It is an offered gift, separate from the unpaid subscription, not an instalment against it. No participation condition is stated; the promise does not establish that the sum was delivered.

### CR/14/A f. 186r, ll. 1–10 — 19 March 1866

| French | English |
|---|---|
| 1. Je vous écris au sujet du sociétaire dont je vous ai parlé.<br>2. Son compte présente encore une cotisation impayée.<br>3. Il peut continuer à consulter les ouvrages dans la salle.<br>4. Veuillez lui laisser les livres nécessaires à sa lecture sur place.<br>5. Si toutefois il demande à emporter un ouvrage,<br>6. rappelez-lui que son compte doit d'abord être réglé.<br>7. Avant de laisser sortir le livre, recevez de lui<br>8. la cotisation qui reste due à la société.<br>9. Remettez ensuite cette somme au trésorier avec son nom.<br>10. Vous voudrez bien me signaler toute difficulté. | 1. I write about the member of whom I spoke to you.<br>2. His account still shows an unpaid subscription.<br>3. He may continue to consult the books in the room.<br>4. Please leave him the books necessary for reading on the premises.<br>5. If, however, he asks to take a book away,<br>6. remind him that his account must first be settled.<br>7. Before letting the book leave, receive from him<br>8. the subscription still due to the society.<br>9. Then hand this sum to the treasurer with his name.<br>10. Please inform me of any difficulty. |

The secretary tells the attendant to collect the member's arrears and pass them, with his name, to the treasurer. Reading on the premises is allowed; settlement is required before a book is taken out. “Subscription arrears before borrowing” replaces “charge at the door”. My earlier inference made payment a condition of reading inside and must be withdrawn. The instruction does not prove that borrowing occurred.

### CR/14/B f. 202r, ll. 1–6 — 13 October 1868

| French | English |
|---|---|
| 1. La dépense de la lampe étant réglée, il reste une partie<br>2. de la somme que je vous ai remise hier pour cet usage.<br>3. Je vous prie de me rendre les 75 centimes qui restent,<br>4. afin que je les remette à la personne qui les avait donnés<br>5. pour la lampe du jeudi. Ce reste ne va pas aux cotisations.<br>6. Je lui rapporterai le solde au prochain service. | 1. The lamp expense having been paid, part remains<br>2. of the sum I handed you yesterday for that purpose.<br>3. Please return to me the 75 centimes that remain,<br>4. so that I may give them back to the person who gave them<br>5. for Thursday's lamp. This balance does not go to subscriptions.<br>6. I shall take the balance back to that person at the next service. |

The attendant asks the treasurer to return the unused balance to him, so he can give it back to the original donor. This is a return of the unused gift, not a rebate or set-off of dues. No new payment by the member arises here, and no participation condition is stated.
