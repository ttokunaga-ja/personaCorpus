## Guests at the November sitting

**§3, p. 3 → notes 23–24, p. 6 · CR/13/A ff.087r–088r**

The 5 November circular allows one companion per member for the sitting on 8 November 1862. The postscript tells the attendant to list companions separately, name the presenting member beside each guest and leave guests out of the membership count. Both notes match the passage. The invitation gives the terms of entry for that sitting. For who came, I would need the entries themselves.

## Two requests on one visit

**§3, p. 3 → note 27, p. 7 · CR/13/B f.123r**

In the letter of 5 December 1865 the evening attendant tells the secretary to count the newspaper and book entries as one visit. The same reader stayed in the room between requests. The note identifies the letter and the pair of lines. Use one visit for this pair.

## The extra Thursday hour

**§5, pp. 4–5 → notes 46–47, p. 7 · CR/14/B ff.198r–199r**

The 6 October 1868 notice sets a Thursday trial for October, moving closing from 19:00 to 20:00. The continuation calls for a separate entry for arrivals in the extra hour, excluding readers already present at seven. The committee proposes to review the journal at month end before deciding whether to keep the hour. Both notes support the limit in the text.
