# What the treasurer could certify

LHT-20250610-PAP-001 | 10 June 2025 | Judith Morris | For Susan Atkinson | v0.1

## Page 1 — What the subscription notice requires

CR/12/A ff.012–013 · Secretary to members · 4 February 1861

**f.012r**

> Le trimestre de la société étant ouvert, j’ai l’honneur de vous rappeler que la cotisation est fixée à un franc cinquante centimes par trimestre. La première échéance est le 15 février prochain.

[Rate and first deadline: 1 franc 50 centimes per quarter, first due on 15 February 1861.]

> Le trésorier inscrira chaque remise sur son état de recettes. Un récépissé sera remis pour le paiement ; la souche du récépissé restera entre ses mains avec les pièces de caisse.

[Treasurer’s action: enter each remittance in the receipts statement, issue a receipt and retain the counterfoil with the cash papers.]

The secretary is to keep the statement sent by the treasurer among the society’s papers. The notice allows payment to reach the treasurer through another member.

**f.013r, beginning of the continuation**

> Si une remise est faite pour plusieurs personnes, veuillez les désigner afin que leur situation puisse être reconnue.

[Where one remittance covers several people, identify the people it covers.]

Susan, I want to keep two places in the bundle for this: the rate and recording duties on the first leaf, and the request to identify those covered by a combined payment at the head of the next. The rate tells us what was due. For an actual receipt, turn to the treasurer’s letter.

## Page 2 — What the receiving officer can say

CR/12/B f.040r · Treasurer to secretary · 2 March 1863

> J’ai reçu hier une remise faite en une seule fois pour trois sociétaires.

[Receipt reported: a single remittance on 1 March for three members.]

> Jusqu’ici, lorsque l’argent m’arrivait par l’intermédiaire du préposé de service, je portais le total sous le nom de celui-ci, sans détailler sur la souche les sommes appartenant à chacun.

[Entry described: when money came through the attendant on duty, the treasurer entered the total under the attendant’s name. The counterfoil did not break it down by member.]

The treasurer can attest to receiving the remittance and can explain how he has been entering such totals. He asks the secretary where to keep the bearer’s name and each person’s share: on the counterfoil at the cash desk or on the statement sent to the secretary. He offers to put both on both records and leaves the old entry as it stands pending an answer.

Keep ‘received in one remittance for three members’ in the working note. I would leave open whether the intermediary paid a subscription of his own. To settle that, we would need the answer and an entry identifying the sums credited to the individual accounts.

Consultation copies: Archives de Saint-Aubin-sur-Rive, CR/12/A ff.012–013 and CR/12/B f.040r.
