# After Roger — 25 April

York, 2 pm. Roger Hewitt and I discussed the outline. Keep six chapters, but do not make the path of print carry the whole argument. Put dues and participation in chapter 2; print and supply move to chapter 4. The opening chapter must explain the units and sources before the comparison begins. Chapter 3 can deal with rooms and opening hours, chapter 5 with committee correspondence, and chapter 6 with the limits of comparison.

A subscription is not a visit. Nor is an address to which a text was sent proof that its recipient came to a meeting. For the next pass I need to look separately for rules and collection, traces of attendance or use, and letters concerning remissions. Only then can I see whether any of them can be put alongside one another. Keep the publication route as an argument of its own, not a substitute for that work.

Next: revise the outline for Roger and make a list of questions and the types of record each would need.
