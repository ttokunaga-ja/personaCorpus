# How the three tables will be used

Document: LHT-20260409-EVD-001 v0.1  
Date: 9 April 2026  
Author: Judith Morris  
For: Stefan Albrecht, desk comparison on 16 April

## Proposed division

| Table | Question and source | Unit and use |
|---|---|---|
| A — receipts by quarter | When was subscription money recorded as received? CR/12/B 043r–054r, grouped by receipt date. | Four receipt quarters: 42, 49, 45 and 48 entries, total 184. A payment entry is one recorded receipt, not a person. |
| B — grounds for inclusion | Which passages record a receipt and which describe a different action? Compare dated receipt entries with demands, gifts, enquiries and instructions. | A source-by-source comparison, with the act and the detail determining its treatment. It explains A's boundary; it is not an additional count. |
| C — names in the subscription papers | How can the evidence for a proposed identification be found again in CR/12/B 043r–054r? | 57 identification items under original name forms, with leaf and line. Fifty-one have an explicit account or earlier-receipt link between two entries; six entries in three same-spelling pairs lack such a link. |

## How to move between them

From A, use the receipt quarter and leaf range to find the underlying entry; B explains why a kind of passage is inside or outside the count. C points back to particular entries and their identifying context. It is not a roster of all payers and cannot be used to derive a per-person payment total from A. A linked pair in C still comprises two separate receipt entries in A; a collective handover can be one receipt without revealing each person's allocation.

For the desk comparison, please try the six unlinked entries individually: Giraud 046r l. 5 and 048r l. 7, Martin 049r l. 8 and 051r l. 4, Petit 052r l. 9 and 054r l. 8. Does each heading return us to its own source? Identical spelling alone is not a link. The proposed treatment is to preserve separate references pending the comparison.
