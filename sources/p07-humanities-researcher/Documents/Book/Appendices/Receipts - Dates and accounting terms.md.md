# Receipt months and the term named

19 February

I tried ordering the appendix by the subscription term to which the money was due. That would put CR/12/B 052r l. 6 under December 1863: its detail reads “reste du terme de décembre”. But the receipt itself is dated 15 January 1864. Moving the entry to December would remove the date at which the cash was received from the quarter count.

Keep the quarter of receipt as the ordering and counting field. Record the term named in the source separately, alongside the entry, rather than using it to move the receipt. Thus 052r l. 6 remains in January–March 1864, with the December term in its detail. The two dates answer different questions.
