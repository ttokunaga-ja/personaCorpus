## Reading the boundary

Table A counts dated entries recording the receipt of subscription money in CR/12/B 043r–054r. The examples below compare the evidence for inclusion and exclusion; they are not additional counts to be added to table A. A request, an instruction or a gift is not itself an entry recording a subscription received. Each included row represents one recorded receipt, irrespective of the number of people or instalments involved.

| Source | Action recorded | Treatment | Decisive detail |
|---|---|---|---|
| CR/12/A 023r ll. 1–7 | The secretary asks a member to pay the quarterly subscription to the treasurer. | Outside the receipt population. | The deadline has passed and no notice of payment has been received (ll. 1–2); the request and promised receipt do not record an actual payment. |
| CR/12/B 037r ll. 4–8 | The treasurer asks the attendant to bring an envelope containing two francs offered for coal. | Outside. | The sum is a coal offering, distinguished from subscriptions (ll. 4–7); l. 8 instructs that subscriptions received be entered separately, without identifying a particular entry. |
| CR/12/B 040r, letter of 2 March 1863 | The treasurer asks how to record a single earlier remittance for three members. | Outside. | The letter discusses where the messenger and shares should be entered and says the old entry will not be changed before a reply. It is a query about a separate earlier handover, not an entry in the receipt series counted in A. |
| CR/14/A 173r ll. 4–7 | The committee defers asking one member for the subscription until after 12 February. | Outside. | The treasurer is told not to ask before that date and to resume reminders afterwards; neither instruction records money received. |
| CR/14/A 186r ll. 3–9 | The attendant is instructed to collect arrears before a book is taken away and pass them to the treasurer. | Outside. | Reading in the room is allowed (ll. 3–4); collection before taking a book out and handover are instructions (ll. 7–9), not evidence that either occurred. |
| CR/12/B 043r l. 4 | Marguerite Bernard, 10 April 1863: 0.50 franc, an instalment. | One receipt in A. | The dated entry records the money received, not merely requested. |
| CR/12/B 044r l. 6 | Bernard, 8 May 1863: 1.00 franc, balance on the same account, referring to the earlier receipt. | One further receipt in A. | A second dated receipt records the balance; the reference joins the account without making the two handovers one. |
| CR/12/B 049r l. 5 | Étienne Roux brings 4.50 francs for three people on 9 October 1863. | One receipt in A. | One handover is recorded, without individual allocations. The number of people mentioned does not turn it into three entries. |

A subsequent dated record of money received could supply evidence of a receipt following a request or instruction, but it would be a separate record. Table C indexes identifiable names on the receipt leaves and does not supply a count of people for table A.
