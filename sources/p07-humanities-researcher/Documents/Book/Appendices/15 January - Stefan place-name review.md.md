# 15 January — what a match needed

York, 15 January 2025, 10.00–11.35 GMT. Stefan Albrecht and I worked through the paper copies Camille sent for consultation, in leaf order. Box 12 gave us 12 occurrences, box 13 gave us 14 and box 14 gave us 20. We could assign 38 of the 46 occurrences to headings. We kept the other eight as independent entries searchable under their original spellings, with the heading reference left empty. The totals are for occurrences on the leaves.

The check that worked was to put two instructions side by side and ask whether one actually took up the other, or whether the terms of a handover identified the same transaction. An explicit reference, or agreement in the object, recipient and terms of delivery, carried weight. A familiar landmark or a similar spelling on its own did very little. Keep the leaf and line for each occurrence even when both go under one heading.

## The two notices at the bridge

On CR/13/A f. 089r, ll. 2–3 (4 November 1862), the secretary asks members to assemble « à dix heures / à Clairpré, près du pont de pierre ». On f. 090r, l. 1 (6 November), « L'avis du 4 novembre demande une correction d'heure ». Lines 2–4 keep the stone bridge in the instruction, spell the name « à Clerpré » at l. 3 and give eleven in place of ten. Line 5 leaves the other arrangements in force. File both spellings under Clairpré: the dated reference and the restriction of the correction to the hour do the work. We have a match between passages and the bridge described in them.

## The return of the June duplicates

On CR/12/A f. 014v, ll. 1–3, the attendant is to send the secretary « les doubles des quittances du dix juin » at « St-Aubin-s.-Rive ». The packet is to carry « Saint-Aubin-sur-Rive, doubles du dix juin ». The letter back to the treasurer, f. 015r, ll. 1–2, says « Les doubles demandés au dos de l'avis du 3 juin / me sont parvenus à Saint-Aubin s/ Rive ce matin. » On f. 015v, ll. 2–3, the receipt of the packet is recorded and the register spelling is written out. Use Saint-Aubin-sur-Rive for the return address: the writer identifies the duplicates by date and refers back to the direction on the reverse. The payment reception at le Pré des Ormes on 014r is a separate part of the notice.

On 176r the fold interrupts the first letter of the address. The turn of the V in « Vu » (l. 2) is the better comparison; the B in « Berger » (l. 8) has two lobes, one above the other. I shall enter Val d’Erne with the uncertain initial noted. Reopen the question of location when we have a contemporary address or endorsement. The copy gives us neither that nor an identified relay for this notice.
