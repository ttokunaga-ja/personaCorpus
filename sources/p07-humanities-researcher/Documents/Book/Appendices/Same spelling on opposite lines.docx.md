# Same spelling on opposite lines

Document: LHT-20260317-EVD-001 v0.1  
Date: 17 March 2026  
Author: Judith Morris  
For: Stefan Albrecht

## Question for Stefan

Can each line be found from an index heading without silently deciding that the two occurrences belong to one person? The spelling is evidence of a name form; it is not by itself an account link. These six entries need separate references while we consider them.

| Name as written | Source | Context in the entry | Comparison |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pierre Giraud | CR/12/B 046r l. 5 | Préposé delivering another person's subscription. | A messenger's name is not necessarily the payer's. No account reference connects this entry to 048r l. 7. |
| Pierre Giraud | CR/12/B 048r l. 7 | Name recorded for payment of his own term. | Keep reachable separately from the préposé entry; identical spelling does not establish identity. |
| Claire Martin | CR/12/B 049r l. 8 | Residence: rue du Four. | No account link found to the other residence. |
| Claire Martin | CR/12/B 051r l. 4 | Residence: rue des Tanneurs. | Different residence is a distinction for retrieval, not proof of a different person. |
| Jules Petit | CR/12/B 052r l. 9 | Relieur. | No number links the entry to 054r l. 8. |
| Jules Petit | CR/12/B 054r l. 8 | Commis. | The occupational term distinguishes the entry; it cannot establish a separate identity alone. |

## A linked comparison

Marguerite Bernard appears at CR/12/B 043r l. 4 for an advance of 0.50 franc on 10 April 1863 and at 044r l. 6 for the 1.00 franc balance on 8 May. The latter points to the earlier receipt on the same account. That explicit connection supports bringing the two references together; it does not turn the two receipts into one payment entry.

For the three pairs above I have found no equivalent account or receipt reference. Please try each leaf and line from the proposed headings before we settle the index.
