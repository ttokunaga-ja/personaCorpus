## Payment entries by receipt quarter

| Quarter in which payment was received | Source, CR/12/B | Payment entries |
|---|---|---:|
| April–June 1863 | 043r–045r | 42 |
| July–September 1863 | 046r–048r | 49 |
| October–December 1863 | 049r–051r | 45 |
| January–March 1864 | 052r–054r | 48 |
| Total | 043r–054r | 184 |

One payment entry is one recorded receipt of subscription money. Separate instalments received on different dates are counted separately; a single handover collected for several people is one receipt. These figures do not count distinct people and cannot supply an attendance denominator.

## Receipt date and term named

The table assigns entries by the date money was received, not by the period for which it was due. At CR/12/B 052r l. 6 the receipt is dated 15 January 1864 and its detail reads “reste du terme de décembre”: the balance concerns the preceding December term. It is counted in January–March 1864. The term named in the source remains part of the description and does not move the receipt into the previous quarter.
