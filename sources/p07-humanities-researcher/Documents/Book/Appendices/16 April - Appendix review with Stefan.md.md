# Keep separate headings

16 April 2026, York  
Judith Morris and Stefan Albrecht, 10:00–11:25

For Stefan and my working copy:

We checked the three appendix tables against the sources. Keep A, B and C as separate tables. A groups the 184 payment entries by the quarter in which money was received. B must give the source and the detail that allows each inclusion or exclusion to be followed back to the passage. C is a route to the evidence for the 57 identification items, not a count of people or a way to calculate payments per person.

Of the 57 items, 51 have two entries explicitly connected by an account number or an earlier receipt number. List both references and the connection. The remaining six are three pairs where the spelling is the same but the evidence does not settle identity. Keep each entry under its original spelling with its role, residence or occupation and leaf and line:

- Pierre Giraud: 046r l. 5, préposé bringing another person's subscription; 048r l. 7, payment of his own term.
- Claire Martin: 049r l. 8, rue du Four; 051r l. 4, rue des Tanneurs.
- Jules Petit: 052r l. 9, relieur; 054r l. 8, commis.

All references are CR/12/B. We found no account or receipt link for these pairs. Keep the six separately retrievable; neither sameness nor difference of person is established by the distinctions alone.
