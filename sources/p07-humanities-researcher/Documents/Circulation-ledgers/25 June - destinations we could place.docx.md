# 25 June: destinations we could place
LHT-20260625-EVD-001 · v1.0 · 25 June 2026
Judith Morris · For Isabelle Chevalier and Susan Atkinson

## Check and decision

Isabelle, Susan and I checked the nine registers online on 25 June, 10.00–11.05 BST. We worked from 117 original entry lines, one record per line. We could identify the destination in 103 records; 14 remain unresolved. These are records, not a count of people, towns or complete routes.

We will not present a complete map of intercity movements on this evidence. The working diagram of Les Veillées du jeudi is retained for internal checking, with its breaks visible.

## Why the decisions differ

| Entry | Reading and decision |
|---|---|
| MRC/02 f.18r l.8, 12 May 1865 | The destination is “B.”. Neither the spelling nor this entry establishes a destination; retain the original form and location as unresolved. The Rochemont receipt on 19 May has no established copy number or sender linking it to this entry. |
| MRC/08 f.11r l.4 | The recipient column is blank, but the same line's note reads “remis au dépôt de Valperche”. The note identifies the destination as the dépôt; retain the blank and the note in the transcription. |
| MRC/04 f.21r l.4 | “le préposé” has no affiliation supplied by the note. Retain the wording and location without assigning a place. |

The unresolved lines are MRC/01 f.12r ll.4, 10; MRC/02 f.18r l.8; MRC/03 f.9v ll.6, 11; MRC/04 f.21r ll.4, 11; MRC/05 f.6v l.9; MRC/07 f.22v ll.3, 12; MRC/08 f.11r l.9; and MRC/09 f.17v ll.2, 8, 15. Their original forms and the reasons for holding each remain in the line-by-line table. None is supplied by an index of names from the letters or subscription receipts.

## Next evidence

Isabelle will look for receiving-side copies. Susan will look for catalogue entries corresponding to the ambiguous destinations. Neither search is recorded here as completed. Please check the decisions and the transcription against the copies before we use the figures in a discussion.
