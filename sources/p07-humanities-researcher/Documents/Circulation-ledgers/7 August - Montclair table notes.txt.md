7 August — Montclair, notes from the table

With Isabelle, 09:00–11:15 BST (10:00–12:15 here). Opened MRC/01 to MRC/09 in number order. Catalogue, cover and one early example in each. Keep the titles and the headings in separate notes.

01 f.2r — Sorti le / Remis à. Brévaux is in the lender’s entry. Nothing signed by the room on this opening.

02 f.3v — À remettre à, written at the dépôt before the other end has made its entry. 03 f.2v is a different book: Reçu le and Provenance. One empty provenance. Leave it empty.

04 f.2v–3r — Took these for two independent tables at first. The reference is on both leaves, next to the fold: 47 on the left and 47 again on the right. In on 16 February, remise on the 18th. It’s the number that crosses, not the ruling. Look at the photograph beside the two headings.

05 f.2r — Intended destination and Remis le have separate places. 06 f.4v — Watch for the expected return with nothing under Repris le. 07 f.3v — Number of the thing kept and another remise.

08 f.2v — Short addition in the left outer margin: reçu au dépôt. Same level as 21 March, with Reçu par still empty. The nick is outside the ruling. Show Susan the edge as well as the line.

09 f.4r — Expédié le and À l’attention de. The addressee tells me where it was sent. I still have no arrival date.

Read the headings with Susan before making any slips for names.
