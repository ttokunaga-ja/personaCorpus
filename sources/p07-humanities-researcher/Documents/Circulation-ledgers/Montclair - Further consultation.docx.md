# Montclair - Further consultation

LHT-20240821-REQ-001 · 21 August 2024 · Judith Morris  
To Isabelle Chevalier, Montclair Reading Archive · v1.0

## Request

Following our visit on 7 August, could we identify which combination of a ledger entry and a record from the other side of the exchange would substantiate a single handover? Susan and I have since gone back over the headings. I would like to start with the entries we saw and find out where we could consult the corresponding receipts or dispatch copies. The object is to establish what was handed over, to which destination and when, wherever the surviving records permit.

## Material to consult

The table follows the order in which we consulted the catalogue, cover and opening example for each volume. The leaf references are to the examples we opened together.

| Cote | Volume and leaf seen | Side making the entry | Record to seek and details to compare |
|---|---|---|---|
| MRC/01 | *Cahier des prêts*, f. 2r | Lender, under « Sorti le » and « Remis à ». | Seek the recipient's receipt using the date, title and destination. For the entry of 18 February 1864, start with *Le foyer du dimanche* and Salle de Brévaux. |
| MRC/02 | *Sorties du dépôt*, f. 3v | Sending deposit, under « À remettre à ». | Look at the specified destination for a receipt. Check for a receipt date and a mark identifying the booklet. |
| MRC/03 | *Entrées au dépôt*, f. 2v | Receiving deposit, under « Reçu le » and « Provenance ». | Work back to the sender's dispatch copy using the stated provenance where available. Where it is blank, use the item and receipt date to look for candidates and leave the origin open. |
| MRC/04 | *Bureau des dépôts*, ff. 2v–3r | Deposit bureau, with intake on the left and handover on the right. | Ask who issued the control number, then seek a receipt at the destination on f. 3r. Within the opening, 47 links the intake on 16 February to the handover entry on 18 February 1864. The destination's receipt still needs to be found. |
| MRC/05 | *Bordereau de remise*, f. 2r | Deposit arranging and recording handover. « Destinataire prévu » and « Remis le » are separate. | Seek the recipient's receipt by control number and actual handover date. For 62 the date is blank, so keep the planned delivery unconfirmed. |
| MRC/06 | *Retours au bureau*, f. 4v | Bureau receiving returns, under « Repris le ». | Trace the returning side's copy by the custody number. Compare it with a recorded date of return. The 9 March 1864 entry gives an expected date and leaves « Repris le » blank. |
| MRC/07 | *Nouvelles remises*, f. 3v | Lender recording a renewed handover. | Seek the next recipient's receipt. Compare « N° de garde », the description of the item and the date entered for the renewed handover. |
| MRC/08 | *Accusés de réception*, f. 2v and outer margin | Receiving side. « Reçu par » is blank beside « reçu au dépôt ». | Seek the dispatch side's copy using the place of receipt given in the margin, the entry date and the description of the item. Leave the recipient's name open. |
| MRC/09 | *Envois*, f. 4r | Lender dispatching, under « Expédié le » and « À l’attention de ». | Look for the package mark in the addressee's receipts and compare the dates. The same title occurs under different marks. |

## Handling and delivery

I will note the original column headings and any marks or numbers on the items, with their volume and leaf references. Susan Atkinson will check the references against the catalogue descriptions. Could you check where the corresponding receipts or dispatch copies are held? It would help to start with the issuer of the control number in MRC/04 and the dispatch copy that might correspond to the receipt in MRC/08.

Please add any shelfmarks you find to the table so we can request the relevant pieces for the next reading-room consultation. Where a field cannot be matched, I will record which detail is missing and leave the handover open.
