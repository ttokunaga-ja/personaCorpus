# Bureau slip comparison

LHT-20240820-EVD-001 · 20 August 2024 · Judith Morris  
For discussion with Susan Atkinson · v0.1

## Numbers on the handover side

Susan, I have put the handover columns together to see what we could take back to the intake leaf. The useful comparison is between the actual entries under « N° de contrôle ».

| Reference | Entry on the handover side | Where the number takes us |
|---|---|---|
| MRC/04, *Bureau des dépôts*, f. 3r | 47 · « Remis le » 18 February 1864 · « Remis à » Salle de Brévaux | The bureau repeated 47 from f. 2v. There the entry is for *Le foyer du dimanche*, received from Martin on 16 February 1864. The two dates are two days apart. « Reçu de » gives the person who brought the item in. |
| MRC/05, *Bordereau de remise*, f. 2r | 62 · *Le jardin des lectures* · « Destinataire prévu » Salle de Brévaux · « Remis le » blank | The destination recurs, but 62 has no match on the intake leaf we saw. The date 12 March 1864 belongs to the next entry, under 63 for *Lectures du soir*. |

## What to put to Isabelle

I can follow 47 back across the fold in MRC/04 because the number actually recurs there. Neither 62 nor 63 takes me back to that left leaf. Both extracts show a control number, but neither has a field identifying who issued it. Even a repeated number across the two volumes would need checking against the issuing side's numbering practice. The shared destination on 47 and 62 gives us somewhere to ask about a receipt. It gives us no date of handover for 62.

At the next consultation, ask Isabelle which side assigned the numbers in each volume and whether the recipient's receipt carries that number or a separate reference. Once we have a possible receipt, we can compare the reference, the item and the date with the bureau's entry.
