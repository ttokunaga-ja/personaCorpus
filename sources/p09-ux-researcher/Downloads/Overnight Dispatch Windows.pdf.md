## Reading the examples

These six planned routes are provided for explaining an oral handoff. Departure is shown in the origin's local date and time; both ends of the planned arrival window are shown in the destination's local date and time. CST is UTC−06:00 and EST is UTC−05:00. These are planned windows, not actual arrival times. The April screen reference describes the current Window on the route list; this separate sheet supplies the local calendar dates for the examples.

| Route | Origin | Departure, local | Destination | Window opens, destination local | Window closes, destination local |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R-611 | Chicago | Thu December 5, 22:30 CST | Detroit | Fri December 6, 06:00 EST | Fri December 6, 07:00 EST |
| R-612 | Chicago | Thu December 5, 23:10 CST | Toledo | Fri December 6, 05:45 EST | Fri December 6, 06:30 EST |
| R-613 | Detroit | Fri December 6, 00:20 EST | Chicago | Fri December 6, 04:30 CST | Fri December 6, 05:00 CST |
| R-614 | Chicago | Thu December 12, 23:00 CST | Detroit | Fri December 13, 06:00 EST | Fri December 13, 07:00 EST |
| R-615 | Chicago | Thu December 12, 19:30 CST | Indianapolis | Thu December 12, 23:30 EST | Fri December 13, 00:30 EST |
| R-616 | Chicago | Fri December 13, 06:10 CST | Milwaukee | Fri December 13, 08:00 CST | Fri December 13, 08:45 CST |

R-611 and R-614 both have a Friday 06:00–07:00 window in Detroit, one week apart. For R-615 the window starts on December 12 and ends after midnight on December 13 at the destination.
