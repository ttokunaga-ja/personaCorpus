# Day Count Desk Instructions

KCW-IC-20240820-DAY-01 · Rev 1  
Issued August 20, 2024 · Ronald Perez  
For day count staff and the count desk clerk

## Record the physical count

At the bin, check the bin number against the physical location and count the stock. Enter the bin number and observed quantity on the paper circulation sheet. Make the entry legible before passing the sheet to the fixed count desk. If a number or quantity cannot be established, leave it for clarification; do not mark it complete.

## Desk reconciliation

The desk clerk opens the same bin in Count List and compares the bin number and quantity with the circulation sheet. Resolve any mismatch with the person who counted it before saving. Once the two agree, Save the entry in Count List, place the completed stamp on the corresponding line of the return sheet, and pass the sheet on for the next count. The stamp follows the Save; it is not a substitute for it.

## If the count is interrupted

Return to the fixed count desk and check the completed stamps on the return sheet with the desk clerk. Use the stamped line to establish what the desk has completed before proceeding to the next line. A line without a completed stamp is not to be treated as complete. Check its status with the clerk and reconcile and Save it before it receives a stamp. Do not infer completion from an entry on the circulation sheet alone.
