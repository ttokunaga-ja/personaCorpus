November 5 — for Jennifer

Patrick said no to recording today. I accepted that and said I would take notes instead; I did not start the recording. But I never said that, if he chose audio, we would keep it for 180 days after the interview.

I had left the time limit until after the permission question in the opening and thought a person who declined did not need to hear it. That leaves them choosing before hearing the terms of the other option. I want to practice saying both choices and the 180 days before asking the first question about recording, then wait for the answer.
