# Paper sketch — bin line on return to E

A single page with two paper slips side by side. The comparison is about where the words sit. Both slips are for the same person arriving back in E after a Save in F. Below the title, write “For Jennifer · Nov 15”.

## Left slip

Above the slip, write “1. Bin only”. Inside the slip, put one line of text:

Last confirmed bin: F-12

Below the slip, write “Back in E. Where would you look?”

## Right slip

Above the slip, write “2. Add where you're standing”. Inside the slip, keep the same single line:

Last confirmed bin: F-12

Just outside its right edge at the same height, write “Aisle E”. Draw a short leader from a handwritten question mark to the space between “F-12” and “Aisle E”. Beneath it, write “Does E sound like where F-12 was saved?”

At the bottom of the page, write “Try reading these out loud before we settle on the wording.” The rectangles represent loose slips of paper. There are no controls, device outlines or count rows.
