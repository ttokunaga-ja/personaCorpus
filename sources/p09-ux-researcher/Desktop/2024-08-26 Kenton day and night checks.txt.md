The desk stamp has nowhere to go
August 26

Ronald's description after our August 21 conversation keeps bothering me. In the day instructions the counter writes the bin and quantity on the circulation sheet; the fixed desk clerk opens the matching bin in Count List, reconciles it, saves it and stamps the return sheet. A person coming back can go to that desk and ask where the stamps stop. An unstamped line is not completed.

At night the counter does the reconciliation and Save at the bin. When that person is called to another job there is no desk clerk taking over the sheet. Someone may have their own paper checks, but I cannot assume a return to a desk or a clerk's stamp. I had started writing “find the desk stamp” into the observation procedure. Strike that. It would make the day route the answer before we have seen the night route.

Keep the day procedure's useful questions: which bin, what quantity, and was the matching entry saved? Watch what people actually consult after they return. A paper mark may matter, but so may a colleague or the bin itself. Need Patrick's view on how we distinguish returning from actually resuming the count.
