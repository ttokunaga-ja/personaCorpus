23 July, after Wada's session

She selected the trip first. I nearly marked that as opening the stop detail. It was not: she only opened the stop detail after Mihoko asked where she had checked the state of the work. The receiving-party callback was inside the stop instructions. Replacement-vehicle confirmation was in the vehicle section; the access-permission response was in the contact section.

Observed first pass: she moved the trip number, loading bay, estimated departure, priority freight, recipient and time the next person had been contacted into the handoff. At 21:44 JST she reread it and stopped. None of the three outstanding items was recorded. After the question, she went back to the three locations and identified them.

Her account, summarized by Mihoko: 「これで引き継げると思います」 at the first stop; after returning she said 「荷受けに折り返すことが残っていますね」 and later 「最初は申し送りに書くことを追っていました」.

My inference: the handoff field may have directed attention to what should be written rather than to where unresolved work lives. I cannot infer that selecting the trip meant reading its stop instructions. Find an entry point that makes all three locations discoverable; do not assume a warning alone supplies it.
