February 5, after the four sessions

TC-02 said “完了しました” when describing why the training seemed finished. Separately, I saw their gaze return to the button. I looked at the button before asking them to point to what they had used, so my own attention may have cued where to look. The gaze alone does not tell me which text they relied on.

Tomorrow, write down the person's own state judgment and their reason before counting clicks or looking toward a control. Ask where they saw it without naming the button. Keep the first stop separate from anything reached after help; check the administration history only after the answer.
