# Why we booked by handoff frequency

CWR-DR-20260413-DEC-01 | v1.0 | April 15, 2026 | Jennifer Salazar

For Carolyn Davis, Mihoko Kaneko and the dispatch interview team · Carver Workplace Research LLC

## Decision

Brian Chen and I checked the allocation together today from 10:00 to 11:05 CDT. Reserve 24 follow-up slots for July. Give 12 to people who gave or received a handoff in at least three of their last five worked shifts, and 12 to people who did so in two or fewer. Count a shift once if the person gave or received a handoff, even if both happened. Confirm the five-shift count before assigning a candidate and check it again at the start of the interview. If we cannot establish the count, hold the candidate outside both groups until we can. A confirmed zero belongs in the two-or-fewer group.

Country, job title and skill do not set slot counts. The desks currently have nine US and three Japan openings in the three-or-more group, and eight US and four Japan openings in the two-or-fewer group. Those are scheduling figures.

## Basis

When Brian and I reviewed the earlier twelve interviews in January 2025, we found four experienced dispatchers who had also needed to check information outside the plan. That took us away from the idea that the problem belonged to beginners. Then we tried sorting by title. Two Desk Dispatchers, UX-033 and UX-042, had handoffs in one and five of their last five shifts respectively. One compared an earlier paper window with the current display. The other also started with the route and window, then checked Vehicle against the coordinator's answer.

The frequency split secures enough examples to ask what each person could establish and pass on, and where they had to go for evidence. Keep the contrary cases visible, including people who hand over often and still have to reconstruct the previous state. Compare the two groups within the July interviews. Keep the earlier twelve interviews separate in counts and tables.

## Open items

Carolyn and Kaneko should schedule within the reserved groups and refill any cancellations within the same group. Bring unresolved counts back for confirmation before assigning a slot. Use Brian's separate allocation sheet for openings and keep interview numbers and dates in the July records.
