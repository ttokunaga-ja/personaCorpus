# Ask what was checked

CWR-DR-20241029-OBS-01 · v1.0 · October 29, 2024 · Jennifer Salazar

To: Mihoko Kaneko and Ayesha Siddiqui

## Use with the existing interview

Keep the 13 questions in their agreed order and the 45-minute session. Use this margin during change and cross-checking (22–32 minutes). If the participant describes a return earlier, mark where it occurred and pick it up at a natural break. Protect 32–42 for what the next person received and 42–45 for closing.

In Adam's session I marked six returns to the R-308 route list. I can name both sides of four comparisons from his account. At 22 minutes I have a return to the same row, and at 27 I have an opened stop Note. The comparison in each of those two instances is still unknown.

## What to put in the margin

| Elapsed time when noticed | Information immediately before the return | Where they returned | What they compared with what | What they did next |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Minute and second, when available | What the person had, saw or heard | Row or field, as precisely as observed | Name both items and where the evidence for the comparison came from | First action or message following the check |

Ayesha, mark the time and the action while it is in view. Add the person's account beside the observation when the moderator follows up. If you can't establish the comparison target or the source of the evidence, write “Unknown” in that part and retain the observations you do have.

## Follow up at a break

Use the participant's own words. “You said you went back to that row. What information did you have just before that?” Then ask “What did you look at when you got there?” If they describe a check, ask “What were you checking that against?” Give them time to answer. Keep candidate fields, people and reasons out of the question.

## Distinctions to keep

| Scene | What to record separately |
|---|---|
| Changed window | Where the earlier timing came from, the current Window, and which value reached the next person. |
| Held route | What the status showed, who supplied any explanation, and whether anyone established who could release it. |
| Relief vehicle | The number visible in Vehicle and the evidence the participant relied on to call it settled. |
| Exception stop | Which Note opened, what the participant read if established, and what they actually passed on. |
