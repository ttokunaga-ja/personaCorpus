## Slide 4 — Table

Title: A second source names the same route

| Order in the account | What happens | What goes in the note |
|---|---|---|
| First | Dispatcher sees Held on the route list. | Route number and status. Reason open. |
| Next | A desk message repeats that route number and says loading confirmation is pending. | Reason established from the matched message. |
| At handoff | The participant says the route is held and names the pending confirmation and source. | Record the words used and what the next person can check. |

Speaker notes: Have the note-taker repeat the route number from both sources. If the message has no number, ask how the participant tied it to that route. A source for the reason doesn't establish who has authority to release it.
