# Overnight window handoff — read-through

CWR-DR-20241203-OBS-01 · v1.0  
December 11, 2024  
Jennifer Salazar  
For: Mihoko Kaneko

## Purpose

Mihoko and I will try a spoken handoff with Christine's six planned routes. The receiver needs enough information to identify the departure date at the origin and the arrival window at the destination, including both dates if the window crosses midnight. We are checking our wording together; these are planned examples, not participant observations or actual dispatches.

## Set-up and procedure

Give the speaker Christine's schedule and give the receiver the blank readback sheet below. Keep the local-date answer key with the person checking the readback, out of the receiver's view. Use one route at a time. Switch speaker and receiver during the exercise and work through all six routes in English and Japanese.

Ask the speaker: “You are passing this route to the next person. Give the route number, the origin and its local departure date, and the destination and its local arrival window. Include the date at each end of the window.”

Ask the receiver: “Tell me the route back in your own words. If a date is missing, ask for it before completing your readback.” Do not supply a missing date or correct the receiver mid-sentence. Record the first readback as spoken. Record a question and its answer separately, then record the completed readback. Only after that, compare it with the answer key. The receiver should not fill a blank by assuming which Friday or by interpreting “tomorrow” relative to the present moment.

## Observation and check

| Step | Where to record | Basis for checking |
|---|---|---|
| Speaker hands over one route | Note the route and what the speaker actually said alongside the receiver's sheet. | Check whether the route, origin and local departure date, destination, and local window with endpoint dates were supplied. |
| Receiver gives the first readback | Enter the first words in the departure and destination columns before opening the key. | Compare the spoken locations, dates and times with the corresponding route in CWR-DR-20241209-DEC-01 v1.0. A matching weekday and time alone do not establish the date. |
| Receiver encounters a missing date | Record the question and the speaker's answer separately; leave an unknown date unfilled until answered. | Check that the question addresses the missing origin departure date or destination endpoint date. Do not treat an inferred date as supplied. |
| Receiver completes the readback | Record the completed words, including any correction after a question. | Check each local date and the window against the key. For R-615, require both December 12 at opening and December 13 at closing, as well as the times. |
| Compare the two Friday windows | Keep the first readbacks for R-611 and R-614 separate. | Both are Detroit 06:00–07:00 EST on Friday; R-611 is December 6 and R-614 is December 13. Neither the weekday nor the clock time distinguishes them. |
