# Overnight window handoff — preparation draft

CWR-DR-20241203-OBS-01 · v0.1  
December 3, 2024  
Jennifer Salazar  
For: Mihoko Kaneko

## Purpose

Try a short spoken handoff using Christine's six planned examples. We are testing our wording together, not recording a participant or an actual dispatch. Listen for what the receiving person repeats and where a further question is needed.

## Procedure

Give the speaker one route at a time from the reference sheet. Ask: “You are passing this route to the next person. Tell them the route number and the destination's planned arrival day and window.” The receiver repeats the route number, weekday and window aloud. Record the words before confirming against the table. Switch speaker and receiver and continue until all six examples have been read in English and Japanese. Do not correct the receiver mid-sentence.
