# Overnight window handoff — preparation draft

CWR-DR-20241203-OBS-01 · v0.2  
December 6, 2024  
Jennifer Salazar; reviewed by Mihoko Kaneko  
For: Mihoko Kaneko

## Purpose

Try a short spoken handoff using Christine's six planned examples. We are testing our wording together, not recording a participant or an actual dispatch. Listen for what the receiving person repeats and where a further question is needed.

## Procedure

Give the speaker one route at a time from the reference sheet. Ask: “You are passing this route to the next person. In your own words, give the route number, the origin and its local departure date, and the destination and its local arrival window, including the date at each end.” The receiver repeats the route number, origin and local departure date, destination and local arrival window in their own words. If a date is missing, ask for it rather than filling it in. Record the first readback and any subsequent question and answer separately, before confirming against the facilitator's local-date reference below. Switch speaker and receiver and continue until all six examples have been read in English and Japanese. Do not correct the receiver mid-sentence.
