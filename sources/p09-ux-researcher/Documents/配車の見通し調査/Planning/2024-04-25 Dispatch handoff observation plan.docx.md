| Situation and starting condition | Information the person saw or heard | Next place or person checked | What was actually passed to the next dispatcher |
|---|---|---|---|
| Changed window: a delivery window has changed before handoff. | Record the earlier and current values separately, and where the person obtained each. | Record where or with whom the person checked the value, in the order described. | Record the value and any source or qualification actually passed on. Do not assume the current value was the one passed. |
| Held: the route is held and the reason has not reached the receiver. | Record the status and what the person understood about the reason; distinguish what was visible from what was heard. | Record the first person or source approached and what was learned, without offering a route to check. | Record what was said about the hold and any unresolved question passed on. Authority to release a hold is not established. |
| Replacement vehicle: another vehicle has been assigned and the receiver needs to know whether it is settled. | Record the displayed vehicle number separately from any other information the person reports. | Record where or with whom the person sought confirmation and what response was obtained. | Record the number and any qualification actually passed on; keep the evidence for treating it as settled separate from the displayed assignment. |
| Exception stop: a stop has an instruction that may need to reach the next dispatcher. | Record separately whether the stop was opened and what the person read in its Note. | Record any source or person consulted after seeing the stop, if described. | Record the instruction actually passed on, if established. Opening the stop does not establish that the Note was read or handed over. |

「Session approach」を次に置き換える。

Invite the dispatcher to reconstruct one recent handoff in their own words, beginning with what happened rather than presenting the screen first. Ask what they saw or heard, what they checked next, and in what order they consulted the screen or spoke with someone. Follow the information through to the next person. Let the participant name the sources and the terms; do not supply a likely field or communication route.

Keep the account of what was seen or said separate from the researcher's interpretation. For each situation, record the trigger, information available, next check and actual message to the next dispatcher as distinct observations. If the basis of a decision or an action cannot be established, mark it unknown; do not convert an unknown into an action that did not happen. A stop Note is not evidence of the reason for a hold. These four situations are starting points, not findings or a measure of frequency. Operation time is not a measure of study success.
