# Dispatch follow-up allocation

CWR-DR-20260413-DEC-01 | v0.1 | April 13, 2026 | Jennifer Salazar

For Brian Chen · Internal working draft · Carver Workplace Research LLC

## Decision

Discussion draft for 24 additional interviews. We can give each desk an equal starting allocation, or reserve the comparison groups first and then check what each desk can fill. Hold the booking instructions until we agree which rule to use.

| Option | Starting allocation | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|
| Country first | 12 US, 12 Japan | Gives each desk a clear target. Frequency coverage depends on who comes through each desk. |
| Frequency first | 12 at 3 or more and 12 at 2 or fewer of the last five shifts | Protects cases on both sides of the comparison. Both desks need to confirm what they can schedule. |

Count a shift once if the person gave or received a handoff, including when they did both. Record title as context for what the person was responsible for.

## Basis

In last April's working profiles, UX-033 and UX-042 were both Desk Dispatchers, with handoffs in one and five of their previous five shifts respectively. UX-033 checked the current Window against an earlier one on paper. UX-042 still began with the route and window and later compared the Vehicle entry with the coordinator's answer. We need room to hear both kinds of account, including people whose starting point runs against what we expected from their handoff frequency.

Country-first is easy to administer but could leave the lower-frequency group thin.

## Open items
