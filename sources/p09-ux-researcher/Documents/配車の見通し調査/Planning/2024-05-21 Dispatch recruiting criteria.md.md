# Who can walk us through a handoff?

May 21, 2024  
To: Carolyn Davis and Mihoko Kaneko  
From: Jennifer Salazar

For recruitment, we need someone who has handed over or received a handoff using a delivery plan within the last 30 days. A job title alone is not enough: the work may be distributed differently at each site. Please establish which side of a handoff the person has experienced and when, before deciding whether the experience fits.

Ask for a brief account of one exception and how far they can explain it using the screen or what was said. We are looking for access to a real sequence, not a correct answer or a particular problem. They do not have to have experienced all four situations. Avoid suggesting the screen field or communication channel we expect them to mention.

If they might bring a plan or other material, ask whether customer names and actual delivery destinations can be removed or obscured before sharing. Do not request the material itself during recruitment. If the person cannot explain their experience or we cannot ask about it, leave eligibility pending and note what remains to be established; absence of an answer is not a rejection.

Please keep eligibility separate from scheduling. We will settle names and available places later, after the experience and privacy questions have been considered.
