Hi Mihoko and Patrick,

Mihoko and I have finished aligning the prompts for the Japanese and US sessions. In our July 10 internal read-through we worked through 18 questions and removed five that supplied the answer or assumed a confirmation. The remaining 13 are in the same order in both languages in the attached comparison.

The shared interview frame is 45 minutes: 5 for the opening, 7 for recall, 10 for state and reason, 10 for change and cross-checking, 10 for passing information on, and 3 to close. The read-through took 51 minutes including our stops to reconsider wording; it was a preparation exercise, not a participant timing result.

Please let the participant use their own terms before offering any translation or label. Record the words they use, even when they differ from ours, and follow their account of what was checked and passed on. If they have already answered a prompt, confirm the essential point rather than asking it again in full.

Thanks,
Jennifer
