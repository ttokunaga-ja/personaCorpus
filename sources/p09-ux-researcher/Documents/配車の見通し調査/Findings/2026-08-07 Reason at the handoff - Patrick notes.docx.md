# Reason at the handoff

CWR-DR-20260806-DEC-01 | v0.2 | August 6, 2026 | Jennifer Salazar | For Patrick Delgado | Reviewed August 7, 2026

## Claim for review

Patrick, please look at both the causal language and the proposed implication before this becomes the client report. In this study, there were handoff situations that support considering a reason beside Held. The observations below support considering that placement, but I need your judgment on whether they support the stronger wording.

## Evidence behind the proposal

Jennifer and Brian reconciled 26 indexed scenes from 24 interviews on August 4. The count is people, not scenes. Fifteen people contributed observations supporting a reason display; their paths are distinct.

| Path | People | What the record shows | What it does not establish |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Reason remained unconfirmed | 8 | A vehicle type, stop instruction, changed status, another route's message, an earlier day's message, or a person to ask did not establish the reason for the selected route. | The reason for each hold or what happened after the interview. |
| Reason learned after handoff | 1 | The inventory check was learned after the route had already been passed to the next person. | That an earlier display would have changed the outcome. |
| Further question and answer | 6 | An additional exchange first made it possible to say what was still outstanding. | That every contact was avoidable. |
| Existing material | 4 | The reason was found by checking material already available. | That the person finding a reason could release the hold. |
| Supplement or freshness check | 2 | One person clarified who was expected to call back; another checked the current situation against an older record. | That the exchange itself granted permission to receive or release. |
| Told before viewing the list | 1 | The reason was already conveyed orally before the list was opened. | How a display would have been used in that handoff. |
| No hold encountered | 2 | The route stayed Ready in the scene described. | Whether a reason display would help when a hold occurs. |

The comparison nine are not a single group with no difficulty. The four who found a reason in existing material, two who supplemented or checked it, one told before viewing, and two who encountered no hold give us different boundaries for the proposal.

## Placement to discuss

Jeremy and I prefer a route-level reason beside Held in the list. Source and Checked at belong in the selected route detail; a stop instruction stays with its own Stops row. If the relevant reason is not confirmed, the value should be Unknown; a known contact can be identified in detail. Ready and Held remain the status values. This is a proposal, not a description of a tested screen or a change to release authority.

## Questions for Patrick

Would the proposed display reduce inquiries? This is a question to investigate in a future prototype, not a finding from these interviews. Neither use of the proposed design nor call counts were measured in these interviews. I would like the client report to distinguish observations from any expected improvement.
