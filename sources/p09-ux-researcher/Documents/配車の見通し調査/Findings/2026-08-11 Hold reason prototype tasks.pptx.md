## Slide 2 — Heading and text

Title: Give the recipient a handoff

Text: “You are taking over the route below. Look at the information and tell me what you would pass to the next person. What, if anything, would you check next?”

For each card, record the participant's own words about the reason, the next check, the stop instruction, and whether the hold can be released. Ask how they reached each answer.

Speaker notes: Do not define Held, explain Unknown, point out the reason, or tell the participant the correct answer before they respond. Present one card at a time. Ask a neutral follow-up: “What tells you that?”

Prompt: “What can you pass on? What would you check next?”

Recording: Reason and source: ______. Next check: ______. Stop instruction mistaken for reason or route-wide instruction: ______. Release permission asserted and evidence given: ______.

Speaker notes: The stop note belongs to the stop. A reason and source do not themselves say who can release the hold. Do not supply either distinction before the response.
