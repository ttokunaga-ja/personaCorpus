## Conditions for the readout

Show the current screen separately from the proposal. Ready and Held remain the two status values; Hold reason is the proposed route-level explanation. Unknown describes an unconfirmed reason, not authority to release. A source or a contact to ask is not evidence that the recipient has release permission.

Jennifer will prepare the anonymized count and readout on that basis. The 24 interviews support investigating the placement; they do not estimate the share of all users affected or demonstrate a reduction in inquiries. The August 12 discussion is an opportunity to choose what to prototype, not approval of a release or a change to permissions.
