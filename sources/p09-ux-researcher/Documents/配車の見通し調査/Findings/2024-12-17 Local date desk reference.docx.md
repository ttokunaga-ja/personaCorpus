# Local date desk reference
CWR-DR-20241217-DEC-01 | v1.0 | December 17, 2024 | Jennifer Salazar
For: Christine Wright and the Oakridge design team

## Handoff order
1. Say the route number, origin, and departure date and time in the origin's local zone.
2. Say the destination and the local date and time at each end of its scheduled window. If both ends share a date, say that date with the window; if not, name both dates.
3. The receiver reads back the dates and window. If a date is missing, stop and ask for the local calendar date before completing the readback. Do not infer it from “Friday,” “tomorrow,” or a clock time alone.

## Short examples
| Route | English | 日本語 |
|---|---|---|
| R-611 | Chicago departure December 5, 22:30 CST; Detroit window December 6, 06:00–07:00 EST. | シカゴ発12月5日22時30分CST、デトロイトの予定帯は12月6日6時〜7時EST。 |
| R-614 | Chicago departure December 12, 23:00 CST; Detroit window December 13, 06:00–07:00 EST. | シカゴ発12月12日23時CST、デトロイトの予定帯は12月13日6時〜7時EST。 |
| R-615 | Chicago departure December 12, 19:30 CST; Indianapolis window December 12, 23:30 to December 13, 00:30 EST. | シカゴ発12月12日19時30分CST、インディアナポリスの予定帯は12月12日23時30分〜12月13日0時30分EST。 |

If the end date is absent: “What is the local calendar date at the destination for the end of the window?” / 「着地の現地日付で、予定帯が終わるのは何月何日ですか。」

Source: Oakridge ODC-DSP-20241202-REF-01 v1.0; Carver local date answer key and December 10 translation review. Scope: reading scheduled examples, not actual arrivals or late-delivery counts. The December 16 outcome decision records why those counts cannot be estimated here.
