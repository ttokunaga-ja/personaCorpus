# Outcome scope decision
CWR-DR-20241216-DEC-01 | v1.0 | December 16, 2024 | Jennifer Salazar
To: Patrick Delgado

## Decision
At our December 16, 10:30–11:00 CST review, Patrick and I agreed that the six scheduled examples cannot support an estimate of actual late deliveries. We have neither actual arrivals nor an agreed population and late rule. An omission in a readback is not an observed delivery outcome.

## What the materials contain
| Material | What it tells us | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| Planned schedule | Oakridge's six examples give local departure and destination window dates and times. | Whether any vehicle actually arrived within or after its window. |
| Oral readback | In the December 12 internal exercise the Friday dates were distinguished; the R-615 window end date needed a follow-up. | A participant rate, an operating error count, or actual lateness. |
| Actual arrival | Not supplied for these six examples. | No matched actual time to compare with a scheduled end. |

## What a count would require
Define the operating period and movements to include; obtain each corresponding scheduled window and actual arrival with dates and zones; agree the late threshold and treatment of missing or changed records before calculating. Neither zero nor an estimate is supported here.

## December 19 explanation
Share the route number, origin local date and destination local dates and window, with bilingual examples. Show the different Fridays and the window crossing midnight. Limit the explanation to reading scheduled information; do not present the exercise as an operational outcome or a decision to change the screen or dispatch process.
