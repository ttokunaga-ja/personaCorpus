# Which checks count as finding a reason?

CWR-DR-20260803-DEC-01 · v0.1 · August 3, 2026

To Brian Chen · From Jennifer Salazar

## For our August 4 comparison

I propose counting people, not the number of times a route appears in the index. For each participant, follow what they had at the handoff and what they could say about the particular route afterwards. The three routes in UX-184 are separate scenes, but they are one person's evidence. Let's keep all three visible while assigning one person to one outcome.

## Proposed inclusion decisions

| Candidate pathway | Include in the recommendation evidence? | What has to be established |
|---|---|---|
| Reason not confirmed | Yes | At handoff the reason for this route is absent or cannot be established, and the account or walkthrough does not establish it later in the observed period. A vehicle type, stop instruction, status change or contact alone is not a reason. |
| Reason learned after handoff | Yes | The person passes the held route on without the reason; an answer establishing what was pending arrives only after the handoff. Keep the later answer separate from what could be passed at the time. |
| Reason learned through an additional question and answer | Yes | The handoff does not establish what is outstanding for the route, and a subsequent question or response identifies that outstanding item for the first time. Record both the starting information and the answer, with the source and order. |
| Existing reason checked or supplemented | Separate comparison | A reason is already available for the route and the additional check establishes its currency, date or a detail such as who is expected to answer. An extra contact alone is not enough to place the person in the third pathway. Do not infer that the entire earlier wording was verified. |
| Reason given before list viewing | Separate comparison | The incoming person receives the route and reason orally before opening the list. The screen check is not the first source of the reason. |
| No hold encountered | Separate comparison | The route remained Ready during the period described. This tells us nothing about how a hold reason would have been obtained. |

## Cases to settle

UX-173, R-405 at 22:05: Held was known at the handoff, but not what remained unfinished. The participant asked the outgoing dispatcher and heard that loading was pending. The stop instruction is separate. This appears to satisfy the additional-answer pathway; let's check the wording and sequence together.

UX-182, R-458 at 18:45: the participant had an old handoff copy with a reason already written, but the copy did not establish whether it remained current. The participant read the route number and Window back to the receiving desk; the desk said permission to receive was still pending. The answer is the participant's account of the call. The copy's specific wording is not established by that account, nor was permission granted. I suggest treating this as a currency check on an existing reason, not automatically as a first reason learned through an additional answer.

Brian, please bring any passage where my proposed starting point is wrong. We can settle the cases against the transcript and leave the source and the limit alongside each decision. No counts are final until we compare them.
