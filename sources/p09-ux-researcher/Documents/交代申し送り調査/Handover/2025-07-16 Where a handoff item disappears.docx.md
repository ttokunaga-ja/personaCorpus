# Where a handoff item disappears
CWR-HO-20250716-OBS-01 · v0.1 · 16 July 2025 · Jennifer Salazar

## Purpose and scope
For Mihoko Kaneko and Ayesha Siddiqui: on 23 July we will observe one incoming handoff with Norie Wada. The nine items represent work immediately after a shift change. We want to see what reaches the handoff note, what Wada returns to on her own, and what emerges only after a question. This is a pilot, not a measure of how often the problem occurs.

## Session and roles
07:30–08:00 CDT / 21:30–22:00 JST. Kaneko moderates in Japanese; Jennifer observes. Ayesha helps confirm the agreed recording scope. Before starting, confirm permission for operation notes and anonymized summaries of answers. Do not record audio or screen. Explain that Wada may stop. Use the handoff screen and the accompanying example; do not name the locations of outstanding work during the task.

## Procedure
Ask Wada to take over the shift and prepare the handoff as she normally would with the material provided. Let her choose where to begin. Do not announce the number still outstanding. Mark her first pass separately from any return she initiates before she says she is done. After the task ends, ask where she checked the status and allow her to return to the material. Record anything found after that question in its own column; do not add it to the first pass.

## Observation sheet
One row per item, in the order noticed. Record timestamps and the actual words or a Japanese summary; leave a field blank when unobserved. A gaze entry is only a tentative direction, never proof of reading.

| Time | Item | Apparent gaze (inference) | Operation | Item named aloud | Item transferred to handoff | Stage: first pass / self-return / after question |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| | | | | | | |

At the end note which of the nine appeared in each stage and which remained unaccounted for. A selection, an opened detail, a verbal mention and a note entry are distinct events; preserve their sequence. Do not treat the end question as part of the unassisted task.

## Close
Ask what Wada understood to be pending and where she would go next. Thank her and check that the notes contain no identifying details beyond what is needed to coordinate the session. Jennifer and Kaneko will compare the operation notes and answer summaries before interpreting the route through the screen.
