# Put the open items where I start
CWR-HO-20250731-DEC-01 · v1.0 · 31 July 2025 · Jennifer Salazar

## Participants and decision
Jennifer Salazar, Patrick Delgado and Jeremy Sutton met on 31 July to decide what to carry forward from Wada's pilot. We will develop the unconfirmed-items list as the leading proposal. It puts a route back to each outstanding item above the handoff note, with items that have a next deadline in time order and a separate, counted place for items without one.

## Why
Wada transferred six items on her first pass. Only after Kaneko's end-of-task question did she return to the vehicle, stop detail and contact areas and identify the remaining three. A banner can make the count noticeable, but it leaves the receiver to find three different destinations and closing it could be mistaken for checking the contents. A list can link each item to its source; displaying or opening it must not itself change the confirmation or reply status.

## Comparison and follow-up
We are removing the warning from the lead design, not from the study. Retain it as condition A for the September comparison; the list is condition B. Jeremy will prepare prototypes of both with equivalent nine-item cases usable in either condition. Jennifer will prepare the observation and questions so noticing an item, checking its source and receiving a reply remain separate. Patrick will review the comparison plan before sessions are run. The pilot gives us a reason to compare the routes, not a result for either condition.

## Open question
Whether the list helps a receiver understand that an item with no deadline still needs handoff, and whether opening an item is confused with receiving the other party's reply, remains to be checked. These are prototypes of the recreated handoff screen; no deployment decision is recorded here.
