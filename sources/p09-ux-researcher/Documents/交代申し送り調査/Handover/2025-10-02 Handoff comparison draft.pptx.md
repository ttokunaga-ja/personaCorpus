## Slide 3 — Table

Title: Missed at the task endpoint

| Condition | Missed | Possible |
|---|---:|---:|
| A · warning | 11 | 90 |
| B · open-items list | 7 | 90 |

Speaker notes: The unit is an item, not a person. Each condition has ten participants times nine items. An item is missed when it was neither mentioned nor recorded by the endpoint: save or the seven-minute cutoff, before the oral question. First-pass counts were 23/90 and 17/90 respectively. The July single-person pilot is not part of these counts.

## Slide 4 — Two columns

Title: Saved does not settle the explanation

Left — HH-03, B: The deadline-free item was treated as outside today's handoff, although it still required action.

Right — HH-10, B: The replacement-vehicle row was opened; the participant then described a reply as received although the detail still said a reply was awaited.

Speaker notes: Both saved within the limit. These are post-save explanations, not observations that the external work was complete. A also had two savers who treated closing the warning as checking the three items. We need to distinguish reaching an item, understanding its status, and receiving a reply.
