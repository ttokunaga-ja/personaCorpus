# What to mark before the probe

CWR-HO-20250918-RPT-01 · v1.0  
September 18, 2025  
Author: Jennifer Salazar  
For: Mihoko Kaneko and Brian Chen

Use this sheet alongside the two-packet plan. For each version, keep the operation, the participant's later explanation, and your inference in separate fields. A blank observation is not evidence that the action did not occur.

| Moment | Mark when you can observe |
|---|---|
| Close warning | In A, the warning is visible and the participant activates its close control. Note the time and whether any original detail was opened before or after. Closing alone does not establish that the three items were checked. |
| Open list | In B, the participant activates the arrow and the list becomes visible. Note the time and visible rows. Opening alone does not change or establish any item's confirmation or reply status. |
| Check original information | Record the item and its original detail when the participant navigates to it; note any visible reading or action separately from what the person later says. A visible list row alone is not evidence of opening the original detail. |
| Save | Record the save action and time when the handover is saved. If no save occurs by seven minutes, mark the seven-minute stop and no save within time. A save is completion for this task, not proof that all items are understood. |

## Two checkpoints, then a question

At the earlier of the participant's voluntary first stop and 90 seconds, record the items among the nine that have been mentioned or entered. At the endpoint, record them again: the endpoint is the save or the seven-minute cutoff, whichever comes first. An item not mentioned or recorded by that point is missed at that point. Keep each version and each case separate.

Only after the endpoint ask what has been checked and what remains. Write the answer as an attributed summary, then add any interpretation in a distinct field. Do not move information learned in this oral probe back into the endpoint observation or amend the endpoint missed count. If a source or state cannot be established, mark it unknown.
