# Two packets and one finishing rule

CWR-HO-20250912-OBS-01 · v1.0  
September 12, 2025  
Author: Jennifer Salazar  
For: Mihoko Kaneko and Brian Chen

## Purpose and setup

Compare the warning (A) and open-items list (B) as ways into a shift handover. Ten people each work once with each version. Give each person different cases East and West, nine handover items per case, with two timed open items and one without a deadline in each. Do not repeat the same case for the second version. Use the version of B with an opening arrow, not the earlier check mark.

Five people start with A and five with B. The schedule assigns odd session numbers A first and even numbers B first. HH-01–06 receive East first; HH-07–10 West first. Thus East and West each occur five times with A and five times with B. Keep recruitment codes and contact details out of the session schedule.

Mihoko moderates in Japanese; Jennifer observes. Explain that the person may stop, and record only the agreed operation notes and anonymized answer summaries. Do not record audio or the screen.

## Procedure for each version

1. Present the assigned case and ask the person to prepare and save the handover. Do not point out missing items or explain what the interface means.
2. Record the first pass at the earlier of a voluntary stop or 90 seconds. An item is missed at a checkpoint if the person has neither mentioned nor recorded it by then.
3. Stop at the save or at seven minutes, whichever comes first. Record whether the handover was saved within seven minutes and which of the nine items had been mentioned or recorded by that endpoint. Record a timeout as not saved; do not treat missing evidence as an observed action.
4. After the endpoint, ask what the person thinks has been checked and what remains. Record the person's answer separately from visible actions and the observer's interpretation. A probe must not change the endpoint count.
5. Reset for the other version and its different case; apply the same seven-minute rule.

| Moment | Evidence to record |
|---|---|
| Warning or list | Whether the warning is closed or the list opened, and when; opening or closing alone is not confirmation of the items. |
| First pass | Time or voluntary stop and items mentioned or recorded by that point. |
| Source detail | Which original detail was opened or checked; distinguish seeing a row from following it to its source. |
| Endpoint and afterward | Save time or seven-minute stop; endpoint items separately from the later account of confirmation. |

Completion means saving within seven minutes, not proving that every item was understood or acted upon. Compare missed item counts and saved sessions separately; do not infer a winner from one person's difference.
