## Review of 2 October draft

### “Fewer things were missed”

Red text — Patrick: Please qualify this headline. At the endpoint B has 7 missed items out of 90, but two people who saved with B still gave an incorrect explanation of an open item's status. Put those two facts on the same page so the smaller count cannot be read as an unqualified win. Label the missed-item denominator on the slide itself: 90 items per condition.

### “Completed”

Red text — Patrick: Put the denominator next to this table: the same 10 people tried both conditions. Define completion as saving within seven minutes. The difference between 8 and 9 people is too small to support a claim of superiority by itself.

Keep Wada's July session as the observation that prompted the design question. Exclude that one-person pilot from any chart showing change between first pass and endpoint in the September comparison. Her three items were recovered only after a question; that is not the same endpoint measure.
