# Hold the training completion signoff

CWR-TC-20260618-DEC-01 v1.0  
Date: June 18, 2026  
Author: Jennifer Salazar  
For: Patrick Delgado and the research team

## Decision

At 08:00–08:25 CDT today, Misako Nakayama, Amanda Shah and I separated the wording decision from the completion decision. Keep the present wording as the candidate for the next confirmation screen. Hold final completion until the heading and explanatory text can both be heard in sequence before the action to continue to confirmation.

## Why keep the candidate

In February 2025, five of eight new employees called the training complete before submission. Three applied 「完了しました」 to the whole training; two understood 「確認へ進む」 as optional review. In the June 16–17 retest, with a different group of eight and the same task and first-stop judgment, one person made the early completion judgment. The other seven judged their state correctly. The candidate distinguishes what has been saved from the submission that completes training: 「ここまで保存しました」 followed by 「研修は未完了です。確認画面で提出すると完了します。」 The comparison supports keeping it for further checking; it does not establish that every new employee will understand it.

## Why hold signoff

Amanda's separate continuous-reading check on a practice training screen this morning began immediately after intermediate saving. She heard 「確認へ進む」 before the heading 「ここまで保存しました」; the explanatory text was visible but did not occur on that reading path. A visual wording result cannot settle whether the same information is available in the needed order by continuous reading. These are two training-screen items, not a count of remaining items from the earlier dispatch review. Nor does this observation establish the cause of the one retest participant's answer.
