# Tsubasa return guidance — review decisions

CWR-TC-20250318-DEC-01 v1.0  
Date: March 18, 2025  
Author: Jennifer Salazar  
For: Patrick Delgado and Mihoko Kaneko

## Review

March 18, 18:00–18:25 CDT (March 19, 08:00–08:25 JST). Misako Nakayama, Mihoko Kaneko and Jennifer Salazar reviewed the practice material and the two guidance options. The six people in this return study were separate from the eight in February.

## Evidence considered

Four of the six found their own saved answer; two began entering again at the start and did not find the saved answer during the observation. In two cases the saved chapter was 取次ぎ. One answer was under 折り返し先 and the other under 対応期限. Both first opened 折り返し先; the second person went on to 対応期限 before confirming the saved entry. Opening the chapter alone did not establish that the saved question had been found.

The comparison showed the same chapter-only cue, 取次ぎ, for both cases. The alternative adds the relevant question heading: 取次ぎ／折り返し先 or 取次ぎ／対応期限. Neither option includes the answer value. These were discussion materials after the observation, not cues shown to the six participants.

## Decision and next actions

Nakayama chose to try the chapter name with the heading of the last answered question beneath it in the practice resume guidance. She will write the two versions on the guidance face of the practice material. Kaneko will conduct a read-through across a break and ask the participant to indicate where their existing entry is, recording the choice and the evidence from the entry separately.

## Open

The next session date is not set. This decision covers practice guidance only; adoption in the production training experience is undecided. Whether the added heading helps people locate their own entry remains a question for the next trial.
