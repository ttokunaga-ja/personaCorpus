| Condition | Observation |

| Excluded | A preliminary pass began at the position left after a click; it is not scored. Reading obtained by pointing with the mouse is not counted in the continuous pass. |

## What the continuous pass reached

The sequence reached the route list heading, Route, Window, and then the identifiers and Notes of stops 01 and 02. It continued to the identifier of stop 03, then the identifier and Note of stop 04. The ten reached items retained their relative order. Plan status and Vehicle were visible as column values, but their values were not heard on the pass. The boundary into the stop detail and the Note on stop 03 also fell out of the route.

| Item | Visible on the selected screen | Heard on the continuous pass |
|---|---|---|
| Plan status value | Held in the selected route row | Value not heard |
| Vehicle value | Van 16 in that row | Value not heard |
| Stops heading | Heading above the selected route's stop rows | Heading not heard |
| Stop 03 Note | “Call receiving before unloading” in the 03 row | Note not heard, although the stop identifier was reached |

Four of the 14 important items were missed. A visible value and a spoken column label should not be taken as a spoken value. Likewise, reaching stop 03 does not establish that its Note was read with it.

## Limits and next check

This result describes one continuous route through one permitted screen and selected route. It does not establish behavior for other routes, configurations or methods of navigation. After a change, repeat from the same selection and page-top start; listen for each column and its value, the Stops boundary, and the Note together with stop 03. No operational dispatch data, interview recording or audio is included here.
