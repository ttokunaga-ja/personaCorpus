# Keep the stop note with its row

CWR-SR-20250515-DEC-01 · v1.0  
May 15, 2025 · Jeremy Sutton  
To: Jennifer Salazar

## Decision

For the next design pass, keep the two route-list values with their column names. Under the selected route, expose the Stops heading before the stop rows; keep the stop 03 Note with the 03 identifier. This is a direction for the change, not a statement that it has been implemented or verified.

## Why not move all four to the top?

Amanda's pass missed Held, Van 16, the Stops heading and the stop 03 Note. Putting all four at the beginning of the list would make them audible earlier, but would detach the handling instruction from the stop it qualifies and obscure the transition from a route to its stops. “Call receiving before unloading” is a stop instruction, not an explanation for Held. A listener needs to know which stop owns it when moving through the detail.

| Location | Direction for revision | Check after revision |
|---|---|---|
| Selected route row | Associate Plan status with Held and Vehicle with Van 16. | Hear each column name with its own value in the route list. |
| Start of selected route detail | Make Stops available before the first stop. | Hear the heading at the list-to-detail transition. |
| Stop 03 row | Keep the Note after the 03 identifier and before stop 04. | Hear 03 and “Call receiving before unloading” together in that position. |

Check the list values at the list and the heading and Note in the detail; do not substitute mouse-point reading for that pass. We still need the revised screen and the result of that repeat before calling this resolved.
