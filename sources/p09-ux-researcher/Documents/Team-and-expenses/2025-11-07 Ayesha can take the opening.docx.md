# Ayesha can take the opening

CWR-OPS-20251107-DEC-01 · v1.0 · November 7, 2025 · Patrick Delgado

To: Jennifer Salazar and Ayesha Siddiqui

## Decision

Following the November 7 redo, Ayesha may independently lead the opening: explain the purpose and the option to pause or stop, explain the recording choice and its retention period, ask and wait for permission, and switch to notes without recording if permission is declined. Start audio only after permission. Jennifer remains the contact for questions about quotation beyond the participant's consent or use for another purpose; Ayesha should bring those questions back rather than promise an answer in the session.

## Basis

The opening needs the purpose and right to stop, the research team's listening purpose and 180-day audio retention period before the recording choice, and an actual response before recording. A refusal must lead to notes without recording. Where identifying information is about to appear, sharing and recording must stop, the description must omit that information, and permission must be sought again before audio resumes.

| Exercise | Evidence from the practice | Judgment |
|---|---|---|
| Nov 4, Jennifer as interviewee, recording accepted | Ayesha stated the 180 days before starting and waited for Jennifer's answer; permission preceded recording. Jennifer found the pause while Ayesha looked for Record ambiguous. | Pass for the recording choice; keep the equipment pause distinct from the request. |
| Nov 5, Patrick as interviewee, recording declined | Ayesha accepted the refusal and moved to notes without recording, but did not explain the audio retention period before asking. | Not yet: the notes transition worked, but the choice was missing the retention explanation. |
| Nov 6, Jennifer as interviewee, identifying information threatened in a share | Ayesha stopped the share and recording, asked for a description without identifying details and obtained permission again before audio resumed. The retention explanation was not missed. | Pass for the interruption and restart. |
| Nov 7, Patrick declining again | Before asking, Ayesha gave the research-team listening purpose, the 180-day period and the notes alternative; she waited for the answer and moved to notes on refusal without recording. | Pass on the missed element and refusal transition. |

## Scope and follow-up

The three exercises and the separate redo were staff practice, not participant interviews. They do not enter participant records or study counts. Jennifer can use the November 6 desk note with Ayesha; a request for a different use or a quotation outside consent returns to Jennifer for a decision.
