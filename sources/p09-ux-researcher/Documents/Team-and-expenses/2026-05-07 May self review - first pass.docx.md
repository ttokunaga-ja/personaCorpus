# Half-year self review: research decisions

CWR-OPS-20260507-DEC-01 · v0.1  
May 7, 2026 · Jennifer Salazar  
To: Patrick Delgado

## What changed my judgment

In January I withdrew the novice-only explanation for returning to check dispatch information. Four people who described independently handling routine and exception handoffs also compared the display with another source. The comparison partners differed: paper and Window, a prior dispatcher's message and Held, an oral vehicle answer and Vehicle, and an exception memo and a stop Note. The interviews undermined the novice label; they did not establish a replacement classification or a rate for job titles.

In October the question was different. The list remained a candidate for the next prototype because individual open items could be revisited, not because saving proved understanding. HH-03 treated an item with no deadline as outside today's handoff; HH-10 thought a response had arrived after reading the vehicle row. Both saved. I proposed separate checks of an action without a time and a response still awaited. Retaining the list for the next design pass was conditional on addressing those distinct questions; it was not a deployment decision.

## What I need to show in the review

I have collected six submitted reports, including two decisions from dispatch. I want to trace each change in interpretation to an observation on the page the recipient received. A count of three correct training judgments, for example, tells me how many, but does a correct-answer cell alone show what the learners used to decide? I have not yet answered that by reading the source responses against the submitted page.

My initial account gives too much credit to having made a careful decision. The stronger question is whether a recipient could check the decision from the page itself. I will bring the relevant pages and the gaps to our conversation, and distinguish what is present in the submitted versions from what I can reconstruct afterward.
