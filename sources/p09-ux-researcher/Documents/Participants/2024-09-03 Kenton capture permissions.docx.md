# What can leave the Kenton floor

CWR-NS-20240903-DEC-01 · v1.0  
September 3, 2024 · Jennifer Salazar  
To: Ayesha Siddiqui and Patrick Delgado

## Decision

Ronald and I agreed on the following boundaries for the two night-count visits. Each of the six people must be asked individually whether they agree to be observed. Ayesha will confirm and record each person's response; do not substitute a manager's permission for the individual's. If someone declines, do not record their work as an observation.

The planned windows are September 4 and 11, 20:30–22:15, with three people in each window. The window is the visit period, not a claim of continuous observation of each person. Jennifer will record actions around an interruption and the return to reconciliation.

## Records and images

| Record | Handling |
|---|---|
| Permission confirmations | Keep separately from action notes. |
| Action notes | Use N01–N06; do not include names in the notes. Record what was seen and leave uncertain details uncertain. |
| Audio | No recording. |
| Photographs | Ronald permits the work desk and posted notice to be photographed only while workers are away. Exclude papers and screens that could identify inventory, customers or individuals. Do not photograph people. |

Before taking a picture, check the whole frame, including its edges and the background. If an identifying paper or screen cannot be excluded, do not take the picture. The permission confirmation and the coded notes remain separate when we compare what happened. Ayesha, please flag any uncertainty about the boundary before the visit; Patrick, this is the scope I will use with the observer guide.
