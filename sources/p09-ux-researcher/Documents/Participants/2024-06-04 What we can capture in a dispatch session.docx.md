# What we can capture in a dispatch session

CWR-DR-20240604-DEC-01 · v1.0 · June 4, 2024 · Jennifer Salazar

To: Ayesha Siddiqui and Mihoko Kaneko

## Decision

At the start of each US and Japan session, ask whether the participant agrees to recording. Explain what we want to record before starting, and record only with their agreement. If they decline, continue without recording and take notes without treating the absence of a recording as a reason to press them.

## If identifying delivery details appear

If a shipper name or an actual delivery destination appears, stop screen sharing and recording. Ask the participant to describe the relevant features of the run in general terms instead. Do not ask them to reveal the name or address to reconstruct the handoff. Continue the conversation without the identifying details; confirm recording permission before resuming any recording.

Keep separate notes on what the person saw, where they checked next, and what they passed to the next person. If we cannot establish a detail, mark it unknown rather than assuming the action did not happen.

## July 10 read-through

This is a read-through between interviewers. Do not bring participant screens or recordings into it. We can practice the wording and timing without participant material. Ayesha, please use this boundary when preparing the session setup; Mihoko, apply the same opening and interruption procedure for the Japanese sessions.
