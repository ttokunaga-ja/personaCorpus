# Tsubasa February note permissions
CWR-TC-20250204-DEC-01 | v1.0 | 2025-02-04 | Ayesha Siddiqui
To: Jennifer Salazar; Mihoko Kaneko

## Decision
For the eight new hires scheduled on February 5 and 6, ask each person at the start of their own session whether we may retain coded operation notes, answers and short original-language excerpts for internal research. Do not record audio or the screen. Give Nakayama only counts and a summary; do not send individual notes or quotations.

## Before the sessions
The arrangement with the client is that the research team will ask each person separately and will not pass participation or refusal to the training-grading process. This arrangement does not itself establish an individual's permission. Prepare coded note sheets and explain the limits to the moderator and observer.

## At each session
Explain what will be noted, who can use it and what will be shared with Nakayama. Ask whether the person agrees before taking notes. Record the decision by code, separately from the observation. A refusal is respected: do not retain that person's operation notes, answers or excerpts. Do not turn the decision into a training result or tell the client who agreed or declined.

## Open check
Jennifer and Kaneko should confirm the applicable permission at the beginning of each slot, not infer it from the scheduling arrangement. If a participant changes their mind, stop retaining notes and clarify the request before continuing. No recording equipment is needed.
