# Start the clock at the interview

CWR-OPS-20260916-DEC-01 · v1.0 · September 16, 2026 · Jennifer Salazar
For Ayesha Siddiqui and Mihoko Kaneko

## Decision

Patrick and I checked the retention rule at 11:05 today. For audio with permission for research-team re-listening, use the interview's Chicago calendar date plus 180 calendar days as the keep-through date. An upload or a completed transcript is not a new starting point. For example, an interview on July 14, 2026 has a deadline of January 10, 2027. Calculate from the date, not by shifting an instant across the autumn time change.

## Apply the permission first

| Permission | Action | Deadline field |
|---|---|---|
| Team re-listening | Keep only for the authorized team and purpose through interview date + 180 calendar days. Close at the deadline unless an extension consent can be confirmed. | Interview date + 180 days |
| Transcription only | Delete audio after transcript and speaker verification. Re-listening retention is not implied. | No re-listening deadline |
| No recording | No audio to retain or delete. | Blank |

Ayesha and Mihoko: check the permission and interview date against the register before assigning a date. Do not enter zero or an export date where there is no recording. If an extension cannot be confirmed, treat the existing deadline as the close date. For a review on a given date, include recordings whose deadline falls on that date as well as earlier ones.
