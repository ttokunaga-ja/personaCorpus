Hi Christine,

> Are you proposing to change the two status values, Ready and Held, or to add an explanation alongside them?

Ready and Held stay as they are. We are considering an explanation for the particular route alongside Held, not a new status value.

> And if the reason for a hold is unknown, does that mean the person receiving the route may release it?

No. Unknown says the reason has not been confirmed; it does not say whether the recipient may release the hold. A place to check the reason is not necessarily the person with release authority either.

On August 12 we can walk through where someone would find the reason and its confirmation details, and the conditions we should check in a prototype, including how people interpret Unknown.
