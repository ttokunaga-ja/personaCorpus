Hi Jennifer,

Before we meet this afternoon, I wanted to put the handoff situations we have been hearing about in one place.

A delivery window can change while the earlier window is still the one passed to the next dispatcher. A route can be marked Held without the reason making it across the handoff. When a replacement vehicle is assigned, the number may be visible but the next dispatcher may not know whether the replacement is actually settled. And a Note on an exception stop can be skipped when the route is handed over.

Could the study follow the information the receiving person uses to make a decision, rather than just asking the outgoing person to describe their steps? I would like to understand where the information comes from and what reaches the next person. The attached screen reference shows the current planning fields for our discussion.
