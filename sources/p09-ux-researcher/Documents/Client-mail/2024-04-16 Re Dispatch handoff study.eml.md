Hi Christine,

Thank you for walking through these today. We agreed to use the four situations below as the scope for observing what reaches the receiving dispatcher, rather than treating the sender's account of the steps as the whole handoff.

For a changed window, we'll follow both the earlier and current values back to their sources and record which value is passed on. For a Held route, we'll see what the receiver knows and whom they approach to learn the reason. For a replacement vehicle, we'll distinguish the number displayed from the evidence the receiver uses to consider it settled. For an exception stop, we'll follow whether the Note is opened and read, and whether its content actually reaches the next dispatcher.

These are starting points for observation, not findings about how often a handoff fails. As we agreed, operation time will not be a measure of the study's success. I'll put the observation approach into a plan for the team.

Thanks,
Jennifer
