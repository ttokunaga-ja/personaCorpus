# Keep the place and leave the count alone

CWR-NS-20240919-DEC-01 · v1.0 · September 19, 2024 · Jennifer Salazar  
For Jeremy Sutton and Patrick Delgado

## Decision

Recommend a read-only display of the last confirmed bin above the count list. It is a reference point for finding where to resume, not a selection or confirmation of the next row. Keep the existing Save action. Do not propose an interrupting warning or the day-shift stamp circulation for the night process.

## Evidence and alternatives

| Option | Basis for decision |
|---|---|
| Warning covering the list | Closing the warning does not identify the row to recheck. N05 returned to C-12 after a colleague named it and began reconciliation in the same minute; a mandatory confirmation would add an action even in that case. |
| Day-shift stamp circulation | The day process routes paper through a fixed reconciliation desk. At night the worker reconciles and saves; there is no fixed clerk to receive that paper. We have not gathered evidence to justify adding a fixed role. |
| Last confirmed bin, read-only | N03 had last confirmed D-03 but had only entered a quantity at D-04 before leaving. A confirmed-bin reference can distinguish that boundary without declaring D-04 complete. The person still checks the next bin and count. |

## Conditions and limits

An entry alone must not make D-04 look confirmed or advance the displayed bin. Only a completed reconciliation and Save would advance the proposed reference. No warning, automatic row selection or automatic confirmation should be required. The six scenes show different cues, not a measured improvement from this idea. Day and evening shifts and night work outside the two visit windows were not observed; implementation details and effects remain to be tested.
