## Slide 2 — Two columns

Title: The day desk is a different route

Left — Day procedure: Physical count → paper circulation sheet → fixed reconciliation clerk opens the bin in Count List → Save → mark the returned sheet. On interruption, the desk mark provides the check.

Right — Night work observed: Worker checks the bin and quantity → worker reconciles and saves → leaves for another task → returns to find the place. There is no fixed reconciliation clerk to take the sheet. Personal paper checks can be a cue, but are not the day desk handoff.

Speaker notes: The day instruction is useful context, not an observed night-shift procedure to copy. The two routes have different custody of the reconciliation step.

Title: Return, cue and resumed reconciliation are distinct

## Slide 5 — Diagram

Title: One proposed reference, without changing the count

Diagram: A conceptual Count List area with a single line above it, labeled “Last confirmed bin: D-03”. Beside it, a three-step sequence: D-03 saved → D-04 quantity entered, not saved: line remains D-03 → D-04 reconciled and saved: line becomes D-04.

Right-hand explanation: Read-only reference to the last saved bin. The worker still checks the next bin and quantity. No warning, automatic selection or automatic confirmation is added.

Speaker notes: This is a proposal for discussion, not the current screen or a proven remedy. It preserves the difference between an entered quantity and a saved reconciliation, without adding a confirmation step to a return like N05's.
