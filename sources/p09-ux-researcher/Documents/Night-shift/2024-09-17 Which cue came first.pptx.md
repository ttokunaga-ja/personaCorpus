Title: A place is not a confirmed quantity

N05: Stop on way to C-12; C-11 last confirmed (21:08) → return and colleague says C-12 (21:14) → begin reconciliation at C-12 (21:14).

Right-hand explanation: N03's label identifies a bin, not its quantity. N05's prompt arrival at the line still has a cue: the colleague's words. Times are recorded to the minute.

Speaker notes: In N03 I initially coded the terminal and Brian coded the bin label. The visible input may have drawn attention to D-04; the notes do not establish whether the screen also helped orient the worker. We agreed on the label as the first observed cue identifying the return bin, not proof that the earlier input was irrelevant. The quantity was not captured. In N05 I initially wrote no cue because there was no conspicuous search; Brian identified the colleague's statement. We cannot tell from minute-level notes whether any brief search occurred, or infer zero seconds. The colleague's words are observed; we cannot establish what N05 might have done without them.
