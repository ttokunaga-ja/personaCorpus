# Return cue paper cards

CWR-NS-20241118-QST-01 · v0.1 · November 18, 2024 · Jeremy Sutton  
For Jennifer Salazar · 11:00 CST reading

Read the three cards in order. For each one, say what you can tell from the slip before describing what you would do at the shelf. Use the same starting point for each card: you've brought the tablet back to aisle E and are about to pick up your count.

## Card 1 — Paper prompt

Last confirmed bin: F-12

1. Which aisle would you look in to pick up the count?
2. What would you check next to decide where to start?

## Card 2 — Paper prompt

Last confirmed bin: F-12  
Aisle at Save: F

1. Which aisle does the completed Save belong to? Which aisle would you look in to pick up your count?
2. What would you check at the shelf before choosing the row to work on?

## Card 3 — Paper prompt

Last confirmed bin: F-12  
Aisle at Save: Aisle unknown

For this card, the aisle where the Save took place cannot be verified.

1. Which aisle would you look in first to find where to resume?
2. What would you compare before choosing a row? What would you do if those checks disagree?
