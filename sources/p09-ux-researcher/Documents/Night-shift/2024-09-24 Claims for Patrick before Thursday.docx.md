# Claims for Patrick before Thursday

CWR-NS-20240924-DEC-01 · v0.1 · September 24, 2024 · Jennifer Salazar  
For Patrick Delgado

## Review requested

Patrick, please check the boundaries below before the September 26 report to Ronald. I propose retaining N05 in the body alongside N03: omitting it could make the report read as though everyone searched for a place after returning. N05 instead received C-12 from a colleague and began reconciliation in the same recorded minute; no conspicuous search was observed. The notes do not establish that search took zero seconds.

## Claims and limits

| Subject | Supported statement | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Observation | On September 4 and 11, within 20:30–22:15 CDT visit windows, we followed one interruption-to-restart scene for each of three consenting night workers per visit. | The window is a visit window, not each person's continuous observation period. Six observed interruptions are not a night-shift incidence rate. |
| Paper | N01 used a check on paper; N04 found a check from a fold; N06 traced from the top to a check. | Three scenes, with different ways of using paper; not a count of all paper use. |
| Colleague | N02 asked a colleague; for N05 a colleague named C-12 at return. | Two scenes. N05 is a counterexample to a claim that everyone visibly searched. |
| Bin label | N03 checked the D-04 label after returning, then recounted and saved. D-04 had been entered but was unsaved when interrupted. | Identifying a bin is not confirming its quantity. The record does not expose every cue or thought. |
| Other work periods | We have no observation data for day, evening or night work outside the visit windows. | Do not generalize frequency or effectiveness to those periods. We did not measure time saved or the effect of the proposed display. |

## Proposed report position

Recommend only a read-only last confirmed bin reference. Keep the current Save action; an unsaved entry must not advance the reference or appear confirmed. Do not propose a blocking warning, automatic selection, automatic confirmation or transfer of the day-shift stamp circulation. The six scenes are a basis for examining the idea, not proof of its effect. Please confirm whether the scope and N05 counterexample are clear enough for the external report.
