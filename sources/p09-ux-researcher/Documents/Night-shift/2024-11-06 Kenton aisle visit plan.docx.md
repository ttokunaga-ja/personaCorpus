# Aisle visit plan

CWR-NS-20241105-OBS-01 · v1.0  
November 6, 2024 · Jennifer Salazar  
For Ayesha Siddiqui and Ronald Perez

## Scope and site access

We’ll meet Ronald at the south entrance of Kenton’s Chicago warehouse on Thursday, November 7 and Thursday, November 14. The observation window is 19:45–20:25 CST on both nights. From the south ends of E and F, watch what a counter uses to find their place when they take the same tablet across the passage and then return to the aisle where they were working.

Ronald, please show us where we can stand clear of the carts. We’ll work from the side and let the count run as usual. I’ll follow the Count List row and the Save action where I can see them. Ayesha will confirm consent and note when the counter comes back through the aisle mouth. If we lose sight of the tablet, let the counter carry on. There’s no need to repeat a move or press anything for us.

The September discussion gives us a question to follow up on: can a verified prior Save help someone find their place after a move? We’re observing the current Count List. We’ll establish the last confirmed bin from the Save sequence we can verify.

## Before the count stops

- **Start of check** — Record the scene code, time, aisle and bin where the check is in view.
- **Confirmed bin before departure** — Record the time, aisle and bin of the last Save you saw after the counter reconciled the count and before the tablet crossed the aisle mouth. Record quantity entry separately. If you can’t verify which Save was last before departure, write “Unconfirmed” and note what was out of view.
- **Tablet out** — Record the time the tablet passes the aisle mouth and the direction it goes, E to F or F to E.

## From interruption to resumption

| Field | What to record |
|---|---|
| Save observed in the other aisle | Time, aisle and bin for each Save seen after departure and before return. If the tablet was out of view, write “Unconfirmed” and note the gap. Use “None observed” only when you could follow the operation throughout and saw no Save. |
| Confirmed bin just before return | Time, aisle and bin of the last Save you can verify as of the return. Carry the departure value forward only if the observation supports it through the return. A Save in the other aisle belongs here if it was the last one verified. Write “Unconfirmed” if the gap prevents you from establishing which Save was last. |
| Return to aisle | Time and aisle when the counter comes back through the aisle mouth with the tablet. |
| First cue | What the counter looks at or refers to first, such as a Count List row, shelf tag or their own count sheet. If a row is open, note its bin and the action that shows whether the counter used it to get oriented. Holding or looking at the tablet alone leaves the cue unconfirmed. |
| Reconciliation resumed | Time, aisle and bin where the counter actually starts checking stock again. Keep this entry separate from the return time and from the first row or shelf tag consulted. |

## Debrief and evidence handling

Ayesha, confirm with each counter that evening before we take notes on their work. Explain what we’ll record and respect any limits they set. Use a scene code on the observation sheet and keep consent records separately at Carver. There will be no audio recording.

If we need a photo to show where we stood, ask Ronald and wait for an empty view of the aisle boundary. Keep people, count sheets, tablet screens and product labels out of the frame. Keep the photo and raw action notes with Carver.

After each visit, Ayesha and I will line up our notes at the point where the counter came back through the aisle mouth. We’ll compare what I saw on the tablet with what she saw on the floor and mark any gaps. Keep the action that supports a reading in the note, especially when the bin the counter looked at differs from the bin where they started checking.
