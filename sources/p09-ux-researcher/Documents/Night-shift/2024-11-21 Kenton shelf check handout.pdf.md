# Shelf check after returning to an aisle

Document: CWR-NS-20241121-RPT-01 · v1.0  
Date: November 21, 2024  
Prepared by: Jennifer Salazar  
Prepared for: Ronald Perez and the night-shift lead, Kenton Contract Warehousing Inc

## Pick up the count at the shelf

Use the existing night-count steps below when you come back with the same tablet. The shelf label tells you which bin you are standing at. Compare it and the stock in that bin with the line in your own count record before you return to the matching Count List row.

| Aisle of the previous Save is known | Aisle of the previous Save is unknown |
|---|---|
| Place the earlier Save in the aisle where it was made. If you saved in another aisle, keep that confirmation with that aisle. | Leave the source open. Start with the shelf label and stock in the aisle you have returned to. |
| At the shelf where you mean to resume, match the label and stock to the line in your own record. Go back to the corresponding Count List row once those agree. | Find the line in your own record that matches the label and stock. Open the corresponding Count List row only after you have that match. |
| Recheck the quantity against the stock at that shelf. Press Save yourself once the check is complete. | Recheck the quantity against the stock at that shelf. Press Save yourself once the check is complete. |

## Two situations to walk through

**F-12 was saved in F and you are back in E.** Leave the F-12 confirmation with F. On November 14 the worker in this situation compared the E shelf label, the stock and their own record, then resumed at E-22. Use the same check to locate your own next row.

**The shelf label and your own record disagree.** Leave the count on hold and ask Ronald. Tell him which aisle and shelf label you are looking at, what stock is there and which line in your record disagrees. Wait to Save until the mismatch is resolved and you have matched the row and rechecked the quantity.

For the lead’s walkthrough, use the shelf labels, stock, personal count records and Save in the current Count List. The aisle wording on the separate paper cards is for discussion.
