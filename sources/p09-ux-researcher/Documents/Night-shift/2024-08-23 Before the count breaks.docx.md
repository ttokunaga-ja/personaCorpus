# Before the count breaks

CWR-NS-20240823-OBS-01 · v0.1  
August 23, 2024 · Jennifer Salazar  
For Patrick Delgado's review

## Observation scope

Ronald has asked us to follow six night-shift count staff over two visits and explain where they return after an interruption. There is no fixed reconciliation clerk on this shift: the person at the bin reconciles and saves the count. This is a working guide for Patrick to review before we settle the visit procedure. We are looking at work as it happens, not asking people to reproduce an interruption.

## Before the stop

For each observed count, note the time, bin number, quantity being handled, and whether Save has occurred. Distinguish a quantity entered on screen from a saved count. Record what the person was doing when another task called them away and what, if anything, they left on paper or on screen. If a value is not visible, leave it unrecorded rather than asking the person to stop work for it.

## Return and restart

Note the time the person returns and the first thing they reach for: paper, a bin label, the terminal, or another person. Record what it shows or what is said, where that can be seen or heard. Treat the bin at the point of return as the restart position. On return, the person enters reconciliation immediately; note the bin and quantity being reconciled and whether Save follows.

## Handling the record

Use participant codes in the action notes. Keep the record of each person's permission separately. Do not interrupt an active count to resolve a gap in the notes; ask after the count if a question remains appropriate. Mark details that could not be observed as unknown.
