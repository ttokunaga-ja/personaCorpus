# When handoff is part of the shift

Subtitle: For Jeremy and Brian · Handoff given or received in 3 or more of the last 5 shifts

Use three horizontal lanes labelled “Outgoing dispatcher”, “Route list” and “Incoming dispatcher”. Read time left to right. Draw two paths through the lanes; each starts in its own box. The paths meet only at the information passed to the incoming dispatcher.

## Path labels and connections

- Upper path label “UX-037 · 4 of 5 shifts”. In Outgoing dispatcher, “Previous route state known” → “Start from the previous dispatcher's message” → “Pick out the pending item”. Lead the message and pending item into “Compare with Held” in Route list. Connect to “Carry the pending status and its source” in Outgoing dispatcher.
- Lower path label “UX-042 · 5 of 5 shifts · Route-first entry”. In Outgoing dispatcher, “Receive route and planned window” → “Relief vehicle still pending”. Arrow down to “Locate route and read displayed Vehicle” in Route list. From there, one arrow back to Outgoing dispatcher reads “Keep vehicle open while waiting”. Connect that box to “Coordinator's oral answer arrives” and then to the later box in Route list, “Compare answer with Vehicle”. From that later box, return to Outgoing dispatcher at “Pass the matched vehicle number as confirmed”. Label the source next to that box “Coordinator's answer, reported by participant”.
- Both outgoing paths lead to the same delivery box in Incoming dispatcher: “Receive the change, the evidence behind it and what is still open”. Connect this to “Keep the source or next contact with the open item”.

## Callouts to place by the checks

- Beside “Compare with Held”: “Held is visible. The message about the pending route came from the previous dispatcher.”
- Beside the first Vehicle box: “A displayed number can be available while confirmation is still open.”
- Below the paths: “UX-042 began with route and planned window despite handing off in all five shifts.”
- Footer: “Working boundary for the 12-interview read. Leave unknown counts unassigned.”

Legend: Solid arrows show the sequence through the handoff. The labelled comparison boxes connect the current display with a reported source from outside it. The numbers by the cases count shifts with a handoff. There are no numeric axes.
