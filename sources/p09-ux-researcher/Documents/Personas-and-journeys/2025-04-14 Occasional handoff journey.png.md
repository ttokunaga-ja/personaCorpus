# When someone is covering today

Subtitle: For Jeremy and Brian · Handoff given or received in 2 or fewer of the last 5 shifts

## Shared entry

Read from left to right. Start with “Incoming dispatcher needs the previous state” → “Identify the route and, if relevant, the stop” → “Recover the earlier account from paper or a message”. Place the question “Who last checked this?” alongside the earlier account. Connect the account box to “Which part of the plan needs checking?” and fork into the two branches below.

## Route window branch

“UX-033 · 1 of 5 shifts” → “Earlier window from paper” → “Compare with current Window” → “Pass current Window and where the earlier time came from”.

Place beside the comparison: “The paper supplies the earlier value. Window supplies the current one.”

## Stop exception branch

“UX-043 · 1 of 5 shifts” → “Paper exception memo from the previous dispatcher” → “Compare with stop Note” → “Tell the incoming dispatcher the additional stop condition”.

Place beside the last box: “The condition was passed orally. The participant described the paper and kept it.” Under the branch heading, add “Stop condition” as the scope label.

## Shared exit

Join the branches at the question “Anything still unconfirmed?” An arrow marked “Yes” goes to “Pass the open condition and who last checked or can verify it”. An arrow marked “No” goes to “Pass the current instruction with its source”. Label the exit branches “Handoff options”.

Footer “Start with the context available for this shift. Both examples are experienced dispatch staff. Unknown handoff count: unassigned.”
