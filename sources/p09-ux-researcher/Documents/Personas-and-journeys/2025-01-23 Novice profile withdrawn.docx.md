## Withdrawal note — January 23, 2025

Brian and I read twelve interviews yesterday. Four people who described handling ordinary routes and exceptions independently still went back to the screen to compare what it showed with information they had elsewhere. UX-033 checked an earlier time on paper against Window. UX-037 checked the previous dispatcher's message against Held. UX-042 compared the vehicle coordinator's spoken answer, as reported in the interview, with Vehicle. UX-043 checked a paper exception memo against the stop Note. The cases are recorded in CWR-DR-20250122-DEC-01, *Four experienced returns*.

I'm withdrawing this sketch from use as an explanation that sorts people by experience. The work it described still needs to be accounted for. At handoff, someone has to establish what the next dispatcher can act on, where that information came from, and whose answer is still outstanding. Keep those questions in the read. There is no replacement grouping in this version.
