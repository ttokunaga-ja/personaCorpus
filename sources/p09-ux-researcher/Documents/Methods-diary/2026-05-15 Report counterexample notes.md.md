# The correct answers went into one cell

May 15

In the training report I gave the two kinds of wrong answer separate rows: three people extended “completed” to the whole training, and two treated confirmation as optional. Then I put all three correct judgments into one count. That made the split in the misunderstandings easy to discuss, but left Patrick unable to see what a correct judgment rested on. Kaneko's notes distinguish TC-04 stopping after saving while saying it was not submitted, and TC-06 reaching the confirmation screen and pointing to Submit before stopping. Neither saw the internal checkmark idea. I cannot use their answers as evidence that it works.

I made a related omission in the reading-order handoff. I pulled the four missed items into the request for Jeremy. The decision recorded where those items should go, but I did not carry the Notes that were heard into it. In the same page-top pass, 01, 02 and 04 had their Notes; 03 reached the identifier without its Note. Moving all Notes to the top would lose the relationship that the passing rows already had. The record supports a targeted check in the same route, not a claim that the change works everywhere.

For my next draft: what observation under the same conditions differs from the one driving the recommendation? Where does that difference make the recommendation stop, and can the reader find both facts on the page where I make the claim?
