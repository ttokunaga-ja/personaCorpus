# Weekday cue trial

December 6

I expected asking for the weekday to make an error visible. Yesterday Mihoko and I found that “Friday, six to seven” fits both R-611 and R-614. The two cards name December 6 and December 13, but that difference is absent from the words the task asked the receiver to say.

Even if someone happens to point to the intended card after that readback, the match is not evidence that they understood its calendar date. I need to record the readback before showing the comparison table, then keep any later correction separate. Ask for the local departure date and the destination date with the window; check how to handle a window whose two ends have different dates.

This was our own reading exercise, not a measure of dispatcher performance.
