# Two bars I took out

August 7

I took the frequency-layer bars out of the readout. The 8/12 and 7/12 counts describe the people whose observations supported this particular proposal within two deliberately filled groups. As a headline, two bars invite a reading that handoff frequency or skill caused the difference. The groups were for recruitment, not a test of either explanation; a one-person difference cannot settle it.

Patrick's notes also caught the sentence about fewer inquiries. It is tempting: if a reason travels with Held, perhaps a recipient will not have to call. But we did not have these participants use the proposed display and we did not measure call counts. Keep that as a question, not a finding or an expected benefit.

Next time, vary whether the reason is current and whether its source can be reached. Ask what the participant would check next and what they could actually pass on. Record the distinction between knowing whom to ask and knowing the reason; do not cue either answer.
