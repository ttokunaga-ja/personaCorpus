Hi Brian and Ayesha,

Thanks for working through the prompts with me today. I've attached the slides from our discussion.

For our next practice, let's listen for the order in which each answer became available: what the person knew before opening the list, what they could read there, and what a later conversation added. Those may be different routes through the information, so let's ask about each point rather than fill the gaps ourselves. If the reason is still unknown, we can leave it there and ask separately whom they would check with next.

The eleven prompts are choices for the conversation, not a checklist to finish. In particular, let's give the person room to tell us what they checked before offering possible answers.
