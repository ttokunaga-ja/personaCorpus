# I gave him two answers to pick from

At 34:13 in Adam's interview I asked whether he came back to reread the status or to look for something the detail had not given him. I had already supplied two motives. He was checking that he had returned to the same route. That is neither of my choices, and it matters: returning to the row did not mean the list had supplied a reason.

Next time let the action finish. Ask what the person checked, then wait. After that, separately ask whether they learned anything new about the reason and where it came from. If the answer is no, keep no answer as an answer; don't turn the return itself into evidence that the reason was found.
