July 9

I said the opening aloud: “Think back to a correct handoff and tell me how you made sure it was right.” It sounds like an inspection. It asks the person to select a success and supplies “right” before we know what happened.

Tomorrow I will start with: “Think of a recent run you handed over or received. Can you walk me through what happened?” If the answer stops, wait a little instead of filling the silence with an example. Once the person has recalled the run, I can ask about a source they themselves brought up.

Mihoko and I still need to read all 18 questions together tomorrow. I have not decided which questions stay or go from this opening alone.
