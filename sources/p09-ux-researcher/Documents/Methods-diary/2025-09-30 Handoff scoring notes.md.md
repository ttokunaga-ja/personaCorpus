# My first column was too easy

30 September

I could score “done” from the save time: the handoff was saved within seven minutes, or it was not. That was useful for the task outcome, but I let the ease of that column stand in for understanding.

HH-10 opened the replacement-vehicle row and saved. Only after hearing Kaneko's account of the answer did I catch the mistaken reading: the participant thought the other party had replied, although the row still said a reply was awaited. Opening and saving did not tell me that the participant understood the status.

Next time I want to ask, after the work is over, 「何が返ってきたと思いましたか」— “What did you think had come back?” Keep the answer separate from the task record. Asking about that meaning while the person is still working could point them to a discrepancy and help them operate the screen; it would no longer be the same unaided task.
