# Ask before I touch Record

November 4, 2025

I played the person agreeing to recording for Ayesha's first opening at 3:00. When she paused to find the Record control, I almost read the silence as a moment when I should hurry up and agree. Those are different things: a person needs room to decide, and a moderator may simply be finding a control. I should not fill that space for them.

Ayesha explained that audio would be kept for 180 days after the interview before she started it. She waited for my answer and only then moved to Record. Pass for this opening; the pause did not become an assumption of permission.

Next time I check the connection myself, I want to say that I am checking sound separately from asking about recording. “Can you hear me?” is not an answer to “May I record?”
