## Scope

Fifteen prompts from the July dispatch interviews, one spoken prompt per row. The time is measured from the start of the interview, not from the start of the recording. The response is a short team-only summary, not a quotation. The 2024 internal trial of 18 questions is a separate exercise and is not included in this count.

## For discussion

Q12 offers examples before the participant names a source. Q13 offers two explanations for returning to the list, neither of which describes the answer. Q14 puts the origin and the person able to clear a hold together. Q15 proposes a causal summary that the participant corrects. I want to revisit what each answer actually permits us to conclude with Patrick before deciding how to use these prompts again.
