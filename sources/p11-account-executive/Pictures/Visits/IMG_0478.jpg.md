# Photograph
In the Waverley meeting room in Chicago, one paper example of an intake notice is pinned to a corkboard. The frame contains only the sheet and the immediately surrounding board. The sheet is legible and has exactly these three lines:

While the administrator is away

Note the time and site

Hold approvals for the administrator

Tracy permitted this single example notice to be photographed on July 30. No person, name badge, contact information or device screen appears in the frame.
