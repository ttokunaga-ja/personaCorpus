# JDH migration — what follows the rehearsal

A left-to-right sequence of nine teal boxes connected by arrows. Each box contains its week and label, in this order:

1. W1 — Roster & responsible contacts
2. W2 — Reconcile recipients
3. W3 — Confirm cutover procedure
4. W4 — Single-site rehearsal: day and night separately
5. W5 — Resolve issues & approve
6. W6 — First group cutover
7. W7 — Second group cutover
8. W8 — Stabilize
9. W9 — Support handoff

Immediately after W4, a vertical brick-colored line crosses the path, labeled “Requested four-week window ends here”. The arrow continues across it to W5. Beneath W5 and the arrow into W6: “Resolve rehearsal findings and obtain approval before live cutover.” Beneath the sequence: “Nine-week estimate starts after the initial roster and site contacts are confirmed; dates are not booked.”
