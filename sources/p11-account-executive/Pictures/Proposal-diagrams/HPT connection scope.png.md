# HPT — connection scope

A left-to-right diagram for the visit. On the left, a grouped HPT area contains two separately labeled boxes: “Terminal users” and “Administrator”. On the right is a box labeled “Halstead Branch Connect”. A vertical boundary immediately before the service box separates HPT's side from the offered service.

The upper route joins the HPT group to Halstead Branch Connect across the boundary with a solid teal line, labeled “Standard service connection”. It represents the ordinary external connection used to explain branch communications and central administration.

The lower route begins at the HPT side and runs toward, but stops before, the boundary. It is a brick-colored dashed line labeled “Dedicated private connection”. Beside its stopped end, write “Outside offered scope”. It does not join the service box; there is no solid dedicated route or depiction of an operating private connection.
