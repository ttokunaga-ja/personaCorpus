## Scope

The pilot ran March 3–17, 2025, at one Hollis site with a roster of 30 seats. For this review we examined the week of March 10–16 inclusive, using Denver local dates. A seat is counted once if it sent at least one operational branch notice or handoff during that week. Sign-ins, views and practice sends do not qualify.

## Weekly result

| Measure | Seats | Basis |
|---|---:|---|
| Pilot roster | 30 | One-site pilot roster reconciled to the seat detail |
| At least one qualifying operational send | 24 | At least one branch notice or handoff in the weekly detail |
| No qualifying operational send | 6 | Both operational send counts are zero in the weekly detail |

The source is **Hollis pilot activity - week of Mar 10.csv**, prepared March 18 after reconciling the service's weekly usage export to the pilot roster. It has one row per pilot seat. The 24 seats are 80.0% of the 30-seat pilot roster; they are not production usage.

## Use in the review

Hollis IT has assembled the material for the adoption review. The weekly detail does not tell us why the six seats had no qualifying sends. We should ask the shift leads before interpreting those six as a training, access or scheduling issue. The result is an input to the review, not a confirmation of an order or an instruction to activate production seats.
