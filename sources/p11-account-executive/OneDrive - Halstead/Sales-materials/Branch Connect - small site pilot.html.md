# Halstead Branch Connect

## A small-site pilot

A single site and up to 30 seats give a team a bounded way to work through familiar branch communications before deciding whether to expand. The customer identifies the participating site and maintains the roster. A central administrator imports the agreed roster by CSV; the people at the site can then select the intended recipients and send a communication.

### Try an everyday handoff

A shift lead can send the next shift a handoff message to the selected site recipients. The sending team should check the recipient selection against the agreed site roster before sending. Any follow-up to establish that a person has received or acted on the handoff remains the customer's operating process.

### Tell the site about a change in hours

A site team can send a change in opening hours to the people on the agreed roster. The same boundary applies: the pilot does not require a company-wide roster or send to other sites.

### Two records, different questions

The delivery log lets an administrator check that a communication was sent. It does not establish receipt or acknowledgment. A usage CSV is an export for reviewing activity across seats and a chosen period; it is not a substitute for the delivery log of an individual send. Agree the activity and period to review before interpreting the export.

### What is in scope

- One participating site, up to 30 seats.
- Customer-owned roster, manually imported by an administrator.
- Site communications and a review of sending in the delivery log.
- Administrator export of usage data for a separate review.

A wider rollout and its commercial terms are separate decisions after the pilot review.
