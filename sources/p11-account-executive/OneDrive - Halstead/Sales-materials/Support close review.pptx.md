# Slide 2 — Follow the record before distribution
Layout: Title and Body

Flow:

1. **Darren — establish the booking.** Open the December booking roster for booking ID, purpose, dates and reserved hours. Open the December 12 booking change for cancellation, unaffected bookings and the separate replacement reservation; the roster has no status column.
2. **Darren — establish work performed.** Match each ID to the session or attendance record and its actual start and end. A reservation is not evidence of time worked.
3. **Darren — reconcile the usage detail.** Compare actual hours with the deduction column for the same ID and month; correct a discrepancy and recalculate the balance.
4. **Michelle — review before sending.** Match the corrected detail and balance to the customer report. If the booking state and deduction disagree, hold distribution and reopen the change and actual-work records with Darren.

Speaker notes: The arrows run from booking and change record to actual-work record, then to usage detail and finally to customer review. Do not infer current status from the original roster alone. Tyler should follow the same sequence when preparing a close.

- The 2.00 h on MER-2418 came from the booking, despite the cancellation.

After review:
- Darren removes the cancelled 2.00 h and keeps the 1.25 h and 1.75 h worked.

Speaker notes: The January 6 review assigned the correction to Darren and the customer report check to Michelle. The January booking is recorded in January after it is performed; it does not repair the December deduction.
