# Slide 2 — Figure

Title: Three distinct steps

Diagram, left to right: Branch sends handoff → Next duty person receives and acknowledges → Administrator reconciles and retains the record. Beneath the first arrow: delivery log records sending. At the second step place a question mark: #24 requires individual acknowledgment after hours; a delivery record alone does not establish it. At the third step place a question mark: #34 requires seven-year retention in the service; viewing a record now does not establish retention.

Caption: Follow the evidence across the handoff, then across time.

Speaker notes: Allison, please check whether the current product records individual acknowledgments and retains activity logs for seven years. Divya, separately map the work a branch and its administrators would have to take on if it does not. Do not mark either requirement met merely because a nearby action exists.
