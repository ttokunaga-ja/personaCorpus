# Slide 4 — December 18: staff transition handoff
Layout: Table

MER-2419 | 1:00–3:30 p.m. | Nasrin, outgoing and incoming contacts

| Time | Facilitation | Material or question |
|---|---|---|
| 1:00–1:15 | Set the handoff scope | Each contact identifies work that needs an owner. |
| 1:15–2:00 | Outgoing contact explains an open item | A repair update has been requested from intake; the callback to the requester is still outstanding. What has been done and what is still awaited? |
| 2:00–2:45 | Incoming contact practices acceptance | Read back the pending update and the commitment to call the requester once it arrives. What contact details are needed? |
| 2:45–3:15 | Identify routes for questions | Where does the incoming contact ask about a missing update or an unclear owner? |
| 3:15–3:30 | Read back acceptance of open work | For each unresolved item, name the incoming owner and next action; ask the outgoing contact to confirm the read-back. |

Speaker notes: Both contacts need to attend. The callback is an exercise, not a claim that an update has arrived. Reserve the final 15 minutes for read-back of acceptance; record any item without an identified owner for follow-up with Nasrin.
