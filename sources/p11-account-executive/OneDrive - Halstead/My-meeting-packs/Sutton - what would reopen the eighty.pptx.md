- Use 220 as the renewal basis while the additional seats remain undecided.

Right column — Additional capacity / separate review
- 80 additional seats are a candidate, not part of the current renewal.
- First establish whether Sutton has budget available for the addition this fiscal year.
- A separate quote and timing discussion would follow only if there is a basis to reopen the addition.

Bottom line: 220 + 80 = 300 seats only if the expansion is approved and implemented. The 300-seat scenario is awaiting a decision.

Speaker notes: Do not treat the 80 as a renewal increase. Ask Reza about available budget before moving a quote or proposing a start date. The 80 is an additional capacity request, not a confirmed roster.
