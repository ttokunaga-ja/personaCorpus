Speaker notes: Use with Nasrin and the outgoing and incoming contacts. Walk through a single unfinished callback before applying the same questions to their own open work.

# Slide 2 — Start with the open callback
Layout: Title and Body

A caller requested a repair update. The outgoing contact has asked repair intake for the update, but has not yet called the requester back.

- What has already been done?
- What is still outstanding?
- What does the incoming contact need before responding?

Speaker notes: At 1:00, set the example up without assuming a repair update has arrived. Invite the outgoing contact to describe the current state first.

# Slide 3 — Outgoing contact: explain the state
Layout: Title and Body

- I have asked repair intake for an update.
- The caller is still waiting for a callback; no update has been passed on yet.
- I will hand over the outstanding request and the caller's contact details.

Question: Can the incoming contact distinguish the completed request for information from the callback that remains open?

Speaker notes: Have the outgoing contact say the status in their own words. Ask for the contact details in the actual handoff; do not put a real caller's details into this example.

# Slide 4 — Incoming contact: accept the next action
Layout: Two Columns

Outgoing handoff:
- Update requested from repair intake.
- Callback to the requester is still open.

Incoming read-back:
- I will wait for the update and then call the requester.
- I will retain the caller's contact details with the open item.

Question: Does the read-back name both the next action and its owner?

Speaker notes: Ask the incoming contact to read the action back to the outgoing contact. Correct any missing step before treating the item as received.

# Slide 5 — After handoff: where to ask
Layout: Title and Body

If the update is missing, the incoming contact asks repair intake for its status. If ownership or the next step is unclear, the incoming contact checks with Nasrin.

Question: Who will take the next inquiry, and where will an unresolved question go?

Speaker notes: Use this as a proposed route to discuss with Nasrin, not as a completed agreement. End by asking each contact to describe their own responsibility.
