Please leave all six as exceptions. The current service does not meet any of these mandatory conditions:

- #5: No automatic two-way synchronization. The customer maintains the source roster and can supply a CSV for manual import; that is not sync.
- #14: No delegation of user administration restricted to a single site. A central administrator can process site requests.
- #24: The delivery log records sending, not an individual recipient's acknowledgment. A callback would be a customer process.
- #34: Seven-year retention within the service is not available. An administrator can export CSV; Aldridge would manage its own storage, access and retention.
- #43: We cannot offer an all-site cutover in one night. Divya's sequence checks one site before proceeding to others.
- #51: We cannot offer a 30-minute overnight initial response. Overnight issues can be received for contact on the next business day.

These alternatives describe the boundary, not compliance with the requirements.
