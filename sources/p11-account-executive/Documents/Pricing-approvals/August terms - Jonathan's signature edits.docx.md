# JDH August terms — signature wording for review

HBC-JDH-20260714-LEG-01 v0.2 | July 15, 2026
Jonathan Salgado to Michelle Whitaker | Account: JDH | Internal working draft

## Proposed term and seats

The annual term begins August 1, 2026 and ends July 31, 2027. The contract ceiling is 520 seats. The existing 480 seats will continue from August 1, 2026. The additional 40 seats will be assigned in stages, in two groups, after JDH confirms the roster and site responsible persons, day and night rehearsals are completed, findings are resolved, and JDH approves the cutover. Divya will check readiness; the second group requires its own confirmation before assignment. Neither an assignment date nor the number of seats in each group is fixed by the term start.

## Annual consideration and support

The annual consideration is USD 248,000 and includes 60 support hours for the annual term. The 60 hours are a shared balance for contact orientation, remote rehearsal support and post-launch review. On-site work and actual seat assignment are outside the support scope; out-of-scope work and work beyond the balance require a separate quote and JDH's written approval before starting.

## Written acceptance

JDH purchasing's written acceptance must identify the issue number and version of the annual terms and match the copy issued by Halstead. Retain Donald's written approval for Halstead and Janet's written acceptance for JDH as evidence of both parties' agreement to that same issue and version.

## Review note for Michelle

The term start and the release of the additional seats are separate. Please carry the issue number and version through to the purchasing confirmation and retain both written approvals with the issued terms. Price authorization and its deadline require separate confirmation before customer release.
