# Aldridge RFP: decision on a conditional technical response

HBC-ALD-20241021-RFP-01 v0.2 | October 21, 2024  
Prepared by Michelle Whitaker | Account: Aldridge Service Group  
Decision requested from Donald Ramirez

## Decision requested

The technical response to ASG-RFP-20241008-01 v1.0 is due October 23 at 4:00 p.m. Denver. Of 56 requirements, 32 are mandatory and 24 are desired. Allison has confirmed that six mandatory requirements are not met by the current service. The other 26 mandatory and 24 desired items have technical answers. I recommend submitting those 50 answers with the following six expressly identified as exceptions. Aldridge decides whether an exception is acceptable in its evaluation.

## Mandatory exceptions

| Requirement | Current boundary and possible operation | Status |
|---|---|---|
| #5 Two-way directory sync | Customer maintains its source and provides a CSV for manual import; this is not automatic two-way synchronization. | Exception |
| #14 Site-level delegated user administration | Central administrator handles site requests; authority cannot be restricted and delegated by site. | Exception |
| #24 Individual acknowledgment on after-hours handoffs | Delivery log shows sending, not each person's acknowledgment; customer may arrange callbacks. | Exception |
| #34 Seven-year in-service retention of activity logs | Administrator can export CSV; Aldridge must determine custody, access and retention. Seven-year storage in the service is unavailable. | Exception |
| #43 Single-night cutover across all sites | Divya's proposed sequence checks a roster and then proceeds site by site after the first site's procedure is reviewed. | Exception |
| #51 30-minute initial response overnight | Overnight issues can be received for contact on the next business day; that is not a 30-minute overnight response. | Exception |

## Options

| Option | Consequence |
|---|---|
| Do not bid | Avoids submitting against unmet mandatory conditions, but gives Aldridge no technical response to evaluate. |
| Submit 50 answers and six explicit exceptions | Lets Aldridge evaluate the available service and the gaps; it may reject the exceptions. Alternatives describe boundaries, not compliance. |

## Recommendation and limits

Approve the second option only as a conditional technical response. I will identify the six exceptions by number in the response and cross-reference, without stating full compliance. I am not asking for authority to commit to a price, contract terms, a new feature, or a delivery date. Commercial terms are a separate discussion. This memo requests a decision; it does not record approval.
