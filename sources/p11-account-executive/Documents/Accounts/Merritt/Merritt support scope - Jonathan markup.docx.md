# Merritt annual support scope — review draft

Document: HBC-MER-20251118-SVC-01 v0.2  
Issued: November 18, 2025  
Prepared by: Michelle Whitaker  
Account: Merritt Equipment Repair Inc

## Scope of services

The annual allowance is 40 hours. Time spent on the following services is recorded against that allowance.

| Service | Halstead responsibility | Merritt responsibility |
|---|---|---|
| Administrator consultation | Answer administrator questions and document the outcome. | Bring questions and identify the administrator attending. |
| Workflow organization | Review the current workflow with Merritt and help organize the work. | Describe the workflow and identify the people involved. |
| Staff transition handoff | Support the handoff when responsibilities change. | Identify the incoming and outgoing contacts and supply handoff context. |

## Operating review

Merritt should identify its busy periods early so that support can be scheduled within the annual allowance. Actual support time is recorded against the allowance. Additional work requires a separate quotation and Merritt's written approval before work begins.
