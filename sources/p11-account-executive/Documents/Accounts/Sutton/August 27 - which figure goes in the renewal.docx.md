# Sutton — which figure goes in the renewal

Document: HBC-SUT-20260827-ADP-01 v1.0  |  Issued: August 27, 2026
Prepared by: Michelle Whitaker  |  Account: Sutton Wholesale Foods LLC
For: Michelle Whitaker and Bradley Nguyen

## Baseline

The current Sutton agreement covers 220 seats. Use that figure for the renewal. The additional 80 seats Reza Moradi asked us to consider are a separate expansion candidate. The 300-seat figure describes the combined position only if those 80 are approved and added; it is not the current contracted or renewal seat count.

| Figure | Meaning | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| 220 | Current contracted seats | Renewal baseline |
| 80 | Candidate additional seats | Keep separate and on hold |
| 300 | 220 plus 80 if expansion is implemented | Conditional scenario, not the renewal baseline |

## Decision and evidence

On August 12, 2025, Reza confirmed that Sutton could not secure the additional budget for the 80 seats that year and asked to return to the proposal in the next budget round. We kept the 220-seat agreement separate from that request.

At the August 27, 2026 review, Reza confirmed that approval for the additional budget had again not been obtained. His 1:36 p.m. email confirms that Sutton will keep the current 220 and asks that the 300-seat scenario remain outside the renewal. The prior Seat Expansion Comparison separates the current seats from the additional candidate; it is not an approved expansion quote.

## Follow-up

Bradley: carry 220 as the seat basis when following the renewal history. Do not add an expansion prospect to renewal revenue or treat 300 as an agreed quantity. Use the existing agreement for renewal terms; this note does not establish a new renewal amount.

Michelle: leave the 80-seat candidate on hold. Reopen it as a separate item only if Reza provides a basis for additional budget. Then confirm the additional need and prepare any separate quote and timing discussion on that basis. Neither a price for the additional seats nor a start date is agreed here.
