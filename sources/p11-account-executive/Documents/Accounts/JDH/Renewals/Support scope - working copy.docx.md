# JDH renewal — scope of services

Document: HBC-JDH-20250414-SVC-01 | April 14, 2025 | v0.1  
Prepared by: Michelle Whitaker | Account: JDH Route Services Inc  
Internal working copy for Jonathan Salgado and Darren Vasquez

## Service scope

Beginning May 1, 2025, Halstead will provide four operational explanation sessions of 60 minutes each for JDH's department contacts. Sessions cover the handoff process, recipient routing and how to handle operational exceptions. The schedule will be coordinated with JDH's contacts as their groups prepare.

For additional seats, Halstead will configure and assign seats as groups become ready. Jonathan and Darren: please review this wording and confirm who owns the roster and actual assignment, and whether this describes the intended work accurately.

## Responsibilities

JDH will identify the department contacts and provide the information needed to prepare each group. Halstead will provide guidance on the operating process and coordinate the sessions. The existing 420-seat pool should be reviewed separately from the proposed 60 additional seats. The new groups are 36 and 24; readiness depends on a roster, a responsible owner and a practice slot for each group.

The sessions are expected to be remote. Please confirm whether an on-site visit is included when a department needs help, and where the boundary should be stated. This draft does not settle that question.

## Operational reviews

Halstead and JDH will hold reviews 30 and 60 days after the May 1 start to discuss readiness, outstanding questions and follow-up owners. Janet will coordinate JDH contacts; Darren and Divya will coordinate Halstead support. The September 2024 company-wide 80% determination remains open and is not a prerequisite represented as achieved in this scope.
