# Service Extension — May through July 2026

Document: HBC-JDH-20260417-REN-01 v1.0  
Issued: April 17, 2026  
Prepared by: Michelle Whitaker, Halstead Branch Communications Inc  
For: Janet Morales, Purchasing Lead, JDH Route Services Inc

## Extension terms

Halstead Branch Communications Inc (“Halstead”) offers JDH Route Services Inc (“JDH”) the following extension of its existing service. The parties' written agreement establishes this extension.

| Term | Value | Application |
|---|---|---|
| Extension period | May 1–July 31, 2026 | Coverage begins after the current term ends April 30, 2026. |
| Seats | 480 | Continue the existing seat limit; JDH remains responsible for its rosters and assignments. |
| Operating support | Current scope | Continue within the existing service and operating support scope described in HBC-JDH-20250417-SVC-01 v1.0. Halstead provides procedural guidance; JDH manages its departmental rosters and seat assignments. |
| Extension consideration | USD 58,000 | One charge for the three-month extension, invoiced once. |

The USD 58,000 is consideration for this separate extension only. It is not part of any annual renewal price. Expansion migration is outside this extension; any annual renewal or expansion is subject to separate terms and decisions.

## Expiration and agreement

This extension ends on July 31, 2026 and does not renew automatically. It is effective upon written agreement by both Halstead and JDH to these terms. No annual renewal or migration start date is established by this extension.
