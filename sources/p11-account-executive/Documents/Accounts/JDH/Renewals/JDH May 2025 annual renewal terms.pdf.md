## Seat readiness and support

JDH will manage the departmental rosters and assignments. The existing 420-seat pool remains subject to departmental review for reassignment. Up to 60 seats for new work are organized into groups of 36 and 24; each group may be assigned when its roster, responsible contact and practice slot are ready. The contract limit does not require all seats to be assigned at the start.

The scope of operating support, responsibilities and exclusions are set out in HBC-JDH-20250417-SVC-01 v1.0. The annual amount above includes that support.

Please return JDH's purchasing confirmation by the offer deadline. The timing of departmental assignments will be coordinated separately with the support team.
