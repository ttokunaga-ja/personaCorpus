The additional 40 seats will be allocated in two stages after Divya confirms readiness and JDH confirms its roster and site owners, completes day and night rehearsals, resolves the issues identified, and approves the releases. JDH will provide the roster and its operational approvals. The allocation dates and the number in each group remain to be determined.
