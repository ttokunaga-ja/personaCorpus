# JDH August renewal — how the 60 hours work

HBC-JDH-20260716-SVC-01 v1.0 | July 16, 2026  
Prepared by Michelle Whitaker | Account: JDH Route Services Inc  
For Janet Morales, Purchasing

## Service scope

For the annual term August 1, 2026 through July 31, 2027, the renewal includes 60 hours of support. The following allocation is a planning guide for the three uses, not three separate balances.

| Use | Planned hours | Included work |
|---|---:|---|
| Contact orientation | 24 | Remote explanations for department contacts on handoffs, recipient routing and operating questions. |
| Remote rehearsal support | 20 | Remote assistance with day and night practice and questions arising before a group moves. |
| Post-launch review | 16 | Review operating questions and outstanding follow-up after a group is in use. |
| Total | 60 | One shared annual balance across all three uses. |

Hours can be used across departments and uses as needed from the shared balance. The planning split does not reserve or separately cap hours for any one use. A booking alone does not reduce the balance; only support actually performed is recorded.

## Responsibilities

JDH supplies and manages the roster and seat assignments and identifies the appropriate contacts. Halstead provides the remote guidance and review described above. Support under this scope does not itself authorize or perform a seat assignment. Janet is the purchasing contact for the monthly record; Darren Vasquez is the Halstead support lead.

## Operating review and remaining balance

Darren records the date, use and time performed in 0.25-hour units, along with the remaining shared balance, and shares the detail with Janet monthly. Janet can raise any discrepancy with Darren for review. The balance is for this annual term and is tracked separately from the May–July extension and the January migration estimate.

On-site work and actual seat assignment are outside this scope. If JDH requests out-of-scope work or work beyond the 60-hour balance, Halstead will provide a separate quote and obtain JDH's written approval before starting that work.
