# JDH May renewal — scope of services

Document: HBC-JDH-20250417-SVC-01 | Issued: April 17, 2025 | v1.0  
Prepared by: Michelle Whitaker | Account: JDH Route Services Inc  
For: Janet Morales, Purchasing Lead

## Service scope

Beginning May 1, 2025, Halstead Branch Communications Inc will provide four remote operational explanation sessions, each 60 minutes, for JDH's department contacts. The sessions cover the handoff process, recipient routing and operational exceptions. Halstead and JDH will coordinate the session schedule with the department contacts as their groups prepare.

The proposed annual renewal runs from May 1, 2025 through April 30, 2026, with a contract limit of 480 seats. The annual amount of USD 232,000 includes the operating support described here. This document accompanies renewal terms HBC-JDH-20250417-REN-01 v1.0.

## Responsibilities and seat readiness

JDH's designated contacts manage the departmental rosters and assign seats. Janet Morales coordinates the JDH contacts and roster information. Halstead provides procedural guidance and confirms readiness with the contacts; Darren Vasquez and Divya Rao coordinate the support work.

JDH's departments will review the existing 420-seat pool separately for possible reassignment. For the new work, up to 60 additional seats are organized into a group of 36 and a group of 24. Each group can be assigned when its roster, responsible contact and practice slot are ready. The groups need not be ready at the same time, and the 480-seat limit does not require assignment of every seat at the start.

On-site work and bulk assignment of unused seats are outside this scope. Halstead's procedural guidance does not transfer roster management or seat assignment from JDH.

## Operational reviews

Halstead and JDH will review progress 30 and 60 days after the May 1 start. At each review, the contacts will discuss group readiness, outstanding questions and follow-up owners. The September 2024 company-wide 80% determination remains open; it is not represented as an achieved result or used as the basis for the additional seats.
