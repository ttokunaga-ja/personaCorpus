# June 24 — Keep one support balance

HBC-JDH-20260624-WRK-01 v1.0 | June 24, 2026
Prepared by Michelle Whitaker for Janet Morales and Darren Vasquez
Meeting: June 24, 2026, 11:00 MDT
Attendees: Janet Morales, JDH purchasing; Darren Vasquez, Halstead support; Michelle Whitaker, Halstead account executive.

## Decisions

The proposed annual 60 hours will be managed as one shared balance across the three support uses. The planning allocation is 24 hours for contact orientation, 20 for remote rehearsal support and 16 for post-launch review. These figures help plan capacity; they are not separate use-it-or-lose-it balances.

Only work performed is recorded, in 0.25-hour units. A reservation alone does not reduce the balance. Darren will record the date, use, time performed and remaining balance and share the detail with Janet monthly. The annual balance is separate from the current extension and the earlier migration estimate.

On-site work and the actual assignment of seats are outside this support scope. For work outside scope or beyond 60 hours, Halstead will provide a separate quote and obtain JDH's written approval before starting.

## Owners and follow-up

| Owner | Responsibility | When |
|---|---|---|
| Darren | Maintain the actual-time detail and remaining shared balance | Monthly during the annual term |
| Janet | Review the monthly detail and raise discrepancies with Darren | On receipt of each monthly record |
| Michelle | Reflect the agreed method and exclusions in the customer support scope | Before the annual terms are issued |

The allocation is a planning guide; the record of actual work determines the remaining balance. The discussion did not set dates for assigning the additional seats.
