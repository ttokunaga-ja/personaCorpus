# JDH workshop decisions

Document: HBC-JDH-20240712-WRK-02 | v1.0 | Issued: July 12, 2024  
Author: Michelle Whitaker | Account: JDH Route Services Inc

## Meeting and decisions

July 12, 2024, 10:00–11:30 a.m., JDH conference room, Denver.

Attendees: Janet Morales, Morgan Hale, Caleb Dunn, Renee Benton and Nora Webb of JDH; Michelle Whitaker and Divya Rao of Halstead Branch Communications Inc.

We agreed to work through examples with a contact in each department instead of relying on one company-wide contact and a single joint demonstration. The April baseline was 318 of 420 contract seats with at least one business use in the March 23–April 21 window. The unused seats require investigation by department; the count does not itself explain the reasons for non-use.

## Department owners and actions

Each department owner will provide the following by July 26, 2024.

| Department | Owner | Example or check to provide |
|---|---|---|
| Dispatch | Morgan Hale | A notification example used to hand off a dispatch. |
| Field Operations | Caleb Dunn | An initial practice immediately after the night-shift handoff and one business-use example for that practice. |
| Customer Care | Renee Benton | Check the recipients for an inquiry and provide the routing example. |
| Billing | Nora Webb | An example of an exception communication for a returned invoice. |

## Collection and check-in

| Owner | Action | Due |
|---|---|---|

For the September 12 check, use the same definition as in April: count each contract seat once if it had at least one business use in the preceding 30 Denver local dates, including seats that were reassigned. At 420 contract seats, the 80% goal corresponds to 336 active seats. This is a target for the check, not a result. If any department's records are missing, leave the company-wide determination open until the missing activity can be verified.
