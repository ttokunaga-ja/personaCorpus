JDH supplies and manages its rosters and seat assignments. Halstead gives procedural guidance and helps assess readiness; the proposed allowance does not itself approve or perform a JDH seat assignment. I need your view on how support would record the work across departments.

| Choice | How the balance would move | Question for Darren |
