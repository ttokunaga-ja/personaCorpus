## Baseline

## Decision
For Donald Ramirez and Darren Vasquez: I withdrew the planned seat-add explanation. We should first separate unassigned seats from seats assigned but inactive, and understand the causes by department. Reassignment may help some teams, but the counts alone do not establish which seats can be moved. Prioritize adoption support before discussing additional capacity.
