# What the departments have to bring

Document: HBC-JDH-20260109-WRK-01 v0.1 | Issued: January 9, 2026  
Prepared by: Michelle Whitaker | Account: JDH Route Services Inc

## Purpose

For Divya's estimate, separate the information JDH must provide from the work Halstead can check. The 2025 allocation used a roster, a responsible contact and a practice slot for each ready group. Those conditions are a starting point for the expansion discussion, not confirmation that a new group is ready or that four weeks is feasible.

## Inputs and checks

| Condition | JDH input | Halstead check or follow-up |
|---|---|---|
| Roster | Janet coordinates the department lists; the departments identify the people and intended assignments. JDH owns the assignments. | Divya can compare the submitted lists and flag missing or conflicting entries for JDH to resolve. |
| Responsible contact | Janet identifies a site or department contact able to answer for each handoff, including the night shift. | Confirm that the named contacts cover the handoff and know who can decide whether a correction is acceptable. |
| Practice slot | JDH proposes windows in which the people who perform the work can practice, including night-side participation. | Check the sequence and availability with Divya; record outcomes and outstanding issues before proposing a release. |

## Example: Dispatch to Field Operations

Ask both departments to trace a handoff using an actual work example. If the same recipient appears twice or the departments give conflicting routing, Divya flags the entries and shows the conflict; the JDH contacts resolve which destination is correct and Janet coordinates a revised roster. Halstead should not choose JDH's recipient on its behalf.

For the practice-to-production decision, identify the JDH contact authorized to accept the corrected routing and the practice outcome. Divya checks the evidence and advises whether the implementation work is ready; the JDH responsible contact makes the operational decision to proceed. Neither the contact nor the decision has yet been confirmed for the expansion.

## Questions for Divya

What work must follow the roster check? Can day and night participants actually attend the same window? Which checks and corrections have to precede the first live group? Estimate the work only after the handoffs and available time are clear.
