Hollis — Friday, March 7

Looking ahead at the activity view, a seat that only signed in can look active. That is not the question for the March review. We need to know whether a rostered pilot seat sent an actual branch notice or handoff in the March 10–16 week, not whether someone opened the service.

Ask Lawrence to confirm the Denver-local date boundaries and how he will match the detail to the 30-seat roster. Count a seat once if it has at least one qualifying send, even if it sends several notices or handoffs. Viewing and practice alone should not qualify; neither should sign-in alone. Check how those records are separated in the export before presenting a rate. A seat with no qualifying send is not automatically an unused or unassigned seat, and the count will not explain why.
