# Hollis Medical Supply — Adoption Order

**Order:** HBC-HOL-20250318-ORD-01  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Issue date:** March 18, 2025  
**Prepared by:** Michelle Whitaker  
**Customer:** Hollis Medical Supply Inc

Halstead Branch Communications Inc (“Halstead”) and Hollis Medical Supply Inc (“Hollis”) agree to the following order for Halstead Branch Connect.

## Order terms

| Term | Value | Application |
|---|---|---|
| Service | Halstead Branch Connect | Branch communications service for Hollis |
| Service period | April 1, 2025 through March 31, 2026 | April 1 is the start of the service period, not a requirement to activate all seats on that date. |
| Seat limit | 180 seats | Maximum number of seats under this adoption order. |
| Allocation | Phased | Seats will be allocated progressively from the roster confirmed by Hollis IT. Hollis IT will confirm the initial production roster with Divya Rao by March 27, 2025. |
| Annual fee | USD 82,000 | Fee for the service period stated above. |

**Total annual order amount: USD 82,000**

The 30 seats used in the pilot are not added to the 180-seat limit under this order. The pilot result does not represent usage of the 180-seat adoption allocation.

## Review reference

Hollis IT report **HMS-IT-20250318-01 v1.0**, issued March 18, 2025, was a source for the adoption review. It reports 24 of 30 pilot seats active for the week of March 10–16, 2025. A seat was counted when it sent at least one operational branch notice or handoff during that week; sign-in, viewing and practice were excluded. The six remaining seats had no qualifying activity in the weekly detail; the reason was not established by that detail.
