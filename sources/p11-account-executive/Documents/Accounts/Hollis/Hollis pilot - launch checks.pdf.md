# Hollis pilot — launch checks

HBC-HOL-20250214-WRK-01 v1.0  
February 14, 2025  
Prepared by Divya Rao, Halstead Branch Communications Inc  
For Lawrence Sutton, Hollis Medical Supply Inc

## Before launch — by February 26

| Check | Owner | When | If the check fails |
|---|---|---|---|
| Confirm the roster is limited to 30 seats at the participating site. | Lawrence | By February 26, before import | Hold the import and correct the roster with Hollis IT. |
| Check that no recipient belongs to a site outside the trial. | Lawrence, with Divya checking the import scope | By February 26 and again before sending | Remove the out-of-scope recipient from the selection; stop and return the list to Lawrence if uncertain. |

## March 3 — start procedure

| Step | Owner | When | If the check fails |
|---|---|---|---|
| Check administrator permissions and manually import the confirmed CSV. | Divya with Lawrence | Before the first send | Stop; resolve access or roster scope with Lawrence before continuing. |
| Select the participating site's intended recipients for the first business communication. | Lawrence with Divya | Before sending | Do not send to an unconfirmed or out-of-scope recipient; correct and recheck. |
| Inspect the delivery log for the first business communication. | Divya with Lawrence | After the first business send | If no sending record is visible, stop and investigate before another send. The log confirms sending, not receipt. |

## Weekly record

| Check | Owner | When | If the check fails |
|---|---|---|---|
| Save the usage CSV covering March 10–16, closing March 16. | Lawrence, administrator | After the week closes | Retain the issue for review and obtain the correct period; do not replace missing activity with zero. |

Keep preparation and practice separate from business communications when the weekly record is reviewed. Hollis handles recipient callback through its own operating process.
