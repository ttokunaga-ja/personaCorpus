# Aldridge — first-site scope for review

HBC-ALD-20260814-SVC-01 v1.0 | August 14, 2026  
Prepared by Michelle Whitaker | Account: Aldridge Service Group  
For: Ahmed Farouk

## Reduced scope

This document sets out a starting scope for discussion on August 19: one site and 200 seats. It covers a business handoff, an explanation for Aldridge's central administrator, and Halstead's manual import of a CSV that Aldridge has checked. It is a proposed scope for review, not an instruction to start work. Pricing will be shown separately in a commercial proposal.

| Included | Boundary |
|---|---|
| Business handoff and practice at the first site | Agree the handoff and check sending and receiving within that site. |
| Central administrator explanation | Review the administration and roster process with the person Aldridge identifies. |
| Manual CSV import | Aldridge checks the roster first; Halstead imports the checked file. This is not automatic directory synchronization. |

Rollout to other sites is outside this scope. Customer-managed retention of records is also outside Halstead's scope; Aldridge would manage its own storage, access and retention. This proposed first site does not resolve the outstanding treatment of the earlier RFP exceptions.

## Questions for both teams

1. Who will check and provide the first-site roster for Aldridge, and who will attend the administrator explanation?
2. Which business handoff and participants should be used to practice sending and receiving within the site? What evidence and corrections will each side review?
3. Who will decide readiness for Aldridge and Halstead, and how will we record the decision?

## Conditions before starting

Aldridge confirms the roster and provides the checked CSV. Halstead performs the manual import and supports the agreed practice. Both teams review the outcome and decide whether the site is ready; any start date and schedule remain to be agreed after those checks. The scope, commercial terms and purchasing approval remain subject to review.
