# Aldridge rollout boundaries

HBC-ALD-20241015-WRK-01 v1.0 | October 15, 2024  
Prepared by Divya Rao | Account: Aldridge Service Group  
For Michelle Whitaker

## Proposed sequence

Aldridge checks its source roster for current users, site assignments and contact details before supplying a CSV. Halstead imports the checked file; this is a manual import, not automatic two-way directory synchronization. Changes in the source roster will need a further customer check and import.

Start with one site. Confirm the local roster, walk through the cutover and verify that the branch can send and receive its handoff. Record the result and any correction before scheduling the next site. Repeat site by site. This is a staged rollout, not the single-night cutover across all sites requested in #43. Do not describe it as meeting that condition.

## Boundary for the response

The sequence is an option to explain to Aldridge, subject to its operational review. It does not promise a schedule or acceptance of an exception. Michelle should keep #43 open as a mandatory exception if the current service cannot offer the requested same-night cutover.
