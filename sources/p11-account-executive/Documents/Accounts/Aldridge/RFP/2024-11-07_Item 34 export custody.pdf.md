# Aldridge #34: who keeps the export

HBC-ALD-20241107-RFP-01 v1.0 | November 7, 2024
Prepared by Michelle Whitaker | For Ahmed Farouk, Aldridge Service Group

## Purpose

This note follows the November 5 discussion and supplements requirement #34 in Halstead's conditional response HBC-ALD-20241023-RFP-01 v1.0. It describes a possible handoff; it does not change the response's exception.

## From export to custody

1. An authorized branch administrator obtains the available activity records as a CSV export from the service.
2. Aldridge identifies the destination where that exported file will be kept and the person or team responsible for receiving it. The transfer and its receipt need to be arranged by Aldridge.
3. Aldridge assigns responsibility for controlling access to the stored copy and for deciding the applicable retention period. Those decisions and the storage itself sit outside Halstead Branch Connect.

Ahmed, please let me know which Aldridge group could own the stored export and who would make the access and retention decisions. Until that is settled, this is a possible operating arrangement, not a confirmed custody process.

## Requirement status

| Requirement | Status | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| #34 — seven-year in-service retention of activity logs | Mandatory exception | CSV export is available; seven-year retention within the service is not. External custody does not satisfy the stated in-service requirement. |

Aldridge's scoring of the six exceptions remains for Aldridge to determine.
