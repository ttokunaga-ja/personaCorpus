# Aldridge clause review: what can go back

Document: HBC-ALD-20250113-WRK-01 v1.0 | Issued: January 13, 2025
Author: Michelle Whitaker | Account: Aldridge Service Group

## Meeting

January 13, 2025, 9:30 a.m. Denver. Participants: Michelle Whitaker, Marisol Torres and Jonathan Salgado. Circulate to Marisol and Jonathan.

## Decision

The group will not return §11.2 with unlimited aggregate liability. We will return a counterproposal limited to that section: Halstead's aggregate liability arising out of or relating to the agreement would be capped at the annual recurring fee stated in the applicable order form, excluding taxes and one-time fees. The order-form amount, not payments made to date or in a recent period, supplies the basis.

This is an internal decision to propose wording. Aldridge has not accepted it. The return does not resolve other provisions or constitute a signed agreement.
