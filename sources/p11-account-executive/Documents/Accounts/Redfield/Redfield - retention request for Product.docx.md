## Customer requirement

Allison, please record the two mandatory conditions separately. Rebecca Cruz's purchasing comparison, RSS-PROC-20250214-01, distinguishes the retention period from the source used by an administrator at audit. Both apply to the activity history of business communications across Redfield's branches.

| Requirement | Redfield's mandatory condition | February 17 answer |
|---|---|---|
| Seven-year retention | Keep activity history for all branches in Branch Connect for seven years. | The USD 124,000 proposal does not provide the required in-service retention; leave this row as an exception. |
| Audit retrieval | An administrator must be able to retrieve that history from the same service at audit. | The proposal does not meet the in-service audit retrieval condition; leave this row as an exception. |

An administrator can export activity history to CSV today. After export, the file is outside the service and Redfield manages its storage and access. The export is reference information for the comparison; it does not satisfy either mandatory in-service condition.

## Commercial disposition

The annual USD 124,000 Branch Connect proposal covered branch communications and administrator support. At the February 17 comparison, Rebecca and I reviewed the two archive rows. I confirmed the exceptions in writing that morning. Rebecca subsequently removed the Halstead proposal from purchasing comparison because the two mandatory conditions were unmet. She said a revised quote is not needed while those conditions remain unchanged. I am closing the comparison as lost; USD 124,000 was the proposed annual price, not a sale.

Please accept the seven-year in-service retention and administrator retrieval at audit as two requirements for Product intake. The comparison and my answer identify the gap; they do not establish a solution. No delivery date or revised proposal has been promised to Redfield. This handoff requests requirements intake only, not a commitment to provide the capability or a new price.
