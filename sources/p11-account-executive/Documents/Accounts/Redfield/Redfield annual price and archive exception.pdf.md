# Annual price and archive exceptions

HBC-RED-20250212-RFP-01 v1.0  
Issued February 12, 2025  
Prepared by Michelle Whitaker  
For Rebecca Cruz, Redfield Site Services LLC

## Scope of proposal

Halstead Branch Connect supports branch communications and includes administrator support. The annual price for this stated scope is **USD 124,000**. This price is valid until February 28, 2025 at 5:00 p.m. Denver time. It is provided for Redfield's purchasing comparison.

## Requirements and exceptions

| Requirement | Response | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| Branch communications | Included | Branch Connect service and administrator support are included in the annual price. |
| Administrator CSV export | Available | An administrator can export activity history as CSV. |
| Seven years of activity history for all branches inside the service | Exception | The stated scope does not meet the required seven-year in-service retention. |
| Administrator retrieval from the same service at audit | Exception | The stated scope does not meet the required in-service audit retrieval condition. |

After export, the CSV is outside Branch Connect. Redfield would manage its storage location and access permissions. A customer-held CSV is offered as reference information and does not satisfy the two required in-service conditions.
