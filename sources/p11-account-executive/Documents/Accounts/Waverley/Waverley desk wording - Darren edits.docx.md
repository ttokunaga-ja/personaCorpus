## Proposed desk notice
While Tracy is away, the Office shift lead receives inquiries on Chicago weekdays from 9:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m. Outside those hours, leave the item for Tracy's next working period.

- Note the time and site and what the inquiry concerns.
- Record the callback contact for this inquiry with the inquiry.
- Hold requests to edit the distribution list or approve access for Tracy's decision.
- Pass the record on when Tracy returns.
