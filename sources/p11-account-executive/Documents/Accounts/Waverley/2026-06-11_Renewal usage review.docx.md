## Baseline

For the 30 calendar days May 7 through June 5, a seat was counted once within its group if it sent a business communication or acknowledged receipt at least once. A login alone did not count. The June 8 activity export and group summary use this same window and definition.

| Group | Contracted seats | Active seats | Not active in window |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Dispatch | 36 | 30 | 6 |

| Office | 36 | 22 | 14 |
| Total | 120 | 89 | 31 |

The aggregate is 89 of 120 seats, or 74.2%; the meeting figure is 74% rounded to a whole percent. The 120 seats opened during implementation are not a measure of current use. A seat without a qualifying event in this window is not necessarily unneeded.

## Decision

Tracy and I agreed to renew at 120 seats and USD 54,000 per year. At 74% active, we did not propose adding seats. The 31 seats without a qualifying event remain within the contracted count; the count alone does not tell us why each was inactive.

Office has 14 such seats. Tracy will distinguish possible changes in the people responsible for those seats from difficulties using the service. We should ask about each separately before deciding whether a reassignment or practice would help. The June 11 renewal terms are a separate decision from that operational follow-up.
