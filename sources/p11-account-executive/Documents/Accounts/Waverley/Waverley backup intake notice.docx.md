# Waverley | backup intake notice
HBC-WAV-20250820-WRK-01 v1.0 | August 20, 2025
Michelle Whitaker | Account: Waverley Commercial Storage Inc
For: Waverley Office and departments

## When to use this notice
Effective August 25, 2025. When Tracy is away, the Office shift lead receives inquiries on Chicago weekdays from 9:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m. Chicago local time. Outside those hours, hold the inquiry for Tracy's next working period.

The shift lead may acknowledge receipt. Do not promise a decision, a change, or a response time. Record the inquiry and pass it to the appropriate owner; a request to change a distribution list is not an instruction for the lead to edit it.

## Record the inquiry
Record the actual site and the callback route given for that particular inquiry, rather than a department name alone or a change to a distribution list. The following illustrates a complete entry; replace every detail with the details actually supplied.

| Field | Example entry |
|---|---|
| Time received | August 25, 2025, 10:20 a.m. Chicago time |
| Site | Waverley Chicago site, Dispatch |
| Callback for this inquiry | Call the requester at the Chicago Dispatch desk, extension 214 |
| Request | Correct the Dispatch distribution list |
| Holding for | Tracy Moreno — administrator decision |

If the caller has not supplied a usable return route or the site is unclear, ask for it and record what is known. Do not substitute a distribution-list edit for a callback note.

## Who handles what
| Work | Owner | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Intake and receipt | Office shift lead | Record the details, acknowledge receipt and pass the inquiry on. |
| Procedure guidance | Darren Vasquez | Help with how to locate a receipt confirmation or other procedure questions. |
| Administrator decision | Tracy Moreno | Decide roster, distribution-list and access requests. Hold these for Tracy; the lead does not make the change. |
| Contract inquiry | Michelle Whitaker | Receive questions about contract terms and confirm the response. |

## Outside hours and return
Outside the coverage window, hold the inquiry for Tracy's next working period. When Tracy returns, the shift lead hands over all outstanding items, including the recorded callback route and the person holding each item. Tracy takes over the outstanding work; the acknowledgment of receipt does not imply that a decision has been made.
