## Proposed plan

North is up and east is right. Show grid lines X5 and X6 at x=0 and x=6,000, and Y3 and Y4 at y=0 and y=6,000. The coordinate origin is the intersection X5/Y3; x increases east and y increases north.

Draw the south setting-out line on the floor at y=1,000, across the plan, labelled “South setting-out line, y=1,000 (floor mark; no wall)”. Dimension 300 between this line and the equipment south edge at y=1,300.

Draw the proposed rotated equipment envelope as a rectangle from (1,800, 1,300) to (3,840, 3,100), with dimension lines “2,040 E–W” and “1,800 N–S”. Its south-west corner remains at (1,800, 1,300). The Rev B envelope has been turned 90° counter-clockwise from the previous orientation. Show an arrow pointing south from the south edge, labelled “Proposed transfer / maintenance face — please confirm”.

Draw the north partition south finished face at y=4,420, remaining in its present position. Dimension from the equipment north edge y=3,100 to that face as “North walkway: 1,320”. The required clear width is 1,200. This is a layout dimension, not a measurement after installation.

Show the proposed southern working area between y=400 and the equipment south edge y=1,300; dimension it “900 south of unit”. Indicate a short proposed transfer roller table on the south side with a broken outline, and label its intended roller top “FL +850”. Show the fixed fence edge at x=4,500 east of the unit and dimension the gap from x=3,840 as “660 to fixed fence”. Distinguish the proposed roller table from the fixed fence in the legend. The south working area and east gap are separate from the north walkway.

## Questions for Merecairn

1. Can the former west transfer and maintenance face be turned to face south as shown, with a short roller table on the south side? Please advise the space and access needed for transfer and maintenance.
2. What clear width is required on the east side for isolation operation? The plan shows 660 to the fixed fence.
3. Where does the power lead exit after turning the unit, and what lead length is available? Can it be taken out to the south without crossing the north walkway?

Please comment on the proposed arrangement and any equipment-side conditions. The unit and roller table have not been installed; floor fixing and final clearances remain to be checked on site.
