To: Shuichiro Sakamoto, Minatoryo Construction

## Plan — existing orientation

North is up, west is left. Draw the guarded unit as a solid rectangle 1,800 mm east–west by 2,040 mm north–south. Within it draw the Rev A outline in chain line, sharing its south and side edges, 1,800 by 1,820 mm. Dimension both north–south extents and the 220 mm increase at the north guard projection. Legend: solid — Rev B; chain — Rev A. Mark the west edge “Transfer / maintenance face” with a westward arrow and show the transfer roller there. Dimensions are to the outermost guard edges.

## Side elevation — transfer roller

Show the floor line FL and the transfer roller surface. Dimension the roller top FL +850 mm, unchanged from Rev A.

## Revision and interface

Rev B increases the guard-inclusive north–south envelope by 220 mm; east–west remains 1,800 mm. The site designer should recheck clearances against finished building faces. This drawing does not establish the site passage width or authorise installation.
