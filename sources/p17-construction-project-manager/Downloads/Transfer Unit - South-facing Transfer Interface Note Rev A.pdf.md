# South-facing Transfer — Interface Note

Project: Kaita West Distribution Building Extension  
Revision: A  
Issued: 24 July 2025  
Issued by: Natalie Chapman, Merecairn Equipment Ltd

## Orientation and plan

The transfer and maintenance face may be oriented south by rotating the unit 90° counter-clockwise from the arrangement shown in General Arrangement Rev B. The guarded envelope in the proposed orientation is 2,040 mm east–west by 1,800 mm north–south. Show north up and the south-facing transfer arrow. Mark a 900 mm work and maintenance area immediately south of the unit, from y=1,300 to y=400 in the project coordinates. On the east side indicate the isolation operating side and the required clear width of at least 600 mm, separately from the southern work area.

## Side view and interfaces

Show the short transfer roller table at the south face with roller top FL+850 mm. The short southern roller table must be removable when maintenance access is required, so that the full 900 mm maintenance area is clear. Indicate the power lead exiting on the south side; lead length 3,000 mm. Route the lead without crossing the northern pedestrian passage.

The building contractor is to check the floor fixing positions and the floor below against the actual site arrangement before fixing. This note defines the equipment interface; it does not confirm site fixing or completion of installation.
