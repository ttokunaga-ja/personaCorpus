# General Arrangement — Transfer Unit

Project: Kaita West Distribution Building Extension  
Revision: A  
Issued: 2 July 2025  
Issued by: Natalie Chapman, Merecairn Equipment Ltd  
To: Shuichiro Sakamoto, Minatoryo Construction

## Plan — existing orientation

North is at the top and west at the left. Draw the guarded unit as a rectangle, 1,800 mm east–west by 1,820 mm north–south, including the guard. Mark the west edge “Transfer / maintenance face” with an arrow pointing west. Show the transfer roller on this face. Dimension the full guarded outline between its outermost edges in each direction. This view identifies the equipment envelope, not the site clearances.

## Side elevation — transfer roller

Show the roller surface and the floor line FL. Dimension from FL to the top of the transfer roller as FL +850 mm. The overall plan dimensions are measured to the guard; the roller elevation is to the working surface.

## Interface

The designer is to check the effective site passage width against the building layout and finished wall faces. This equipment drawing does not establish the available passage width or authorise installation.
