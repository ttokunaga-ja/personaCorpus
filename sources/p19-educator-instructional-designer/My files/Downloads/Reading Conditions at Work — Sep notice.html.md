# Reading Conditions at Work — September submission

## Optional consultation: 9 September

Bring your work-in-progress decision memo to Room 2 on Wednesday 9 September at 13:00. The optional consultation lasts 30 minutes. You can work with a partner to see whether your advice leads a reader to the relevant section and reception record. Bring the notes you have so far; you do not need a finished page to attend.

## Decision memo: submit by 11 September

Submit your one-page decision memo by Friday 11 September at 16:00, Toronto time. Give the advice you would offer at the counter, identify the applicable source and section, and connect it to the recorded facts. Include what still needs checking and whom you would ask.

If you read the case on paper, the submission is the same one-page decision memo. The case packet is for reference; it does not replace your own memo.
