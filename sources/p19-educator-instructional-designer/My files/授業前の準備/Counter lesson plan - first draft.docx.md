# Opening the service counter — first sequence

ELM-CRW-2026-LP-002 · v0.2 · 21 April 2026  
Heather Callahan · For Erin MacDonald

## Learning aims

In 70 minutes, participants identify the normal reopening time, find the exception in the staff instruction, and connect the exception to the counter record before writing advice to a visitor.

## Teaching sequence

| Time | Activity |
|---|---|
| 0–10 min | Read the visitor notice individually. Write the time a visitor would be told from that notice alone. |
| 10–25 min | Read the staff instruction together. Locate section 2 and explain what changes the normal time. |
| 25–40 min | Before reading the record together, use one short “Checked?” field in a working table for the two checks. Examine the record: passage checked at 12:10, approval field blank at 12:20. |
| 40–55 min | In pairs, write whether the counter may reopen at 12:30 and give the reason and source. |
| 55–70 min | Compare answers; revise the visitor sentence and identify what must be checked before announcing early reopening. |
