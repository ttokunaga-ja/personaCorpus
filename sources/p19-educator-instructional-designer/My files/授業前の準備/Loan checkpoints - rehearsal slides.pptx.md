## Slide 2
Layout: Heading and body  
Title: When is the usual return?

**Preparation room notice**  
Equipment borrowed for training is normally due back by 16:30 on the day of the loan.

**Write one line.** When is the equipment due, and which words in the notice tell you?

Notes: Switch at elapsed 15 minutes. Allow three minutes, beginning with everyone writing before you call on anyone. If you hear only “4:30,” ask the reader to show the line.

## Slide 4
Layout: Heading and body  
Title: What can reception say?

- Who can confirm the approval?
- What can you tell the borrower while you check?
- What return time applies if the deadline passes without recorded approval?

Notes: Put this up for the closing nine minutes. Have someone give the borrower a sentence they can act on. Listen for the distinction between checking with the duty lead and advising the ordinary 16:30 return when the approval deadline has passed.
