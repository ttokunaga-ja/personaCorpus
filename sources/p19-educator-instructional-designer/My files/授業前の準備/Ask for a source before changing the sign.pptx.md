## Slide 1 — What can you tell the visitor now?

Layout: title and content

Reading Conditions at Work · ELM-CRW-2026-SLD-004 · v1.0  
1 Jun 2026 · Heather Callahan

Visitor notice: “The counter normally reopens at 13:00. Please return at that time for service.”

What would you tell a visitor now? Write one provisional sentence and identify its source.

Speaker notes: At 15 minutes, pause the reading. Invite a provisional sentence and its source; put both on the board. Keep the staff instruction and record closed for this first decision. Ask what the notice permits the counter to say, not what may be written elsewhere.

## Slide 2 — What can you tell the visitor now?

Layout: two columns

**Staff instruction**

Section 2: The counter may reopen at 12:30 only if both the responsible person's approval and the passage check have been recorded by 12:15. One record does not replace the other.

Section 3: Check both entries before announcing early reopening. If a required entry is blank, seek confirmation. Until the conditions are established, use the normal time on the notice.

**Counter confirmation record, viewed at 12:20**

| Required check | Recorded time |
|---|---|
| Passage checked | 12:10 |
| Responsible person's approval | |

Which entries and which instruction matter? Revisit your sentence and identify anything you still need to check.

Speaker notes: At 30 minutes, ask pairs to point to the passage and approval entries one at a time, then to section 2. Have them check whether the records needed for early reopening are present by 12:15. A blank record does not prove approval was refused; section 3 directs a check. Ask pairs to explain any change to their provisional advice using the instruction and the record.
