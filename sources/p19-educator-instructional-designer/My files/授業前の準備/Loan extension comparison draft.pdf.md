# Can the register support an extension?

Reading Conditions at Work · ELM-CRW-2026-HO-001 · v0.1 · 12 Jan 2026 · Heather Callahan  
Desk copy for Erin MacDonald

## Read the same instruction against both records

Both borrowers have asked for more time on the day of the loan. Use the memo and the entries below to decide what reception can advise.

> 1. Receive an application for an extension no later than 15:45 on the loan day and record the time received.
> 2. An extension requires the duty lead's approval to be recorded no later than 16:15 on the loan day.
> 3. Only when both clauses 1 and 2 are satisfied may reception advise a return by 09:00 on the next business day.

Source: Duty lead's reception memo, clauses 1–3. The preparation room notice, clause 1, gives the ordinary same-day return of 16:30.

| Register A | Register B |
|---|---|
| Application received: 15:42 | Application received: 15:46 |
| Duty lead approval recorded: 16:18 | Duty lead approval recorded: 16:10 |
| Requirement missed and supporting entry: ____________________ | Requirement missed and supporting entry: ____________________ |
| Return time you would advise and why: ____________________ | Return time you would advise and why: ____________________ |

Mark both entries against the relevant numbered clauses. Then explain why the two records lead to the same advice even though they miss different requirements.

## For Erin and me after the read-through

In A, 15:42 meets clause 1 and 16:18 misses clause 2 by three minutes. In B, 15:46 misses clause 1 by one minute and 16:10 meets clause 2. Clause 3 requires both conditions, so in each case reception advises the same-day 16:30 return under the notice. Ask which entry made the difference before sharing this reading.

Keep this as a separate sheet outside the packet pagination and question numbering. Q5 should stay on what reception can establish from an approval field that is still blank.
