# Board sketch

Landscape sketch of a single wide board outline divided into two columns by one vertical line. At the top left write “Source” and at the top right write “Decision”. A small “Q5” sits at the left edge below the headings. Two entries run across the same board area:

| Source | Decision |
|---|---|
| “Register: application received 15:32” | “Application received in time” |
| “Register at 16:05: approval field blank” | “Approval still unconfirmed” |

Each source entry has a short arrow across the divider pointing to its corresponding phrase. A single box under “Decision” encloses both phrases. Place a short margin note on the right of that box, with its arrow pointing just beside “Approval still unconfirmed”. The note reads “Give ‘Still to check’ its own column. Keep the source and clause together on the left.”

The sketch shows the original two-column attempt. The proposed extra column appears only in the margin note. The table and margin note give all the visible lettering.
