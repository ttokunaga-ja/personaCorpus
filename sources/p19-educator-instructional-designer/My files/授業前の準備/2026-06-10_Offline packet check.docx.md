# June 10 — read both sheets without a connection
ELM-CRW-2026-DD-002 v1.0 | 10 Jun 2026 | Heather Callahan

## Participants and purpose

10 June, 10:00–10:30, Room 2. Heather Callahan, Kathleen Roy and Arjun Deshmukh checked the instructor's preparation for Reading Conditions at Work. We needed the notice, staff instruction and counter record available when a connection is unavailable.

## What we found

With the instructor device disconnected, the saved counter exercise packet opened and the notice, instruction and record could be read together in one file. A reference to the counter record held only in the cloud did not open without the connection. The check concerned the instructor's device and preparation; it did not establish that any learner's settings are ready.

## Decision

Use the saved single packet as the default instructor copy. It keeps the visitor's 13:00 notice, the staff instruction on the two checks, and the record with passage checked at 12:10 and the approval field blank together. Keep the two record fields distinct. Kathleen's guidance gives an alternative sequence for someone who cannot view sources side by side; it does not replace the exercise or decide the answer.
