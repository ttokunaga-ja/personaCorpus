# Booked and confirmed are separate checks

A small flow diagram read from top to bottom. The starting box says “Friday pickup: arrives 4:20”. An arrow leads to “Booked?” The “Yes” arrow leads to “Confirmed before 4:00?”; the “No” arrow leads to “Next business day”.

From “Confirmed before 4:00?”, “Record not at hand” leads to “Hold item; check supervisor's confirmation log at the desk”. From that box, arrows return to the confirmation question when the time is found. “No — confirmed 4:05” leads to “Next business day”; “Yes — confirmed 3:40” leads to “Pickup by 5:30?” The “No” arrow from that question leads to “Next business day”; “Yes” leads to “Release in the later collection window”.

A continuous example is labelled “Booked → confirmed 3:40 → pickup 4:20 → release”. The booking box has a circle around “Yes”, but its arrow goes through the confirmation and pickup checks, never directly to release.
