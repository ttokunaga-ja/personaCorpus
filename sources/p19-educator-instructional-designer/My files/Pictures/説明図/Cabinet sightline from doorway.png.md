# Sightline at the doorway

## Figure layout

The main panel is an overhead view with north at the top, marked by an upward arrow labelled “N”. A gap in the south wall is the “South entrance”. A dotted “Observation line” crosses the gap, with a point on it labelled “Viewpoint at entrance”. The north-wall segment for the posted notice is labelled “Current notice”. A rectangle inside the room toward the east is labelled “Storage cabinet”. A dashed sightline runs northeast from the viewpoint towards the north-wall notice and ends at the cabinet's near edge. A leader labelled “Hidden from this viewpoint” points to the part of the current notice behind the cabinet along that direction. A separate dashed outline along the east wall is labelled “Candidate position”. It has no sightline or observation result attached to it.

The right panel has an entrance point, a second point one step inside and a curved arrow showing a turn at the second point. The caption reads “A step and turn can change the view”.

## Figure text

Dotted line — observation line. Dashed ray — line of sight. Dashed outline — candidate position.

One viewpoint only. This sketch is not to scale and gives no measured proportion of the notice visible from the entrance.
