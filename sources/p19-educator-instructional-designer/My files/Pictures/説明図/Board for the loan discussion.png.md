# Room 2 — Board for the loan discussion

Draw a landscape setup diagram for Tristan. The whiteboard fills the upper part of the diagram. Divide it into three vertical regions with these headings, in order from left to right:

| Source and clause | What we can decide | Still to check |
|---|---|---|

Divide the writing area under the headings into two horizontal rows labelled “Q5” and “Q8” in the left gutter. The text and arrows below are the setup cues. Draw separate empty writing boxes alongside or beneath the cues in all three regions in each row, to show where Tristan will write what the group brings to the board.

**Q5.** Put the following two cue groups in the source region, one above the other:

- “Register · Application received: 15:32” and “Reception memo · cl. 1 · by 15:45”.
- “Register · Duty lead approval blank at 16:05” and “Reception memo · cls. 2 and 4”.

Draw an arrow from the first cue group to “Application received in time” in the middle region. Route a second arrow below the first, from the blank-field cue to “Check recorded approval with duty lead” in the right region. End that arrow inside the right region. Under the right-hand cue put “Hold the promise while checking”.

**Q8.** Put “Preparation room notice” and “Duty lead's reception memo” in the left region, with an empty box beside each to add clause numbers. Leave empty boxes in the middle and right regions. The small cues above the empty boxes read “Issuer · reader · scope” in the middle and “Which instruction applies here?” on the right.

Below both rows place one separate empty box across the board labelled “Q10 · One sentence at the window”. Leave room to write the full sentence during discussion.

Beneath the board draw a small plan of Room 2. At one end label the entrance “Door” and show the “Start here” notice just inside, with “Handout shelf” immediately below it. At the far end, label the board “Whiteboard”. Draw a route arrow beginning at the shelf and going up the aisle between the tables towards the board. The route arrow belongs to the room plan, separate from the two evidence arrows on the board.
