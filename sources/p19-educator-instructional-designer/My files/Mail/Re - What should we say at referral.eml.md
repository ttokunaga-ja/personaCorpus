Hi Bernadette,

I've attached a one-page explanation for the advisers to use at the first appointment. You could read the introduction aloud and pause for questions.

There are eight sessions. In the room, learners will read an invitation with the tutor, following its headings to find what to bring and where to meet. They can point to the words that helped them answer.

If someone misses a session, we'll post the paper for that session. They can start with the notice, work through the questions and use the check section to compare their answers with the notice; they won't need a device. The reply slip in the material is for practice and does not need to be sent in. Please confirm the application to attend the course separately with the person at the Ashford appointment.

I hope that gives your team a clear starting point.

Thanks,
Heather
