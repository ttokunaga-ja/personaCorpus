Hi Glenn,

I've attached the final report for Communicating in Workplace Meetings. Of the 24 people in the group, 22 submitted the final exercise. Of those 22, 17 met all four conditional proposal criteria: an action and its scope, a supporting fact from the agenda, the staffing coordinator's confirmation by Thursday noon, and the action to take if the key rota remains unconfirmed. Five submissions did not yet meet all four criteria; two people did not submit and were not assessed.

The report describes the written exercise, rather than course completion or a change in workplace behaviour. The Saturday trial was a practice scenario, so these results do not tell us whether it went ahead.

For a future group, one possible five-minute practice would start with the unconfirmed condition: ask participants to say first which weekday arrangement will remain, then add the confirmation condition and the proposed Saturday trial. That should give them a chance to make the alternative explicit before putting the whole proposal together.
