# Duty trial review

ELM-CRW-2024-DD-005 v1.0 · 31 Oct 2024 · Heather Callahan  
Reading Conditions at Work · For David Ashcroft, Arjun Deshmukh and Kathleen Roy

## Context

At the 21 Oct review, Arjun and I found six late deliveries among 28 confirmed absences in Sessions 1–6. Four of those delays began with difficulty identifying the exercise. We tried matching the session and material on the day reception confirmed the absence, with a handoff to Arjun as soon as delivery by the end of the next calendar day looked unlikely.

## Change and rationale

### Monday 28 Oct, 13:10 EDT — Desk rehearsal

I took part with Arjun, Kathleen and Erin MacDonald. We rotated through reception, instructor and receiver. The practice timetable had two rows headed “Room change”. We went back to the date on the absence entry and read down to 15 Oct, Session 6. With the session identified, the instructor could confirm Room change check, HO-006 v1.0, as the two-page exercise.

In the missing-page exercise, the sender filled in the receipt time while asking Arjun to take over. We cleared the entry and did the readback again. The sender gave the session, exercise and version, deadline and missing page. The receiver repeated what remained and when the full copy was due. We agreed that from Tuesday's duty Arjun himself would return the time he accepted the work. Until that reply, the person passing it on would still own the job.

### Tuesday 29 Oct to Thursday 31 Oct — Session 8 duty

Reception confirmed all four absences on 29 Oct. That day we matched all four to Session 8 and Print allowance (ELM-CRW-2024-HO-009 v1.0). The same deadline applied to all four, the end of 30 Oct. All times below are EDT.

| Ref. | Confirmed and matched | Due by end of | Arjun accepted | Delivered to learner | What happened |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| T01 | 29 Oct | 30 Oct | — | 30 Oct, 09:20 | Within deadline. |
| T02 | 29 Oct | 30 Oct | — | 30 Oct, 11:05 | Within deadline. |
| T03 | 29 Oct | 30 Oct | — | 30 Oct, 14:10 | Within deadline. |
| T04 | 29 Oct | 30 Oct | 29 Oct, 16:12 | 31 Oct, 10:20 | Kathleen handed over the missing-page work. The complete large-print copy arrived a day late. |

The desk slip records Arjun taking over T04 on Tuesday because the large-print copy lacked page 2. His 30 Oct, 11:02 proof note records why the one-sided option was set aside that morning. Page 1 has the two 26-page requests and page 2 explains how the instructor's recorded check changes the decision. The learner needed those worked answers to check both decisions independently. On Thursday the learner received both pages of v1.0 at 10:20.

Three of the four exercises reached the learners by the end of 30 Oct. One required a handoff and arrived a calendar day after the deadline. Those figures describe the four cases from this duty.

## Next review

At the next duty, the person preparing the handoff will check the exercise title, version and every page of the copy the learner is to receive, including the answer page. If a complete set looks unlikely to arrive by the deadline, reception or Kathleen will give Arjun the remaining work and due date. Arjun will return his receipt time, and the desk will enter the actual delivery separately once the learner has the full set. At the desk change we will read back the open work, its owner and the deadline.
