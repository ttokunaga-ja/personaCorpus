# Where to take pages out first

Reading Conditions at Work · ELM-CRW-2025-DD-002 · v0.1 · 02 Dec 2025 · Heather Callahan  
For circulation to David Ashcroft and Yusuf Mahmood

## Context

The autumn equipment-loan packet runs to 30 pages. I am looking for a cut of 12 pages so we can aim for 20 minutes of independent reading instead of 40. I want people to arrive at the discussion with a place in the text they can point to. If the shorter packet makes a decision quicker to find but leaves less to explain, we will have spent the time badly.

## Change and rationale

| Where I would start | Proposed change | What I want checked |
|---|---|---|
| Vocabulary on pp. 3–4 | Remove the separate repeated terms; explain essential words where they occur. | Check that readers still meet the distinction between an application and recorded approval before they need to use it. |
| Copying exercises on pp. 9–11 | Remove the transcription exercises. Ask readers to mark the exact clause in the notice or memo and use it beside the register entry. | Check that the case pages still give people somewhere to record both pieces of evidence. |
| Worked explanations and independent practice | Look for a further seven pages among worked explanation and independent practice, to reach twelve pages removed in total. | Flag the places that give the same explanation twice. Keep any example that makes a reader change a decision when a case fact changes. |
| Q8 comparison | To make the page shorter, summarise the reception memo and place Q8 on the same page as the notice. | Would learners still be able to tell which instruction came from the preparation room and which came from the duty lead, and who each is for? |

The first two cuts would free five pages. The other seven are still to be located. The summary is an option for fitting the comparison into less space. I have left it open because we'd lose the chance to have readers examine the two issuers' own wording side by side. The notice gives the ordinary return to borrowers, while the memo tells reception what to check before giving a later time. That difference is part of what Q8 asks the reader to find.

## Next review

Yusuf, please mark any parts of the memo where its own wording matters to Q8, and point me to any worked explanations that repeat the same reading. David, I'd like your view on whether the proposed cut leaves enough room for a learner to explain the decision from the documents. I'll use those notes to draw up the page map. For now, 20 minutes is the target for the reading slot.
