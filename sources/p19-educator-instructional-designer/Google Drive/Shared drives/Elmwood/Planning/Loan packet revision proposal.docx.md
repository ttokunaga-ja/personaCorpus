# What has to stay visible in eighteen pages

Reading Conditions at Work · ELM-CRW-2026-DD-001 · v0.2 · 20 Jan 2026 · Heather Callahan  
To David Ashcroft · For the 22 January discussion

## Context

The December page map takes the equipment-loan reading from 30 pages to 18. Before we agree the cut, I'd like us to look at what each question asks a person to do with the documents. We can lift the separate vocabulary pages (3–4), transcription (9–11), repeated worked explanations (16–18) and extra independent practice (22–25) and still give people the source texts, the case records and space to explain a decision.

## Change and rationale

| Course aim | Evidence that needs to stay on the page |
|---|---|
| 1. Find the ordinary rule | Q2 asks the reader to find the same-day 16:30 return in clause 1 of the preparation room notice and quote the words that establish it. |
| 2. Identify the conditions under which a rule applies | For Q2, the equipment was borrowed for the day and no extension was requested. For Q5, the register entry of 15:32 has to be read against the 15:45 application deadline in memo clause 1. |
| 3. Check the requirements for an exception | Q5 needs both the register and the memo. Receipt at 15:32 meets the first requirement. The blank approval field at 16:05 leaves the second requirement open. The reader has to use clauses 2 and 4 to identify what to check with the duty lead and hold off on promising a later return. |
| 4. Compare competing instructions by issuer and scope | In Q8, keep the preparation room's notice to borrowers and the duty lead's memo to reception in full. Readers can then see which text gives the ordinary rule and which governs the handling of extensions. The wording and addressees are part of the evidence. |
| 5. Explain a decision with a clause and the facts | Q10 moves the clock past 16:15 with approval still unrecorded. Keep space for one sentence a receptionist could use, with the ordinary return and the reason, followed by the relevant clauses. |

Check the new page references before we circulate the booklet. In particular, step 2 on the instruction page needs to send the reader to the Q5 case register.

| Time to consider | Proposed use |
|---|---|
| Independent reading, 40 minutes down to 20 | Ask readers to mark their own sources and case facts as they work through the shorter booklet. |
| The 20 minutes released | Add time to compare marked evidence in pairs, set the two instructions alongside each other as a group, and check the reason by rewriting it. Agree the split on Thursday. |

Keep the existing 5-minute introduction, 15-minute group discussion and 10-minute exit task within the 70 minutes. The exit task should still ask for an action and its basis.

## Next review

On 22 January, check both the material to retain and how we want to spend the released time. After approval I will update the packet and instructor sequence together. When we next use the work, take an exit response that shows whether the reader can carry the source and the case fact into the explanation.
