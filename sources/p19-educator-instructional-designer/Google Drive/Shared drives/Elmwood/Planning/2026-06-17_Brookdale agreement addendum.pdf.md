# What to settle before July starts
Brookdale Service Cooperative | ELM-BSC-2026-AGR-002 v1.0 | 17 Jun 2026 | Heather Callahan

## Purpose and reference

This preparation addendum records Heather Callahan's confirmation with Janice Mitchell and Arjun Deshmukh under the 7 April agreement, ELM-BSC-2026-AGR-001 v1.0. The July–September courses remain separate. Planned places are not a count of people enrolled or attending.

## Nominations and places

Janice will coordinate Brookdale nominations and close them at 15:00 EDT on 24 June. On 25 June Arjun will organise nominations by course and primary nomination route, removing duplicates within each course. Arjun will compare the resulting course totals with the planned places and bring any unfilled places to Heather and Janice for a decision on additional nominations. At the end of June an unfilled place remains unconfirmed until that decision; it is not automatically assigned to another route.

## Reading Conditions at Work

The plan provides 36 places. Before the course begins Heather will check the learner-facing task instructions against the case packet and the delivery plan: the submission is a decision note linking the applicable instruction and recorded facts. Arjun will coordinate access arrangements before the first session. Reporting will distinguish enrolment at the planned deadline, valid submissions and the number meeting the criterion.

## Building Proposals from Evidence

The plan provides 24 places. Before the course begins Heather will check the separate learner-facing task instructions against the evidence packet and delivery plan: the submission is a one-page recommendation with evidence, a comparison and an action if the condition is unconfirmed. Arjun will coordinate course arrangements. Reporting will distinguish enrolment and valid submissions, and count those valid submissions showing all three elements.

## Follow-up

Heather will share the two task descriptions for the pre-course check; Janice will resolve the route for any additional Brookdale nominations with Arjun. The two courses' results will be reported separately and without learner names or answers.
