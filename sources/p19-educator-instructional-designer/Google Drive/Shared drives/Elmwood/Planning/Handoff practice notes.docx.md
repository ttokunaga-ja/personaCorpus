# Handoff practice notes

ELM-CRW-2024-IR-003 v1.0 · 28 Oct 2024 · Heather Callahan  
Reading Conditions at Work · Instructor copy for ELM-CRW-2024-SLD-008 v1.0

## Set up the two questions

Assign reception, instructor and Arjun. Hold slides 4 and 6 until the desk has attempted the entries on slides 3 and 5. Use each slide’s own practice record. Rotate roles after the first readback.

## Question 1 — Same title, two sessions

**Ask on slide 3.** “Which line in the timetable goes with the absence entry? What will you say to the instructor?”

**Worked response.** “The absence is for the class on 15 Oct. That line is Session 6. Which exercise and version do we give the learner for Session 6?” The instructor confirms Room change check, HO-006 v1.0, with both pages ready. The desk enters 16 Oct at end of day as the deadline, and reception retains the delivery action.

**Listen for the match.** The class date in the absence entry selects the line. The timetable supplies Session 6 and the instructor supplies the exercise and version. If someone chooses the first “Room change” line, ask what in the absence entry would support 1 Oct. Open slide 4 and have the relief worker read the entries back.

## Question 2 — The right version is missing a page

**Ask on slide 5.** “You have the session and version. What work remains, who has it now, and what tells the desk that it has changed hands?”

| Response to discuss | What to check with the group |
|---|---|
| “I’m sending it at 14:00. I’ll put 14:00 in Receipt time and write Arjun’s name.” | Ask who supplied the time and whether that person has accepted the work. The sender can record that they asked at 14:00. They retain the task, and the receipt fields stay blank until Arjun replies. |
| “Session 6, Room change check HO-006 v1.0, due by the end of 30 Oct. Page 2 is missing. Arjun, please get it and check both pages against v1.0. When did you take it?” | Have Arjun repeat the missing page, the version check and the deadline, then give his practice receipt time, 29 Oct at 14:05 EDT. Enter Arjun and that time. Leave Delivered to learner empty. |

Open slide 6. Ask both roles to repeat the deadline and owner and compare their answers with the row. To close, ask what would let the desk fill the last cell. Listen for the complete two-page exercise reaching the learner.
