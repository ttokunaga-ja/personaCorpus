# Where recall was enough
ELM-BPE-2025-DD-002 | 13 May 2025 | Heather Callahan | To: David Ashcroft

## Context
Sean Desjardins and I reviewed FCR-REV-2025-001 together from 11:00 to 11:35 today. His review concerns the match between the 16 questions in the May practice booklet v0.9 and their aims. Four questions assigned to “Compare options under constraints” can be answered by locating a single fact: Q04 asks for the in-house maximum, Q08 for staff needed, Q11 for the print-room deadline and Q15 for the count entered on the attendance sheet. None requires a learner to compare the routes.

## Change and rationale
Keep the question numbers and the comparison aim, but change what each of these four questions asks the learner to reason through. For Q04 vary the number of kits; for Q08 vary staff availability; for Q11 compare receipt times; for Q15 make the staff information unconfirmed and ask what must be checked. In each case the response should account for the relevant conditions rather than merely repeat one number.

Keep the wording of the other twelve questions. We considered adding separate comparison questions to the booklet, but that would increase both the sixteen-question total and the time needed. Replace these four instead, within the existing sequence.

## Next review
Heather will draft the four replacements and ask Yusuf to check the reading order. The alignment sheet should show the change in required reasoning before I bring the replacement sheet to David for approval. The review does not establish learner performance or the effect of the revision.
