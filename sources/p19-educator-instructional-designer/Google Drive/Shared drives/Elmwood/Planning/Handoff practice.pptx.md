## Slide 2 — From absence to delivery

Layout: Diagram  
Title: From absence to delivery

Diagram: Reception confirms absence → Reception checks class date and session → Instructor confirms exercise and version that day → Desk checks complete copy and next-day deadline.

- From “Desk checks complete copy and next-day deadline”, branch labelled “Can deliver by deadline” → Desk arranges delivery.
- From the same check, branch labelled “Deadline at risk” → Desk reads remaining work and deadline to Arjun → Arjun accepts and gives receipt time → Arjun arranges delivery.
- Both paths join at “Learner receives complete exercise” → Record actual delivery date and time.

Speaker notes: Have reception point to where the absence is confirmed and the instructor point to where the exercise is chosen. Ask which box lets the incoming worker know the handoff has happened and which box closes delivery.
