# Two returns for Janice

ELM-BSC-2026-DD-001 · v1.0 · 14 April 2026  
From: Heather Callahan  
To: David Ashcroft and Arjun Deshmukh

## Context

The 7 April agreement calls for different work and different reporting units. My first pass put both outcomes in one field, although the reading denominator is enrolled learners and the proposal denominator is valid submitters. That line would invite a misleading comparison.

## Change and rationale

Use a separate return for each course. Capacity is a planning figure, not a result.

| Course | Return fields | Note alongside the fields |
|---|---|---|
| Reading Conditions at Work | Enrolled at deadline; people with valid submission; people meeting the standard | If an attainment percentage is shown, use enrolled as the denominator. A decision memo connects the applicable provision to the reception record. |
| Building Proposals from Evidence | Enrolled at deadline; people with valid submission; valid submitters whose writing shows all three aspects | The three aspects are evidence-based recommendation, comparison with an alternative and response to an unconfirmed assumption. The denominator for the all-aspects figure is valid submitters. |

Count people rather than files. For a resubmission by the deadline, use the last valid version. Do not include learner names or answers in Janice's return, and do not combine course outcome rates. The final reading scale remains to be settled.

## Next review

Arjun and I should check that the enrolment and submission records can supply these separate counts before the returns are needed. Fields for information collected from July onward remain unfilled in the planning copy.
