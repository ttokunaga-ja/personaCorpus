# Desk handoff — working draft

ELM-CRW-2024-DD-004 v0.1 · 22 Oct 2024 · Heather Callahan  
Reading Conditions at Work · For Kathleen Roy

## Context

Kathleen, would you try this from the reception side? Yesterday we had several entries where attendance was settled and the person at the desk still could not say what to give out. I'd like the title the instructor chooses to sit beside the attendance entry.

## Change and rationale

At confirmation, reception records the learner's anonymous reference and class date. The instructor writes the exercise title beside the entry. I’ve used F27 to show the intended entries once the instructor has supplied the title.

| Reference | Class date | Absence confirmed | Instructor’s exercise title | Check on the following day |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| F27 | 15 Oct | 15 Oct | Room change check | 16 Oct: has the learner received the exercise? Enter the actual delivery date when known. |

The following day, the person at the desk checks whether the selected exercise has reached the learner. If it has not been delivered by the end of that day, enter the remaining work in the Arjun handoff box below.

| After next-day close | Remaining work for Arjun | Date passed on |
|---|---|---|
| __________ | _________________________ | __________ |

The box only comes into use after the closing check. If the desk changes hands earlier while the exercise is still incomplete, this sheet leaves the incoming worker guessing who is supposed to finish it.
