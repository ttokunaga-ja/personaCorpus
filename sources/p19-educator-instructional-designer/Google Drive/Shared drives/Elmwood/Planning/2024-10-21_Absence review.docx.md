# October absence materials review

ELM-CRW-2024-DD-003 v1.0 · 21 Oct 2024 · Heather Callahan  
Reading Conditions at Work · Reception and Learner Support

## Context

Arjun Deshmukh and I met on 21 Oct from 13:30 to 14:05 EDT. We checked the 28 confirmed absences from Sessions 1–6 (10 Sep to 15 Oct), one per learner. The deadline for each was the end of the next calendar day after reception confirmed the absence. Twenty-two learners got their exercise by that deadline. The other six had received theirs by the time we met, a total of 11 calendar days past their deadlines.

## Change and rationale

| Point in the work | Owner | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| Day the absence is confirmed | Reception, then the instructor | Reception sends the class date and session. The instructor identifies the exercise title and version that day and sends the match back for the desk record. |
| Once delivery by the end of the next calendar day looks unlikely | Reception or Learner Support | Contact Arjun then. Give him the session, material and version if known, deadline and exact work left. Say what is still unconfirmed. |
| When the work changes hands | Arjun and the person passing it on | Arjun repeats what is left and gives his receipt time. The desk records his name and that time so the relief worker can tell who has it. |
| When the learner gets the complete exercise | Person arranging delivery | Enter the actual delivery separately from any desk transfer. |
