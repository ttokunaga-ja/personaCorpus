# Reading aims for Thursday
Reading Conditions at Work | ELM-CRW-2024-DD-001 | v0.1 | 16 Apr 2024 | Heather Callahan
To: David Ashcroft

## Context
The 4 April lesson asks learners to find three facts quickly. At the 9 April desk check, Erin located the usual deadline readily but applied it to a case that could be released under an exception. Finding the time did not show whether the reader could use the conditions around it.

## Change and rationale
I propose a 12-minute reading task using a short workplace notice and cases requiring a decision. These aims are for discussion, not yet agreed. I would time the activity to manage the lesson, but look at what a learner can do with the text rather than use speed as the achievement condition.

| Proposed aim | Observable action in twelve minutes |
|---|---|
| 1. Find the usual rule | Underline the sentence stating what normally happens and identify its deadline. |
| 2. Identify an applicability condition | Mark the words saying which cases the rule covers and match one case to them. |
| 3. Check exception requirements | List the requirements and compare each with the case facts before deciding. |
| 4. Compare competing instructions | Select the more restrictive instruction and indicate the words that make it more restrictive. |
| 5. Explain a decision with evidence | State a decision and connect a relevant clause to the case facts in a short explanation. |

A public notice and an instruction to reception may address different readers. I would like to settle this on 18 April before writing the comparison task.
