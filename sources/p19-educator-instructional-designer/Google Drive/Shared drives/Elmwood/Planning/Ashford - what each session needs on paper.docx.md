# Ashford: what each session needs on paper

Everyday Document Basics | ELM-EDB-2025-DD-001 v1.0 | 04 Feb 2025 | Heather Callahan

To: David Ashcroft and course operations

## Context

Bernadette McLean and I met at 11:00 today about the people Ashford Community Employment Centre plans to refer. We will plan for 18 referrals and eight sessions. Some people may miss a session; a device or connection should not be a prerequisite for catching up.

## Change and rationale

For each confirmed absence, send the paper material for that session by post. The paper should use the same notice that the tutor and learners read together in the room, so the learner is not asked to start with a different example at home.

In class the tutor can point back to the relevant heading. The learner should be able to find what to bring and where to meet in the notice, and check an answer against the words on the page. The paper version needs a way to do that without the tutor sitting beside the reader. We need to work out where to put those directions as the materials are prepared.

## Next review

Heather will assemble the teaching and paper materials and check whether the reading sequence works on paper. Bernadette will check the wording that advisers use when introducing the course at an initial referral appointment. We will keep the referral explanation distinct from the practice form used in class.
