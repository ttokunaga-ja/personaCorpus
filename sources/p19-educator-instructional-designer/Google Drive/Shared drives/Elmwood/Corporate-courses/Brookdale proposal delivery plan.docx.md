# Building Proposals from Evidence — Brookdale delivery plan

ELM-BPE-2026-LP-001 · v1.0 · 7 May 2026  
Heather Callahan · For Erin MacDonald and David Ashcroft

## Learning aims

For up to 24 participants, principally team leads: identify a requirement and deadline; compare workable routes using evidence; write a recommendation that distinguishes a preferred route from a commitment dependent on confirmation.

## Teaching sequence — 75 minutes

| Time | Activity and instructor action |
|---|---|
| 0–10 min | Individually read the intake request. Mark the required 72 packs, 09:00 deadline and person who can decide priority allocation. |
| 10–25 min | Read the work sheet and duty note. Record capacity, time and required confirmation for each route; identify the blank transport entry. |
| 25–40 min | In pairs compare the reception desks and shared worktable. Ask which route can meet the request, and on what evidence and condition. |
| 40–55 min | One partner acts as reception lead and asks what can be recommended while transport remains unconfirmed and what happens if confirmation does not arrive. Swap roles. |
| 55–70 min | Each participant revises a one-page proposal to the reception lead, including the other route and an action for the unconfirmed condition. |
| 70–75 min | Check that the source for each material claim is identifiable and that a recommendation is not phrased as an unconditional promise. |

Materials: Intake packs — evidence for a recommendation, ELM-BPE-2026-HO-001; participant assignment, ELM-BPE-2026-HO-002.

## Check for understanding and reporting

The planned submission is one page to the reception lead by 17 September 2026 at 16:00. Check the writing for all three: a recommendation grounded in evidence, comparison with an alternative, and a response when a necessary assumption is unconfirmed. Report course enrolment and people with a valid submission, then the number of valid submitters whose writing shows all three aspects. Count each person once and use the last valid version submitted by the deadline. The all-aspects denominator is valid submitters. Do not combine this measure with the reading course or include learner names and answers in the sponsor return.
