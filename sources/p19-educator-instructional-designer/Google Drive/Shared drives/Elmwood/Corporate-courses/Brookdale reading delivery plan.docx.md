# Reading Conditions at Work — Brookdale delivery plan

ELM-CRW-2026-LP-002 · v1.0 · 6 May 2026  
Heather Callahan · For Erin MacDonald and David Ashcroft

## Learning aims

For up to 36 participants, principally counter staff: identify the normal instruction, find the conditions that permit an exception, and use the relevant record to decide what can be said to a visitor. Participants should be able to explain a decision by connecting the applicable provision to the recorded facts, and distinguish an empty entry from a confirmed absence.

## Teaching sequence — 70 minutes

| Time | Activity and instructor action |
|---|---|
| 0–15 min | Give each participant the visitor notice only. Ask them to write the time they would tell a visitor and where they found it. Invite two provisional sentences and their source; keep these visible for later revision. |
| 15–25 min | Add the staff instruction. Participants find the normal rule and section 2, then mark the two conditions and the 12:15 recording deadline. Ask what evidence they would need before changing their sentence; do not treat either check as a substitute for the other. |
| 25–40 min | Add the counter record viewed at 12:20. In pairs, use separate fields for passage check and responsible person's approval; enter the recorded time or identify the blank. Ask partners to point to both the instruction and each record entry and decide whether the evidence permits the earlier time. |
| 40–55 min | Each participant revisits the provisional advice and drafts a counter decision memo: visitor-facing sentence, applicable section, confirmed record facts, and the outstanding question and person to ask. Prompt them to say what the blank does and does not establish. |
| 55–65 min | Partners trace each other's advice back to the provision and record. Where a claim is unsupported, mark the missing link and return the memo for revision. Ask how an answer to the outstanding question could affect the advice. |
| 65–70 min | Individuals revise their memo and identify one place where the later documents changed or supported their initial advice. Collect the short check for discussion; explain the planned submission separately. |

Materials: *Counter reopening — documents for the exercise*, ELM-CRW-2026-HO-004, including the visitor notice, staff instruction and 12:20 record; *Write the counter decision*, ELM-CRW-2026-HO-005. In the exercise the notice gives 13:00; the passage check is recorded at 12:10 and the approval field is blank. Section 2 requires both checks recorded by 12:15 for 12:30 reopening; section 3 directs staff to seek confirmation of a blank before announcing early reopening. Do not infer whether approval was given elsewhere.

## Check for understanding and reporting

The planned submission is a one-page decision memo by 11 September 2026 at 16:00 Toronto time. Check whether the visitor-facing decision is supported by the applicable instruction and the facts in the record, with the missing approval identified for inquiry rather than assumed. The final marking scale remains to be settled.

For this course report the number enrolled at the deadline, the number of people with a valid submission, and the number meeting the standard as separate figures. If an attainment rate is reported, use enrolled people as the denominator. Count a person once and use the last valid version submitted by the deadline. The 36 places are planned capacity, not an enrolment or outcome figure. Keep the return separate from the proposal course and omit learner names and answers from the sponsor report.
