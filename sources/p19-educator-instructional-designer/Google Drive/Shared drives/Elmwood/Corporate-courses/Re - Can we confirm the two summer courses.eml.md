Thank you for meeting this morning. I have attached our record of the 11:00 agreement, ELM-BSC-2026-AGR-001 v1.0.

We have 36 planned places for Reading Conditions at Work. Participants will write a decision memo connecting the applicable provision and the reception record to their advice. The separate return will show enrolment at the deadline, valid submitters and those meeting the standard; any attainment rate will use enrolment as its denominator.

For Building Proposals from Evidence we have 24 planned places. Participants will submit a recommendation to the desk lead, with evidence, an alternative and a response to an unconfirmed assumption. Its return will show enrolment and valid submitters, then the number of valid submitters whose writing includes all three aspects. We will keep the reporting fields separate and will not combine the rates. These places are capacity, not participation or results.

Brookdale will coordinate nominations and Elmwood will prepare and deliver the courses and compile the returns. The next step is to introduce the colleagues responsible for nominations and course administration so they can agree how the nominations will reach us.
