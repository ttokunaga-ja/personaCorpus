# What Janice wants in the return
ELM-BSC-2026-DD-002 · v1.0 · 12 May 2026 · Heather Callahan

## Context
On 12 May, 10:00–10:30, Janice Mitchell, Arjun Deshmukh and I reviewed the two examples and the proposed reporting fields for Brookdale's summer courses. The agreement of 7 April (ELM-BSC-2026-AGR-001) keeps the courses and their outcomes separate. The reading course has 36 planned places; the proposal course has 24. These are capacities, not post-course counts.

## Change and rationale
Janice needs to see what learners can do as well as how many submitted. The passages in the briefing are examples for explaining the intended work to the sponsor; they are not the answers to the learner exercises and are not learner submissions.

| Course | What the return needs | Denominator and limits |
|---|---|---|
| Reading Conditions at Work | Count learners whose decision connects the applicable clause to the relevant record; show valid submissions and enrolled learners separately. | If reporting an attainment proportion, use those enrolled at the 11 September 16:00 deadline. The final marking scale is not yet settled. |
| Building Proposals from Evidence | Count valid submitters whose work shows an evidence-based recommendation, comparison with the alternative, and action if the required condition is unconfirmed. | Show valid submitters and those enrolled at the 17 September 16:00 deadline; count the three-part result within valid submitters. |

Count people, not versions: where a person resubmits within the deadline, use the last valid version. Do not combine the two course rates or include names or answers in the sponsor return. Keep the planned-place figures away from fields reserved for actual enrolment, submissions and attainment.

## Next review
Arjun will check nomination categories against each course's allocation. Heather will align the terms used in the report fields and course materials so that “enrolled”, “valid submission” and the course-specific result are not used interchangeably. Review the fields again before the return is prepared; no attendance or submission result is available at this planning stage.
