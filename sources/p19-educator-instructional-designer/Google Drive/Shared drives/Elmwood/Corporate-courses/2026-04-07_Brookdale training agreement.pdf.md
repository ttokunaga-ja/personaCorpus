# Brookdale summer training agreement

Brookdale Service Cooperative · ELM-BSC-2026-AGR-001 · v1.0  
7 April 2026 · Heather Callahan, Elmwood Adult Learning Cooperative

## Agreement and scope

Janice Mitchell for Brookdale Service Cooperative and Heather Callahan for Elmwood Adult Learning Cooperative agreed the following at their meeting on 7 April 2026, 11:00–11:35 EDT. Elmwood will provide the two distinct courses during the twelve-week July–September 2026 term. The places below are planned capacity, not enrolment or results.

## Reading Conditions at Work

36 places. Participants will prepare a decision memo that identifies the applicable provision and uses the reception record as evidence for the decision and the information given to a visitor. Elmwood will report, separately, the number enrolled at the submission deadline, the number of people with a valid submission, and the number meeting the course standard. If a rate is shown, its denominator is the number enrolled. The final marking scale is not yet settled.

## Building Proposals from Evidence

24 places. Participants will prepare a proposal to the desk lead with a recommendation supported by evidence, a comparison with an alternative, and an action to take if a necessary assumption is unconfirmed. Elmwood will report the number enrolled, the number of people with a valid submission, and, among those valid submitters, the number whose writing shows all three aspects. The denominator for that last measure is valid submitters.

## Responsibilities and reporting

Brookdale will coordinate nominations. Elmwood will prepare materials, deliver the courses, make the assessments and compile a separate return for each course. A person is counted once as a submitter; if they resubmit by the deadline, the last valid version is used. The sponsor returns will contain neither learner names nor submitted answers. The two course outcome rates will not be combined.
