# Notice proposal prompt for July

Building Proposals from Evidence · ELM-BPE-2024-DD-001 v1.0 · 14 Jun 2024 · Heather Callahan

For David Ashcroft, Education Lead

## Context

Erin and I read the 14 anonymous paragraphs collected on 12 June. In five, the count of stops became a count of people who read or could read the notice. Six kept the stopping count and explicitly left reading open. The other three made no definite claim about reading, but either omitted the observation or left the distinction unclear.

The underlying record is the 22 May noon row: nine complete stops at the south entrance line between 12:10 and 12:30, with the notice still on the north wall. The sketch gives a reason to ask about the cabinet. The record gives us no reading count. In June we asked for the observation, a reason and a recommendation in one paragraph. Once those were folded together, it was difficult to see whether the writer had noticed where the count ended and the explanation began.

## Change and rationale

Erin and I agreed to give each step its own space in the July prompt.

| Section | What the writer will put there |
|---|---|
| Observation | One sentence anchored in the noon row, naming the time, entrance line and action counted. |
| Inference | One possible effect of the cabinet, with the room sketch as the reason for considering it. |
| Comparison | One way to compare the north-wall position with the east-wall candidate before asking for a permanent move. State what stays the same, what is recorded and what that measure would leave open. |

The comparison is a proposal to the person responsible for the break room. The east-wall position has yet to be observed.

## Next review

I'll write the three prompts for July. Erin and I will check that a reader can tell from the page which sentence comes from the observation and which describes a possible explanation. When we read the comparison plans, we'll listen for what the proposed measure can establish. If it is a stop count, that tells us about behaviour at the line. Reading would require different evidence.
