# Reading memo marks — before we meet
Reading Conditions at Work · ELM-CRW-2026-IR-004 · v0.1 · 14 Sep 2026 · Heather Callahan

For: Erin MacDonald

## Independent reads

Working proposal for the one-page counter decision. Please mark the same eight memos independently before we compare. The total proposed is 20 points; the allocation and treatment of the two issues below are for our discussion at 11:00.

| Area | Proposed maximum | What I am looking for |
|---|---:|---|
| Evidence | 8 | Names the applicable source and entry, identifies the facts in it and connects them to the advice given. A list of documents on its own does not show that connection. |
| Organisation | 6 | Gives the next person a usable visitor-facing answer and a route from that answer to the relevant record and next action. |
| Understanding conditions | 6 | Distinguishes the normal 13:00 time from the conditional 12:30 time, checks both requirements and identifies what to ask when an entry is blank. |

## Points to discuss

1. For organisation, should the handover require a separate paragraph, or can a brief single paragraph put the action first and still allow the next clerk to follow the reason and the question?
2. If a memo calls the blank approval field a refusal, how much should that unsupported conclusion affect understanding of the conditions? What should the feedback ask the writer to check?

## Agreement for the next read

To complete together: record the handling of these points and the three individual decisions separately from our independent marks. Then use the agreed treatment when reading the remaining submissions.
