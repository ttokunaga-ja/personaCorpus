# April 22 — marking decisions

Building Proposals from Evidence | To: Erin MacDonald

## Context
Heather Callahan and Erin MacDonald met on 22 April, 13:30–14:15, to compare independent readings of 18 valid proposals. Seven totals differed by at least three points on the 12-point guide. We used the submitted wording and the capacity information to find what each reader had credited; we did not replace the independent scores with an average.

## Change and rationale
| Proposal | Point to resolve in the reading |
|---|---|
| P02 | Heather recognised the Room B row and the 24-to-18 connection; Erin looked for the exact source title and read the unconfirmed wording more narrowly. |
| P04 | Erin supplied a capacity comparison from the choice of Room B. The response itself names the room and says everyone should fit, without a number or source. |
| P06 | Both saw the Monday check; Erin also inferred two sittings. The answer does not state that action. |
| P09 | Erin missed the capacity fact and the paraphrased proposal to postpone if confirmation is absent. |
| P12 | Erin treated sourced equipment information as evidence of demand; the source does not establish the demand. |
| P15 | Erin missed the stated two sittings in Room A because the conditional wording differed from the expected phrase. |
| P17 | Erin read expected attendance as confirmed and a request to revisit dates as a specific alternative. |

The differences fall into three overlapping reading questions: identifying the source passage, connecting a fact to the proposed decision, and reading an unstated alternative into the answer. A source title need not match word for word if the place and fact can be identified. Credit a connection that the answer makes; do not supply a missing fact or action. A confirmation deadline and the action if confirmation is absent are separate things to read.

Erin will review the examples before they are used. Keep the two original independent score files as the record of this calibration; retain the three criteria and four points per criterion.
