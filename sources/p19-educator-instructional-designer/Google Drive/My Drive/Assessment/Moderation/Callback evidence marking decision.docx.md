# Only credit what's on the page
ELM-BPE-2025-DD-004 | 14 Jul 2025 | Heather Callahan  
Building Proposals from Evidence | For instructors reading the next proposal

## Context

At the 11:00 reading with Erin MacDonald today, we looked again at Alexandre Pelletier's callback answer. It recommends Wednesday but gives no supporting fact or source. My first feedback gave full marks because the choice was correct; I had supplied the reason from my own knowledge of the case. The recommendation and the evidence for it are different things.

## Change and rationale

Keep the April guide's instruction: a source title need not be reproduced word for word when the answer identifies the place and fact. A recognisable paraphrase can tell the reader where to check. That is different from this answer, where no supporting fact is stated at all. We cannot credit a reason that the reader has supplied.

On return, comment separately on the recommendation, the support actually written, and what the answer says to do if a condition is unconfirmed. A correct choice is useful and should be acknowledged; it does not establish either the reason or a response to uncertainty. Keep the original full-marks feedback as a record of how the judgment arose, alongside the replacement explanation.

## Next review

At the next comparable read, underline the words credited for each part before checking the case sheet. Compare the identified words with the April annotated guide and discuss any place where a reader has inferred a fact or an action.
