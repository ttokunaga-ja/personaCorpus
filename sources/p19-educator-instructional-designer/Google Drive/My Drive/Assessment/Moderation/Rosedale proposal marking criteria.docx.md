# What counts as a conditional proposal
ELM-CWM-2024-IR-001 | v1.0 | 18 Nov 2024 | Heather Callahan and Erin MacDonald
Communicating in Workplace Meetings — instructor marking guide

## Evidence from the exercise
The individual task asks for a proposal on agenda item 4. Read the response for all four elements below. Use the substance of the answer, not an exact phrase or attendance, as the basis for the decision.

| Element | Evidence required in the response |
|---|---|
| Action and scope | A Saturday morning trial for two weeks, limited to six visits each Saturday. |
| Agenda-based reason | A relevant fact from the agenda, such as 8 of 30 weekday visits being rescheduled in the preceding four weeks. |
| Confirmation and deadline | The scheduling lead confirms the key rota by Thursday noon before each Saturday. |
| Unconfirmed action | Keep the weekday slots if the rota is not confirmed by that time. |

## Item decisions
**Condition without an alternative — not yet met.** “Try Saturday mornings for two weeks, six visits each day, because 8 of 30 weekday visits were rescheduled. The scheduling lead should confirm the key rota by Thursday noon.” The action and scope, reason, and confirmation with deadline are present. It does not say what happens if the rota is still unconfirmed. Do not infer the weekday action from the condition alone.

**Different wording, all elements present — met.** “Because eight of the last 30 weekday visits needed a new date, I propose a two-week Saturday morning trial, capped at six visits per Saturday. The scheduling lead needs to verify the key rota by noon Thursday before each Saturday; without that verification at noon, retain the weekday appointments.” The stated rescheduling is the reason; the trial length and daily limit give the scope; the scheduling lead, rota and noon Thursday give the condition; retaining weekday appointments supplies the unconfirmed action.

For each submitted response, record the evidence for each element and the overall decision. Mark a submitted response that lacks one or more elements as 0; mark a response with all four as 1. A non-submission has no assessment decision: leave the decision blank, rather than entering 0. Do not substitute attendance for a submitted response.
