# What we agreed about the counter memos
Reading Conditions at Work · ELM-CRW-2026-DD-005 · v1.0 · 14 Sep 2026 · Heather Callahan

## Context

Heather Callahan and Erin MacDonald, 11:00–11:45 EDT, 14 September. We compared independent reads of eight anonymous memos against the counter packet and the one-page task. The marks below are evidence / organisation / understanding conditions, in that order. Our original marks remain in the comparison sheet.

## Agreement and rationale

Use Evidence 8, Organisation 6 and Understanding conditions 6, for 20 points. Evidence asks which source and recorded fact support the decision; organisation asks whether the next clerk can find the advice and follow the next action; conditions asks whether the writer distinguishes normal advice from the requirements for early reopening and identifies what must be checked.
