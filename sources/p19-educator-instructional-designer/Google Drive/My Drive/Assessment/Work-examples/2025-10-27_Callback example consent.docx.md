# What we can teach from the callback comparison
ELM-BPE-2025-DD-006 | 27 Oct 2025 | Heather Callahan
Building Proposals from Evidence | Instructor record

## Context
On 27 October, 14:00–14:15, Grace Lim and I discussed whether the way she compared the callback options in the July trial could inform a class activity. I wanted to preserve the reasoning without presenting her individual work as a model answer.

## Change and rationale
Grace explained that showing the three roles in sequence makes the middle of the comparison visible: the 12 callbacks fit in either slot of 16; the 10 due by Wednesday 16:00 give a reason to prefer Wednesday; and the 11:40 confirmation of Wednesday cover allows the time to be promised. A choice alone would leave the links between those observations invisible.

I will use that structure with fresh wording and classroom records. The class copy and projection will contain no name, image of the answer, Grace's wording or individual feedback. We agreed to limit distribution and projection to the class using this activity. It is not for external material or another course.
