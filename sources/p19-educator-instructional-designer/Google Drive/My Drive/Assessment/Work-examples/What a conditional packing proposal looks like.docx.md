# What a conditional packing proposal looks like

ELM-BPE-2026-IR-001 · v1.0 · 4 May 2026  
Heather Callahan · For discussion with Erin MacDonald and for sponsor explanation

## Separate example record

Reception needs 80 packs by 09:00. The shared worktable can deliver 100 by 08:50 if transport duty is confirmed; the record shows confirmation at 08:10. The reception desks can prepare 60 by 09:00.

## Completed proposal

“To the reception lead: I recommend the shared worktable. Its 100 packs can arrive by 08:50, covering the 80 needed by 09:00, and transport duty was confirmed at 08:10. The reception desks could prepare 60 by 09:00, but that would leave 20 of the requirement uncovered. If transport duty cannot be confirmed, I would not promise the shared-table delivery; please decide the priority allocation of the 60 desk packs and how to cover the remaining 20.”

## Annotations

The recommendation cites the requirement, the shared-table capacity and time, and the recorded transport confirmation. The comparison states the other route's capacity and the difference from the requirement. The final sentence is a distinct contingency: if confirmation is absent, withhold a delivery commitment and ask the reception lead how to allocate the available 60 and cover the shortfall of 20. Comparing capacities alone does not supply that response. This is a constructed example, not a learner's submitted work.
