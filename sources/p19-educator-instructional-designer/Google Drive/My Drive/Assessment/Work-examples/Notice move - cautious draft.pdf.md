## Writing on the paper

To the break room coordinator,

During the twenty minutes at lunch there were nine stops at the entrance line. We do not know from that count whether the people who stopped read the notice. The cabinet might block the notice from the doorway, because it sits between the entrance and part of the north wall in the sketch. The east wall could be another place to put the notice. Before deciding to move it, I would want to know whether it is any easier to notice there. I am not sure how to check that against the present position.
