# One reopening time — three ways of getting there
Reading Conditions at Work · ELM-CRW-2026-IR-006 · v1.0 · 17 Sep 2026 · Heather Callahan

For Heather and Erin · anonymous instructor copy

## CRW-06 — an inference to check

> Please return at 13:00. The passage was checked at 12:10. Section 2 requires the passage check and approval by 12:15. Approval was refused, so the early time cannot be offered.

The ordinary advice and the passage entry are identifiable, but the record at 12:20 does not say approval was refused. It is blank. Ask the writer to replace the last sentence with: “Approval is not confirmed in the record; ask the responsible person whether and when it was recorded before offering an earlier time.” That keeps the advice distinct from the enquiry.

## CRW-17 — a usable handover

> Please return at 13:00. Section 2 requires both entries by 12:15; the passage was checked at 12:10 but approval is blank. Under section 3, ask the responsible person whether and when approval was recorded before changing the advice.

The next clerk can tell the visitor 13:00 now and ask the responsible person whether and when approval was recorded. The single paragraph gives both actions in order; it does not need a separate paragraph to be useful. Feedback can acknowledge that practical route while asking the writer to keep the record and the question explicit.

## CRW-29 — connect the evidence

> Tell the visitor 13:00 until both conditions are recorded. Sources: visitor notice; staff instruction; counter confirmation record.

The sources are named but the reader must make the connection. A possible rewrite is: “The notice gives 13:00; section 2 permits 12:30 only when the passage check and approval are both recorded by 12:15. The counter record shows the passage checked at 12:10, but at 12:20 the approval field is blank, so advise 13:00 and ask the responsible person whether and when approval was recorded.” This adds the link; it is not a fact present in the original excerpt.

## For the next feedback discussion

All three give 13:00, but that alone does not show the same understanding or use of evidence. Compare the record-to-decision link separately from the handover's usability and from the meaning assigned to the blank. Refer to the marking guide when deciding how to phrase feedback; do not add the proposed rewrites to the submitted answers.
