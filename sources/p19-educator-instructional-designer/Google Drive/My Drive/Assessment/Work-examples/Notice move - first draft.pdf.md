## Writing on the paper

To the person responsible for the break room,

At lunchtime, in twenty minutes, nine people were able to read the notice. The sketch shows that the tall cabinet is in front of part of the notice on the north wall when someone comes in from the south door. I think the notice would be easier to see on the east wall, away from the cabinet. Please move the notice permanently to the east wall so that people entering the room have a clearer view of it. The present position leaves the cabinet between the entrance and the information staff need to see.

## Sketch on the paper

The room is drawn as a small rectangle with north at the top. The opening in the south wall is labelled “door”, and a short rectangle along the north wall is labelled “notice”. Inside, towards the east, a block marked “cabinet” interrupts a pencil line from the doorway towards the notice. A separate arrow at the east wall reads “move here”. Those four labels are all the writing in the sketch.

## Paper and scan

One unsigned sheet, with the short paragraph and the sketch below it on the same side. Handwritten in pencil, with the sketch lines lighter than the words. The scan is slightly off square and the full page is visible. There are no instructor marks or scores.
