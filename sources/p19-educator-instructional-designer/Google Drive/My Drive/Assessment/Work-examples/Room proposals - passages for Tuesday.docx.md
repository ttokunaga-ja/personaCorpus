# Room proposals — passages for Tuesday
ELM-BPE-2025-IR-002 | 22 Apr 2025 | Heather Callahan
Building Proposals from Evidence — reading extracts for Erin MacDonald and Heather Callahan

## Evidence and recommendation
**P02**
> I recommend Room B at 09:00. The Room B line in the capacity table gives 24 places, so the expected 18 people can attend together. Check the assistant by Monday at 15:00; if that is not settled, we will need to reconsider the arrangement.

The reference identifies the table and Room B line and connects 24 places to the 18 expected. The final sentence does not name a specific replacement arrangement.

**P04**
> I recommend Room B at 09:00. Everyone should fit in Room B. Please use that room for the group.

The passage names a room and predicts that everyone fits; it gives no capacity number or source for that prediction.

## Confirmation and action
**P06**
> I suggest Room B for the group at 09:00. The capacity table gives it 24 places for the 18 expected. Please confirm the assistant instructor by Monday at 15:00.

The check and deadline are present; the passage does not state what to do if the assistant is unconfirmed.

**P15**
> I recommend Room B at 09:00 because the capacity table gives it 24 places for the 18 expected. Please confirm the assistant by Monday at 15:00. Without that confirmation at 15:00, run two sittings in Room A, nine people at 09:00 and nine at 11:00.

The last sentence identifies an action for the unconfirmed condition, using different wording from “if not confirmed”.

## Question for our reading
Which words did each of us credit? Separate the words on the page from an action or fact we supplied ourselves. The extracts are for discussion; consult the full proposals when assigning marks.
