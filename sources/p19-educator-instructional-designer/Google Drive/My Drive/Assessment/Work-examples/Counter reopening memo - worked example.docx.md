# What a supported reopening decision looks like

ELM-CRW-2026-IR-001 · v1.0 · 30 April 2026  
Heather Callahan · For discussion with Erin MacDonald and for sponsor explanation

## Separate example record

The visitor notice gives the normal reopening as 13:00. Section 2 of the staff instruction permits 12:30 only when both responsible-person approval and a passage check are recorded by 12:15. In this separate example the approval was recorded at 12:08 and the passage check at 12:11.

## Completed decision memo

“Please return at 12:30; the counter will reopen then. Section 2 permits early reopening when both checks are recorded by 12:15. The record shows approval at 12:08 and the passage check at 12:11, so both conditions are met.”

The visitor sentence states the decision. The section reference identifies the exception, and the two times show why each required condition is met before the cutoff. Neither time alone establishes the exception.

## Contrast for discussion

“Reopen at 12:30.” This gives a time but does not identify the provision or either recorded check. The reader cannot see why the exception applies. The example is constructed for discussion; it is not an individual learner's answer or score.
