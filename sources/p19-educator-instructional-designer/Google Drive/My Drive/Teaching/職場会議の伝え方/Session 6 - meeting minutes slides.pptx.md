# Slide 1 — Cover

What the recorder puts in the minute

Communicating in Workplace Meetings · ELM-CWM-2024-SLD-006 · 20 Nov 2024 · Heather Callahan

Speaker notes: Today we move from saying a proposal to recording what the chair has actually confirmed. The final exercise is individual.

# Slide 2 — Two columns

## A proposal is not yet a decision

| Speaker | Chair and recorder |
|---|---|
| “I propose a two-Saturday trial, up to six visits each Saturday, if the staffing coordinator confirms the key rota by Thursday noon.” | “Are we agreeing to the trial subject to that confirmation?” |
| “If it is not confirmed, keep the weekday slots.” | Record the conditional decision and what happens if the condition is not met. |

Speaker notes: Ask the group where the speaker stops and the chair's confirmation begins. Do not write “approved” simply because a proposal was made.

# Slide 3 — Table

## Three possible entries

| What the chair has confirmed | Minute entry | What it does not say |
|---|---|---|
| Approval without a condition | Approved: two Saturdays, up to six visits each Saturday. | Use only if the chair has confirmed unconditional approval. |
| Conditional decision, confirmation pending | Conditional trial held pending the staffing coordinator's confirmation of the key rota by Thursday noon; if unconfirmed, keep weekday slots. | The Saturday bookings are not yet confirmed. |
| No confirmation at noon | Key rota unconfirmed at Thursday noon; retain weekday slots. | This is not approval to schedule Saturday visits. |

Speaker notes: These are alternative meeting records, not three decisions in one meeting. Ask what the chair said before choosing an entry.

# Slide 4 — Diagram

## From speech to record

Proposal: two Saturdays; six visits each Saturday; reason from the agenda → Chair checks the proposed condition and fallback → Recorder writes the confirmed decision, including who checks and when → Chair reads the entry back.

Speaker notes: The staffing coordinator confirms the key rota by Thursday noon. The agenda reports 8 rescheduled visits out of 30 in four weeks; it is a reason to consider the trial, not evidence that the trial succeeded.

# Slide 5 — Heading and body

## Practise, then write your own proposal

In your group, take turns as proposer, chair, staffing coordinator and recorder. The chair hears that the rota is still unconfirmed at noon. State the action, check it aloud and agree the minute entry. What happens to weekday slots?

Now work individually with “Put a conditional proposal on the record”. Include the action and scope, an agenda fact, who confirms by when, and the action if confirmation is missing. Submit the final exercise by 26 November at 4:00 p.m.

Speaker notes: Listen for a complete fallback before learners leave the role practice. Do not collect the group's minute as the individual submission.
