# Slide 1 — Cover
Title: Support with a question attached
Subtitle: Communicating in Workplace Meetings | ELM-CWM-2024-SLD-005 | 06 Nov 2024 | Heather Callahan
Speaker notes: We can support an idea while asking for the information needed to act on it.

# Slide 2 — Heading and body
Title: Start with agreement
“I support trying Saturday mornings for two weeks, with no more than six visits each Saturday. Eight of the 30 weekday visits in the last four weeks had to be rescheduled.”
Speaker notes: Ask which words express support and which provide the agenda-based reason. This is a proposal, not a recorded approval.

# Slide 3 — Heading and body
Title: Then ask for confirmation
“Can the scheduling lead confirm the key rota by Thursday noon before each Saturday?”
Scheduling lead: “There is a change in the rota. I cannot guarantee confirmation by that time.”
Speaker notes: Reveal the response after the question. Ask what is known and what remains open.

# Slide 4 — Two columns
Title: Let the answer change the action
If confirmed by Thursday noon: propose proceeding with the two-Saturday trial, up to six visits each day.
If unconfirmed at noon: hold confirmation of Saturday bookings and keep the weekday slots; agree who will communicate that action.
Speaker notes: Have the proposer say both branches aloud. Do not let support for the trial stand in for the key rota confirmation.

# Slide 5 — Heading and body
Title: The chair checks the time
Chair: “The scheduling lead needs to confirm the key rota by Thursday noon before each Saturday. If it remains unconfirmed at noon, we keep the weekday slots. Who will communicate that?”
Speaker notes: Demonstrate the chair repeating the confirmation point after hearing the new information. Ask the recorder to distinguish the action agreed now from a Saturday proposal left for later.
