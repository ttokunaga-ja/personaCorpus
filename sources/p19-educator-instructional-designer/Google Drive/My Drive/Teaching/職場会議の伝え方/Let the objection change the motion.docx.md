# Let the objection change the motion
ELM-CWM-2024-LP-002 | v1.0 | 04 Nov 2024 | Heather Callahan
Communicating in Workplace Meetings — session 5, 7 November, 10:00–11:15
Instructors: Heather Callahan and Erin MacDonald | 24 learners, six groups of four

## Learning aims
Learners will respond to new information by changing a conditional proposal, distinguish support from a decision, and record the action taken separately from an item carried forward.

## Teaching sequence
| Minutes | Activity | Instructor action |
|---|---|---|
| 0–8 | Recall the two branches from session 4. | Ask for the action when the rota is unconfirmed at Thursday noon. |
| 8–18 | Read the role scenario individually, then identify the new information in pairs. | Check that a rota change is not treated as confirmation. |
| 18–28 | Demonstrate a proposal, the scheduling lead’s objection and a revised response. | Heather chairs; Erin models the revision. Ask where the action changes. |
| 28–52 | Six groups practise four six-minute rounds, rotating chair, proposer, scheduling lead and recorder. | Visit groups and listen for the person and slot named in the unconfirmed branch. |
| 52–65 | Compare records from two rounds. | Separate a decision made now from a proposal held for later; ask what would permit a fresh Saturday proposal. |
| 65–75 | Individual exit response and collection. | Ask each learner to write who will communicate the retained weekday slots if the rota is unconfirmed at noon. |

## Check for understanding
Listen for a change in the proposer’s action after the chair conveys the scheduling lead’s information. Ask: “At noon, if it remains unconfirmed, who said they would communicate which slots remain?” A group may assign a person in the exercise; do not supply an unrecorded confirmation. The recorder should distinguish the agreed present action from any Saturday proposal carried forward. Use the exit responses to identify who needs another opportunity to express both branches before the individual final exercise.

## Materials
An objection that changes the action (ELM-CWM-2024-HO-004); Support with a question attached (ELM-CWM-2024-SLD-005); paper for the recorder. Next session moves from role practice to an individual proposal for the record.
