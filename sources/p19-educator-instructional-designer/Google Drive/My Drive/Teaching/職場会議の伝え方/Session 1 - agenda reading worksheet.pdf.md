# What can we decide from this agenda?
Communicating in Workplace Meetings | ELM-CWM-2024-HO-001 | 06 Sep 2024 | Heather Callahan

## Read the agenda

Read the two items below. One reports information to the meeting; the other asks the meeting to make a decision. Mark the words that help you tell them apart.

> **3. Weekday repair attendance — update**
>
> Over the most recent four weeks, 30 weekday repair visits requiring attendance were arranged. Eight of those visits had to be rescheduled. The meeting is asked to receive this update and note the pressure on the weekday arrangements.

> **4. Saturday morning appointments — decision**
>
> Decide whether to try Saturday morning appointments for two weeks, with a maximum of six visits on each Saturday. For each Saturday, the person responsible for staffing must confirm the key rota by noon on the preceding Thursday. If the rota is not confirmed by then, retain the weekday appointment slots instead.
>
> The meeting is asked to consider the proposed trial and its conditions. No decision has yet been recorded.

Source: Maintenance handover — operations meeting agenda extract, RWHC-AGD-2024-021, Glenn Fletcher, 19 Aug 2024.
