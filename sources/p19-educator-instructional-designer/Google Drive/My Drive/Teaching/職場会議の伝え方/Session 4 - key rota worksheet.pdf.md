# What changes at noon?
Communicating in Workplace Meetings | ELM-CWM-2024-HO-003 | 17 Oct 2024 | Heather Callahan

## The decision before the meeting
Item 4 asks whether to try Saturday morning maintenance visits for two weeks, up to six visits each Saturday. In the preceding four weeks, 8 of 30 weekday visits had to be rescheduled. The scheduling lead must confirm the key rota by noon on the Thursday before each Saturday. If it is not confirmed, keep the weekday slots.

## Two versions of the same Thursday
**A — 11:40 a.m.** The scheduling lead reports that the key rota for the coming Saturday is confirmed.

**B — noon.** The scheduling lead has not confirmed the key rota for the coming Saturday. There is no confirmation at the deadline.

## Work in your group of four
1. In A, write what you would say to propose proceeding. Include the two-week trial and the limit of six visits per Saturday.
2. In B, write what you would say about the Saturday booking and the weekday slots. Do not assume an answer will arrive later.
3. Underline the information in each version that supports your action. What changed between the versions? The trial and its limit have not changed.
4. Take turns as speaker and listener. The listener points to the words that connect the action to the confirmation, then asks for anything missing.
