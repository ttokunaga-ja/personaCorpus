# Workplace meetings — outcomes

ELM-CWM-2024-RPT-001 · v0.3 · 29 Nov 2024 · Heather Callahan

## Training scope

For David Ashcroft, for review before sending to Glenn Fletcher, Rosedale Workers Housing Cooperative.

Communicating in Workplace Meetings was designed for 24 participants over six 75-minute sessions from 12 September to 21 November. We moved from identifying the decision in an agenda to making a short contribution with a reason, then to a conditional proposal. In groups of four, participants practised proposer, chair, staffing coordinator and recorder roles using the maintenance handover agenda.

The final individual exercise asked for a proposed action and scope, an agenda-based reason, the staffing coordinator's confirmation by Thursday noon, and the weekday action if the rota remained unconfirmed. Erin and I checked the submissions against all four elements. Attendance and particular wording were not conditions for the assessment.

## Interpretation and follow-up

These results describe the written final exercise. They do not establish whether the Saturday trial was implemented or whether workplace behaviour changed. Five submissions lacked at least one element; two people had no submission to assess. For a future group I suggest a five-minute practice beginning with the unconfirmed branch: name the weekday arrangement to keep, then state the condition and the proposed trial.
