# Put a conditional proposal on the record
Communicating in Workplace Meetings | ELM-CWM-2024-HO-005 | 14 Nov 2024 | Heather Callahan

## Item 4: Saturday maintenance visits
The meeting is considering a two-week trial of Saturday morning visits, with no more than six visits each Saturday. The item follows a report that 8 of 30 weekday visits were rescheduled over the preceding four weeks. The scheduling lead must confirm the key rota by Thursday noon before each Saturday. If it is not confirmed at noon, keep the weekday slots.

## Your individual proposal
Write a proposal for item 4 in the order below. Use your own words; write enough for a reader who did not hear your group discussion to follow the decision.

1. **Action and scope:** What do you propose, for how long and with what limit?
2. **Reason from the agenda:** Which fact supports trying it?
3. **Confirmation:** Who must confirm what, and by when?
4. **If unconfirmed:** What action should be taken if the confirmation is still missing at the deadline?
