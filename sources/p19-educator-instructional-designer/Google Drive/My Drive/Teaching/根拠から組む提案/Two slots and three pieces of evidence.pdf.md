# Two slots and three pieces of evidence
Building Proposals from Evidence | ELM-BPE-2025-HO-010 | 28 Oct 2025 | Heather Callahan

You are advising the duty lead about 12 callbacks. Read the records in order. After each one, write what can be said at that point; do not use a later record to fill the earlier space.

## 1. Intake and capacity
**Intake list:** 12 callbacks are waiting.

| Callback slot | Maximum number |
|---|---:|
| Wednesday 14:00–15:00 | 16 |
| Thursday 09:00–10:00 | 16 |

What can you say about whether the callbacks fit? Can the count alone tell you which slot to prefer?

____________________________________________________________

## 2. Deadline list
| Deadline | Callbacks |
|---|---:|
| By Wednesday 16:00 | 10 |
| May wait until Thursday | 2 |

What can you now say about which slot to consider first? Explain with a detail from the list.

____________________________________________________________

## 3. Duty rota
| Slot | Cover |
|---|---|
| Wednesday 14:00–15:00 | Confirmation required by Wednesday noon; confirmed 11:40 |
| Thursday 09:00–10:00 | Cover secured |

What can you now tell the duty lead? Identify the entry that makes that statement possible.
