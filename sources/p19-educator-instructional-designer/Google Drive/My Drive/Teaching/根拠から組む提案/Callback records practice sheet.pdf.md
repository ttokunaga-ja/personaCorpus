# What a callback record can establish
Building Proposals from Evidence | ELM-BPE-2025-HO-009 | 16 Sep 2025 | Heather Callahan

A record can answer one question without answering every question. Read each extract on its own before you combine them.

## 1. Will the work fit?
**Intake count:** 12 callbacks are waiting. The Wednesday 14:00–15:00 and Thursday 09:00–10:00 slots each hold up to 16 callbacks.

Both slots can accommodate the 12 callbacks. Does this count tell you which slot meets the earlier deadlines? Why or why not?

____________________________________________________________

## 2. Which should be considered first?
**Callback deadlines:** 10 of the 12 callbacks are due by Wednesday 16:00. The remaining 2 can wait until Thursday.

Which slot would you consider first, and which detail makes the difference?

____________________________________________________________

Does the deadline list tell you whether anyone is available to cover the Wednesday slot?

____________________________________________________________

## 3. Can we make the promise?
**Duty rota:** Wednesday cover must be confirmed by noon on Wednesday. It was confirmed at 11:40. Thursday cover is already secured.

What can you now tell the duty lead about Wednesday? Which entry lets you say it?

____________________________________________________________

If the Wednesday confirmation were not visible, what would remain uncertain? What would you check before promising a time?

____________________________________________________________

A count establishes capacity; a deadline can distinguish the options; a rota confirmation establishes cover. Keep the questions separate when you explain a recommendation.
