## Slide 1 — Cover

Title: Reading the doorway count

Subtitle: Building Proposals from Evidence · ELM-BPE-2024-SLD-002 v1.0 · 19 Jun 2024 · Erin MacDonald

Speaker notes: This is the short review before you return to your paragraph. The two sentences coming up were written for us to practise on.

## Slide 2 — Two columns

Title: What did we count? What would we need to check?

Left column, first practice sentence, underlined in full: “From 12:10 to 12:30, nine entries came to a complete stop at the entrance line.”

Right column, second practice sentence, underlined in full: “More people came in at lunch, and that's why there were more stops.”

Question below both columns: Which sentence can you check against a row of the table?

Speaker notes: Read both sentences aloud. Ask someone to point to what was actually counted. In the right-hand sentence, ask them to identify both the claim about how many entered and the explanation that links it to the stops.

## Slide 3 — Table

Title: May 22 observation — notice on the north wall

| 20-minute window | Complete stops at entrance line |
|---|---:|
| 10:00–10:20 | 4 |
| 12:10–12:30 | 9 |
| 14:40–15:00 | 3 |

Below the table: Total entries in each window: not recorded.

Speaker notes: The lunch row has more stops than the other two recorded rows. Ask where to find the total entries, and wait. Without that figure for each window we cannot say which was busier or compare the share of entries that stopped.

## Slide 4 — Two columns

Title: Ask two different questions

Left column heading: Reading the notice

Left column body: Did any of the people who stopped read it? What would tell you?

Right column heading: Traffic through the doorway

Right column body: Did more entries occur at lunch? What count would you need in each window?

Speaker notes: For the first question, ask for evidence about what someone read. For the second, ask for all entries during the same windows, including entries that did not stop. Have learners reread the sentence in their paragraph that uses the number nine.
