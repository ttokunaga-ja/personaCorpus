## Slide 2
Layout: Heading and body
Title: Start with the request

The desk needs **72 intake packs by 09:00**.

Find that requirement in the request. Underline the quantity and time. What would the desk lead need to know before relying on a route?

Notes: Have learners identify the source before reading the estimates. Keep quantity and deadline visible during comparison.

## Slide 3
Layout: Diagram
Title: Put both routes on the same page

Comparison grid to complete from the two estimates:

| | Counter work | Shared table |
|---|---|---|
| Capacity | __________ | __________ |
| Time available or delivered | __________ | __________ |
| Person or condition to confirm | __________ | __________ |
| Advantage and limit against the request | __________ | __________ |

Notes: Leave cells empty for learners. Ask where each number comes from and whether each route meets both quantity and time. Do not infer a confirmation from a blank entry.

## Slide 4
Layout: Two columns
Title: If the condition is still unconfirmed

| Check | Sentence to the desk lead |
|---|---|
| Who can confirm the duty? By when? | “I will ask __________ by __________.” |
| What can proceed while waiting? What remains? | “Until confirmed, I propose __________; I will ask you about __________.” |

Notes: Invite distinct interim actions. Check that learners identify the remaining need without presenting an unconfirmed route as certain.

## Slide 5
Layout: Heading and body
Title: Recommendation or commitment?

Address one page to the desk lead: your recommendation, both options with their evidence, and what you will do if the duty check is not available in time.

Use *Intake packs – evidence for a recommendation* and *Send the desk lead a packing recommendation*. Submit by **17 September, 16:00**.

What can you recommend now, and what can you actually promise?

Notes: End with the distinction between preferring a route and guaranteeing it. Point to the assignment sheet for the order and source references.
