## Slide 2 — Diagram

Title: What counted as a stop?

Diagram: A close overhead view of the south doorway. A dotted segment bridges the gap in the wall and is labelled “Observation line”. An arrow from outside ends at a point on the line, labelled “Complete stop”.

Text: The count went up by one each time an entry came to a complete stop at the line. Entries that kept moving did not add to the count. Repeat entries were counted by the same rule.

Speaker notes: Point to the line before talking through the movement. Ask whether an entry that slows down and continues would be counted. Confirm that the observer needed to see a complete stop at the line.

## Slide 4 — Heading and body

Title: Write one sentence

Text: Choose one row and write on your own. Give the time window, the count and the action that was recorded. Check your wording against the headings in the table.

Speaker notes: Leave time for each learner to write before taking any answers. During the next discussion, ask learners to point back to their row. Hand out the paper floor sketch after the first writing and the check against the table.
