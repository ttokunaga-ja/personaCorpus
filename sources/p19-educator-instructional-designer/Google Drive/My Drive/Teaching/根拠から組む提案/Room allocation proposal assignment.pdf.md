# One room or two sittings?
Building Proposals from Evidence | ELM-BPE-2025-HO-001 | 15 Apr 2025 | Heather Callahan

## The decision
The operations team expects 18 participants on Tuesday. It needs your recommendation for a room and schedule. The information available today is below.

| Room capacity and schedule | Places | Tuesday arrangement | Staffing |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Room A | 16 | 09:00 and 11:00; nine participants at each sitting | One instructor can run both sittings. |
| Room B | 24 | 09:00; all 18 together | An assistant instructor must be confirmed by Monday 15:00. |

Source: Room capacity and schedule, operations planning information for Tuesday.

## Your task
Write a proposal of 120–180 words to the operations team. Recommend an arrangement and compare the burden of both options. Identify where your supporting facts come from and explain how they support your recommendation. Say what needs to be checked and what action you propose if the assistant instructor for Room B has not been confirmed by Monday at 15:00. Do not treat an unconfirmed assistant as available.

Submit by Thursday 17 April, 16:00.
