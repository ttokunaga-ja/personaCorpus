# Compare first, then make the recommendation
ELM-BPE-2025-LP-001 | 27 May 2025 | Heather Callahan
Building Proposals from Evidence | 28 May 2025, 10:00–10:35 | Heather Callahan and Erin MacDonald

## Learning aims
Learners compare in-house preparation and the print room against quantity, staffing and time conditions; identify the evidence behind each route's feasibility; and name a contact and question when a necessary fact is unconfirmed.

## Teaching sequence

| Time | Activity | Lead and materials |
|---|---|---|
| 10:00–10:05 | Read the kit preparation conditions. Mark the capacity, staff requirement, print-room receipt deadline, delivery time and cost rule separately. | Heather; booklet conditions and four-question replacement sheet |
| 10:05–10:15 | In pairs, complete Q04 and Q08. Compare 18 and 22 kits with two staff, then one and two staff for 18 kits. Give each route's status and evidence before choosing. | Erin circulates; Heather invites pairs to explain which condition changed |
| 10:15–10:23 | Compare Q11 at 11:40 and 12:10 for the same 22 kits. Use the time and capacity diagram; discuss why capacity fails both times but the deadline changes the print-room result. | Heather; Time and capacity are different checks |
| 10:23–10:32 | Individually write Q15's question to the operations contact and a message to the requester if confirmation is unavailable. Discuss the timely print-room alternative and the after-noon situation. | Erin; When neither route is available |
| 10:32–10:35 | Exit reread: check each route's feasibility separately, then identify the contact for an unconfirmed fact and the message to the requester if no route can be promised. | Heather collects responses |

## Check for understanding
For Q04, 18 with two staff permits both routes and the lower-cost in-house choice; 22 exceeds in-house capacity. For Q08, one available person does not meet the two-person requirement; with two, both routes can work and in-house has no added cost. At 11:40 Q11's 22 kits can go to the print room; at 12:10 neither route is feasible under the stated conditions. Do not infer an expedited service. For Q15, unconfirmed does not mean zero. Ask operations by 11:30; if confirmation is unavailable, use the print room before noon. After noon without staff secured, tell the requester and discuss the date rather than promise delivery.

Ask learners separately: “Can each route meet the conditions?” and “Who will you contact if the fact remains unconfirmed?” A correct deadline alone is not an explanation of both routes.
