# Write the question before you promise a date

Building Proposals from Evidence  
ELM-BPE-2025-HO-005 · 29 May 2025 · Heather Callahan

## Before you write

Kits are needed by Thursday 10:00. In-house preparation can handle up to 20 kits and needs two staff; it uses stock at no additional cost. The print room takes orders for 16–32 kits at CAD 2.50 each. It must receive the request by Wednesday noon for delivery Thursday at 09:00. When both routes are possible, choose the lower additional cost.

For each situation, use the information above. Write a separate line for the evidence, the question and your reply. Do not assume an arrangement that the information does not confirm.

## Check your reply

- Have I separated an unconfirmed fact from a fact that is known to be false?
- Have I named the person and the information I need before deciding?
- Have I avoided promising a date or service that the available information does not support?
- If the check is unresolved, have I said what I can tell the requester now?
