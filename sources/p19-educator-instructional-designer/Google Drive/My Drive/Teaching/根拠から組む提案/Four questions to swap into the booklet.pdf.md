## Preparation notes
Kits are needed by Thursday at 10:00. The requester chooses the route after receiving your recommendation. The operations contact can check the attendance record and staff availability.

| Route | Quantity and staffing | Cost | Timing |
|---|---|---|---|
| In-house | Up to 20 kits; two staff needed | Supplies in stock; no additional cost | Prepare for Thursday 10:00 |
| Print room | 16–32 kits | CAD 2.50 per kit | Order received by Wednesday noon; delivery Thursday 09:00 |

If both routes are feasible, choose the lower additional cost. No expedited service is promised in these notes.
