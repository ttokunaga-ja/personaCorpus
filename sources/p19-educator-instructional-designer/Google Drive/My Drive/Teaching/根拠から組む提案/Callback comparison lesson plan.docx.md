# Wait before making the promise
ELM-BPE-2025-LP-002 | 29 Oct 2025 | Heather Callahan
Building Proposals from Evidence | 30 Oct 2025, 10:00–10:30 | Heather Callahan and Erin MacDonald

## Learning aims
Learners distinguish capacity, deadline and cover as separate grounds for a callback recommendation. They can explain why a preferred slot is not yet a promise when cover is unconfirmed, and identify a next check without losing sight of the deadline.

## Teaching sequence
| Time | Activity | Instructor action |
|---|---|---|
| 10:00–10:04 (4 min) | Identify the three record types: intake count, deadline list, duty rota. | Heather asks what question each record can answer; Erin listens for capacity being confused with priority. |
| 10:04–10:14 (10 min) | In pairs, read the records in order and write one judgment after each: both slots fit; Wednesday is preferred for the 10 deadlines; confirmed cover permits a promise. | Heather reveals one record at a time and pauses for writing. Erin asks pairs which detail supports each line. |
| 10:14–10:23 (9 min) | Hide Wednesday's rota confirmation. Discuss what remains known, what must be held open and what to do next. | Heather waits before pointing to the missing entry. Erin asks each pair what they are holding back and exactly where in the records they found the facts behind their judgment. Discuss a Wednesday alternative with the duty lead; only two can wait until Thursday. |
| 10:23–10:30 (7 min) | Individuals complete the anonymous exit prompt and hand it in. | Heather collects the slips; Erin helps check whether the record selected supports each statement. |

## Check for understanding
Use the comparison sheet, projection and exit prompt. Look for a statement that both slots fit, a reason to prefer Wednesday based on the 10 deadlines, and a distinction between preferring Wednesday and promising it. If cover is unknown, ask who can check it and how the duty lead can consider an alternative Wednesday response. Do not treat an unknown confirmation as proof that cover is unavailable.
