## Slide 2 — Heading and body
Title: Start with the request
Body: The desk needs 72 intake packs by 09:00. Find the required quantity and deadline in the request. What decision must go to the desk lead?
Speaker notes: Allow 10 minutes to read and mark the request. Ask where the decision authority appears before taking an answer.

## Slide 3 — Two columns
Title: Compare the routes
Left: Reception counters: simultaneous work produces up to 54 packs by 09:00.
Right: Shared table: up to 90 packs delivered at 08:50 if a carrier is confirmed by 08:15. The carrier field is blank.
Speaker notes: Spend 15 minutes reading the work sheet and duty note; identify quantity, delivery time and condition. Ask learners to point to the source of each number. A blank confirmation is not a confirmed carrier.

## Slide 4 — Diagram
Title: Before and after the check
Diagram: Two separate empty speech bubbles labelled “My recommendation before confirmation” and “My recommendation after confirmation”. Between them: “What was confirmed? Where is the record?”
Speaker notes: Give 15 minutes for paired comparison and writing. Keep the bubbles distinct: a conditional preference is not a promise that all 72 will arrive.

## Slide 5 — Two columns
Title: Desk lead questions
Left: “When will that number arrive?”
Right: “If you cannot confirm the carrier, how many packs can you put through first?”
Speaker notes: Allow 15 minutes in pairs. One person is the desk lead and asks both questions; the other recommends a route using the papers. After the first exchange, swap roles. Each person writes their own before-and-after bubbles; do not supply a confirmation. The 54-pack counter capacity leaves 18 of the 72 to be addressed. The lead decides priority allocation; ask rather than assume which packs go first.

## Slide 6 — Heading and body
Title: Revise the one-page recommendation
Body: Address the desk lead. State the recommended route and the evidence, compare the other route, and say what you will ask or do if the carrier remains unconfirmed.
Speaker notes: Use 15 minutes to revise and 5 minutes to close. Pick up evidence actually written on the page, including its relationship to the recommendation and the unconfirmed condition. Do not judge the work by speaking fluency or infer a written comparison from an oral explanation.
