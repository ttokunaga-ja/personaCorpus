# Notice recommendation example
Building Proposals from Evidence · ELM-BPE-2024-HO-008 v1.0 · 12 Jul 2024 · Heather Callahan

If you missed the 10 July session or are revising your plan, read the request below. Find the sentence with the recorded observation and the sentence that asks for a next step.

## To the person responsible for the break room

On 22 May, we counted nine entries that came to a complete stop at the south entrance line between 12:10 and 12:30, with the notice on the north wall. The cabinet may block the view of the notice from that part of the doorway. Could we discuss a short comparison before deciding whether to move the notice permanently? On two nearby weekdays we could use the same notice, first at the current position and then at the east-wall candidate, with one notice up each day. We would count complete stops at the same line from 12:10 to 12:30 each day. That would give us two counts of doorway behaviour. Whether people read the notice would still be open.

Margin note: The nine stops came from the north-wall observation on 22 May. Both 20-minute windows in the suggested trial are future observations. So far there is no east-wall count to compare with the May record.

## Read it back

Underline the words that make the cabinet explanation tentative. In your own paragraph, check that the request tells the reader both positions, the shared window and what you would count.
