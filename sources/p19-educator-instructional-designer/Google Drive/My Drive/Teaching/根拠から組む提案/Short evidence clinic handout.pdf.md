# Short evidence clinic
Building Proposals from Evidence · ELM-BPE-2024-HO-009 v1.0 · 18 Jul 2024 · Heather Callahan

Bring these three short answers to the consultation. Start with this summary of the midday row in the doorway record.

> On 22 May, with the notice on the north wall, nine entries came to a complete stop at the entrance line from 12:10 to 12:30.

Imagine someone stopping to hold the door and let another person through. That is one possible reason for a stop. The record gives the number of stops and leaves their reasons open.

## Before you bring it in

Read your recommendation and your question together. Would the break room contact's answer address the gap in the evidence you used? Underline the claim you can trace to the record and put a question mark beside anything you still need to find out.
