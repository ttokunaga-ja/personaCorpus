# Send the desk lead a packing recommendation
Building Proposals from Evidence · ELM-BPE-2026-HO-002 · v1.0 · 22 May 2026 · Heather Callahan

## The request
The desk needs 72 intake packs by 09:00. Read *Intake packs – evidence for a recommendation* (ELM-BPE-2026-HO-001), including the request and both work estimates. Write a one-page recommendation addressed to the desk lead.

## Organise your page
1. Start with your recommendation and when it can be acted on. Make clear whether it is a recommendation subject to a check or a commitment already supported by the record.
2. Compare both routes. For each, identify its useful feature and its limit against the requested quantity and time. Point to the relevant estimate and figures rather than saying only that one is faster.
3. Identify the duty confirmation that matters, the person you would ask and the time by which the check is needed. If the confirmation cannot be obtained in time, tell the desk lead whom you will consult and what you propose as an interim measure for the available packs and the remainder.
4. Close with the decision or confirmation you need from the desk lead. Refer to the request and each estimate by title and the relevant entry so that the comparison can be checked.

You may use a short comparison table followed by a recommendation. Do not assume that a blank duty entry means either that a person is available or that nobody is available.

## Submit
Submit one page addressed to the desk lead by 17 September 2026 at 16:00 Toronto time. Your page must contain the recommendation, comparison and response to an unconfirmed condition.
