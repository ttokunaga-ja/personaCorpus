# Notice trial recording card
Building Proposals from Evidence · ELM-BPE-2024-HO-007 v1.0 · 08 Jul 2024 · Heather Callahan

## One possible set-up

Choose two nearby weekdays. Observe for 20 minutes, from 12:10 to 12:30, on each day. Use one copy of the same notice each day, with the same heading, paper size and page layout. Use the north wall on one day and the east-wall candidate on the other. Display only one copy during each observation.

## Record at the south entrance line

Make one tally each time an entry comes to a complete stop at the line. Write the date and the count for each window. Note any interruption that made the timing or the line hard to observe.
