# A proposal for the break room notice

Building Proposals from Evidence | ELM-BPE-2024-HO-003 | v1.0 | 05 Jun 2024 | Heather Callahan

## Writing task — 12 June

Write to the person responsible for the break room. The notice is on the north wall. From 12:10 to 12:30 on 22 May, nine entries came to a complete stop at the entrance line.

Write one paragraph of 80–120 words. Use the count of nine, explain a reason suggested by the room sketch and ask for a specific change to the notice's location. Look at where the cabinet stands in relation to the current notice and the possible position on the east wall. Give your reader enough detail to understand where you want the notice moved and why you think that location would help.

Leave your name off the page. Hand in your paragraph at the end of class.
