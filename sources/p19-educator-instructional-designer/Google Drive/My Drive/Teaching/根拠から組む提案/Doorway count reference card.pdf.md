Building Proposals from Evidence · ELM-BPE-2024-HO-004 v1.0 · 19 Jun 2024 · Heather Callahan

## The rule used at the door

On 22 May the notice was in its current position on the north wall. During each 20-minute window, the observer counted an entry when it came to a complete stop at the line across the south entrance. Slowing down did not meet the rule. Repeat entries were counted as events without identifying the people entering.
