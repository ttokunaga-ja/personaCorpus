# Proposal practice: preparing training kits

Building Proposals from Evidence  
ELM-BPE-2025-HO-003 | v0.9 | 06 May 2025 | Heather Callahan

## Before you begin

Read the preparation notes and answer all 16 questions. Use the notes as your evidence. When a question asks for a reason, point to the relevant detail and explain how it affects the decision. If a detail has not been confirmed, say what you would check rather than treating it as settled.

## Preparation notes

A training session needs kits by **Thursday at 10:00**. The person who requested the kits will choose between in-house preparation and the print room after receiving your recommendation. Give the requester your recommendation before the print room's Wednesday noon order deadline. The operations contact can check the attendance record and whether staff are available for in-house preparation.

| Route | Conditions |
|---|---|
| In-house | Up to 20 kits. Two staff members are needed. Supplies are already in stock, so there is no additional cost. |
| Print room | 16–32 kits at CAD 2.50 per kit. An order must be received by Wednesday at noon for delivery at 09:00 Thursday. |

If both routes are feasible, choose the route with the lower additional cost. The notes do not promise an expedited print-room service.

### Records available on Wednesday morning

- Attendance sheet, updated Wednesday 09:00: **18 kits** in the count field.
- Earlier attendance sheet, Tuesday 15:00: 16 kits in the count field.
- Draft staffing schedule, Wednesday 09:15: two people are **expected** for in-house preparation. The operations contact has not yet confirmed their availability.
- Print-room rate: CAD 2.50 per kit, as shown in the print-room conditions above.
