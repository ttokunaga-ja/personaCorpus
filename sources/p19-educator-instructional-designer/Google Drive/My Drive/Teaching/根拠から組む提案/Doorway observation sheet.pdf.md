## Break room observation — 22 May 2024

Use the dotted observation line at the south entrance on the break room floor sketch. Keep the notice in its current position. For each 20-minute window, write the start and finish times in local time. Use a fresh row for each window.

Make one tally each time an entry comes to a complete stop at the line. Leave out anyone who slows down and keeps moving. Count a later entry by the same rule without trying to match it to an earlier entry. At the end of the window, add the marks in that row.
