# Which callback slot should we offer?
Building Proposals from Evidence | ELM-BPE-2025-HO-006 | 08 Jul 2025 | Heather Callahan

Brookdale Service Cooperative needs a callback slot for the people on its current intake list. Read the three extracts separately. Write a short recommendation to the reception lead. Explain which record supports your choice and what you would communicate if a condition for the slot changed.

## Intake list — extract

| Current intake | Number of callbacks |
|---|---:|
| Calls awaiting a callback | 12 |

## Callback deadline list — extract

| Latest acceptable callback | Number of calls |
|---|---:|
| Wednesday by 16:00 | 10 |
| Thursday is acceptable | 2 |
| Total | 12 |

## Slot capacity and duty rota — extract

| Proposed slot | Maximum callbacks | Cover |
|---|---:|---|
| Wednesday 14:00–15:00 | 16 | Duty cover must be confirmed by noon Wednesday; confirmed at 11:40 Wednesday. |
| Thursday 09:00–10:00 | 16 | Duty cover secured. |

## Your recommendation

To: Reception lead

1. Which slot do you recommend? State your choice clearly.
2. Which facts from the records support it? Explain how they affect your choice, rather than simply listing numbers.
3. If the condition for your chosen slot is no longer met, what would you tell the reception lead and what would you ask them to check or arrange?
