# Eight Thursdays — first pass

Everyday Document Basics | ELM-EDB-2025-LP-001 v0.2 | 06 Feb 2025 | Heather Callahan

For discussion with Erin. Eight sessions for the 18 people Ashford expects to refer; session dates and timings still to be settled.

## Learning aims

Read an everyday notice for the action it asks for, find the words that support an answer, and distinguish an original instruction from a later change. Practise using a form and asking for information a notice does not supply.

## Teaching sequence

| Session | Reading and practice | Tutor prompt |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Invitation headings: find what to bring and where to meet. Show the full invitation and reply slip together on one screen. | Point to the relevant words: “Look here.” Ask for the answer, then check together. |
| 2 | Read the reply slip and enter a name and item in the appropriate spaces. | Which box asks for the item? Which date asks for a reply? |
| 3 | Read a notice changing an earlier instruction. | What has changed and where was it stated? |
| 4 | Find a reply deadline and distinguish it from the date of an activity. | What must be done by this date? |
| 5 | Read a list of things to bring. | Which items apply to you? |
| 6 | Compare two communications on the same activity. | Which details still agree and which are different? |
| 7 | Identify an unanswered question and practise asking it. | What does the notice tell us already? What do we still need to ask? |
| 8 | Read an unfamiliar notice independently and check the answer against its text. | Show a partner where you found each answer. |

## Check for understanding

For each exercise ask for both an answer and the words or heading used to find it. In the first session, point to the part of the invitation and compare the group's answers after they have tried it.

## Open question for Erin

The tutor can point and check in the room. Can someone who misses the session follow that same sequence on paper at home, without hearing “look here”? We need to try the paper before deciding what directions belong on it.
