# What goes on the reply slip?
Everyday Document Basics | ELM-EDB-2025-HO-002 v1.0 | 28 Feb 2025 | Heather Callahan

## Page 1 — Read and practise

### Invitation
Saturday Repair Table

Saturday 3 May, 10:00–11:30

Bring an item to the repair table and join us for the morning.

**What to bring:** one small item and this invitation

**Where to meet:** East entrance, beside the bicycle rack

### Reply slip — practice only
Please reply by 30 April, 16:00.

Name: __________________________________

Item: ___________________________________

Imagine your name is Alex Morgan and you are bringing a small box with a loose handle. Write the practice name and item in the correct blanks above. Keep the slip; do not send it.

1. When must the reply be sent? ____________________
2. When does the repair table begin? ____________________
3. Which words tell you what each time is for? ____________________

Turn over to compare your answers with the invitation and slip.

## Page 2 — Check the slip

Name: **Alex Morgan**. The Name label asks for the person's name.

Item: **small box with a loose handle**. The Item label asks what is being brought. If you put the box in Name, move it to Item and put the practice name in Name.

## Check the two times

The reply is due **30 April, 16:00**: the words “Please reply by” are immediately above the Name and Item blanks on the Reply slip. The repair table begins **3 May, 10:00**: the invitation says “Saturday 3 May, 10:00–11:30” beneath its title. The 11:30 is the end of that time range.

Compare each time with the action you wrote. If you swapped them, return to the reply instruction and the invitation date and correct both answers.
