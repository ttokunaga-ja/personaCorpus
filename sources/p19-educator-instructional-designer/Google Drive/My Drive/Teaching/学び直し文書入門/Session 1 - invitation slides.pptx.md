# Read the invitation before the reply slip

## Slide 1 — Title
Layout: title
Title: Read the invitation before the reply slip
Subtitle: Everyday Document Basics | ELM-EDB-2025-SLD-001 v1.0 | 26 Feb 2025 | Heather Callahan
Speaker notes: Today we will find an answer and show the words that support it before opening the slip.

## Slide 2 — Invitation
Layout: content
Title: Saturday Repair Table
Body:
Saturday 3 May, 10:00–11:30

Bring an item to the repair table and join us for the morning.

What to bring: one small item and this invitation

Where to meet: East entrance, beside the bicycle rack
Speaker notes: Give everyone time to read the invitation. Keep the slip out of view. Ask what sort of information the headings might contain.

## Slide 3 — Find what to bring
Layout: content
Title: What two things should you bring?
Body: With a partner, point to your answer and the heading that helped you.
Speaker notes: Return to slide 2. Invite pairs to point to “one small item and this invitation” and “What to bring”. Check both items before continuing.

## Slide 4 — Find where to meet
Layout: content
Title: Where should you go?
Body: With a partner, point to your answer and the heading that helped you. Include the landmark.
Speaker notes: Return to slide 2. Ask pairs to point to “East entrance, beside the bicycle rack” and “Where to meet”. Check the landmark as well as the entrance.

## Slide 5 — Open the reply slip
Layout: content
Title: Reply slip — practice
Body: Please reply by 30 April, 16:00.

Name: __________________________________

Item: ___________________________________

This is a practice slip. Keep it; do not send it.
Speaker notes: Open the slip only after pairs have located both answers and headings. Ask what each blank requests; do not treat this as the course application.

## Slide 6 — Check before leaving
Layout: content
Title: Two answers and where you found them
Body: What two things should you bring? Where should you meet? For each answer, name the heading you read.
Speaker notes: Listen for both items and the full location, including the bicycle rack, with What to bring and Where to meet as the respective evidence. Return to the invitation if anything is missing.
