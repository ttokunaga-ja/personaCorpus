# Paper reading — fix the two pointers

Everyday Document Basics | ELM-EDB-2025-IR-001 v1.0 | 19 Feb 2025 | Heather Callahan

To: Yusuf Mahmood

## What we saw on 18 February

We read the first paper proof of the Saturday Repair Table without relying on a tutor's voice. Q1 says “this invitation” and Q2 asks where to go “there”, but neither question tells the reader which heading in the invitation to return to. The marks on the print trace Q1 back to What to bring and Q2 to Where to meet. The small circle at Name also records where attention went to the form before the invitation was read.

## Revision to make

Keep the invitation before the questions. Immediately below Q1, add a direction to return to What to bring; immediately below Q2, add a direction to return to Where to meet. Put “practice” directly above the Reply slip so the reader knows before reaching the boxes that it is an exercise. These cues should let the reader proceed without a tutor pointing at the page.

## Check after the questions

Place the answer check after the questions, so there is room to try first. Show the invitation's own wording alongside each answer. For the meeting place, check both the East entrance and the landmark beside the bicycle rack; naming the entrance alone leaves out part of the direction. The check should invite the reader to add missing words after comparing the answer with the notice.

Heather
