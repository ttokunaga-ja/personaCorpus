# Which meeting place changed?

Everyday Document Basics | ELM-EDB-2025-HO-003 v1.0 | 4 Mar 2025 | Heather Callahan

## Page 1 — Read and compare

Read the two notices. Imagine it is raining on the day of the event. Circle your answers on this page. Then turn the sheet over to check them.

### Original invitation

Issued 14 April 2025

**Saturday Repair Table**

Saturday 3 May, 10:00–11:30

Bring an item to the repair table and join us for the morning.

**What to bring:** one small item and this invitation

**Where to meet:** East entrance, beside the bicycle rack

### Update: meeting place in rain

1 May 2025

For Saturday Repair Table, if it is raining, meet at the **Main entrance, inside the glass doors**, instead of the East entrance. This update changes the meeting place in rain only. The event time and what to bring stay the same.

### Your turn

1. If it is raining, where should you meet? Circle the new place in the update.
2. Circle the event's starting time in the original invitation. Has it changed? ______
3. Circle what you should bring in the original invitation. Has it changed? ______

Turn over to check what changed.

## Page 2 — Check what changed

Look at the dates and headings first. The original invitation was issued on **14 April 2025**. The update is dated **1 May 2025**, so the update came later. Its heading says **meeting place in rain**: it tells you which part of the invitation to check and when the change applies.

1. **Changed in rain:** Under Where to meet, the original says “East entrance, beside the bicycle rack”. The later update says “Main entrance, inside the glass doors”. Check that you circled the new place in the update, including “inside the glass doors”.
2. **Still the same — starting time:** The original says Saturday 3 May, **10:00**. The update says the event time stays the same. Check that you circled 10:00 and wrote “no”.
3. **Still the same — what to bring:** Under What to bring, the original says **“one small item and this invitation”**. The update says what to bring stays the same. Check that you circled both things and wrote “no”.

If you thought the update changed the time or what to bring, read its heading and the last sentence again. The change is to the meeting place **if it is raining**. Cross out the extra change you marked, then return to the original invitation for the time and what to bring. The update does not say that the meeting place changes when it is not raining.
