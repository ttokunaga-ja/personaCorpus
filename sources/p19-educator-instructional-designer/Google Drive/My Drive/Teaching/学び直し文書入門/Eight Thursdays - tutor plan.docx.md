# Eight Thursdays — tutor plan
Everyday Document Basics | ELM-EDB-2025-LP-001 v1.0 | 19 Mar 2025 | Heather Callahan

For Heather Callahan and Erin MacDonald. Eight planned sessions for the 18 people Ashford expects to refer. Thursdays 10:00–11:00, 3 April to 22 May 2025. For a confirmed absence, send the corresponding paper listed below.

## Learning aims

Learners find an action in an everyday notice, show the heading and words supporting an answer, distinguish a later change from unchanged instructions, and identify information that needs a question rather than a guess. They practise transferring information to a form and checking an unfamiliar notice independently.

## Teaching sequence

### 1 — 3 April: invitation and evidence
Aim: Find both things to bring and the full meeting place, and point to the supporting headings.

0–10 min: Introduce the task and read the invitation together. 10–25: Project SLD-001 slide 2, invitation only; pairs locate “one small item and this invitation” and “East entrance, beside the bicycle rack”. 25–40: Pairs point to each answer and its What to bring or Where to meet heading. 40–50: Open slide 5 and discuss the practice Reply slip. 50–60: Individual exit check: give both answers and say where each was found.

Postal: HO-001 v1.0, Read the invitation at home, one double-sided sheet. Q1 and Q2 point back to the same headings immediately beneath each question; the reverse Check your answers gives the words to compare and repair an incomplete answer. These correspond to pointing and checking in class.

### 2 — 10 April: reply slip
Aim: Put a person's name and an item in their respective fields and distinguish reply deadline from event start.

0–10: Re-read the Repair Table invitation. 10–25: Locate Name, Item and “Please reply by”. 25–40: Practise with Alex Morgan and a small box with a loose handle. 40–50: Compare 30 April 16:00 for replying with 3 May 10:00 for the event start, using SLD-002. 50–60: Each learner labels both times with the action and points to the source.

Postal: HO-002 v1.0, What goes on the reply slip, one double-sided sheet; Check the slip and Check the two times.

### 3 — 17 April: change notice
Aim: Read the scope of a later change and retain unchanged details.

0–10: Recall the original invitation. 10–25: Read the 1 May rain update and compare its date and heading with Where to meet. 25–40: Pairs identify the changed entrance and the unchanged start and items, using SLD-002. 40–50: Discuss why the change applies in rain. 50–60: Individually state one changed and two unchanged details with sources.

Postal: HO-003 v1.0, Which meeting place changed, one double-sided sheet; Check what changed.

### 4 — 24 April: two Fridays
Aim: Associate each date with the correct action, including the date and time.

0–10: Read the equipment loan notice. 10–25: Find Return by and Next collection. 25–40: Transfer each action into a separate diary entry. 40–50: Compare Friday 16 May at noon with Friday 23 May at 10:00. 50–60: Ask learners to explain which heading supports each entry.

Postal: HO-004 v1.0, Which Friday does the form mean, one double-sided sheet; Check the two Fridays.

### 5 — 1 May: list of items
Aim: Distinguish what everyone brings from what applies only to a role.

0–10: Read the indoor preparation notice. 10–25: Mark the What to bring list. 25–40: Compare gloves for everyone with pencil for the marking role. 40–50: Pairs describe their selected role and items. 50–60: Individually give an item and the wording that makes it applicable.

Postal: HO-005 v1.0, Read the list beside the reminder, two double-sided sheets; use What to bring and its check.

### 6 — 8 May: additional message and role
Aim: Apply a change only to the role named in an additional message.

0–10: Revisit the list and roles. 10–25: Read the additional message. 25–40: Match a person's role to the instruction changing the marking role's meeting place to West door. 40–50: Compare answers in pairs. 50–60: Each learner states who uses the west door and identifies the supporting words.

Postal: HO-005 v1.0, the complete two-sheet booklet; use Who uses the west door and its check.

### 7 — 15 May: ask reception
Aim: Separate a stated arrival door from missing late-arrival information and ask one clear question.

0–10: Read the room preparation notice. 10–25: Use SLD-003 to place West door in the known column and late-arrival direction in the missing column. 25–40: Draft one question including the known door. 40–50: Practise asking in pairs. 50–60: Each learner identifies the missing information and asks the question without inventing an answer.

Postal: HO-006 v1.0, Ask one thing and check a new notice, two double-sided sheets; Ask reception and its check.

### 8 — 22 May: unfamiliar notice
Aim: Independently find items, place and reply deadline, then show the supporting words.

0–10: Explain the independent reading task. 10–25: Read the 7 June notice-board notice and write three answers with evidence. 25–40: Pairs compare the What to bring, Where to meet and Reply by headings using SLD-003. 40–50: Correct incomplete answers. 50–60: Individually identify the three answers and their headings.

Postal: HO-006 v1.0, the complete two-sheet booklet; Read a new notice and its check.

## Check for understanding

Listen for the answer and its source each time. Return learners to the text when an item, landmark, date, condition or role is missing. The class checks are practice; the planned sequence and referral count are not attendance or completion results.
