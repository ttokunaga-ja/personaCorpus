# Find what the notice leaves open

## Slide 1 — Title
Layout: title
Title: Find what the notice leaves open
Subtitle: Everyday Document Basics | ELM-EDB-2025-SLD-003 v1.0 | 17 Mar 2025 | Heather Callahan
Speaker notes: Sessions 7 and 8 use the two practice notices in HO-006 v1.0.

## Slide 2 — Session 7: arrival
Layout: content
Title: Ask reception
Body: Elmwood practice notice — room preparation

Please arrive at the West door. Reception will welcome the group before the room preparation begins.
Speaker notes: Read the notice together. Ask learners to identify the stated arrival door.

## Slide 3 — Known and missing
Layout: two columns
Title: What can we say? What do we need to ask?
Left column — Arrival door in the notice: West door.
Right column — If arrival is late: Where should I go? The notice does not say.
Speaker notes: Separate the stated door from the missing late-arrival direction. Ask learners to select one missing piece rather than guess.

## Slide 4 — Ask one thing
Layout: content
Title: A question for reception
Body: “The notice says to arrive at the West door. Where should I go if I arrive late?”

Practise saying your own short question. Include what you know and ask for the missing direction.
Speaker notes: Invite pairs to practise. Listen for the known door and a single question about where to go when late. The question is practice and need not be sent.

## Slide 5 — Session 8: read a new notice
Layout: content
Title: Elmwood — making a notice board
Body: Join us on Saturday 7 June to make a notice board.

What to bring: pencil and ruler.

Where to meet: Room 2 outside the lift.

Reply by: 4 June, 12:00 noon.
Speaker notes: Give individuals time to read and write the three answers and their evidence before comparing with a partner.

## Slide 6 — Compare the headings
Layout: content
Title: Show your partner where you found it
Body: What should you bring? Where should you meet? When must you reply?

Point to the heading and words for each answer. Compare with your partner; add any missing detail.
Speaker notes: Check pencil and ruler under What to bring, Room 2 outside the lift under Where to meet, and 4 June, 12:00 noon under Reply by. Distinguish the 7 June activity date from the reply deadline.
