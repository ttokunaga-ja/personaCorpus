## Page 1 — Ask reception

Session 7. Read the practice notice. You will write one question to reception. You do not need to send it.

### Elmwood practice notice — room preparation

Please arrive at the West door. Reception will welcome the group before the room preparation begins.

1. Which door is given for arrival? ____________________
2. You expect to arrive late. Does the notice say where you should go then? ____________________
3. Write one question to reception. Include what you already know and ask where to go if you arrive late.

____________________________________________________________

Read the notice again. Use its arrival information, but do not guess the missing direction. Check your answers on page 3 under **Check: Ask reception**.

## Page 2 — Read a new notice

Session 8. Read this different practice notice on your own. Write an answer and the words in the notice that helped you for each question.

Go to page 4, **Check: Read a new notice**, to check your answers. If an answer is incomplete, return to its heading and add the missing words.

## Page 3 — Check: Ask reception

1. The notice says **West door**. These are the words under the practice notice about arrival.
2. No. It does not say where to go if you are late. West door is the stated arrival door, not an answer to the late-arrival question.
3. One possible question: “The notice says to arrive at the West door. Where should I go if I arrive late?” Your wording can differ. Check that it includes the known door and asks one question about the missing direction.

## Page 4 — Check: Read a new notice

1. **Pencil and ruler**. Look under **What to bring**. Include both items.
2. **Room 2 outside the lift**. Look under **Where to meet**. Include the location outside the lift, not just the room number.
3. **4 June, 12:00 noon**. Look under **Reply by**. This is the reply deadline; 7 June is the day of the activity.

Compare each answer with the words you wrote as evidence. Return to the notice if a date, time or location is missing.
