# Slide 1 — Cover

Layout: Cover

Title: Read the date next to the action

Subtitle: Everyday Document Basics — Sessions 2 and 3 | ELM-EDB-2025-SLD-002 v1.0 | 06 Mar 2025 | Heather Callahan

Presenter notes: Use the session 2 and session 3 sections independently. Each ends with its own check.

# Slide 2 — Session 2: two times, two actions

Layout: Two columns

Left: **Reply slip**

Please reply by 30 April, 16:00.

Name: __________

Item: __________

Right: **Saturday Repair Table**

Saturday 3 May, 10:00–11:30

What to bring: one small item and this invitation

Where to meet: East entrance, beside the bicycle rack

Presenter notes: Ask learners to read both sides before offering an answer. The two times do different jobs.

# Slide 3 — Session 2: what happens when?

Layout: Heading and body

- What must happen by 30 April at 16:00?
- What begins on 3 May at 10:00?
- Point to the words next to each time that helped you decide.

Presenter notes: Let people answer first. Return to “Please reply by” and the event heading rather than relying on the numbers alone. The reply slip is for practice, not submission.

# Slide 4 — Session 2: check on your own

Layout: Heading and body

Write an action beside each time:

30 April, 16:00 — ______________________

3 May, 10:00 — ________________________

Which words on the page tell you?

Presenter notes: Check independently: reply by 30 April at 16:00; the Repair Table begins 3 May at 10:00. Ask for the reply instruction and event heading as evidence.

# Slide 5 — Session 3: a dated change

Layout: Two columns

Left: **Original invitation**

Saturday Repair Table — 3 May, 10:00–11:30

What to bring: one small item and this invitation

Where to meet: East entrance, beside the bicycle rack

Right: **Rain notice — 1 May**

If it rains, the meeting place for Saturday Repair Table will change.

Where to meet: Main entrance, inside the glass doors

The time and what to bring stay as shown on the invitation.

Presenter notes: Ask what the later notice changes in rain. Have learners point to its date and the meeting-place line.

# Slide 6 — Session 3: return to the invitation

Layout: Heading and body

In rain, where do you meet?

Go back to the original invitation:

- When does the Repair Table start?
- What two things should you bring?
- Which heading or line gives each answer?

Presenter notes: Compare the new meeting place with the original. The start is still 10:00 on 3 May; the items are one small item and this invitation. Do not treat the rain notice as changing all details.

# Slide 7 — Session 3: check on your own

Layout: Heading and body

For a rainy Saturday, write:

1. The meeting place and where you found it: ______________________
2. The starting time and where you found it: ______________________
3. The two things to bring and where you found them: ______________

Presenter notes: Check the full new location, “Main entrance, inside the glass doors”, against the 1 May notice. Check start and both items against the original invitation. Ask learners to name the source for each answer.
