# Read the list beside the reminder
Everyday Document Basics | ELM-EDB-2025-HO-005 v1.0 | 12 Mar 2025 | Heather Callahan

## Page 1 — What to bring

Session 5. Read this practice notice. Then answer the questions below. You can write on this page.

### Elmwood room preparation — practice notice

We are getting a room ready together. Please read the list for your role before you come.

**What to bring**

- Everyone: bring gloves.
- Marking team: also bring a pencil to mark the list.
- Moving team: no other item is listed.

**Where to meet**

Both teams meet at the East entrance. Please check any later message for a change.

1. You are on the moving team. What do you bring? ____________________
2. You are on the marking team. What two things do you bring? ____________________
3. Which words in the list apply to both teams? ____________________

Look again at **What to bring**. “Everyone” includes both teams. Keep this booklet for the next session too.

## Page 2 — Who uses the west door

Session 6. Read the notice on page 1 and this later message together.

### Additional message — room preparation

The marking team will now meet at the West door. This changes the meeting place for the marking team only. The moving team still meets at the East entrance. The list of things to bring has not changed.

1. Your role is the marking team. Where do you meet now? ____________________
2. Your role is the moving team. Where do you meet? ____________________
3. You are on the marking team. What do you bring? ____________________
4. Underline the words in the message that tell you who has a different meeting place.

First find your role in the message. Then return to **What to bring** on page 1 for the items. A change to one team's meeting place does not change the list.

## Page 3 — Check: What to bring

Compare your answers with the list on page 1. Add any missing words.

1. Moving team: gloves. The first line says **Everyone**; the moving team line lists no other item.
2. Marking team: gloves and a pencil. The first line applies to everyone and the marking team line adds a pencil.
3. **Everyone** applies to both teams. If you wrote only “pencil” for the marking team, go back and add gloves.

## Page 4 — Check: Who uses the west door

Compare your role with the later message on page 2, then check the list on page 1.

1. Marking team: **West door**. The message names that team and says its meeting place has changed.
2. Moving team: **East entrance**. The later message says it still meets there.
3. Marking team: **gloves and a pencil**. The message says the list has not changed; check both the Everyone and Marking team lines on page 1.
4. “The marking team will now meet at the West door” and “for the marking team only” show who uses it. If you gave West door for both teams, return to the sentence about the moving team and correct your answer.
