## Page 2 — Check your answers

Q1. **one small item and this invitation**. Look at **What to bring**. Compare both things with your answer. If one is missing, return to Q1 and add it.

Q2. **East entrance, beside the bicycle rack**. Look at **Where to meet**. Compare the entrance and the landmark with your answer. If either is missing, return to Q2 and add it.
