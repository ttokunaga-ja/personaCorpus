# What to say at an Ashford appointment

Everyday Document Basics | ELM-EDB-2025-DD-002 v1.0 | 11 Feb 2025 | Heather Callahan

For Bernadette McLean and Ashford advisers

## A short introduction

“You will have eight sessions to practise finding the information you need in everyday notices. In the room, you and the tutor will read an invitation together and look for things such as what to bring and where to meet. You can show the tutor the words that helped you find an answer.

“If you miss a session, we will post the paper for that session. You can begin with the notice on the page, work through the questions and use the check section to compare your answers with the notice. You do not need a device to read it.

“Some pages include a reply slip to practise filling in. That slip is part of the exercise; you do not need to send it in. We can separately discuss and confirm your application to attend this course here at your Ashford appointment.”

## Adviser note

Pause after the introduction for questions. The practice slip is not the course application. Confirm the course application separately with the person at the referral appointment.
