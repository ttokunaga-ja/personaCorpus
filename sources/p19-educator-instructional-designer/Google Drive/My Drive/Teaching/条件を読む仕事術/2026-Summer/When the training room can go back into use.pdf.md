# When the training room can go back into use

Reading Conditions at Work · ELM-CRW-2026-HO-009 · v1.0  
20 Jul 2026 · Heather Callahan

For the 27 July session

Read the notice, the temporary instruction and the records together. Prepare the advice someone at the desk can give to a room user on each day. Point to the words and the record that support it.

## A. Notice to room users

> Room B normally reopens at 16:00 after cleaning. Please return at 16:00 to use the room.

Source: Brookdale Service Cooperative, service desk notice to room users. Issued by the service desk; addressed to people using Room B.

## B. Temporary instruction to facilities staff

> For 13–15 July 2026 only: Room B may reopen at 15:30 if cleaning completion is recorded by 15:15. Check the cleaning record before advising the service desk. Outside these dates, use the normal reopening time.

Source: Brookdale Service Cooperative, facilities instruction. Issued by facilities management; addressed to facilities staff.

## C. Cleaning completion record

| Room | Date | Cleaning completed and recorded |
|---|---|---|
| B | 15 July 2026 | 15:10 |
| B | 16 July 2026 | 15:10 |
