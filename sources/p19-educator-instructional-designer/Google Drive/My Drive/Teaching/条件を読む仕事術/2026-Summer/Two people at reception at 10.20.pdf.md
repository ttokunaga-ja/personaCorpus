# Two people at reception at 10:20
Reading Conditions at Work · ELM-CRW-2026-HO-011 · v1.0 · 10 Aug 2026 · Heather Callahan

## Read both instructions

**Visitor notice**

If you arrive 15 minutes or more after your appointment time, please check in again at front reception.

Source: Brookdale Service Cooperative, notice for visitors.

**Instruction to receiving staff**

From 10:00 to 11:00, use the east door for scheduled deliveries only.

Source: Brookdale Service Cooperative, instruction for receiving staff.

## Reception record

| Arrival | Appointment or delivery | Time recorded |
|---|---|---|
| Visitor | Appointment at 10:00 | Arrived 10:20 |
| Delivery | Scheduled delivery | Arrived 10:20 |

## Decide separately

Before choosing a destination, underline the people each instruction addresses. Then use the record to answer for each arrival.

1. Where should the visitor check in? Which document applies, and what in the record makes its condition relevant?

Destination: ____________________  Document and evidence: ______________________________

2. Which entrance should receiving staff use for the delivery? Which words in the instruction and record support your choice?

Entrance: _______________________  Document and evidence: ______________________________

Compare your answers with a partner. Both arrivals have the same time. Explain why that fact alone does not make the two instructions interchangeable.
