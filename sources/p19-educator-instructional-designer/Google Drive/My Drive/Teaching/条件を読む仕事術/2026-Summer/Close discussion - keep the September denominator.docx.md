# Close discussion — keep the September denominator
ELM-CRW-2026-DD-007 · v1.0 · 28 Sep 2026 · Heather Callahan

## Context

David Ashcroft and Heather Callahan met on 28 September, 13:30–13:55, to check the Reading Conditions at Work summer report and decide how to describe the follow-up.

## Change and rationale

David confirmed the report's denominator: 36 learners enrolled at the 11 September 16:00 close. The report records 34 valid submissions and 27 at 14 or more. The seven submitted memos below 14 are distinct from the two learners with no valid submission or score.

We agreed that a memo submitted in October as follow-up will be recorded in the next-term support record. It will not retrospectively change the September report's fixed submission count or marks. The September report remains a description of the close; the later opportunity is a separate record of support.

The case also exposed a distinction worth making more explicit: the visitor can be given the ordinary 13:00 time while the responsible person is asked to confirm the blank approval entry. The time given now and the answer to the later inquiry must not be presented as the same decision.
