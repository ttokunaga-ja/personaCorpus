## Slide 1 — Keep the visitor sentence on the board

Layout: Cover

Reading Conditions at Work · ELM-CRW-2026-SLD-005 · v1.0  
13 July 2026 · Heather Callahan

Speaker notes: Ask each person to write a provisional sentence before opening the staff instruction and record. Keep a sentence and its source visible on the board for the whole comparison.

## Slide 2 — Start with the notice

Layout: Two columns

Left: **Visitor notice**  
“The counter normally reopens at 13:00. Please return at that time for service.”

Right: **Your visitor sentence**  
What would you say now? Write the sentence and the source. Leave it visible as you read the other documents.

Speaker notes: Invite a sentence and the source, not a guess about a different document. Write it on the left of the board.

## Slide 3 — What must be recorded?

Layout: Two columns

Left: **Visitor sentence**  
Keep your first sentence here: ______________________________

Right: **Staff instruction, section 2**  
12:30 is possible only if both the passage check and the responsible person's approval are recorded by 12:15.

| Check separately | Recorded by 12:15? | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Passage check | __________ | __________ |
| Responsible person's approval | __________ | __________ |

Speaker notes: Ask pairs to find each requirement and the deadline. Do not fill either record field yet. What evidence would justify changing the sentence?

## Slide 4 — Read the record at 12:20

Layout: Two columns

Left: **Visitor sentence**  
Your first sentence: ______________________________________  
Change it or keep it? Explain to your partner.

Right: **Counter record, viewed at 12:20**

| Check | Entry |
|---|---|
| Passage checked | 12:10 |
| Responsible person's approval | |

What does each entry establish? What will you ask, and whom?

Speaker notes: Point first to the passage entry, then to approval. Ask “What can a blank tell us?” A blank does not establish refusal. Section 3 says to seek confirmation before announcing early reopening; until the conditions are established use the normal time. Ask partners to connect their advice to the instruction and record.

## Slide 5 — Trace the advice

Layout: Heading and body

Keep or revise your sentence: ______________________________

Point to the relevant section and the two record entries. Tell your partner what remains to be checked and who can confirm it.

Speaker notes: Invite a pair to trace the sentence without supplying their answer for them. Ask whether the proposed visitor advice is supported by the record available at 12:20.
