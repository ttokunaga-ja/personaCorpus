## Slide 2
Layout: Heading and body
Title: What can you tell the visitor now?

The notice says: **Normal reopening 13:00.**

Write a provisional sentence to a visitor using only the notice. What does the notice tell you? What would you need to check before changing that advice?

Notes: Give everyone time to write before asking for a sentence and its source. Keep this first decision provisional; do not display the staff instruction yet.

## Slide 3
Layout: Two columns
Title: Put the instruction beside the record

| Staff instruction | Check record, 12:20 |
|---|---|
| Section 2: both the passage check and the responsible lead's approval must be recorded by 12:15 for reopening at 12:30. | Passage check: 12:10. |
| Section 3: check empty fields before giving early-opening advice. | Approval: blank. |

Which requirement does each entry address? What is still a question? Return to your sentence and decide whether the documents support changing it.

Notes: Ask pairs to point to one clause and one entry at a time. An empty field is a reason to check, not evidence of a recorded approval.

## Slide 4
Layout: Diagram
Title: Make the link visible

Three connected empty boxes, left to right:
- Applicable section: __________
- Record entry and time: __________
- What I can say to the visitor: __________

Below: What must I ask, and whom? __________

Notes: Learners fill the boxes with their own reasoning. Ask them to link the advice to both the instruction and the record, rather than copying a time alone.

Layout: Heading and body
Title: Your decision memo

Use *Counter reopening – documents for the exercise* (ELM-CRW-2026-HO-004) and *Write the counter decision* (ELM-CRW-2026-HO-005).

Submit one page by **11 September, 16:00**: visitor sentence, applicable section, confirmed facts and the question to take to the appropriate person. Cite the relevant entries.
