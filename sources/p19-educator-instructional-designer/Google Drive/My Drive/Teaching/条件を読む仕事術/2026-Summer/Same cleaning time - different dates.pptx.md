# Slide 1 — Cover

Title: Same cleaning time — different dates

Subtitle: Reading Conditions at Work · ELM-CRW-2026-SLD-006 · v1.0  
21 Jul 2026 · Heather Callahan

Speaker notes: Use with the Room B extracts on 27 July. Ask learners to keep both the notice and the instruction visible.

# Slide 2 — Two columns

Title: What does the record say?

Left: 15 July · Room B · cleaning completed and recorded at 15:10

Right: 16 July · Room B · cleaning completed and recorded at 15:10

Question: Is the same time enough to choose the same reopening time? Find another fact before deciding.

Speaker notes: Give pairs time to point to the record. Do not supply the decision yet.

# Slide 3 — Diagram

Title: When can the temporary instruction be used?

A date band has three labelled dates: 13 July → 14 July → 15 July, bracketed “temporary instruction in effect”. A separate 16 July marker follows the end of the bracket. Underneath: “15:30 only if cleaning completion is recorded by 15:15.”

Speaker notes: Ask learners to locate the end date in the staff instruction, not just on the projected band.

# Slide 4 — Two columns

Title: Write the two notices again

Left box: 15 July — Room B can reopen at ______. Source and record: ______.

Right box: 16 July — Room B can reopen at ______. Source and record: ______.

Prompt: Explain what stayed the same and what changed.

Speaker notes: If a pair selects 15:30 for 16 July, first ask “Which date in the instruction lets you use that time?” Let them find its end date. Then confirm 16:00 for 16 July using the normal notice; 15 July is 15:30 because it is within the period and 15:10 meets the recording condition.
