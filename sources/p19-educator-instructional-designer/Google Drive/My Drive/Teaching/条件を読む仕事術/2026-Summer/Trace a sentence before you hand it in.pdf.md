# Trace a sentence before you hand it in
Reading Conditions at Work · ELM-CRW-2026-HO-012 · v1.0 · 31 Aug 2026 · Heather Callahan

## Bring one sentence

Choose a sentence from your own one-page decision memo. In the optional 9 September consultation, a partner will try to find the information behind it. You can revise your memo after the conversation.

## Follow the trail

| Step | Your notes |
|---|---|
| 1. Advice: what would you say to the visitor now? | __________________________________________ |
| 2. Source and section: which document and provision support that advice? | __________________________________________ |
| 3. Record: what can you confirm, and from which entry and time? | __________________________________________ |
| 4. Still to check: what is not confirmed, and whom will you ask? | __________________________________________ |

Let your partner use your source and section reference to find the passage, then find the record entry. If either is hard to locate, add enough detail for another person to follow the trail. An empty field is not itself a refusal or an approval.

## Quick practice: Room B

“The room can reopen at 15:30 because cleaning was recorded at 15:10.”

What date would you need to add or check before relying on the temporary instruction? Write the missing qualification: __________________________________________

Now return to your own sentence. Check that your source, the applicable conditions and the record all support what you plan to say.
