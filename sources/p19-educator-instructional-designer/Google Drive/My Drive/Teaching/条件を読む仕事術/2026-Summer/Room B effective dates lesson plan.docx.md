# Read the date at the point of use

Reading Conditions at Work · ELM-CRW-2026-LP-004 · v1.0  
23 Jul 2026 · Heather Callahan · For Erin MacDonald  
Session: 27 Jul 2026 · 70 minutes

## Learning aims

Learners will use the date on a temporary instruction and the time in a record together, then write a separate practical notice for each date. They will distinguish the notice for room users from the instruction for facilities staff.

Materials: When the training room can go back into use (ELM-CRW-2026-HO-009); Same cleaning time — different dates (ELM-CRW-2026-SLD-006); paper copies of the extracts and exit slips.

## Teaching sequence

| Minutes | Activity | Teacher action |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 | Access check | Confirm each person can open the local packet or has the same paper extracts. Resolve access separately from the reading activity. |
| 5–15 | Independent reading | Ask learners to mark the issuer and reader of each extract and the dates and times in the record. |
| 15–30 | Individual notices | Each learner writes one room-user sentence for 15 July and another for 16 July, naming a source for each. |
| 30–45 | Pair check | Partners locate the beginning and end of the temporary instruction and check the 15:10 record against the 15:15 condition for each date. |
| 45–60 | Exchange explanations | Pairs exchange their notices and point to the words and record used. Ask what changes although the cleaning time stays the same. |
| 60–70 | Individual rewrite and collection | Learners revise both sentences and identify their evidence on an anonymous slip. Collect the slips. |

## Check for understanding

Look for 15:30 on 15 July, supported by the instruction effective through 15 July and the 15:10 record, and 16:00 on 16 July, supported by the normal notice because the temporary period has ended. Ask a learner who writes 15:30 twice to locate the final effective date before offering the answer. A time without a reason is not enough to show which condition was used. Keep access observations apart from the anonymous reading slips.
