# Two checks at the start and end

Reading Conditions at Work · ELM-CRW-2026-HO-008 · v1.0  
8 July 2026 · Heather Callahan

For Heather and Erin · 13 July, Room 2

## At the entrance — short spoken check

“Do you have the visitor notice, the staff instruction and the counter record available to read, either in the saved packet on your device or in the paper packet?”

If the packet will not open, offer a paper copy before the exercise. Arjun records the number using each route separately. This check is about access to the three documents; it is not an answer to the case.
