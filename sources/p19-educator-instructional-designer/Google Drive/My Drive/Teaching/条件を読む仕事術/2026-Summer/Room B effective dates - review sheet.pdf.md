# Date on the notice and in the instruction

Reading Conditions at Work · ELM-CRW-2026-HO-010 · v1.0  
29 Jul 2026 · Heather Callahan

After our Room B discussion, compare the date condition with the cleaning record. The two records show the same time; the instruction does not cover both dates.

| Date | What the record says | What the instruction says about dates | Notice to the room user and why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 15 Jul | Cleaning completion recorded at 15:10, before 15:15. | Temporary instruction applies 13–15 July inclusive. | Room B may reopen at 15:30: both the date and the recorded completion meet the temporary instruction. |
| 16 Jul | Cleaning completion recorded at 15:10, before 15:15. | The temporary instruction ended on 15 July. | Room B normally reopens at 16:00: use the room-user notice because the temporary instruction no longer applies. |

The 15:10 entry answers when completion was recorded. It does not extend the instruction beyond its final date. The normal notice and the facilities instruction are addressed to different readers; together they tell us what to check before giving a time.

## Try one change

Suppose the temporary instruction ended on 16 July instead, with its other words unchanged. What would change for the 16 July notice, given the same 15:10 record? Write one sentence that includes your reason.
