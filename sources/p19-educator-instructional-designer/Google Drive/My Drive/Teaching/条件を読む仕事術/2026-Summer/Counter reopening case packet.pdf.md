# Counter reopening — documents for the exercise

Reading Conditions at Work · ELM-CRW-2026-HO-004 · v1.0  
17 April 2026 · Heather Callahan

Read the visitor notice, the staff instruction and the record together. The documents have different readers and purposes.

## Document 1: Visitor notice

Brookdale Service Cooperative — Service counter

The counter normally reopens at 13:00. Please return at that time for service.

## Document 2: Staff instruction — reopening the counter

### 1. Normal reopening

The normal reopening time is 13:00. The visitor notice states the time to give visitors unless the early reopening conditions below have been met.

### 2. Early reopening

The counter may reopen at 12:30 only if both the responsible person's approval and the passage check have been recorded by 12:15. A record of one does not replace the other.

### 3. Before announcing an early reopening

Check both entries in the counter record. If a required entry is blank, seek confirmation before announcing an early reopening. Until the required conditions are established, use the normal reopening time on the notice.

## Document 3: Counter confirmation record

Record viewed at 12:20.

| Required check | Recorded time |
|---|---|
| Passage checked | 12:10 |
| Responsible person's approval | |

The blank approval field shows no recorded approval at 12:20. It does not establish whether approval was given elsewhere.
