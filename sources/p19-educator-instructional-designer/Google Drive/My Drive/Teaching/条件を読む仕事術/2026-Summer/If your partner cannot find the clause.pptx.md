# Slide 2 — Put your sentence here
Layout: Heading and body

Your advice to the visitor:

____________________________________________________________

Give the sentence to your partner. Can the next person use it and find the source behind it?

Presenter notes: Keep the writer's actual wording in view. Do not give the class a model answer to the counter case.

# Slide 3 — Listener: follow the trail
Layout: Diagram

Diagram: “Advice sentence” → “Source name” → “Section and conditions” → “Record entry and time” → “What remains unconfirmed / who to ask”.

Point to each place. Does the record establish the conditions the sentence relies on? Say where the trail stops.

Presenter notes: The listener follows the references before asking the writer for the conclusion. An empty entry remains unconfirmed.

# Slide 4 — Repair the trail, then switch
Layout: Heading and body

If your partner cannot point to the passage:
1. Name the document and add the section or record entry.
2. Check that the words there actually support your advice; revise the advice if necessary.
3. State what remains to be checked and who can confirm it.

Switch: listener becomes writer; writer becomes listener. Try the trail again with the other memo.

Submit one decision memo by 11 September, 16:00.

Presenter notes: Give both partners a turn. Ask them to locate the evidence, not to wait for the instructor to announce a time.
