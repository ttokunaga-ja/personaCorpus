# Write the counter decision
Reading Conditions at Work · ELM-CRW-2026-HO-005 · v1.0 · 21 May 2026 · Heather Callahan

## Your task
Use *Counter reopening – documents for the exercise* (ELM-CRW-2026-HO-004): the visitor notice, staff instruction and check record as at 12:20. You are advising a visitor at the counter. Prepare one page that someone taking over the counter can follow.

## Your one-page decision memo
1. Write one sentence you would actually say to the visitor now. State the time you can give and avoid promising what the records do not support.
2. Identify the applicable section of the staff instruction. State the conditions it requires and where you found them.
3. List the facts you can confirm from the check record, with the relevant entry and time. Distinguish an empty field from a negative confirmation.
4. Say what still needs checking, whom you would ask and what you would ask them to confirm. Explain how the answer could affect your advice.

Keep your decision, clause and record linked: a reader should be able to trace each part of your advice to the material. You may use short sentences or a compact table, but the visitor-facing sentence must stand on its own.

## Submit
Submit your one-page judgment memo by 11 September 2026 at 16:00 Toronto time. Include the name of each source and its section or record entry; do not submit the case packet as a substitute for your own decision.
