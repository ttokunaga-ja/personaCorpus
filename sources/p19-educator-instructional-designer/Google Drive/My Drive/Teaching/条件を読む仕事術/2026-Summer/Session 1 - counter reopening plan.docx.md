# First Monday — from the door to the counter exercise

ELM-CRW-2026-LP-003 · v1.0 · 9 July 2026  
Heather Callahan · For Erin MacDonald

## Learning aims

Reading Conditions at Work, Monday 13 July, Room 2, 13:00–14:10. Heather and Erin guide participants from a visitor notice to a decision grounded in the staff instruction and counter record. Participants identify the normal rule, distinguish the two early-reopening conditions, and explain what a blank entry leaves to be checked.

## Teaching sequence

| Time | Activity | Instructor action |
|---|---|---|
| 13:00–13:05 | Check viewing access | Ask whether the same notice, instruction and record are available on a device or paper. Arjun counts the routes; offer a paper packet if needed. |
| 13:05–13:15 | Notice only | Ask each person for a visitor-facing sentence and its source. Keep a sentence visible on the board. |
| 13:15–13:35 | Pair comparison | Open the staff instruction and the record. Pairs identify the two required entries separately, the 12:15 deadline and the 12:20 record. Ask what can be inferred from the blank. |
| 13:35–13:50 | Individual draft | Each person writes advice, the relevant section and recorded facts, plus the question and person to consult. |
| 13:50–14:05 | Partners trace the evidence | Exchange drafts. Find the named section and each entry; return unsupported claims for revision. |
| 14:05–14:10 | Anonymous exit slip | Use the end section of *Two checks at the start and end*. Heather collects slips separately from the entrance count. |

Materials: *Counter reopening — documents for the exercise* (ELM-CRW-2026-HO-004), *Write the counter decision* (HO-005), *Two checks at the start and end* (HO-008), and *Keep the visitor sentence on the board* (SLD-005). Paper packets are available at the entrance.

## Check for understanding

Arjun handles the aggregate count of device and paper routes; it establishes access, not reading performance. Heather looks at whether each anonymous slip connects the visitor's time to a section and the record, and identifies the outstanding confirmation. The record shows passage checked at 12:10 and an empty approval field at 12:20; it does not show a refusal. Do not link the entrance record to individual answers. The one-page decision memo is a separate submission, due 11 September at 16:00.
