## Slide 2

Layout: Heading and body  
Title: Where does the notice give the usual return?

**Preparation room notice**

Equipment borrowed for training is normally due back by 16:30 on the day of the loan.

**Write the return time and copy the words that tell you when it applies.** Put a mark on the line you used.

Notes: At 15:00 elapsed, start the three-minute check with quiet individual writing. When everyone has written, ask neighbours to point to the words they marked and compare them. Then take a response from the room. If you hear only “4:30,” ask the reader to show where the notice gives the time and what tells them it is on the day of the loan. Resume explanation at minute 18.

## Slide 3

Layout: Two columns  
Title: Which fields did you check?

| Reception memo | This loan’s register entry |
|---|---|
| Application received by 15:45. | Application received: 15:40 |
| Duty lead approval recorded by 16:15. | Duty lead approval: |
| Both conditions are required for return by 09:00 the next business day. | |

**On your own, match each condition to its register field. Write what you can establish from each and what you would say at reception.**

Notes: At 30:00 elapsed, start the second three-minute check. Leave the approval value cell empty. Give everyone quiet time to mark and write about both fields before asking for a response. Then ask them to compare their two marks with a neighbour. Take the receipt evidence and the approval evidence separately from the room. Wait for readers to identify the fields before advancing at minute 33.

## Slide 4

Layout: Table  
Title: What can reception say?

| What we checked | What the record supports |
|---|---|
| Receipt by 15:45 | The 15:40 entry meets the application deadline. |
| Duty lead approval recorded by 16:15 | The field is empty. Check with the duty lead and hold off on promising the later return while checking. |
| Both conditions met and recorded in time | Reception can advise return by 09:00 on the next business day. |
| Approval still unrecorded at 16:15 | Advise the usual 16:30 return that day. |

Notes: From minutes 33 to 41, retrace the decision from the two entries to their respective conditions. Ask where a recorded approval would appear and who could confirm it. For the last nine minutes, have someone give the borrower one sentence while the check is underway and another for after 16:15 if approval remains unrecorded. Listen for the reason alongside the advice.
