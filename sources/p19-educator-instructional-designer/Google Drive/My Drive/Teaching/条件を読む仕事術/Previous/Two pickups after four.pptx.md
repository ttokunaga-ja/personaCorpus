Left: Usual Friday collection: by 16:00.
Right: Case A arrives at 16:20. Case B arrives at 16:20.

# Slide 3 — Heading and body
Title: A later window
Both collections were booked. A booked collection may be released up to 17:30 when the conditions in the reception instruction are met.
Question: What does the booking tell you? What might you still need to check?
Presenter notes: Separate the latest collection time from evidence that this collection qualifies.

# Slide 4 — Heading and body
Title: One more requirement
The supervisor must have confirmed the booked collection before 16:00.
Question: Which record would you look for before releasing either item?
Presenter notes: Ask learners to name both requirements, not simply repeat the later time.
