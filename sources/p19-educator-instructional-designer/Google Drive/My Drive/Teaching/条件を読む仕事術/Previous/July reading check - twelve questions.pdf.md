# July reading check: twelve questions
Reading Conditions at Work | ELM-CRW-2024-HO-004 | 09 Jul 2024 | Heather Callahan

Time: 25 minutes. Write your anonymous key: __________. Choose one answer for each question. You may return to the documents as often as you need.

## Document A — Friday collection: reception instructions
Brookdale Service Cooperative, BSC-OPS-2024-014, 02 May 2024, Janice Mitchell. Excerpts.

1. On Fridays, release items for collection by 16:00. Direct visitors arriving after the usual collection time to reception so staff can check whether the exception below applies.

2. A reserved collection may be released up to 17:30 on Friday only when it was booked and a supervisor confirmed the collection before 16:00. Check both the booking and the supervisor’s confirmation time in the record before releasing the item. The 17:30 limit is the latest collection time; it is not the deadline for the supervisor’s confirmation.

3. If the conditions in section 2 are not met, arrange collection for the next business day. If the confirmation record is not at hand, check the supervisor confirmation log before deciding; do not release the item while confirmation is being checked.

## Document B — Counter notice for visitors
Brookdale Service Cooperative: Friday collections. The usual collection time on Fridays is up to 16:00. If you arrive after that time, please speak to reception.

## Document C — Dispatch handover instruction
Pass on dispatch sheets completed by 14:00 for the usual handover. If the temperature-check field is blank, put the sheet on hold. A hold may be cleared up to 14:30 only if the person on duty has remeasured the temperature and recorded the remeasurement. A spoken report alone is not a recorded remeasurement.
