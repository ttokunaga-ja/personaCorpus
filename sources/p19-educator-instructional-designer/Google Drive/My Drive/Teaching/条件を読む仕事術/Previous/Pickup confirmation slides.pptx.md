# Slide 1 — Cover
When the confirmation is missing
Reading Conditions at Work | ELM-CRW-2024-SLD-005 | v0.1 | 30 Jul 2024 | Heather Callahan

Speaker notes: Use with R3. Ask for a decision and the missing fact before showing the log.

# Slide 2 — Heading and body
## At reception, Friday
Booked item. Visitor arrives at 16:20. Confirmation record is not at the desk.
What should reception do now? What would you look for?

Speaker notes: The booking alone does not establish the exception. Ask learners to say in their own words why the item stays at the desk while the supervisor confirmation log is checked. Missing at the desk is not proof of late confirmation.

# Slide 3 — Diagram
## The log shows 15:40
Booked → supervisor confirmed at 15:40, before 16:00 → Friday arrival at 16:20, within the collection limit of up to and including 17:30 → release permitted under section 2.

Speaker notes: Ask learners to name both requirements and distinguish the confirmation cutoff from the latest permitted collection time. A collection at exactly 17:30 is within the limit.

# Slide 4 — Diagram
## Instead, the log shows 16:05
Booked → supervisor confirmed at 16:05, after the before-16:00 cutoff → section 2 condition not met → arrange next-business-day collection under section 3.

Speaker notes: Ask learners to distinguish in their own words the temporary hold while checking from the carry-over decision once a late confirmation is established. Why does arrival before 17:30 not fix the late confirmation?

# Slide 5 — Two columns
## Compare the two moments
**Record unknown:** check supervisor confirmation log; do not release while checking.
**Late confirmation established:** arrange collection for the next business day; record the outcome and tell the visitor.

Speaker notes: Invite a learner who has not yet spoken to explain when the decision changes and which fact supports it.
