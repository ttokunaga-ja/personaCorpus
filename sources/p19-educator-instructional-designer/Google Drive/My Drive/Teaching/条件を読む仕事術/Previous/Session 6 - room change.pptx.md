Reading Conditions at Work · Session 6  
ELM-CRW-2024-SLD-007 v1.0 | 15 Oct 2024 | Heather Callahan

Notes: Open one document at a time. Ask learners to keep their first direction on the page so they can say later what evidence would make them change it.

# Slide 2 — The notice in Room 2

Layout: Heading and body

**Room notice**

> Afternoon group: 14:00 in Room 2.

Write the direction you would give someone in the afternoon group.

Notes: Point to 14:00 beside “Afternoon group”. Ask which group the time applies to. Put “14:00 — Room 2” on the board and keep it there through the final question.

# Slide 3 — The instruction staff see

Layout: Heading and body

**Staff instruction**

> Move the afternoon group to Room 4 only when approval of the equipment check has been recorded on the reception record.

Underline the condition for moving. Where should staff look to see whether it's met?

Notes: Ask someone to read from “only when” through the end of the sentence. Circle approval on the board, then draw an arrow to reception record. Ask whether hearing that the equipment was checked would be enough to change the direction.

# Slide 4 — The reception record at 12:20

Layout: Heading and body

**Reception record — 12:20**

Equipment check approved: __________  
Recorded by: __________

What can you tell from this entry? What is still open?

Notes: Point to the blank on the first line under the 12:20 heading, immediately after “Equipment check approved”. Show the separate “Recorded by” line below. Ask anyone who reads the blank as a failed check to find where the record gives a result. This entry shows no recorded approval at 12:20.

# Slide 5 — What will you say at 12:30?

Layout: Two columns

| Ask reception | Direct the group |
|---|---|
| What evidence would you ask the desk to look for? | Someone in the 14:00 group asks where to go. What will you tell them while the desk checks? |

Point to the words that support your direction.

Notes: Give a minute to write. Take the two answers in order. Listen for a request to check the current reception record for approval of the equipment check. While that approval is unconfirmed, use the posted direction of Room 2 at 14:00. Ask what would have to appear in the reception record before the direction changes to Room 4.
