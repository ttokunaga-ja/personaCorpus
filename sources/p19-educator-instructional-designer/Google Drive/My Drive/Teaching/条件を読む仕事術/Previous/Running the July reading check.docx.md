# Running the July reading check
Reading Conditions at Work | ELM-CRW-2024-LP-003 | v1.0 | 10 Jul 2024 | Heather Callahan
Prepared for Erin MacDonald and Heather Callahan

## Learning aims
Observe how learners identify usual rules, conditions and exceptions, compare scope and issuer, and choose judgments supported by the documents. This check is a look at the questions as well as the answers.

## Teaching sequence
16 July, 10:00. Give the 24 learners the twelve-question booklet ELM-CRW-2024-HO-004 and their distributed anonymous keys. Ask them to put the key, not a name, on the answer sheet. Explain that they have 25 minutes, can return to the texts, and should mark changes in the margin.

During the 25 minutes, answer procedural questions only. Do not paraphrase a condition or point to a passage. Collect all selection sheets before any discussion or hearing an explanation. Keep keys with response records; do not record names or put a name-to-key list with the results.

Immediately after collection, identify Q07 correct responses and invite eight of those learners who agree to speak briefly. Ask each: “What made that the right choice? What did you check in the notice?” Do not supply the conditions. Record against the anonymous key whether the explanation mentions the booking and separately whether it mentions the supervisor's confirmation before 16:00. Capture the explanation briefly, including uncertainty, without prompting for a missing element.

## Check for understanding
Keep the 24 selection results as the denominator for each item. The eight conversations are a subset of those learners, not additional answers. Compare the two observation fields with Q07's selection result; do not infer that a correct selection establishes both checks. Heather and Erin review the evidence together after the conversations.
