# Four reasons to put in writing
Reading Conditions at Work | ELM-CRW-2024-HO-005 | v0.2 | 23 Jul 2024 | Heather Callahan

Read both cards. For each situation, write what reception should do and explain using the relevant section and facts. A short, complete explanation is enough.

## Card A — Friday collections: counter notice
Brookdale Service Cooperative, for visitors. The usual Friday collection time is up to 16:00. Visitors arriving later should speak to reception.

## Card B — Friday collection: reception instructions
Adapted from Brookdale Service Cooperative, BSC-OPS-2024-014, 02 May 2024, Janice Mitchell; for reception staff.

**1. Usual collection.** Release Friday collections by 16:00; direct later visitors to reception for an exception check.

**2. Reserved collections after 16:00.** A Friday collection can be released up to and including 17:30 only if it was booked and a supervisor confirmed it before 16:00. Check the booking and confirmation time in the record. The 17:30 limit applies to collection, not confirmation.

**3. Carry-over and record.** If the section 2 conditions are not met, arrange collection for the next business day. If the confirmation record is not at hand, check the supervisor confirmation log and do not release while checking. Record the outcome and tell the visitor what happens next.
