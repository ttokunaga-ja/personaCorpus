## Page 6 — Document B: reception memo

**Elmwood preparation room — reception memo**  
From: Duty lead, preparation room  
To: Reception staff

1. Receive an application for an extension no later than 15:45 on the loan day and record the time received.
2. An extension requires the duty lead's approval to be recorded no later than 16:15 on the loan day.
3. Only when both clauses 1 and 2 are satisfied may reception advise a return by 09:00 on the next business day.
4. If the approval field is blank, check with the duty lead and do not promise the extension while checking. If no approval is recorded by 16:15, advise the ordinary 16:30 return.

Source: Duty lead's reception memo, clauses 1–4.

## Page 16 — Worked explanation: start with the source

“There's a request on time, so I'll tell you tomorrow morning.”

Read that proposed reply against the two source documents. The notice sends a borrower to reception to apply. Clause 2 of the notice says the application alone does not change the return time. Clause 3 of the memo gives reception the later return only when both of the memo's conditions are satisfied.

At 16:05 in Q5 we can establish on-time receipt. Reception should check for approval with the duty lead and hold the promise in the meantime. Notice how the two documents both help: one explains why the request did not itself change the return, and the other gives reception its next action.

## Page 17 — Worked explanation: check the entry against its own deadline

**Memo clause 1** sets 15:45 as the last time for receipt. Q5 gives 15:32 in the application field, so that condition is met.

**Memo clause 2** sets 16:15 as the latest time to record the duty lead's approval. In Q5 the approval field is empty at 16:05. The receipt time cannot supply the separate approval evidence.

The current decision is to ask the duty lead and leave the extension unpromised. The next-business-day time in clause 3 is what reception can advise once both conditions are supported by the record.

## Page 18 — Worked explanation: speak to the borrower

You can give a useful answer while you are still checking the extension:

> “We received your request at 15:32. I’ll check with the duty lead before I can tell you whether the return time has changed.”

This acknowledges the entry reception can see. It gives the borrower a next step without promising a later return while the approval field is empty. Clause 4 of the memo supports that advice. At 16:05 the deadline has yet to pass. If approval is still unrecorded at 16:15, clause 4 tells reception to advise the usual 16:30 return.
