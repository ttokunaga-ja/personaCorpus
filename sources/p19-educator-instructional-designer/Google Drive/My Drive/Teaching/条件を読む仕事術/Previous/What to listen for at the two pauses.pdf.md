# What to listen for at the two pauses
Reading Conditions at Work | ELM-CRW-2025-HO-001 | v1.0 | 19 Nov 2025 | Heather Callahan  
Instructor card for Tristan McLeod

## 15 minutes · Usual return

Let everyone write first and compare the words they marked with a neighbour. If you hear “4:30,” ask, “Can you point to the line on the borrower notice? When does that line apply?” Listen for 16:30 on the day of the loan and the words giving the ordinary return.

## 30 minutes · The extension record

“Put a mark beside the application time and another beside the approval field. What can you establish from each?” Give people time to read both entries before you ask what reception can say.

The 15:40 receipt is by 15:45. Approval by the duty lead must also be recorded by 16:15. If that field is empty, ask who the desk would check with and what it can say while waiting. With both conditions recorded in time, the return can be by 09:00 on the next business day. If approval is still unrecorded at the deadline, advise 16:30 that day.

## If we observe again

Note the words or register entries people actually point to and the reason they give. Leave names off the card.
