## Slide 4 · Heading and body

**Title**  
Q10 · Rewrite what you would say at the window — 5 minutes

1. Reread your Q10 sentence. Give the borrower a return time and the reason it applies.
2. Under the sentence, write the document name and clause that support the reason.
3. Read the sentence once as if you were the borrower. Can you tell when to bring the equipment back and why?
