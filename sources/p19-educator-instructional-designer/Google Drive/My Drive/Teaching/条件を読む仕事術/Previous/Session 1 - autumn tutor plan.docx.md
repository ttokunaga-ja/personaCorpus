# Visitor hours — Session 1 tutor plan

Reading Conditions at Work | ELM-CRW-2024-LP-004 v1.0 | 10 Sep 2024 | Heather Callahan

Teaching copy for Heather Callahan and Erin MacDonald

## Learning aims

Learners find the usual counter hours, identify the readers of a separate entry instruction and explain why two arrivals at the same time can receive different directions. Ask for the words that support each direction.

## Teaching sequence

**0–5 min · Opening question.** Ask where a visitor would look for the opening time at a workplace they haven't visited before. Take one answer and ask who would need that information.

**5–12 min · Read the counter notice first.**

> Visitor counter: Monday–Friday, 9:00–16:00. Individual visitors should report to the counter on arrival.

Give everyone a quiet read after reading it aloud. Ask “Who reports?” and “When is the counter open?” Erin, write the answers under Reader and Time and leave the first line on the board.

**12–20 min · Put up the group entry instruction.**

> Booked groups: enter through the north entrance between 08:30 and 09:00. The group leader checks in at the desk.

Have learners underline “Booked groups” before anyone calls out a door. Add the group and its entry window to the board alongside the first notice. Ask who actually checks in for the group.

**20–40 min · Individual exercise — Visitor hours.** Give each person the two notices and the Visitor hours response sheet, with this prompt at the top:

> An individual visitor and a booked group arrive at 08:45 on a weekday. For each arrival, underline the intended reader and circle the applicable time in the relevant notice. Write what you would tell each arrival. If a notice doesn't give you enough information to direct someone, write what you would still need to find out.

Use the last three minutes of the exercise for a slip. Each learner names one notice and writes one intended reader and one applicable time from that same notice.

**40–50 min · Discuss the two directions.** Read one answer for each arrival. Ask which words let the booked group go through the north entrance at 08:45. For the individual, ask when the counter opens and what the notice says about waiting or entering before opening.

## Check for understanding

Look for either individual visitors paired with weekday counter hours 09:00–16:00 or booked groups paired with north entrance access 08:30–09:00. At 08:45 the booked group may use the north entrance and its leader checks in. The counter opens at 09:00. The visitor notice gives no early-entry direction for the individual. If a slip gives the individual the group's entry window, ask the learner to read the first two words of the group instruction and correct the pairing.

## Materials for an absence

Leave Kathleen the day's visitor counter notice and booked group entry instruction with the Visitor hours individual exercise sheet. Keep the full projection in the tutor folder. Before putting the set aside, check that the learner can see both time ranges on the papers they'll receive.
