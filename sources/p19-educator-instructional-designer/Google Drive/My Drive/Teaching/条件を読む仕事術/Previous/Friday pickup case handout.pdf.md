# When Friday pickup can go ahead
Reading Conditions at Work | ELM-CRW-2024-HO-001 | 07 May 2024 | Heather Callahan

Read the extracts, then decide what reception should do in each case. Both visitors arrive on Friday at 16:20 and both collections are booked. Write the section and confirmation time that support each decision.

## Extracts from the reception instructions

**1. Usual Friday collection**

“On Fridays, release items for collection by 16:00. Direct visitors arriving after the usual collection time to reception so staff can check whether the exception below applies.”

**2. Reserved collections after 16:00**

“A reserved collection may be released up to 17:30 on Friday only when it was booked and a supervisor confirmed the collection before 16:00. Check both the booking and the supervisor’s confirmation time in the record before releasing the item. The 17:30 limit is the latest collection time; it is not the deadline for the supervisor’s confirmation.”

**3. Carry-over and record**

“If the conditions in section 2 are not met, arrange collection for the next business day. Record the booking reference, reason for carry-over and date arranged in the reception record.”

Source: Janice Mitchell, *Friday collection: reception instructions*, Brookdale Service Cooperative, BSC-OPS-2024-014, 02 May 2024, sections 1–3.
