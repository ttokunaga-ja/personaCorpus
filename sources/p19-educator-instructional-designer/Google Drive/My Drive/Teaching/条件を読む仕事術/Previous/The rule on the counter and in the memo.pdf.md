# The rule on the counter and in the memo
Reading Conditions at Work | ELM-CRW-2024-HO-002 | 22 May 2024 | Heather Callahan

Work with a partner. Read both documents before deciding what the reception desk can do. Write the reader and the scope of each, then answer the two separate questions below.

## Document A: notice at the counter
**Friday collections**

The usual collection time on Fridays is up to 16:00. If you arrive after that time, please speak to reception.

Audience: visitors collecting items. Issuer: Brookdale Service Cooperative.

## Document B: reception instructions
Brookdale Service Cooperative, Friday collection: reception instructions, BSC-OPS-2024-014, 02 May 2024, Janice Mitchell. The following are excerpts from sections 1–3.

**1. Usual Friday collection**
“On Fridays, release items for collection by 16:00. Direct visitors arriving after the usual collection time to reception so staff can check whether the exception below applies. Do not assume that a booking alone permits a later release.”

**2. Reserved collections after 16:00**
“A reserved collection may be released up to 17:30 on Friday only when it was booked and a supervisor confirmed the collection before 16:00. Check both the booking and the supervisor’s confirmation time in the record before releasing the item. The 17:30 limit is the latest collection time; it is not the deadline for the supervisor’s confirmation.”

**3. Carry-over and record**
“If the conditions in section 2 are not met, arrange collection for the next business day. Record the booking reference, reason for carry-over and date arranged in the reception record. If the confirmation record is not at hand, check the supervisor confirmation log before deciding; do not release the item while confirmation is being checked. Once the facts are established, record the outcome and tell the visitor what happens next.”
