# Whose queue — first wording

Reading Conditions at Work · ELM-CRW-2026-LP-005 · v0.1  
05 Aug 2026 · Heather Callahan · Desk review for Erin MacDonald  
Draft for review before distribution

## Learning aims

Learners will read an instruction about the east door at Brookdale reception and decide who should use it. They will compare the destination written for a visitor with the destination written for a delivery.

## Teaching sequence

Show the reception instruction: “Scheduled deliveries between 10:00 and 11:00 use the east door.” Tell learners there is a visitor with a 10:00 appointment and a delivery at reception. Ask the first question as written: “Who uses the east door?”

Ask each pair to put both people in the same comparison table and write the destination. Invite pairs to present their choices. After the presentations, ask them to return to the instruction and fill in the intended reader column.

| Person at reception | Appointment or purpose | Destination to write | Intended reader of the instruction (after presentations) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Visitor | Appointment at 10:00 | __________________ | __________________ |
| Delivery | Scheduled delivery | __________________ | __________________ |

## Check for understanding

Listen to the stated destinations and then ask which words identify the instruction's intended reader. Erin, please look at whether my opening question gives the pair enough to compare the two people before they fill in the final column.
