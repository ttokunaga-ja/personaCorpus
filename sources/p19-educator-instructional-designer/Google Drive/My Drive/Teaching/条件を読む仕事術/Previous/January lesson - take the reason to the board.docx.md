# January lesson — take the reason to the board

Reading Conditions at Work · ELM-CRW-2026-LP-001 · v1.0 · 23 Jan 2026 · Heather Callahan  
For Tristan McLeod · 70 minutes

## Learning aims

By the end of the lesson, ask readers to show that they can:

1. Locate the ordinary same-day return rule in the preparation room notice.
2. Identify the cases it covers and the conditions reception must consider when someone requests an extension.
3. Check each requirement for an exception against a separate entry in the register.
4. Compare the notice and memo by issuer, intended reader and scope.
5. Explain what reception should do using the relevant clause and case fact together.

Use the 18-page January packet (ELM-CRW-2026-HO-002 v1.0). The notice and memo are on pp. 3–4 and the Q5 case register is on p. 9. Ask readers to keep these pages open when they bring evidence to the board.

## Teaching sequence

| Elapsed | Activity | What to ask for |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 min | Introduction | Give out the packet. Ask what you'd need to check before telling a borrower they can return something tomorrow. Take ideas briefly and leave the decision open. |
| 5–25 min | Independent reading | Work through Q2, Q5, Q8 and Q10. Mark the words or register entry you will use and write the document and clause beside your answer. For Q8, mark where each document shows who issued it and who should use it. |
| 25–32 min | Pair evidence check | Give each person two minutes to choose and locate their own evidence. Use two minutes to take turns explaining and saying each other's reason back in their own words. Use the final three minutes to write what reception can establish in Q5 and what it still needs to check. |
| 32–47 min | Whole-group discussion | Start with Q2. Take a document name and clause from one reader before asking for the return time. With Q5, hear the evidence for the recorded receipt separately from the evidence about approval. Keep things reception can decide and things it still has to check in separate board columns. |
| 47–55 min | Compare the two instructions | Bring up Q8. Ask one reader for the preparation room notice and one for the duty lead's reception memo. Put issuer, intended reader and scope beside each source. Ask where the ordinary rule applies and what the memo requires before the later return can be advised. |
| 55–60 min | Rewrite the reason | Return to Q10. Ask each person to revise their one sentence to the borrower after 16:15. Ask them to circle the words stating the action and underline the reason in the same sentence. Have them check both against their cited clauses. |
| 60–70 min | Exit task | Ask for an individual response to the case below. Collect the advice along with the reference to the document, clause and case fact that support it. |

## Check for understanding

Use three spaces on the board: **Source and clause**, **What we can decide**, and **Still to check**. Ask the reader to give the reference before you supply a conclusion. These are the links to listen for and put on the board as they come up:

| Item | Source and clause or field | Link to the case |
|---|---|---|
| Q2 | Preparation room notice, clause 1 | Same-day equipment loans ordinarily come back by 16:30. The borrower in Q2 has made no request for an extension. If someone only says “16:30”, ask “Who is that notice for, and which loans does that rule cover?” |
| Q5 | Duty lead's reception memo, clause 1; register, Application received | Receipt at 15:32 meets the 15:45 application deadline. Put “Application received in time” under what we can decide. |
| Q5 | Duty lead's reception memo, clauses 2 and 4; register, Duty lead approval and Record checked at | The approval field is blank at 16:05. Keep “Check recorded approval with duty lead” under still to check. While checking, hold the promise of a later return. |
| Q8 | Preparation room notice, clauses 1–3; duty lead's reception memo, clauses 1–4 | The prep room tells borrowers the ordinary rule and how to ask. The duty lead tells reception when it can give a later return. Have someone point to clause 3 of the memo and name both conditions for a return by 09:00 the next business day. |
| Q10 | Preparation room notice, clause 1; duty lead's reception memo, clauses 2 and 4 | With no approval recorded by 16:15, reception advises the ordinary 16:30 return. Ask the group to put the reason into a sentence they could say at the window. |

**Exit prompt to give at minute 60:** “A borrower asks what time the equipment is due back. The register shows that the request was received at 15:32. It's now past 16:15 and the duty lead approval field is still blank. Write one sentence giving the borrower the return time and reason. Below it, give the document name, clause and register fact you used.”

Look for the ordinary same-day 16:30 return and the connection between approval still being unrecorded and the 16:15 deadline in the memo. Keep the reference with the response. If the time is correct but the link is missing, make a note to ask the reader which field and clause they used.
