# Two pauses in the loan lesson
Reading Conditions at Work | ELM-CRW-2025-LP-001 | v0.1 | 12 Nov 2025 | Heather Callahan  
Prepared for Tristan McLeod

## Learning aims

Give the ordinary return time using the borrower notice. For a request to return equipment the next business day, compare the application time and the recorded approval with the two deadlines in the reception memo. Explain what the front desk can tell the borrower while approval is still being checked.

## Teaching sequence

Before starting, have the borrower notice, reception memo and extension register extract to hand. Keep the register back until you have introduced the two conditions. The times below are elapsed from the start.

| Elapsed time | Minutes | What to do |
|---|---:|---|
| 0–15 | 15 | Read the preparation room notice. Have learners locate the sentence addressed to borrowers about ordinary same-day return. |
| 15–18 | 3 | Ask, “When is the equipment ordinarily due back, and what words in the notice tell you?” Start with quiet individual writing. Ask for one line giving the time and the words that support it. Use the remainder to look back at the notice together. |
| 18–30 | 12 | Read the reception memo. Match application receipt by 15:45 and duty lead approval recorded by 16:15 to their respective register fields. Explain that a return by 09:00 the next business day requires both. |
| 30–33 | 3 | Show an application received at 15:40 with the approval field empty. Ask, “Is this record enough to advise a next-morning return? Which entries support your answer?” Begin with everyone writing their own decision and reason. Use the remainder to hear which fields they checked. |
| 33–41 | 8 | Compare the 15:40 entry with the application deadline. Then trace what the approval requirement would need from the register. |
| 41–50 | 9 | Summarize the steps at the desk. Check with the duty lead when approval is missing and hold the promise while checking. If approval remains unrecorded at the deadline, advise the ordinary 16:30 return. End by having someone put that advice into words for the borrower. |

## Check for understanding

For the first response, listen for 16:30 **and** a line from the notice giving the ordinary return. For the second, ask the reader to point to receipt at 15:40 and the separate approval field. The receipt meets the 15:45 condition. An empty approval field supplies no evidence that approval was recorded by 16:15. Ask what the desk can say now and what advice it gives if the deadline passes with the field still empty.
