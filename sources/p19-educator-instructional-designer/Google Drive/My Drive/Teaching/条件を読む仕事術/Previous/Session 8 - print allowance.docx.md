# Session 8 — Print allowance

ELM-CRW-2024-LP-005 v1.0 · 29 Oct 2024 · Heather Callahan  
Reading Conditions at Work · Session 8 · For Erin MacDonald and Heather Callahan

## Learning aims

Given a printing notice and a receipt, the learner can find the ordinary limit, name the condition for the extra pages and check whether the receipt records that condition. Have each learner give a decision and show the line of the notice and the receipt field that support it.

## Teaching sequence

1. Heather shows the first line of the notice by itself. “Printing requests are limited to 20 pages.” Ask, “What can the desk print under this line alone? Show me the words that set the limit.”
2. Uncover the next line. “An additional 10 pages may be printed when the instructor has checked the exception box on the request.” Ask, “What would you have to see on the request before you could count those ten pages?” Give learners a moment to underline the clause beginning with “when”.
3. Erin puts the two receipts side by side. Give learners time to write separate answers before asking anyone to speak. For each receipt, ask them to state what the desk can print now and underline the words and mark the receipt field they used. On B, ask them to include what the desk could do next.

| Receipt | Pages requested | Instructor exception checked |
|---|---:|---|
| A | 26 | Yes |
| B | 26 | |

4. Start the discussion with A. Ask, “Six pages sit above the ordinary allowance. What lets the desk print them?” Look for the check on A and the resulting 30-page allowance. Then turn to B. Ask, “The request is still 26 pages. What could the desk do while that box is blank?” Accept printing up to 20 now and holding the rest, or waiting for the instructor to check the box before printing all 26.

## Check for understanding

Have the learners read their decision on B to a partner. Erin listens for the three parts of the explanation: the ordinary limit of 20, the extra 10 only with the recorded instructor check, and the empty check field on B. If a learner reads the empty box as a refusal, ask them to point to a recorded decision. If they allow 26 on B, ask them where the instructor’s check appears.
