# July 6 — a way in for all 36

ELM-CRW-2026-DD-003 · v1.0 · 6 July 2026  
Heather Callahan · For Arjun Deshmukh and Kathleen Roy

## Context

Arjun's 10:00 check shows 36 people enrolled in Reading Conditions at Work: 32 have their offline setting in place and four do not. The four have two proposed collection points: training reception for two and the Room 2 entrance for two. This is a setting check, not a count of who can open the packet or a judgment about reading.

On 10 June we opened the single saved packet on the instructor device without a connection; the cloud-only record did not open. That check did not establish the learners' settings. We need a route into the same three documents for everyone at the first meeting.

## Change and rationale

Give each of the four a paper copy of the directions by 10 July. The directions will show how to save and reopen the packet without Wi-Fi and where to obtain a paper packet if it still does not open. At the Room 2 entrance on 13 July, put paper copies of the case packet within reach before the 13:00 start. The directions and the packet are different items: handing over directions does not establish that the packet has opened.

| Action | Owner | When |
|---|---|---|
| Check settings and hand over one directions sheet to each of the four, using the arranged collection points | Arjun | By 10 July |
| Prepare the packet and check the available viewing routes at the session | Heather | Before and at the first session |
| Offer operational help with opening or navigating the material when needed | Kathleen | As needed before the exercise |

## Next review

At the first session, ask separately whether people can use the packet on a device or on paper. Keep that access observation apart from the work they write in response to the case. If a device route fails, offer the paper packet before proceeding; do not ask the learner to answer while the material is unavailable.
