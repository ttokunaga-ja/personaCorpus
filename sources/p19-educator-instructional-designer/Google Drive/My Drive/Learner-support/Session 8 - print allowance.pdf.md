## Page 2 — Check your reasoning

### Receipt A

**Print all 26 pages.** The first line of the notice gives the ordinary limit of 20. The next line makes another 10 available when the instructor checks the exception box. “Yes” in that box on A records the check, so this request can use up to 30 pages. All 26 fit.

If you wrote “26 is less than 30,” look at the second line again. Add what needs to be recorded before the desk can use 30, then point to the entry on the receipt that supplies it.

### Receipt B

**The ordinary limit of 20 applies for now.** The exception box is blank. The receipt gives the desk no recorded instructor check to apply the extra 10. The desk can split the job, print up to 20 pages now and hold the remaining six. It can also wait for the instructor to check the box before printing all 26 together.

If you wrote “the instructor refused,” go back to the blank box. An empty field leaves the check unconfirmed. Compare A with B once more. Both request 26 pages. Put your finger on the one field that changes the decision.
