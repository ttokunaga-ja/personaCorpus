# Start with the useful part of the answer
ELM-BPE-2025-IR-005 | 14 Jul 2025 | Heather Callahan  
Building Proposals from Evidence | Individual support note

## Starting point

At our 15:15 check I first asked Alexandre to point to his recommendation in the submitted answer. He identified the sentence choosing the Wednesday slot. Starting there let us keep the useful choice visible while looking for what the reception lead still needed.

## What we checked aloud

I asked which line in the callback deadline list he would bring to the lead's attention. He indicated the entry for 10 calls due by Wednesday 16:00. We discussed connecting that fact to the choice in one sentence. His pointing and our oral check are separate from the submitted paper: the paper itself contains the recommendation but no supporting fact or source. I cannot add what he showed me afterwards to what was submitted.

## Next practice

For the next independent exercise, look only at whether Alexandre can point the reader to the place in the record and connect the fact there to his reason. Ask him to put that connection in writing; do not treat this conversation as a revised mark for 9 July.
