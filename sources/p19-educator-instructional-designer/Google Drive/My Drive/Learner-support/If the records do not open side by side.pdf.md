# If the records do not open side by side

Reading Conditions at Work · ELM-CRW-2026-HO-006 · v1.0  
5 Jun 2026 · Kathleen Roy

For Heather and Arjun — pre-course access support

## Working through one document at a time

If a learner cannot display the documents next to one another, offer a sequential route through *Counter reopening — documents for the exercise* (ELM-CRW-2026-HO-004). Ask what format the learner can use comfortably and allow time to move between the documents. The learner can make their own notes; the support person need not supply a decision.

1. Open Document 1, the visitor notice. Ask the learner to note the source as “visitor notice” and write the normal reopening time in their own notes.
2. Open Document 2, the staff instruction. Its sections are numbered. Ask the learner to distinguish this instruction for staff from the notice for visitors, then note section 2's two requirements and section 3's direction for an empty entry.
3. Find Document 3, the counter confirmation record, after the instruction. It is viewed at 12:20. Locate the row labelled “Passage checked” and the row labelled “Responsible person's approval”. The learner can copy each label and what appears beside it, keeping a blank field distinct from a recorded refusal.
4. Return to the notes and the separate task sheet *Write the counter decision* (ELM-CRW-2026-HO-005). The learner writes the visitor-facing sentence, identifies the applicable section, links the record facts to it and states what still needs checking. They can revisit each document in sequence as often as needed.

## Record the support separately

Arjun can record the access difficulty and the support offered in the learner-support record, without entering a judgment on the answer there. Heather should assess the learner's written decision against the course criteria separately: help opening or navigating the materials is not evidence that the reading decision is right or wrong. If the materials still cannot be opened, agree a usable way to view them before asking the learner to complete the task.
