# Can the duty lead trace the reason?
Building Proposals from Evidence | ELM-BPE-2025-HO-008 | 17 Jul 2025 | Heather Callahan

## For Alexandre Pelletier

A busy reception lead needs to see both your recommendation and where to check its support. Use these two extracts from the callback exercise.

**Intake list:** 12 calls are waiting for a callback.

**Callback deadline list:** 10 of the 12 calls need a callback by Wednesday 16:00; Thursday is acceptable for the other 2.

Write a short subject line and two sentences to the reception lead. Recommend a slot and explain why the deadline matters, using the intake count and the deadline entry. Give the reader enough information to find the entries again.

## Check your own writing

- Circle each fact from the records in your two sentences.
- Underline the words that tell the reader where you found each fact.
- Read the reason aloud: does it connect the deadline to the slot, or merely repeat a number?
