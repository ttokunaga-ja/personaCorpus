# Desk handoff reference

Reading Conditions at Work  
ELM-CRW-2024-HO-007 v1.0 · 24 Oct 2024 · Heather Callahan  
For reception, instructors and Learner Support

## Confirm the match that day

Use the timetable to check the class date and session. Give both to the instructor: “The learner missed the class on ___, Session ___. Which exercise and version should we give them?”

| On the desk record | Enter |
|---|---|
| Class | Date and session number |
| Exercise | Title and version returned by the instructor |
| Due to learner | End of the next calendar day after the absence was confirmed |

Check that the copy includes every page the learner needs. If an answer or reference page is separate, check for it with the exercise.

## Hand over work as soon as the deadline is at risk

Reception or Learner Support calls Arjun when it looks as though a usable copy won’t reach the learner by the deadline. Read the record back to him:

> “This is for Session ___, exercise ___, version ___. The learner needs it by the end of ___. We still need ___ before it can go.”

Name the actual work, such as asking the instructor to confirm the version or obtaining the missing page and checking the whole set. If a field is unresolved, say which field needs checking.

Ask Arjun to repeat the remaining work and deadline and give the date and local time he took it. Record **Arjun Deshmukh** and the time he gives in **Taken by / receipt time** (for example, 24 Oct, 11:05 EDT). The person passing it on keeps the work until Arjun confirms. At the desk change, read the deadline, current owner and next action back to the relief worker.

## Enter delivery when the learner receives it

Fill in **Delivered to learner** with the actual date and time once the learner has the complete exercise. Until then, leave that field empty. Keep the receipt time in its own field.
