# Room change check

Reading Conditions at Work · Session 6  
ELM-CRW-2024-HO-006 v1.0 · 17 Oct 2024 · Heather Callahan

## Page 1 — Decide what to tell the group

### 1. Room notice

> Afternoon group: 14:00 in Room 2.

### 2. Staff instruction

> Move the afternoon group to Room 4 only when approval of the equipment check has been recorded on the reception record.

### 3. Reception record — 12:20

Equipment check approved: __________  
Recorded by: __________

### Your decision at 12:30

Someone in the 14:00 group asks where to go. The 12:20 entry is the latest one you have seen. Write what you would tell the person while reception looks for a more recent entry. Then write the question you would ask at the desk. Use the three extracts to explain your decision before you turn to page 2.

Usual time and room (go back to the room notice in 1): __________________

What must be recorded for the move (read from “only when” in 2): __________

What the 12:20 entry shows and what is still unknown (look at 3): __________

What I would ask reception: __________________________________________

What I would tell the person for now: __________________________________

## Page 2 — Check your answer against the extracts

### A reply at 12:30

“Please go to Room 2 for the 14:00 group. I'll check with reception to see if that direction needs to change.”

At the desk: “Could you check the current reception record for the 14:00 group? Has approval of the equipment check been entered since the 12:20 entry?”

### Where the decision comes from

1. **Usual direction.** In the room notice, “Afternoon group: 14:00 in Room 2” gives both the time and the room. Use that direction while the approval entry is still unconfirmed.
2. **Condition for a move.** In the staff instruction, read from “only when” to the end. To direct the group to Room 4, you need approval of the equipment check recorded on the reception record. If reception finds that entry, you can give the Room 4 direction.
3. **What an empty field tells you.** The approval line in the 12:20 entry is blank. That entry supplies no recorded approval. It leaves open whether the check took place, what the result was and whether approval has been entered since 12:20.

If you sent the group to Room 4 because the equipment was being checked, go back to extract 2 and add the step that has to happen at reception. If you took the blank for a refusal, look at extract 3 again. Write down just what the 12:20 record shows, then name the evidence you would ask reception to find.
