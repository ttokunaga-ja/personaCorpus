# If you still need to hand in the reading memo
Reading Conditions at Work · ELM-CRW-2026-HO-015 · v1.0 · 25 Sep 2026 · Heather Callahan

## A further opportunity

You can complete the one-page decision memo in the next term. You may work with the same case on paper. You do not need to attend a meeting in order to submit.

## Tell us what would help

By **8 October at 12:00 noon**, reply to Heather or Kathleen to say whether you would like paper materials or a short meeting. If you want paper, we will agree with you how you can receive it. If you would like to meet, an optional 20-minute consultation is planned for **15 October, 12:30–12:50, Room 2**. Tell us by the reply date so Kathleen can arrange it. If you have a copy of your earlier work, you can bring it to the meeting.

## Work on the memo

Read the visitor notice, the applicable sections of the staff instruction and the check record in *Counter reopening – documents for the exercise*. Use the same task whether you read the packet on paper or on a device. Write the advice you would give the visitor now, identify the applicable clause and the facts in the record, and explain what remains unconfirmed. Include the question you would put to the person responsible and how the answer could affect your advice. An empty field is not itself a refusal.

## Hand it in

Submit your one-page decision memo by **22 October at 16:00 Toronto time**, whether or not you attend the consultation. If you need help arranging paper or submission, contact Kathleen or Heather before the deadline. This opportunity will be recorded with next term's support work.
