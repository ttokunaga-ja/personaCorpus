# Open your reading packet without Wi-Fi

Reading Conditions at Work · ELM-CRW-2026-HO-007 · v1.0  
7 July 2026 · Heather Callahan

## Before you leave a connection

1. While connected to Wi-Fi, download **Counter reopening case packet.pdf** to your device. Wait for the download to finish.
2. Turn Wi-Fi off. Open **Files**, then choose **My files > Downloads**.
3. Find **Counter reopening case packet.pdf** in Downloads and open it from there. This is the copy saved on your device.
4. Check that you can read all three parts: the visitor notice, the staff instruction and the counter confirmation record. You can move through them one at a time.

## If it does not open

Before the first session starts on Monday 13 July, pick up a paper case packet at the Room 2 entrance. It contains the same three documents. Ask for help at the entrance if you need help opening or navigating the packet. Bring either the saved packet or the paper copy to the exercise.
