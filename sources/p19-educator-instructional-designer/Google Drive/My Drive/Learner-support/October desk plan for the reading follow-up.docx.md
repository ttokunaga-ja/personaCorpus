# October desk plan for the reading follow-up
ELM-CRW-2026-DD-006 · v1.0 · 25 Sep 2026 · Heather Callahan
For Kathleen Roy and Arjun Deshmukh

## Context

Two learners did not have a valid memo at the 11 September close: one has no receipt record; one sent a file that could not be opened. Neither has a mark. Both will be offered the same paper and optional meeting routes. The follow-up belongs in the next-term support record, separate from the September close.

## Change and rationale

| Step | Responsible | Arrangement |
|---|---|---|
| Receive preferences | Kathleen | Check replies by 8 October at 12:00; arrange paper materials or the optional meeting as requested. Agree the paper collection or delivery method with each learner, rather than assuming it. |
| Consultation | Kathleen | Arrange the 15 October 12:30–12:50 Room 2 slot for those who want it. A meeting is optional, not a condition of submission. |
| Receive work | Arjun | Record receipt and check that the submitted file or paper is readable. If it is not readable, contact the learner about a readable copy. |
| Read memo | Heather | Read the one-page decision memo submitted by 22 October at 16:00 and record the follow-up in next-term support records. |

The handout asks for an advice sentence, the applicable clause, record evidence and a question about the unconfirmed approval. Paper carries the same task. Where the original file remains on the learner's device, offer to check whether it can be opened before asking for new work.

## Next review

Kathleen will check the response list after noon on 8 October and coordinate arrangements with each learner. Arjun and Heather will keep receipt and reading distinct. At present these are arrangements, not completed consultations or submissions.
