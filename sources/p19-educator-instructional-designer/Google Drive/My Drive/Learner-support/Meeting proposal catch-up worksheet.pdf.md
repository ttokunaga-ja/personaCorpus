# Finish the proposal after the meeting

Communicating in Workplace Meetings · ELM-CWM-2024-HO-006 · 22 Nov 2024 · Heather Callahan

If you missed the meeting practice or did not finish writing, use this page to prepare your final exercise. You can work through it on your own before returning to “Put a conditional proposal on the record”.

## Start with what is still open

It is Thursday at noon. The staffing coordinator has not confirmed the key rota. The proposed trial is two Saturdays, up to six visits each Saturday. The agenda reports that 8 of 30 weekday maintenance visits were rescheduled in the last four weeks.

1. What fact is not confirmed? Write one line.

____________________________________________________________

2. What weekday arrangement should be kept while the rota is unconfirmed? Write one line.

____________________________________________________________

## Check your own proposal

Read your draft against each question. Add what is missing in your own words.

- **Action and scope:** Have I said what I propose, for how many Saturdays and the maximum visits each Saturday?
- **Reason:** Have I used a fact from the agenda to explain why the proposal is worth considering?
- **Confirmation:** Have I named the staffing coordinator and Thursday noon as the confirmation point for the key rota?
- **If unconfirmed:** Have I said to keep the weekday slots, rather than only saying that we must wait?
