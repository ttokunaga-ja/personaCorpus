12 September

Two of the six groups started with a long account of the repair history. By the time they reached item 4 I still couldn't hear whether they wanted the chair to put the Saturday trial to a decision. The history was relevant, but it crowded out the request.

Next time: ask for “What I need the meeting to decide today is…” in the first breath. Then choose just one fact from the repair history to support that request. Have the listener mark the point where a chair could actually ask for a yes or no; don't solve it by asking the speaker to talk faster.
