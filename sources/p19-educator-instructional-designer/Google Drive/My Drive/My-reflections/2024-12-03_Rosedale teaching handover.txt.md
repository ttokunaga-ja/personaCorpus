3 December 2024 — putting the meeting materials away

For the next instructor, keep these together and read in this order:

1. “What counts as a conditional proposal” — action and scope; reason from the agenda; staffing coordinator and Thursday noon; weekday action if unconfirmed. All four must be present, even when the wording differs.
2. The anonymous marked practice, “Saturday only if someone has the keys”. It shows why “if someone has the keys” needs both a named confirmation and a separate fallback. It is a practice response, not the final assessment.
3. “Counting the November proposals” — the report-time reconciliation: 24 in the group, 22 submissions, 17 meeting the criteria. The five submitted but not yet meeting them and the two without submissions are different groups.

Some could say Thursday noon but still omitted the switch back to weekday slots. Next time begin with the rota unconfirmed and ask what can be kept in place. Only then work backwards to the condition and Saturday proposal. Don't open by asking for a vote: last time the vote pulled the discussion away from the reason and the two possible actions.
