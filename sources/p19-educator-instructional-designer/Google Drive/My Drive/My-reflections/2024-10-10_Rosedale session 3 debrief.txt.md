10 October — after session 3

I asked for a show of hands on the Saturday trial before the groups had put their reasons on the table. Once the hands went up, the discussion followed the number who wanted Saturday. We ran out of time to say what had to be confirmed by Thursday noon. A preference is not the same thing as a reason or a decision.

Next time, put the condition card up before asking for a judgement. Each group should write both actions: proceed with two Saturdays, up to six visits each day, if the key rota is confirmed; keep the weekday slots if it is still unconfirmed at noon. Only then ask the listeners which action the information supports. Leave enough time to point to the fact that changed the action.
