21 November 2024 — after session six

Several people could begin the proposal: two Saturdays, six visits each day. When I asked what would happen if the rota was still open, a few stopped at “waiting for the key rota to be confirmed.” That describes the uncertainty but leaves the weekday arrangement unsaid. I had moved them from the role-play to the individual box too quickly.

For the follow-up, start at Thursday noon with no confirmation. Ask for one action that can still be taken: keep the weekday slots. Then return to the submission box and put that action alongside the proposed trial, the agenda fact and the staffing coordinator's confirmation deadline. The follow-up is a bridge back to the final exercise, not another submission to count.
