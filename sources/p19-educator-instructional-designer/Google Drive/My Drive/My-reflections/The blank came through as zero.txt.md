22 September, before closing the count

My trial count turned the blank marks on the receipt list into zeros. That made nine look as if they had submitted and scored below 14. CRW-12 has no receipt; CRW-31 has only the 15:47 file we could not open. Neither has a mark.

I will not use that trial. Restricting the score count to the 34 valid marked memos gives seven below 14 and 27 at or above 14. Keep the two without a valid submission outside the score bands. The enrolment denominator is still 36 when we report the course; 34 is the number of marked submissions, not the number enrolled.
