# After the first session — 13 July

I asked “Did they approve it?” too early. The record had no approval entry at 12:20, but my question asked people to decide whether approval happened or did not happen. That makes the blank sound like a no.

Some answers gave 13:00 and still said “They refused approval.” Getting the time does not tell me whether the writer distinguished an empty field from a refusal. Section 3 says to check the blank before announcing an early reopening; it does not say the responsible person refused.

Next time, start with: “What is recorded as of 12:20, and what do you still need to check?” Then ask what can be said to the visitor while that check remains open. Keep this separate from Arjun's count of who could open the packet.
