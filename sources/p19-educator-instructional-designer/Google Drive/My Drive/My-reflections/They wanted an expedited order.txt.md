28 May 2025

One pair answered Q11 at 12:10 with “ask for an expedited order.” They had noticed that 22 kits could not be made in-house, but jumped from a missed noon receipt deadline to a service the print-room information did not offer. The diagram separated the quantity limit from the time check, yet I had not made the absence of an express option explicit enough in our discussion.

I brought us back to what we could say before promising a delivery: neither route is available on the stated conditions, so speak to the requester about whether the deadline can change. We can ask about other arrangements, but cannot present an expedited order as available without confirmation.

Next time, give the pairs two separate columns: conditions established by the material, and questions still to ask. Have them check their recommendation against both before saying when the kits will arrive.
