16 July — after the check

Q07: 22 of 24 chose B. Of the eight correct-answer volunteers Erin and I spoke to after collection, three mentioned both the booking and the supervisor's confirmation time before 4:00. The other five gave only one clue, such as the booking. Eight is a subset of the 24, not another set of answers.

Did “booked” in B tell them where to go? Or did the question itself fail to make them read the time? We cannot tell from the letter alone. Next, ask about a booked case on its own: what else must be checked before release? Keep the confirmation time separate from the arrival time.
