# Methods Abstracts: Submission Guide

February 3, 2025  
Charlotte Pearson, Program Committee  
Applied Document Research Forum

## Deadline

Submit Methods Abstracts by February 14, 2025 at 5:00 p.m. Pacific time. Please allow time to check the text in the submission form before sending it.

## Submission fields

Enter the title and the author names and affiliations in their separate fields. The Abstract field is limited to 500 words of plain text. The limit applies to the text in that field, including any quoted examples; it does not include the separate title or author and affiliation fields.

The Abstract field cannot accept figures, tables, footnotes, or file attachments. Explain any essential definitions and examples in the text itself. Work whose comparison is still pending may be described as a planned method; make its stage clear rather than presenting planned results as completed findings.

## After submission

Review the pasted text and submit the form. Record the confirmation number shown after sending, so that the committee can locate the submission if you need to ask about it.
