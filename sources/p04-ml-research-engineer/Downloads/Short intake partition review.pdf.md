## Finding

In an independent reconciliation of case keys for the January short-intake readout, I found 24 distinct cases with documents in more than one partition. Together those cases account for 84 documents. They are not 84 distinct cases. None of the identified cases spans all three partitions.

| Old partition pair | Distinct cases | Documents belonging to those cases |
|---|---:|---:|
| Training / evaluation | 12 | 42 |
| Training / development | 7 | 24 |
| Development / evaluation | 5 | 18 |
| Total | 24 | 84 |

## Why this matters

One example links a request to change a visit in training with a confirmation concerning that same visit in evaluation. Their wording is not identical, but the two documents concern the same underlying case. A document-level draw can place related observations on opposite sides of a boundary without producing an exact duplicate. The evaluation is therefore not independent at the case level; matching only identical text would miss this example.

Please suspend use of the January 16 slide 3 macro-F1 of 0.851 as a comparison result while the scope is confirmed. Then specify a case-level allocation and verify the partition boundaries before reporting a new score. Preserve the existing label decisions separately from the allocation question, and tell the original audience how the January figure should be treated.

I can review the proposed grouping evidence and the description of the new evaluation condition.
