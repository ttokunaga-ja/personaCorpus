# The status in the opening line

July 22, 2025 — Joshua opened a record with me before we start the inspection study. The status in the first line is entered at the start of the round. It can be superseded by the disposition entered just before signature.

In the record we discussed, the opening says the equipment may continue in use. The final disposition calls for a repeat inspection. Reading only the opening would give the wrong current action. The status field in the ledger is useful for finding the record, but I should not make that field the answer without reading the signed disposition. Ask Fatima how we should record the words that make the later action effective, especially when an older instruction is quoted again.
