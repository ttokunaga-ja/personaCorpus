# February 7 — examples for Fatima

The July guide's schedule pair is the best candidate for the external abstract:

- “Move the visit to Thursday.” — Reschedule. “Move” identifies the requested action and the visit is its object.
- “Thursday visit?” — Hold. Thursday and a visit are present, but the body does not say whether to check or change the appointment. Do not supply an action from the channel or a presumed context.

The shared day is useful because it shows that an object and time alone are not enough. The contrast can fit in one sentence without a table.

The cancellation pair takes more room. The invoice pair needs us to explain again the difference between a request to send a document and a question about its contents; the ambiguous “take care of” then needs its own explanation. For a 500-word external abstract, use the schedule pair and keep the other pairs for the guide. Fatima, please check that the sentence does not imply Hold is a model confidence threshold.
