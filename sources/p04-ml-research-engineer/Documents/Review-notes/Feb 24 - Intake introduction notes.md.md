# February 24 — stop opening with the old score

Starting with 0.851 and then 0.832 makes the difference look like a drop measured on the same evaluation documents. It was not: the 940-document January evaluation used the withdrawn document allocation, while the April allocation kept cases together. The difference is not an estimate of the effect of leakage.

Open the comparison with the word-frequency linear classifier at macro-F1 0.832 and the training-majority baseline at 0.021 on the same 935 evaluation documents. Give the unit and conditions alongside both. If the withdrawn 0.851 is needed, put it in the history of the allocation change, with its withdrawal and different evaluation set, not as the first performance comparison.
