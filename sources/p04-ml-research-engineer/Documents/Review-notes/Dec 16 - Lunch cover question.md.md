# Lunch cover question

December 16, 2024 · for Vanessa

An hour from 12:00 to 13:00 gives 20 possible three-minute reviews on paper. The workbook tells us that much about the rate assumption. It doesn't tell us how many people could cover lunch or whether any of them could spare the full hour.

If we try cover in the next comparison, where does ownership change? We need to decide when the cover reviewer takes the queue and what happens if they're still reading an item at 13:00. If both people open it, we'd count two people's work for one completion. If the cover reviewer finishes it, who takes the items still waiting when the assigned reviewer returns?

I'd leave a shift change out of the December finding. We have the restart count under one assumed schedule. To compare cover we need the handoff rule and the time someone can actually give it.
