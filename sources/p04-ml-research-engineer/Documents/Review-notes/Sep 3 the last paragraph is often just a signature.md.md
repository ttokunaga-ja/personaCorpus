# The last paragraph is often just a signature

September 3, 2025

Emily and I tried feeding only the last 2,048 body tokens for development records. In records with long signature and closeout sections, that window can discard the opening and middle without gaining a current instruction. A signature confirms completion of the record; it does not by itself replace an instruction earlier in it.

Two readings to keep apart:

- In one record, the opening directs a replacement, but a middle condition says to hold the replacement if the repeat check shows the component is within tolerance. The later entry says the repeat check was within tolerance, and the closing signature does not change it. The condition and the later result together support holding the replacement. The last-only window retains the result and signature but drops the condition connecting that result to the hold; the result alone does not say what to do about replacement.
- In the guard record, the middle says “Repair the damaged guard before returning the unit to use.” The last paragraph is a routine signature. A last-only window drops the repair instruction and leaves no direction that replaces it. The opening-window reading retains the instruction in this example.

These are development readings, not a score on the fixed evaluation partition. I will not carry the last-only proposal into that comparison. Keep the full record beside each window when checking the evidence; the presence of a closing paragraph is not evidence that the operative instruction is there.
