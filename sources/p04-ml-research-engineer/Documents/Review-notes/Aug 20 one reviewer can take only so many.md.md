# One reviewer can take only so many

August 20

I started a threshold proposal with “one reviewer can clear the queue in an hour.” That was a convenience, not a measurement. At 144 items in 60 minutes, the arithmetic leaves 25 seconds per item. It would have to cover reading the body, establishing the right destination, and any return to an uncertain case; I have measured none of those times. It also assumes all 144 arrive together in a conveniently available hour, while the replay says nothing about the shape of arrivals.

I am taking that assumption out of the threshold proposal. The 144 include 122 correct saved predictions, so their inspection still consumes attention; the 22 mismatches sent to review are not automatically corrections. Meanwhile 78 errors remain on the automatic side.

Question for Brandon: what does a reviewer actually do from opening the original body to confirming the right destination, including a second look, and how could we measure capacity across a realistic arrival pattern? Until we have that, show the queue and errors left automatic together rather than choosing a threshold from an invented hour.
