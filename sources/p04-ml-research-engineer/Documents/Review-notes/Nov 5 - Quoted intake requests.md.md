# Quoted intake requests — November 5

In June, Emily and I tried the last sentence on its own. In the visit example it gave us a thank-you and dropped the cancellation at the top. We left that shortcut out. A reply with quoted history raises a different question. The latest wording may give me the right action and still leave me looking elsewhere for its target.

“Can we move the earlier slot?” tells me someone wants a change. If the earlier message is the only place that names the booking, deleting the quote leaves “move” intact but takes away the words that tell me what to move. Reading the old booking text as a fresh request would also get the action wrong.

Ask Emily tomorrow if we can read a few whole replies against shortened copies. For each read, jot down the current action, the slot or other thing it refers to, and the words that identify each. I want to see what goes missing even when the verb survives.
