# June 10 — route is where we saw it

The released band gave me an attractive shortcut: 4 of the 24 Shared inbox documents were wrong, versus 2 of 31 Web form and 1 of 17 Service desk relay. I considered holding the inbox back as a substitute for the score cutoff, leaving the other routes automatic.

I am withdrawing that idea. The route tells us where this set arrived, not that requests from that route will keep the same mix or difficulty. The wording and types of requests entering each channel can change; seven errors in this band cannot establish a route rule. A rule based on these counts would also release the three observed errors from the other routes. Keep the route breakdown as a reading aid, and compare the fixed score rules on independent cases before proposing any routing change.
