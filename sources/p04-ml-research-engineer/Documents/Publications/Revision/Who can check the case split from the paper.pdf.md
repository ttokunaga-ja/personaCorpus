# Who can check the case split from the paper?

Document ID: MDR-STI-20260324-RM-01-R01  
Issued: March 24, 2026  
Author: Vanessa Nguyen  
For: Michael Serrano

## What the paper can state

The April 3, 2025 freeze assigned 2,804 training, 935 development and 935 evaluation documents. Cross-partition comparisons found zero shared cases for each of training/development, training/evaluation and development/evaluation. The unit of assignment was the case; a received body was the unit scored. The 4,674 labeled candidates were assigned once, while 126 Hold documents remained outside the allocation.

## How we can check it

Joshua's freeze checks compare cross-channel case keys in the three membership lists and record zero for each pair, as well as the document counts. His case-key description traces a key either to receipt records sharing a case reference or to a receipt and a confirmed alias-table entry. A similar subject alone is not proof of a shared case. Those records, the membership lists and their underlying evidence let the research team inspect a particular join and repeat the overlap check. The freeze table reports the aggregate result; it does not itself publish each underlying receipt or prove each link in isolation.

The paper should report the partition counts, case-level allocation and zero overlap, so a reader can understand the check and its result. Individual intake IDs and case keys are not needed in the submitted manuscript. A reader of the paper alone cannot independently reconstruct each join without the underlying research records; we should not imply otherwise.
