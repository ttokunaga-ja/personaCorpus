# Figure 1. Fixed evaluation comparison

Vertical axis: macro-F1 across 12 classes, from 0 to 1, with ticks at 0, 0.2, 0.4, 0.6, 0.8 and 1.0. Horizontal axis: method. Two bars share the same zero origin:

| Method | macro-F1 | Direct value label |
|---|---:|---:|
| Training majority-class baseline | 0.021 | 0.021 |
| Word-frequency linear classifier | 0.832 | 0.832 |

Below the two bars, centered across the comparison: “Same 935 evaluation documents”.

Three separate leader lines point to: the zero origin (“Start at zero”), the numbers above the bars (“Write the values on the bars”), and the shared denominator below the bars (“Put the common evaluation set with both methods”). The figure in the paper uses these same bars, scale, values and denominator without the three leader lines or their annotations.
