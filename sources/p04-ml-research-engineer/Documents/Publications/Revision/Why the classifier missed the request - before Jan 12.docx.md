# Why the classifier missed the request

Document ID: MDR-STI-20260108-RM-01-R01  
Issued: January 8, 2026  
Author: Vanessa Nguyen  
For: Fatima Rahman — working discussion, not for external circulation

## Working explanation

A recurring difficulty may be a mismatch between the action suggested by a conspicuous word and the action the sender actually wants. In “Please don't cancel the visit; move it to Tuesday,” the word cancel names an action, but the sender rejects it and asks for a move. A classifier that treats the cancellation cue as the request could choose Cancel request rather than Reschedule.

My working hypothesis is that this mismatch provides a common explanation for the 160 selected development misses. Negation reverses the apparent action in the clearest cancellation examples; perhaps the remaining errors can also be understood as the model taking the negated or salient action for the desired one. This is a provisional account for Fatima to challenge, not a result from an intervention on the model.

## What we need to read

The 160 are selected misclassifications from saved predictions on the 935-document development partition. They do not describe the frequency of any error in the fixed evaluation set. ## Questions in the margin

Do schedule inquiries without a request to move belong in this explanation? What about an indirect question about a charge, where the sender does not name an action to negate? If neither contains a rejected action, I need to know whether I am stretching the account beyond what the text supports. Ask Nicole for her reading before presenting this explanation.
