# June copies to keep

June 23, 2026

When I come back to the review-queue question, start with these rather than reconstructing the comparison from the slides:

- `Documents/Confidence-abstention/Error capture metric definitions.pdf` — the June 5 definition. Hold means confidence strictly below the cutoff; a score equal to it stays automatic. The hold rate uses all 800 scored documents; error capture uses the 100 errors in the saved predictions before either hold rule. Keep those denominators beside the percentages.
- `Documents/Confidence-abstention/Cutoff routing transitions.tsv` — the June 8 movement between the two cutoffs. At 0.60 to 0.45, 72 leave hold: 65 correct predictions and 7 errors. Another 72 remain on hold. This is the table to use when explaining what a smaller queue releases, not just the change in the two rates.
- `Documents/Confidence-abstention/Next cutoff evaluation plan.pdf` — the June 19 proposal for the next comparison, awaiting approval. It proposes fixing the cutoffs and measures before scoring independent labeled documents, and measuring recovery from detection to handoff separately. The way to obtain the receiving time and the acceptable workload still need agreement. Neither the independent evaluation nor the recovery measurement has been done; application remains on hold.

For a presentation, use `Pictures/Charts/Cutoff rates by denominator.png` from June 9. Its separate panels make 144/800 and 72/800 distinct from 22/100 and 15/100. Keep `Pictures/Charts/Jun 4 - Cutoff counts draft.png` as the uncirculated working plot: the shared count scale can invite the wrong comparison.

The June 8 reading with Emily is a set of things to check next time, not a rule fitted to these cases. In the seven released errors, requests to reschedule, ask for status, or obtain a quote were confused with nearby background or another action. Look for those distinctions in the independent documents after the comparison conditions are fixed. The seven cases do not establish how often those mistakes will occur elsewhere.
