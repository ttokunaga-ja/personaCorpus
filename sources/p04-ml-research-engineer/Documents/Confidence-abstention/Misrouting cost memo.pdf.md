# What a wrong route would cost us

Document ID: MDR-CA-20260610-RM-01-R01  
Issued: June 10, 2026  
Author: Vanessa Nguyen  
For: Brandon Romero

## The trade to examine

On the same 800 labelled documents, moving the hold cutoff from 0.60 to 0.45 releases 72 documents to their saved predictions. Sixty-five predictions match the reference and seven do not. The benefit is 65 correct automatic decisions that would otherwise have waited for review; the additional seven wrong routes may require work to find and repair. This count is not a measurement of time saved or time lost.

## Where work could arise

| Request and saved route | Potential consequence to check |
|---|---|
| A request to move an appointment goes to schedule confirmation | A confirmation queue may answer the old date while the requested change waits. Someone must identify the change and pass it to the person who can reschedule. |
| A progress inquiry goes to cancellation | The cancellation history in the text is background; the present question asks whether the repair is signed off. A recipient must recognize the inquiry and send it to the status owner, possibly correcting a reply. |
| A price request for a replacement part goes to work intake | A quote is wanted before any work is ordered. Treating it as an order could prompt unnecessary intake work; the request needs to reach whoever can quote and any mistaken communication needs correction. |

The seven are a small, deliberately selected reading set. These examples describe plausible consequences, not observed incidents or their frequency in service. We cannot derive field correction time from the research records.

## Questions for June 11

Brandon, which role detects a wrong destination, confirms the original routing and puts the request back on hold? Who receives it at the correct destination, contacts the requester if needed, and confirms recovery? For a separate measurement, I propose starting at the recorded detection of the wrong route and ending when the correct owner receives it. We should also record later correction and completion points without treating them as the same interval. Please identify the records that can supply those timestamps and any missing handoff evidence before we decide whether the smaller queue is usable.
