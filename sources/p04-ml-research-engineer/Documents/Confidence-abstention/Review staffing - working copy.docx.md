## Working assumption

For the first pass, give review to one person available throughout the 09:00–17:00 PST shift. Take items in arrival order and allow three minutes per review from start to finish. This is a desk calculation with a fixed handling time. We haven't timed anyone doing the work.

Use the three daily counts from *Three-day replay design* (MDR-CA-20241203-RM-02-R01). Each date represents 200 arrivals. Only the rows already marked for review contribute handling minutes.

| December 4 | 200 | 28 | 84 |

Review effort is the number requiring review multiplied by three minutes. The table adds time spent checking items over the day.

## Provisional staffing read

On this daily-hours calculation, an average of 30 reviews takes 90 minutes of the 480-minute shift, so one reviewer looks sufficient under the working assumption. Even December 5, the largest of the three days by review count, requires 96 minutes of checking.
