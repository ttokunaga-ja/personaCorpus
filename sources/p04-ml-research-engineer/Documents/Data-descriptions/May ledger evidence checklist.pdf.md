# Evidence checklist for additional intake candidates

Document ID: MDR-STI-20250515-DD-01-R01  
Issued: May 15, 2025  
Author: Joshua Ramirez  
For: Vanessa Nguyen and the research meeting

## Scope

Use this checklist on the 96 additional documents proposed for a future comparison. These are separate from the 935 documents in the evaluation partition fixed on April 3. This checklist sets out the review; it does not report the review's results or change the April evaluation.

## Order of checks

1. Record the channel and document ID. Follow the ID to the original receipt record. If it identifies the document, record the receipt reference and mark the origin directly confirmed.
2. If the ledger uses an alias key, locate the record that establishes the correspondence between that alias and the receipt. Record both references. An alike subject or channel alone does not establish the link. Mark alias confirmed only when the correspondence is supported.
3. If neither route establishes the source, record the missing receipt reference or unsupported alias correspondence, assign an owner for follow-up and exclude the row from the next comparison candidates until the evidence is resolved.
4. Rows with supported origins remain candidates. Source evidence alone does not establish partition independence, class support or eligibility to score. Check those separately before fixing comparison conditions.

## Handoff

Keep one row per document with channel, document ID, evidence and disposition. Count direct confirmations, confirmed aliases and unresolved origins separately, and reconcile them to 96. Send Vanessa the worksheet and the channel totals before the meeting. For the evidence standard and examples, use MDR-STI-20250220-DD-01-R01, Where the case key came from. Case and channel keys are for reconciliation, not classifier inputs.
