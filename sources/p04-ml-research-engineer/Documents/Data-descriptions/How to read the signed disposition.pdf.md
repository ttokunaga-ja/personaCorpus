# How to read the signed disposition

Document ID: MDR-LDI-20250805-GD-01-R01  
Issued: August 5, 2025  
Author: Fatima Rahman  
For: Vanessa Nguyen and Emily Chen

## What to record

Read the complete inspection record in order. Assign one of the eight labels to the disposition effective when the record is signed, and copy the exact phrase that supports that action. Record its location and note any earlier finding or quoted instruction that could be mistaken for the current disposition. Do not decide from the opening status or from the mere presence of a phrase at the end.

## Disposition classes

| Label | Action at signature |
|---|---|
| Repair required | Repair is directed. |
| Repeat inspection | A further inspection is directed. |
| Remove from service | Equipment is to be taken out of service. |
| Engineering review | The matter is referred for engineering review. |
| Routine closeout | The inspection is closed routinely. |
| Monitor at next service | The item is to be monitored at the next service. |
| Record correction | The effective action corrects the record. |
| No equipment finding | The record establishes no equipment finding. |

## Two readings

| Record text in order | Reference and evidence | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Opening: “Continue in service.” Observation: vibration noted. Final disposition: “Repeat inspection before the next shift.” Signed. | Repeat inspection; “Repeat inspection before the next shift.” | The final instruction changes the earlier permission. The observation explains the context but is not the disposition. |
| Opening instruction: “Repair the loose guard before use.” Observation confirms the loose guard. Closing: “Inspection completed and signed.” | Repair required; “Repair the loose guard before use.” | The closing is a routine signature and does not cancel or replace the instruction. |

## When the text is difficult

Distinguish an instruction currently in force from a cancelled instruction or a past action quoted for context. Read any conditions attached to the action and check whether a later statement supersedes it. If the effective action or the evidence cannot be resolved from the record, flag it for joint reading rather than supplying a label from the structured ledger. Preserve the candidate phrases and the reason for uncertainty so the adjudication can be checked.
