## Collection and unit

Joshua and I checked the intake inventory on May 2 against the April 30 collection. The short intake texts were created within Mercer for the research. One document is one received intake message body. A document count is not a count of cases, labels or scored predictions; more than one message may concern the same case.

| Entry channel | Received documents as of April 30 |
|---|---:|
| Web form | 2,160 |
| Shared inbox | 1,440 |
| Service desk relay | 1,200 |
| Total | 4,800 |

These are inventory counts. They do not say how many labels are established or how many documents will be in an evaluation partition.

## What the reader sees

The unit to read is the sender's received body, including its request and any surrounding sentences that belong to that body. A subject line, destination, routing tag or subsequent handling outcome is not part of that body. Nor is a comment added by a staff member when forwarding the message. For example, in a received body “Could you move the visit to Thursday? Thank you,” both sentences belong to the body; a later “Please handle this today” added by a forwarding staff member does not.

## Inventory and handoff

| Field | Purpose | For the reader |
|---|---|---|
| Reconciliation ID | Match a reading to its inventory record | Provide |
| Received body | Evidence of the sender's request | Provide |
| Channel and receipt information | Check the collection and counts | Retain in inventory |
| Subject and destination | Describe intake and routing | Withhold |
| Routing tag | Record the handling route | Withhold |
| Handling outcome | Record what happened after receipt | Withhold |
| Forwarding staff comment | Record later handling context | Exclude from received body |

The precise handoff should preserve the body boundaries so that a later comment cannot be mistaken for a sender's request. Readers should base a proposed intent on the received words; where the words do not settle the action, record the uncertainty for review instead of using the destination to decide it.
