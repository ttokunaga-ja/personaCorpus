Hi Vanessa,

The 500-word limit applies only to the Abstract field. The title, author names and affiliations go in their separate fields and do not count against it. Any examples quoted in the abstract do count.

There is no place for figures, tables or footnotes in that field. You can put the definition of Hold and a short pair of examples directly in the text. You can also describe the unfinished comparison, provided you make clear that it is planned and has not been scored.
