# Inspection classification: proposal for July 28

## Decision requested Monday

Michael, please decide whether we should begin research on the candidate collection of 2,400 long inspection records with eight disposition classes. This is a research start, not a request to deploy a classifier. Keep the short intake study and its denominator separate.

## Question and motivation

A record can open with a status recorded at the start of a round and end with a different disposition entered before signature. Does truncating the record remove the language that changes the correct classification? We should read examples in full and compare the evidence available at different cut positions before interpreting model results. An opening status alone is not necessarily the final action.

The candidate unit is a complete inspection record, including observations, additions, disposition and signature. The reference should describe the action effective at signature, rather than an earlier instruction that was cancelled or quoted later. We need a reading rule before treating any structured status as a reference.
