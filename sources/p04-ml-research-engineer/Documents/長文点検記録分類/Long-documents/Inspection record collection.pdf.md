# Inspection record collection

Document ID: MDR-LDI-20250731-DD-01-R01  
Issued: July 31, 2025  
Author: Joshua Ramirez  
For: Vanessa Nguyen and the evaluation team

## Unit and collection

The collection contains 2,400 inspection records for the eight-class study approved July 28. One record is one complete inspection form, not one observation, one piece of equipment, or one series. Original inspections, subsequent reinspections and corrections can each be separate records even when they concern the same equipment. These are long-document counts and are separate from the short intake study.

## Order within a record

The form begins with equipment identification and the status entered at the start of the round. Observations made during the round follow. Additions can follow those observations; the final disposition is entered before the signature. The signature closes the record. A later effective disposition may change an earlier finding, so the beginning alone does not establish the action at signature. Equally, the signature may be routine text rather than a new instruction.

## Record series and partitioning

The ledger links an original inspection, a reinspection and a correction when they belong to the same equipment record series. Keep that series together when assigning partitions. Otherwise a related reinspection or correction could appear in another partition and make the assessment less independent. Equipment identification in the text does not replace the ledger's reviewed series linkage: the latter records which forms actually belong together.

## Fields and use

| Field | Where it belongs | Model input |
|---|---|---|
| Equipment identification in the form | Inspection body | Included as recorded in the form |
| Opening status, observations and additions | Inspection body | Included |
| Final disposition and signature | Inspection body | Included |
| Series key | Partition ledger | Excluded; use only to keep linked records together |
| Structured reference class | Reference ledger | Excluded; used for scoring and reading checks |

The input is the recorded inspection body, not a concatenation of the body with the ledger. Fatima's reading guidance will specify how to decide which disposition remains effective. The ledger fields must remain available for partition and reference checks without being added to the text shown to the model.
