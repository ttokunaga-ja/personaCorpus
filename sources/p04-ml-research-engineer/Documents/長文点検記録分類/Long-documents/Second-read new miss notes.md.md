# Read the six that went the other way

August 7 — with Fatima

We went back to the signed originals for the six action-required records that the opening-only route caught but the long-record second read missed. This is a subset of the paired comparison, not six extra records. Four lost a middle condition or effective instruction from the second window. In the other two, the relevant evidence was still in the window but the prediction favored an older disposition.

One middle-section case has a stop condition attached to the current instruction. The opening and final spans do not carry that condition across the omitted middle; treating the ends as a complete account would erase why the equipment must stop. In a contrasting case, the end quotes an earlier disposition; the current instruction is also available, yet the second prediction follows the quoted older action. The problem there is not simply absence of the evidence.

The net reduction does not mean that a length-only branch improves every record. Keep the newly missed six beside the records recovered by the route, and keep window coverage separate from the model's choice when evidence remains visible.
