# If the second answer comes back late

Document ID: MDR-LDI-20260820-RM-01-R01  
Issued: August 20, 2026  
Author: Vanessa Nguyen  
To: Brandon Romero  
Status: Draft for discussion

## Decision still on hold

Brandon, the August 11 use decision leaves the current human confirmation route in place. The second-read condition improved the research comparison, but its estimated USD 4,800 at 100,000 original records exceeded the USD 4,500 monthly ceiling. We also have no peak response measure or agreed recovery responsibility. I want to separate a late answer from a wrong answer before we discuss any change to the route.

## Candidate to examine — unresolved

If the second inference is not ready in time, return the first prediction for the moment. This is a proposal for discussion only: neither the deadline nor the response behavior has been established or implemented. We would need a visible provisional status and a way to associate the eventual second result with the original record before assessing whether this is safe to consider.

For example, a first prediction of “no action” might be presented while the second read is pending. A reader could take that answer alone as the final disposition and close the record; if the later answer calls for repair, it may not be seen in time. The label and its delivery therefore matter as much as model latency. We have not established who would notice, reopen or correct such a record.

## Times to capture

For each original record, retain the following events on a common clock. An absent event remains pending, not zero. The deadline and acceptable elapsed times still need agreement.

| Event | Time to record | Question for measurement |
|---|---|---|
| Receipt | Time the original record is received | When does the clock start? |
| First inference complete | Time the first prediction is ready | How long from receipt to first result? |
| Second inference complete | Time the reread result is ready, if selected | How long after receipt and first result? |
| Human review | Time a person checks the record and result | What delay occurs before review? |

Please look at whether these points can be observed at the application boundary and what a missing second result would look like to the person checking. We also need the arrival distribution by time of day and a separate estimate of the work to correct a misrouting. A faster average alone would not settle the peak or recovery questions.
