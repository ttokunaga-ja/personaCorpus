# Inspection use decision — August 11

Document ID: MDR-LDI-20260812-RM-01-R01  
Issued: August 12, 2026  
Author: Vanessa Nguyen  
To: Michael Serrano

## Decision

Following the August 11 review with Brandon, hold the proposed second-read route for application use and continue the current human checking. Retain the comparison as research evidence. A lower observed miss rate on the evaluation does not by itself establish that the route is acceptable in operation.

## Monthly cost under a common boundary

The normal month is 100,000 original inspection records. The proposal assumes a second inference for 25% of originals, with both passes included in host and accelerator use. The same input preparation, equipment, concurrency and measured boundary are used for comparison. A USD 600 standby monitoring charge is added once per month.

| Cost, USD/month | First-only | Second-read proposal | Difference |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Input preparation | 400 | 400 | 0 |
| Host | 600 | 800 | 200 |
| Accelerator | 2,000 | 3,000 | 1,000 |
| Standby monitoring | 600 | 600 | 0 |
| Total | 3,600 | 4,800 | 1,200 |
| Application ceiling | 4,500 | 4,500 | — |

The proposed total is USD 300 over the ceiling. The main increment is accelerator use, USD 1,000; the host adds USD 200. Substituting only the accelerator amount into the first-only total gives USD 4,600, already USD 100 over. These are monthly estimates from three timed runs per condition on the same 10,000 original records, scaled to the stated volume, not a peak-time measurement. Unmeasured reruns and human recovery are not spare capacity under the ceiling.

## Research result and its limit

In the independent 4,000-record evaluation, the reference contained 1,000 action-required records. Misses were 32/1,000 (3.2%) for first-only and 24/1,000 (2.4%) for the proposal. Fourteen first-only misses were rescued, but six records became new misses; 18 were missed under both conditions. Among the other 3,000 records, false alarms increased from 90 to 103. The evaluation is a paired comparison, not evidence of improvement for every record or all eight categories.

## Conditions for reconsideration

Before reconsidering application use, obtain a new measurement showing a feasible cost within the ceiling at the applicable workload and establish response behavior under peak arrivals. Identify who receives and recovers misrouted records, and measure the associated work. Brandon will develop the arrival and peak-cost questions; I will work through the review and recovery path and conditions for additional evaluation by August 25. The present evidence does not set a tolerance for that work or demonstrate that these conditions have been met.
