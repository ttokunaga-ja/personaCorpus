# Could the day shift use it first?

August 17 — before talking with Brandon

At 80,000 original records the second-read estimate is $3,960 for the month, including the $600 standby charge once. That fits under $4,500 on the stated assumptions, but the normal month is 100,000. We have no destination for the remaining 20,000 records if this is treated as a cap on the route.

I do not yet have a measurement of the work imposed by limiting the route to daytime, or of how arrivals concentrate by day of the week. The monthly average cannot answer either question. Keep 80,000 as an example of a volume within the ceiling, not as a proposed adoption scope. Ask Brandon about the distribution before choosing any smaller scope; for now leave the route on hold.
