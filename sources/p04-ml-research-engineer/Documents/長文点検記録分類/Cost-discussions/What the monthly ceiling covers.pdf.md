# What the monthly ceiling covers

Document ID: MDR-LDI-20260810-RM-01-R01  
Issued: August 10, 2026  
Author: Brandon Romero  
To: Vanessa Nguyen and Michael Serrano

## Boundary for the comparison

The application-side ceiling is USD 4,500 per month. It covers input preparation, host and accelerator use, and USD 600 per month for standby monitoring. Use 100,000 original inspection records as the normal monthly volume. A second inference consumes resources but does not create another received record. The ceiling is for application processing costs; it is not the amount of a research funding request.

Do not treat unmeasured reruns, human recovery work, or money from another source as available headroom under this ceiling. We have not measured those items sufficiently to include them in an operating estimate.

## Correct the volume comparison

This morning I found that the August 7 worksheet carries 80,000 records from another use in the opening-only column and a provisional daytime allocation of 60,000 in the second-read column. The resulting monthly totals do not compare the two conditions at the same workload. Please replace both volume entries with the same original-record count before using the totals to assess the proposal against the ceiling. Include both inference passes in the second-read resource use and add standby monitoring once per month.

Separately, we need to establish how arrivals concentrate at peak times and how much checking work the route would require. A monthly average alone cannot answer either question.
