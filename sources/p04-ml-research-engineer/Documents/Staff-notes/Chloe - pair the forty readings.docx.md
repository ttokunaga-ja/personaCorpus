# Pair the forty readings

Document ID: MDR-LDI-20250811-RM-01-R01  
Issued: August 11, 2025  
Author: Vanessa Nguyen  
For: Chloe Pearson  
Discussed with: Michael Serrano

## What we reviewed

Chloe, Michael and I reviewed your explanations for 40 known misclassifications selected from the long-document development partition. Your explanation of the evidence matched the reference reading in 30 of 40 cases, or 75%. This is agreement on the explanation for this selected set; it is not a model accuracy estimate or a measure over all inspection records.

In one kind of mismatch, a past repair was read as the current instruction. Please trace the sequence from the observation through the final disposition and signature, and distinguish a completed action or quoted earlier instruction from an action still directed at signature. The August 5 signed-disposition guide gives the paired examples to use as a starting point.

## Next month

Michael and I agreed to move the start of independent adjudication back by one month. We will continue reading together, starting with the cases where an instruction and a past action were confused. For each case, write the proposed class and the exact effective-disposition phrase before we compare it with the reference. Keep the competing phrase and why it does or does not govern; a matching label alone is not enough to check the reading.

I will review the next paired readings with you and discuss readiness with Michael before assigning independent decisions. The 30 matches are useful evidence of progress, but do not settle the remaining ten explanations.
