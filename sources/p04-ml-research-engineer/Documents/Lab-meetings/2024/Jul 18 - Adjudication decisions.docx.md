## Decisions

A first-candidate match is not sufficient on its own. Both readers’ marked phrases must support the same single requested intent. “Move the visit to Thursday” specifies a change; “Thursday visit?” does not. “Cancel the Friday visit” specifies cancellation; “Can you do something about the Friday visit?” does not. “Could you send the invoice again?” specifies a request for a document; “Could you take care of the invoice again?” leaves the action open. Where the body does not establish one intent, use Hold as the disposition, leave the gold-label field blank, and retain both first candidates as reading history.

| First comparison | Candidate with textual support | Hold | Documents |

| Same first candidate | 4,456 | 32 | 4,488 |
| Different first candidates | 218 | 94 | 312 |
| Total | 4,674 | 126 | 4,800 |

The 94 held from disagreements and 32 held from agreements lack sufficient textual evidence. The 4,674 are candidates for a later evaluation partition, not the partition itself. The 126 held documents are outside the scoring population and remain visible in the accounting.
