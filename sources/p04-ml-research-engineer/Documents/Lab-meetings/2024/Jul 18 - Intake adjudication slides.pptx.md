# Slide 3 — Two Columns

## Schedule and cancellation: action words matter

**Enough to consider a label**
- “Move the visit to Thursday” — Reschedule; “Move” and “visit” identify action and object.
- “Cancel the Friday visit” — Cancel request; “Cancel” identifies the requested action.

**Question for disposition**
- “Thursday visit?” — Schedule check or Reschedule? The body names a visit and day, but no action.
- “Can you do something about the Friday visit?” — Cancel request or Reschedule? “something” does not identify the action.

Speaker notes: Compare “Move” with the bare question, and “Cancel” with “something.” Ask whether either right-hand example specifies an action, rather than supplying one from context.
