## Slide 3 — Two Columns

Title: The queue drops on completion

Left column, Three clock events:

- Arrival: item enters intake.
- Start: reviewer takes the item.
- Completion: review finishes three minutes later.

Right column, What we count:

- Wait to start = start less arrival.
- Outstanding = review arrivals less review completions.
- An item in review remains outstanding.

Speaker notes: At restart one item can move into review without a single completion. Keep elapsed time to completion separate from wait to start. The largest values in the lunch run were 58 and 55 minutes respectively.
