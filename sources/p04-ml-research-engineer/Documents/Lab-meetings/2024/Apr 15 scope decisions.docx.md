## Decisions

We will study one classification task covering twelve intake intents: New work request, Quote request, Schedule check, Reschedule, Cancel request, Job status, Send document, Request document, Correct record, Access issue, Report defect and Invoice question. A request to move a visit and a question about its time belong in different categories. The categories are to be assessed together, not as twelve separate reports.

The first-year research target is macro-F1 of 0.82. It is a target, not a result. A per-class average makes the less frequent intents visible; Vanessa will specify the evaluation population and comparison in the plan.

Whether a classifier should be used is a separate decision. A score at the target does not establish that the handling process can accommodate its errors or costs.
