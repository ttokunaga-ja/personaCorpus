# Intake queue review

Document ID: MDR-CA-20241218-LR-01-R01  
Issued: December 18, 2024  
Author: Vanessa Nguyen

Meeting time: December 18, 2024, 14:00 PST  
Present: Michael Serrano, Brandon Romero, Emily Chen, Vanessa Nguyen  
Material reviewed: Brandon Romero, *December review workload*, MDR-CA-20241216-LM-01-R01

## What we checked

Brandon walked us through the same 600 intake positions under continuous review and under a 12:00–13:00 review pause. Both runs had one reviewer, three minutes per item and the same 90 items marked for review. Emily’s correction keeps the arrivals during lunch in the queue. The daily work minutes are therefore the same in both runs, even though the afternoon starts with different balances.

We checked the December 5 restart against Emily’s reconciliation (MDR-CA-20241213-RM-01-R01). Seven items arrived and finished before noon. Another 18 arrived while review was paused. At 13:00 the balance was 25 arrivals less seven completions, or 18 outstanding. QD-287 started at 13:00, leaving 17 waiting to start and one in review. The outstanding count first dropped at 13:03, when QD-287 completed. The last lunch arrival, QD-327, completed at 13:54.

## Decision

We withdrew the proposal to set staffing from the daily average of 30 reviews and 90 minutes. Both runs cleared by 17:00, and the pause still left 18 reviews open at restart on December 5. We agreed to keep the current intake process and reviewer assignments. A staffing proposal would need an agreed wait limit and a workable account of who covers the queue.
