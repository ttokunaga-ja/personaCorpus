## Scope and decision

Michael approved starting the long inspection-record study with 2,400 records and eight disposition classes. We will keep this population and its results separate from the short intake task; neither its document count nor its scores will be added to this study. Approval is for research, not a decision to use a model in an application.

We discussed a record whose opening status allows continued use but whose later disposition calls for reinspection. The question is whether a cut removes words that determine the action effective at signature. The opening status should not automatically stand in for the reference answer.
