## Research context

On the independent August evaluation, action-required misses were 32/1,000 with the first-only condition and 24/1,000 under the existing second-read rule. Fourteen misses were rescued and six new ones appeared; among 3,000 non-action records, false alarms rose from 90 to 103. The six new misses make a paired comparison essential when the selection is narrowed.
