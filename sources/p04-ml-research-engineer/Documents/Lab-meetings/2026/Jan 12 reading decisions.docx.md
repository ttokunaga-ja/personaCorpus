# January 12 reading decisions

For: Fatima Rahman

## Meeting

January 12, 2026, 2:00 p.m. PST, Sacramento. Present: Vanessa Nguyen and Nicole Foster. Vanessa recorded the decisions for Fatima.

## What we read

We read 160 selected misclassified bodies from the saved development-time predictions on the April 2025 development partition of 935. For each body we marked the phrase and our initial scope flags before revealing the reference and prediction and discussing the reading. The selection was intended to span reference/prediction combinations; it is not a sample from the fixed evaluation set.

The cancellation pair made the problem clear: “don't cancel” rejects cancellation, while “move it to Tuesday” states the request. “Not Friday” can reject a date without rejecting a move. The word adjacent to a negative is not by itself the action the sender wants.

## Decisions

We agreed that 41 of the 160 involve the scope of negation. Assign exactly one scope category to each: rejection of an action, 18; negation of an attribute, 14; an explicit alternative request, 9. Where an alternative action is explicitly requested, use that category rather than also counting the rejected action. The first flags agreed for 29 and differed for 12 of these 41.

The other 119 do not receive a scope category. A date reference, an omitted request, or another turn of phrase must be read against its own body. Their first flags agreed for 102 and differed for 17. Those agreements are between readers, not classifier accuracy. We cannot extend my single explanation to all 160 on the basis of the cancellation examples.

## Follow-up

Vanessa will rewrite the discussion, separating the scope readings from the remaining text-specific evidence and from any claim about the classifier's internal cause. Nicole will check that the paired examples reflect the agreed readings. The reference labels, fixed evaluation scoring and features remain as they were; the reading exercise does not revise them.
