# June 11 — leave the routing decision open

Document ID: MDR-CA-20260611-LR-01-R01  
Issued: June 11, 2026  
Author: Vanessa Nguyen

## Meeting

June 11, 2026, 2:00 p.m. PDT. Present: Michael Serrano, Emily Chen, Brandon Romero and Vanessa Nguyen. Circulation: all attendees.

## Evidence reviewed

The same 800 labelled documents and saved predictions were compared with a strict less-than hold rule. At 0.60, 144 were held and 22 of 100 pre-hold errors were caught. At 0.45, 72 were held and 15 of the same 100 errors were caught. Of the 72 leaving hold, 65 predictions were correct and seven were wrong. Automatic-side errors rose from 78 to 85. The 10-document reading helped explain the seven, but does not measure operational recovery costs.

## Decision

Michael deferred adoption for use and decided not to change the routing configuration. The smaller review queue is promising in volume but adds seven wrong automatic routes in this comparison; we do not yet know the effort to detect and recover from those routes. The current research conditions remain in place.
