# Reply selection notes — November 6

Emily and I used the 934-document development partition from the document-level split of the end-of-April intake collection. It's the partition named in *Larger model comparison plan*. The 940-document evaluation partition stayed closed.

We went through development in matching-ID order. For each ID we opened the received body and looked for two things in it: wording written as the current reply and wording carried over from an earlier message. We took the ID once when both were recognizable. A reply could qualify even when we couldn't point to a clean line where the older wording began.

- A reply-looking subject or a routing clue was insufficient. We needed both pieces in the body.
- A body consisting only of quoted material had no current reply to read.
- Forwarding comments added later by the person handling intake didn't count as the sender's reply or as quoted history.

That gave us 120 distinct IDs. Keep the IDs with the two readings so we can get back to the same bodies. The count describes the replies we selected for this reading exercise within development. It doesn't estimate how often replies come through across intake.
