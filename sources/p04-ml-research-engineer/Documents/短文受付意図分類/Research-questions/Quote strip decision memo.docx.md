## Decision

At the November 21 lab meeting, we agreed to set aside uniform removal of the first candidate quote line and everything below it. Keep the full received body as the text we return to when we check an action or its target. Retain the trial excerpts with the paired readings so we can see which evidence the cut removed. Keep the existing reference labels in place.

「What the shorter body loses」節の直後に次の節を追加する。

## Source-date loss by boundary

Fatima and I checked each of the 12 cases against the received body and the excerpt on November 18. We used the original to identify the date linked to the referring expression, then looked for evidence for that date in the excerpt. Each of these 12 excerpts still supported a move and gave the requested new date.

| Quote boundary in received body | Replies read | Replies that lost the source date |
|---|---:|---:|
| Clear | 86 | 9 |
| Unclear | 34 | 3 |
| Total | 120 | 12 |

Even in the clear group, the line that marked where to cut could come just before the evidence for the date. The row-level check is in *Reschedule date check.xlsx*. The full distribution of reading changes is in *Reply reading changes.csv*.

「Working recommendation」節を削除し、「Next comparison」節を次の内容に差し替える。

## Next comparison

Keep the reading ID with the received body and use that ID across comparison conditions. Record whether the boundary in the received body was clear or unclear. In the summary, give each boundary group its own reply count, action-change counts, and count of replies that lost the date of the appointment being moved. Within the action-change counts, distinguish a different action from an action that can no longer be identified.

Record the date of the appointment being moved separately from the requested new date. When the excerpt gives the new date and still asks for a move but gives no evidence for the original date, leave the source date blank on the excerpt row and record why. Emily will prepare the boundary field for the next comparison, and Fatima will check that the source-date-loss field captures those cases.
