# January 13 — drop the single explanation

The January 8 working draft says a mismatch between a salient action word and the requested action may explain all 160 selected development misses, with negation as the mechanism. I am withdrawing that generalization. Nicole and I agreed that 41 readings concern negation scope: 18 action rejections, 14 attribute negations and 9 explicit alternative requests. Even for those 41, that is an account of what readers needed to distinguish, not evidence of what caused the linear model's prediction.

“Has the crew been yet?” is a useful counterexample to the broad account. There is no negation word. Depending on what the body establishes, the question can sit at the boundary between work progress and the timing of a visit. It cannot be explained as the model misplacing a negation merely because it is another miss. Do not use this sentence by itself to establish a reference label where the rest of the body is needed.

For the remaining 119, retain the evidence for each body instead of assigning all of them this cause. The 160 were selected from development predictions, not the fixed evaluation set; do not turn 41/160 into an evaluation-set error rate. Rewrite the discussion for Fatima without changing labels or scores.
