# What counts as evidence

Document ID: MDR-STI-20240617-RM-01-R01  
Issued: June 17, 2024  
Author: Vanessa Nguyen  
For: Fatima Rahman and Emily Chen

## Purpose

For the full reread, keep enough of the received text to show why a candidate is warranted. For each independent reading, record the words indicating the requested action and the object of that action. Read the complete received body; neither the last sentence nor another fixed position is a reliable shortcut. The June 13 desk reading included a cancellation at the beginning followed by a courteous closing, so a last-sentence rule would miss the request.

The reading view contains the ID and received body. A subject, routing tag, processing outcome or forwarding comment is not part of the evidence available to the reader. Do not infer the intended action or object from those fields.

## Applying the rule

| Received body | What the words establish | What remains unsupported |
|---|---|---|
| “The visit is on Thursday.” | A statement about a visit and a day. | It does not itself ask to check or move the appointment. Do not turn a notification into a request. |
| “Please fix this.” | A request to fix something. | The object and nature of the correction are not identified in the body. A subject line cannot supply them. |
| “Send the invoice and move the visit to Thursday.” | Two explicit requests with different actions and objects. | The words give no priority between them; do not silently pick one as the sole intent. |

For an unclear or competing reading, retain the candidate or candidates and the supporting words for discussion. State what the body does not establish rather than filling the gap with a likely context.

## Checking agreement

Compare evidence as well as the selected candidate. Even where two readers select the same candidate, return to the original body and confirm that its words support the action and object. “Checking on the visit.”, for example, does not say whether the writer asks about the appointment time or progress of work. A shared choice alone cannot establish which was meant. Keep the independent readings available when the item is discussed.
