# January 12 reading packet notes

Document ID: MDR-STI-20260107-RM-01-R01  
Issued: January 7, 2026  
Author: Vanessa Nguyen  
For: Nicole Foster and Fatima Rahman

## Purpose and selection

Nicole and I will read together at 2:00 p.m. on January 12; Fatima will have the notes afterward. I selected 160 misclassified documents from the saved development-time predictions of the linear classifier on the April 2025 development partition of 935 documents. The selection was made to look across combinations of reference and predicted intents, not to estimate the prevalence of an error. It is not the fixed evaluation set and should not be used as its denominator.

The reading sheets contain the received body and a reading ID. They do not show the intake channel or the processing outcome. Keep the reference label and saved prediction covered until the first reading is recorded.

## Reading order

1. Each reader reads the entire received body independently, without opening the reference or prediction. Mark the phrase that supports the intended action and write the intent the sender appears to request.
2. Still independently and before seeing either label, record an initial yes/no flag for whether the reading turns on the scope of a negation. Separately name the action or attribute to which the negative applies. If there is no such phrase, record that rather than inventing one.
3. Reveal the development reference and the saved linear-classifier prediction. Compare them with the two first readings and marked phrases; retain the first flags as recorded.
4. Discuss differences against the body. Record an agreed scope flag and, where applicable, a single scope category and a short statement of the textual evidence. Distinguish a rejected action, a rejected attribute such as a day, and an explicitly requested alternative action. When an explicit alternative is the point of the request, record that category once rather than also counting its rejected action.

Please bring examples where the same word sits next to a negative but the sender wants different actions. We should ask what is rejected and what is requested separately: a rejected cancellation need not be a cancellation request, and rejecting Friday need not reject moving the appointment.

## Boundaries of the exercise

The original reference labels and saved predictions remain intact. A phrase that helps us read a body is not, by itself, evidence of the internal reason for the classifier's prediction. Keep individual evidence for readings that do not turn on negation; do not force them into one explanation. We will give Fatima the agreed readings and any unresolved questions, not a new score.
