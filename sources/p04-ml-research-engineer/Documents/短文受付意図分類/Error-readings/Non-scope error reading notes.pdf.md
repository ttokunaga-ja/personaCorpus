# Notes on the other 119

For: Fatima Rahman

## Purpose

Nicole and I set aside 41 of the 160 selected development errors for the scope discussion. The other 119 require individual readings. These examples are short paraphrases for choosing what to read next, not a classification of all 119 into three groups. The selected errors do not represent the fixed evaluation set.

## Places to look again

| Paraphrase | Word that caught my eye | What the body supports and what to check |
|---|---|---|
| “Has the crew been yet?” | “been” | The question asks whether the visit has happened: Job status, rather than a request for the time of a future visit. Check the surrounding body for an actual request to confirm a date before treating it as Schedule check. There is no negation to explain here. |
| “Please send the inspection report.” | “send” | The sender asks the recipient for a document: Request document. A destination is not stated, but that omission does not reverse the direction of the request. Check the whole body for the requested report and who is being asked; do not infer a delivery address. |
| “Please correct the amount recorded for the visit.” | “amount” | A correction to a specified recorded amount is requested: Correct record. If the body instead asks why a charge is a particular amount, the evidence would support Invoice question. Check the verb and object, not “amount” alone. |

## Next reading with Fatima

Find the original bodies behind these paraphrases and mark the clause supporting the action or question. In the first, distinguish a completed visit from a scheduled time; in the second, distinguish a request for a document from supplying one; in the third, distinguish a requested correction from a question about a charge. These are places to examine, not counts or a claim that the same cue caused the model's prediction. The January 13 note withdraws the single explanation; neither the frozen labels nor the scores were changed by that decision.
