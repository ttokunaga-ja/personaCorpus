# Date evidence in replies — November 13

Before I sit down with Fatima, go back through the reschedules that look unchanged when I scan the action column. Read the date of the booking being moved separately from the date being requested.

Q27 is the one to start with. “Could we move that one to Apr 26?” is still there in the excerpt. In the full body, “that one” points to the appointment in the quoted line, “> My appointment is on Apr 23.” The excerpt supports a move to Apr 26, but I can't tell from it which appointment is to be moved. Leave Apr 23 on the original side, where we have the words for it.

We counted 12 replies where the reschedule and the new date survived this way and the original date dropped out. A tally of action changes alone would miss all 12. For the readback, ask Fatima to point to the words that identify the old booking before we put the original and the excerpt side by side.
