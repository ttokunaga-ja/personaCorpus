# Withdrawal of the January intake score

Document ID: MDR-STI-20250128-RM-01-R01  
Issued: January 28, 2025  
Author: Vanessa Nguyen  
To: Participants in the January 16 short intake research meeting

## Correction

Please withdraw the macro-F1 of **0.851** shown on slide 3 of *Short intake intent classification: January readout* (MDR-STI-20250116-LM-01-R01) and stated in the meeting. It was reported for 940 evaluation documents from a document-level allocation. Do not use that value for business decisions or as evidence in model comparisons.

Nicole Foster’s independent review and the subsequent reconciliation found 84 documents belonging to 24 distinct cases across the old partition boundaries. Related documents from a case could appear in different partitions, including training and evaluation. The assumption of case-level independence needed to interpret that result did not hold. These counts are documents and cases respectively, not two counts of the same unit.

## What remains and what follows

The twelve-intent task and the July revised decisions on reference-label evidence remain in place. The 126 documents on Hold remain outside scoring; this correction concerns the allocation and the score, not a replacement label for those documents.

The January deck will remain in the meeting record for audit history, with this notice identifying the withdrawn value. We will document a case-level allocation and its checks before a subsequent evaluation. Any later score will be reported with its own conditions and denominator, rather than substituted into the old slide as though the conditions were unchanged.
