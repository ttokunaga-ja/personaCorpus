# Review minutes by day

One bar for each simulated day, with a horizontal reference line at 90 minutes across the three bars.

- Horizontal axis: “Simulated date (PST)”. Categories: “Dec 4”, “Dec 5”, “Dec 6”.
- Vertical axis: “Required review time (minutes)”, starting at zero.
- Legend: bars “Daily review time”, horizontal reference line “Daily mean (90 min)”.

| Simulated date | All arrivals | Review required | Bar height (min) | Review count label |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Dec 4 | 200 | 28 | 84 | 28 of 200 |
| Dec 5 | 200 | 32 | 96 | 32 of 200 |
| Dec 6 | 200 | 30 | 90 | 30 of 200 |

Print the minute value at each bar and the review count label inside it. The bar values are review counts multiplied by three minutes per item. The reference line is the arithmetic mean of 84, 96 and 90.

Chart note: “Working assumption: one reviewer, 3 minutes per item, 480 minutes available with no break. Daily totals do not show when review work falls within the shift.”
