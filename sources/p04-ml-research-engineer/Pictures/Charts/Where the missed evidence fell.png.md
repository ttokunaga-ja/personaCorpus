# Where the missed evidence fell

Records missed by at least one window (n = 39); fixed inspection holdout.

Horizontal axis: endpoint of the effective evidence phrase as a percentage of the full body, in quartile bands. Vertical axis: records (count). Use stacked bars with a distinct color for each legend category.

| Endpoint band | Missed by first only | Missed by ends only | Missed by both | Total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0–25% | 0 | 0 | 2 | 2 |
| >25–50% | 0 | 0 | 4 | 4 |
| >50–75% | 7 | 2 | 1 | 10 |
| >75–100% | 21 | 0 | 2 | 23 |

Legend: First only; Ends only; Both. Mark the two ends-only records in the third band and the total of 23 in the final band. This is the distribution among the 39 records missed by either window, not among all 480 records.
