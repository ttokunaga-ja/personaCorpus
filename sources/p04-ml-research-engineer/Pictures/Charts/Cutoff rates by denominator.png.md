# Same cutoffs, two denominators

16:9 figure with two side-by-side bar charts. Each horizontal axis is labelled “Cutoff” with categories 0.60 and 0.45. Each vertical axis is “Share (%)”, from 0 to 25%. The bars represent a single series in each panel and carry the fraction and percentage as labels.

| Panel | Cutoff | Numerator | Denominator | Bar label |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Share of all 800 scored documents | 0.60 | 144 | 800 | 144/800 (18%) |
| Share of all 800 scored documents | 0.45 | 72 | 800 | 72/800 (9%) |
| Share of 100 baseline errors | 0.60 | 22 | 100 | 22/100 (22%) |
| Share of 100 baseline errors | 0.45 | 15 | 100 | 15/100 (15%) |

Footnote: Baseline errors are counted before either hold rule is applied, using the same saved predictions and reference labels.
