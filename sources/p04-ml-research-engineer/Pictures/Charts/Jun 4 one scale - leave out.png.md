# Cutoff comparison: counts on one scale

Working plot — do not circulate

Grouped bar chart. Horizontal axis: Confidence cutoff, categories 0.60 and 0.45. Vertical axis: Documents (count), from 0 to 160. Two bars per cutoff:

| Cutoff | Held documents | Baseline errors caught on hold |
|---|---:|---:|
| 0.60 | 144 | 22 |
| 0.45 | 72 | 15 |

Legend: “Held documents (of 800 scored documents)” and “Baseline errors caught (of 100 errors before hold)”. Label each bar with its count.

Note beneath chart: Held counts are out of 800 documents; caught-error counts are out of the 100 errors before either hold rule. The shared count axis can invite a misleading comparison of shares.
