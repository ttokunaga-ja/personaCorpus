# Monthly cost at the same original-record volume

Horizontal axis: original inspection records per month. Vertical axis: monthly cost (USD). Legend: First-only; Second read for records over 3,200 tokens; Monthly ceiling.

| Original records/month | First-only (USD/month) | Second-read proposal (USD/month) |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 80,000 | 3,000 | 3,960 |
| 100,000 | 3,600 | 4,800 |
| 120,000 | 4,200 | 5,640 |

Draw a horizontal ceiling at USD 4,500. Label its intersection with the second-read line approximately 92,857 original records/month. Mark the normal-month point at 100,000 and the USD 300 excess above the ceiling.

Note on figure: Estimates scale the mean of three timed runs per condition on the same 10,000 original records. The second-read share is held at 25%; USD 600 in standby monitoring is included once per month. Both passes are included in costs, not counted as additional original records.
