# Changes hidden by the net miss count

Chart title: Reverse movement on the same action-required records

Horizontal axis: Number of records. Two opposing directions share the center; values on each side are nonnegative counts, not signed rates. Vertical axis: Reference disposition at signature, in the order below. Legend: “Recovered by second read” (miss on opening only) to the left; “New miss on second read” (miss on second read only) to the right.

| Reference disposition | Recovered by second read | New miss on second read |
|---|---:|---:|
| Repair required | 6 | 2 |
| Repeat inspection | 4 | 1 |
| Remove from service | 2 | 1 |
| Engineering review | 2 | 2 |
| Total | 14 | 6 |

Figure note: Among 1,000 action-required reference records, 20 changed miss status. The 18 missed by both conditions are outside the bars. The net reduction of 8 conceals movement in both directions.
