# Reply reading order

## Writing on the board

Three hand-drawn boxes run left to right, linked by right-pointing arrows. Their text in order is “Current reply”, “Action”, and “Find the referent”. Only the “Find the referent” box has a downward arrow. It points to a fourth box labeled “Quoted history”.

The small black date next to “Current reply” reads “Apr 26”. The small blue date next to “Quoted history” reads “Apr 23”. These six labels are the only readable text.

## View

Vanessa's photograph of Emily Chen's board at the end of the November 21, 2024 research meeting, 2:48 pm PST, in the Sacramento meeting room. The camera faces the board from slightly to the left, with all four boxes and the marker tray in frame. The lettering has the variation of quick dry-erase handwriting. At the lower right, a short dividing line is partly wiped away. A narrow strip of wall is visible around the board.
