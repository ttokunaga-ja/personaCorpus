# Whiteboard, January 12

A photograph taken during the reading session in the Sacramento meeting room shows a whiteboard. The board fills most of the frame, with its tray visible along the bottom and a little of the room wall at the edge. The handwriting is legible and informal.

## Writing on the board

On the left, “Don't cancel” is circled as an action, with “rejected action” underneath. Beside it “Move it to Tuesday” is separately circled, with “request” underneath.

On the right, “Not Friday” has a short arrow to “date”. “Friday” is circled separately from the action “move”, which is also written and circled. The two circles make the day and the action distinct.
