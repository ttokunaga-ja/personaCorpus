If they ask after the talk

“How much better is it than last year?”
“Last year we used a different evaluation set, so I can't give a year-to-year change from those scores. The comparison I can give is 0.852 for R173 and 0.873 for R184 on the same 1,200 July documents.”

“Do the smaller classes get swamped?”
“Each of the 12 intents contributes one F1 value, with equal weight in the average. Access issue has 70 reference documents and New work request has 160. Their F1 values count equally. You still have to look at the individual results to see which intents gained or lost.”

“Could we actually use it for intake?”
“That's a separate decision. Here we scored one label on each document. We didn't measure what happens to a low-confidence item in the queue or how long it takes to get a misrouted item to the right person.”

For my eyes only: April 2025 was 0.832 on a different 935 documents. The other 0.021 in that report was the majority-class baseline on those 935. Keep both April numbers off the mic.
