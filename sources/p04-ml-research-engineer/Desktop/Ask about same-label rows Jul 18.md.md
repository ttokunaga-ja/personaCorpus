# Ask Fatima tomorrow

The agreement mark is a starting point, not evidence. For rows where our first choices match, put both marked phrases next to the original body. If we pointed to different words and those words imply different actions, ask whether the body actually supports one common intent.

“Checking on the visit.” is the reminder: checking a time and checking progress are different readings. Neither follows just from “checking” and “visit.” What action and object can we both point to without the subject or routing tag? If the text does not settle it, bring the row into the disposition discussion instead of letting the agreement mark carry it through.
