# Case allocation and scoring

A single schematic case contains three boxes labelled “Received body 1”, “Received body 2” and “Received body 3”, enclosed together by a larger box labelled “One linked case”. An arrow labelled “Assign together” leads from the case to a box labelled “One partition”. A second arrow, explicitly labelled “If assigned to the common evaluation partition”, leads from that box to a box labelled “Common evaluation partition”. From this evaluation box, three separate arrows lead to “Score body 1”, “Score body 2” and “Score body 3”. No scoring arrows leave the generic partition box directly.

Caption: “A case is allocated as a unit; in the common evaluation partition, each received body is scored separately.”
