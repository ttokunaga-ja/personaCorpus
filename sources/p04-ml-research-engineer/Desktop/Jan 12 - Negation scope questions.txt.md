Monday, January 12 — before 2

“Please don't cancel the visit; move it to Tuesday.”
“Not Friday; could we move the visit to Tuesday?”

Nicole: can we point separately to the word immediately after “no” or “not” and to the action or attribute being rejected? In the first, cancellation is refused and the move is requested. In the second, Friday is refused; is the move still the request? Ask what evidence in each body makes that distinction.
