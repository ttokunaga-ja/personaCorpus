# Say the denominator first

Before Michael and Fatima come in:

**Are 940 and 935 cases?** No. Both are counts of scored documents. Cases are the unit used to keep related documents together in the new split; a case can contain more than one document.

**Did Nicole find 84 cases?** No. She found 84 documents belonging to 24 cases that crossed the old partitions. That is why the old evaluation was not independent.

**So did leakage inflate F1 by 0.019?** We cannot say that. The rounded scores differ by 0.019, but the partition and evaluation documents changed. This is not a same-test estimate of leakage's effect. Start with the withdrawal, then give 0.832 and its fixed 935-document condition.
