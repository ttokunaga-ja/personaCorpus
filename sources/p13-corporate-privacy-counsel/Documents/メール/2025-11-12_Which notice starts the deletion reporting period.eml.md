Dear Catherine,

Thank you for discussing the proposed retention clause with Sugiyama and me today. We cannot accept a uniform 90-day retention period after transfer.

Could you revise section 6 on the understanding that the date Toto gives written notice that its reconciliation is complete is the starting date, and that the following day is day one of the 30 calendar days allowed for the deletion report?

A successful transfer can be confirmed before a discrepancy has been reconciled. We need the period to follow our confirmation of counts and readability and resolution of any differences. The report should also cover the temporary area used for restore verification, because transfer copies may be used there when a discrepancy is checked again.

Please let me know whether the wording can reflect that understanding.
