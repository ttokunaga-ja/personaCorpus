# Document Processing Services Renewal: Clause Review and Proposed Revision

Document No.: TDS-LG-202507-REV-001 / Version: 0.2  
Date: 22 July 2025 / Prepared by: Minako Takada, Toto Document Data Services / To: Andrea Powell, Winslow Records Services / Reviewer: None

## Scope and status

This is Toto's proposal for negotiation of the document processing services renewal. The table covers all 18 clauses in contract order. Clauses 1–17 are to be retained for this proposal; the proposed replacement is confined to clause 18. This proposal is subject to Winslow's response and is not an agreed or signed text.

## Clause-by-clause review

| Clause | Topic | Treatment in this proposal |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Purpose | Retain; the purpose provides the context for the processing instructions. |
| 2 | Data covered | Retain; identify the covered data when considering return and deletion. |
| 3 | Processing instructions | Retain; keep the scope of instructions consistent with the data covered. |
| 4 | Restrictions on use | Retain; use restrictions do not establish deletion of copies. |
| 5 | Access control | Retain; access restrictions are distinct from evidence of deletion. |
| 6 | Confidentiality | Retain; confidentiality does not replace deletion reporting. |
| 7 | Subprocessing | Retain; consider the processing scope when identifying material remaining at the end. |
| 8 | Processing locations | Retain; location information alone does not establish completion of deletion. |
| 9 | Security controls | Retain; controls and evidence of deletion serve different purposes. |
| 10 | Incident notification | Retain; an incident notice is not a termination report. |
| 11 | Inspection | Retain; inspection does not itself certify deletion after termination. |
| 12 | Cooperation | Retain; an inquiry alone does not provide a reporting deadline or certificate. |
| 13 | Ordinary retention period | Retain; distinguish the ordinary retention period from the termination date used for reporting. |
| 14 | Fees | Retain; any cost implications of return or deletion are a separate question. |
| 15 | Contract term | Retain; identify the service termination date for the clause 18 reporting period. |
| 16 | Termination | Retain; use the service termination date regardless of the reason for termination. |
| 17 | Handover on termination | Retain the return and handover arrangements and their records. Receipt of returned material does not establish deletion of copies remaining with Winslow. |
| 18 | Deletion certificate and reporting on termination | Replace the certificate exemption with the proposed requirement below. Report completed and outstanding items separately. |

## Proposed clause 18

Within 30 days after the date on which the services terminate, regardless of the reason for termination, Winslow shall provide Toto Document Data Services with a written report on deletion of the data covered by the services, including any remaining working copies and backups. The report shall include a certificate of deletion identifying the material deleted, the date of deletion and the person responsible.

If any material remains unprocessed within that period, the report shall identify that material, the reason it remains unprocessed and the planned processing. Winslow shall supplement the certificate after the outstanding material has been processed. Reporting outstanding material does not certify its deletion.
