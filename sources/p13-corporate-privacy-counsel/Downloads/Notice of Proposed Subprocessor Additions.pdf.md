# Notice of Proposed Subprocessor Additions

Notice number: WR-202410-SPN-001  
Date: 18 October 2024  
From: Andrea Powell, Winslow Records Services  
To: Toto Document Data Services

## Proposed additions

We are notifying you of three proposed additions to the subprocessors supporting the document processing work. The proposed activities, in the order of this notice, and the status of the supporting materials are as follows.

| Order | Proposed activity | Supporting materials |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Image intake | Materials describing the scope of work and the obligations applicable to the proposed subprocessor are available. |
| 2 | Index verification | Materials describing the scope of work and the obligations applicable to the proposed subprocessor are available. |
| 3 | Image deskewing | Materials showing the scope of access to the data concerned and the application of the subcontracting provisions have not yet been provided. |

## Review and approval

All three additions are at the notification stage. Your prior approval is required separately for each proposed addition; this notice is not a request to treat notification as approval. Please review the available scope and obligations materials for the first two proposals. We will coordinate the additional materials needed for the third proposal.
