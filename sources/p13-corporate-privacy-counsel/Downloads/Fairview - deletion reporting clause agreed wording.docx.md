# Deletion Reporting Clause — Agreed Wording

Document: FDT-202511-CL-001  
Version: 1.0

For: Minako Takada and Koichi Murata, Toto Document Data Services

## 6. Deletion of transfer copies and reporting

Following Toto's reconciliation of counts and readability and resolution of any discrepancies, Toto shall give Fairview written notice that its reconciliation is complete. The date of that notice is the starting date for the reporting period; the following day is day one.

Fairview shall delete the copies used for the transfer and provide Toto with a deletion report within thirty (30) calendar days counted from day one. The scope includes copies in working areas, temporary outputs and the temporary area used for restore verification. For each category and location of transfer copies, the report shall identify the category, location, deletion date, responsible person and reference to the supporting evidence.

Where an item remains outstanding or the evidence is insufficient, Fairview shall provide a supplement within the same original reporting period. Toto shall determine whether the matter is complete after checking the supporting evidence against the reported categories and locations.
