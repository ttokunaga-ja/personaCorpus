# Proposed Post-Transfer Retention Clause

Document: FDT-202511-CL-001  
Version: 0.1

For: Minako Takada, Toto Document Data Services

## 6. Post-transfer retention and deletion

Fairview may retain the copies used for the transfer for ninety (90) days after the transfer in order to respond to queries and carry out any further reconciliation of discrepancies. After that period, Fairview shall erase those copies. Fairview will provide Toto with a report of the erasure after it has been carried out.
