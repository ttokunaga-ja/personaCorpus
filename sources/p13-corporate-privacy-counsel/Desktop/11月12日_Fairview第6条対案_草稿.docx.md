## 目的

Fairviewの第6条案（FDT-202511-CL-001版0.1）への回答を検討する。差分照会に備える必要は理解するが、移管後に一律90日保管する案は採らない。

## 前提

転送が終わっただけでは、件数や読取の照合が済んだとは限らない。東都が確認を終えた後に報告期限を置き、複製の所在と削除の確認を結び付けたい。

## 対案：Section 6

Following Toto's completion of the transfer reconciliation, Fairview shall delete the copies used for the transfer and provide Toto with a deletion report within thirty (30) calendar days after Toto confirms completion of that reconciliation. The report shall identify the categories of copies deleted, their locations, the deletion dates and the responsible persons.

The copies covered by this section include copies in working areas and temporary outputs created for the transfer and reconciliation.
