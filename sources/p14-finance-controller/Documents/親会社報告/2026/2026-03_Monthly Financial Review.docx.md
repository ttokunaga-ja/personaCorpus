# Monthly Financial Review — Japan, March 2026

CWH-MR-202603-JP-01　Version 1.0  
As of March 31, 2026　Prepared April 8, 2026  
From: Mariko Senda, Controller, Cedar Works K.K.  
To: Dana Whitlock, Controller, Cedar Works Holdings

## Close status and results

Kenji Oba, CFO, approved the March close and the full correction of the cash difference on April 7. March revenue was JPY 800,000,000 (USD 5,369,127.52) and profit was JPY 35,900,000 (USD 240,939.60). Profit before the corrections was JPY 38,000,000. The JPY 2,100,000 expense impact comprises bank charges of JPY 1,300,000 and an FX settlement loss of JPY 800,000; the two cut-off corrections did not affect profit.

## March cash reconciliation

The March 31 book balance exceeded the bank balance by JPY 12,400,000. The four corrections were posted on April 6 with a March 31 accounting date. The bridge below runs from books to bank, at the closing reporting rate.

| Item | JPY | USD |
|---|---:|---:|
| Book cash before correction | 498,600,000 | 3,324,000.00 |
| Aoba Stay receipt recorded early | (7,200,000) | (48,000.00) |
| Sunline Logistics payment not recorded in March | (3,100,000) | (20,666.67) |
| Unposted bank charges | (1,300,000) | (8,666.67) |
| Unposted FX settlement loss | (800,000) | (5,333.33) |
| Book cash after correction / bank balance | 486,200,000 | 3,241,333.33 |

Aoba Stay's JPY 7,200,000 was received on April 1, after an early March clearing. We reversed the March entry and recorded the receipt on its actual April 1 accounting date. The JPY 3,100,000 logistics payment was debited by the bank on March 31 but its bank interface was imported on April 1; the March payable and cash were corrected. These are cut-off corrections. The JPY 1,300,000 comprises JPY 1,200,000 for March guarantee services and JPY 100,000 in transfer fees. The JPY 800,000 is a settlement loss deducted from the yen account. All four items have been processed; the corrected book balance equals the bank certificate, with no reconciling item carried forward.

At March 31, receivables were JPY 99,600,000 (USD 664,000.00) and payables were JPY 65,400,000 (USD 436,000.00).

## Reporting rates

The March 25 Monthly reporting rates notice from Holdings Finance specifies 150 JPY/USD for closing balances and 149 JPY/USD for March income and expenses. At the average rate the JPY 2,100,000 expense impact is USD 14,093.96. The bridge difference is USD 82,666.67 at the closing rate; it is not the profit impact. Reporting translation does not create an additional journal entry and is distinct from the JPY 800,000 settlement loss.

## Controls and follow-up

The CFO decided that Shinkawa must not clear receipts before matching the bank statement. Horiguchi will update the procedure by April 10 to distinguish the bank debit date, interface import date and accounting date, including month-end debits imported in the following month. We will confirm completion separately. The March estimated accruals have been checked against the ledger; the related invoices remain to be collected and actual amounts confirmed.
