## Close and operating results

Kenji Oba, CFO, approved the February close and the JPY 600,000 reduction in the electricity accrual estimate on March 6. Revenue was JPY 760,000,000 and profit was JPY 31,000,000. Profit before the estimate correction was JPY 30,400,000.

| February income and expense | JPY | USD at 150 JPY/USD |
|---|---:|---:|
| Revenue | 760,000,000 | 5,066,666.67 |
| Profit before electricity correction | 30,400,000 | 202,666.67 |
| Reduction in electricity expense | 600,000 | 4,000.00 |
| Profit after correction | 31,000,000 | 206,666.67 |

The original JPY 2,000,000 factory estimate carried forward a rounded January actual. The factory reported February use of 140,000 kWh, reflecting the February 16–20 paint line maintenance shutdown. At the estimated JPY 10 per kWh, the revised accrual is JPY 1,400,000. The JPY 600,000 reduction is an expense estimate adjustment, not a cash receipt or an exchange gain. The February invoice has not yet been collected, so actual charges remain unconfirmed.
