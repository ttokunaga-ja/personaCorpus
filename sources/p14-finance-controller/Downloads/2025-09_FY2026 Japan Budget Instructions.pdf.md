# FY2026 Japan Budget Instructions
Reference: CWH-BUD-FY2026-JP-01  
Issued: September 3, 2025  
Prepared by: Dana Whitlock, Cedar Works Holdings, Group Finance  
To: Kenji Oba and Mariko Senda, Cedar Works Japan

## Submission
Please prepare the Japan subsidiary's FY2026 budget for January through December 2026. Submit the amount proposed for the December 18 board meeting to Group Finance by December 12, 2025 at 17:00 JST. Include the assumptions and material changes from the initial departmental submissions so that we can follow the proposed amounts.

## Currency and material assumptions
Present the income statement in JPY and the corresponding USD translation using the fixed budget rate of JPY 148 per USD. Identify the exchange rate as a budget translation rate; do not substitute a monthly reporting rate. Apply a year-on-year increase of 6% to prices of timber subject to price changes. Identify fixed-price purchases and explain any contract-specific price changes separately, including the relevant quantities and supporting basis.

## Operating results and capital expenditure
Show sales, cost of sales, operating expenses and operating profit in JPY and USD. Support sales with expected acceptance timing and explain material changes in purchase prices. Present capital expenditure separately from the income statement. For each material project provide the planned timing of expenditure and conditions that must be met before commitment. Distinguish requests from the amount proposed for approval.

Please contact me if the submission cannot be completed by the deadline or if the assumptions need clarification.

Dana Whitlock  
Group Finance, Cedar Works Holdings
