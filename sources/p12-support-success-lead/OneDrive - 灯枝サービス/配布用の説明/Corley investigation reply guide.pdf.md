# What Corley can say while we investigate

Document: TS-CS-202601-ANS-002  
Version: 1.0  
Issued: 8 January 2026  
Prepared by: Yumika Ueda  
To: Jacqueline Howard and the Corley English duty desk

Use the replacement replies for the seven identified entries when the stated condition fits the case. Adapt the confirmed facts to the actual record; the examples below are not a promise that every case has the same result or update time.

## What we have confirmed

Start with what the support record or the development response actually establishes. Describe the observation and its limits; reproducing a symptom does not identify the cause. Seeing one request again on our list does not establish that every request is visible or that the customer's screen has recovered.

| | Confirmed observation | What remains open |
|---|---|---|
| A | Support reproduced the issue where a saved request does not appear in the list. | The cause is still under investigation. |
| B | Support reopened the list and saw one request. | Visibility on the customer's screen and the status of other requests have not been confirmed. |

## What we will do next

| | Customer-facing next step |
|---|---|
| A | “We will contact you by 15:00 JST on 9 January to share what we have confirmed and what is still open.” The English queue's sender must have accepted this update before it is written. This is an update time, not a restoration deadline. |
| B | Ask the customer whether that same request appears when they reopen their list. Do not say that all requests are back or that the customer's display is restored. |

For an English-queue case assigned to Corley, identify the person who will send the update and obtain that person's acceptance of the date and time, including across a shift change. Write the date, time and JST in a separate paragraph after the confirmed facts. Do not copy example A's time into another case without acceptance.

At the accepted time, contact the customer even if development has not yet answered. State the facts confirmed so far, identify what remains unconfirmed, and say what will be checked next. Do not imply that a fix or a cause has been established.

If the customer communication belongs to a domestic sender, return the case and update responsibility to that sender through the domestic duty lead. Corley follows the domestic contact for progress; it does not invent a time on the sender's behalf. If the sender or time is not yet accepted, resolve that assignment before promising a time to the customer.
