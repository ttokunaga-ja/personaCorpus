For a case assigned to the Corley English queue, please first state the facts confirmed in the record and their limits. Put the next customer contact in a separate paragraph. Before giving a time, identify the person who will send it and confirm that they have accepted the date and time, including across a shift change. Write JST with that time. The time is a promise to update, not a deadline for resolving the issue. Even if development has not answered by then, the sender should share what is confirmed and what remains open.

For a case where a domestic colleague is responsible for contacting the customer, please return the update responsibility to that sender through the domestic duty lead rather than choosing a time for them. The attached PDF explains the distinction with the two examples we read today; the CSV contains the replacement text.
