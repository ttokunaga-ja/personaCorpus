# Replies while we investigate

For the English duty desk

Choose the saved reply that matches the stage of the request. Rewrite it to fit the customer's situation before sending; check names and details against the request.

## Acknowledgement

Use an acknowledgement when a new request reaches the queue. Restate what the customer asked about and choose the appropriate saved reply. For example, reply 06 says:

> We'll fix this today.

## Progress

When work is under way, choose a progress reply and adapt it to the request. For example, reply 21 says:

> The cause has been identified.

## More information

Choose a request for information when the team needs a detail to continue checking. Make the requested item clear and remove any question that does not apply.

## Next update

When another contact is due, select a next-update reply and adapt it to the case. For example, reply 49 says:

> We'll have this resolved by our next update.
