# Investigation replies for the duty desk

Effective from 10:00 JST, 9 January 2026

Use the seven replacement replies for entries 06, 12, 21, 25, 29, 37 and 49 when their conditions fit. The other 47 replies remain in use. Adapt the text to the record; a saved reply is not evidence of what happened in a particular case.

## Before writing the update

1. Read the case history or the development response and put the confirmed observation first. Include its scope. A reproduced symptom is not an identified cause; one request visible on our list is not proof that all requests are visible to the customer.
2. Check who is responsible for sending the next customer update. For a Corley-owned English reply, obtain that person's acceptance of the date and time, including the handover between shifts. Write the accepted time with JST in a separate paragraph. The time is for the update, not a resolution deadline.
3. If the domestic team owns the customer communication, return the case to the domestic sender via the domestic duty lead. Follow up with the domestic contact; do not choose a sending time for them. If ownership or the time has not been accepted, settle it before promising a time.
4. At the accepted time, send the confirmed facts and what is still open even if development has not yet replied.

## When a reference number arrives

Match the number against the case record first. Reply with what the record actually confirms and any remaining question. Receiving a number is not itself a reason to tell the customer that the case is closed.

The duty-desk link to “Replies while we investigate” has been removed. Use this guidance and the replacement replies instead.
