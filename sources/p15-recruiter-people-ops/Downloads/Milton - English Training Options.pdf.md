# English training options for customer enquiries

Proposal: MLP-202504-PRO-001  |  Version: 1.0  |  9 April 2025  
Prepared by Diane Mitchell, Milton Learning Partners  
Prepared for Mihoko Sato, みらい職場研修株式会社

## Options

Thank you for outlining the first-response work at your customer enquiry desk. The two options below differ in audience and teaching scope as well as duration. Each class has a maximum of 18 participants; the fees are in Japanese yen, excluding tax.

| Option | Participants and classes | Hours per participant | Delivery | Fee excluding tax |
|---|---|---|---|---:|
| Company-wide | 178 employees; 10 classes of up to 18 | 20 | Broad workplace English: receiving enquiries, asking for and confirming details, explaining a next step, and passing information to colleagues. Guided practice includes paired conversations and short written replies across these situations. | ¥4,400,000 |
| Customer enquiry pilot | 18 customer desk staff; 1 class of up to 18 | 8, delivered as four 2-hour sessions | First response to a training enquiry: listen for the enquiry and relevant details, give an expected reply time and hand the enquiry to Sales. Participants practise these exchanges in pairs. | ¥320,000 |

## Fee breakdown

| Component | Company-wide, excluding tax | Customer enquiry pilot, excluding tax |
|---|---:|---:|
| Programme design | ¥240,000 | ¥100,000 |
| Trainer delivery | ¥4,000,000 | ¥160,000 |
| Materials | ¥160,000 | ¥60,000 |
| Total | ¥4,400,000 | ¥320,000 |

The company-wide amount covers delivery of 20 hours to each of ten classes; the pilot amount covers four two-hour sessions for one class. These are separate proposals with different design and delivery scopes.

## Preparation and arrangements

For the company-wide option, participants are asked to review the session material outside class before the next session; this preparation is additional to the 20 taught hours. The time needed will vary by participant and is not included in the taught hours. The pilot does not depend on individual homework or preparation outside class; its exercises are completed during the four sessions.

For the pilot, the practice stops at collecting the enquiry, communicating when a reply can be expected and passing the information to Sales. Decisions on a price or a confirmed schedule remain with Sales. みらい職場研修 will provide the venue and arrange work coverage and schedules for both options. Dates and the detailed timetable are to be agreed before delivery.
