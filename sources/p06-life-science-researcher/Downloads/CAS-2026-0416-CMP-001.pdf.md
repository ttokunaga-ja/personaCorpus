# Revised-method comparison — observations

Caldwell Assay Services | CAS-2026-0416-CMP-001 | Apr 16, 2026

To: Melissa Watkins, Bennett Cell Evaluation Inc

Dear Melissa,

We reviewed the 30 new pairs matched by shared label on Apr 14. Each pair contains one Caldwell and one Bennett reading. The mean signed difference, calculated as Caldwell minus Bennett, was +0.30 ng/mL across all 30 pairs.

Using the Bennett reading to define the bands, the eight pairs below 3.00 ng/mL had a mean signed difference of +0.75 ng/mL. The corresponding means were +0.20 ng/mL for 12 pairs from 3.00 to less than 5.00 ng/mL and +0.06 ng/mL for 10 pairs at or above 5.00 ng/mL. The overall mean is calculated across the individual pairs.

I suggest additional confirmation in the low concentration range. The present comparison is descriptive and does not establish equivalence across the range, including the higher bands. We should review the band boundaries and candidate records before setting the number or timing of any further comparison.

Angela Price  
Caldwell Assay Services
