# Budget categories for the revised request

Guidance ref. WSF-2025-1106-BUD-001  
Issued Nov 6, 2025  
Grants officer Monica Robinson  
To Melissa Watkins, Bennett Cell Evaluation Inc

## Cost categories

- **Outside measurement services.** List the amount payable to the outside facility for the measurement work it will deliver. Identify the facility and show the service units and rate in the workbook.
- **Bennett staff.** Show Bennett's own staff hours and rate here, including time spent arranging and coordinating the outside work.
- **Materials.** Show the quantities or allowances and their basis separately from labor and outside services.
- **Records review.** Show review hours and rate on a separate line. Identify the work covered so the same hours can be distinguished from the other Bennett staff hours.

## Joint measurement

If the joint-measurement line combines a Caldwell service estimate with Bennett's time coordinating that service, separate the two amounts. Carry the Caldwell service blocks under joint measurement and put the Bennett coordination hours under Bennett staff. The workbook should show whose estimate supports each part. The revised justification should explain where the coordination hours are carried so the reader can tie the explanation back to the workbook without counting them twice.

## Revised package

Please send me the revised budget justification as a PDF and the itemized workbook by 4:30 p.m. Philadelphia time on Monday, Nov 10, 2025. Use the same category amounts and request total in both documents.
