# CE-Reader-2 annual service — for discussion
BCE-CE-2026-REV-001-R00 | Jan 14, 2026 | Melissa Watkins | Working draft for Anthony Mendoza

## Question
Robert asked whether we could remove one planned stop from the calendar each year. Before I ask Mason, I would like your view of the evidence and what an annual interval would leave us unable to see. The current routine schedule is April and October. The question is open; the annual option below is not a recommendation to change the schedule.

## Evidence to date
Anthony's range check (BCE-CE-2026-LAB-002-R00) found all four recent as-found and as-left deviations within the inclusive ±0.05 ng/mL band at 5.00 ng/mL. This describes the results at those visits, not what happens throughout a longer interval. Two visits were supplemental; the other two were routine.

The active-use comparison in BCE-CE-2026-TBL-001-R00 is a separate observation. Jul 1–Sep 30 and Oct 1–Dec 31, 2025 each contain 92 calendar days. Active use rose from 240.0 to 292.8 hours, or 52.8 hours and 22.0% relative to Jul–Sep. Setup, idle reservations and Mason service are excluded.

## Options for review
| Option | Planned routine stops per year | What four in-band visits support | What increased use leaves unresolved |
|---|---|---|---|
