If Robert asks: what happens at 36 hours?

We did not observe a 36 h group. There is no study value to report at 36 h and no supported concentration curve to sketch between the observed groups. The 24 h result is descriptive; the 48 h eligible group mean was 18.0% below the 0 h mean.

The question for the room is the proposed operating rule. If Robert adopts the inclusive 24 h ceiling, 36 h is outside routine use and goes to Anthony for review before routine use. It is not a prediction of the 36 h concentration.
