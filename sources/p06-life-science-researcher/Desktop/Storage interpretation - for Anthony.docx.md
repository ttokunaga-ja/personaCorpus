## Initial findings

Anthony, the 24 h group mean is 99.1% of the 0 h mean. I have called it “unchanged” here on the strength of that group mean; please check whether that wording is appropriate. The 48 h group mean is 82.0% of the 0 h mean, which is a drop of 18.0%, not a drop of 82.0%.
