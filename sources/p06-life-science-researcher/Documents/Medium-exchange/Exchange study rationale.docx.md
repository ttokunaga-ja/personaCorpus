# Medium-exchange interval study: rationale

BCE-CE-2024-REV-004-R00 | Aug 21, 2024 | Melissa Watkins | Discussion

To: Robert Nguyen, Patricia Jensen and Lauren Brooks

## Purpose

We propose a nonclinical comparison of medium-exchange intervals using 24, 48 and 72 hours as candidate groups. The comparison should help us describe how the interval used during culture relates to the observations recorded for those groups. It is a planning question; this note does not authorize a run or report results.

## Record linkage before comparison

Patricia's preparation record and Lauren's observation sheet describe different parts of the work. Before interpreting a group comparison we need to be able to trace each observation to the relevant preparation record, including its date and source row. The same interval can appear on more than one preparation date, so the group name alone may not identify a record. Patricia confirms that the printed preparation record can retain the preparation date and ledger row reference. We should test whether that reference remains usable from the observation side.

| Candidate interval | Question for design |
|---|---|
| 24 h | How many samples and how will each observation be linked? |
| 48 h | How many samples and how will repeated preparation dates be distinguished? |
| 72 h | How many samples and how will the same linkage be recorded? |

## Discussion on Aug 23

Agree the number of samples per group and the fields and responsibilities needed on the two records. Review an existing observation example before fixing its layout. Record any unresolved field placement for follow-up rather than assuming the group label supplies the preparation date.

This study concerns the interval between medium exchanges. The length of time supernatant is stored is a separate question and is not the variable being designed here.
