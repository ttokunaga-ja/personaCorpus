# Medium-exchange interval study plan

BCE-CE-2024-PLN-006-R00 | August 30, 2024 | Melissa Watkins | Approved

For: Lauren Brooks and Patricia Jensen

## 1. Purpose

Compare observations across three medium-exchange intervals in nonclinical cell material. Preparation and observation records must be linked before any group comparison is made. This plan establishes the allocation and record method; the study date remains to be set.

## 2. Allocation

| Exchange interval | Sample IDs | Planned samples |
|---|---|---:|
| 24 h | MX24-01–MX24-10 | 10 |
| 48 h | MX48-01–MX48-10 | 10 |
| 72 h | MX72-01–MX72-10 | 10 |
| Total | | 30 |

## 3. Record reconciliation before use

Patricia records preparation information in the preparation register. Lauren records observations on the observation sheet. Each observation entry has separate Sample ID and Prep date fields, and carries the preparation register row reference. Match the ID and date together to Patricia's register and use the row reference to retrieve the source. Check exchange interval and rack section against the matched record; neither alone identifies the preparation.

Enter the planned time before observation. Lauren enters the actual time read from the clock in a separate field. Leave the actual field blank until recorded; a planned time is not an actual time.

If the preparation date is missing or unreadable, return the entry to Patricia to confirm it from the register. Do not infer a date from interval or time. Keep an unmatched or incomplete entry unresolved and do not adopt it for comparison until the source has been checked and the missing information confirmed.

On August 30 the team checked this method with one desk example per group. Patricia showed the preparation rows, Lauren entered actual desk times, and Benjamin retrieved the corresponding rows. The three examples identified P01, P02 and P03 uniquely; the separate 48 h candidate P04 was not one of those examples. The desk times describe the paperwork check and are not study measurements. See BCE-CE-2024-TBL-015-R00 for the reconciliation.
