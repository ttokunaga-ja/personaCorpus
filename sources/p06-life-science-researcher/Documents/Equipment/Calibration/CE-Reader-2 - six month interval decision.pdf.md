# CE-Reader-2 service interval decision
Document: BCE-CE-2026-DEC-001-R00
Decision date: Jan 27, 2026
Prepared by: Melissa Watkins
Approved by: Robert Nguyen
Distribution: Bennett laboratory staff

## Decision
Retain the six-month routine service and calibration cadence for CE-Reader-2, serial LXR-4187. Keep the April 22, 2026 target in the equipment schedule; the appointment window remains subject to Mason's confirmation.

## Basis
Anthony's history and range check, BCE-CE-2026-TBL-002-R00 and LAB-002-R00, show four visits with both as-found and as-left deviations within the inclusive ±0.05 ng/mL band at the 5.00 ng/mL reference. Two were supplemental visits and the observed gaps were shorter than twelve months. These results do not establish performance over a year between checks.

Separately, BCE-CE-2026-TBL-001-R00 compares active use over two equal 92-day windows: 240.0 h on Jul 1–Sep 30, 2025 and 292.8 h on Oct 1–Dec 31, 2025. The increase is 52.8 h, or 22.0% relative to the first window. Setup, idle reservations and service are excluded. Increased use is not itself evidence of drift.

At the Jan 27 discussion Christopher advised that the observed visits provide no twelve-month evidence. Avoiding one planned stop each year does not justify a longer interval between checks when the evidence does not cover that interval and active use has increased. Robert retained six months; see BCE-CE-2026-MIN-001-R00.

## Application
Keep the six-month cadence and April 22 target in the equipment schedule. Christopher will confirm the April booking; Lauren will prepare the bench handoff. After the April visit is complete, Anthony will review the new calibration and current usage evidence. Record the actual release on the day of service.
