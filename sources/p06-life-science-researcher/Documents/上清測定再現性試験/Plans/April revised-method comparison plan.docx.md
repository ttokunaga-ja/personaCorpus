# April revised method — matching and review plan

BCE-CE-2026-PLN-001-R00 | Apr 7, 2026 | Melissa Watkins | Reviewed

## Purpose and scope

Lauren and Angela: use this plan to retain and compare the readings from the revised method on Apr 14. The comparison covers 30 new nonclinical samples, with one reading at each facility for each sample. It is separate from the January comparison of 18 pairs; the earlier 10% criterion does not apply here.

## Design and records

Use MC30-01 through MC30-30 as the shared labels. Each facility retains its own identifier, measurement date and original concentration in ng/mL alongside the shared label. Do not overwrite a native ID or reading when preparing the comparison. Lauren will retain Bennett's original export and bench record; Angela will retain the Caldwell accession and original reading in its export.

At the Apr 14 review, Melissa and Angela will match one Bennett record and one Caldwell record to each shared label. Check that all 30 labels appear once at each site and retain both native IDs in the crosswalk. Resolve any missing, duplicate or conflicting label with the source record before calculating a difference; do not substitute an inferred value.

## Comparison and interpretation

Classify each matched pair by its Bennett reading: below 3.00, 3.00 to less than 5.00, or 5.00 ng/mL and above. For each pair calculate the signed Caldwell reading minus the Bennett reading in ng/mL. Report the number of pairs and the mean signed difference in each band as well as the mean across all matched pairs. Retain the individual values so that the overall mean does not conceal a band pattern.

This is a descriptive comparison. No pass/fail threshold is set. Any further review or additional comparison will be considered in light of the paired records, not inferred from the overall mean alone.

## Review and responsibilities

Melissa Watkins prepared the plan; Robert Nguyen reviewed it. Lauren Brooks records the Bennett measurements and source location. Angela Price provides the Caldwell records with the common labels and local accessions. Melissa and Angela review the pairings together on Apr 14.
