# A-24 single-run review plan

BCE-CE-2026-PLN-003-R00 | Jul 13, 2026 | Melissa Watkins | Approved

## 1. Purpose and scope

Lauren Brooks and Anthony Mendoza: this plan sets the record and review for the Jul 21 Bennett A-24 run of nonclinical samples. It concerns one run at Bennett. The separate low-range interfacility follow-up is not assessed here.

## 2. Study design and records

Lauren will measure 24 distinct A-24 samples and retain one concentration reading per sample in the original output. Record the measurement date, instrument, run and sample IDs on the work sheet and retain the output with all 24 rows. The signed paper work sheet must allow the output's ID ranges and date to be traced. Record any repeat or exclusion rather than silently removing a row.

## 3. Acceptance and reporting

Calculate the arithmetic mean of the 24 retained readings. Calculate the sample standard deviation of those same readings and divide it by the unrounded mean; express the result as the run CV. The run criterion is CV ≤15.0%. Report the number of readings, mean, CV and criterion, and retain the underlying values. This is a CV across the 24 readings in the run, not a within-sample CV. The within-sample criterion in BCE-CE-2024-PLN-001-R01 retains its original scope.

Anthony will compare the paper work sheet with the original output for date, run, row and ID ranges, verify the number of readings and review the aggregation and calculation. His confirmation should identify the records and the scope of the check.
