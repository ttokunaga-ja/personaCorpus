# Supernatant Measurement Repeatability

BCE-CE-2024-PLN-001-R00 | Apr 9, 2024 | Melissa Watkins | Review draft

## 1. Purpose and scope

Assess the repeatability of measurements of nonclinical culture supernatant across three medium conditions. This study will compare the recorded measurements and their within-sample variability; it is not intended to establish a cause for any difference between conditions.

## 2. Study design and records

Twenty-four samples are planned: eight Reference, eight Buffered, and eight Supplemented. Record each sample's identity and condition at handoff, and retain the measurement output with the study records. The handoff record must allow results to be traced back to the samples provided to the measurement operator.

For review: who owns the handoff record, and how should culture date accompany each result in the reporting record? Please resolve these points before the plan is approved.

## 3. Acceptance and reporting

Evaluate the within-sample coefficient of variation (CV) for each of the 24 samples against a limit of 15%. A sample with CV at or below 15% is within criterion; one above 15% is outside criterion. Report the individual decisions along with a condition summary. All measured rows, including those outside criterion, remain available for later review; a summary does not replace the individual records.

For review: confirm where the individual decisions and culture-date information should appear in the report.
