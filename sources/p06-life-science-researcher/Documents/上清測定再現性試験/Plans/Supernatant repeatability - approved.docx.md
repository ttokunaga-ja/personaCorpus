# Supernatant Measurement Repeatability

BCE-CE-2024-PLN-001-R01 | Apr 17, 2024 | Melissa Watkins | Approved

## 1. Purpose and scope

Assess the repeatability of measurements of nonclinical culture supernatant across three medium conditions. The study compares recorded measurements and within-sample variability; it is not designed to establish the cause of differences between conditions.

## 2. Study design and records

Measure 24 samples: eight Reference, eight Buffered, and eight Supplemented. Record the full sample identity and condition at handoff and retain the measurement output with the study record. Lauren Brooks owns the dated handoff record and will ensure that culture date accompanies each sample's identity so the measurement can be traced to the dated handoff.

## 3. Acceptance and reporting

Evaluate the within-sample coefficient of variation (CV) for each of the 24 samples. A sample passes at CV ≤15%; a sample above 15% is outside criterion. Show all 24 individual results and acceptance decisions in the report, even if it opens with a condition summary. Retain every measured row, including values outside criterion. If review flags appear concentrated, compare results by culture date as well as condition; retain the underlying sample records for the comparison.
