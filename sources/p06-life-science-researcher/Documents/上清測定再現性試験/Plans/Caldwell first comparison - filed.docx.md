# First Bennett–Caldwell comparison

BCE-CE-2024-PLN-003-R01 | Nov 15, 2024 | Melissa Watkins | Filed — design confirmed

For Lauren Brooks and Robert Nguyen

## 1. Purpose and scope

Compare supernatant measurements at Bennett Cell Evaluation Inc and Caldwell Assay Services under the current conditions. Measure the same 18 fresh nonclinical samples independently at the two sites. Use the shared labels MC18-01 through MC18-18 for the handoff and comparison. The 44 October results remain in the separate hold under BCE-CE-2024-HLD-001-R00 and are outside this comparison.

## 2. Study design and records

Lauren Brooks will check the shared labels against the handoff list and retain Bennett's measurement date, native record ID, shared label and original reading in ng/mL in the Bennett export. Angela Price will provide the Caldwell export with the measurement date, native accession, shared label and original reading in ng/mL. Keep each export in its original order.

I will reconcile the exports by shared label. The comparison table will carry one row per shared label, with both native IDs and both original concentrations in ng/mL. Check that each label appears exactly once in each export and each native ID traces to one source record. Refer unclear Bennett records to Lauren and unclear Caldwell records to Angela. Record any confirmation next to the affected pair and retain the original exports unchanged.

## 3. Acceptance and reporting

For each verified pair, take the absolute value of Caldwell minus Bennett, divide it by the Bennett reading and multiply by 100. Use the source readings at their recorded precision. Take the arithmetic mean of the 18 percentages, weighting each pair equally. The comparison meets the criterion when this unrounded mean is at or below 10%. Report the mean and retain each pair's difference for review.

Keep the comparison open if a label or native ID is unresolved, a reading is missing or a Bennett value prevents calculation of the relative difference. File the crosswalk and comparison table with the two exports so that each pair can be checked against the original readings.

## 4. Review and approval

Robert Nguyen, Laboratory Director, confirmed the comparison design and acceptance criterion on Nov 15, 2024. Melissa Watkins filed R01 after Lauren's Nov 14 review. R01 records the source IDs as required fields and the shared label as the reconciliation key. Bring the completed 18-pair comparison to Robert for the continuation decision.
