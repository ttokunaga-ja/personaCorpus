# Supernatant repeatability — repeat proposal
BCE-CE-2024-PLN-002-R00 | Jul 24, 2024 | Melissa Watkins | Proposed

To: Robert Nguyen, Laboratory Director

## Purpose and basis
The July 11 readout retained all 24 results. Four individual CVs exceeded 15%; after reconciling full IDs to the dated handoffs, none of 12 samples from July 8 and four of 12 from July 10 exceeded the limit. Each date had four samples in each condition. The grouping merits another balanced assessment, but the cause is open and the first run does not establish that it will recur.

## Proposed design
Prepare two separately dated sets of 12 samples, with four Reference, four Buffered and four Supplemented samples in each set, for 24 in total. Repeat dates remain open. Lauren's dated handoff should preserve culture date, slot, condition and full run ID in a crosswalk; check uniqueness before joining to the readout. Keep every measured row and its original value.

## Criterion and review
Apply the original criterion to each sample: CV ≤15% is within criterion; CV >15% is an exceedance. Produce separate summaries for each culture date and condition from the outset, including sample counts and exceedance counts, alongside the pooled summary. Compare the two dates to see whether a concentration of flags like July 10 recurs or whether flags are dispersed. Neither result alone would identify a cause.

## Questions and approval
Robert: is the proposed balance and date-level review sufficient to plan a repeat, and may capacity be reserved? Dates, availability and authorization to run are still open. This proposal does not authorize a repeat.

Prepared by Melissa Watkins. Approval: pending Robert Nguyen's decision.
