For Robert Nguyen, Laboratory Director

## Initial findings

I recommend continuing the first Caldwell comparison under the current conditions. The January data cover 18 fresh samples measured at Bennett on Jan 6 and at Caldwell on Jan 7. The mean absolute relative difference for the 18 pairs is 8.2% at reporting precision. The unrounded mean, 8.16701856…%, meets the inclusive 10% limit in BCE-CE-2024-PLN-003-R01. Robert's decision is pending.

I calculated the absolute Caldwell-minus-Bennett difference for each pair as a percentage of that pair's Bennett reading. Each of the 18 percentages contributes equally to the arithmetic mean. Four individual absolute differences exceed 10% (MC18-08, MC18-10, MC18-13 and MC18-18). None equals 10%. All four remain in the calculation and are available for pair-level review.
