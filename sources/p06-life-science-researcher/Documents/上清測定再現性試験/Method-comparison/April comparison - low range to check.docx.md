# April comparison — low range to check

BCE-CE-2026-REV-002-R01 | Apr 17, 2026 | Melissa Watkins | Discussion revision

For Robert Nguyen, Laboratory Director

## Initial finding

On Apr 14, Angela Price and I compared 30 new matched pairs measured by the revised method, with one reading per facility for each sample. The mean signed difference was +0.30 ng/mL, calculated as Caldwell minus Bennett across the 30 individual pairs. This is a descriptive result, not a conclusion that the methods are comparable across the range. The April plan does not set a pass/fail criterion for this comparison.

The pairs were matched on common labels MC30-01 through MC30-30, retaining the Bennett and Caldwell IDs and readings in the comparison workbook. The overall mean does not show how the differences are distributed across concentration bands.

## Pair-level reconciliation

The bands below use the Bennett reading for each pair. The means for each facility refer to the same matched pairs; the final column is the mean of their signed pair differences.

| Bennett concentration, ng/mL | Pairs | Bennett mean, ng/mL | Caldwell mean, ng/mL | Mean signed difference, ng/mL |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Below 3.00 | 8 | 2.25 | 3.00 | +0.75 |
| 3.00 to less than 5.00 | 12 | 4.05 | 4.25 | +0.20 |
| 5.00 and above | 10 | 6.55 | 6.61 | +0.06 |
| All pairs | 30 |  |  | +0.30 |

The eight low-band pairs have the largest of the three signed mean differences. The overall +0.30 ng/mL is calculated from all 30 pair differences, not by taking a simple average of the three band means. The workbook retains the individual values, including negative differences; these averages do not establish equivalence in any band.

## Scope and next step

Anthony Mendoza marked the earlier heading “Comparable across the range” and asked that the overall value be identified as signed and based on 30 pairs, and the low-band value as eight pairs below 3.00 ng/mL. Angela's Apr 16 note likewise recommends additional confirmation in the low range and does not conclude equivalence across the range.

I recommend that we take forward only the Bennett-below-3.00 ng/mL band for additional confirmation. That follow-up has not been performed; the number of candidates and timing remain to be determined with Angela. The middle and high bands are outside the scope of this proposed follow-up. Their exclusion from the follow-up is not a finding of equivalence or a decision that no further question could arise.
