# Storage timing — discussion of missing and over-limit entries

BCE-CE-2025-MIN-005-R00 | Sep 18, 2025 | Melissa Watkins | Record of discussion

## Meeting

Sep 18, 2025, 10:30. Present: Melissa Watkins, Lauren Brooks and Anthony Mendoza.

## Records reviewed

BCE-CE-2025-REV-003-R01, the September wording of the storage findings, proposes a maximum elapsed hold of 24 h for the evaluated nonclinical supernatant workflow. Approval is pending. We discussed how that proposed boundary would be read at the desk, not a new concentration estimate.

## Discussion and agreed approach

Lauren asked that an elapsed time of exactly 24 h remain inside the proposed limit. Anthony asked that a documented time over the limit be recorded distinctly from a blank elapsed-time entry: an unknown time is not evidence that the time was over the limit, or that it was within it. Under the proposal, either exception would be referred to Anthony before routine use.

We will take the boundary example and the two distinct exceptions to Robert for a decision. This is a proposal, not an approved instruction; operations remain unchanged pending his decision.
