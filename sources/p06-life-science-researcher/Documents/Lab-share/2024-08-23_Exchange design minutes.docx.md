# Medium-exchange design meeting

BCE-CE-2024-MIN-006-R00 | August 23, 2024 | Melissa Watkins | Meeting record — final

Meeting: August 23, 2024, 10:30–11:00 EDT  
Present: Melissa Watkins, Patricia Jensen, Lauren Brooks

## Purpose

Agree the scope and sample allocation for the medium-exchange interval comparison and identify the records needed to connect preparation to observation.

## Decisions

Use three exchange-interval groups: 24, 48 and 72 hours, with 10 samples in each group, for 30 planned samples. These are allocation places, not observations already made. Patricia's preparation register and Lauren's observation sheet must be traceable to one another before a group comparison is made.

| Interval | Planned samples |
|---|---:|
| 24 h | 10 |
| 48 h | 10 |
| 72 h | 10 |
| Total | 30 |

## Record discussion

Patricia can retain the preparation date and register row reference on the printed preparation record. She will take responsibility for the register reference. Lauren will take responsibility for the time field on the observation sheet. An interval is not a clock time, and a group label can recur on another preparation date.

The position of the preparation date on the observation sheet remains open, including whether it needs its own field. Patricia and Lauren will read the current group-centred example against the preparation record on August 26 before that choice is made.
