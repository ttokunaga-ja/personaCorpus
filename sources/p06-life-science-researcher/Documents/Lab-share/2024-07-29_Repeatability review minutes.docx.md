## Discussion and decisions

The Apr 17 plan specified eight samples in each of the Reference, Buffered and Supplemented conditions, with each sample within criterion at CV ≤15%. The Jul 11 readout has 24 readings; four individual CVs exceed the limit. The pooled concentration mean is 7.10 ng/mL and the mean of the individual CVs is 12.29%. Neither mean changes the individual decisions.

The dated comparison has four samples in each date and condition cell. No samples exceed the limit in the Jul 8 group (0/12); four exceed it in the Jul 10 group (4/12), two each in Buffered and Supplemented. Anthony's review reconciled 24 of 24 IDs and the four exceedances to the saved readout. The cause of the grouping remains open. Melissa proposed a repeat with two dated sets of 12 and four samples per condition in each set, retaining separate date summaries and the original per-sample limit.

Robert kept the first readout under review and directed that all 24 values remain available, including the four flagged readings. He permitted a provisional reservation of capacity while the proposal is completed. This is permission to hold capacity, not authorization to conduct a repeat run. Robert will consider authorization after the dated proposal, availability and crosswalk review are brought back together.
