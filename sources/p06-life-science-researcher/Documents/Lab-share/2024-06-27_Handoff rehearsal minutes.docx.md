# June handoff check before the July run

BCE-CE-2024-MIN-001-R00 | Jun 27, 2024 | Melissa Watkins | Handoff rehearsal

## Meeting

Participants: Melissa Watkins, Lauren Brooks, Patricia Jensen, and Benjamin Ortiz. We walked through the expected handoff and the information needed to connect each sample with its later measurement.

## Reference records

The approved supernatant repeatability plan calls for eight samples in each of the Reference, Buffered, and Supplemented conditions. The July handoffs are expected to supply 12 samples on Jul 8 and 12 on Jul 10: four per condition on each date.

## Discussion and decisions

Lauren will check all 24 record IDs against the dated racks. Benjamin pointed out that rack positions can be reused. A position by itself therefore cannot distinguish the two racks: the paper handoff sheet must preserve the culture date alongside the position. The date and position are to be checked together when reading either sheet.

Patricia confirmed that the planned distribution gives four positions per condition on each date. Melissa will use the dated handoff records when reviewing the results; the planned counts are not a substitute for the actual handoff entries.
