## Meeting
Jan 27, 2026, 10:00 EST; call. Present: Christopher Medina (Mason Laboratory Maintenance), Anthony Mendoza (quality), Robert Nguyen (laboratory director) and Melissa Watkins (Cell Evaluation Laboratory).

## Records reviewed
- BCE-CE-2026-BRF-001-R00, January reader interval discussion.
- BCE-CE-2026-LAB-002-R00 and TBL-002-R00: four calibration visits, two routine and two supplemental, all as-found and as-left deviations within the inclusive ±0.05 ng/mL band at 5.00 ng/mL.
- BCE-CE-2026-TBL-001-R00: active use was 240.0 h for Jul 1–Sep 30 and 292.8 h for Oct 1–Dec 31, 2025. Each window spans 92 calendar days; the 52.8 h increase is 22.0% of the first period. Setup, idle reservations and service are excluded.

## Discussion and decision
Christopher advised that the four in-range visits show acceptable results at the observed visits, but the supplemental checks shorten the observed gaps. They do not provide evidence for performance across twelve months without calibration. Anthony distinguished that range check from the hours comparison: more active use is not itself a measured drift, but it increases the reason to check rather than extend the unobserved interval.

Robert decided to retain the six-month routine cadence. Removing one planned stop per year does not outweigh the lack of twelve-month evidence, particularly with active use 22.0% higher in the later equal-length period. The April 22 target stays in the schedule; Christopher will confirm the booking.
