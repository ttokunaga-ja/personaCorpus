# October 16 storage review — decision and actions
BCE-CE-2025-MIN-006-R00 | Oct 16, 2025 | Melissa Watkins | Final

## Meeting
Oct 16, 2025, 10:00. Attendees: Robert Nguyen, laboratory director; Melissa Watkins; Lauren Brooks; Anthony Mendoza.

## Records reviewed
BCE-CE-2025-BRF-003-R00, October storage review; BCE-CE-2025-TBL-005-R00, group means and baseline; and BCE-CE-2025-DEV-001-R00, identity exclusions. The approved study plan is BCE-CE-2025-PLN-001-R01.

| Observed storage group | Eligible n | Mean relative to 0 h |
|---|---:|---:|
| 0 h | 12 | 100.0% |
| 24 h | 11 | 99.1% |
| 48 h | 11 | 82.0% |

The two July identity discrepancies were excluded, one from each later group. The observations are independent groups. At 24 h the descriptive mean is 0.9% below baseline; no equivalence test was performed. At 48 h the eligible mean is 18.0% below baseline, with n = 11.

## Decision
Robert adopted a maximum elapsed storage time of 24 h inclusive for routine Bennett work under the nonclinical supernatant measurement workflow evaluated in PLN-001-R01, effective Oct 20, 2025. The 24 h descriptive result is close to the baseline mean, while the 48 h mean shows a substantial observed loss; together these support a practical ceiling at the observed 24 h point. This decision does not establish equivalence at 24 h or a concentration curve between observations.

Exactly 24 h is within the ceiling. Elapsed times that are undocumented or over 24 h must be referred to Anthony before routine use. A blank is not evidence that the limit was met.
