All four exceedances fall in the July 10 Buffered or Supplemented groups. The comparison locates the flags; it does not establish their cause. The 15% criterion is applied to each sample, and all 24 readings, including the four exceedances, remain in the record.

## Recommendation

Robert, I recommend a repeat to see whether this date-and-condition pattern recurs. Use two dated groups of 12, with four samples in each condition on each date, and retain the original per-sample CV limit of 15%. Put both culture date and condition in the review table from the outset, alongside the full run IDs, so that any flags can be examined by date as well as in the pooled summary. This is a proposal for review; the cause of the initial grouping remains open.
