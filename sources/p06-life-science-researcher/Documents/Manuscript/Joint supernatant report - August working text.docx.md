# Joint supernatant measurement report

BCE-CE-2026-RPT-001-R00 | August 31, 2026 | Prepared by Melissa Watkins | Internal working text for Anthony Mendoza

## 1 Scope

Bennett and Caldwell's April comparison describes paired differences under the revised method. The July A-24 results describe variation across the samples in a single Bennett run. The earlier Bennett storage study compares means at three observed times. Each set of results uses its own denominator. The work concerns nonclinical samples.

## 2 April paired comparison

### 2.1 Coverage

On April 14, we matched 30 shared labels, each with one reading from Bennett and one from Caldwell. We assigned each pair to a band using its Bennett reading. There were eight pairs below 3.00 ng/mL, 12 from 3.00 to below 5.00 ng/mL, and ten at or above 5.00 ng/mL (Figure 1).

Figure 1. Matched pairs by Bennett concentration band. The bars count 8, 12 and 10 pairs in ascending band order, for a total of 30.

### 2.2 Differences

The mean signed difference across the 30 pairs was +0.30 ng/mL. The band means were +0.75, +0.20 and +0.06 ng/mL in ascending Bennett band order (Figure 2, BCE-CE-2026-TBL-004-R00). The largest mean difference was in the low band. The additional check of that band remains open.

Figure 2. Mean paired difference in ng/mL, calculated as Caldwell minus Bennett. The bands contain 8, 12 and 10 pairs. The zero line marks equal readings.

## 3 Bennett A-24 run

### 3.1 Retained concentrations

The July 21 run yielded one retained reading for each of 24 Bennett samples. Figure 3 orders these readings by concentration. The displayed mean is 6.8 ng/mL. The point labeled A24-24 is 7.01 ng/mL and falls at rank 15.

Figure 3. Retained samples in ascending concentration order (n=24, ng/mL). The dashed line shows the run mean at 6.8 ng/mL.

### 3.2 Run check

Dividing the sample standard deviation by the mean of the 24 readings gives a run CV of 9.4%, within the approved ceiling of 15.0% (Figure 4). Anthony's quality confirmation BCE-CE-2026-QCF-001-R00 covers the records and calculation for this Bennett run.

Figure 4. Run CV of 9.4% for n=24 and the separate 15.0% run criterion.

## 4 Storage evidence

### 4.1 Eligibility

The July 2025 record set retained 12 records at each time, 36 in all. Two labels could not be verified against the signed handoffs. Anthony excluded one record at 24 h and one at 48 h under BCE-CE-2025-DEV-001-R00, leaving 34 eligible records (Figure 5). The source records were retained as filed.

Figure 5. Eligible and excluded record counts at 0, 24 and 48 h. Eligible counts are 12, 11 and 11. Excluded counts are 0, 1 and 1, with one label discrepancy at each of 24 and 48 h.

### 4.2 Observed concentrations

The concentrations in Figure 6 are means of the eligible readings within each independent group (BCE-CE-2025-TBL-005-R00). The table gives the concentration and its percentage of the unrounded 0 h group mean at each observed time.

|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 h | 12 | 7.0475 | 100.0% |

Figure 6. Group mean concentrations in ng/mL at 0, 24 and 48 h, eligible n=12, 11 and 11. Two records with label discrepancies were excluded, one at 24 h and one at 48 h.

### 4.3 Baseline comparison

The 24 h group mean is 99.1% of the 0 h mean. The 48 h mean is 82.0%, or 18.0% below baseline (Figure 7). The comparison at 24 h is descriptive. The three observations give no evidence about means at intervening times.

Figure 7. Percent of the 0 h group mean at 0, 24 and 48 h: 100.0%, 99.1% and 82.0%, respectively; eligible n=12, 11 and 11.

## 5 Conclusion

The results support short-term storage before measurement. The April low-band difference remains a separate follow-up. The A-24 result describes the Bennett run only.
