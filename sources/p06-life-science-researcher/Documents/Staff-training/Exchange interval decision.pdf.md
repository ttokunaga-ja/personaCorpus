# Exchange intervals agreed for the next cycle

Document: BCE-CE-2026-DEC-002-R00  
Decision date: Sep 23, 2026  
Prepared by: Melissa Watkins  
Approved by: Robert Nguyen, Laboratory Director  
Distribution: Lauren Brooks, Patricia Jensen, Anthony Mendoza

## Decision

Carry forward the 24 h and 48 h medium-exchange intervals for the next cycle. Leave 72 h out of that cycle. Robert Nguyen approved this scope at the Sep 23 evaluation with Melissa Watkins (BCE-CE-2026-MIN-005-R00).

## Basis

The decision uses MX-250626, the repeat prepared on Jun 23, 2025 and measured on Jun 26, 2025. It contributes ten adopted readings per interval and 30 distinct sample IDs overall. The 24 h and 48 h groups were within the study criterion of CV ≤15.0%, at 8.0% and 12.0% respectively. The 72 h group was at 21.0%, exceeding the criterion by 6.0 percentage points.

The Jun 17, 2025 round (MX-250617) also showed 21.0% for 72 h and had two preparation dates. Use that result as historical comparison. The adopted readings and group calculations are in BCE-CE-2026-TBL-008-R00.

## Application

Use this scope when preparing the next cycle of the medium-exchange interval study. Retain the 72 h records from both June rounds. Keep the ten initial readings as comparison evidence and the ten repeat readings as part of the 30 adopted readings that support this decision.

The next measurement date remains open. The Sep 23 evaluation did not change any general process criterion.
