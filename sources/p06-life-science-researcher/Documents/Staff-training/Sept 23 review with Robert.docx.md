# September 23 review with Robert

BCE-CE-2026-MIN-005-R00 · September 23, 2026 · Prepared by Melissa Watkins · Status: Final

## Meeting

September 23, 2026, 10:00–10:35 EDT. Present: Robert Nguyen, Laboratory Director, and Melissa Watkins. We reviewed the archived medium-exchange measurements and agreed which intervals to include in the next cycle.

## Records reviewed

- BCE-CE-2026-REV-005-R01, “Why we would stop at 48 hours,” the findings circulated before the meeting.
- BCE-CE-2026-TBL-008-R00, “Medium exchange readings and decision basis,” specifically the adopted rows 8–37 in Reading trace.

## Discussion and decision

I showed Robert the two June 2025 results for the 72 h interval. The initial round, MX-250617, measured on June 17, gave a CV of 21.0% across samples with two preparation dates. For the repeat, MX-250626, the three groups shared a June 23 preparation date and were measured on June 26. We reviewed one adopted reading per ID from the repeat, with ten samples apiece at 24, 48 and 72 h. The group CVs were 8.0%, 12.0% and 21.0%, respectively.

Robert compared the repeat 72 h result with the criterion of CV ≤15.0%. It was 6.0 percentage points above the limit even after matching the preparation date. The 24 and 48 h groups both met the criterion. Robert agreed to include only 24 and 48 h in the next cycle. The June 17 result explains why we revisited the group. The June 26 result supports the decision.

We confirmed that the ten adopted 72 h values remain among the 30 repeat readings in the review record. Retain the ten initial 72 h comparison readings as well, with the original outputs in their respective rounds. The agreement applies to the intervals planned for the next cycle. Measurement dates and the handling of data from the new cycle are open for separate discussion.
