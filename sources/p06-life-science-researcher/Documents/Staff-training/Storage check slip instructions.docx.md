# What goes on the storage check slip
BCE-CE-2025-REV-004-R00 | Oct 17, 2025 | Lauren Brooks | Desk reference

For Benjamin and the Oct 20 desk briefing. Authority: BCE-CE-2025-DEC-001-R00, approved Oct 16 and effective Oct 20, for routine Bennett work under the evaluated nonclinical supernatant measurement workflow.

## Recording the time
Enter the documented elapsed storage time in hours and minutes on the check slip. Use the recorded timing evidence; do not infer a time from an empty field. The ceiling includes exactly 24 h. If the time is over 24 h or cannot be documented, identify Anthony Mendoza as the reviewer on the slip and refer the case to him before routine use. Keep an unknown time distinct from a known excess.

## Desk examples
| Elapsed time on slip | Timing entry | Route under DEC-001-R00 |
|---|---|---|
| 24 h exactly | 24 h 00 min, documented | Within the timing ceiling; may proceed under this rule |
| 24 h 15 min | 24 h 15 min, documented | Over ceiling; Anthony reviews before routine use |
| Blank | Time undocumented; leave elapsed time blank | Anthony reviews before routine use |

These examples describe the paperwork route, not a predicted measurement. Other requirements for the work still apply. If the timing evidence is absent, write “time undocumented — Anthony Mendoza review” rather than entering zero or marking the ceiling met.
