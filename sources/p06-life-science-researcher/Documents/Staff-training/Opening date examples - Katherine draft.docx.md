Katherine, would you look at these examples before we use them this afternoon? I have left two questions in the last sections.

## What the walk found

This morning we found two opened containers with no date in the Opened field. CL-206 on the middle shelf of the common cabinet had a received date; ST-411 on the lower tier of the reserve supply shelf had opener initials. Neither supplies the opening date. We removed the containers from research use at 09:26 and referred the record to Anthony. See walk record BCE-CE-2026-LAB-007-R00.

## Example 1: completed label

| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| Received | February 2, 2026 |
| Opened | February 4, 2026 |

Received February 2 → Opened February 4. Both entries are present; read the named field when recording a date.
