# Whitmore — revised budget justification

BCE-CE-2025-REV-005-R01 | Nov 10, 2025 | Melissa Watkins | R01 · For resubmission

Prepared for Monica Robinson, Whitmore Science Fund

## Request summary

Bennett Cell Evaluation Inc requests USD 56,000. Following our November 10 consultation, I have separated the charge for Caldwell's measurement services from Bennett's time coordinating that work. The revised allocation matches Cost detail in BCE-CE-2025-TBL-010-R01.

| Cost category | Basis | Requested (USD) |
|---|---|---:|
| Joint measurement | 3 Caldwell service blocks × $6,000 | 18,000 |
| Bennett staff | 155 staff hours + 45 coordination hours, at $100 per hour | 20,000 |
| Materials | 5 allowances × $2,000 | 10,000 |
| Records review | 80 review hours × $100 | 8,000 |
| **Total requested** | | **56,000** |

## Joint measurement

The joint-measurement line is capped at USD 18,000 for three Caldwell Assay Services blocks at USD 6,000 each, as shown in Caldwell's November 7 estimate. Under WSF-2025-1106-BUD-001, the 45 hours Bennett needs to coordinate the work belong under Bennett staff. Moving those hours at USD 100 per hour reduces joint measurement by USD 4,500, from USD 22,500 to USD 18,000.

## Other requested costs

The Bennett labor worksheet carries 155 staff hours and the 45 transferred coordination hours at the same rate of USD 100 per hour. That brings the staff line from USD 15,500 to USD 20,000.

The November 7 materials allowance sheet supports USD 10,000 for five allowances of USD 2,000. The separate November 7 records-review estimate supports USD 8,000 for 80 hours at USD 100 per hour. The review hours are outside the 200 hours on the staff line.

## Resubmission

Joseph Fernandes checked the four revised lines against the November 7 estimates after the consultation. The USD 4,500 transfer leaves the request at USD 56,000.
