# Supernatant storage — 24 hour operating limit
BCE-CE-2025-DEC-001-R00 | Decision date: Oct 16, 2025 | Prepared by: Melissa Watkins | Approved by: Robert Nguyen
Issued: Oct 16, 2025 | Effective: Oct 20, 2025 | Distribution: Laboratory team

## Decision
Robert Nguyen approved an inclusive maximum elapsed storage time of 24 hours for routine Bennett work under the nonclinical supernatant measurement workflow evaluated in BCE-CE-2025-PLN-001-R01. Exactly 24 hours is within the ceiling. This decision takes effect Oct 20, 2025.

## Basis
The July study planned 12 independent records at each observed time. The group summary BCE-CE-2025-TBL-005-R00 uses eligible records after Anthony's identity determination BCE-CE-2025-DEV-001-R00.

| Storage time | Eligible n | Mean relative to 0 h mean |
|---|---:|---:|
| 0 h | 12 | 100.0% |
| 24 h | 11 | 99.1% |
| 48 h | 11 | 82.0% |

BN-250715-05 and BN-250716-07 were excluded because their identities could not be verified against the signed handoffs. The 24 h mean is descriptively 0.9% below the baseline mean; this is not an equivalence finding. The 48 h mean is 18.0% lower than the baseline mean. These independent groups do not establish a curve between the observed times.

## Application
For the evaluated workflow, record the elapsed time on the applicable handoff or check record. When a documented time is at or below 24 hours, it meets this timing ceiling. Refer undocumented elapsed times and known times over 24 hours to Anthony Mendoza for review before routine use. Do not treat a missing time as zero or as within the limit. This decision applies to the evaluated nonclinical workflow, not other materials or workflows.
