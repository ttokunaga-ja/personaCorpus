# Supernatant storage time study
BCE-CE-2025-PLN-001-R01 | Apr 11, 2025 | Melissa Watkins | Approved

## Purpose
Lauren and I are opening this study to compare concentration after different supernatant storage times. This is a separate question from the medium conditions work.

## Design
Use independent sample records for each planned time point. Record concentration in ng/mL and compare the 24 h and 48 h groups with the 0 h group as the baseline.

| Planned storage time | Independent sample records | Role |
|---|---:|---|
| 0 h | 12 | Comparison baseline |
| 24 h | 12 | Comparison group |
| 48 h | 12 | Comparison group |
| Total | 36 | Planned records |

Lauren will record the handoff and retain the instrument output for each group. Keep the three time points identifiable in the records so the baseline and later groups can be reviewed separately.

## Criteria and records
The table specifies planned numbers, not results. Record the received count and concentration for each group. Before counting a record as eligible for analysis, compare its label on the signed handoff with the label in the instrument export. Receipt alone does not establish eligibility. Refer identity exceptions to Anthony Mendoza for a decision; do not infer a corrected identity from row order. The report must show planned and eligible counts separately for each of the 0, 24 and 48 h groups, alongside the concentration comparison.
