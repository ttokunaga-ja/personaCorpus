# RISK-031 Corrective Action Review Summary
Document: CD-IM-202510-ANS-02 | Version: v1 | Date: 7 October 2025
Prepared by: Miyuki Yamada | Approved by: Kazuo Sasaki | To: Chiyomi Ikeda

## Scope
On 18 July 2025, the information management owner deferred acceptance of the risk arising from three unperformed supplier checks. Evidence was to be collected by 30 September. Completion requires traceability of the population and scope, date performed, reviewer and underlying records; receipt alone is not completion.

## Evidence received and results
| Item | Evidence available by 30 September | Assessment |
|---|---|---|
| RISK-031-01 Hidaka storage areas | Signed check CD-IM-202509-CHK-02 v1, performed 16 September and received 18 September; area map HK-25Q3-02 and the walkthrough reconciliation. | East and west areas match the map; Matsumoto and Hashimoto signed the check. |
| RISK-031-02 Hidaka return boxes | Signed check CD-IM-202509-CHK-03 v1, performed 22 September and received 25 September; 12 transfer entries and correction HN-C02. | Eleven entries matched directly. HN-0827-04 was traced to the receiving side through HN-C02, making 12 matches after correction. |
| RISK-031-03 Fuji dispatched personnel | Saito's 29 September message reports Ishikawa's statement that six people were checked on 19 September. | The roster version in effect that day and the signed original check are unavailable. The six people cannot yet be matched to the check results. |

## Corrective action and independent review
Kimura independently checked the area scope, signatures, transfer entries and correction trail on 3 October, recording the result in CD-IM-202510-MTG-01 v1. The two Hidaka items were suitable for a completion decision; the Fuji item remained pending. The 29 September message is evidence of a reported check, not evidence that its population and results were verified.

## Approval and outstanding action
At the 7 October management meeting Sasaki approved completion of 01 and 02 effective 7 October and continuation of 03 due to missing records, as recorded in CD-IM-202510-COR-01 v1. Saito is to obtain the roster version and signed original from Ishikawa by 17 October; Kimura is scheduled to reconcile them on 21 October. We will report the outcome after that review. If copies cannot be obtained, the item remains open. Any replacement check will be recorded on its actual date. This summary is approved for submission.
