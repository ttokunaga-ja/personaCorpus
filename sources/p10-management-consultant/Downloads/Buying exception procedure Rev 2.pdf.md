# Buying exception requests — evidence and approval

Procedure: PTD-BUY-EXC-2025-001  
Revision: 2  
Effective: 06 Jan 2025  
Issued by: Meera Kulkarni, Buying Director

## Purpose and application
Use this route when a requirement cannot be placed through the normal buying route. A request marked urgent still needs a Buying decision before an order is placed. The priority marking assists the routing of a request; it is not a substitute for the reason or supporting evidence.

## What the requester records
State who is requesting the purchase, the required date and quantity, why the normal route cannot be used, and the evidence available to support the request. Provide a contact for any clarification. The reason field records the explanation given when the request is submitted. If a required detail is missing, Buying returns the request for clarification before deciding.

## Routing and decision
| Step | Request or evidence | Route and response |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Requester records the originator, required date, quantity, reason for exception and supporting evidence. | Send the request to Buying for review. |
| 2 | Requester indicates whether the request is urgent and identifies the immediate need. | Buying gives an urgent request prompt attention; it remains subject to approval. |
| 3 | Buying reviews the information and seeks missing evidence or clarification from the requester. | Return incomplete requests to the requester; record the response in the request. |
| 4 | Buying's approver decides whether the exception may proceed. | Record the decision and its time in the request. Where not approved, return the decision to the requester. |
| 5 | Approved request and decision record. | Buying proceeds to place the order through the applicable purchasing route. Retain the request and decision together. |

## Record
Keep the request, supporting evidence, clarification and approval decision together so that the reason and the time of the decision can be followed. Contact Buying if the required evidence is unavailable or the urgency is unclear.
