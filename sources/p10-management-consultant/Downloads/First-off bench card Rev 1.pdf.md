# First-off bench card

Marston Carton Packaging Ltd · Production · Trafford T2 and Warrington W1

**Card:** MCP-OPS-QRC-2024-001 · **Revision:** Rev 1  
**Issued:** 21 Nov 2024 · **Effective:** 22 Nov 2024  
**Issued by:** Caroline Wood, Production Director

## Front — Hold and hand over

**Identify.** After changeover, take the first three sheets. Identify them against the job instruction and keep all three out of good stock.

**Box and tag.** Put the three sheets in a box on the hold shelf. Enter the line, run, event, specification and sending shift on the tag. Tie the tag to the box handle.

**Hand over.** Show the incoming lead the tagged box and tell them what still needs checking. Leave the tag on the box and keep the sheets on hold.

**Work standard:** MCP-OPS-STD-2024-014 Rev 3

## Back — Check, approve and return

**Receive.** Record receipt. Keep the box on hold while you check it.

**Compare.** Match the tag and all three sheets to the job instruction and specification. If the tag is missing, anything differs or approval is unavailable, leave the box on the hold shelf. Keep any tag attached and tell the line coordinator what needs resolving.

**Approve.** The receiving shift lead records approval and its time after a satisfactory check.

**Return.** When the sheets actually move to good stock, the receiving lead attends the move, signs and records the actual return date and time.
