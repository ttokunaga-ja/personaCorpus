# Changeover and restart standard

Marston Carton Packaging Ltd — Production Department

**Standard number:** MCP-OPS-STD-2024-014  
**Revision:** Rev 3  
**Effective:** 01 Aug 2024  
**Issued by:** Caroline Wood, Production Director  
**Issued to:** Shift leads, Trafford T2 and Warrington W1  
**Copy exported:** 06 Aug 2024

## When to use this standard

Use this procedure when a job changes or when the line restarts after a jam. The operator holds the first-off sheets for inspection. The shift lead authorises their release to good output.

## Work sequence

| Step | Work | Control and release criteria | Record and action |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Set up the next job | Check the board grade and box specification against the new job instruction. Keep sheets from the previous job separate. | Enter the job and changeover on the run record. |
| 2 | Hold the first three sheets after changeover | Keep all three out of good output. Compare each sheet against the job instruction and approved specification. | Present the three sheets and the check to the shift lead. |
| 3 | Obtain first-off approval | Release to good output only after the shift lead has approved the three sheets. If a sheet fails the check, hold the output, correct the setting and check another three sheets. | The shift lead records the approval and time on the run record. |
| 4 | Check the first three sheets after a jam | Once the jam has been cleared and the line restarts, hold the first three sheets and repeat the same comparison and approval before allowing output into good stock. Repeat the check after any correction. | Record the restart. The shift lead enters the restart approval and time. |
| 5 | Enter the two loss weights separately | Enter loss from changeover to first-off approval in the start-up loss field. Enter loss from restart after a jam to approval in the restart loss field. Enter each loss once. | The operator completes the run record. The shift lead checks that each weight is in the correct field. |

## Checks that cannot be completed

Keep the affected output on hold and tell the shift lead if the specification or release approval cannot be confirmed. Record the reason for the hold and resolve it before release. If a loss weight still needs checking, leave the entry open for the shift lead to resolve.
