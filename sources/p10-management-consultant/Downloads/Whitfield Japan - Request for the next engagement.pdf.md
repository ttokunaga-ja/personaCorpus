# Request for the next engagement

Document: WCL-JP-FIN-REQ-2026-001 v1.0  
Issued: 13 Apr 2026  
Issued by: Kenneth Townsend, CFO, Whitfield Components Ltd  
Category: ENGAGEMENT REQUEST  
To: Nicholas Hargreaves and Elizabeth Hargreaves, Holt & Reeves Advisory Ltd

Nicholas and Elizabeth,

I would like us to consider the next piece of work for our Japan business. There are two candidate areas: payment controls in Japan Finance and order exceptions in Sales. Please assess them separately before recommending what the first engagement can responsibly cover. Their inclusion here is a request for consideration, not an instruction to begin either piece of work.

## Information requested for each candidate

| Candidate | Questions for your response |
|---|---|
| Japan Finance payment controls | Who would own the work on each side? Which existing approval, evidence and return records can be made available, and which still need to be confirmed? What specific deliverable could be committed to in a first contract? |
| Sales order exceptions | Who would own the work on each side? What records and criteria could be prepared to establish the current exception process? What specific deliverable could be committed to in a first contract? |

Please distinguish records already available from records that must still be obtained, and identify any dependencies on our people or access. A recommendation may cover one candidate rather than both. I would like a clear account of what would be left out, and the basis on which we could judge completion.

## Decision and commercial questions

I would like to make a decision on 20 July 2026. Please propose the duration, resource requirements and fee once the scope and the evidence we can supply are sufficiently clear. Neither budget nor duration is fixed by this request. Please also tell me what decisions or information you need from Japan before then.

Kenneth Townsend
