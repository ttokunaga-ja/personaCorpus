The measures describe different things. Order lateness concerns dispatch timing; the saving compares purchase amounts on verified terms. These observations do not establish that the timing change caused the saving.

## What was observed
The order population is orders at two sites to the 12 suppliers selected in the prior year. The approved deadline date in EDT assigns the month: 01–30 June or 01–31 August 2026. Compare each PO's first supplier dispatch with the last deadline approved before that dispatch. Later is late; equal is on time.

June's 416 register rows comprise 400 judged orders, five duplicate resends, five cancellations before first dispatch and six timing enquiries. August's 438 comprise 420 judged orders, seven resends, five pre-dispatch cancellations and six timing enquiries. Resends, cancellations and unresolved timing enquiries are outside the judged denominator. In June 364 were on time and 36 late; in August 399 were on time and 21 late. Where a date alone cannot establish which side of a same-day deadline the dispatch falls, retain Review and request the send history.

The 14-row handover extract is an exercise, not the population behind 5%: ten judged rows include two late; two rows are excluded and two require review.

## How the saving was assessed
For five confirmed groups among the 12 suppliers, we took August receipts and linked returns posted by 04 Sep. We valued actual net purchased quantities at each group's approved comparison baseline price, excluding tax and freight. The USD 800,000 basis compared with confirmed net purchases of USD 752,000 yields USD 48,000, or 6.0%. This is August's observed amount, not a forecast for later months.

| Confirmed group | Comparison basis USD | Confirmed net purchases USD |
|---|---:|---:|
| S018 | 180,000 | 168,000 |
| S042 | 160,000 | 152,000 |
| S008 | 140,000 | 133,000 |
| S036 cleared range | 200,000 | 187,000 |
| S011 cleared range | 120,000 | 112,000 |
| Total | 800,000 | 752,000 |

Separate S036 receipts require the approved terms version effective in August; separate S011 receipts require approval of the August commercial credit and Finance's posting evidence. These groups do not overlap the confirmed ranges and are outside both numerator and denominator until supported. Their differences are blank, not zero. January's USD 5,500 relates to the earlier checkpoint and is not carried into August.
