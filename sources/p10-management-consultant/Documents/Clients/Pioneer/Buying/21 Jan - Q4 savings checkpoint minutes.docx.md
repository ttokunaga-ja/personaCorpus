## Purpose and basis

Confirm the January checkpoint against the terms held by Buying and agree what remains to be checked. The discussion used HRA-PIO-BUY-BRF-2026-001 v1.0 and Deepa’s reconciliation HRA-PIO-BUY-SAV-2026-001 v1.0.

The comparison covers the five selected conditions for S018, S042, S036, S011 and S008: receipts from 01 Oct to 31 Dec 2025 and linked returns, with postings through 12 Jan 2026. The common denominator is the actual net quantity valued at the October baseline prices, USD 1,000,000 excluding tax. The forecast applies the October assumptions to those same quantities; it is not a whole-company or full-year result. Freight and tax are excluded.

## Discussion and decisions

| Item | Discussion | Decision / next response |
|---|---|---|
| Checkpoint | On the common basis, forecast savings are USD 81,000 (8.1%) and posted savings are USD 56,000 (5.6%). The difference is USD 25,000, or 2.50 percentage points. | Keep the 12 Jan posting cut and the 5.6% interim result intact while the outstanding evidence is checked. |
| S018 — quantity | Net receipts were 10,000 cases after the linked 200-case return, below the 12,000-case threshold. The written terms therefore applied USD 19.60 per case; the forecast used the qualifying price of USD 18.40. The difference on actual net volume is 10,000 × USD 1.20 = USD 12,000, or 1.20 points. | Describe this as a quantity threshold not reached, not a failure to apply the agreed price. |
| S036 — timing | The forecast assumed 10,000 of the 12,500 cases would be received after the price change; 6,250 were received from 01 Dec. The difference of 3,750 cases at USD 2 is USD 7,500, or 0.75 points. The receipt date, not invoice date, determines the applicable price. | Show the difference separately as timing. |
| S011 — additional allowance | The USD 5,500, or 0.55 points, is the remaining difference between the forecast and posted result. The additional commercial credit requires separate approval; neither approved terms nor a posting through 12 Jan had been confirmed. | Keep the residual open. It is not an established credit or an agreed recovery. |
