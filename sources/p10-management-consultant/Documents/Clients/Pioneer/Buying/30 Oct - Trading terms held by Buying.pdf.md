## Terms on file

The following records the terms held by Buying for the identified item and condition line for receipts from 01 Oct to 31 Dec 2025 inclusive. Prices are USD excluding tax. The conditions apply only to the item identified; freight and tax are outside these prices.

| Line / supplier | Item and unit | Period | Condition | Price and application |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| T1 / S018 | Shipping labels / case | 01 Oct–31 Dec 2025 | At least 12,000 net cases in the quarter | USD 18.40 per case on all qualifying quarter volume if the threshold is met; otherwise USD 19.60 per case. |
| T2 / S042 | Sealing tape / case; 10 cases per pack | 01 Oct–31 Dec 2025 | At least 7,000 net cases in the quarter | USD 18.00 per case on all qualifying quarter volume if met; otherwise USD 20.00 per case. USD 180 per pack corresponds to USD 18 per case. |
| T3 / S036 | Protective sleeves / case | 01 Oct–31 Dec 2025 | Receipt date; no minimum quantity | USD 20.00 per case for receipts before 01 Dec; USD 18.00 per case for receipts on or after 01 Dec. |
| T4 / S011 | Bin liners / case | 01 Oct–31 Dec 2025 | No minimum quantity | USD 19.50 per case. Any additional commercial allowance requires separate approval; it is not established by this record. |
| T5 / S008 | Packing dispensers / unit | 01 Oct–31 Dec 2025 | No minimum quantity | Fixed USD 27.80 per unit. |

## Applying the terms

For the quantity thresholds, count eligible receipts for the condition in the quarter less returns linked to those receipts. Keep the original receipt and return references together. Convert S042 packs to cases using 10 cases per pack before comparing with its threshold, and preserve the original unit and amount in the purchase record. For S036 use the receipt date, not the invoice posting date, to choose the price. Where an invoice or credit does not agree with the applicable condition, retain the evidence and refer it to Buying for confirmation. Do not infer approval of a separate allowance from the base price.
