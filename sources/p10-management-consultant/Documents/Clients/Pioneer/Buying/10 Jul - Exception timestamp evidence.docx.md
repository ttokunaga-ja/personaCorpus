# What the forwarded copy cannot prove

Document: HRA-PIO-BUY-EV-2025-002  
Date: 10 Jul 2025  
Prepared by: Neha Patel  
For: Nicholas Hargreaves

## Purpose

Nicholas, Meera has answered the two questions I sent on 9 July. I have separated the evidence she confirmed from what we can infer about the sequence. The classification depends on when both quantity and required date were first known, the previous-business-day 15:00 EDT cut-off, and when Buying first received the demand. A forwarding or application time alone does not establish the first of those clocks.

## Results of the enquiry

| Request | Evidence and enquiry | Finding and treatment |
|---|---|---|
| EX-052 | Connor's 7 July extract shows RP-6118 at 14:20 EDT on 10 June with an 11 June required date, but leaves quantity to be confirmed. It records the request received by Buying at 08:42 EDT on 11 June. In her 10 July reply, Meera confirmed that RP-6118 already contained 240 cases and the 11 June required date at 14:20. | Both demand fields were known 40 minutes before the 10 June 15:00 EDT cut-off. Buying's first recorded receipt was the following morning, after that cut-off. Move from pending quantity confirmation to planning delay: known before cut-off, shared with Buying after it. The record does not establish that an exception would have been avoided. |
| EX-077 | The 7 July extract records a forward at 08:13 EDT and application at 08:31 EDT on 24 June. I asked Meera whether the source retained the original demand time. She replied that she could not confirm a record establishing the quantity before the forward. | The forward preceded the application; neither timestamp establishes when quantity and required date were first known, and the extract does not establish Buying's first receipt. The cut-off comparison cannot be completed. Record as insufficient evidence following enquiry, not as an unasked question or as proof of either planning delay or a late change in demand. |
