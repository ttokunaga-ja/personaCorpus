## Slide 2 — Process Figure
**Normal and urgent requests meet at the Buying decision**
Figure: Requester records reason normal route cannot be used, required date, quantity, originator and evidence → priority indicated → two branches labelled “Normal attention” and “Urgent attention” → Buying reviews and asks for missing information → Buying approver records decision and time → if approved, order placed. A return arrow from Buying review goes to requester for clarification; a declined decision goes back to requester.

Caption: Source: PTD-BUY-EXC-2025-001 Rev 2, effective 06 Jan 2025. Urgent changes the attention given to a request; approval is still required before ordering.

Speaker notes: Both branches converge before the order. This is the existing route in the guidance, not a proposed new control.

## Slide 3 — Two Columns
**The reason field and the demand clock answer different questions**
Left: The reason field records the requester's explanation when submitting the exception, alongside the required date, quantity and evidence. It can tell us what the requester said.
Right: The field alone does not establish when the quantity and required date first became known. Locate the earliest supporting demand record and the first sharing with Buying before inferring why the request was urgent.

Speaker notes: A short phrase can describe several histories. Do not equate an urgent label or a reason alone with late planning.

## Slide 4 — Headline and Body
**Proposed evidence cut**
- Start with every uniquely identified exception received in the same defined period, across suppliers; consolidate resubmissions under the same request.
- Separate the priority assigned at submission, then trace the urgent requests to their demand and submission records.
- Keep missing or ambiguous timing open for clarification; do not force a cause from the wording.
- This population is separate from the 12 suppliers chosen for transaction-record review.

Speaker notes: Agree the period and obtain records before counting. The 12-supplier selection is not a filter for the exception analysis.
