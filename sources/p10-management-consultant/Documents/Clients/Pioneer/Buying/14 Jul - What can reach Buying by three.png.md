# What can reach Buying by three?

Proposed demand-sharing route · 14 Jul 2025 · For discussion with Meera and Buying

One flow, read from left to right. Boxes and connecting arrows contain the following text:

1. **Requesting team: record the demand** — Quantity; required date; time when both quantity and required date first became known. Keep the source record.
2. **Is the demand known by 15:00 EDT on the preceding business day?**
   - Yes → **Responsible person: send the sharing sheet to Buying by 15:00 EDT** — Include the three fields and source reference.
   - Known after the cut-off → **Same-day exception** — Send the request and evidence through the current exception route.
   - Time unknown → **Ask Buying to check** — Send the available record and identify the missing original time. Do not estimate it.
3. **Buying: decide the route** — Consider the sharing sheet and route through current approval, or continue the same-day exception route where appropriate. Buying's approval and record of decision and time remain required before ordering.

A separate branch enters the same-day exception box: **Supply-side change after an order** → **Continue current exception route; record the change and evidence**. Arrows from the two exception branches and the time-unknown enquiry lead to Buying. The diagram proposes an earlier handoff; it does not replace PTD-BUY-EXC-2025-001 Rev 2.
