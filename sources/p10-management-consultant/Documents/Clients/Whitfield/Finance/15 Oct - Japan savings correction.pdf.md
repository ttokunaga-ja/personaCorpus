# Correction to the Japan savings estimate

Document: HRA-WHI-DIAG-NTE-2024-001 v1.0  
Date: 15 Oct 2024  
Prepared by: Nicholas Hargreaves, Holt & Reeves Advisory Ltd  
To: Kenneth Townsend, CFO, Whitfield Components Ltd

## Replacement annualised estimate

Kenneth, following your request to check the basis before the figure is used internally, Connor and I have found that the annual estimate carried one item twice. Please replace JPY 26m with JPY 22m per year in the Japan Finance explanation and use the definition below. I am sorry that the earlier figure included this duplication, and thank you for asking us to check it before wider use.

| Item | JPY per year | Basis |
|---|---:|---|
| Previous annualised estimate | 26,000,000 | Five lines carried in the original estimate |
| Duplicate exception pack preparation | (4,000,000) | Finance model!D11 duplicates the preparation in AP model!D14 |
| Corrected annualised estimate | 22,000,000 | Previous estimate less the duplicate |

AP assembles the exception documents once and passes the same packs to Finance. The old calculation counted that preparation under both AP exception packs and Finance handover preparation. We traced three packs between AP preparation and Finance receipt; the annual 2,000-pack volume comes from the separate closed quarterly totals. We retained the AP value and removed the Finance duplicate.

## What the corrected figure means

Of the JPY 22m, JPY 18m values work capacity that could be redeployed; it is not a committed reduction in headcount or payroll. The other JPY 4m is an estimate of overtime premium that could be avoided, conditional on the approved premium hours actually reducing. The annualised estimate describes a steady operating year, not realised results. This correction does not provide an amount for the first year.

Please use that distinction when replacing the amount and its annual definition in the internal explanation. We can take any questions on the underlying lines together with Connor.
