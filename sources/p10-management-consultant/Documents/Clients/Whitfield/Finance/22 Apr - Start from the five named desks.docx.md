## How to check

For each desk ask Japan Finance to name the primary and deputy and confirm that both may process the relevant 07 approval. Access to a folder alone is not processing authority. Check separately that the receiving desk can receive and open the 07 decision and any applicable exception-resolution evidence. Trace a return from 07 to AP and a deficiency from 09 back to 08, including the party responsible for follow-up. Record the source, confirmer and date for each check, then establish separately whether the changed route has actually been applied.

The 06 first eligible circulation time must remain distinct from a later recirculation. At 12, Treasury's existing reconciliation and two checks remain unchanged; a corrected proposal requires both checks again. Payroll, tax and emergency manual transfers are outside the payment procedure's pilot scope.

Until a desk's position is verified, use its existing route as the working assumption and raise the gap with Japan Finance.
