# Slide 4 — Headline and Body

## Nine months is a conditional operating assumption

- Establish 07 first; 09 needs recorded 07 approval and exception closure.
- Keep current return points and Treasury's two checks at 12.
- Confirm the route is operating consistently and compare work-time and approved overtime premium evidence with a comparable baseline.
- Steady effect at nine months is a planning assumption, not an observed result or a first-year amount.

Speaker notes: Even after the cost peak has passed, evidence of actual overtime premium and reallocatable work capacity still needs to be checked. Do not infer realised savings from the cost schedule.
