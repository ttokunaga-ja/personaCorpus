# Where an invoice stops

HRA-WHI-DIAG-EV-2024-002 | 15 Apr 2024 | Neha Patel

To: Oliver Hewitt and Connor Atkinson

These are two accounts from the start of the AP interviews, not a frequency estimate. I have separated what was said from the location of the stop, which remains to be tested with the cost-centre side.

## Amount does not match

**Question:** What happens when the entered invoice does not agree with the order or receipt?

**AP account:** The item goes to exception resolution. AP obtains the missing explanation or correction and matches again before routing. The invoice can be present in the receipt mailbox while that work is still outstanding.

**Working interpretation:** This stop is before the cost-centre approver's inbox. Time spent obtaining and rechecking the evidence must not automatically be called time waiting for that approver.

**To confirm:** Which record shows the return and the successful re-match? Does the cost-centre owner see a copy before formal routing?

## Cost allocation is not settled

**Question:** Can a complete set of invoice and match documents be sent when the cost centre is still uncertain?

**AP account:** AP holds the item while the appropriate cost-bearing centre is identified. The supporting documents can all be present without the destination being settled.

**Working interpretation:** I have provisionally placed this stop before the approver's inbox too; it is not yet a ready hand-off.

**To confirm:** Ask the cost-centre side how the allocation is resolved, who holds the item and whether an informal copy has already arrived. Connor should look for an event that distinguishes receipt from routing, rather than assuming the former is the latter.
