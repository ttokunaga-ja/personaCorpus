# What makes a hand-off ready

| Document | Date | Prepared by |
|---|---|---|
| HRA-WHI-DIAG-EV-2024-001 v1.0 | 03 Jun 2024 | Neha Patel |

For Nicholas Hargreaves and Connor Atkinson

I spoke to 18 people between 15 Apr and 24 May, with no repeat participants in that count. Oliver helped with the interview questions and notes. I have grouped the accounts by the work each person sees. The interviews establish what people say they need at a hand-off and help us identify records to look for.

## What each role sees

| Role | People | What they described |
|---|---:|---|
| Accounts Payable | 6 | An invoice can arrive before there is evidence to match it. A complete invoice and match can still sit with AP if the cost allocation is unresolved. |
| Cost-centre owners | 4 | The package needs to show what is being charged to their centre and why. A copy of the invoice alone can reach them before they can review the charge. |
| Japan Finance | 5 | The cost-centre decision and closure of any exception have to be visible together. Finance follows up with the owner of whichever evidence is missing. |
| Treasury | 3 | The authoriser needs the checked payment proposal and evidence of both checks on that version. A proposed run can exist before it is ready for the authoriser. |

## What to carry across each hand-off

| Approval | Evidence at complete routing | Who takes back an incomplete item |
|---|---|---|
| 07 · Cost centre | AP routes the invoice, PO and receipt match evidence, and confirmed cost allocation to the named cost-centre owner. The routing record and the review start mark the two ends of the wait. | AP owns correction of a missing match explanation or allocation. Where match evidence is disputed, AP resolves it at 05, re-matches at 04 and routes again at 06. |
| 09 · Finance | Japan Finance has the recorded cost-centre approval and evidence that any exceptions are closed. The checked file goes from 08 to the Finance approver. | Japan Finance takes back the file at 08 and tracks the correction. The cost-centre owner supplies any missing approval. AP resolves missing match or exception evidence. |
| 12 · Bank release | Treasury reconciles the payment proposal to the approved items and completes both checks at 11. The routed proposal carries the check records for the version the authoriser will review. | Treasury takes back a discrepancy, corrects the proposal and repeats both checks before sending it again. |

The May return walk-through exposed a difference in what people meant by “holding” an invoice. The cost-centre side expected to see it again and still regarded it as theirs. AP described taking it back to obtain the missing explanation. For timing the renewed approval, the operative hand-off is when AP sends the corrected and re-matched package back. The intervening correction belongs with AP.

## What Connor can verify

At the 29 May discussion we separated first arrival, complete routing and the start of review. Use corresponding routing and review-start records for the same submission. Where there is a return, identify the fresh routing and the review it led to. A timestamp can confirm when review started where that event is recorded. A person's explanation for why they could only open the package later remains an interview account. The interviews give us neither the prevalence of each reason nor a recorded duration for any particular wait.
