# Returned invoice walk-through

HRA-WHI-DIAG-EV-2024-003 | 22 May 2024 | Neha Patel

To: Connor Atkinson

## The two accounts

In the case discussed with AP and the cost-centre side, a cost-centre reviewer returned an invoice because the evidence did not explain a difference against the order and receipt. The cost-centre account was that the invoice remained with the cost-centre owner after the return, awaiting a corrected explanation for renewed review. AP described taking ownership on return: AP obtained the missing support, checked it and routed the package again. These descriptions differ in who each side regarded as holding the item during correction; they do not establish that either person was idle throughout.

## Walk through the same invoice

| Interval | What happened | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| 05: exception resolution | After the return, AP assembled the missing explanation and supporting match evidence. Until those are complete and the invoice has been re-matched at 04, the package cannot be approved. | Keep this correction interval outside the 07 approval queue, including any inactive time within it. |
| 06 to 07: fresh routing | AP sent the corrected, re-matched package with the cost allocation to the cost-centre owner. The reviewer later began the renewed review. | Measure the elapsed interval from complete re-routing to review start as the 07 wait, if both events can be evidenced. |

The original arrival in a mailbox does not tell us whether the supporting explanation was present. Nor does a return establish the time when a corrected package became ready. If the correction or fresh routing cannot be distinguished in the record, leave the wait undetermined rather than treating the entire interval as a queue.

## For the timing work

The 10 May mailbox-to-completion intervals include both correction and approval work; they cannot answer this question. Connor, please look for the return, re-match, fresh routing and review-start events as separate points. I will use the role descriptions to check what “complete” means; a person's account of why the review was not started is not itself a recorded timestamp.
