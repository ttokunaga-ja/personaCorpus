# Japan Finance — Supplier payment procedure

| Procedure | Issue date | Approved by |
|---|---|---|
| WCL-JP-FIN-PAY-2024-001 | 08 Apr 2024 | Kenneth Townsend |

Version 1.0 · Issued by Whitfield Components Ltd, Japan Finance  
For Japan Accounts Payable, cost-centre owners, Japan Finance and Treasury

## Application

Follow this procedure for standard payments to domestic suppliers in Japan, from receipt of the invoice to reconciliation of settlement. Payroll, tax payments and emergency manual transfers follow separate procedures.

## Processing and hand-off

| Step | Activity | Control and hand-off |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Receive invoice | Accounts Payable (AP) records receipt and opens the invoice record. Attach the invoice to the record for the completeness check. |
| 02 | Check completeness | AP checks that the supplier, invoice reference, date, amount and order reference are present. Obtain missing information before passing the invoice for entry. |
| 03 | Enter invoice | AP enters the invoice against the supplier record and checks the entered reference and amount against the invoice. Pass the entry and invoice to matching. |
| 04 | Match PO and receipt | AP checks the invoice quantities and amounts against the purchase order and receipt. Record the match and proceed to 06 when the evidence agrees. Send any unresolved difference to 05. |
| 05 | Resolve exceptions | AP obtains the missing evidence or correction from the party responsible for the order or receipt. Record how the discrepancy was resolved and send the invoice back to 04 for a fresh match. |
| 06 | Route for cost-centre approval | AP sends the invoice, successful match evidence and confirmed cost-centre allocation to the responsible cost-centre owner. Hold the item in AP while any of these is outstanding. |
| 07 | Approve cost centre | The cost-centre owner checks that the invoice and match support the charge to their cost centre. Record approval and pass the file to Japan Finance. Return a discrepancy to AP with the reason. |
| 08 | Review for Finance approval | Japan Finance verifies the recorded cost-centre approval and checks that any exceptions are closed and documented. Refer missing approval to the cost-centre owner and unresolved evidence to AP. Route the complete file to the Finance approver. |
| 09 | Approve in Finance | The Finance approver checks the cost-centre decision and exception closure against the file and records the approval before it passes to Treasury. Send an incomplete file back to 08 for resolution. |
| 10 | Build payment proposal | Treasury selects invoices with Finance approval for the intended payment date. Compile the approved items and totals and pass the proposal with its supporting records to the payment-run checks. |
| 11 | Check payment run | Treasury reconciles the proposed items and total to the approved list and records both checks. Resolve differences and repeat the checks on the corrected proposal before routing it with both check records to the bank release authoriser. |
| 12 | Authorise bank release | The designated Treasury authoriser reviews the checked proposal and the evidence of both checks. Record the authorisation for the checked version. Return a discrepancy to Treasury for correction and renewed checks before resubmission. |
| 13 | Release payment | Treasury executes the authorised payment and records the bank release reference. Send the release record to Japan Finance for settlement reconciliation. |
| 14 | Reconcile settlement | Japan Finance matches the bank settlement to the released payment and the invoice record. Investigate unmatched or rejected payments with Treasury and close the record once settlement is accounted for. |

## Items returned for correction

Record the return reason and send the item to the role responsible for correcting it. Keep the item out of the approval queue while the required evidence is outstanding. A match exception returns through 05 to 04. When the correction is complete, route the current evidence again and obtain the applicable approval on that version. Where payment instructions are corrected, complete both Treasury checks again before requesting bank release authorisation. Keep the release and settlement references against the payment record.
