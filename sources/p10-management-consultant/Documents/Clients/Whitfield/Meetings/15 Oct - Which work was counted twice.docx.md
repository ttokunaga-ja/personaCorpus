# Which work was counted twice

HRA-WHI-DIAG-MIN-2024-005 v1.0 | 15 Oct 2024 | Recorded by Nicholas Hargreaves  
Meeting: 09:00–09:45 BST  
Attendees: Nicholas Hargreaves, Connor Atkinson

## Discussion and decision

Connor took me through the name-based join. AP's exception document packs and Finance's handover preparation had not matched by department or description, although each used 2,000 packs for the same July 2023–June 2024 period. A tie to the JPY 26m total alone did not show that the work was independent.

We compared the AP preparation records with the Finance receipt counterparts for EP-184, EP-219 and EP-267. Each Finance identifier refers to the pack assembled by AP; no record of a separate preparation of those packs was found. The three traces identify the work; the annual 2,000 comes separately from Japan Finance's closed quarterly totals.

AP model!D14 and Finance model!D11 both apply 30 minutes and JPY 4,000 per hour to EXC-PACK. Each therefore values the same preparation at JPY 4m per year. We will retain the AP line and remove Finance model!D11 from the annual estimate. The corrected annualised amount is JPY 22m: JPY 18m of work-capacity value and JPY 4m of assumed avoidable overtime premium. Neither the capacity value nor the annualised total is a headcount reduction or a first-year result.
