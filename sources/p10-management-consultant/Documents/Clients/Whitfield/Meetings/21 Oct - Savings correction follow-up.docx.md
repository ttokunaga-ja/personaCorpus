# What the correction changes

HRA-WHI-DIAG-MIN-2024-006 v1.0 | 21 Oct 2024 | Recorded by Nicholas Hargreaves  
Meeting: Monday, 09:00 BST  
Attendees: Kenneth Townsend, Nicholas Hargreaves, Connor Atkinson

## Discussion

Kenneth confirmed that Japan Finance's internal explanation and his own explanatory note had both been changed from JPY 26m to JPY 22m per year. The replacement is an annualised estimate, not an instruction to reduce headcount: JPY 18m values work capacity and JPY 4m is a conditional estimate of avoidable overtime premium. We will retain the JPY 22m as an estimate, not report it as a realised first-year amount.

Kenneth asked for the one-off cost and the time needed before the benefits could be realised to be explained alongside the figure. Connor noted that the work still needs a cost estimate; the annual correction does not establish either the cost or the pace of implementation. Nicholas will arrange the review once the team has a sequence and a cost basis to examine.
