# Payment pilot desk instructions — five departments
| Procedure | Issue date | Approval |
|---|---|---|
| WCL-JP-FIN-PAY-2025-002 v1.0 | 08 Oct 2025 | Kenneth Townsend — decision pending |

Issued by Whitfield Components Ltd, Japan Finance. For AP, cost-centre approvers and Japan Finance.

## Application
Components and Packaging continue the existing pilot instructions WCL-JP-FIN-PAY-2025-001 v1.0. The proposed additions are Tooling, Maintenance and Facilities. Subject to Kenneth's approval and Japan Finance confirming each department's primary and deputy, authority and receipt of the required evidence by 17 October, the changed hand-off applies to that additional department from 20 October. An additional department without confirmation continues on the existing route. Payroll, tax and emergency manual transfers remain outside scope.

## Processing and hand-off
| Step | Activity | Control and hand-off |
|---|---|---|
| 06 | AP assembles and routes | Bring together the invoice, matching evidence and confirmed cost allocation. Record the first eligible circulation date and time before sending the complete file to 07. Keep incomplete files at AP. |
| 07 | Cost-centre approval | Primary or authorised deputy checks and records the decision. Return an incomplete or inconsistent file to AP with the reason; AP corrects and recirculates it. |
| 08 | Japan Finance review | Check the recorded 07 approval and applicable exception-resolution evidence. If either is missing or inaccessible, retain at 08 and request the record from AP or the responsible desk. Pass a complete file to 09. |
| 09 | Finance approval | Record the approval. Return a deficient file to 08 identifying what is missing; 08 owns follow-up and asks AP or the responsible desk for correction. |
| 12 | Bank release authorisation | Treasury retains the existing reconciliation and two checks under WCL-JP-FIN-PAY-2024-001 v1.0. Corrected proposals require both checks again before authorisation. |

## Returns and records
Record the reason, receiving desk and time of each return and recirculation. Keep the first 06 time separately; do not replace it with the time of a later circulation. A file missing required evidence remains outside the relevant approval queue until corrected. Continue Treasury release and settlement under the existing procedure. Cases already in progress remain on their original route until complete.
