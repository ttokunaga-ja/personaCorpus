## Decision

Kenneth confirmed that the close assignments for both candidate weeks must be protected. He decided to defer the start this period. Standard domestic supplier payments will continue through the existing route in Japan. The decision on a change at 09 remains separate and Treasury's two checks at 12 stay in place. No replacement week was agreed.

## Basis for the decision

Connor took us through the six rows in March slot totals. Each candidate requires a 90-minute 07 window with both roles clear on each of its first three consecutive days. The 17 March week has cover on the 17th and 18th, with both roles on account reconciliation on the 19th. The 24 March week has cover on the 24th and 25th, with both roles on the variance review on the 26th. That leaves two working slots out of three in each week. The counts reflect the corrected match between the two rotas. Authority and readiness to exchange work still require evidence.

The 10 March rota check ruled out moving the blocked slots to 13:30 or 16:30 JST. At 13:30 AP's complete bundle is still 75 minutes away. At 16:30 the regular 07 duty window has ended.
