# Japan Finance Phase 1 engagement agreement

WCL-JP-FIN-AGR-2026-001 v1.0  
Issued and effective: 20 Jul 2026  
Issued by: Kenneth Townsend, Chief Financial Officer, Whitfield Components Ltd  
ENGAGEMENT AGREEMENT  
To: Whitfield Components Ltd and Holt & Reeves Advisory Ltd

## 1. Parties and term

Whitfield Components Ltd (Whitfield) engages Holt & Reeves Advisory Ltd (Holt) to perform the Japan Finance Phase 1 work described below. This agreement takes effect on 20 July 2026. The planned service period is 27 July to 16 October 2026, twelve weeks.

## 2. Scope and deliverables

The work concerns normal domestic supplier payments in Components, Packaging, Tooling, Maintenance and Facilities. Treasury's step 12 dual check remains in place. Payroll, tax, urgent manual transfers and Sales are excluded. Previous conditional approval to extend the process does not establish that the additional departments have completed transition.

**(a) Hand-off cards.** Holt will prepare a card for each of the five departments covering the responsible primary and deputy, step 07 authority, step 08 evidence and step 09 returns, including the reason for return. Where the necessary authority or receipt of evidence is not confirmed, the existing route remains in use.

**(b) Weekly queue sheet.** Holt will provide a sheet for the existing five departments using an authorised weekly CSV imported manually. The first eligible step 06 submission is the start point; resubmissions are recorded separately. Routine corrections to this deliverable are included. Live or daily automated connections are excluded.

**(c) Handover pack.** Holt will provide Japan Finance with the cards, the weekly sheet and instructions and outstanding confirmation items needed to take responsibility for them.

Historical reprocessing, additional departments and new automation are excluded. Corrections within the stated deliverables do not extend their scope; new work follows clause 4.

## 3. Responsibilities, client inputs and fee

Holt will prepare the deliverables and maintain the review evidence. Whitfield's Japan Finance team retains operational decisions and provides, by 24 July, the five departments' primary and deputy contacts, confirmation that both can process step 07 and receive evidence, and an authorised weekly CSV. Individual records remain in the Whitfield VDI; only authorised extracts may be taken outside it. If an input is missing, the affected department's route remains unchanged pending confirmation.

The fixed fee is JPY 42,800,000 excluding tax. Holt may invoice JPY 12,840,000 (30%) on commencement, JPY 17,120,000 (40%) on the W6 design review, and JPY 12,840,000 (30%) on W12 written acceptance. Applicable tax is additional.

## 4. Changes

A proposed new activity must describe its scope and the effect on fee and schedule. No work on it may begin until authorised representatives of both parties have approved the change in writing. A request or estimate alone is not approval.

## 5. Review and acceptance

At W12, Kenneth will review the five hand-off cards, the queue sheet reconcilable to the authorised weekly CSV, and the handover pack against clause 2. Whitfield will record acceptance in writing when all three deliverables satisfy those conditions. Outstanding items will be identified for correction and re-review; a review meeting alone is not written acceptance.
