# Whitfield | 07 deputy readiness checks

EVIDENCE NOTE

| Document | Date | Prepared by |
|---|---|---|

For Kenneth Townsend and Siobhan Barrett

## Walk through one complete bundle

Before we propose another week, ask the primary and the proposed deputy to work through a handover at 07. Start with the papers AP would send at 06: the invoice, evidence of the successful match and the confirmed cost-centre allocation. The allocation tells Kenneth which authority to check. Have the primary identify the bundle and its current status, then have the deputy take receipt and agree who owns any decision still outstanding. Use the existing route for any live item.

| Check | Who checks | Evidence to keep |
|---|---|---|
| Authority for this charge | Kenneth, with Japan Finance | Match the deputy and the cost centre on the bundle to the applicable approval authority record. Keep the authority reference and the charge allocation together. A rota entry supplies the person's name but does not establish authority for the charge. |
| Receipt by the deputy | Siobhan and Neha, with the deputy | Obtain the deputy's own acknowledgement. It should name the bundle, show when the deputy received it and confirm receipt of the invoice, match evidence and allocation. A send entry from the primary alone leaves receipt unconfirmed. |
| One owner for an unresolved bundle | Siobhan and Neha, with both roles | Retain a handover entry with the bundle reference, status, transfer time and the person responsible for the next action. Have both roles reconcile their lists to the same entry so the primary and deputy cannot each pick up the same outstanding decision. |

## When a record is missing

If one of the three checks lacks evidence, leave readiness open and record the gap and the person who will resolve it. Return any affected bundle to its current owner under the existing route. Where the bundle itself lacks a required document or allocation, AP holds it before 06 or takes the return from 07 with the reason recorded. Route it again when the required material is complete.

This check ends with the handover at 07. Use the readiness register for Japan Finance's separate receipt check at 08. File the evidence for Kenneth's consideration before another week is proposed.
