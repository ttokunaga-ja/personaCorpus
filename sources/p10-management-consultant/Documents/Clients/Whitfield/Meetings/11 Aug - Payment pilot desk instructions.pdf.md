# Payment pilot desk instructions

| Procedure | Issue date | Approved by |
|---|---|---|
| WCL-JP-FIN-PAY-2025-001 v1.0 | 11 Aug 2025 | Kenneth Townsend |

Issued by Whitfield Components Ltd, Japan Finance. For AP, Components, Packaging and Finance desks.

## Application and start

These instructions apply to standard domestic supplier payments for Components and Packaging. Payroll, tax and emergency manual transfers are outside the pilot. Begin the 07-side hand-off on 18 August only where primary and deputy cover and authority have been confirmed by 15 August. If a department cannot meet that condition, keep it on the current route.

The 09 hand-off change is subject to the separate 22 August confirmation of access to the 07 approval and exception-closure evidence. Until permission is given, use the existing 09 route. Do not move an in-progress file between routes merely because a later change is authorised.

## Processing and hand-off

| Step | Activity | Control and hand-off |
|---|---|---|
| 06 | AP routes for cost-centre approval | Assemble invoice, matching evidence and confirmed cost allocation. Record the first eligible circulation time and send the complete file to 07. Keep an incomplete file with AP. |
| 07 | Cost-centre decision | Check the charge against invoice, match and allocation. Record approval. Return missing or inconsistent information to AP with the reason; AP corrects and recirculates the complete file. |
| 08 | Japan Finance review | Check the recorded 07 approval and applicable exception-closure evidence. If missing or inaccessible, retain at 08 and request it from AP or the responsible desk. Do not send to 09 until complete. |
| 09 | Finance approval | Check the file and record approval. Return an incomplete file to 08, identifying the missing item; 08 owns follow-up and requests AP correction as needed. |
| 12 | Bank release authorisation | Retain Treasury reconciliation and two checks under WCL-JP-FIN-PAY-2024-001 v1.0. Corrected proposals require both checks again before authorisation. |

## Return records

Record the reason, receiving desk and time of each return and recirculation. Retain the original 06 time separately; do not overwrite it with the recirculation time. An item lacking required evidence is outside the relevant approval queue until corrected. Treasury release and settlement continue under the existing procedure.
