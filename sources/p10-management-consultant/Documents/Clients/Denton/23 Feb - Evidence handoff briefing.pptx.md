## Slide 2 — Record receipt; decide whether the evidence is usable
Layout: Two Columns

Accounts Payable: record when the supplier statement arrives, even if the account number is blank. Hold reconciliation; request a statement identifying the account, the month and month-end balance. Record the replacement and check it against the account before resuming.

Stock Finance: record the first arrival of the GRNI extract even if unsigned. Hold reconciliation and request a signed extract with the close date and receipt reference. Record the replacement separately; check those fields before resuming.

Speaker notes: Ask each desk to identify who records receipt and who will request a replacement. A receipt timestamp alone is not completion.

## Slide 4 — Before the pack goes round
Layout: Title and Content

- Each desk records receipt, checks the version, and records reconciliation separately.
- At the 15:00 same-day reconciliation window, leave an unresolved item on hold with an owner and next check.
- Tell the pack owner whether the item is complete or held before the 11:00 circulation window.
- Angela's pack approval is the end of the measured close; circulation is a separate event.

Speaker notes: Ask Angela and the desks whether the log makes the handoff visible. Do not fill in trial outcomes in this exercise.
