# Desk checks before the pack goes round

HRA-DEN-CLOSE-NTE-2026-001 v1.0 | 16 Mar 2026 | Nicholas Hargreaves
To: Angela Wilkins, Accounts Payable and Stock Finance, Denton Prepared Foods Ltd

## Purpose

Use these two checks at the next close. Record each receipt, validation and reconciliation separately in the two-line sheet. The February times below are an example of what happened, not the result of the next close. All times are GMT.

## Accounts Payable — supplier account

1. Record the first statement's receipt time and reference. Check that it is for the close month and the correct supplier account and contains the month-end balance.
2. If any required detail is absent or wrong, keep the first receipt in the record, hold reconciliation and request a corrected statement. Record the missing item, the person responsible and the next check time.
3. If the first statement meets all three requirements, record it as usable and proceed to reconciliation. Otherwise, record the replacement's receipt and reference; check all three requirements before recording it as usable and restarting reconciliation.
4. Reconcile the account. Record actual completion time and supporting reference, and tell the pack owner whether the work is complete or held.

In the trial the statement arrived on Friday D5 at 15:42, after the 15:00 same-day window. Reconciliation completed on Monday D6 at 10:25, before 11:00. The record does not establish a separate validation time.

## Stock Finance — inventory cutoff

1. Record the first GRNI (Goods Received Not Invoiced) extract's receipt time and reference. Check the close date, receipt reference and signature.
2. If any is missing or incorrect, retain the initial receipt, hold reconciliation, request the corrected extract and record the reason, owner and next check time.
3. If the first extract meets all three requirements, record it as usable and proceed to reconciliation. Otherwise record the replacement's receipt and reference; check the close date, receipt reference and signature before recording it as usable and restarting reconciliation.
4. Reconcile the cutoff, record actual completion time and supporting reference, and tell the pack owner whether it is complete or held.

In the trial the D6 09:18 extract lacked a signature. Stock Finance held it; the usable replacement arrived at 11:26 and reconciliation finished at 13:10. Both were after the 11:00 circulation window; the pack circulated on D7 at 10:37.

## Windows and communication

The D5 15:00 window is the same-day reconciliation cutoff. Evidence arriving after it carries to the next working day for the desk to validate and reconcile; arrival is not completion. If evidence is incomplete, continue the hold until a valid version is received and checked. On D6, by 11:00, each desk tells the pack owner either the completion time and reference or the hold reason, owner and next check time. If the item cannot be completed for circulation, make the risk explicit rather than marking it complete. Angela reviews the two lines at the next close.
