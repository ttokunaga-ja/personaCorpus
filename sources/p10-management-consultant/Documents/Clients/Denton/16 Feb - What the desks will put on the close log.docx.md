# What the desks will put on the close log
HRA-DEN-CLOSE-EV-2026-001 v1.0 | 16 Feb 2026 | Neha Patel
For Angela Wilkins, Accounts Payable and Stock Finance, Denton Prepared Foods Ltd

## Purpose
The February close log should let the pack owner see when evidence was received, when it became usable and when each reconciliation was completed. Record the source reference alongside each time. The examples below are for the teams to practise the entries before the close; they are not trial results.

## Accounts Payable — supplier account
1. Record the first statement's receipt time and source. Check that it identifies the close month and the correct supplier account and includes the month-end balance.
2. If any of those details is missing or wrong, retain the first receipt in the log, mark the reconciliation on hold and request the corrected statement. Enter the reason, owner and next follow-up time. Do not reconcile against the incomplete version.
3. On receipt of the replacement, record its time and reference separately. Check the month, account and balance again. Once all are present and match, record the time the usable statement was received and resume reconciliation.
4. Record the reconciliation completion time and supporting reference; tell the pack owner whether the item is complete or still held.

Example: a statement arrives at 09:00 without an account number. Log 09:00 and “account missing”; a corrected statement arrives at 10:30 with the month, account and balance. Log 10:30 as usable only after the checks, then record completion when the reconciliation is actually finished.

## Stock Finance — cutoff
1. Record the first GRNI extract receipt and its source. Check the close date and receipt references and that the extract is signed.
2. If the signature, date or references are absent or incorrect, keep the receipt in the history, record the hold and request a replacement. Do not treat that version as usable or start the reconciliation against it.
3. Record the replacement's receipt time and source. Check the date, references and signature together before resuming. Retain both versions' entries so that the first arrival cannot be mistaken for readiness.
4. Record the actual reconciliation completion time and reference, then notify the pack owner of completion or the reason for the continuing hold.

Example: an unsigned extract arrives at 09:15. Log receipt and hold for signature. A signed replacement arrives at 10:40; after checking the close date and receipt references, record it as usable. Completion is a separate event, entered only when the reconciliation is finished.

## Close log minimum fields
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| Desk and item | Accounts Payable / supplier account or Stock Finance / cutoff |
| First receipt | Date, GMT time and source reference, including rejected versions |
| Hold | Missing or incorrect detail, owner and next follow-up time |
| Usable version | Date, GMT time and reference after required checks |
| Reconciliation | Restart and completion times and supporting reference, when known |
| Pack communication | Status and time reported to pack owner |

A blank time means it has not been confirmed. The 15:00 evidence reconciliation window and 11:00 pack circulation deadline are separate; tell the pack owner about a hold rather than assuming that an initial receipt clears it.
