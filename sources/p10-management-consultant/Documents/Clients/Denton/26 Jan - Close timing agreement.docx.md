# One clock for both closes
HRA-DEN-CLOSE-MIN-2026-001 v1.0 | 26 Jan 2026 | Recorded by Nicholas Hargreaves

Attendees: Angela Wilkins, Connor Atkinson, Nicholas Hargreaves. For Denton Finance close owners.

## Decision
Count the first working day after the month end as D1. Exclude Saturdays, Sundays and England and Wales public holidays. Include the day Angela approves the close pack as the final day. Use the same boundary for December and the February trial. Calendar DPF-FIN-CAL-2026-001 v1.0 records 02 January as the December start and 14 January as the approval date; the February timetable targets 09 March, subject to the actual approval.

## Why the boundaries matter
The first journal may be posted before or after the first working day and can move independently of the close timetable. Using its timestamp as the start would change the interval between months. Pack circulation or assembly is a hand-off, not approval: taking it as the end would omit the time Angela needs to review and approve. Keep both events in the record, but do not substitute either for approval.
