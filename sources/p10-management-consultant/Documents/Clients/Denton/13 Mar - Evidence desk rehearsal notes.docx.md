# At the two evidence desks

HRA-DEN-CLOSE-EV-2026-002 v1.0 | 13 Mar 2026 | Neha Patel

For Angela Wilkins, Accounts Payable and Stock Finance, Denton Prepared Foods Ltd

## Purpose and scope

This note records the walk-through of the two checks before the next close. It uses the February trial to establish what the desk needs to see and what it should record when it cannot proceed. It is a check of the hand-off, not a result for the next month.

## Accounts Payable — supplier account

The desk traced the statement to the close month and supplier account, then checked that the month-end balance was present before reconciling it. A statement for the wrong month or account, or without the closing balance, cannot release the reconciliation. Record the receipt, identify the missing or incorrect item, and ask for the appropriate statement. Leave the line on hold with the responsible desk and the next check time; record the usable replacement separately when it arrives.

In February the statement arrived on Friday 6 March at 15:42, after the 15:00 same-day reconciliation window. Reconciliation completed on Monday 9 March at 10:25, before the 11:00 pack circulation window. The late receipt crossed a working-day boundary, but this reconciliation was complete before the Monday pack window.

## Stock Finance — inventory cut-off

The desk checked the GRNI (Goods Received Not Invoiced) extract for the close date, receipt reference and signature. Each is needed before reconciliation resumes. An unsigned extract is a receipt, not a usable hand-off. Record it and retain the hold until a signed extract with the correct date and receipt reference arrives. The desk records the owner and next check time against the hold; it then records the replacement receipt and reconciliation completion separately.

On Monday 9 March the first extract arrived at 09:18 without a signature. The usable replacement arrived at 11:26 and reconciliation completed at 13:10. Both were after the 11:00 pack window. The pack went round on Tuesday 10 March at 10:37.

## Walk-through outcome

| Desk | Check tried | Result and record to carry forward |
|---|---|---|
| Accounts Payable | Match close month and account; check month-end balance on statement | A missing or mismatched item stays on hold. The line has space for the reason, owner and next check time; do not record reconciliation as complete until the statement is usable and the reconciliation is done. |
| Stock Finance | Check close date, receipt reference and signature on GRNI extract | An unsigned or incomplete extract stays on hold. The same hold fields identify who will check again and when. Receipt of a replacement and completion are separate events. |

For each desk, report either completion or the hold and its next check time to the pack owner by 11:00 on the circulation day. Angela can review the two lines at the next close; the February timestamps remain the observed example, not next month's entries.
