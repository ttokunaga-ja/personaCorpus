| What the receiving lead needs | Both W1 observations ended within the originating shift. Gareth and Martin to arrange the brief with Julie and Helen. |
| Approval and return | The receiving shift lead matches the sheets and tag to the job instruction and records the specification check and approval time. The lead attends the actual move to good stock and signs and writes the time of that move on the tag. Approval and actual return may occur at different times. Record each at its own time. | Each receiving lead to keep the two entries traceable to the same run and event from 21 October. |
| Stop conditions | A box with a missing tag, a mismatch or no approval stays on the hold rack. The receiving lead flags the reason and follows it back to the source records before considering release. Receipt of the box leaves the hold open. | Gareth and Martin to review unresolved boxes with the shift leads at the weekly ticket check, including any cases passed on without enough information. |

## Boundary of the change

MCP-OPS-STD-2024-014 Rev 3 still governs the three-sheet check and lead approval after changeover or jam restart. Keep the two loss weights separate. The trial change covers boxes that cross a shift while still held. Keep the 17 October evidence gap open and use the new method prospectively from 21 October.
