## Slide 4 — Process Figure

Title: The tag stays with the box across the shift

Figure: Draw five stages from left to right. The same box and tag continue through the stages. Keep the hold shelf and good-stock area visibly separate.

| Stage | Location | Box and record action |
|---|---|---|
| 1 · Send | Changeover bench | Identify the first three sheets. Record line, run, event, specification and sending shift. Tie the HOLD tag to the box. |
| 2 · Hold | Hold shelf | Set the box apart from good stock. Show the incoming lead the box and name the check still due. |
| 3 · Receive | Hold shelf | Incoming lead records receipt. The box and tag stay on the shelf. |
| 4 · Check and approve | At the held box | Match the tag and all three sheets to the job instruction. The receiving lead records approval and its time when the check passes. |
| 5 · Return | Good-stock area | At the actual move, the receiving lead attends, signs and records the actual return date and time. Post once against the original run and event. |

Connect stages 1–5 in order. Label the arrow from stage 4 to stage 5 “Approved · actual move to good stock”. Loop from stage 4 to stage 2 with “Tag missing, specification differs or approval outstanding · keep on hold and tell the line coordinator”.
