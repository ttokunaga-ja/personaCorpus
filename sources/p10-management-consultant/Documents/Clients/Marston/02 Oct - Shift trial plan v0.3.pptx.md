## Slide 3 — Process Figure
Title: Hold the first sheets until the lead approves release

Figure: Flow from left to right: “Stop and clear the jam” → “Restart and hold the first three sheets” → “Match all three to the job instruction and specification” → “Shift lead approves; write approval time and approver” → “Release to good output; record restart loss separately”. From the comparison box, branch on “Check fails or approval unavailable” to “Keep output on hold, notify the lead and record the issue”. After correction, arrow back to the three-sheet check. Changeover callout: “Use the same hold, match and approval sequence after changeover. Post the weight to the changeover field.”

Caption: MCP-OPS-STD-2024-014 Rev 3. Use the two recording fields for losses to the respective approvals.

Speaker notes: Rev 3 already covers both events. Walk the leads through the order in which the entries would be made, especially when the restart straddles a shift handover. Release follows lead approval.

## Slide 4 — Evidence Table
Title: What the next shift needs to be able to trace

| Common field | Entry at both sites | When evidence is incomplete |
|---|---|---|
| Shift and run | Site, line, date, shift, run reference, B420 and S3 | Flag the missing reference or scope check |
| Weights | Input kg and good output kg from the linked records | Leave unavailable values blank and name the record to check |
| Losses | Separate changeover kg and restart kg | Trace a combined entry back to its weights and event times before allocating |
| Release | First-off approval time and approving shift lead at changeover and at restart | Keep output on hold where approval is pending; query any missing entry |
| Handover | Recorder and a reference to the open issue or source | Pass the open item to the incoming lead |

Speaker notes: Confirm which records supply each field and who can reopen them. A blank field can mean an event did not occur or that something is unconfirmed. Ask the recorder to verify which applies and document it before the weekly review.
