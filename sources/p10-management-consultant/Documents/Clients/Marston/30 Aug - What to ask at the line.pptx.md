## Slide 2 — Two Columns

Title: Changeover — pick one job and follow it

Left — Ask the shift lead

- Can you take me through the last changeover on this line?
- Which job was coming off and which one was starting?
- When did the changeover begin, and where did you put the board lost before first-off approval?

Right — Check in the record

- Match the changeover entry to the job, board grade and box specification.
- Find the start-up loss weight for that run.
- If a time cannot be established, enter “Unconfirmed”.

Speaker notes: Use an actual job so that the lead can show us an entry. At handover, ask each lead the same questions and record who saw which part of the sequence.

## Slide 3 — Two Columns

Title: First-off — find the point of release

Left — Ask the shift lead

- Where do the first three sheets go while they are being checked?
- What do you compare them with, and who authorises release to good output?
- If one fails the check, what happens to the output and when is the check repeated?

Right — Check in the record

- Find the job instruction and the first-off check for that run.
- Look for shift-lead approval and its time. Check whether the record puts approval before entry into good output.
- Mark a missing release or approval time “Unconfirmed”.

Speaker notes: Rev 3 requires the first three sheets to be compared with the job instruction and approved specification, then approved by the shift lead. Keep the description of the check, the approval entry and any direct observation separately attributable.

## Slide 4 — Two Columns

Title: Restart — trace the sheets and the loss after a jam

Left — Ask the shift lead

- Walk me through the last restart after a jam. What happened to the first three sheets?
- Who approved them before release to good output?
- Where did you enter the weight lost between restart and approval?

Right — Check in the record

- Locate the restart and shift-lead approval for the same run.
- Follow the restart loss to its own field. The start-up field covers changeover.
- Leave any time we cannot establish as “Unconfirmed”.

Speaker notes: Rev 3 requires the same three-sheet check after a jam. If the two shifts describe a different handover, keep both accounts and ask where the underlying entries can be found. One example gives us a sequence to verify.
