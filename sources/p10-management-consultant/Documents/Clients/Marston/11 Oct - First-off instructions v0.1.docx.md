# First-off instructions for the morning shift

| Document | Date | Prepared by |
|---|---|---|
| HRA-MAR-COST-EV-2024-002 v0.1 | 11 Oct 2024 | Siobhan Barrett |

**To:** Gareth Pritchard, Trafford T2, and Martin Devlin, Warrington W1  
**For:** B420 / S3 trial from 14 October  
**Production standard:** MCP-OPS-STD-2024-014 Rev 3

## At a B420 / S3 changeover

Use the new job instruction at the changeover bench. Keep the preceding job's sheets clear of the three first-off sheets for the new run. Complete the check and record the lead's decision during the morning shift.

| Step | At the bench | On the run record |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Check that the new job calls for B420 board and the S3 box specification. | Identify the shift, run and changeover under the new job. |
| 2 | Put the first three sheets together in a separate box and keep them clear of good output. Compare each sheet with the job instruction and approved specification. | Keep the job instruction with the run details so the lead can make the same check. |
| 3 | Present the box and the check to the shift lead. If a sheet does not match, hold the output, put the setting right and bring a fresh set of three for checking. | Note the reason if approval is pending. The lead records their approval and the time in the changeover entry once the check passes. |
| 4 | Put the approved sheets into good output after the lead has made the approval entry. | Use the changeover loss field for the loss to first-off approval. Check the kg against the weight record. |

## After clearing a jam

At restart, hold the first three sheets and repeat the check and lead approval before release. Keep the restart approval and its time in the restart entry. Use the restart loss field for the weight from restart to its approval, separate from the earlier changeover loss.

Before filing the morning record, check that the approval points to the right event and the same weight has only been entered once. Ask the lead to resolve any unclear weight against the source record.
