# Shift handover exercise

## Slide 1 — Cover

Title: Shift handover exercise

Subtitle: For the Trafford T2 and Warrington W1 shift leads · 14 Nov 2024 · Siobhan Barrett · HRA-MAR-COST-BRF-2024-010 · v1.0

Speaker notes: T2 runs on 14 November in two groups before handover so the line stays covered. W1 runs on 15 November with Martin and Helen together. Use the exercise papers at both sites. Set the held box back on the shelf and give the receiver a fresh station sheet between rounds.

## Slide 2 — Evidence Table

Title: Set up the handover

| Role | What to do | What to have ready |
|---|---|---|
| Sending lead | Pass on the held box and say what is still open. Leave the tag attached. | One box, three first-off sheets and hold tag EX-04 |
| Receiving lead | Take the box, check what has been handed over and record what you decide. | Attached tag, job instruction for the round, blank station sheet |
| Shift lead observing | Watch the box from handover to the receiver's decision. | Hold shelf and a separate place representing good stock |
| Recorder | Write down what happened and where the receiver paused. | Observation sheet and pen |
| Facilitator | Hand over one instruction at a time and keep the second out of view. | Two exercise job instructions and spare station sheets |

Speaker notes: Use three B420 / S3 exercise sheets, with B420 / S3 written on EX-04 as the sending identification. Prepare instruction A for B420 / S3 and instruction B for B420 / S4. Keep the box and sheets the same between rounds. Point out the shelf and the good-stock position. At receipt ask “What have you just done?” and wait for the receiver to describe taking custody. Give no cue to sign.

## Slide 3 — Evidence Table

Title: Work the two handovers

| Round | At the bench | Record before moving on |
|---|---|---|
| A | EX-04, three sheets and the first job instruction | Write down what you checked and what you did with the box. |
| B | EX-04, the same three sheets and a second job instruction | Make a fresh check and record the box's position and who needs to know. |

Speaker notes: Run A first. Pass the matching instruction quietly. Let the receiver complete the check and record the decision. If the receiver asks to move the box, allow a pause after approval so they can enter the return time when they actually move it. Reset the box on the hold shelf. In B, substitute the instruction for the different specification without drawing attention to it. Wait for the receiver to choose what to do and complete the station sheet. Hold both answers until the two records have been collected.

## Slide 4 — Evidence Table

Title: What did you see at the handover?

| Observer | Look for |
|---|---|
| Shift lead | Which details did the receiver compare on the tag, the sheets and the instruction? Where was the box when the receiver made the decision? |
| Recorder | Which field did the receiver reach for first? Where did they put receipt, approval time, signature and actual return time? |
| Both | If the box stayed on hold, did the tag stay with it? Who would be told and what would they need to resolve? |

Speaker notes: Ask the receiver to talk through A and then B from their own entries before showing either answer. Reveal A first: the instruction, tag and three sheets match, so the receiving lead can record approval and its time. At the actual move to good stock, the same lead attends, signs and records the actual return date and time. Then reveal B: the box is S3 and the instruction asks for S4. Leave it tagged on the hold shelf, tell the line coordinator which specification differs and leave the approval, return signature and return time blank. If someone reached for the signature at receipt, replay the handover before finishing. Keep observations with the station sheets.
