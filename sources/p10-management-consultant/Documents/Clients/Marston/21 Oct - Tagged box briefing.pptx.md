Title: Handing over a tagged first-off box

Speaker notes: Start with Gareth, Julie, Martin and Helen. The new step is for a first-off box still held when the shift changes. The three-sheet check and lead approval under MCP-OPS-STD-2024-014 Rev 3 continue on both lines.

## Slide 2 — Process Figure

Title: Follow the three sheets and their tag

Figure: Five stages with a box bearing the same attached HOLD tag in each stage. Draw the first four stages from left to right, with the good-stock stage beyond the receiving check. Place “Outgoing shift” above stages 1 and 2, “Change of shift” above stage 3, and “Receiving shift” above stages 4 and 5.

| Stage | Location | Label inside the stage |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Changeover bench | Place three first-off sheets in a separate box. Write the run, event and specification on the HOLD tag and attach it. |
| 2 | Hold rack | Set the box apart from good output. Leave the tag on the box. |
| 3 | Handover at the rack | Identify the box to the incoming lead. Keep the box and tag together on the rack. |
| 4 | Receiving check | Match the tag and all three sheets to the job instruction. Record the lead's approval and time when the check passes. |
| 5 | Good-stock area | At the actual return, the receiving lead signs and writes the return time. |

Connect stages 1 through 4. Label the arrow from stage 4 to 5 “Approved and actually moved”. Add a return arrow from stage 4 to stage 2 labelled “Tag missing, details differ or approval pending · keep on hold and tell the line coordinator”. The box remains on the rack while waiting for the good-stock move.

Caption: The HOLD tag follows the same box until the return is signed and timed.

Speaker notes: Point along the first three stages without moving the box off the rack at handover. At the check stage, ask the leads which job instruction they will use. Trace the loop back to hold for a missing tag, a specification mismatch or an outstanding approval. Gareth or Martin can follow up the source ticket.

## Slide 4 — Headline and Body

Title: Approval at 08:10, returned at 08:15

Body:
- At 08:10, Gareth records the specification match and approval. The box remains held while it is still on the rack.
- At 08:15, he attends the move to good stock, signs the return and enters 08:15 as the actual return time.
- Update the original run and event under the same tag. One box gives one recorded return, even when two shifts touch its record.
- Keep any unmatched or unsigned return open for the line coordinator. Keep changeover and restart losses in separate fields under Rev 3.

Speaker notes: Ask the receiving lead what they would write if they approved at 08:10 but did not move the box before their next task. The return signature and time stay blank until the move. Remind both lines to name open boxes at handover and keep the box separate from good output while a check is outstanding.
