Present **Caroline Wood** (Marston Production Director), **Nicholas Hargreaves**, **Siobhan Barrett** and **Connor Atkinson** (Holt & Reeves Advisory Ltd)

## Discussion and decisions

| Item | Discussion and decision | Follow-up |
|---|---|---|
| Scope and timing | Siobhan opened with the 05 Aug choice of Trafford T2 and Warrington W1. Both run B420 / S3 and can reconcile input and good weight. Moving the visits to 17 and 18 Sep let the team speak to both shifts. The 30 Sep slot became an interim check and the final readout moved by one week to today. | Diagnosis readout complete. |
| Yield and price | Connor showed 88.0% at Trafford and 92.0% at Warrington, each from 12 July runs and 30,000 kg of input. In questions we confirmed that the 2.4 points apply to the 720 kg changeover and restart comparison only. The full difference is 4.0 points and the trial has yet to measure recovery. The £0.09/kg change adds £2,700 at the same weight, with no effect on either yield. | Keep the fixed-weight price illustration separate from any trial result. Use measured recovery and eligible throughput before attempting a monthly tonnage estimate. |
| Trafford feeder | Siobhan showed £384,000 initial outlay and £60,000 annual net benefit, giving a 6.4-year simple payback. The volume basis is T2 net B420 input across specifications, rounded from 4.018 to 4.0 million kg for a comparable next year. Additional recovery of 2.0% is a modelling assumption. Caroline agreed to hold the equipment option at its present stage. | No additional quotation. Retain the economics for reference if Marston reopens the case. |
| Standard first | Siobhan proposed recording the existing Rev 3 first-piece checks and both loss categories consistently for four weeks. Caroline agreed to consider common standards first and said she would name a shift lead at each plant before setting a trial date. | Caroline to name a lead for T2 and one for W1 before the start date is agreed. Siobhan to agree recorders and weekly reconciliation with the named leads. |
