## Slide 2 — Two Columns

Title: Read the front at sending and the back at receipt

Left — Front / Sending shift

1. Identify the first three sheets after changeover against the job instruction. Keep them clear of good stock.
2. Put the three in a box, write the run and sending shift on the hold tag and tie it to the box.
3. Put the box on the hold shelf. At shift change, tell the incoming lead what is still open and hand over the box with the tag on it.

Right — Back / Receiving shift

1. Take custody and record receipt. Match the attached tag and all three sheets to the job instruction.
2. Where the match cannot be confirmed, leave the box on the hold shelf and tell the line coordinator.
3. Lead stamp: return to good stock [*]
4. At the actual move to good stock, sign and record the actual return time.

[*] Wording to change before issue.

Speaker notes: Mark the stamp line on the mock-up so Caroline can find it immediately. A reader could take it as sufficient authority to move the box without completing the check or making the separate return record. Read the next slide with the back of the card in view.
