# What the shifts said and what we saw

| Document | Date | Prepared by |
|---|---|---|
| HRA-MAR-COST-EV-2024-001 v1.0 | 19 Sep 2024 | Neha Patel |

To Nicholas Hargreaves and Siobhan Barrett

## The check we took to both sites

Oliver and I spoke with the outgoing and incoming shift leads at Trafford T2 on 17 September and Warrington W1 on 18 September. Caroline's production standard, MCP-OPS-STD-2024-014 Rev 3, has been effective since 01 Aug 2024. After a changeover or a jam restart, the first three sheets are held and checked against the job specification. The shift lead approves them before release to good output and records the approval time. The loss record has separate fields for changeover and restart.

## Trafford T2 | 17 Sep, 14:26 BST

**What the leads told us.** The team checks the first three sheets at restart as it does at changeover.

**What we saw.** When the jam was cleared at 14:26, sheets went into good output before the three-sheet check was complete. The release sequence in this instance differed from the account given at the handover.

**What the records supported.** In the first-off section of the shift record, Oliver found no separate restart approval entry for the event. The same run's loss record included the restart loss in the preceding setup entry. There was no separate restart posting. The absence of an approval entry cannot identify an approver or supply an approval time. No photographs were permitted at Trafford. To reopen the check, use the run identified by the 14:26 restart in the T2 shift record and follow it to the setup and restart fields.

## Warrington W1 | 18 Sep, 15:04 BST

**What the leads told us.** The three-sheet check and shift lead approval apply on restart as they do at changeover.

**What we saw.** On the 15:04 recovery from a jam, the first output was held while three sheets were checked. The shift lead approved release before the output went into the good stream.

**What the records supported.** The shift record shows the approval for that restart. The run loss record contains a separate restart entry. The observation supplies the sequence, and the entries corroborate the approval and separate posting for this run.

## Use in the diagnosis

Each visit yielded one observed restart. Keep both cases with the September field evidence. They give us two sequences to examine and no basis for estimating an adherence rate, assigning personal responsibility or explaining the weight gap across July. Connor should reconcile the July weight logs on their own terms, tracing any combined loss entry to its event window before allocating the weight. Siobhan can use these examples to check whether both sites can make approval and separate loss entries visible at handover.
