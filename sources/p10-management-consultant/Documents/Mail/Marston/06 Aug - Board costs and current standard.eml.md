As agreed yesterday, I’ve attached the June and July board-cost extract and the current work standard for Trafford T2 and Warrington W1.

Connor, the CSV is from our purchase lines for the two sites under the same price contract. The weights are kg purchased and the costs are net material spend in pounds. It leaves out purchases under other contracts, so it cannot give you all board bought for T2 or the amount of B420 that actually went through the feeder.

For sizing the feeder option, I pulled these figures this morning, 6 August, from the Trafford T2 production issues and returns ledger. These are net kg of B420 fed through that feeder across all box specifications. I’ve taken out returns and counted board that went back through the feeder only once.

Aug–Oct 2023: 952,000 kg
Nov 2023–Jan 2024: 1,067,000 kg
Feb–Apr 2024: 987,000 kg
May–Jul 2024: 1,012,000 kg
Twelve months to 31 Jul 2024: 4,018,000 kg

If T2 runs at much the same level next year, use 4,000,000 kg for the annual illustration, rounded to the nearest 100,000 kg. The July B420 runs to S3 specification you’ll compare are part of that wider stream.

Neha, please use MCP-OPS-STD-2024-014 Rev 3 when you speak to the shift leads. It’s the version in force from 1 August. It calls for the first three sheets to be checked and approved at both changeover and restart after a jam, with the two loss weights entered separately.
