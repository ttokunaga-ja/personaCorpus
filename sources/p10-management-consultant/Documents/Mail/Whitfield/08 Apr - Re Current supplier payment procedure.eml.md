Thank you for the procedure. We will follow the 14 steps from receipt through settlement reconciliation and test the entry question against the time evidence. In particular, we will distinguish time spent entering and doing other work from time an item waits for the next person; a long elapsed interval alone will not tell us which it is.

Neha will speak to the people involved about the hand-offs, including what must be available before an item can go forward and what happens when it is returned. Connor will check the available timestamps against those accounts and establish where we can measure the intervals reliably. We will examine approval waiting first, while keeping the existing checks in view. Let us leave any discussion of a system update until we understand the route and the evidence.

I will come back to you with what we find.
