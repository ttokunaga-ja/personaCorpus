## Evidence needed for a first-year amount

A first-year amount needs an approved start date and a comparable scope and count, together with observed work time and actual approved overtime premium paid over the relevant post-start periods. The proposed operational baseline is the four weeks before start, subject to agreement; it is separate from the July 2023–June 2024 closed quarterly totals used in the annualised estimate. Connor's 18 December measurement record leaves monthly results blank until they are observed. A nine-month planning horizon alone supplies no monthly accrual profile, so I have not apportioned the annual estimate.
