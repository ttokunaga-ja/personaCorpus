The 03 entry intervals total 19.0 hours. Removing that result would make it look as though we did not examine Kenneth's concern. Entry may still have room for improvement; the evidence does not establish that improving it would be pointless. For this diagnostic, 07 and 09 together account for 560 of the 610 approval-wait hours, so I propose examining those hand-offs first. This is a priority decision, not a claim of a future saving.
