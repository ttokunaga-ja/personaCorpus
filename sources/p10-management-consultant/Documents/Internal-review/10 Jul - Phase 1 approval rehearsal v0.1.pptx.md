## Slide 3 — A weekly sheet is not a live feed
Layout: Two Columns

Left — Objection card: “Can you connect the report to a new system so we see it daily?”

Right — Response: The proposed queue sheet uses an authorised weekly CSV, manually supplied. Viewing an existing report daily is different from automatically producing new data daily. A new connection needs a separate change decision; it is not included in the weekly sheet.

Speaker notes: After the answer, read draft clause 2(b) for the weekly output and clause 4 for the change process. Ask whether the customer intends frequency of viewing or a new automated feed.

## Slide 4 — Correction or change?
Layout: Two Columns

Left — Objection card: “If a label is wrong, will you ask us to approve and pay for a change?”

Right — Response: Correcting the same agreed deliverable is within the fee. Adding a new input or function is new work. Clause 4 requires written approval of its scope, fee and schedule impact by authorised people on both sides before starting.
