# Put the gate in the agreement

HRA-WHI-DIAG-REV-2025-004 v1.0 | 07 Oct 2025 | Prepared by Nicholas Hargreaves  
To: Joanne Hill and Elizabeth Hargreaves  
Subject: Whitfield payment pilot — scope and conditional commencement

## Advice requested

Joanne and Elizabeth, please review the proposed scope-change wording before it goes to Kenneth. The desk instruction WCL-JP-FIN-PAY-2025-001 v1.0 covers Components and Packaging. I propose retaining that basis and naming only Tooling, Maintenance and Facilities as the additional departments. This changes the pilot's application; the additional departments have not yet been measured.

## Proposed clauses

| Point | Proposed wording | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Scope | Add Tooling, Maintenance and Facilities to the existing Components and Packaging pilot scope. | Names the three additions rather than an open-ended extension. |
| Readiness gate | Japan Finance confirms by 17 October, for each additional department, a primary and deputy, their authority and ability to receive required evidence. | Each department needs an accountable recipient and access to the 07 approval and applicable exception-resolution evidence. |
| Commencement | From 20 October the changed hand-off applies only to additional departments whose readiness is confirmed. An unconfirmed department continues on the existing route until it meets the condition. | Avoids implying all five automatically start the changed route on the same date. |
| Existing control | Treasury's double check at 12 remains under the existing procedure. | The hand-off change removes no control. |
| Commercial scope | Additional fees and a subsequent contract are outside this change. | They need separate consideration and are not part of this decision. |

## Points to settle

Joanne: please check the conditional commencement, department-by-department fallback and reference to the existing procedure. Elizabeth: please confirm the limit and whether the decision paper should show the readiness gate alongside the request. I will incorporate your comments before the client decision; this note does not itself approve the extension.
