# What counts as delivery

HRA-WHI-DIAG-REV-2026-001 v0.4 | 19 Jun 2026 | Prepared by Nicholas Hargreaves; reviewed by Joanne Hill

To: Nicholas Hargreaves and Elizabeth Hargreaves

## Purpose

Please review these candidate schedule clauses against Siobhan's three proposed Finance outputs before we issue terms to Kenneth. The distinction between ordinary corrections to a defined deliverable and new work needs to be explicit.

## Candidate wording

**2. Services and deliverables.** Holt will provide: (a) a hand-off card for each of the five named departments, recording the 07 approval, 08 evidence and 09 return responsibilities, authority and evidence receipt; (b) a weekly queue sheet for those departments using an authorised weekly CSV imported manually; and (c) a handover pack for Japan Finance. Ordinary corrections within these deliverables are included. New inputs, functions or work beyond the defined outputs require the change procedure in clause 4. Sales and live production connections are excluded.

**4. Changes.** A proposed change will describe the new scope and its effects on fees and timetable. Neither party may start the additional work until authorised representatives of both parties have approved the change in writing.

**5. Completion.** At W12 Kenneth will review the five hand-off cards, the queue sheet reconcilable to the authorised weekly CSV and the handover pack against clause 2. Acceptance will be recorded in writing when the three deliverables meet those conditions; outstanding items will be corrected and re-reviewed.

## Points for review

| Clause | Question | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | Are the five departmental cards, manual weekly CSV sheet and handover pack identified sufficiently? | Each output needs its own evidence of completion. |
| 2 / 4 | Does the distinction between a correction and a new input or function hold? | A label correction stays within the sheet; a new connection changes the work and dependencies. |
| 4 | Can the authorised representatives approve the scope, fee and timetable impact in writing before work starts? | Discussion of impact alone is not authority to proceed. |
| 5 | Can Kenneth inspect the three outputs and their evidence at W12? | A review meeting alone is not written acceptance. |

Please mark the candidate language and identify the intended boundary. This version is for internal discussion, not an agreed contract.
