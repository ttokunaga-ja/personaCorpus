# What counts as delivery

HRA-WHI-DIAG-REV-2026-001 v0.3 | 17 Jun 2026 | Prepared by Nicholas Hargreaves

To: Joanne Hill and Elizabeth Hargreaves

## Purpose

Please review these candidate schedule clauses against Siobhan's three proposed Finance outputs before we issue terms to Kenneth. The phrases below are deliberately still in the draft: I cannot tell from them where routine corrections stop and new work begins.

## Candidate wording

**2. Services and deliverables.** Holt will provide hand-off materials for Japan Finance, a weekly queue report and transition materials, together with ongoing reporting support and other Japan requests as agreed.

**4. Changes.** The parties may agree changes to the services, fees and timetable. Where additional work is requested, the parties will discuss its impact before it is undertaken.

**5. Completion.** The parties will review the deliverables at the end of the engagement and confirm completion against the agreed scope.

## Points for review

| Clause | Question | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | How should we identify the five departmental 07/08/09 hand-off cards, the weekly CSV queue sheet and the transition pack? | The three outputs have different evidence of completion. “ongoing reporting support” could be read more broadly than an ordinary correction to the same weekly sheet. |
| 2 / 4 | Which words distinguish correcting a column label or an error in that sheet from adding an input or a new function? | The latter can change the work and its dependencies; the present phrase “other Japan requests as agreed” does not draw that boundary. |
| 4 | Should a separate approval be required before additional work starts, and if so by whom and in what form? | Discussion of impact alone may not establish authority to proceed. |
| 5 | What evidence should Kenneth inspect to decide whether each output has been delivered? | A general completion statement leaves the acceptance basis unclear. |

Please mark the candidate language and identify the intended boundary. This version is for internal discussion, not an agreed contract.
