# The forecast tier is a scenario

17 Dec 2025

The October S018 plan assumes 12,500 cases, above the 12,000 net-case threshold. On that assumption the written rate is $18.40 per case, against a $20.00 baseline. The plan saving is therefore 12,500 × $1.60 = $20,000.

For a like-for-like price bridge, suppose actual net purchases were 10,000 cases. This is a scenario, not a result for Q4. Reprice those same 10,000 cases at the forecast tier: 10,000 × ($20.00 − $18.40) = $16,000. Below the threshold, the written rate is $19.60 and the same 10,000 cases would save $4,000. The difference is $12,000, or 10,000 × ($19.60 − $18.40).

Comparing the original $20,000 plan directly with $4,000 would mix the effect of 2,500 fewer cases with the price-tier effect. Keep the volume change outside the price bridge. The $18.40 is a conditional rate, not an entitlement on purchases below the threshold; confirm returns and the net count before drawing a conclusion.
