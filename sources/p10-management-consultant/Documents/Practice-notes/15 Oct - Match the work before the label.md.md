# Match the work before the label

15 Oct 2024

The first join looked reassuring because the total still came to JPY 26m. It matched department and item names, though, and AP's “exception document packs” never met Finance's “handover preparation”. Different labels were enough to hide a candidate overlap.

For the next review, start with the original row references and put the periods alongside each other. Then trace the 2,000 EXC-PACK count to the four closed quarters, rather than scaling up a few examples. Ask what the 30 minutes covers, check the JPY 4,000 hourly value and identify who does the work. Follow an identifier across the handover: is the second team making another set of documents or receiving the same set?

Connor's traces of EP-184, EP-219 and EP-267 show the same packs prepared by AP and received by Finance. We found no record of a separate preparation for Finance. Both old lines value the same work; retain AP model!D14 and remove Finance model!D11, JPY 4m a year. The three examples show identity, not the annual quantity.

Next time record the evidence for the same work and the line retained together. Matching amounts alone are not grounds to delete a benefit. Keep the remaining capacity value apart from the conditional overtime premium, and don't call either a realised first-year saving.
