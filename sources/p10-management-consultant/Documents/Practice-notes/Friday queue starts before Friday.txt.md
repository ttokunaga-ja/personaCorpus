Connor's intervals run on elapsed time, including nights and weekends. I considered subtracting every weekend, but that would impose one availability pattern on AP, cost-centre owners, Finance and Treasury. We do not have evidence that the same hours are available to each role. Leave the elapsed intervals intact and say so when presenting them.

The hours are summed across cases that can overlap. Do not turn the total into calendar days or read it as an individual's utilisation. A long interval tells us where to ask about the hand-off; it does not say that someone was working or available for every hour of it.
