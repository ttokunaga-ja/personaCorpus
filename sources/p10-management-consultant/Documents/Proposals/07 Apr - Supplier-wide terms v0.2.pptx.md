## Slide 2 — Headline and Body
**A single request could establish the terms currently offered**
- Ask all 42 suppliers for current prices and their quantity conditions.
- Buying would sort replies, identify gaps and bring comparable terms back for review.
- This reaches the whole base, but relies on the same Buying staff who handle existing exception requests.

Speaker notes: The attraction is breadth. We have not established whether the replies can be compared or whether Buying has capacity to handle them alongside its current work.

## Slide 4 — Two Columns
**What this option asks for — and what it cannot yet claim**
Left: A current price submission and an explanation of quantity conditions from each supplier. Buying groups the responses and flags terms needing clarification.
Right: No quantity commitment has been established. There is no evidence for a unit-price effect, and no basis to quote a saving. Do not set a negotiation start date.

Speaker notes: Before requesting terms, agree the purpose and the burden on Buying. Current price submissions alone will not establish an achievable reduction.
