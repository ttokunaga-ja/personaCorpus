# Put every open price back to Buying
HRA-PIO-BUY-BRF-2025-009 • v0.1 • 05 Dec 2025 • Nicholas Hargreaves • For Elizabeth Hargreaves

## Slide 1 — Title
Layout: Title
Put every open price back to Buying
Pioneer | Options for a Q4 evidence request
Speaker notes: An early option for discussion, not an instruction to Buying.

## Slide 2 — One bundle makes all five wait
Layout: Two Content
Left: Re-query all five conditions together. One response would cover quantity, effective date and missing commercial evidence. Buying would need to retrieve and reconcile every line before returning the bundle; a slow approval on one line could delay the other four.
Right: Ask only where the decision depends on evidence. Retain the five-condition comparison, but request receipt dates for S036, pack conversion for S042 and original-document links or approval for credits. Track each response independently.
Speaker notes: Neither route establishes the quarter's result before the receipt and posting cutoffs.

## Slide 3 — Evidence needed to resolve each question
Layout: Evidence Table
| Question | Evidence to request | Decision supported |
|---|---|---|
| S018 return | Quantity-bearing credit and original receipt key | Whether a linked return reduces net cases |
| S042 unit | Pack description and cases per pack | Whether quantities and prices are comparable |
| S036 effective date | Receipt and invoice dates on the same line | Old or new rate by receipt date |
| S011 additional allowance | Separate approval and credit record | Whether any extra credit is supportable |
Speaker notes: Keep the S008 fixed price in the comparison without opening a fresh negotiation absent a specific issue.

## Slide 4 — Recommendation for the request
Layout: Title and Content
Do not reopen all five prices for renegotiation before year end. Ask Buying for the unresolved receipt, conversion and credit evidence; preserve the signed quantity and the source reference. Reassess the gaps after the quarter closes rather than treating incomplete evidence as a supplier failure.
Speaker notes: Elizabeth's decision is whether to narrow the request. This option has not been adopted.
