20 July — correction or extra?

A column label wrong on the same weekly queue sheet: correct the deliverable within the fee. Do not call that a new connection.

Adding a new destination to connect to the report is different work. Take scope, price and timetable impact through clause 4; no start until the authorised people at both firms approve in writing.

Ask Kenneth two separate questions:
1. Does Finance need to look at the report every day? A weekly manually supplied CSV can still be viewed between updates, though it will not acquire new data by itself.
2. Does Finance need the report produced automatically every day from a connection? That is a new feed, not a correction to the weekly sheet; bring it back for clause 4 approval.

Do not answer the second question by promising the first.
