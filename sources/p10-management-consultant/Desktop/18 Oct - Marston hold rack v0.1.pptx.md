## Slide 3 — Two Columns

Title: What to carry into Monday's briefing

Left heading: Box and tag stay together
- Leave the tag on the held box at change of shift.
- The incoming lead uses the tag and job instruction to verify the run, event and specification.
- A missing tag or a mismatch keeps the box on the hold rack.

Right heading: Approval and return get separate entries
- Record the approval when the check passes.
- When the sheets actually go to good stock, the receiving lead signs and records the time of the move.
- Leave the 17 October return fields unresolved in the original record.
