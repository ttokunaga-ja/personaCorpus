## Slide 2 — Headline and Body
Title: The feeder case pays back in 5.3 years

Body: Trafford T2 replacement requires £384,000 upfront. On annual gross benefits of £73,000, the simple ratio is £384,000 / £73,000 = 5.3 years. The additional equipment recovery assumption is 2.0% of eligible B420 throughput after allowing for the standardisation contribution.

Speaker notes: I have used gross benefit for the headline comparison. I still need to settle the treatment of the extra running cost with Deepa before taking this outside the team.
