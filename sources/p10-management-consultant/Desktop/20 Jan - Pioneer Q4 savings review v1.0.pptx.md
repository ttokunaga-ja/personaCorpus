## Slide 3 — S018: the lower price depends on the Q4 quantity

At 10,000 net cases, the written terms apply the usual USD 19.60 tier. The difference to the USD 18.40 tier available at 12,000 net cases is USD 12,000: 10,000 × (19.60 − 18.40).

Speaker notes: On 15 January Meera confirmed that the linked return of 200 cases reduced 10,200 received to 10,000 net cases. The threshold was not reached and the applied USD 19.60 was in accordance with the written terms. The calculation compares the tier prices on the actual net quantity; it is not an assertion that an agreed discount was withheld.

## Slide 4 — Timing and the allowance remain separate
Layout: Two Columns

Left — S036: receipt timing

The October scenario assumed 10,000 of the actual 12,500 cases would be received from 01 Dec at USD 18.00. In fact, 6,250 were received from that date. The difference is 3,750 × USD 2.00 = USD 7,500. Meera confirmed that receipt date determines the price.

Right — S011: allowance to confirm

The USD 5,500 difference is the residual against the assumed additional allowance. Separate approval and a posting through 12 Jan have not been evidenced. It is not included in posted savings or treated as a confirmed credit.

Speaker notes: Discuss the receipt-date assumption separately from the quantity threshold. Ask Meera what evidence is available for the S011 allowance; retain the 5.6% posted result pending evidence. Do not assign responsibility for the differences. The reconciliation and Buying's written terms provide the supporting detail.
