T2 tomorrow · cues for the handover exercise

Set out the EX-04 box with three sheets, a matching instruction, one instruction for a different specification and a clean station sheet for each round. Reset the box on the hold shelf between rounds.

Hand the box over and ask, “What's happened so far?” Wait for “I've only received it” or words to that effect. Leave the pen on the table. If they reach for it, let them say which field they intend to fill before stepping in.

First pass with the matching instruction. Let the incoming lead do the comparison and state the decision. Watch where they put the approval time. The signature and actual return time go down at the point they move the box to good stock.

Second pass with the other instruction mixed into the papers. Say nothing about the specification. Watch whether they spot the difference and choose to keep the tag with the box on the hold shelf. Ask them to write who they'd tell and what they'd need before trying again.

Get both decisions onto the exercise station sheets before talking through the two answers. Collect the sheets from both groups. Keep the live shift tickets and tags clear of all exercise answers. If receipt and return still get muddled, repeat the handover and have the recorder mark where the decision changed.
