# Pilot briefing board

A photograph taken after Neha and Siobhan finished explaining the pilot to Japan-side participants in a Manchester meeting room. The whiteboard fills most of the frame, with its edges and part of the meeting-room wall visible. No people or screens are in view.

The board shows, legibly: “Components” and “Packaging” at upper left; below them “07 first” → “08 check” → “09”. A separate arrow leaves “08 check” and points back to “Return to AP”. At lower right the words “Two checks stay” are visible. A few erased traces contain no other legible text.
