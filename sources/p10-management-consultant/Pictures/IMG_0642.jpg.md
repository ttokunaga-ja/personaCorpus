## Trafford T2 hold shelf · 22 Oct 2024, 16:54 BST

An oblique view from just above the front of the shelf. One shallow, open brown corrugated box sits on a galvanised metal shelf. Inside, the three S3 first-off sheets lie flat, slightly staggered at one corner so all three edges can be counted. The camera sees into the box and still faces the tag squarely enough to read both of its lower fields. The box and its attached tag fill most of the frame. The right-hand shelf upright and the yellow floor line remain in view. The good-stock side is beyond the line across a clear strip of aisle, separate from the box on the shelf.

A white paper tag with clipped top corners hangs below the horizontal rounded handhold in the near wall. The same pale doubled cotton cord passes through the handhold and the tag's centred round hole, with a small knot above the hole and a frayed end to the right. The lower half of the tag hangs freely in front of the shelf lip. The sheet edges, handhold, cord and complete face of the tag are all in focus.
