# T2 hold rack — where the box waits

## Diagram content

For Caroline Wood · Oliver Hewitt · 18 Nov 2024

A schematic route, read left to right. Show three distinct areas labelled **T2 workbench**, **Hold shelf** and **Good stock**. Mark the boundary immediately before **Good stock** with the label **Release point**. Keep the hold rack on the workbench side of that boundary.

- **T2 workbench:** Show a stack labelled **First 3 sheets after changeover** beside an open box. The sending-shift arrow leads to the hold shelf and reads **Box the sheets and attach the tag**.
- **Hold shelf:** Show HT-07 on the shelf deck with the shallow box facing the viewer and three overlapping sheet edges visible. Use the shelf position in the 22 Oct photograph, IMG_0642, as the reference. A leader reading **HT-07 was here** points to the box's place on the shelf. A different leader, **Tie through the near handle**, points to the string and its attachment to the box. Show the hand-lettered **HOLD** and **HT-07** on the attached tag.
- **Receiving check:** Show a node alongside the shelf, before the release point. Its label is **Receive here · check tag, sheets and job instruction**. Link the shelf to this node with **Take custody; keep on hold**. The receiving-lead arrow from this node crosses the release point to good stock and reads **Match and approve → sign and time the actual move**. A return arrow from the node leads back to the shelf and reads **Missing tag, mismatch or no approval → keep on hold**.
- **Good stock:** Show a separate landing area labelled **Released sheets only**.

## Legend and callout

**Legend:** Solid arrow — sending shift. Double-line arrow — receiving lead. Broken arrow — keep on hold. Fine leader without an arrowhead — location note.

**Callout at the receiving check:** **Could the card go here?** See the receipt and actual return lines before moving the box.
