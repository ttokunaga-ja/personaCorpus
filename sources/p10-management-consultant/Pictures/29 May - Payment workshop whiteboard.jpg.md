A candid meeting-room photograph taken by Nicholas in the Holt & Reeves Manchester office at 09:37 on 29 May 2024. Neha, Connor and Oliver stand around a whiteboard with a window behind them showing a cloudy sky. The board carries a loop of fourteen small sticky notes representing the payment steps. The only legible handwriting is “Ready” at the far left and “07”, “09” and “12” by three points in the route; all other marks and note contents are too small to read. Neha points to a sticky note immediately before 07. A notebook and a glass of water remain on the table in the foreground.
