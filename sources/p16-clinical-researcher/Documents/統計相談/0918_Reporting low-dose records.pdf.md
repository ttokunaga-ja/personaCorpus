# How to describe the low-dose observation records

Document No. KRC-LDO-20260918-SUM-001 · Version v1.0  
Date 2026-09-18 · Author Mitsuko Suzuki  
To Yumika Ishikawa

## Wording for the classification check

“The records were fixed as of 09:00 on 2 September 2026. We checked the classification of all 48 observations against the study's classification record and found no instances of misclassification.”

The classification check covers the category entered for each observation. Use the 48 when describing the extent of that check. For a question about a particular entry on the form, go to the table for that item.

## Wording for the additional-entry count

“An additional-entry count was recorded for 41 of the 44 observations for which that entry was required. Three eligible observations had a missing count. Four observations fell outside the scope of this item.”

In Table 6 of KRC-LDO-20260914-TAB-001 v0.2, the denominator of 44 includes the three missing counts. A zero entered after checking that there were no additional entries is one of the 41 recorded counts.

## If asked to compare categories

As agreed in the read-through with Koichi Goto, describe the availability of records for each eligible item. The classification check and the item counts answer different questions. These results do not establish that recording was more complete in one category, or that the study material caused any difference. For the discussion wording, see KRC-LDO-20260915-QRY-001 v0.2.
