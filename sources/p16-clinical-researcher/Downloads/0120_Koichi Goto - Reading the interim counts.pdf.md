# Reading the interim counts
Reference: YST-LDO-20260120-NOT-001
Date: 2026-01-20
Author: Koichi Goto, Yoshimura Statistics LLC
To: Mitsuko Suzuki, Katsuragawa Clinical Research Center

## Scope
This note concerns the interim table KRC-LDO-20260120-TAB-001 v0.2, cut at 09:00 on 20 January 2026. It describes progress and the state of the records available at that cut, not a final study result.

## Progress and item-specific records
The implementation ledger records a first observation for 31 of the 48 planned participants; 17 remain unobserved. Show these figures together as progress. The observation count is established from the implementation ledger and is not the number with an entry in the visit field.

For the four items, first apply each item's eligibility condition, then describe the presence of a record and the stated reason for any missing entry within that eligible set. The visit record has 30 recorded of 31 eligible participants, dose category 28 of 30, and additional-entry count 26 of 29. A confirmed zero is a recorded value. The 17 not yet observed should remain in the progress description, not be allocated to item-specific missing reasons.

## Query response dates
The response-date denominator consists of the 12 query rows issued for these observation records by the cut. Eight rows have a response date; four are awaiting confirmation. Restricting the denominator to the eight rows with dates would remove the very rows whose status needs to be described. These are query rows, not participants.

## Limits of interpretation
Four items have missing entries; that does not establish four participants with missing data. The eligible sets can differ and the query rows are a different unit, so do not add missing counts across items or convert them to a participant count. The interim records do not support a conclusion from a between-group comparison. Continue to follow the pending queries and reassess at the next cut.
