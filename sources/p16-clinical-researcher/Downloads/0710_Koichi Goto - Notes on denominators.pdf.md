# Notes on denominators
Reference: YST-LDO-20240710-NOT-001
Date: 2024-07-10
Author: Koichi Goto, Yoshimura Statistics LLC
To: Mitsuko Suzuki, Katsuragawa Clinical Research Center

## Scope
These preliminary notes address questions 4, 6 and 8 in KRC-LDO-20240628-QRY-001 v1.0. They concern the planned assessment of recordability, not an estimate of an effect.

## 4. Denominator
Define the eligible set separately for each item before assessing whether a value is present. Exclude records outside that item's scope from its denominator; do not count them as missing. The planned 48 participants are not automatically the denominator for every item. For the response-date item, please bring the proposed definition of an issued query row to our discussion on 18 July, including how multiple queries for one participant are handled.

## 6. Entered zero
A zero actually recorded after checking the number of additions is a valid recorded value. Preserve it as zero and include it in the recorded count. An empty field within scope is not evidence of zero; an outside-scope field is a different state again. The old output obscures these distinctions.

## 8. Reason unresolved
Show an unresolved reason explicitly as under query or awaiting confirmation rather than assigning zero or a definite reason without evidence. Keep the outstanding count visible alongside the other reasons. We should discuss a footnote for cases still unresolved at the reporting cut-off and how the classification is revisited after a response.
