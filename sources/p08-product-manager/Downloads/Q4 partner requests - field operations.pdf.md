# Q4 partner requests — field operations

DDP-202609-RQ-001 | v1.0 | 02 Sep 2026 09:14 JST  
Prepared by Nathan Hammond, Partner Sales  
To: Yumiko Ishida, Olta Field Systems  
Sales period: October–December 2026

Yumiko,

The following questions have come to our existing partner sales desk. I have separated the situations even where they may touch the same screen or service. There are 16 requests here, not 16 customers or sites. Could we go through what the current product can support and which questions need further work before our team answers them?

| No. | Request | Use and question |
|---|---|---|
| DDP-01 | Site Board at all sites | A prospect wants to use the board at each site at the same time. When could all sites start, and what would need to be confirmed first? |
| DDP-02 | Start from an earlier request | An intake colleague has a recurring inspection to arrange and wants to begin with a request already submitted at the same site. Where should they start, and which earlier request can they use? |
| DDP-03 | Reuse across sites | A coordinator wants to start a request at one site using an earlier request from another. Is that possible, and what can the person see across the site boundary? |
| DDP-04 | Approve several requests together | An approver has several requests waiting after a shift. Can they approve them in one action, and what happens if one cannot be processed? |
| DDP-05 | Review copied details | An inspection lead is preparing a repeat request but the location or date may have changed. Which details come across, which can be checked or changed, and what happens to the requested date? |
| DDP-06 | Carry forward the requested date | A team often repeats a visit on a similar schedule. Could the previous requested date be carried into the new request so the team can adjust it? |
| DDP-07 | Email due-date reminders | A supervisor wants a reminder before an inspection request is due. Can a reminder be emailed to the people responsible, and how are recipients chosen? |
| DDP-08 | Help when the original cannot be opened | An intake colleague cannot open the earlier request they were told to use. Who can check whether that person may view that particular request, and what should the desk say while waiting? |
| DDP-09 | View aggregates across sites | A manager wants a combined view of results across sites. Which roles could see the aggregates, and how would access be removed when a role changes? |
| DDP-10 | Scheduled CSV delivery | An operations team wants a CSV of the results delivered on a regular schedule. Can it be sent to a chosen destination and recipients, and can the delivery be traced? |
| DDP-11 | Distinguish a draft from a submission | A new colleague is being trained to repeat a request and is unsure whether starting a draft sends it. What must they do to submit, and does the original change? |
| DDP-12 | Single sign-on | An administrator wants staff to use an existing sign-in process. Can this be connected, and what happens when access is withdrawn? |
| DDP-13 | English product interface | English-speaking staff want to work in the product itself, not just read a sales explanation. Which screens could be shown in English? |
| DDP-14 | Setup performed for the customer | A customer asks for help configuring the service at introduction. Can Olta perform the setup, and where would the customer's responsibilities begin? |
| DDP-15 | Day-to-day operations service | A customer asks whether someone can run the recurring inspection process for them. Is this part of the usual support desk, or a separate operational service? |
| DDP-16 | Longer request retention | A team wants to refer to older requests beyond its present working period. Can the retention period be extended, and what information and deletion conditions would need discussion? |

Please help us distinguish an explanation of an existing procedure from a new capability or an operational commitment. A question in this brief is a request for clarification; the sales desk will wait for your response before describing availability or dates.
