# Answers to keep beside the call
OFS-202609-RP-005　v1.0　11 September 2026　Yumiko Ishida
For Nathan Hammond and the Dalton partner sales desk
Calendar Q4 2026 / FY2026 Q3

## Use on the call
These answers describe existing steps in Patrol Check. They do not authorize a new feature, access change or rollout. Record the question and return it when the conditions below are not met.

## Questions and answers
| Question | Answer on the call | Return or check |
|---|---|---|
| Can I start from a request at another site? | Start from a submitted request at the same site that you can view. Cross-site reuse is under review. | Send the site boundary question to Ishida; do not suggest using another site's request. |
| Does the previous requested date come across? | The new requested date starts blank. Review the copied equipment category, request type and inspection location, then enter the date needed for the new request. | If the customer needs the old date carried over, record the need for Ishida. |
| Can you open the original for me? | Ask your site administrator to confirm access to that request for you. | Record the person and request for the administrator. Until an answer about both is received, it is awaiting confirmation. The sales desk does not grant access. |
| Is the new draft already sent? | A new draft is unsent until you review and submit it. The original request stays unchanged. | If the user is unsure of the current step, ask them to review the draft and submission status. |
| When will Site Board be released everywhere? | A wider Site Board rollout remains under review. No rollout dates have been set. | Record the sites in question and refer the timing question to Ishida. Do not promise a date or enablement. |

## Scope
English briefing material does not establish English product UI availability. Record the screens needed and refer that question to Ishida separately from a rollout question.
