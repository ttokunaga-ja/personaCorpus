# Slide 2 — “Can you give me access to the original?”
レイアウト：2段
左：A user cannot open the submitted request they want to reuse. They ask the partner to give them access.
右： “Ask your site administrator to confirm access to that request for you. We will wait for the administrator's answer.” Record the person and the particular request.
発表者ノート：Continue to the site administrator for a decision about that person's access to that request. Record whether the answer has arrived, its content and date. A request sent to the administrator is still pending; the partner does not change permissions or route around the problem via another site.

# Slide 4 — “Which month will every site start?”

左：The customer asks for a start month across all sites for Site Board.
右： “A wider Site Board rollout remains under review. No rollout dates have been set. Which sites are you asking about?”
