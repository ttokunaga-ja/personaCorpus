# What to record when a rollout question comes back
OFS-202609-EX-001　v1.0　17 September 2026 14:40 JST
Prepared by Yumiko Ishida for Shuichi Fujiwara and Yuji Nishimura
Calendar Q4 2026 / FY2026 Q3

These four requests remain on hold. Capture the question and pass it to Ishida; a recorded request is not a release decision.

| Request | Record from the sales desk | Internal follow-up after Ishida receives it |
|---|---|---|
| DDP-01 — Site Board rollout | Which sites are requested? Is simultaneous use required? Record the requested scope, not a promised date. | Matsuda checks excluded rows and counts and exclusion after revocation; Miura checks the source table for the same cutoff and scope. Fukuda decides publication after evidence is reviewed. |
| DDP-09 — cross-site aggregate viewing | Which roles need to view which aggregates? Keep viewer scope separate from the requested sites for DDP-01. | Matsuda checks role scope and evidence of exclusion after access is revoked. |
| DDP-10 — scheduled CSV delivery | What destination and recipients are needed? Record the intended schedule as a request, not a commitment. | Ota and Matsuda check destination, audit records and expired permissions. |
| DDP-13 — English UI | Which screens and terms are needed? English briefing documents do not establish English UI coverage. | Ishida and Okamoto identify the screens and terminology to check. |

If a person cannot open an original request, separately record the administrator's answer about that person and that request, including the answer date; a request for confirmation alone remains pending. Keep that answer separate from the UI screen request and Fukuda's publication decision. This sheet is for routing enquiries; it does not revise the distributed product briefing.
