Hi Laura,

Thank you for the careful questions. My answers in order:

> Is the person handling an application expected to change the destination routing, or only to check the destination shown before submission?

The intended action is to check the displayed destination, not change the routing. I will use “review the destination”. I also accept “received” for the receipt state: a number does not mean that the business process is complete.

> Does an ordinary user need to investigate the attachment history, or is that an investigation task for someone else?

The history belongs to the investigation side. Similarly, checking the log is an investigation task, so I will remove it as an instruction to the user. The user can check the result before considering another submission.

> Who should receive the explanations for the ten operation topics and the six operational topics?

I will hold the 10-to-6 placement until I confirm the responsibilities with Takahashi and Watanabe on 19 May. I will come back to you after that discussion.
