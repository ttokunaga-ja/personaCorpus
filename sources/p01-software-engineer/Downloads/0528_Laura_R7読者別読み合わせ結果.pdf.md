# R7: audience and language read-through

HES-2026-ER-004　v1.0  
28 May 2026  
Laura Nichols, Harlow Editorial Services  
Prepared for Miyu Tanaka, Asahi Business Systems

## Material and purpose

I read through the two v0.3 PDFs shared on 22 May: ABS-2026-EX-002, guidance for reception staff, and ABS-2026-EX-003, investigation guidance for administrators. This report records the audience and English-language check against those versions. It is not a check of the R7 system or a release recommendation.

## Read-through findings

| Material | Passage checked | Reading and outcome |
|---|---|---|
| EX-002, U01 | “It does not mean that the business process has finished.” | Retain this distinction. “Received” identifies receipt, not completion of the business work. |
| EX-002, U02 | “check the saved result before deciding whether another submission is needed” | Retain the sequence: check first, then consider a resend. The following sentence directs a reader who cannot establish the result to raise an enquiry. |
| EX-003, A03 | “distinguish the attachment name currently displayed from the history of replacements” | Retain the distinction for the investigation team. The current name is not a complete history. |

The reception draft has ten points, U01–U10, organised around checks before submission, checking a result before a possible resend, and enquiries using list and detail. The administrator draft has six points, A01–A06, for comparing records and recording or handing over an investigation. The reception text does not instruct its reader to investigate logs; the history comparison is addressed to the investigation side. I would keep “review the destination” separate from the administrator's comparison with internal routing, so that a check is not read as a request to change settings.

## English retained and matters outside this review

| English and audience review: retained | Matters remaining with Tanaka's team |
|---|---|
| Receipt versus business completion; the result check before a possible resend; the separation between the current attachment name and investigation of its history. | Compare the proposed R7 behaviour with the actual system, including destination, replacement display and resend result. The present wording is not evidence that those behaviours have been verified. |
| Ten reception points and six administrator points in separate drafts; no reception instruction to investigate logs. | The reception method and the decision whether or when to publish remain open. This review does not supply approval or a publication date. |

The reservations in both drafts should stay with the text until the relevant checks and decisions are made. The language and audience review is complete for the two shared v0.3 PDFs; it does not promote either document to a release notice.
