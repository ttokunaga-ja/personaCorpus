# Application Desk R7: meaning and reader actions

HES-2026-ER-001　v1.0  
8 May 2026  
Laura Nichols, Harlow Editorial Services  
Prepared for Miyu Tanaka, Asahi Business Systems

## Purpose

I reviewed ABS-2026-EX-001 v0.1 for expressions that could blur receipt and completion or assign a task to the wrong reader. The draft combines screen actions and investigation. The examples below describe possible readings, not confirmed behaviour of R7.

## Findings

### U01 — Receipt number and completion

The draft says: “Your application is completed when a receipt number appears.” A reader could see the number and stop checking, believing that the underlying business process has finished. The number indicates receipt, not the outcome of subsequent work. I suggest replacing that sentence with: “Your application has been received when a receipt number appears.” The following instruction to retain the number remains useful, but the distinction from processing should also be clear in the surrounding explanation.

### U03 — Destination and routing

The draft says: “Change the routing before you submit.” A person preparing an application could look for a routing setting before sending it, rather than checking the destination displayed on the application. Who is expected to change the routing, and what can the person submitting actually check? Please confirm the actor before I suggest a final instruction.

### A01 — Investigation before resending

The draft says: “Check the log before sending the application again.” An ordinary reader could try to open a log before resending, even if investigation is another person's responsibility. Who examines the log when a possible duplicate is reported? Please confirm the actor and what the submitting reader should do when the result is uncertain.

## Reader placement and next step

It may help to move investigation material to a separate administrator explanation while retaining the checks a submitting reader can perform in the user notice. This is a proposal for discussion, not an agreed allocation. Please clarify the U03 and A01 responsibilities so that the next revision can distinguish a visible check from internal investigation. This review does not approve implementation or publication.
