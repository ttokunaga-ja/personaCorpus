# R7: editorial questions after the audience split

HES-2026-ER-003　v1.0  
20 May 2026  
Laura Nichols, Harlow Editorial Services  
Prepared for Miyu Tanaka, Asahi Business Systems

## Purpose and scope

At the 19 May discussion the team agreed to retain U01–U10 for reception staff and move A01–A06 to an explanation for administrators investigating enquiries. The split gives each reader a clearer purpose. This report sets the reading criteria for the two drafts planned for 21 May; it does not establish that the proposed screen behaviour works or that R7 is approved for release.

## Points to resolve in the drafts

| Item | Reading criterion | Question for the draft |
|---|---|---|
| U02 | Distinguish a receipt number that can be checked from a number the reader cannot find. | If the number is available, direct the reader to check the result before considering resending. If it is unknown, do not imply that resending is automatically safe; explain that the situation needs to be raised for enquiry. |
| U04 | Before confirmation, identify the displayed attachment name as the object of the check. | Do not ask the reception reader to inspect replacement history or imply that a proposed update has already been verified. |
| A03 | Place history comparison with the administrator responding to an enquiry. | Separate the current displayed name from the replacement history being investigated; do not make this a new reception duty. |

For U03 and A02, preserve the distinction between reviewing a displayed destination and investigating internal routing. The former must not sound like an instruction to change a setting. For U01, receipt is not completion of the underlying business process.

## Review boundary and next check

On 28 May I will check the English and intended readership with Tanaka. We should identify wording that promises behaviour not yet verified or implies additional permissions. Those matters need confirmation, not an editorial assumption. The review will not verify the live screen, decide the reception method, or approve publication. Please retain the open status in both drafts.
