# p18 Full content spine — manufacturing quality engineering

## Purpose and boundary

This is the fixed, synthetic facts contract for p18 Full artifacts. It is a
content-consistency aid only: it does not claim Kio preparation, indexing,
history, chunks, search, or performance. Every artifact is fictional,
defensive quality-system material; it contains no real supplier, employee,
customer, patient, credential, or production data.

- `term.fmea` links failure modes, effects, controls, and residual risk.
- `term.capa` links NCR-042, the 5-Why record, containment, verification, and
  effectiveness evidence.
- `term.traceability` links lot, inspection result, evidence ID, and revision.
- `term.synthetic-only` marks records that must remain fictional and safe.
- `rel.ncr-to-capa` connects NCR-042 to CAPA-018.
- `rel.capa-to-eco` connects CAPA-018 to ECO-009.
- `rel.supplier-to-evidence` connects SQ-184 to evidence set 184.

- `rel.scan-source` requires an image-only scan PDF to be derived from its

## Required safety statement

Every Full artifact must preserve `term.synthetic-only`, use only reserved
documentation addresses if a network example is necessary, avoid executable
or operational industrial-control instructions, and make no claim beyond
synthetic artifact creation. `term.no-kio-claim` is required wherever a
content-spine identifier is recorded.
